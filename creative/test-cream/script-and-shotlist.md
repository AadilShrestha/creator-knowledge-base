# Test Cream: script and production plan

## Character and continuity

**Maya:** fictional adult presenter, approximately 29. Shoulder-length dark wavy hair clipped loosely back; warm medium-brown complexion with natural skin texture; charcoal cotton T-shirt; short unpainted nails. Keep face, hairstyle, wardrobe, skin texture, voice, and hand appearance consistent. No real-person likeness is requested.

**Performance:** lightly amused at the routine, never disgusted with her face. Conversational English, warm mid-range voice, gentle dry humor. Address the viewer like a peer. Leave small pauses for the joke; one subtle half-smile is enough. Avoid exaggerated influencer reactions.

**Location:** modest bathroom, off-white tile, pale wood counter, three generic unbranded skincare bottles, a phone, one towel. Warm diffused vanity lighting; no abrupt switch to a glam beauty-lighting setup at the reveal. Keep the skin and exposure consistent throughout.

**Product design assumption:** small ivory cylindrical jar, matte sage lid. Add the exact name **TEST CREAM** as a controlled graphic/label where needed; check its spelling and orientation. This is a prop concept, not a supplied product pack shot.

Approximate target: 30 seconds. The schedule is editorial, not a promise that a generation model accepts these exact durations. Render the chosen voice, measure it, then conform the edit. The draft is about 65 spoken words.

## Hook variants

Change only the opening line and its matching first-shot performance. Retain the body and all other production variables.

- **A / recognition:** “Why does getting ready for bed need this many instructions?”
- **B / humor:** “Is this skincare, or am I revising for an exam?”
- **C / routine overload:** “The skincare routine is getting longer than the actual evening.”

These are alternative openings, not a claim that one is proven to win. Measure actual audio duration before fitting each into the same opening window.

## Edit plan

- 9:16 composition; target 1080 × 1920 delivery if available. Keep critical faces, jar, and text away from placement-specific UI overlays. Check the actual placement template before export.
- Simple hard cuts between creator shots and inserts. A little real phone-camera texture; no cinematic orbit, dramatic rack focus, sparkle, or beauty filter.
- One voice for the whole ad. Sync visible speaking shots to the matching audio segments; use voiceover for inserts. Split generated footage into model-supported durations and leave edit handles where possible.
- Add captions from the final spoken audio after generation. Keep product name and closing CTA under direct editorial control. A quiet room tone is enough; optional music must not compete with speech.
- Small concept label for the review version: **FICTIONAL PRODUCT · AI PRESENTER CONCEPT**. Review version is a demo, not a published ad.

## QA before approval

- Identity, hairstyle, shirt, nails, room layout, and lighting match across shots.
- Mouth movement follows the selected audio in visible talking shots.
- Hands and lid opening are anatomically/plausibly continuous; jar dimensions and label do not drift.
- The application shot changes no skin marks, complexion, texture, or exposure.
- Script is exact; no model-added claims, extra CTA, fake review, discount, ingredient, or endorsement.
- Product name is TEST CREAM, readable where intended and never mirrored.
- Every line can be spoken comfortably; audio and total runtime have been measured before calling the video finished.
- Hook test changes only the opening; no performance guarantees are inferred from generation or an automated ranking score.
