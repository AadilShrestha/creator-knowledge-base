# Write a product page / landing page

You turn real product facts into a page that sells to the traffic arriving on it. Quality comes from, in order: **the page's job** (what the ad already sold), **one ownable mechanism**, **specific scenes in customer language**, and **an offer that is easy to say yes to**. Proof can only be what is real; everything else is a labelled slot.

```
- [ ] 0 Intake: product truth sheet, policies, traffic source, real proof inventory
- [ ] 1 Job + length + awareness level set (one sentence)
- [ ] 2 Mechanism: UMP → UMS written in one line each
- [ ] 3 Section outline picked (blueprint below or a reference PDP's structure)
- [ ] 4 Draft written (copy + design notes, no HTML)
- [ ] 5 Claim strip: every proof element real or a [REAL:] slot
- [ ] 6 Scored with references/pdp-scorecard.md (fresh reviewer, blind if several drafts)
- [ ] 7 Revised until ≥ 90 and no auto-fail
- [ ] 8 Output in the template
```

## Step 0: Intake
| Input | Source |
|---|---|
| Product truth sheet: ingredients/specs, doses, price, variants, what it does and how | label, product JSON, brief |
| Policies: refund, shipping times, subscription terms, support email | store policy pages; the page must match them word for word |
| Traffic: ad format and length, pre-sell page yes/no, awareness level of the ads | media buyer or ad library |
| Real proof inventory: review count/rating from a review app, real reviews with consent, COA, certificates, named experts, founder story, real order counts | owner only. Anything not listed here does not exist |
| Claims allowed / forbidden: regulated category (supplements, kids, health) | [compliance red flags](../../../knowledge-base/wiki/courses/stefan-georgi/rmbc-ii/concepts/compliance-red-flags.md) |

Missing inputs: ask once, listing every gap. If you can't ask, bracket the gaps and carry on.

## Step 1: Job, length, awareness
- Length by traffic (RMBC [PDP build](../../../knowledge-base/wiki/courses/stefan-georgi/rmbc-ii/frameworks/pdp-build.md) Step 1): cold traffic from statics or <60 s video with no pre-sell → **long "mini sales letter"** PDP (why other fixes failed, real cause, why ours works). Long video or long advertorial in front → short page.
- Awareness (Start Here [market awareness levels](../../../knowledge-base/wiki/courses/evolve/start-here/concepts/market-awareness-levels.md)): match the page to the ad's level. Problem/solution-aware parents who have "tried everything" need the reassignment ("it was never you, it was the wrong half") before the product.
- Sophistication (Start Here [market sophistication levers](../../../knowledge-base/wiki/courses/evolve/start-here/concepts/market-sophistication-levers.md)): in a saturated market, win on a new mechanism or new information, not a bigger claim.
- Write: "Traffic arrives from [X] at [awareness]; the page must still sell [Y]; length [long/short]."

## Step 2: Mechanism
- UMP (why the problem persists / why other fixes failed) → UMS (why this works). One plain line each, visible to a skimmer in the hero. See [unique mechanism](../../../knowledge-base/wiki/courses/stefan-georgi/rmbc-ii/concepts/unique-mechanism.md) and Start Here [product overview and mechanism](../../../knowledge-base/wiki/courses/evolve/start-here/concepts/product-overview-and-mechanism.md).
- The mechanism must be **true to the label** and **checkable** (Evolve falsifiable test). Rename it so nobody else can say it.

## Step 3: Section blueprint (long PDP, cold traffic)
Use this unless a working reference PDP in the vertical gives a better structure (RMBC Steps 2–5: extract structure, never copy).

| # | Section | Job | Course basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Announcement bar | Guarantee or shipping fact that is true | RMBC 10-02 |
| 2 | Hero + buy box above the fold (mobile) | One "you" message, mechanism in one line, 3–5 benefit bullets ≤2 lines, real proof strip, CTA that echoes the offer | Tether message pass; RMBC mechanism visibility |
| 3 | Problem in scenes | 2–4 specific moments in the customer's words | Evolve show-don't-tell, customer language |
| 4 | Reassignment | Why everything they tried failed (UMP), without blaming them | Evolve objections L28; RMBC UMP |
| 5 | Mechanism (UMS) | How it works, steps tied to label ingredients, honest research with limits | RMBC UMS; Evolve authority |
| 6 | Buy point | CTA right after conviction | Tether CTA pass |
| 7 | What changes (future pacing) | The routine and the days they want, not promised results | RMBC Step 7 |
| 8 | Proof | Real proof today + labelled slots for reviews/counts | see Proof slots |
| 9 | Comparison | Us vs what they already tried, checkable facts only | Evolve comparisons, different not better |
| 10 | Ingredients/label | Every dose, why each is there | Evolve facts |
| 11 | Offer / buy box | Per-bottle and per-day price vs category, tiers, guarantee in policy words | RMBC price justification; Tether Pass 5; offer psychology |
| 12 | FAQ | Top objections, safety, medication, age, shipping | Tether ability (Fogg) |
| 13 | Guarantee | Risk reversal that matches the policy | Tether buy box |
| 14 | Final CTA | Restate mechanism + offer | RMBC repeat core value props |

Sticky mobile CTA bar. Buttons say what they get ("Start My Child's First Bottle"), never "Shop" or "Learn more".

## Step 4: Draft
- Copy with formatting and short `[DESIGN: …]` notes. No HTML.
- Grade ≤ 6 for body copy; spoken, not written; facts over opinions; one idea per line. Evolve [copy checklist](../../../knowledge-base/wiki/courses/evolve/copywriting/rubrics/copy-checklist-scorecard.md).
- No repetition of the same explanation in two sections (Tether: page fatigue).

## Step 5: Proof slots (never invent proof)
- Numbers, ratings, reviews, testimonials, survey results, expert names, press logos and sold-out counts appear **only** as slots: `Trusted by [REAL: customer count from Shopify]+ parents`, `★ [REAL: rating] from [REAL: review count] reviews`, `[REAL: review — verified buyer, first name + child's age]`.
- A slot is a design decision (where the number goes, how it is worded). It is never a guess at the value. No example numbers inside slots.
- The page must convert with **every slot empty**: lead with proof that is true today (label transparency, doses, COA link, guarantee, mechanism you can check, founder story if real).
- Run the RMBC [LLM output claim audit](../../../knowledge-base/wiki/courses/stefan-georgi/rmbc-ii/rubrics/llm-output-claim-audit.md) on the draft. Supplements: structure/function wording + FDA disclaimer; no disease, cure or drug-replacement claims; no "safe with everything".

## Step 6–7: Score and revise
- Score with [references/pdp-scorecard.md](references/pdp-scorecard.md). Auto-fails first.
- Use a **fresh reviewer** (new agent or model) that did not write the draft. With several drafts, label them X/Y/Z and hide which model wrote which.
- Fix the lowest-scoring sections first. Re-score. Stop at ≥ 90 with no auto-fail, or after 4 rounds (report the score honestly if it stalls).

### Multi-model mode
1. Write one brief (product truth sheet, job sentence, mechanism, constraints) to a file.
2. Three writers draft independently from the brief and this skill (e.g. Opus, Sonnet, Haiku).
3. One blind reviewer scores all drafts and names the best plus the best parts of the others.
4. The best draft's writer (or Opus) revises using the review, folding in named parts of the losers.
5. A fresh reviewer re-scores. Loop to ≥ 90.

## Step 8: Output template
```
# <Product>: product page copy v<n>
## Owner checklist      ← every [REAL:] and [VERIFY:] slot listed, sign-offs needed
## Job + mechanism      ← Step 1 sentence, UMP line, UMS line
# PAGE COPY             ← sections in order, [DESIGN:] notes inline
## Scorecard            ← final reviewer score block
```
