# Script checklist

Answer every item **yes / no / n.a.**, quoting the line or beat behind the answer. "n.a." needs a reason.
- Any ⛔ "no" → fix before delivering.
- 3 or more other "no" → revise and re-check.

This merges Tether's UGC pre-flight checklist with Evolve's copy checklist and truth gates, as yes/no checks. The thresholds are our pipeline design, not the courses'.

## ⛔ Truth and originality (blocking)
1. ⛔ **Claims traceable.** Every product claim, number and ingredient appears in the inputs, or is in [brackets] under "Verify before filming". Assumed usage details (where/how much/how long) are bracketed in **every** place they appear, including hooks and on-screen text. [E G1–G2, T F]
1b. ⛔ **Mechanism and offer exact.** The "why it works" is the mechanism in the product facts, not a reviewer's opinion. The offer, guarantee and returns are stated exactly as given, not broadened. [E G1–G2]
2. ⛔ **No invented proof.** No made-up reviews, quotes, counts, ratings, studies, endorsements, results or before/afters. Customer quotes appear only if supplied, and close to the original wording. A customer's result is never restated as a general promise to the viewer. [E G3, T F]
3. ⛔ **No fake urgency or fear.** Scarcity and deadlines are real; fear is not exaggerated. [E G4, T F]
4. ⛔ **Mechanism, not asset.** No lines (5+ consecutive words), characters, footage or claims from a competitor, reference or golden example. [E G5, T F]
5. ⛔ **Honest voice.** AI narration is third person. Every creator "I" experience line (what they tried, felt, doubted) is listed under "Verify before filming" as "creator must really have experienced this". No reviewer's experience is put in the creator's mouth unless it is also true for them. [E G6]
6. ⛔ **Category limits.** No disease, cure or "clinically proven" claims beyond what's documented; required qualifiers present. [T F]

## Angle and hook
7. **One core message and one core emotion.** The first 3 seconds make the problem or payoff clear enough for the right viewer to self-select (the product can wait for the bridge). [T A, B; default E#28]
8. **Swap test passes.** Put a competitor's name in the hook and key lines: they no longer fit, because of a specific mechanism, detail or situation. [E B3]
9. **The hook has a gap, an emotion and stakes**, starts in the action, and has no "In this video…" or backstory. [E A3]
10. **On-screen hook text ≤7 words**, and each hook has a first-visual note. [T B]
11. **3 hooks test 3 different levers** (not 3 wordings of one idea). [T B, E L72]

## Body
12. **Customer language.** At least 2 phrases come from the supplied customer language (n.a. only if none was supplied, and the output says so). [E D6, T D]
13. **Specific situation.** The problem is a concrete moment (time, place, object, behaviour), not a general claim. [T D "four levels deep"]
14. **Mechanism explained and shown.** The script says how it works, not just what it's called, and a visual shows it. [E A4]
15. **Specific objection or realism.** Names the actual past attempts or the doubt; no overnight results. [E D8, T C]
16. **Benefit shown as behaviour or experience**, not a list of benefits. [E D4]
17. **Facts over opinions.** The key claim can be checked true or false ("made with four ingredients", not "high quality"). [E B2, D5]

## Language and production
18. **Spoken and simple.** Every line sounds natural said aloud; grade-6 words; contractions; nothing can be deleted without losing meaning. [E C2–C4]
19. **No AI tells:** "game-changer", "say goodbye to", "unlock", "elevate", "revolutionize", "Are you tired of", "Introducing", stacked adjectives, rhetorical triplets. *(Our addition.)*
20. **Definitive CTA** tied to the hook and the real offer. Never "Shop now" or "Learn more". [T G]
21. **Fits the length.** About 2.5 spoken words per second; one-take demos get their real duration (or a marked speed-up). Every beat has a visual note and at most one on-screen line. [T C, E]
22. **Test-ready.** 3 hooks share one body (and one hold/CTA); "verify before filming" items and locked words are listed. [T H, E packaging]

## Verdict
```yaml
checklist:
  blocking_failed: []        # any → fix before delivering
  other_failed: []           # 3+ → revise and re-check
  evidence: {"8": "H2 'sleep better tonight' fits any brand → rewritten to …"}
  verdict: pass | revise | block
```
