**Findings**
- The skill won 5 of 6 pairs, with bigger gains on Sonnet (3/3, one clear) than on Opus (2/3, slight). With a strong model working inside the repo, the old knowledge base was already decent. The skill mainly helps cheaper models, consistency and speed: about 10–15 tool calls and no wandering through the wiki.
- Nobody invented proof. The slips were small: assumed usage details not bracketed in hooks or on-screen text, mechanism drift (a reviewer's "it's heavy" became the mechanism), a customer result restated as a promise, offer terms paraphrased more broadly, and one-take demos squeezed into slots that are too short.
- **Changes made after this review:** SKILL.md step 7 and checklist items 1, 1b, 2 and 21 now target exactly those slips. Re-test: `runs/skill-sonnet-v2/`.
- Caveats: n = 3 briefs per model, one judge, and an AI judge (which may prefer AI-style writing). The briefs are fictional. Add real briefs and a human pick before trusting small margins.
