# GripBoard: on-camera UGC video script (Meta / TikTok), 3 hooks + full script

**Length:** about 42 s (cap 45 s). **Format:** one creator, selfie plus counter-top demo, 9:16, captions on. **Packaging:** 3 hook-and-bridge openings × 1 shared body. Hold and CTA are identical across the three, so a test result tells you which opening worked.

## What I did and assumed (the client was not available)
- I wrote only from the product facts in the brief. I added nothing the brief doesn't support.
- **I did not claim any of these**, because the brief doesn't support them: antibacterial or sanitary, scar-proof or knife-sharpening (as our own claim), "lasts for years", dishwasher-safe, a discount, or scarcity. The brief says not to claim antibacterial. Review 5 ("my knives stay sharper") is a customer's opinion. It stays out of the creator's mouth and is only an optional on-screen quote.
- **Assumption on the weight claim:** the brief credits the silicone feet for "does not slide". Review 3 credits the weight. The script says the board is heavy and the feet grip, and never says the weight is the reason.
- **Assumption on oiling frequency:** the "once a month" oiling comes from review 6, not the product facts. The script says only that the board comes with oil.
- **Creator-confirm lines:** lines marked (C) are first-person experience (an old plastic board that slid and scarred, saying "$79 for a cutting board" out loud). They should only be filmed by a creator for whom they are true. If not true, switch to the fallback in the brief notes below.
- **Reviews:** I used them as customer language to shape the hooks and the pain point. I did not turn them into proof the creator says. Each review needs a source (platform, date) and permission before it goes on screen. Neither is in the brief.

## 3 hook options
Each tests a different lever. The spoken line is the creator's. The on-screen text is 7 words or fewer.

| # | Spoken (0-3 s) | On-screen text | First visual | Lever tested |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | "My cutting board kept skating across the counter." (C) | "Your cutting board shouldn't skate." | Real shot of her old plastic board creeping as she chops a squash. | Specific pain situation. The viewer self-selects as someone who has been there. |
| H2 | "I shoved this cutting board as hard as I could." | "Shove test." | Hand shoves GripBoard across the counter. It doesn't move. | Demo-first proof of the main benefit, with an open loop ("what happened?"). |
| H3 | "Seventy-nine dollars. For a cutting board." (C) | "$79. For a cutting board?" | Creator holds the board up to camera with a deadpan look. | Price objection said out loud first. The creator voices the viewer's doubt, then answers it. |

### Matched bridges (3-8 s)
Each bridge hands off to the same body. Film them as separate takes.

| Hook | Bridge line (spoken) | Visual |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | "Squash, onions, whatever. Chop, shove it back, chop, shove it back." (C) | Old board creeping; hand nudging it back. |
| H2 | "Nothing. Not an inch. My old plastic one? It crept every single cut." (C) | Slow-mo of the shove; cut to the old board. |
| H3 | "Yeah, I said that too. Then I thought about how much I fight my old board." (C) | Quick cut of old, scarred plastic board. |

## Full script (shared body, same for H1, H2, H3)

| Beat | Time | Spoken (creator may rephrase, except the locked items listed below) | On-screen text | Visual / b-roll | Delivery |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hook + bridge | 0-8 s | See the table above | See the table above | See the table above | Fast, deadpan, a little annoyed. |
| Problem | 8-13 | "I cook most nights, and my old plastic board was scarred and never stayed put." (C) | "Cooks most nights." | Old board on the counter: visible scars, sliding on a cut. | Plain, relatable. |
| Turn: product and mechanism | 13-19 | "So I switched to GripBoard: end-grain acacia on this side, silicone feet on all four corners." | "End-grain acacia. 4 silicone feet." | Tilt the board to show the grain. Close-up of a corner foot. | Show, don't oversell. |
| Proof 1: grip | 19-25 | "Watch me lean on it. Not moving. And yes, it's heavy: six pounds." | "6 lb." | One continuous shot: chopping and leaning with a chef's knife, board stays put. Lift it toward camera so the weight shows. | Calm, pleasantly surprised. |
| Proof 2: groove | 25-31 | "Flip it: juice groove. Meat juice stays in the groove, not on my counter." | "Juice groove side." | Flip the board. Rest or cut real meat. Juice collects in the groove. Counter stays clean. | Matter-of-fact. |
| Honest beat | 31-37 | "Hand wash only, no dishwasher. Comes with board oil. It's 17 by 12, so measure your counter." | "Hand wash only. 17 x 12 in." | Hand-washing the board in the sink. Hold up the oil bottle. Tape measure or hands showing the size. | Straight. This line builds trust. |
| CTA | 37-42 | "It's $79, free shipping, 30 days to send it back. Tap to see GripBoard." | "$79 · Free shipping · 30-day returns" | Product in hand, end card with the offer. | Definitive. Looks to camera. |

**About 107 spoken words.** Read it aloud once and time it. If it runs over 45 s, use the 30 s cut below.

### 30 s cut
Use hook and bridge (8 s), then the turn, Proof 1, Proof 2, a shortened honest beat ("Hand wash only, 17 by 12"), and the CTA. Cut the Problem line, since the bridge already covers it. Do not cut the hand-wash and size honesty.

## Locked words vs creator's own words
- **Locked (say as written):** end-grain acacia, silicone feet on all four corners, six pounds, 17 by 12, hand wash only / no dishwasher, comes with board oil, $79, free shipping, 30-day returns.
- **Creator's own words:** everything else, including the hooks and bridges. Keep the meaning. Don't add claims.
- **Creator must not add:** "antibacterial", "won't scar", "lasts forever", "never slides on any surface", a sale or countdown, or an oiling schedule.

**Optional on-screen review cards.** If the client supplies sources and permission, these can run as quotes during Proof 1 and Proof 2:
- Review 1: "This thing does not move. At all."
- Review 4: "Turkey juice stayed ON the board."
- Do not use review 5 as a claim.
- Do not use review 6 in the ad, or use it only as the honest beat. Its "oil once a month" detail is the customer's, not a product spec.

## Notes for the editor and creator
- **Frame 1 carries the message** in the on-screen text. About 2 in 3 viewers may watch muted, so the text must work alone.
- **Cut pace:** a cut every 2-4 s. Slow down and pause on "Not moving".
- **One real demo take.** Don't fake or speed up the shove or the lean test. This is the proof.
- **Pre-flight check:** wipe the counter and use a visibly smooth surface so the "doesn't slide" demo is easy to read. Film the old plastic board for real if the creator has one.
- **No fabricated reactions or results.** No "transformed my cooking". Her reaction should be small and believable.
- **If no creator has a true "old board" story:** drop the (C) lines. Use H2 or H3, and replace the Problem line with a general one: "Plastic boards slide and scar. I wanted one that stays put." Check that the creator can honestly say it.

## Test plan (one learning goal)
- **Goal:** which opening earns attention and spend: pain situation (H1), demo-first (H2), or price objection (H3).
- **Hold constant:** creator, body, CTA, offer, length, landing page.
- **Cuts:** 3 (H1, H2, H3, each with its own bridge, same body). Suggested names: `EVAL_PAIN_slide_ugc_h1b1_v1`, `..._h2b1_v1`, `..._h3b1_v1`.
- **First iteration if a hook wins:** Body B changes one thing only. Extend the Problem section so the product turn comes at about 20-25 s instead of 13 s. The KB defaults diverge on setup share (see the note under "Checks run" below), so this tests which is better here.
- **Landing page must repeat the promise:** double-sided, grip feet, groove, hand-wash and oil, $79, free shipping, 30-day returns. Landing page content wasn't provided, so I could not check it.

## Checks run (from the KB's UGC checklist and copy scorecard)
- One core message and one core emotion: yes (relief, from a board that stays put). Message in frame 1: yes.
- Three hooks test three different levers: yes (pain, demo, price).
- No fabricated proof, urgency or claims: yes. No scarcity or discount claim; not-antibacterial respected.
- Honest first person: lines flagged (C) need creator confirmation.
- Realism beat: yes. The honest beat names the downsides (hand wash only, size).
- CTA definitive, with the offer stated exactly: yes. "Tap to see GripBoard" instead of "Shop now".
- **Setup share:** this script shows the product at 13 s, so setup is about 0.3. Tether's playbook wants about 0.67 for story-led UGC. Evolve's playbook puts the claim at about 11-16 s of a 30-45 s ad. The KB (contradictions X19) says to use Tether's setup share for story-led UGC and Evolve's timing otherwise. This ad is demo-led, so I used Evolve's timing and put the longer-setup version in the test plan as Body B.

## Open items for the client
1. Source and permission for each review you want on screen.
2. Recommended oiling routine, if you want it stated.
3. Landing page URL or copy, to check the promise matches.
4. Whether the creator has the true "old plastic board" story.
5. Confirmation that the juice-groove demo has been tested with real meat on camera.
