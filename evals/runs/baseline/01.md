# Magnesium sleep spray: 30-second UGC ad (3 hooks + full script)

**Brief:** a 30-second UGC-style video ad for a magnesium sleep spray. You spray it on your skin before bed, and it's meant to help you unwind.
**Status:** the copy is ready for a creator read-through. It can't ship until the client confirms the facts in section 4. All I had to work from was the one-line brief, so the script makes only two product claims: *it's magnesium you spray on your skin* and *it's part of winding down before bed*. Everything else is either a placeholder in `[BRACKETS]` or a line the creator must swap for their own true experience.

---

## 1. Strategy in one glance

| Field | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Angle | **"Tired body, loud brain."** The spray is the 10-second wind-down signal that replaces the midnight scroll. | Every sleep brand says "better sleep." The KB rule is to sell the real painful moment instead of the category cliché. Here that moment is lying down and the brain starting up (Tether, *perception management*: mattresses sell "waking up," not "better sleep"). |
| Core emotion | Relief, one emotion only. Zone 3 (quiet pain) in the setup, resolving to Zone 1 (calm). | One core emotion per ad. Negative tone points at the *situation*, never at the viewer, and ends in relief. |
| Self | Actual self ("this is me at 11pm") | |
| Awareness / stage | Problem-aware, cold traffic | |
| Story structure | Pixar-style lived experience: every night I… → I tried… → now… → honest realism → CTA. About 2/3 of the runtime is setup before the product appears. | Tether default for story-led creator UGC (setup share ~0.67) |
| Test packaging | 3 hook+bridge openings × 1 shared body = 3 cuts | Evolve default: test hook and bridge together, keep the body and CTA identical |
| Execution | On-camera creator, self-shot, bedroom at night | |

**Core message (frame 1 in every version):** *My body's done, but my brain won't switch off, and this is my wind-down.*

## 2. The 3 hooks (each tests a different lever)

Each hook comes with its own matched **bridge** (3–8 s) that hands off to the shared body. Lock the hook words. The bridge can be said in the creator's own words.

| # | Spoken hook (0–3 s), locked | On-screen text (≤7 words) | First frame | Bridge (3–8 s) | Lever being tested |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **H1** | "Anyone else's brain start a to-do list the *second* you lie down?" | **Tired body. Loud brain.** | Close-up: creator lying in bed in the dark, eyes open, phone glow on her face. | "Like, my body's *done*. But my brain's replaying every email from today." | **Pain validation / "is it just me?"** An in-group question with slightly lower valence |
| **H2** | "I *really* rolled my eyes at spraying magnesium on my legs before bed." | **I rolled my eyes at this.** | Creator sitting on the edge of the bed, holding the bottle up to camera with a skeptical face. Show the face and bottle before any skin. | "But I was so over lying there, body done, brain replaying every email from today." | **Reluctant convert / skepticism.** The creator stands in for a doubtful viewer |
| **H3** | "This replaced my midnight scroll. And it takes about ten seconds." *(only if ~10 s is true for this product)* | **What replaced my midnight scroll** | Overhead: a hand puts the phone face down on the nightstand next to the bottle. | "Because every night my body was done, and my brain was still replaying every email from today." | **Habit swap / open loop.** Curiosity about the replacement behaviour |

Backup hooks, if one of the three fails the creator read-through:
- "Things I do at 10pm so 11pm me isn't doom-scrolling." (routine list format)
- "My bedtime routine used to be 45 minutes of my phone." *(creator-true only)*
- "I'm not a 'sleep spray' person. Here's why I kept this one." (skeptic variant)

## 3. Full script: shared body (8–30 s)

Spoken target is ~80–85 words including hook and bridge. Deliver it a little slower and quieter than normal UGC, like someone who is tired, not hyped. Cut every 2–4 s and pause on the **bold** lines.

| Beat | Time | Spoken (creator: say it your way, except **locked** words) | On-screen text | Shot / b-roll | Delivery |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hook + bridge | 0–8 s | *(H1, H2 or H3 + its bridge, from section 2)* | *(per hook)* | *(per hook)* | Low voice, real |
| Failed attempts | 8–19 s | "No phone after nine? Lasted two days. Tea. Sleep podcasts. Still scrolling at midnight." ⚠ *Swap in the creator's REAL past attempts. Keep the rhythm: one short failed try per cut.* | No phone after 9pm… (2 days) | Quick cuts: phone in a drawer, then back in hand. Mug of tea. Earbuds in. Phone glow at **12:04** on the lock screen. Lamp-lit, muted, slightly cool grade. | Dry, self-aware, a small eye-roll |
| Turn: the product | 19–25 s | "So now **this** is my wind-down. **[BRAND]** magnesium spray. Few sprays on my legs, rub it in, phone goes face down." | My 10-second wind-down | Close-up of the spray mist under the lamp. Hand rubbing it into shin or shoulders. Phone flipped face down next to the bottle, label toward camera. Warm grade from here on. | Calmer, slower |
| Honest realism | 25–28 s | "**It's not a sleeping pill.** It's just my 'okay, the day's over' signal." *(optional, only if true for the formula: "It tingles a bit the first few times.")* | Not a sleeping pill. | Creator settles back against the pillow and reaches for the lamp. | Matter-of-fact, half-smile |
| CTA | 28–30 s | "If your brain works the night shift too, **grab one for your nightstand.** Link's below." | Grab one for your nightstand | Bottle on the nightstand, lamp clicks off. End card: product + [OFFER, if real]. | Warm, direct to camera |

**Setup share:** the product appears at 19 s of 30, so ~63% of the runtime is setup. The CTA closes the loop opened by the hook (the busy brain → "night shift").

**Assembled example, H1 path (read-aloud check):**
> "Anyone else's brain start a to-do list the second you lie down? Like, my body's done. But my brain's replaying every email from today. No phone after nine? Lasted two days. Tea. Sleep podcasts. Still scrolling at midnight. So now this is my wind-down. [Brand] magnesium spray. Few sprays on my legs, rub it in, phone goes face down. It's not a sleeping pill. It's just my 'okay, the day's over' signal. If your brain works the night shift too, grab one for your nightstand. Link's below."

**15 s cutdown:** hook (0–3), one bridge line (3–6), turn (6–12), CTA (12–15). Drop the failed attempts and the realism line.

**Cut names (suggested):** `E1_RELIEF_tired-body-loud-brain_ugc_h1b1_v1`, `…_h2b1_v1`, `…_h3b1_v1`.

**After a hook wins:** test a second body with exactly one change. Options: a partner narrator ("He used to fall asleep in 2 minutes while I lay there…"), or a "people keep asking me what's on my nightstand" proof tail, which is only allowed once real customer quotes exist.

## 4. Notes for the client: what I need before this can ship

### 4a. Facts to send (the script has placeholders until then)
1. **Brand / product name** and a photo of the bottle (label must read on camera).
2. **Formula:** magnesium form (e.g. chloride), amount per spray, other ingredients, and **scent** (if it's lavender or similar, we'll show and say it, because scent sells calm well).
3. **Usage:** how many sprays, where on the body, and the real time it takes (H3 says "about ten seconds"). Does it tingle, itch or feel sticky? If yes, we say so on camera. Admitting it builds trust and gets ahead of the #1 complaint about magnesium sprays.
4. **Claims you can substantiate,** with the source for each (study, test, regulatory review). See 4b.
5. **Real proof:** review count, star rating, and 5–10 verbatim customer reviews with permission. There are none in the script now. **We will not invent testimonials, counts or "thousands of happy sleepers."**
6. **Offer:** price, any real guarantee, trial or bundle, and the landing page URL. The page must repeat the ad's promise ("wind-down ritual," not "cures insomnia").
7. **Customer research:** any reviews, Reddit threads or surveys about *when* people use it. The "brain's to-do list at bedtime" moment is my hypothesis from the brief. Real customer phrases would make H1 sharper.
8. **Past tests:** any sleep or magnesium ads you've already run, with which hook got spend and which metrics were off.

### 4b. Claim guardrails (supplements / health is a regulated category)
| OK as written (from the brief) | Needs proof from you before we say it | Don't say |
|---|---|---|
| "magnesium spray," "my wind-down," "before bed," "helps me unwind" (first person, the creator's own experience) | Anything about how much magnesium the skin absorbs, "absorbs better than pills," mg per spray, "clinically shown," any expert or "doctor-recommended" line | "cures/treats insomnia," "fall asleep in X minutes," "knocks you out," "fixes magnesium deficiency," "better than melatonin" (a comparison you can't prove), invented urgency like "selling out" |

- The line **"It's not a sleeping pill"** is deliberate. It makes the ad believable and keeps it out of drug-claim territory.
- The creator must actually use the product and may only describe what really happened to them. The "failed attempts" lines are examples to replace with their own.
- *(My note, not from the knowledge base:)* Meta restricts ads that assert or imply a viewer's health condition. That's why H1 is a group question ("Anyone else's…?") rather than "You have insomnia." Run the final copy past your compliance reviewer.

### 4c. Creator and production notes
- **Casting:** a relatable peer (a working adult who looks believably tired). Not a wellness influencer and not an "expert" in a lab coat. Natural, slightly deadpan delivery.
- **Set:** a real bedroom at night. Lamp light only, a lived-in nightstand, the phone always in shot as the "enemy." No studio look.
- **Grade:** muted and slightly cool for 0–19 s (the problem), warm from the product reveal on (the relief).
- **On-screen text:** one line per beat, ≤7 words, high contrast, in the calm part of the frame. Burn it in, because most people watch without sound.
- **Deliverables:** the body (8–30 s) filmed once; three separate takes of the hook+bridge openings; 3–5 s of b-roll each of spray mist, rub-in, phone flip and lamp off.
