# Brief 03: "Upright Pro" posture trainer (medium brief, skeptical market)

Write a 30-second UGC video ad script. 3 hooks + full script. The audience has seen lots of posture-corrector ads and doesn't trust them.

## Product facts (true for this test)
- Small sensor that sticks to the upper back with a skin-safe adhesive pad. Vibrates gently when you slouch for more than 10 seconds.
- App shows daily "upright time". Battery lasts about 7 days.
- $59. 60-day money-back guarantee.
- It is a habit reminder, not a medical device. No clinical studies. Must not claim to fix back pain, scoliosis or any condition.

## Audience
Desk workers 25-45 who notice they hunch at the laptop and have tried straps that were uncomfortable.

## Known customer language (from fictional test interviews)
- "The straps felt like a backpack I couldn't take off."
- "I don't need a brace, I need someone to poke me when I'm hunching."
- "The buzz is annoying for like two days, then you just sit up before it buzzes."
