# Instagram email creator research

The free-source research archive holds **880 relevant public posts from 20 accounts**, with exact source publication timestamps, format/cadence data, **274 full-caption semantic annotations**, **11 new sampled-video reviews** and **five carousel reviews covering 48 original slides**. This is a research checkpoint, not a claim all 880 media items were watched. The original every-creator 30 recent Reels plus ten strong posts selection remains unverified.

## Agent use

- **Use when:** researching email-marketing Instagram content, choosing an operator-level lesson, planning a format mix, or checking a creator's observed posting habits.
- **Inputs → outputs:** intended audience/question + source artifact + truthful conditions → an original explanation, source-linked brief and one learning objective.
- **Rules:** check the manifest stage before citing a source; don't infer a video's script from its teaser caption; keep public engagement separate from sales; distinguish actual emails from illustrative mockups; treat cadence as descriptive, not optimal.

## What changes content decisions

1. **Choose the operator question before the wrapper.** The reviewed subscription/customer-state examples teach a decision, not just a complaint. Show the signal, message change and condition. This is an organic B2B adaptation of the KB, not a paid-ad benchmark.
2. **Show the actual artifact beside the claim.** The strongest inspectable teaching structures pair an email/screen with a reason and proposed change. A portfolio reveal is a different content job from an implementation lesson.
3. **Keep format mix visible when comparing volume.** In the observed Sep 28–Oct 27 Nepal window, Chase's captured profile has 198 posts (6.6/day lower bound) and Max's has 54 (1.8/day lower bound). Chase's reviewed 40-post subset is predominantly images; those totals are not a recommendation to produce the same number of narrated Reels.
4. **Separate creator identity from relevance.** Some correctly identified marketers use Instagram for personal life, comedy or events. Vendor announcements and portfolio promotions are not automatically email tutorials. Forty posts from a wrongly matched Phil Brennan account were quarantined; the correct email source is @thephilrivers.
5. **Test a transferable mechanism, not borrowed proof.** Creator revenue figures, agency scale, generic review stories, lift claims and “best time” assertions are not our results. The archive marks their claims and our derived applications separately.

## Timing and coverage limits

Times are retained in UTC and Nepal time (UTC+05:45); creator-local time zones are not established. The recent cadence window uses 30 completed Nepal calendar days. Old pinned posts are retained but excluded from that window by timestamp. Anonymous profile grids may omit off-grid Reels and other content; Stories/DMs/private activity and competitors' retention/qualified leads are not available here. A zero in the observed snapshot is not proof of no account activity.

Eight new videos had usable full ASR read; three new videos yielded empty ASR and are labeled visual/caption-led. Video review used periodic frames rather than continuous playback or a human voice audition. All 48 carousel slides were captured and prominent content reviewed; embedded fine print is not comprehensively transcribed. Unreviewed collected sources remain pending in the manifest.
