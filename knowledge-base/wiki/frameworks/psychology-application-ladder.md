# Psychology Application Ladder
> Two of Sarah's answers to "which psychology do I use, and when?", merged. **May 7 (priority stack):** identity first, emotion second, behavior/tactics (heuristics, arrows, colours, format) third, because tactics only boost a message that already hits. **June 18 (depth ladder):** heuristics change micro-behavior, mental models shape whole campaigns, science of belief (core beliefs from childhood and generation) disrupts a category. Use the first to **build and check** an ad. Use the second to **choose how deep to go** for the problem you have.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** (1) tearing down a competitor ad: tag identity → emotion → tactic, in that order; (2) deciding whether a performance problem needs a heuristic tweak, a new campaign built on a mental model, or deeper belief research; (3) sequencing a test plan (headline → heuristics → format).
- **Inputs → outputs:** an ad or an account problem (metric, symptom, goal) → a layer tag for each element of the ad, a missing-layer diagnosis, and the lever to pull (heuristic variant batch / new mental-model campaign / belief research brief).
- **Rules:**
  - DO prioritise **identity → emotion → behavior/tactics**. "If the core message doesn't hit the identity, arrows aren't going to do anything." [May 7 0:06:14–0:09:40]
  - DO treat heuristics as "**secondary characters**, what we just call D2C best practices". Headline / core message first, heuristic second. [May 7 0:13:03–0:13:29]
  - DO test in this order: core message on a static → the same message in 5–10 heuristic variants → the same message in moving formats. [May 7 0:12:23–0:15:48]
  - DO treat **format as step four**: identity → emotion → behavior → draft the core message → choose the format that carries it. [May 7 0:38:29–0:39:00]
  - IF you only want to boost what the account already does (CTR, CVR) THEN use **heuristics**. IF you're building a new campaign THEN use a **mental model** (self-discrepancy / three selves, valence-intensity). IF you want to disrupt a category THEN research **core beliefs** (upbringing, generation). [Jun 18 0:24:04]
  - DON'T sell a heuristic as the strategy. Social proof "helps… but it's just a hack, because it's a shortcut." [Jun 18 0:23:02]
  - DON'T build avatars from demographics. Start with identity, then behavior. [May 7 0:16:25–0:17:51]
  - DO translate the psychology into the metric your team is moving: "CPA can actually be changed just by targeting a different identity." [May 7 0:32:39–0:33:21]
- **See also:** [Heuristics](../concepts/heuristics.md) · [Three selves diagnostic](three-selves-diagnostic.md) · [Valence-intensity diagnostic](valence-intensity-diagnostic.md) · [Creative testing system](creative-testing-system.md) · [Customer research stack](customer-research-stack.md) · [Core identity map surveys](core-identity-map-surveys.md) · [TEEP journey](teep-journey.md)

## Definition: the two ladders and how they fit

### Ladder 1: priority stack (May 7, 2025)
| Layer | What it is (Sarah) | Ad elements at this layer | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Identity** | "At the core of ourselves… almost always identity." Formed roughly **from year one to year seven**, while parents teach how the world works, so the parents' generation shapes it. Identities switch on and off all day (entrepreneur mode, "mom mode") | Who the ad says the viewer is: the self, the role, the situation ("you're the type of pet parent who…") | [May 7 0:06:14–0:07:16](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-07-live-with-sarah.md) |
| **2. Emotion** | Built *from* the identity: "the subconscious, the one that actually holds all the identity information, is the one that's using emotion to push the identity out." Emotions are "like silly putty": easy to push and pull once the identity is right | Headline feeling, zone and intensity, the core emotional promise | [May 7 0:07:16–0:08:17; 0:17:05](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-07-live-with-sarah.md) |
| **3. Behavior / tactics** | "Arrows, rounded buttons, colors… even like ad format, all secondary to identity and emotion." Boosts results, "not necessarily the core piece" | Heuristic badges (social proof, authority, urgency, scarcity, price/offer), arrows, buttons, colours, format | [May 7 0:08:28–0:09:40; 0:12:45](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-07-live-with-sarah.md) |

### Ladder 2: depth ladder (June 18, 2025)
| Rung | What it is (Sarah) | What it changes | Use it when | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Heuristics** | Mental shortcuts that "apply to basically everybody". The marketer is "an architect… build basically a Disney World for your customers to walk through… we put a bush there, a tree there, a wall there" | "Small baby behavior": conversion rate, clicks, where people go on a page | "Just boost a little bit of what the account is doing" | [Jun 18 0:21:55–0:23:02](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-18-live-with-sarah.md) |
| **Mental models** | "Self-discrepancy theory… valence and intensity, that's a mental model." "More about how people operate instead of just the short hacky stuff" | "The entirety of your marketing campaign" | "Create completely new campaigns" | [Jun 18 0:22:55–0:23:02](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-18-live-with-sarah.md) |
| **Science of belief / core beliefs** | "Down in the core of people": childhood, siblings, where you grew up, language, "did you watch a lot of SpongeBob… did you see a lot of ads". Harder to apply, "very much attached to **generations**" | Category-level positioning | "Really trying to understand our customers to actually disrupt an entire industry" | [Jun 18 0:23:02–0:24:04](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-18-live-with-sarah.md) |

### How they line up (wiki synthesis)
| Ladder 1 (what matters most in an ad) | Ladder 2 (how deep the intervention goes) | Shared ground |
|---|---|---|
| Identity (formed years 1–7, generation-shaped) | Science of belief (childhood, generation) | Both locate the deepest driver in childhood and generation [May 7 0:06:14; Jun 18 0:23:02] |
| Emotion | Mental models (three selves = identity model; valence-intensity = emotion model) | Mental models are the tools for working the identity and emotion layers |
| Behavior / tactics | Heuristics | Same layer: "secondary characters" (May 7) = "change micro behavior" (June 18) |

**Direction differs, and that's fine.** May 7 says *build* from identity down to tactics. On June 18 Sarah says she *starts* with heuristics: "I start with those, I'll move up into mental model and see which one of those will apply to my customers and then we'll go into belief systems" [Jun 18 0:23:02]. Read it this way: Ladder 1 is the order of importance when you write or judge an ad. Ladder 2 is the order of effort when you pick a lever, cheapest and fastest first, going deeper only when the goal needs it.

## Why it matters
- Most "why isn't this converting?" questions are really asking "which piece can I go mess with?" The usual answer is "a very weak message-to-market match… usually an emotional disconnect". You fix that at the identity and emotion layers, not with tactics. [May 7 0:34:24–0:36:00]
- The brain decides in about **0.2 seconds** whether to stop, "not three". The core message has to land before any tactic is even seen. [May 7 0:13:29]
- Use-case ads that miss the identity fatigue fast and hit a CPA floor. The identity-level fix ran for a long time (case below). [May 7 0:17:51–0:20:05]

## Inputs
| Input | Notes |
|---|---|
| The ad (or account) under review | Copy, visual, badges, format |
| The problem / goal | Metric symptom (CTR, CVR, CPA floor, fatigue) or strategic goal (new campaign, category entry) |
| Customer identity data | Reviews, [CIM surveys](core-identity-map-surveys.md), Reddit ([customer research stack](customer-research-stack.md)) |
| Generation mix | Needed for the belief layer (e.g. Gen X → nostalgia) |

## Procedure

### A. Teardown / ad check (Ladder 1)
1. **Tag identity:** who does the ad say the viewer is? Which self ([three selves diagnostic](three-selves-diagnostic.md))? If you can't name one, stop: "If the core message doesn't hit the identity, arrows aren't going to do anything." [May 7 0:09:40]
2. **Tag emotion:** which emotion does that identity push out? Zone and intensity ([valence-intensity diagnostic](valence-intensity-diagnostic.md)). Is it specific? [May 7 0:07:16–0:08:17]
3. **Tag tactics:** list every heuristic, arrow, badge, colour choice and the format. These are "secondary characters". [May 7 0:13:03]
4. **Score the stack:** a strong ad has all three layers lined up. A weak ad usually has tactics stacked on a missing identity.
5. **For the remix:** copy the identity → emotion mechanism. Rebuild the tactics for our product afterwards.

### B. Choosing the lever (Ladder 2)
1. State the goal: lift an existing ad / account, launch a new campaign, or change how the category is seen.
2. Pick the rung with the decision table below.
3. Run it in the testing order: core message (static) → 5–10 heuristic variants of that message → motion formats. [May 7 0:12:23–0:15:48]
4. If heuristic batches stop moving the metric, step up to a mental-model campaign. If mental-model campaigns plateau or the whole category messages the same way, step up to belief research (derived from June 18's "start with heuristics, move up").

## Decision table: which rung for which problem

| Situation / symptom | Rung | What to do | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Winning message, want more CTR / CVR | Heuristics | Same creative, 5–10 heuristic variants (social proof, authority, goal gradient, affect, Von Restorff…) | May 7 0:13:46–0:15:20; Jun 18 0:24:04 |
| Landing page / ad flow: people go the wrong way | Heuristics | "Put a bush there, a tree there, a wall there": guide the path | Jun 18 0:21:55 |
| Ads work briefly then fatigue, CPA won't go below a floor | Identity (mental model: selves) | Find the identity the ads miss. Rewrite from use case to identity | May 7 0:17:51–0:20:05 |
| New campaign / new product launch | Mental models | Build the campaign on three selves or valence-intensity (e.g. campaign = emotion, ad sets = zones) | Jun 18 0:24:04; Jun 11 0:21:13 |
| Team only talks in metrics | Any, framed as the metric | "How can I apply psychology to CPA… run some ads that are actually just targeting different identities" | May 7 0:32:39 |
| Category all says the same thing / want to disrupt | Science of belief | Research the generation and its core beliefs. Find the belief that drives behaviour | Jun 18 0:24:04; Jun 11 0:29:47 |
| Audience skews to one generation | Science of belief | Use generation-specific references (Gen X → nostalgia, "an MTV reference") | Jun 18 0:24:04 |
| "What format should I run?" | Format is step 4 | Settle identity → emotion → message first; format is "just a vehicle" | May 7 0:38:29–0:40:48 |

## Output template
```markdown
### Psychology stack: <brand / ad>
- Identity (L1): <who the viewer is told they are> · Self: <A/I/O> · Evidence: "<line/visual>"
- Emotion (L2): <specific emotion> · Zone <1-4> · Intensity <low/high>
- Tactics (L3): <heuristics / badges / arrows / colours> · Format: <static/GIF/UGC/...>
- Missing / weak layer: <identity | emotion | none>
- Problem → rung: <symptom> → heuristics | mental model | science of belief
- Next test: <core message static> → <5–10 heuristic variants> → <format>
- Belief hook (if rung 3): generation <…> · core belief "<…>" · cultural reference <…>
```

## Common mistakes
- Stacking badges, arrows and urgency on an ad whose core message misses the identity. [May 7 0:09:40]
- Iterating the core message before it's proven. Sarah prefers to iterate the proven message across formats and heuristics. [May 7 0:14:42]
- Choosing a format first ("should I run UGC?") instead of last. [May 7 0:38:29]
- Demographic avatars ("She's Sarah, she's 29, lives in Chicago with two cats"). [May 7 0:16:25]
- Jumping to belief-level research when the job is a quick CVR lift, or using heuristics to try to reposition a category (derived from June 18).

## Contradictions and open points
- **Start point:** May 7 = start with identity; heuristics last. June 18 = "I start with [heuristics], I'll move up into mental model… then belief systems." Resolved above: importance order vs effort order.
- **Is format a tactic?** May 7 puts format in the behavior/tactics layer [0:08:28] and also calls it "step four" after drafting the message [0:38:29]. Same idea, two phrasings.
- **Iterate the message or not:** May 7 "I don't like to iterate the core message… I like to iterate the core message in different formats" [0:14:42]. July sessions iterate winning headlines into variants (see [creative testing system](creative-testing-system.md)). Both agree on changing one thing at a time on a proven message.
- A "Psychology 101" course and a "science of belief" book were promised [Jun 18 0:21:55; Jun 4 0:45:15]. Neither is in this course dump.
