# Format Variation Ladder
> When a format is winning and you (not the audience) are tired of it, **keep the format, the message, the zone and the intensity, and move the product**: close-up → one side → two products → shrunk → in hand → cropped → "cherry on top" → personified. One variable per variant, one product per shot. This page also maps each rung to a UGC-video equivalent (derived).

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** a winning static or UGC format needs fresh variants; a competitor's evergreen ad needs to be remixed without copying the asset; a creator or client says "people must be sick of this"; planning the "variations of winners" bucket.
- **Inputs → outputs:** one winning ad (format, copy, zone, intensity, product) + asset bank (shoot photos, video screen grabs, product renders) → a ranked list of 3–8 variants, each changing exactly one visual variable, with a test order and a creative brief line per variant.
- **Rules:**
  - DON'T call a frequency of ~2 "fatigue". "People are only seeing maybe one percent of the ads that we put out… a frequency of two… I've seen it twice now in my whole life… that's no time." [Jul 23 0:51:41–0:52:41](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md)
  - DO separate **creator fatigue** from audience fatigue: "I think it more comes down to like I'm fatigued… it's boring." [Jul 23 0:53:44–0:54:47]
  - DO keep the winning format "in the roster" and vary **one product treatment at a time**; IF a rung doesn't perform THEN move to the next. [Jul 23 0:52:41–0:55:52]
  - DON'T add more products to the shot ("matcha, ice cream and candy and cookies and pickles… in one shot"). One product. [Jul 23 0:54:47–0:55:52]
  - DO keep the same valence zone and intensity while you vary visuals. [Jul 23 0:58:30–0:59:01]
  - DO try **weird** (personified products): "Weird works, especially… in the algorithm and the actual feed… because it jumps out at you." [Jul 23 0:57:59–0:58:30]
  - DO mine existing assets first: crops of shoot images and **screen grabs from videos**; "change the angle and everything about the story changes." [Chase 0:53:38–0:55:46](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-guest-session-02-chase-mohseni.md)
  - DO put colour on *your* product: desaturate the competitor/"old way" half of a comparison; push product saturation up. [Jul 23 0:35:44–0:37:49; 0:48:30–0:50:35]
- **See also:** [Creative testing system](creative-testing-system.md) · [3 Vs of Creative](three-vs-of-creative.md) · [Visual weight hierarchy](visual-weight-hierarchy.md) · [Creative primitives](../concepts/creative-primitives.md) · [Iteration vs variation](../concepts/iteration-vs-variation.md) · [Valence-intensity diagnostic](valence-intensity-diagnostic.md)

## Definition
A ladder of **product-treatment variations** for a format that already wins. Sarah gave it while roasting Bren Harper's Milky lactase-card statics (product centre-shot beside a trigger food on a clean background), which took most of the account's spend after two months: "Your ad account clearly likes this style… we have to keep it in the roster somehow, but to mix it up." [Jul 23 0:50:35–0:55:52](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md)

It's the visual counterpart to message iteration: [creative primitives](../concepts/creative-primitives.md) (crop, angle, background, colour, product shot) changed one at a time, which is what Chase means by variety ("a new concept is a new color, a new icon") and what the course calls *iteration* rather than net-new ([Iteration vs variation](../concepts/iteration-vs-variation.md)).

## Why it matters
- The audience has barely seen the ad; the team has seen it hundreds of times. Abandoning a winning format throws away what the account has learned. [Jul 23 0:51:41–0:54:47]
- Small visual moves give the algorithm "different ranges of the same concept" without breaking the message: "basically different ranges of the same concept." [Jul 23 0:53:44]
- Existing assets become new ads for free (crops, screen grabs). [Chase 0:53:38–0:55:08]
- "Even a colour or picture change would do something totally different to somebody's brain." [Jun 18 0:57:11–0:59:15](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-18-live-with-sarah.md)

## The ladder (statics, from the source)
Sarah's order, as she said it [Jul 23 0:52:41–0:58:59]. Test one rung at a time; "if it just doesn't perform very well, okay, let's test" the next.

| # | Rung | What to do | Why / Sarah's note | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Close-up** | Push in until the product is recognisable only by its signature shape (ice-cream swirl up the side of the frame; side of a green iced-matcha cup) | "I can tell it's ice cream still because I know what ice cream looks like… that swirl up the side of the page can do a lot for me" | [0:52:41–0:53:44] |
| 2 | **One side** | Move the product to one side of the frame | Works for tall products; hard for a splash shot | [0:52:41–0:53:44] |
| 3 | **Two products** | Two of the same product: "one for you, one for another person" | Adds a social story: "now you can go out to ice cream together" | [0:53:44] |
| 4 | **Shrunk** | Scale the product down "to 80%… tiny" | Tests scale/negative space | [0:53:44] |
| 5 | **In hand** | Product held in a hand | Shows size when size is the point (wallet-sized card); Bren's idea | [0:53:44] |
| 6 | **Cropped** | Top of the cup at the bottom of the screen, "you can only see into the cup" | Partial view creates curiosity | [0:54:47–0:55:52] |
| 7 | **Cherry on top** | Add one small topper element | "Cherry on top is its own message" (wiki: an idiom doing double duty) | [0:55:52] |
| 8 | **Personified** | Give the product a face: googly eyes, "evil eyes on ice cream… a really evil smile, like he's about to do some damage"; Darian's AI cow "punching someone in the stomach" (Chick-fil-A cows style), cow in a robber mask | Anthropomorphism (wiki label; Sarah couldn't recall the term). "Weird works." Sarah on the cow: "That would crush this… I would click." | [0:56:53–0:58:59] |

## UGC / video equivalents (derived)
*(derived: Sarah's ladder is for statics. These are the wiki's adaptations for UGC and short video, keeping the same rule: one variable, same script/message, same zone and intensity.)*

| # | Static rung | UGC / video equivalent (derived) | What stays fixed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Close-up | Open on an extreme macro of the product's signature detail (texture, pour, swirl) before the creator appears | Script, creator, hook line |
| 2 | One side | Reframe so the product sits in the side third while the creator talks; or a split-screen with product on one side | Script, creator |
| 3 | Two products | Creator hands a second unit to a friend/partner on camera ("one for you, one for…"); duet-style two-person version | Script beats, claims |
| 4 | Shrunk | Wide shot where the product is small in a real environment (bag, counter, car) | Script |
| 5 | In hand | Hand-held demo for the whole video; size comparison against a familiar object | Script |
| 6 | Cropped | Partial-reveal opening (inside the cup, half-open box) that resolves later | Script, hook text |
| 7 | Cherry on top | One added prop or topper that carries a second meaning | Script |
| 8 | Personified | Googly-eyed / animated product cutaway, or a product "character" voice line inside the UGC | Script, zone, intensity |
| — | Screen grab | Pull the strongest frame of a winning UGC into a static (source-backed in reverse: [Chase 0:53:38]) | Message |
| — | Background swap | Same creator and script filmed in a different setting/use case | Script, creator |
| — | Angle change | Re-cut the same footage from a different camera angle or b-roll order | Script, voiceover |

**Static → video carry-over rule (from source):** when a winning static becomes a video, keep its message **in frame 1** and carry over its winning badge. Net Positive's video showed the model first and "Ditch Polyester" halfway through: clicks, no sales. [Jul 23 0:18:56–0:21:32](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md)

## Where this sits in the format ladder
This page varies *within* a winning format. Moving *between* formats is the separate format ladder in the [creative testing system](creative-testing-system.md): static → GIF / animation / carousel → UGC / pro video / founder / "fake podcast", only after the static concept wins. [Jun 18 0:59:15–1:01:19](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-18-live-with-sarah.md); [May 7 0:15:20–0:15:48](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-07-live-with-sarah.md)

## Procedure
1. **Confirm it's a winner, not fatigue.** Format still takes spend; frequency low (~2). If every new ad "works for a minute and then fatigues", the problem is the message, not the visuals: see [creative testing system](creative-testing-system.md) (read-the-signal table).
2. **Freeze** copy, offer, zone, intensity, format, layout.
3. **Pick the next rung** (start at 1; skip rungs that don't fit the product, e.g. a splash can't go "one side").
4. **Make one variant per rung** (2–3 rungs per batch to fit the 3-per-batch rhythm).
5. **Test** next to or alongside the winner, using the account's usual structure.
6. **Keep the rung that holds or beats the winner**; continue the ladder from there. Try personification as the "weird" swing once the safe rungs are mapped.
7. **Log** which visual variable moved the metric: that's a reusable fact about this audience.

## Common mistakes
- Replacing a winning format because the team is bored. [Jul 23 0:53:44–0:54:47]
- Changing product treatment *and* copy *and* colour in one variant (the cherry-coded Net Positive pair). [Jul 23 0:26:19–0:30:30]
- Crowding the shot with more products or SKUs. [Jul 23 0:54:47–0:55:52]
- Shifting the emotional zone or intensity while "just changing the visual". [Jul 23 0:58:30–0:59:01]
- Converting a static to video and losing its message. [Jul 23 0:18:56–0:21:32]
