# The 3 Vs of Creative (Vibe, Variety, Velocity)
> [Chase Mohseni](../people/chase-mohseni.md)'s (CEO, [CreativeOS](../tools/creativeos.md)) internal check for any creative program, in priority order: **Vibe** (does the ad match the brand and the post-click page?), **Variety** (does each customer cohort get a version that speaks to it?), **Velocity** (how fast can an idea become an *approved, live* asset?). Velocity is "subservient to the other two".

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** QA-ing a remixed competitor ad or UGC script before it ships (vibe check against our landing page); planning the variant set for a batch (variety by cohort and by [creative primitive](../concepts/creative-primitives.md)); designing the brief → approval → launch workflow (velocity).
- **Inputs → outputs:** the ad/script + its landing page + the ICP's cohorts/selves + the team's approval path → a pass/fail per V with fixes: vibe mismatches to fix, a cohort × variant map, and a "seed asset" approval plan.
- **Rules:**
  - DO check **Vibe first**: ad and post-click page must feel like the same brand. "You have less than half a second" before people bounce. [0:08:26–0:09:38](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-guest-session-02-chase-mohseni.md)
  - DO build **Variety** as versions for each cohort inside the ICP, made from variations of what works (new image, copy line, background, colour, icon, badge, crop), not net-new concepts. [0:09:38–0:10:43; 0:19:10–0:22:31]
  - DO define **Velocity** as "the quality and quickness with which you can get from an idea to an agreed-upon asset that can go live", not "test fast, test a lot". [0:10:43–0:11:47]
  - DON'T optimise velocity before vibe and variety: "if you have bad stuff that doesn't align, it doesn't matter if you're able to turn out 50 ads in a day." [0:11:47–0:12:50]
  - DO get one **seed asset** approved, then ship its variations without fresh sign-off: "permission equals speed". [0:48:08]
  - DO use templates as the "sandbox" and "spine" that raise velocity for non-designers. [0:13:50–0:15:12]
  - DON'T "spray and pray" 20 ads hoping the algorithm sorts out the cohorts. Test in tight cycles of 3–5. [0:09:38–0:10:43]

## Definition
"Volume is evil. Volume is evil until you have data." Chase introduces the 3 Vs as "a framework that we use internally that I'm going to start putting on social". [0:07:35–0:08:26](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-guest-session-02-chase-mohseni.md)

| V | Chase's definition (near-verbatim) | Tether Lab translation *(wiki interpretation)* | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Vibe** | "How does your creative match your brand so that when people are interacting with an ad and then they go to the post-click experience… do those two things align?" "The first port of call in your creative journey." | Same [emotional zone](../concepts/emotional-zones.md), [self](../concepts/three-selves.md), tone, palette and promise in the ad and on the page. Sarah: "vibe comes down to how well can you actually communicate the message." [0:12:50] | [0:08:26–0:09:38] |
| **2. Variety** | Your ICP holds several archetypes/cohorts, "and within that group of people they have variety that they want to see within your creative." Found through "tight testing cycles of three to five". | One variant per cohort/self/zone, built from [primitives](../concepts/creative-primitives.md); Sarah's equivalent is *creative diversity* = one message in many formats [Jun 18 0:57:11] | [0:09:38–0:10:43] |
| **3. Velocity** | "The quality and quickness with which you can get from an idea to an agreed-upon asset that can go live, whether that's you agreeing with yourself or a five-person committee." Then you get signal from "your real boss, which is your customer". | Brief → script → approval → live; shorten with templates and seed-asset approval | [0:10:43–0:11:47] |

**Priority:** Vibe → Variety → Velocity. "Velocity being subservient to the other two." [0:11:47–0:12:50]

## Why it matters
- **Mismatch kills the click you paid for.** Chase's example: going from a Dr. Squatch ad to the Venus et Fleur site: "yo, what are we… I'm out." He cites his time at Heatmap (website data): people bounce in under half a second. [0:08:37–0:09:38]
- **Platforms profit from volume, not you.** "Follow the dollars… they want to know that you, Sarah, are going to test 25 ads a month." [0:10:43–0:11:13]
- **Variety is where the edge is.** "Diversity and variety: those are the two terms that people don't understand." "A new concept is a new color, a new icon." [0:19:42; 0:21:11]
- **Speed without fit is waste.** Fifty misaligned ads a day "never convert at the highest level possible", nor make customers stay, expand and refer. [0:11:47–0:12:50]

## How to use it (procedure)
1. **Vibe check (gate).** Put the ad (or the competitor ad you're remixing) next to *our* landing page. Score: tone (fun vs serious), palette, promise, zone, self, proof style. Any mismatch → fix the ad, or point it at a cloned page: "make a clone of your home page and change the copy." Only ~30% of ads Chase sees go to a unique landing page. [0:18:43–0:20:26]
2. **List the cohorts.** From research ([customer research stack](customer-research-stack.md), [CIM surveys](core-identity-map-surveys.md)) or the three selves. Chase's anchor: Ferrero building for one customer, "Mrs Valeria". [0:09:57–0:10:22]
3. **Variety plan.** For each cohort, one variant of the proven concept that changes the fewest primitives needed to speak to that cohort. Keep three buckets live: net-new experiments, scaling winners, variations of winners. [0:21:46–0:22:31]
4. **Batch 3–5** and read signal; keep, kill, add 3 ([creative testing system](creative-testing-system.md)). [0:57:55–0:59:12]
5. **Velocity plan.** Map every step from idea to live asset. Remove approvals: get the seed approved once, then ship colour/badge/copy variations without new sign-off. Use templates (CreativeOS, Canva; Butter for motion-graphic video). [0:13:50–0:16:50; 0:48:08]
6. **Re-check vibe** on every variation before launch: a new badge or colour can still break the match.

## Decision table: diagnose with the 3 Vs
| Symptom | Failing V | Fix | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Good CTR, poor landing-page conversion / high bounce | **Vibe** | Align page to ad (or clone page + change copy) | [0:08:26–0:09:38; 0:18:43–0:20:26] |
| One audience buys, others don't respond; account depends on one ad | **Variety** | Variants per cohort/self/zone, via primitives and formats | [0:09:38–0:10:43]; [Jun 18 0:57:11](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-18-live-with-sarah.md) |
| Ideas pile up; weeks from idea to live; committee rewrites | **Velocity** | Templates; seed-asset approval; smaller batches | [0:10:43–0:11:47; 0:48:08] |
| 50 ads a day, none convert | Vibe/variety skipped for velocity | Stop; do vibe check and cohort plan first | [0:11:47–0:12:50] |
| Team burnt out making net-new concepts | Variety misunderstood | Shift effort to variations of winners | [0:21:11–0:22:31] |

## UGC application (derived)
*(derived: Chase talks mostly about statics; this maps the 3 Vs onto UGC.)*
- **Vibe:** the creator's energy, setting and language should match the landing page (a chaotic bathroom-mirror rant should not land on a hushed luxury page). Check the first 3 seconds of the video against the hero section.
- **Variety:** one creator per cohort/self, or one script with cohort-specific hooks; vary setting, creator, opening shot before writing new concepts.
- **Velocity:** approve one script skeleton and creator brief as the "seed"; allow hook swaps, b-roll swaps and caption changes without a new approval round.

## Common mistakes
- Treating velocity as "make more ads". [0:10:43]
- Running a fun ad to a serious page (or vice versa). [0:08:37]
- Calling every new ad "variety" while really launching net-new concepts at 3× the needed rate. [0:21:38]
- Faking social proof as a "variety" badge. Chase says you can put "whatever you want" on a badge; don't. Use real numbers only. [0:19:10–0:20:11]
