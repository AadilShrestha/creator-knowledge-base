# Creative Testing System
> One consolidated playbook for testing ad creative the Tether Lab way: **precision over volume** (3–5 new ads a week), **small batches** (launch 3, keep 1, add 3), **message first, then heuristics, then format**, iterate winners **one variable at a time**, find copy × image "customer genomes", climb the **format ladder** (static → GIF/carousel → UGC → pro video), scale **10–15% a day**, and don't mistake a frequency of 2 for fatigue.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** turning a competitor teardown or a fresh angle into a test plan; deciding what to change next on a winning ad; deciding whether a winner is "fatigued"; turning a winning static into a UGC/video brief; diagnosing why a video isn't converting; sizing a weekly/monthly creative calendar for a sub-8-figure brand.
- **Inputs → outputs:** brand budget/revenue tier + core message(s) + current winners (with test history and metrics) → a test plan: what to test (message / heuristic / format / primitive), batch size, ad-set structure, one-variable iteration list, format-ladder step, scaling rule and read-out. For a failing video: a fix sequence (speed → hook → story → visuals).
- **Rules:**
  - DO decide **what you are trying to learn** first, then pick the structure. "Decide what you're testing and then pick a structure." [Jul 23 0:25:44–0:26:19](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md)
  - DO test the **core message (headline) first**, then 5–10 **heuristic** variants of that message, then the message in **other formats**. Heuristics are "secondary characters". [May 7 0:13:03–0:15:48](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-07-live-with-sarah.md)
  - DO run **3–5 new ads a week** (~20 a month, ≤50 a month) below 8 figures. "You can win with 20 ads a month." [Jun 18 0:51:57–0:54:59](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-18-live-with-sarah.md)
  - DO build up in small batches: **launch 3 → keep the one with signal → add 3** until ~10 are live and ~3 are "crushing". "Volume is evil until you have data." [Chase 0:07:58; 0:57:55–0:59:12](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-guest-session-02-chase-mohseni.md)
  - DO iterate a **proven winner one variable at a time** (headline, badge, product treatment, image). Keep what's known to work. [Jul 9 0:29:12–0:32:21](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-09-live-with-sarah.md); [Jul 23 0:52:41–0:55:52](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md)
  - DON'T test a *new* message as five identical statics with one headline each; for new messages, vary **format** around one message (≈5 messages × 4 formats). [Jun 11 0:51:49–0:52:52](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-11-live-with-sarah.md)
  - DO put expensive formats (UGC, pro video, "fake podcast") on a concept **only after its static has won**. [Jun 18 0:59:15–1:01:19](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-18-live-with-sarah.md)
  - DO scale a winner **+10–15% budget a day** while CPA and CVR hold (cautious: +10% every other day). [Jun 18 0:38:03–0:39:07](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-18-live-with-sarah.md)
  - DON'T kill a winning format because *you* are bored. "A frequency of two… I've seen it twice now in my whole life… that's no time." [Jul 23 0:51:41–0:54:47](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md)
  - IF a video doesn't convert THEN A/B test **speed first**, then the **hook**, then the **story**, then **visuals/creator**. [May 14 0:44:50–0:45:41](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-14-live-with-sarah.md)
  - DON'T drop the winning message when you turn a static into a video. **Message in frame 1**, model/product second. [Jul 23 0:18:56–0:21:32](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md)
  - DON'T blend learning goals in one test (TEEP × zone × self × generation all at once). One model per campaign. [Jun 11 0:48:40–0:49:43](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-11-live-with-sarah.md)

## Definition
A creative test is "a mini study. You guys are scientists." [Jun 11 0:48:40](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-11-live-with-sarah.md) The system has three layers:
1. **What to test, in what order:** core message → heuristics → format (and, inside a format, one [creative primitive](../concepts/creative-primitives.md) at a time).
2. **How much to test:** precision cadence (3–5 new ads/week) built up in small batches.
3. **What to do with the result:** iterate the winner one variable at a time, find the copy × image pairs, climb the format ladder, scale slowly, and diagnose failures in a fixed order.

It sits on top of the targeting frameworks: [valence/intensity zones](valence-intensity-diagnostic.md), [the three selves](testing-ads-by-self-type.md) and [TEEP stages](teep-journey.md) decide *what the message is*; this page decides *how it gets tested and grown*.

## Why it matters
- **Volume is a crutch.** "I do not love volume-based testing… it is kind of a crutch for not knowing enough about… humans." Volume works for 8-figure-plus brands with production *and* analytics staff. [Jun 18 0:50:56–0:51:57](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-18-live-with-sarah.md); same view [May 7 0:40:48–0:41:36](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-07-live-with-sarah.md)
- **Big batches starve the best ad.** "What if the best ad you had out of that batch of 50 gets four dollars of spend?" [Chase 0:42:06–0:44:13](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-guest-session-02-chase-mohseni.md)
- **The message is matched, not the budget.** "It doesn't matter if you're the smallest fish in the smallest pond with a whale… if your message is better", Meta serves your ad. [Jun 18 0:51:57–0:52:58](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-18-live-with-sarah.md)
- **Net new is a myth at scale.** People generate net-new concepts "at 3× what they need to"; "nobody can come up with that many ideas". [Chase 0:21:38; 0:39:00](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-guest-session-02-chase-mohseni.md) See [Iteration vs variation](../concepts/iteration-vs-variation.md).

## Procedure
1. **Pick the test front.** Write one hypothesis: "Which of these heuristics lifts the winner?" or "Which emotion buys?" or "Does this beat the winner?" [Jul 23 0:24:54–0:26:19]
2. **Choose the structure** from the structure table below. Keep it once the account works: "pick a method and stick with it, because you're continuously training the math on the back end." [Jul 23 0:23:50–0:24:54]
3. **Test the message on statics first.** New concept = a small batch of statics around one person + one emotion (~10, or 9 = 3 emotions × 3) [Jun 18 1:00:16–1:01:19]; Chase's version is 3–5 [Chase 0:57:55]. Minimal heuristics: headline + power image (+ maybe USPs). Narrow by CTR. [May 7 0:14:12–0:14:42]
4. **Add heuristics to the winning message,** one per variant, as a 5–10 batch (social proof, authority, scarcity, goal gradient, affect, Von Restorff…). Change only the headline or badge. [May 7 0:13:03–0:15:20]; [Jul 9 0:24:56–0:30:15]
5. **Mine the winner for the desire type.** Ask ChatGPT "give me 10 different ways to say *<winning headline>*", group the outputs by the *kind* of desire they hit, test one per kind. [Jul 23 0:43:07–0:46:24]
6. **Run combination tests** (copy × image): lock the best copy and rotate images; lock the best image and rotate copy; then cross each winning copy line with the image set. [Chase 0:56:48–0:57:55]
7. **Climb the format ladder** with the proven message: static → GIF / animation / carousel → UGC / pro video / founder / "fake podcast". Put the formats of one concept in one ad set. [Jun 18 0:56:08–1:01:19]; [May 7 0:15:20–0:15:48]
8. **Scale** the winner +10–15%/day while CPA and CVR hold. Feeding it budget is how you learn whether it's a core message. [Jun 18 0:38:03–0:39:07]
9. **Refresh, don't replace.** When the creator/team is bored, vary the product treatment ([format variation ladder](format-variation-ladder.md)) one variable at a time, keeping zone and intensity. [Jul 23 0:51:41–0:59:01]
10. **Keep three buckets live:** net-new experiments (small share), scaling winners, variations of the scaling winners (most effort). [Chase 0:21:46–0:22:31] Keep testing the losing zones even after one wins. [Jun 11 0:18:05–0:19:08]
11. **Baseline honestly.** Judge against the median CAC/CPA, not the best month. [Chase 0:30:40–0:31:44]

## Decision table 1: what kind of test is this?
| Situation | Test type | What changes | What stays fixed | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| New angle / message, no data | **Message test** (statics) | Headline / core message (one person, one emotion per batch) | Format (static), layout | [Jun 18 0:59:15]; [May 7 0:13:03] |
| New message, want algorithm reach | **Format-diverse message test** | Format (static, GIF, carousel, video) | The core message | [Jun 11 0:51:49–0:52:52] |
| Message has won | **Heuristic test** | One heuristic headline/badge per variant | Image, format, claims, offer | [Jul 9 0:29:12–0:32:21]; [Jul 23 0:12:36–0:18:56] |
| Headline won but "didn't scale" | **Desire-type test** | Headline rewritten per desire type (+ clarifying subtext) | Everything else | [Jul 23 0:43:07–0:46:24] |
| Copy and image both promising | **Combination test** | Image (copy locked), then copy (image locked) | The other half | [Chase 0:56:48–0:57:55] |
| Static concept won | **Format-ladder step** | Format: static → moving → UGC/video | Message (verbatim, frame 1) | [Jun 18 0:59:15–1:01:19]; [Jul 23 0:19:30–0:21:32] |
| Winning format, team bored | **Product-treatment variation** | One visual variable (close-up, side, pair, size, in hand, crop, personified) | Format, zone, intensity, copy | [Jul 23 0:52:41–0:58:59] |
| Two emotions each won separately | **Blend test** | Headline (emotion A) + subhead (emotion B) in one ad | Product, layout | [Jun 4 0:46:18–0:49:22](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-04-live-with-sarah.md) |
| Want to learn what an element does | **Removal test** | Same ad with that element removed | Everything else | [Roast #1 0:22:01–0:23:01](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-28-roast-my-ads-1.md) |
| Self-type question | **Self test** | Actual / Ideal / Ought copy | Offer, LP, price, visual | [Testing ads by self type](testing-ads-by-self-type.md) |

**Reconciling the "one variable" advice** (wiki synthesis): one-variable tests are for **iterating a known winner** (Jul 9, Jul 23, Chase's locked copy/image, Roast #1 removals). For **new, unproven messages**, Sarah says a set of near-identical statics confuses the algorithm, so she varies format around a constant message (Jun 11). Both keep the *message* as the controlled unit.

|---|---|---|---|

## Scaling rule
- **+10–15% budget per day** while CPA stays stable and conversion rate holds; cautious version +10% every other day. "Keep adding logs to that fire." [Jun 18 0:38:03–0:39:07] *(Whisper first renders "10 to 50 percent"; 10–15% is the consistent reading.)*
- While scaling, iterate the winner with a hypothesis about *why* it works (e.g. "lived it" vs "it's funny"), two threads × ~3 statics. [Jun 18 0:39:07–0:41:19]

## Test plan output template
```markdown
### Creative test plan: <brand> / <week of …>
- Learning goal (one): <message | heuristic | desire type | format | primitive | self | zone | TEEP stage>
- Hypothesis: "<X> beats <control> because <psychology>"
- Structure: campaign <…> / ad set <…> (same as account's current method: yes/no)
- Control: <winning ad id + headline> · Held constant: <image, offer, LP, CTA, format…>
- Variants (one variable each):
  1. <variable changed> → "<copy/visual>" · tags: zone <…> · self <…> · heuristic <…> · TEEP <…>
  2. …
  3. …
- Batch size this week: <3–5> (monthly running total: <n>/20)
- Read-out: CTR (day 2–3), CPA/CVR (day 4–7) *(read-out days are an agent default, not from the course)*
- Keep rule: keep the 1 with signal, kill the rest, add 3 new variations of the keeper
- Scale rule: +10–15%/day while CPA and CVR hold
- Next ladder step if it wins: <heuristic batch | desire-type batch | GIF/carousel | UGC brief>
```

## UGC application (derived)
*(derived: the course examples are mostly statics; this maps the same rules onto UGC scripts.)*
- **Message test → hook test.** Same body script, 3 different hooks that each state a different core message; or the winning static headline spoken verbatim in the first line (Sarah builds video scripts from the static's winning message: [Jun 11 0:32:53–0:33:57](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-11-live-with-sarah.md)).
- **Heuristic test → on-screen text/badge or one inserted line** ("keeps selling out", "most customers buy…"), everything else identical.
- **Combination test → creator × script grid:** lock the script, rotate creators; lock the creator, rotate scripts.
- **Primitive swap → one production variable:** setting, creator, opening shot, product-in-hand vs product-on-counter, caption style.
- **Fix order** for the UGC cut: speed → hook → story → creator.

## Common mistakes
- Spraying 20–100 ads and "letting the algorithm figure it out". [Chase 0:10:43; 0:43:13]
- Changing several variables at once on a winner (cherry-coded Net Positive variants changed colour, layout and copy: "test incrementally"). [Jul 23 0:26:19–0:30:30]
- Iterating the core message instead of its format/heuristics. [May 7 0:14:42]
- Building video before the static proves the message; or video that hides the message. [Jun 18 0:59:15]; [Jul 23 0:19:30]
- Mixing TEEP, zones, selves and demographics in one test. [Jun 11 0:48:40–0:49:43]
- Calling creator boredom "fatigue". [Jul 23 0:53:44–0:54:47]
- Baselining off the best month. [Chase 0:30:40]
- Fabricating proof in a badge variant (Chase's "put whatever you want" aside). Never invent counts or reviews. [Chase 0:19:10–0:20:11]
