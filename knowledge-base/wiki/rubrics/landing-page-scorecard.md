# Rubric: Landing-Page Scorecard
> A 0–100 score for any page an ad sends traffic to (lander, PDP, advertorial, listicle, home), built from Sarah's fixed-order audit — **cognitive load → message → CTA** [R2 0:03:42; 0:11:51; 0:19:19] — plus the ad ↔ page congruence check, the ability pass and the buy-box pass. Use it on competitor pages during teardowns and on ours before launch.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** before launching ads to a page; tearing down a competitor's funnel; diagnosing "good CTR, poor conversion".
- **Inputs → outputs:** page URL or desktop **and** mobile screenshots + the feeding ad(s) + category/price + product truth sheet (ours) → the [scoring template](#scoring-template): six section scores with evidence, auto-fail check, top 3 fixes, CTA/headline rewrites.
- **Rules:** score desktop and mobile separately for cognitive load (mobile "changed a ton of the cognitive load" [R2 0:32:52]); apply the page-type modifiers below; run auto-fails first. Weights are pipeline design; anchors come from the [landing-page psychology audit](../frameworks/landing-page-psychology-audit.md).

## Weights
| # | Section | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ad ↔ page congruence (vibe) | 20 |
| 2 | Cognitive load (avg of desktop and mobile) | 20 |
| 3 | Message | 20 |
| 4 | CTA | 15 |
| 5 | Ability | 15 |
| 6 | Buy box / offer | 10 |
| | **Total** | **100** |

Points = weight × score ÷ 3.

## Anchors (0–3)

### 1. Ad ↔ page congruence (20)
| 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Different brand feeling (irreverent ad → hushed luxury page: "I'm out") | Same brand, but the ad's promise isn't visible above the fold | Promise and tone match; one element off (palette, zone or self) | Tone, palette, promise, zone, self and every ad claim/badge repeated near the top |

Basis: vibe [Chase 0:08:26–0:09:38]; mirror ad claims [R3 0:15:00–0:17:00].

### 2. Cognitive load (20)
| 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Too much going on": many nav items, oversized images, competing motion, tiny text | Several checklist failures | 1–2 checklist failures | Passes the checklist: ~3 nav items, images within one screen, 3–5 bullets ≤2 lines, icons/numbers/arrows, mobile text ≥17px, no competing pulsing elements, sharp images |

Basis: [Pass 1 checklist](../frameworks/landing-page-psychology-audit.md#pass-1-checklist-cognitive-load) [R2 0:07:14–0:58:54; Jul9 0:10:14–0:11:19].

### 3. Message (20)
| 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rotating/competing hero messages; "us" copy; generic emotion | One message, but generic or brand-centred | One "you" message with a specific emotion; minor order issues | One core message on hero slide 1, matched to the model's expression; you-language; specific emotion ("boardroom-level confidence"); customer vocabulary; innovation early; claims in a logical order |

Basis: [Pass 2 checklist](../frameworks/landing-page-psychology-audit.md#pass-2-checklist-message) [R2 0:14:49–0:18:30; Jul16 0:55:36–0:59:47].

### 4. CTA (15)
| 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Only "Shop", "Browse", "Learn more"; one buy button at the bottom of a long page | "Add to cart" only | Action verbs, but buy points come late | Action verbs everywhere ("Get yours", "Order yours now", "Pre-order now"), buy points right after the moments of conviction (after the demo video, after fit/details), few clickable distractions |

Basis: [Pass 3](../frameworks/landing-page-psychology-audit.md#pass-3-cta-wording) [R2 0:12:19–0:13:47; 0:38:05; 0:47:34–0:49:14]; [contradictions](../contradictions.md) row 11.

### 5. Ability (15)
| 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| The biggest ability crusher (fit, size, "is this for me", taste, how long it lasts) isn't addressed | Addressed in a paragraph deep down | Addressed with a qualifier or reviews from people like me | Answered visually or in 4–5 words where the doubt occurs: who-it's-for labels, qualifier ("Designed for women 45+ experiencing hair thinning"), time sequence, scale on a body, typed social proof |

Basis: [Fogg](../frameworks/fogg-behavior-model.md) [Jul16 0:06:30–0:10:24; 0:27:14; 0:31:23].

### 6. Buy box / offer (10)
| 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hard mental math, % only, "0 reviews" shown, competing badges | Some pricing rules met | Dollar savings and clear tiers | Anchoring considered, $ savings shown, easy-math tiers (79 → 229), each number its own weight, true frame-of-reference badge ("Most customers buy X" on the true best seller), reason for any sale |

Basis: [Pass 5](../frameworks/landing-page-psychology-audit.md#pass-5-buy-box-pricing-psychology-jul-16) [Jul16 0:35:50–0:44:07]; [offer psychology](../frameworks/offer-and-bundle-psychology.md); [contradictions](../contradictions.md) rows 12–13.

## Page-type modifiers
| Page type | Adjust |
|---|---|
| Fashion at normal price | "Show, don't tell": score Message on product look/drape/colour; reward early buy buttons [R2 0:42:17–0:43:31] |
| Info product / service | Long copy is fine; score Cognitive load on formatting (big/bold first, highlights, ellipses, one CTA everywhere) [R2 0:50:20–0:52:42] |
| Luxury | No bundles/discounts expected; reward white space and clean images [Jul9 0:12:43] |
| Advertorial / story page | Score Message on whether it continues the ad's story and character (recurring character = fluency mechanism) [May14 0:48:12–0:48:52]; see [native advertising](../concepts/native-advertising.md) |
| No dedicated lander | Accept a cloned home page with changed copy [Chase 0:18:43–0:19:10] |

## Auto-fail conditions
| # | Condition |
|---|---|
| LP1 | An ad claim, badge, offer or price is missing from or contradicted by the page [R3 0:15:00–0:17:00] |
| LP2 | Fabricated reviews, ratings, counts, press or results on the page |
| LP3 | Fake countdowns or stock counts not backed by real inventory/deadlines |
| LP4 | Claims beyond `claims_allowed` (or regulated claims without the required wording, e.g. "*not typical results" [Jul16 0:56:36]) |
| LP5 | Mobile page unusable (text unreadable, buy button unreachable) |

Bands: 80–100 launch · 65–79 fix the lowest section first, then launch · <65 or any auto-fail = fix before spending.

## Scoring template
```yaml
lp_scorecard:
  id: LPS-<page>-<date>
  url: "<…>"
  page_type: lander | pdp | advertorial | listicle | home
  fed_by: ["<ad names / SCR ids>"]
  owner: ours | competitor
  auto_fail: {checked: [LP1, LP2, LP3, LP4, LP5], triggered: []}
  sections:
    - {n: 1, name: congruence, weight: 20, score: 0-3, evidence: ""}
    - {n: 2, name: cognitive_load, weight: 20, desktop: 0-3, mobile: 0-3, score: "<avg>", evidence: ""}
    - {n: 3, name: message, weight: 20, score: , evidence: ""}
    - {n: 4, name: cta, weight: 15, score: , evidence: "buttons: [...]"}
    - {n: 5, name: ability, weight: 15, score: , evidence: "biggest crusher: …"}
    - {n: 6, name: buy_box, weight: 10, score: , evidence: ""}
  total: <0-100>
  band: launch | fix_then_launch | fix_first
  top_fixes: ["<…>", "<…>", "<…>"]
  rewrites: [{before: "Shop now", after: "Get yours", why: "action verb"}]
  section_order_recommendation: "hero → enjoy → see it move → details → why better → objections → comparison (buy buttons after 3 and 4)"
  for_the_ad: "<what the ad/UGC must set up so the page pays it off>"
```
