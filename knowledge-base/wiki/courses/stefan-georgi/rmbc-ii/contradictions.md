# RMBC II: contradictions and agent defaults
> Where the 58 RMBC II lessons disagree with each other, with their guests, and with Evolve. Each row cites both sides and gives the **default an agent should follow**. Rows come from the conflicts logged while the course's concepts, frameworks, libraries, playbooks and rubrics were written, plus the conflict tables in the [rules](libraries/rules.md#conflicts-at-a-glance) and [benchmarks](libraries/benchmarks.md#conflicting-values-side-by-side) libraries, with duplicates merged.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** two RMBC II pages give different numbers or rules; before you put a VSL length, section budget, lead or hook count, upsell length, discount ladder, page type or test method into a brief or plan; before you run any course prompt that generates people, stories or proof.
- **Inputs → outputs:** a conflicting instruction plus the offer's context (channel, price, awareness stage, traffic volume, margin) → the default to apply, the pages that already apply it, and what to test if it matters.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** follow the "Agent default" column unless the brand's context or the user says otherwise. Name the default you applied and its row.
  - **DO** check who is speaking. Many conflicts are Stefan vs a guest (16-01 to 16-05, 12-02 to 12-04), or a lesson doc vs the spoken lesson. Guest figures are "(guest claim)"; Stefan's are instructor claims; none come from a controlled test with raw data.
  - **DO** prefer the course's measured or written value over Claude's own estimate, and recompute any figure from the [arithmetic table](#course-arithmetic-and-slide-vs-speech-errors-12) before reuse.
  - **DO** use Evolve's numbers for Meta budget, testing and viability decisions; RMBC is a funnel and copy course, so it wins on page type, VSL structure and LLM workflow.
  - **DON'T** execute rows marked ⛔. They are course tactics that break repo rule 2 (never invent proof); use the compliant alternative in the [compliance table](#compliance-hard-stops-10).
- **See also:** [course hub](README.md) · [rules library (conflicts at a glance)](libraries/rules.md#conflicts-at-a-glance) · [benchmarks (conflicting values side by side)](libraries/benchmarks.md#conflicting-values-side-by-side) · [VSL length bands](concepts/vsl-length-bands.md) · [compliance red flags](concepts/compliance-red-flags.md) · [LLM output claim audit](rubrics/llm-output-claim-audit.md) · [Evolve Start Here contradictions](../../evolve/start-here/contradictions.md) · [Evolve Copywriting contradictions](../../evolve/copywriting/contradictions.md) · [global contradictions](../../../contradictions.md)

"A" and "B" are the two sides as logged. Cites link to lesson pages: "doc", "docx", "PDF", "page" and "slide" = a course document, the lesson page text or a deck slide with no transcript timestamp; "v1"–"v6" = the video part within a lesson.
