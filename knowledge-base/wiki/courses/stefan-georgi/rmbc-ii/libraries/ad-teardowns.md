# Ad and page teardowns library: RMBC II

> Every real ad, VSL, advertorial, PDP, checkout, upsell page and webinar ad that RMBC II critiques (131 harvested rows → 122 entries), normalised to the KB teardown schema and tagged by niche, format, length band, awareness and claim risk.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** tearing down a competitor asset, choosing which funnel step to build next, finding a structural model in a niche, or checking what Stefan and the guests flagged as broken.
- **Inputs → outputs:** a competitor ad/page (or our own) → find the closest rows by `Format` + `Niche` → copy the `Principle` and the fix, record a new row for our teardown in the same schema.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** read `What fails` and `Claim risk` before `What works`; most course examples sell with claims we cannot use. ([compliance red flags](../concepts/compliance-red-flags.md))
  - **DO** copy section order, page length logic and offer mechanics; never copy names, numbers, testimonials, review counts or doctors. ([AGENTS.md](../../../../../../AGENTS.md))
  - **DO** judge length by what the ad already revealed: a tease ad needs a long pre-sell; a 1–2 min video that showed the product needs only a short page. [09-01 36:42–45:09](../lessons/09-01-advertorials-overview-and-examples.md)
  - **DO** audit checkouts and upsells by buying through the funnel and saving link + screenshot + full copy for every page. [14-02 v1 10:15–11:02](../lessons/14-02-upsells-case-studies.md)
  - **DON'T** swipe an in-feed VSL as an on-page VSL; the on-page version needs a classic lead. [04-11 09:59–11:01](../lessons/04-11-vsls-on-landing-pages-vs-vsls-as-in-feed-ads.md)
  - **IF** a page shows live-viewer counts, "only N left", same-day timers or review counts we cannot verify **THEN** mark it `do not reuse` for that element. [04-02 30:12](../lessons/04-02-vsl-fundamentals-classic-structure.md); [12-02 v3 14:45–19:00](../lessons/12-02-native-ads.md)
- **See also:** [hooks](hooks.md) · [templates](templates.md) (section orders pulled from these assets) · [rewrites](rewrites.md) · [funnel-audit rubric](../rubrics/funnel-audit.md) · [vsl-review rubric](../rubrics/vsl-review.md) · [ad-to-page-journey](../concepts/ad-to-page-journey.md) · [Evolve teardowns](../../../evolve/copywriting/libraries/ad-teardowns.md) · [Evolve Start Here teardowns](../../../evolve/start-here/libraries/ad-teardowns.md) · [Tether teardowns](../../../../libraries/ad-teardowns.md)
