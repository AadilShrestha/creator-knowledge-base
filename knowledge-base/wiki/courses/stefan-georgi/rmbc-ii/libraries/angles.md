# Angles library: RMBC II

> Every angle RMBC II names or demonstrates (37 harvested rows → 37 entries, plus 11 upsell bridges and 7 abandon-cart email angles from the lesson pages), tagged by niche, awareness stage, best format, length band and claim risk.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** choosing what an ad, advertorial, VSL lead, upsell or email argues before writing hooks; mapping one product to angles per awareness stage; turning psychographic research into lines.
- **Inputs → outputs:** product, unified research doc (psychographics, complaints, beliefs), awareness split → 3–5 candidate angles from the rows here, each with one example line rewritten for our product and our proof.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** pick the angle by awareness stage first: problem aware → hidden cause; solution aware → "do these really work?"; product aware → "why ours beats the others". [02-01 42:22–43:24](../lessons/02-01-product-market-awareness.md)
  - **DO** match the belief the market already holds (rigged system vs meritocracy) instead of arguing against it. [02-03 v1 06:35–07:40](../lessons/02-03-psychographic-research.md)
  - **DO** treat "suppressed cure" and "paradise lost" angles as story structure only; health claims need substantiation. [02-03 v1 17:13–24:05](../lessons/02-03-psychographic-research.md)
  - **DON'T** reuse example lines with invented stats, testimonials or experts ("3X faster than prescriptions", "90% clear in one week"). [09-02 26:36; 44:37–54:55](../lessons/09-02-claude-project-for-advertorials.md)
  - **IF** you write an upsell **THEN** choose among stock-up, expanded problem, new problem or improved version, and never assume the earlier upsell was bought. [14-01 v2 02:53–11:22](../lessons/14-01-upsells-overview.md); [14-03 doc 3.1](../lessons/14-03-claude-projects-for-upsells.md)
  - **IF** the market is skeptical after failed products **THEN** test the "credible exception" angle. [02-03 v2 14:03–15:05](../lessons/02-03-psychographic-research.md)
- **See also:** [product-market-awareness](../concepts/product-market-awareness.md) · [unified-research-document](../concepts/unified-research-document.md) · [hooks](hooks.md) · [templates](templates.md) · [Evolve angles](../../../evolve/copywriting/libraries/angles.md) · [Evolve Start Here angles](../../../evolve/start-here/libraries/angles.md) · [Tether angles](../../../../libraries/angles.md)

## Cross-course notes
- **Agreement with [Evolve angles](../../../evolve/copywriting/libraries/angles.md):** both start angles from the market's existing beliefs and awareness level ([Evolve market awareness](../../../evolve/copywriting/concepts/market-awareness.md)); RMBC's belief-matched pair (A012) is the same move as Evolve's "beliefs" concept ([link](../../../evolve/copywriting/concepts/beliefs.md)).
- **Difference, source of customer language:** RMBC accepts LLM-gathered "quotes" from deep research as voice-of-customer ([02-03 v2 42:30](../lessons/02-03-psychographic-research.md)); Evolve collects real reviews and comments ([Evolve labels](../../../evolve/copywriting/concepts/labels-and-customer-language.md)). Agent default: real reviews/comments first; LLM summaries only as leads to verify. (Already logged in contradictions notes.)
- **RMBC adds:** post-purchase angles (A035–A048) and abandonment-reason angles (A049–A055), which Evolve and Tether do not cover.
