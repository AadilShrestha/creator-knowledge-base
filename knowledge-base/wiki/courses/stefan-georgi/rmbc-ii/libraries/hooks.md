# Hooks library: RMBC II

> Every hook shown, written or generated in RMBC II, de-duplicated (167 harvested rows → 149 entries), grouped into 10 families and tagged by niche, awareness, format, length band and claim risk, with the lesson and timestamp it came from.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** writing VSL openers, in-feed ad hooks, advertorial or native headlines, UGC first lines, or a hook stack; also when you need a pattern for a niche you have not worked in.
- **Inputs → outputs:** our product facts, offer, audience, awareness level and real customer language → pick a family and a pattern here → write **new** lines for our product, then run every line through the claim-risk check below.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** filter by `Awareness` and `Format` first, then read the `Why it works` column; copy the move, never the words. ([AGENTS.md](../../../../../../AGENTS.md))
  - **DO** treat every `do not reuse` row as structure only: claims not reusable. Write the compliant variant with `[VERIFY: …]` brackets. ([compliance red flags](../concepts/compliance-red-flags.md))
  - **DO** prefer hooks that the mechanism actually pays off; Stefan rejects a hook "not really explained by the mechanism". [07-04 v1 03:29–08:18](../lessons/07-04-short-vsl.md)
  - **DO** keep the mechanism wording identical across hook swaps; MiamiMD's hook test left an "NMF" vs "fibroblast failure" mismatch. [04-08 v3 15:49](../lessons/04-08-medium-vsls-structures-and-examples.md)
  - **DON'T** reuse any hook marked `LLM-generated` as if it were tested; those came out of Claude/Gemini/Perplexity during the course demos and carry invented numbers and authorities. [05-02 16:48](../lessons/05-02-the-brief-generator-with-claude-project-unity-eye-serum-exam.md)
  - **IF** the hook names a doctor, a university, a celebrity or a study **THEN** it is `do not reuse` unless we hold the real person's consent or the citation.
  - **IF** you are writing a 15–60 s TikTok/Reels hook **THEN** build a three-channel stack (text overlay + visual + first spoken line that do not repeat each other). [12-04 v7 03:49–04:45](../lessons/12-04-tiktok-ads.md)
- **See also:** [hook-generation-system](../frameworks/hook-generation-system.md) · [modular-creative-and-hook-swaps](../concepts/modular-creative-and-hook-swaps.md) · [templates](templates.md) (fill-in hook formulas) · [angles](angles.md) · [prompts](prompts.md) (hook-database prompts) · [Evolve hooks library](../../../evolve/copywriting/libraries/hooks.md) · [Tether hooks library](../../../../libraries/hooks.md)

## Cross-course notes
- **Agreement with [Evolve hooks](../../../evolve/copywriting/libraries/hooks.md):** both libraries favour specific, curiosity-gap openers tied to a mechanism; Evolve's open-loop and "curious, not confused" rule matches Stefan's rule that the mechanism must pay off the hook ([07-04 v1 03:29–08:18](../lessons/07-04-short-vsl.md)).
- **Difference, hook density:** RMBC's "ADHD super-hook" stacks many hooks in the first 30 s ([04-10 45:07–45:41](../lessons/04-10-using-ai-to-generate-endless-good-hooks.md)); Evolve wants one sticky hook plus a custom bridge ([Evolve hooks concept](../../../evolve/copywriting/concepts/hooks.md)). **Agent default:** one primary hook per ad; stack extra hooks only in long in-feed videos and only after the single-hook version has data. (Logged in contradictions notes.)
- **Difference, unaware hooks:** RMBC rarely targets unaware audiences outside biz-op ([02-01 19:29–20:38](../lessons/02-01-product-market-awareness.md)); Evolve teaches unaware hooks ([Evolve unaware ads](../../../evolve/copywriting/concepts/unaware-ads.md)). Rows tagged `unaware` here are mostly financial/biz-op and TikTok.
- **Tether shared library:** [libraries/hooks.md](../../../../libraries/hooks.md) carries emotion-zone and heuristic tags; use it for UGC scripts and this page for VSL and long-form openers.
