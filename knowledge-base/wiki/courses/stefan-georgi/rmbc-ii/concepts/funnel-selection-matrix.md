# Funnel Selection Matrix (the 15 hidden factors)
> Score the offer 1-5 on 15 factors (5 = less copy needed), sum to 15-75, and the band (Lean 46+, Medium 26-45, Heavy 25 or less) gives the starting page chain between ad and checkout. A hypothesis to test, not a law.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** choosing the page chain for a new offer (PDP only vs advertorial/quiz + VSL vs mini-VSL ad + PDP vs webinar/application), deciding whether a pre-sell is worth building, explaining why a competitor runs a long or short funnel, or auditing a funnel that converts poorly.
- **Inputs → outputs:** offer facts (price, proof assets, regulatory category, SKU count, guarantee), traffic source and temperature, device split, brand/review count, current step metrics → a 15-row score sheet with evidence per row, a total, a band, a starting chain, the 3 lowest rows and one lever + test per row.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** score every row 1-5 in the direction stated for that row; "the higher the rating, the less copy you need to add." [13-01 10:17](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md)
  - **DO** map the total: 46+ Lean, 26-45 Medium, 25 or less Heavy. [13-01 08:21; 43:51](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md)
  - **DO** read factors in combination; a short attention span does not rescue a $2,000 offer with no trust. [13-01 39:20](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md)
  - **DO** raise a weak factor (demo, mechanism animation, UGC, guarantee, BNPL, milestone timeline) before adding pages. [13-01 11:46–12:44](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md)
  - **DO** change one factor at a time and re-score. (Doc, rule-of-thumb step 4.) [13-01 44:00](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md)
  - **DON'T** treat the total as a "golden rule": "It doesn't mean you're not going to test things." [13-01 48:27](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md)
  - **DON'T** guess a score. Write `UNKNOWN - need: <fact>` and ask. (derived, from AGENTS.md rule 1)
  - **DON'T** assume high price forces long copy when demonstrable superiority is real (Purple egg drop). [13-01 15:40](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md)
  - **IF** the market is hyper-aware of the solution and believes it works **THEN** skip VSL and pre-sell; sell price, trust and why you are the better source. [13-01 07:24](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md)
  - **IF** the problem is not urgent **THEN** lead with "an immediate pain point that is symptomatic of the long-term one". [13-01 31:13–32:46](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md)
  - **IF** the funnel converts poorly **THEN** check step conversion rates, then ad metrics (CPM/CPC), then AOV, before adding pages. [13-01 48:59–53:03](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md)
- **See also:** [ad-to-page-journey](ad-to-page-journey.md) · [funnel-economics](funnel-economics.md) · [congruence](congruence.md) · [product-market-awareness](product-market-awareness.md) · [launch-a-cold-traffic-funnel](../playbooks/launch-a-cold-traffic-funnel.md) · [funnel-audit rubric](../rubrics/funnel-audit.md)

## Definition
A scoring rubric from the 13-01 live call. Stefan seeded it with his own factors (awareness, demonstrable proof, price), had ChatGPT extend the list and draft a rubric, then edited it and **inverted the scale** so 5 = least copy needed. [13-01 00:00–01:32; 07:24](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md) Each factor is rated 1-5; the sum (15-75) picks a band and a template chain.

## Why it matters
- Funnel length is the most expensive build decision: a 35-45 min VSL or webinar costs far more than a PDP. The matrix tells an agent which assets to build first. (derived)
- It turns "do we need a VSL?" into evidence per row. Stefan: "there can still be confusion about what sort of funnel you need… it kind of depends on what you're selling and what the state of the market [is]." [13-01 02:30–03:02](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md)
- Rows scoring 1-2 are the explicit reasons the funnel is long; raising one is cheaper than writing more copy. [13-01 11:46–12:44](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md)

## How to use (steps)
1. Collect facts for every row. Mark missing ones `UNKNOWN`.
2. Score each row with the direction column below.
3. Sum and map to the band table.
4. List the three lowest rows. For each, name one lever that would raise it and the test to run.
5. Write the chain as a hypothesis with the metric that would falsify it (step CVR, CPA vs break-even; see [funnel-economics](funnel-economics.md)).
6. Check [congruence](congruence.md) between the ad and the first page the chain implies.
7. Re-score after each change and each batch of new proof.

### The 15 factors (verbatim action items from the doc; scale direction)
| # | Factor | 5 means | Low score (1-2) implies | High score (4-5) implies | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Market and product awareness | Most aware | Story-driven long VSL, webinar or multi-day launch | 1-2 page advertorial → short PDP or cart; push price, differentiation | [13-01 03:02–08:21](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md) |
| 2 | Demonstrable proof / visual wow | Most demonstrable | Longer copy, animations, science sections, extended guarantee | 15-45 s video ad + short lander | [13-01 10:17–15:40](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md) |
| 3 | Price point / perceived spend | Lowest price | $1k-$3k: long webinar, application call, multi-email indoctrination | <$9 free+S&H: micro-lander or two-step opt-in; $10-$497: VSL + order-form bump | [13-01 16:12](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md) |
| 4 | Traffic temperature and source | Warmest | Cold: quiz, advertorial, VSL warm-up | Warm/retarget: straight to cart or short video | [13-01 17:39](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md) |
| 5 | Brand equity / credibility reserve | Highest | Authority stack, earned-media presell, founder-story VSL | UGC-style video + concise PDP | [13-01 19:12](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md) |
| 6 | Perceived risk / required risk reversal | Lowest risk | Longer testimonials, ingredient science, guarantee, FAQ | Impulse single-page checkout | [13-01 21:44](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md) |
| 7 | Regulatory / compliance load | Lowest load | Eligibility quiz, KYC, extra TOS pages | No gatekeeping | [13-01 24:16](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md) |
| 8 | Offer complexity / choice overload | Least complex | Quiz/configurator, step-by-step cart | Single SKU → direct-to-buy | [13-01 25:23](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md) |
| 9 | LTV vs CAC flexibility | LTV "least important" | Nurture webinar/mini-class → nurture emails | Tight ROAS-first lander, one-click upsells | [13-01 27:51](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md) |
| 10 | Urgency of problem | Most urgent | Narrative VSL, transformation roadmap | "Get relief" headline, no fluff | [13-01 30:14](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md) |
| 11 | Competitive saturation | Least saturated | Comparison charts, competitor tear-down presell, unique mechanism, social proof | Curiosity hook + fast CTA | [13-01 33:45](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md) |
| 12 | Buying context (device, attention window) | Lowest attention span, mobile | Desktop: in-depth letter, calculator | Mobile: punchy vertical video, accordions | [13-01 36:18](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md) |
| 13 | Payment friction / financing | Least friction | Gated application funnel with trust copy | BNPL/Apple Pay: shorter checkout, more upsells | [13-01 36:49](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md) |
| 14 | Community / social-proof assets | Most proof | Credibility narrative, case studies | Carousel lander heavy on UGC | [13-01 40:50](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md) |
| 15 | Time to result / visible progress | Fastest | Milestone timeline, expectation-setting VSL | Demo-first hero video, "watch it work" loop | [13-01 42:22](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md) |

Watch the inverted rows: 6, 7, 8, 9, 11, 12 are phrased "5 = low risk / low load / simple / LTV least important / least saturated / shortest attention". [13-01](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md)

### Bands and starting chains
| Total | Band | Starting chain (doc) |
|---|---|---|
| 46+ | Lean | Ad → PDP → Cart |
| 26-45 | Medium #1 | Static ad or 30-60 s video ad → Quiz/Advertorial → 8-15 min VSL or longer-form landing page → Cart/OTO |
| 26-45 | Medium #2 | 3-10 min in-feed video ad (mini VSL) → optional quiz/advertorial (test) → PDP → Cart/OTO |
| 25 or less | Heavy | Ad → possible story presell → 35-45 min VSL/webinar → application/checkout → possible ascension emails |

Source: [13-01 44:00–45:15](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md). The PDF labels Medium #2 "Long (26-45 pts)"; the docx and on-screen doc say Medium. Use Medium. Stefan: structure #2 "is actually a structure a lot of people in here run." [13-01 45:15](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md)

### Agent scoring prompt (derived, not from the course)
```text
Score this offer on the 15 Hidden Factors using the scale directions below (5 = least copy needed). For each factor give: score, one-line evidence from the product/offer facts I supplied, and "UNKNOWN - need: <fact>" if the facts are missing (do not guess). Then sum, map to Lean (46+), Medium (26-45) or Heavy (<=25), propose the matching chain, list the three lowest-scoring factors, and for each name one lever that could raise it and the test to run. Do not invent proof, stats or compliance clearances.
[paste the 15 action-item lines from 13-01]
[paste offer, price, traffic source, proof assets, category, device split]
```
The 15 verbatim action-item lines are in [13-01, Prompts section](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md) and [libraries/prompts](../libraries/prompts.md).

## Common mistakes
- Reading one factor alone ("I'm more than $10, so I need a VSL"). Stefan: "well, maybe… rate each of the ones." [13-01 16:42–17:39](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md)
- Adding pages when a cheaper asset (demo, UGC, guarantee, BNPL) would raise the score. [13-01 11:46](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md)
- Scoring the platform instead of the actual buyer device split: ask "what percentage of my traffic and buyers and customers are mobile?" [13-01 39:20](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md)
- Treating complexity as a reason for a longer VSL: complexity means "you need more funnel assets. It doesn't mean you have a longer VSL necessarily." [13-01 27:51](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md)
- Building a long VSL for a solution-aware market: GetThin's 7-minute VSL tests ran "20% worse" or "neck and neck" over about 18 months (instructor claim). [13-01 57:20–1:00:01](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md)
- Trusting the doc's "80% of TikTok traffic buys (or bounces) in <30s" figure: the transcript says "8%"; it is an unverified ChatGPT number. Don't cite it. [13-01 36:18](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md)
- Copying the "black hat" celebrity free-trial landers Stefan mentions as an example of borrowed brand equity. Fake endorsements are deceptive; hard stop. [13-01 20:12](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md)
- Using the attendee's "AOV × CVR ≈ 350" rule as a target. Stefan: "I wouldn't make that my Bible metric"; track effective CPA. [13-01 53:39–56:43](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md)
