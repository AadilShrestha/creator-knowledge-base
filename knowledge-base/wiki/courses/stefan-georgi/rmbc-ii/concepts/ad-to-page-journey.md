# Ad-to-page journey
> Pick the page after the ad by three tests: what the ad already revealed (product, mechanism, proof), what the unit economics need (CPA, AOV), and the platform's context and compliance (native needs an article-style interstitial); then hold the page constant and test ads and pre-sells in front of it.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** deciding what sits between an ad and checkout (advertorial, jump page, listicle, VSL page, long or short PDP); writing a brief for a pre-sell; reverse-engineering a competitor funnel from the Ad Library.
- **Inputs → outputs:** platform, ad format and length, what the ad reveals, awareness, price, current CPA/AOV, compliance category → the chain (one of the five core flows), page length, CTA style, and a one-variable test plan.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** pick from the five core flows, not endless combinations: Ad > VSL > Checkout; Ad > Advertorial > VSL > Checkout; Ad > Advertorial > VSL > PDP > Checkout; Ad > PDP > Checkout; Ad > Advertorial > PDP > Checkout. [01-02 25:40–27:20](../lessons/01-02-foundational-knowledge-and-the-state-of-dtc-marketing-in-202.md)
  - **DO** do the selling somewhere: short ad (static, <60 s video, short advertorial) → longer PDP; long video ad (60 s+) or long advertorial → shorter PDP. [10-01 05:07–07:44](../lessons/10-01-pdp-overviews-and-claude-processes-for-writing-new-ones.md); [12-01 00:32:42](../lessons/12-01-meta-ads.md)
  - **DO** put a long advertorial between a static/curiosity ad and a short PDP when the product needs belief (claimed: CPA "way lower", AOV "way higher"; instructor claim, no data). [09-01 30:35–32:32](../lessons/09-01-advertorials-overview-and-examples.md)
  - **IF** the ad revealed the product **THEN** send to a product page (or product-aware advertorial), never a blind VSL. [12-01 00:39:15–00:40:17](../lessons/12-01-meta-ads.md)
  - **IF** a 1-2 min video ad already covered problem, product and mechanism **THEN** use a shorter, graphics-heavy advertorial that names the product early. [09-01 36:42–38:00; 44:14–45:09](../lessons/09-01-advertorials-overview-and-examples.md)
  - **IF** the destination is a long VSL **THEN** keep the advertorial short and image-led; let the VSL educate. [09-01 v2 31:23–33:26](../lessons/09-01-advertorials-overview-and-examples.md)
  - **IF** traffic is native **THEN** use an article-style advertorial or jump page "for 99% of offers" (guest claim); exception: commoditised products buyers already know. [12-02 v2 03:01–03:31](../lessons/12-02-native-ads.md); [09-01 v2 10:37–13:40](../lessons/09-01-advertorials-overview-and-examples.md)
  - **IF** the market is hyper-aware of the solution and believes it **THEN** skip the pre-sell (GetThin: static → short lander → questionnaire). [13-01 03:02–07:24](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md); [02-01 32:40–36:28](../lessons/02-01-product-market-awareness.md). Counter-case: NAD+ funnels built as if the market knew NAD+ did not sell on paid social; "the real solution is a VSL and long advertorials" (instructor claim). [02-01 36:28–39:25](../lessons/02-01-product-market-awareness.md)
  - **DO** label advertorials as advertising (Stefan praises the explicit "Advertorial" label; criticises a brand page that omits it). [09-01 47:58; v2 28:06](../lessons/09-01-advertorials-overview-and-examples.md)
  - **DON'T** move an in-feed VSL onto a landing page unchanged; rewrite the lead as a classic lead. [04-11 09:59–11:01](../lessons/04-11-vsls-on-landing-pages-vs-vsls-as-in-feed-ads.md)
  - **DO** hold one PDP constant and test many ads and advertorials in front of it. [09-01 32:32–33:42](../lessons/09-01-advertorials-overview-and-examples.md)
  - **DO** compare VSL vs e-com page on revenue per visitor, not CVR. [04-05 58:47–59:14](../lessons/04-05-vsl-product-buildup-and-reveal.md)
- **See also:** [sell-the-click](sell-the-click.md) · [congruence](congruence.md) · [funnel-selection-matrix](funnel-selection-matrix.md) · [funnel-economics](funnel-economics.md) · [advertorial-build](../frameworks/advertorial-build.md) · [pdp-build](../frameworks/pdp-build.md) · [paid-traffic-by-platform](../frameworks/paid-traffic-by-platform.md) · [launch-a-cold-traffic-funnel](../playbooks/launch-a-cold-traffic-funnel.md)

## Definition
Journey 1 = prospect → purchaser (Point A to B); Journey 2 = customer → repeat buyer (upsells, email). [01-02 03:26–04:54](../lessons/01-02-foundational-knowledge-and-the-state-of-dtc-marketing-in-202.md) The ad-to-page journey is the chain of pages in Journey 1. The advertorial's four jobs: "do the legwork of selling", "sell the click", compliance (an "interstitial", "an in-between page"), and congruence between ad and landing page. [09-01 v2 00:00–00:33](../lessons/09-01-advertorials-overview-and-examples.md)

## Why it matters
- Wrong page = unprofitable funnel: cold, short, curiosity ad → short PDP for a product that needs belief: "Your CPA is going to be way too high. Your AOV is going to be way too low… you will lose money every day and never be able to scale." [09-01 07:45](../lessons/09-01-advertorials-overview-and-examples.md) Diagnosis line (doc): "Prospects are not educated enough. They don't believe it works. They don't know enough about the product. Plus the pain point doesn't feel urgent enough." [09-01 09:41](../lessons/09-01-advertorials-overview-and-examples.md)
- Stefan's "dirty secret": "unless you're a really big brand… these pages [plain Shopify PDPs] don't convert… Shopify's a gajillion dollar company because of pages like this that go in front of it." (instructor claim) [09-01 46:57–47:58](../lessons/09-01-advertorials-overview-and-examples.md)
- Copying a big brand's journey without its economics fails: people swipe Gundry in-feed VSLs on-page and "it never works for them"; Gundry has a call center and is "really dialed in". [04-11 v2 00:00–01:56](../lessons/04-11-vsls-on-landing-pages-vs-vsls-as-in-feed-ads.md)

## How to use (steps)
1. **What did the ad reveal?** Nothing (curiosity) / problem + mechanism / product + proof.
2. **Pick the next page** with the table below.
3. **Check economics:** current CPA vs break-even CPA; if AOV is low, a story advertorial can push multi-unit ("one in every room"). [09-01 18:33; 32:06](../lessons/09-01-advertorials-overview-and-examples.md) See [funnel-economics](funnel-economics.md).
4. **Check platform and compliance:** native → article-style, labelled; regulated categories add gates (quiz, eligibility). [13-01 24:16](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md)
5. **Set length** (advertorial bands: 200-500 short, 500-1,000 medium, 1,000-2,000 long; "guidelines, not like golden rules"). [09-01 19:00–19:33](../lessons/09-01-advertorials-overview-and-examples.md)
6. **Check [congruence](congruence.md)** (topic and experience).
7. **Make the checkout congruent** and plan Journey 2. [01-02 24:39](../lessons/01-02-foundational-knowledge-and-the-state-of-dtc-marketing-in-202.md)
8. **Test:** hold the page; vary ads and pre-sells; measure CPA and revenue per visitor.

## Common mistakes
- Assuming a long on-page VSL is the default: "often not the move" now, though it "can work". [01-02 19:27–22:39](../lessons/01-02-foundational-knowledge-and-the-state-of-dtc-marketing-in-202.md)
- Native ad straight to a PDP: "I clicked on what looked like an article and now I'm on a product page. I wanted to consume content, not shop for hearing aids." [09-01 v2 29:20](../lessons/09-01-advertorials-overview-and-examples.md)
- Long sales letter dressed as an advertorial (hair-loss page); format must read native. [09-01 v2 21:23](../lessons/09-01-advertorials-overview-and-examples.md)
- Reusing the long story advertorial behind a video that already told the story. [09-01 36:42](../lessons/09-01-advertorials-overview-and-examples.md)
- Copying the pages' fake proof, deepfakes, invented protagonists or "reviewed by a nurse" lines. Stefan calls the pink-salt deepfake funnel "super black hat". [09-01 v2 06:41–09:06](../lessons/09-01-advertorials-overview-and-examples.md) See [compliance-red-flags](compliance-red-flags.md).
- Ripping a native page one-to-one; guest: "not a big fan of… rip and then run ads one-to-one". [12-02 v3 00:00](../lessons/12-02-native-ads.md)

## Cross-course notes
- **Evolve Start Here** picks the page by awareness (unaware/problem-aware → advertorial or listicle) and runs at least two pages per batch against a control LP ([market-awareness-levels](../../../evolve/start-here/concepts/market-awareness-levels.md)). **RMBC 09-01** picks by what the ad revealed, unit economics and native compliance, and holds one PDP constant while testing ads and advertorials. **Agent default:** use RMBC's tests to decide whether a pre-sell is needed and how long; use Evolve's rule to decide which pages to split-test; never change ad and page in the same test.
- **Within RMBC:** 09-01 says plain Shopify PDPs "don't convert" without a pre-sell unless you're a big brand; 13-01's Lean band (Ad → PDP → Cart) and Stefan's own Opal Beauty default (video ads → PDP) skip it. Reconciled: the long video ad is the pre-sell. **Agent default:** a PDP-direct funnel needs either a 60 s+ selling video ad or strong brand equity/social proof; otherwise add an advertorial.
