# Sell the click vs sell the solution
> Decide before writing whether the ad's job is to earn a qualified click (product hidden, curiosity, "watch this") or to sell the solution itself (product shown, mechanism and proof in the ad); showing the product moves the viewer to product-aware, which changes the page they must land on.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** writing or briefing any ad, short VSL ad or advertorial; deciding whether the ad shows the product; choosing the CTA; mapping a competitor's ad to its page.
- **Inputs → outputs:** destination page type (VSL, advertorial, long PDP, short PDP, registration page), target awareness, format, price → an ad objective label (sell the click / sell the solution / both), a product-reveal decision, CTA wording, and the matching destination.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** label the objective before writing: acquire customers, generate leads, sell the solution, sell the click, or raise awareness. [12-01 00:03:22](../lessons/12-01-meta-ads.md)
  - **DO** sell the click when a page behind the ad will do the selling: "it's a lot easier to sell the click than it is to sell the prospect." [12-01 00:03:22](../lessons/12-01-meta-ads.md)
  - **IF** the ad feeds a VSL **THEN** don't reveal the product; lead with curiosity or "the surprising reason why…" and let the VSL educate and reveal. [04-05 55:23–56:52](../lessons/04-05-vsl-product-buildup-and-reveal.md)
  - **DON'T** send product-revealing traffic to a blind VSL: "A blind VSL is basically never going to talk to product aware or most aware." Ads that show the product go to a product page. [12-01 00:39:15–00:40:17](../lessons/12-01-meta-ads.md)
  - **DO** end a sell-the-click ad with a watch/learn CTA ("you need to watch this", "check out this video", "go watch the full presentation"). [04-10 47:10](../lessons/04-10-using-ai-to-generate-endless-good-hooks.md); [07-04 v4 05:35–06:26](../lessons/07-04-short-vsl.md)
  - **DO** let the destination set the ad's ending: to a VSL, tease; to a product page, name and sell the product. [04-09 v2 06:35–07:06](../lessons/04-09-short-vsls-structures-and-examples.md)
  - **DO** "tease, don't teach" when the destination is a long presentation: "Now, I can't reveal all the details in this short video…" [07-04 doc, concept 3](../lessons/07-04-short-vsl.md)
  - **DO** judge on revenue per visitor, not CVR: Stefan's rule of thumb is product-reveal → e-com page converts about double at about half the AOV (instructor claim, no data). [04-05 58:16–59:14](../lessons/04-05-vsl-product-buildup-and-reveal.md)
  - **DON'T** buy a click with a promise the page can't keep or a claim you can't substantiate; curiosity must not become a hidden disease or cure claim. (derived; see [compliance-red-flags](compliance-red-flags.md))
  - **IF** the ad is short (static, <60 s video, short caption) **THEN** the PDP or advertorial must be long; "You have to do the selling somewhere." [10-01 05:07–07:44](../lessons/10-01-pdp-overviews-and-claude-processes-for-writing-new-ones.md)
- **See also:** [ad-to-page-journey](ad-to-page-journey.md) · [congruence](congruence.md) · [product-market-awareness](product-market-awareness.md) · [hook-generation-system](../frameworks/hook-generation-system.md) · [paid-traffic-by-platform](../frameworks/paid-traffic-by-platform.md) · [hooks library](../libraries/hooks.md)

## Definition
- **Sell the click:** the ad earns a qualified click and nothing more; the product stays hidden and a page behind it (advertorial, VSL) does the persuasion. Odorless Cat statics: "we're not trying to sell this cat pee device. We're trying to sell the click to the advertorial." [12-01 00:03:22](../lessons/12-01-meta-ads.md)
- **Sell the solution:** the ad itself carries problem, mechanism, product and proof (long in-feed VSLs, product-reveal videos). "You're always going to sell the click when you sell the solution." [12-01 00:10:23](../lessons/12-01-meta-ads.md)
- **Blind ad / blind VSL:** an ad or VSL that never names the product until the reveal (the "this weird liver trick" pattern). [12-01 00:39:15](../lessons/12-01-meta-ads.md)
- An advertorial that feeds a VSL also sells the click, "the qualified click"; detail sits in the VSL. [03-01 08:44–09:47](../lessons/03-01-unique-mechanisms-overview.md); [09-01 v2 00:00–00:33](../lessons/09-01-advertorials-overview-and-examples.md)

## Why it matters
- **Awareness shift:** showing the product moves the viewer to product-aware at least; a VSL that opens blind then repeats what they already know. [12-01 00:39:15–00:40:17](../lessons/12-01-meta-ads.md)
- **Economics:** Stefan frames the choice as roughly 4-5% CVR at $100 AOV (product shown → e-com page) vs 2% at $220 AOV (curiosity → informed VSL viewer) (instructor claim). [04-05 58:16–59:14](../lessons/04-05-vsl-product-buildup-and-reveal.md) Compute revenue per visitor for both before choosing (derived: $4-5 vs $4.40 per visitor on his numbers, so it is a test, not a rule).
- **Testing:** sell-the-click ads with copy held constant isolate which image or hook earns the click. [12-01 00:06:36; 00:11:54–00:12:30](../lessons/12-01-meta-ads.md)

## How to use (steps)
1. Name the destination page. If none exists yet, pick it with [ad-to-page-journey](ad-to-page-journey.md).
2. Choose the objective with this table:

| Destination | Ad objective | Product in ad? | CTA | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| VSL (on-page) | Sell the click | No; tease breakthrough/ritual/reason | "Watch this" / "go watch the presentation" | [04-05 55:23](../lessons/04-05-vsl-product-buildup-and-reveal.md); [04-10 47:10](../lessons/04-10-using-ai-to-generate-endless-good-hooks.md) |
| Long story advertorial → short PDP | Sell the click | No or teased | Soft, curiosity | [12-01 00:03:22–00:07:11](../lessons/12-01-meta-ads.md); [09-01 07:45](../lessons/09-01-advertorials-overview-and-examples.md) |
| Product-aware advertorial or PDP | Sell the solution + click | Yes, with mechanism | Product/offer CTA | [12-01 00:10:23–00:10:55](../lessons/12-01-meta-ads.md) |
| Short/medium PDP after a 12-15 min in-feed VSL | Sell the solution | Yes | Offer CTA | [12-01 00:23:32](../lessons/12-01-meta-ads.md) |
| Registration page (webinar/masterclass) | Generate leads | Teased method | "Register" | [12-01 00:40:59–00:48:20](../lessons/12-01-meta-ads.md) |

3. If selling the click: write hook-heavy, curiosity-heavy copy; tease the mechanism, don't explain it; CTA to watch/read. For the Claude Project instruction, use the verbatim block below.
4. If selling the solution: include problem, mechanism, product reveal, proof, offer; send to a product-aware page.
5. Test both if unsure; "the default for a VSL is curiosity, not product". [04-05 55:51–56:52](../lessons/04-05-vsl-product-buildup-and-reveal.md)
6. Check [congruence](congruence.md): the page must open on the promise the ad teased.

### Verbatim Claude Project instruction (04-10, doc 6)
```text
Here's something very important: unless I SPECIFICALLY ask you for it, when writing ads do NOT talk about the product in the ad. Instead we want to tease a breakthrough/solution/ritual and the call to action is "you need to watch this" or "check out this video" etc. We're basically "selling the click" meaning we want to sell them on clicking the link, which will take them to the VSL and sell the product on that page.
```
Source: [04-10 47:10–47:24](../lessons/04-10-using-ai-to-generate-endless-good-hooks.md). Session starter (same lesson): "Your goal is to write ads that are hook-heavy, have tons of curiosity, leverage the hook database, and ultimately 'sell the click' to the VSL." Full prompts: [libraries/prompts](../libraries/prompts.md).

## Common mistakes
- Revealing the product in an ad that feeds a blind VSL. [12-01 00:39:15](../lessons/12-01-meta-ads.md)
- A short ad into a short PDP for a product that needs belief: "Your CPA is going to be way too high. Your AOV is going to be way too low." [09-01 07:45](../lessons/09-01-advertorials-overview-and-examples.md)
- Teaching instead of teasing in a 2-minute ad that feeds a presentation; Stefan's fix: "Let's make it more teasing… Let's not [reveal] so much." [07-04 v4 08:45–09:47](../lessons/07-04-short-vsl.md)
- Letting an LLM mention the product by default; the project instruction forbids it unless asked. [04-10 47:10](../lessons/04-10-using-ai-to-generate-endless-good-hooks.md)
- Clickbait that breaks [congruence](congruence.md): the page doesn't deliver the teased thing ("bait-and-switched"). [09-01 v2 29:20](../lessons/09-01-advertorials-overview-and-examples.md)
- Fake deadlines in the CTA ("before it's taken down") unless true. [07-04 Templates](../lessons/07-04-short-vsl.md)
