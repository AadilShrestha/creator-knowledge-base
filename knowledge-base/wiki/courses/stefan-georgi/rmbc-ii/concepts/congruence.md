# Congruence
> "Maintaining a consistent feeling experience for a prospect throughout their journey from point A to point B": the next page must continue both the ad's topic (what was promised) and its experience (the mode the person was in: reading, watching, shopping).

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** writing a page that sits behind an ad; auditing a funnel for drop-off between steps; writing an advertorial from a winning ad; adapting a VSL between placements; writing upsells for a branded store.
- **Inputs → outputs:** the ad (platform, format, hook, promise, visuals, tone) and the page/upsell draft → a congruence check (topic match, experience match, internal story logic, CTA-offer match), a list of breaks, and fixes.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** match the landing experience to the platform: video platform → video page; article-style native ad → advertorial. [01-02 11:23–15:52](../lessons/01-02-foundational-knowledge-and-the-state-of-dtc-marketing-in-202.md)
  - **DO** open the page on the exact promise the ad teased (the "seven-second… ritual" named in the ad is what the VSL teases). [04-11 08:53](../lessons/04-11-vsls-on-landing-pages-vs-vsls-as-in-feed-ads.md)
  - **DO** paste the brand's best-performing ad caption into the advertorial prompt "so you can make the advertorial congruent with this story". [09-02 26:24–28:06](../lessons/09-02-claude-project-for-advertorials.md)
  - **IF** the ad covers one pain point and the PDP sells a multi-use product **THEN** put a pain-point-specific advertorial in between rather than rebuilding the PDP ("That is the congruence thing."). [09-01 v2 26:54–27:24](../lessons/09-01-advertorials-overview-and-examples.md)
  - **DO** fix internal story gaps: if a reader would ask "why would he do that?", add the motive. [09-02 50:27–52:41](../lessons/09-02-claude-project-for-advertorials.md)
  - **DO** keep the micro-lead congruent with the lead when testing new hooks on a VSL. [04-02 51:14–52:45](../lessons/04-02-vsl-fundamentals-classic-structure.md)
  - **DO** make the CTA label match the offer headline (Dr. Clean "Get -65% discount" under a 65%-off banner). [10-02 03:28](../lessons/10-02-in-depth-case-studies-dr-clean-and-calvin-tran.md)
  - **IF** a winning page was built for one ad angle **THEN** re-test the page when the ad angle changes (guest claim, Ethan Bence). [16-01 1:17:31](../lessons/16-01-cro-with-4080-marketing.md)
  - **DO** match UGC setting and tone to real product use ("morning routine", unpolished); congruence with real use "is going to increase conversion rates" (guest claim, Max Finn). [12-04 v7 10:32–11:29](../lessons/12-04-tiktok-ads.md)
  - **DO** keep upsells on-brand for branded stores; balance "branded congruent experience versus get that AOV up". [14-02 v1 10:15–12:51](../lessons/14-02-upsells-case-studies.md)
  - **DON'T** use "it's an editorial" to make a story untrue. Stefan: "you can figure out ways… to ensure anything that you have is congruent, truthful… or, you know, it's an editorial, so you can kind of not". **Hard stop under repo rules:** congruent must also mean true; no invented doctors, quotes, studies or stats. [09-02 53:09](../lessons/09-02-claude-project-for-advertorials.md)

## Definition
Stefan's definition: "maintaining a consistent feeling experience for a prospect throughout their journey from point A to point B". [01-02 15:52](../lessons/01-02-foundational-knowledge-and-the-state-of-dtc-marketing-in-202.md) The 09-01 doc splits the failure into two types:

| Type | What breaks | Doc example (verbatim) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Topic congruence** | The page is about a different thing than the ad | "I clicked on what looked like an article about a miracle oven cleaner, now I'm on a product page about an all purpose cleaner, I feel bait-and-switched." | [09-01 doc *4. Native Ads*; v2 29:20](../lessons/09-01-advertorials-overview-and-examples.md) |
| **Experience congruence** | Same topic, wrong mode (reading → shopping) | "I clicked on what looked like an article and now I'm on a product page. I wanted to consume content, not shop for hearing aids." | [09-01 doc *4. Native Ads*; v2 29:20](../lessons/09-01-advertorials-overview-and-examples.md) |

Two more layers used in the course (derived grouping): **internal congruence** (the story's own logic holds; [09-02 51:22–52:41](../lessons/09-02-claude-project-for-advertorials.md)) and **offer congruence** (CTA, price and page match the headline; [10-02 03:28](../lessons/10-02-in-depth-case-studies-dr-clean-and-calvin-tran.md)).

## Why it matters
- A mismatch breaks the spell: a short video ad sent to a plain landing page "is a very obvious advertisement. The spell is broken." [01-02 11:23–13:05](../lessons/01-02-foundational-knowledge-and-the-state-of-dtc-marketing-in-202.md) Reading a news story then landing on hard sell: "what the fuck? Why am I reading this ad?" [01-02 15:52–17:18](../lessons/01-02-foundational-knowledge-and-the-state-of-dtc-marketing-in-202.md)
- On native, "the problems are compliance and congruence. Generally" — more than conversions or AOV. [09-01 v2 10:37–11:24](../lessons/09-01-advertorials-overview-and-examples.md)
- Placement mismatch drops engagement: an in-feed VSL moved on-page unchanged lost engagement because "there was no classic lead". [04-11 09:59–11:01](../lessons/04-11-vsls-on-landing-pages-vs-vsls-as-in-feed-ads.md)

## How to use (steps)
1. **Write down the ad's promise, hook, visuals, tone and format.**
2. **Topic check:** does the first screen of the page restate or continue that exact promise? If the PDP is broader, insert a pain-point-specific bridge (advertorial). [09-01 v2 26:54–27:24](../lessons/09-01-advertorials-overview-and-examples.md)
3. **Experience check:** same mode? Article → article-style page; video → video page; UGC → unpolished, real-use page assets. [01-02 11:23–15:52](../lessons/01-02-foundational-knowledge-and-the-state-of-dtc-marketing-in-202.md)
4. **Awareness check:** the page must not repeat what the ad already taught or skip what it didn't. Product shown in ad → product-aware page. [12-01 00:39:15](../lessons/12-01-meta-ads.md); [09-01 36:42](../lessons/09-01-advertorials-overview-and-examples.md)
5. **Internal logic check:** read as a third party ("entering a dissociative state… what objection would you have?"); add motive where a step feels unexplained. [09-02 51:22–52:41](../lessons/09-02-claude-project-for-advertorials.md)
6. **Offer check:** CTA label matches the headline offer; checkout continues the page's style. [10-02 03:28](../lessons/10-02-in-depth-case-studies-dr-clean-and-calvin-tran.md); [01-02 24:39](../lessons/01-02-foundational-knowledge-and-the-state-of-dtc-marketing-in-202.md)
7. **Upsell check:** the upsell's problem connects logically to what was bought ("there's always that logical connection") and stays on-brand. [14-02 v6 04:44; v1 10:15](../lessons/14-02-upsells-case-studies.md)
8. **Truth check:** every congruence fix uses true facts; bracket unknowns `[VERIFY: …]`.

### Congruence audit checklist (derived)
| # | Check | Pass if |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Topic | Page headline/first screen names the ad's promise |
| 2 | Experience | Page format matches platform mode |
| 3 | Awareness | Page starts where the ad left off |
| 4 | Visual | Hero image/creator matches the ad's look and real use |
| 5 | Story logic | No "why would he do that?" gaps |
| 6 | Offer | CTA text = offer in headline; price/discount consistent |
| 7 | Checkout | Same brand voice; no new surprises (hidden subscription) |
| 8 | Upsell | Logical problem link; on-brand |
| 9 | Truth | No invented proof used to bridge a gap |

## Common mistakes
- Treating congruence as topic only; experience mismatch fails even when the product matches (the $99 hearing-aid doc example). [09-01 v2 29:20](../lessons/09-01-advertorials-overview-and-examples.md)
- Swiping a big-brand in-feed VSL for an on-page funnel. [04-11 v2 00:00–01:56](../lessons/04-11-vsls-on-landing-pages-vs-vsls-as-in-feed-ads.md)
- Testing a new VSL hook without updating the micro-lead (BHMD "super agers"). [04-02 51:14–52:45](../lessons/04-02-vsl-fundamentals-classic-structure.md)
- Changing the ad angle and keeping the old page untested (guest claim). [16-01 1:17:31](../lessons/16-01-cro-with-4080-marketing.md)
- Using an invented persona or "composite story" to make a page feel congruent. Stefan allows "made-up story or composite story" if disclosed; repo default is real stories only. [09-02 53:09](../lessons/09-02-claude-project-for-advertorials.md)
- Re-using a brand's real images and policy links in a swipe demo; use our own assets and make the destination domain match the ad (derived). [09-02 Caution](../lessons/09-02-claude-project-for-advertorials.md)
