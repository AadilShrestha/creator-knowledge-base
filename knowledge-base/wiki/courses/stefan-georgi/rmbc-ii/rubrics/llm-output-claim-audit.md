# LLM output claim audit
> A blocking checklist run on every LLM draft (brief, VSL, ad, advertorial, PDP, checkout, upsell, email) before anyone reads it as usable copy: it finds the invented doctors, testimonials, stats, review counts, scarcity, disease and income claims that the RMBC II workflows reliably produce, and replaces each with a compliant pattern.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** any Claude Project, Gemini, ChatGPT or Perplexity output from the RMBC pipeline is about to leave the chat: a brief, a mechanism draft, a VSL outline or script, hooks, an advertorial, a PDP, checkout microcopy, an upsell script or an email sequence. Also run it on research docs before they feed a brief, because the claims travel downstream.
- **Inputs → outputs:** the draft + the **truth sheet** (product facts, real offer, real prices, real reviews with counts and sources, real studies with links, real people with consent, real inventory/deadline rules) → a claim register (every claim, its check ID, status, source or `[VERIFY: …]`), a verdict (**PASS / FIX / BLOCK**) and a cleaned draft.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** treat every number, name, study, rating, count, credential and deadline in model output as invented until matched to the truth sheet. The course's own runs prove it: the brief generator wrote "Harvard researchers…", "97% of eye creams fail" and "45% wrinkle reduction in 60 days", none of which is in the supplied assets ([05-02 Agent use](../lessons/05-02-the-brief-generator-with-claude-project-unity-eye-serum-exam.md)); the advertorial project invented "Dr. Erik Johansson", "3X faster than benzoyl peroxide" and two named testimonials absent from the source page ([09-02 compliance flags](../lessons/09-02-claude-project-for-advertorials.md)).
  - **DO** let the generator use placeholders, then resolve them: the brief generator instruction says "For anything where you don't have information, you can use placeholders to start" ([05-02 00:00](../lessons/05-02-the-brief-generator-with-claude-project-unity-eye-serum-exam.md)). Convert every placeholder to `[VERIFY: what fact, from which source]`; never ship one.
  - **DO** check claims against the source page the way Stefan does: "Let me check on that 95% real quick here. Is there 95 anywhere on this?" ([09-02 30:24–30:55](../lessons/09-02-claude-project-for-advertorials.md)); and pricing: "You need to update pricing. Pricing is off on this." ([15-05 SRT 20:16](../lessons/15-05-claude-project-for-writing-email-sequences.md)).
  - **DO** tell the model up front to make less aggressive claims; Stefan says an LLM "would just make less aggressive claims, especially if you told it to" ([06-01 07:18–08:18](../lessons/06-01-claude-vsl-projects-setup-overview.md)). That reduces, not removes, the audit.
  - **DON'T** accept "it's an editorial" or "it's a metaphor" as a licence. Stefan, on the advertorial story: "it's an editorial, so you can kind of not, but you'd have to say, hey, this is like a sort of made-up story or composite story" ([09-02 53:09](../lessons/09-02-claude-project-for-advertorials.md)). **Hard stop under repo rules.** See check C11.
  - **DON'T** run the audit only at the end. Run it on the research doc and the brief too: the unified research brief's promises are "LLM-synthesised leads" ([02-04 compliance flags](../lessons/02-04-unified-research-process.md)), and the brief is what every later asset copies.
  - **IF** any ⛔ check fails **THEN** the verdict is BLOCK whatever else is good. **IF** only ⚠ checks fail **THEN** FIX and re-run.
  - **IF** the offer is health, finance, biz-opp or weight loss **THEN** also get a human/legal review; the course's own speakers flag these categories ("probably not compliant" [07-04 v4 02:26–02:36](../lessons/07-04-short-vsl.md); "you can't use as much social proof with make money stuff for FTC reasons" [12-01 00:43:19](../lessons/12-01-meta-ads.md)). Nothing on this page is legal advice.

## Why this gate exists
RMBC II teaches copy production through Claude Projects loaded with aggressive example VSLs, advertorials and upsells. The lesson pages flag the same failure in nearly every generation run: the model reproduces the examples' proof devices (a doctor, named customers, a study percentage, a review count, a sold-out warning) and fills them with invented content. Some of the input material was invented on purpose, e.g. the Airbnb offer overview prompt asks Gemini to "create a fictional spokesperson and other relevant data" ([05-03 03:31](../lessons/05-03-airbnb-arbitrage-example.md)). All of it is **structure only; claims not reusable**. Under [AGENTS.md](../../../../../../AGENTS.md) rule 2 ("Never invent proof") none of it can reach a live asset.

## Procedure
1. **Load the truth sheet.** If it does not exist, stop and ask the user for: product facts and label/ingredient doses, the real offer and prices (including any real prior price), guarantee terms, real reviews (count, platform, date, link), real testimonials with permission, real studies (link, population, dose, human or not), real spokesperson and their actual credentials, real inventory or deadline rules, subscription terms. List what is missing.
2. **Extract claims.** Go sentence by sentence. Every sentence that asserts a fact, a number, a person, a result, a comparison, a cause, a deadline or a stock level becomes one row in the claim register.
3. **Tag each row** with one or more check IDs from the table below.
4. **Resolve.** For each row: SOURCED (cite truth-sheet line), FIX (apply the compliant pattern) or CUT.
5. **Verdict.** Any unresolved ⛔ row = BLOCK. Any unresolved ⚠ row = FIX. All rows resolved = PASS.
6. **Re-run** the audit on the fixed draft (fixes introduce new claims).
7. **Log** the verdict block at the top of the deliverable.

## Checks (pass/fail, why, fix)
⛔ = blocking. ⚠ = must fix before use but not a hard block once corrected.

| ID | Check | FAIL if | Why (course evidence, cited) | Compliant fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 ⛔ | **Invented expert / doctor / spokesperson** | Any named expert, doctor, researcher, founder or host who is not a real, consenting person on the truth sheet; any credential (MD, "Johns Hopkins PhD", "three biotech patents", "anti-aging doctor") not verified | "Dr. Blane Schilling, MD" and a physician narrator are asserted by the brief or model ([07-02 compliance flags](../lessons/07-02-medium-length-vsl.md)); "Dr. Elias Vance" with Big Pharma history and patents is a fictional teaching prop ([05-05 Agent use](../lessons/05-05-financial-promo-example.md)); "Persona and authority are fabricated (a doctor with invented credentials)" ([05-04 compliance check](../lessons/05-04-blood-sugar-supplement-example.md)); "Dr. Erik Johansson" is model-generated ([09-02 compliance flags](../lessons/09-02-claude-project-for-advertorials.md)); "Marcus Hayes" persona is fictional ([05-03](../lessons/05-03-airbnb-arbitrage-example.md)) | Use the real founder, formulator or a real, contracted expert with their true credentials: `[VERIFY: expert name, credential, consent on file]`. No real expert → write without one (third-person "studies on [ingredient] show…" with a real citation) |
| C2 ⛔ | **Invented testimonials / named customers** | Any quote, first name + age/city, before/after story or result attributed to a customer that is not a real review with permission | Laura script testimonials "Carol M., Patricia L., Elizabeth K." are copied from the brief ([07-02](../lessons/07-02-medium-length-vsl.md)); "Michelle from Arizona", "David from Texas" are model-generated ([09-02](../lessons/09-02-claude-project-for-advertorials.md)); named persons "Sarah Martinez, Margaret" and A1C testimonials ([05-04](../lessons/05-04-blood-sugar-supplement-example.md)); Stefan: "to be compliant, you would obviously want to put real testimonials" ([09-02 38:01](../lessons/09-02-claude-project-for-advertorials.md)); research quotes drift between LLM summaries, so they are "paraphrase leads, not verbatim testimonials" ([02-03 compliance flags](../lessons/02-03-psychographic-research.md)) | Paste real reviews verbatim from the truth sheet (platform, date); or `[VERIFY: real review about <theme>]`. Use research quotes only as language inspiration ("some of our customers share that ___" [15-05 doc 2](../lessons/15-05-claude-project-for-writing-email-sequences.md)), never as attributed testimony |
| C3 ⛔ | **Invented stats, studies or "clinical" language** | Any percentage, study result, "clinically proven", "clinical studies show", "X% of [people/products]" without a linked source on the truth sheet | "We can't say clinical studies. We're not doing clinical studies with the ingredient." ([07-03 v1 09:56](../lessons/07-03-3-7-minute-vsl.md)); "clinical studies showing up to 39% wrinkle reduction", "97% of eye products target the wrong cause" ([07-02](../lessons/07-02-medium-length-vsl.md)); "3X faster than benzoyl peroxide", "87%", "92%", "Zero reported side effects" ([09-02](../lessons/09-02-claude-project-for-advertorials.md)); "over 20 human clinical trials", "40+ peer-reviewed papers" ([05-04](../lessons/05-04-blood-sugar-supplement-example.md)); "After helping hundreds of people" is a fabricated proof line from the model ([03-03 41:18](../lessons/03-03-ump-evaluation-prompt-and-process-airbnb-arbitrage.md)) | Cite the actual study in plain words: "A [year] study of [population, n] taking [dose] of [ingredient] found [result] [link]" only if it is on the truth sheet; otherwise `[VERIFY: study for <claim>]` or cut. Never "clinically proven" unless the finished product was tested |
| C4 ⛔ | **Ingredient-study result presented as product result** | A number or effect from an ingredient study, animal/cell study, or a different dose is stated as what our product does | Script numbers (32% wrinkle depth, 14% firmness, 51%, 134%, 440%) "come from ingredient-level studies… not from tests of the finished product" ([07-02](../lessons/07-02-medium-length-vsl.md)); brief doses (berberine 500 mg etc.) not in the research; HOMA-IR figure mislabelled "meta-analysis"; rodent/cell findings turned into benefit statements ([05-04](../lessons/05-04-blood-sugar-supplement-example.md)); "A study of an ingredient at a given dose does not substantiate our finished product at its dose" and animal/cell results "cannot be presented as human efficacy" ([02-05 compliance flags](../lessons/02-05-ingredient-deep-research.md)) | Attribute to the ingredient and the study conditions: "[Ingredient] has been studied at [dose] in [population]…"; match dose to our label; drop animal/cell data from consumer copy or label it as lab research with no human claim |
| C5 ⛔ | **Invented review counts, ratings, customer counts, sales volume** | Any "X reviews", "4.8/5", "X happy customers", "helped X people", "X sold", "Y% reorder" not matching a live, checkable source | "15,000 five-star reviews" vs 356 on Trustpilot, Stefan's own check ([09-01 40:34–41:09](../lessons/09-01-advertorials-overview-and-examples.md)); "4.8 out of 5 across more than 3,400 reviews", "5,800 happy customers" ([07-02](../lessons/07-02-medium-length-vsl.md)); "Over 12,000 patients" ([07-03](../lessons/07-03-3-7-minute-vsl.md)); "helped over 300,000 people" ([04-08](../lessons/04-08-medium-vsls-structures-and-examples.md)); "1,168,778 pairs sold" swipe ([12-02 Agent use](../lessons/12-02-native-ads.md)); "347% increase in order volume", "customer reorders up 400%" ([15-03](../lessons/15-03-1-3-promotional-sequences.md)) | Use the real number with platform and date, rounded down ("[N]+ reviews on [platform]"); below a meaningful count, use qualitative proof or none. Evolve's tier rule applies: "12,000 women" corrected to "thousands" only when thousands is true ([Evolve scorecard G3](../../../evolve/copywriting/rubrics/copy-checklist-scorecard.md)) |
| C6 ⛔ | **Fake scarcity / urgency / live counts** | Any stock level, sold-out history, capacity limit, "live viewers", countdown, "before it's taken down" or expiry that is not a real, enforced rule | Stefan on "almost 1,000 people live shopping": "Obviously not compliant." ([04-02 30:12](../lessons/04-02-vsl-fundamentals-classic-structure.md)); "59% left in stock" is "probably not true… like fake scarcity… not condoning it" ([11-01 04:04](../lessons/11-01-checkouts-walkthrough-part-1.md)); "sold out twice", "supplies are limited" ([07-02](../lessons/07-02-medium-length-vsl.md)); "can only accept 500 new patients", "before this presentation gets taken down" ([07-03](../lessons/07-03-3-7-minute-vsl.md)); timers set to the current date are disallowed by "most… bigger native traffic sources" (guest claim, [12-02 v3 19:00](../lessons/12-02-native-ads.md)); "we broke the internet" fake-outage SMS ([16-04 v2 17:20–17:51](../lessons/16-04-webinars-live-talk.md)); "this stuff expires in 24 hours" when it does not ([15-02](../lessons/15-02-1-2-welcome-sequences.md)) | Only real constraints: a sale that really ends `[VERIFY: end date/time, enforced in checkout]`, a real batch size, a real price window. Otherwise use reason-why urgency without a deadline: cost of waiting, the problem continuing ("reason why copy" [04-06 38:42](../lessons/04-06-vsl-closes.md)) |
| C7 ⛔ | **Disease, treatment or drug-comparison claim (supplements and devices)** | Copy names a disease or says the product treats, cures, reverses, prevents a disease, replaces or beats a medication, or lets someone stop medication | "reversing Type 2 diabetes", "works better than… metformin", "reduce dependence on diabetes medications", "stopped taking insulin/metformin", "A1C drop" testimonials, "Clinically proven" ([05-04](../lessons/05-04-blood-sugar-supplement-example.md)); "my doctor actually let me stop taking insulin", "A1C dropped from 8.4 to 6.2" ([07-03](../lessons/07-03-3-7-minute-vsl.md)); "comparable to metformin", HbA1c outcomes ([02-05](../lessons/02-05-ingredient-deep-research.md)); "relief in just 15 minutes", "it can even affect your heart and lungs", diuretic testimonial ([04-08](../lessons/04-08-medium-vsls-structures-and-examples.md)); "Reduces A1C by up to 2 points in 90 days" ([10-01](../lessons/10-01-pdp-overviews-and-claude-processes-for-writing-new-ones.md)); "reverse hearing loss" ([09-01](../lessons/09-01-advertorials-overview-and-examples.md)); "root cause" is diagnostic: "you're really not compliance-wise supposed to" ([03-01 18:46](../lessons/03-01-unique-mechanisms-overview.md)) | Structure/function wording only, backed by the truth sheet: "supports [normal function] `[VERIFY: substantiation]`", plus the standard supplement disclaimer where required (derived: FDA/DSHEA structure-function limits; FTC substantiation; Meta health-ad policy). Replace "root cause" with "one reason" / "a factor" |
| C8 ⛔ | **Cosmetic claim worded as a drug effect** | Skincare/topical copy says reverse, repair, rebuild collagen, inhibit enzymes, anti-inflammatory, "comparable to" a drug | "reversing", "reverses structural damage" ([07-02](../lessons/07-02-medium-length-vsl.md); Stefan: "I'm reversing, and clinical studies we would have to clean up" [07-03 v1 08:04](../lessons/07-03-3-7-minute-vsl.md)); "inhibits MMPs", "new collagen synthesis" ([03-04](../lessons/03-04-ideation-validation-and-expansion-walkthrough-eye-serum-exam.md)); "comparable to a mild NSAID effect" ([02-05](../lessons/02-05-ingredient-deep-research.md)); "wrinkles shrink by up to 12% in just 14 days" ([04-08](../lessons/04-08-medium-vsls-structures-and-examples.md)) | Appearance language: "visibly reduces the look of [fine lines]" only with a supporting test `[VERIFY: test, n, duration]`; otherwise sensory/experience language ("feels", "looks smoother after application") the brand can stand behind |
| C9 ⛔ | **Income / earnings / return claims** | Any income figure, student result, success rate, ROI multiple or "guaranteed" outcome in biz-opp, coaching, finance or investing copy without documented, typical results | "$12,000 monthly passive income", students "$3,200 monthly", "85% success rate" ([07-02](../lessons/07-02-medium-length-vsl.md)); "10x, 20x", "1,000% or more", "dump immediately" ([07-04](../lessons/07-04-short-vsl.md)); "$5,000 to $15,000 to get started", "15 to 35% profit margins" unverified ([03-03](../lessons/03-03-ump-evaluation-prompt-and-process-airbnb-arbitrage.md)); "virtually GUARANTEE SUCCESS" ([14-02 claims check](../lessons/14-02-upsells-case-studies.md)); "passive income", "sure bet" ([02-03](../lessons/02-03-psychographic-research.md)) | No numbers unless documented and typical, with a typical-results disclosure (derived: FTC earnings-claim rules; Meta income-claim policy). | C10 ⛔ | **Fabricated endorsement, authority, press or real-person likeness** | Any named institution ("Harvard", "Nobel Prize", "Johns Hopkins"), celebrity/investor, press logo, "reviewed by a nurse/doctor", or real person's name, face or voice without documented permission and accuracy | "Peter Thiel / Mark Cuban invested", "leaked memo", "former Big Pharma insider" ([07-04](../lessons/07-04-short-vsl.md)); "Harvard scientists", "Nobel-Prize-winning research" ([09-01](../lessons/09-01-advertorials-overview-and-examples.md)); deepfake and invented quotes of a real doctor: "Do not build this format" ([09-01](../lessons/09-01-advertorials-overview-and-examples.md)); Stefan: "one of these studies comes from Harvard. I have to confirm if Harvard actually does" ([07-03 v1 02:31](../lessons/07-03-3-7-minute-vsl.md)); he says he does not advocate deepfakes ([01-02 10:31–11:23](../lessons/01-02-foundational-knowledge-and-the-state-of-dtc-marketing-in-202.md)); celebrity-endorsement free-trial pages flagged ([13-01](../lessons/13-01-hidden-factors-for-optimal-funnel-structure.md)) | Name an institution only when the cited study was actually done there `[VERIFY: link]`; no celebrity, investor or press reference without written proof; no real person's likeness without consent. "We noticed X about [group]" framing, never "X endorses us" |
| C11 ⛔ | **Invented story presented as true ("it's an editorial" / "it's a metaphor")** | A founder, patient, discovery, prototype or "trial and tribulation" story that did not happen, presented as fact | Stefan on the Norse advertorial: "it's an editorial, so you can kind of not [be truthful], but you'd have to say… this is like a sort of made-up story or composite story" ([09-02 53:09](../lessons/09-02-claude-project-for-advertorials.md)); on the Nooro build-up: "This is all [made up], right?… I doubt they really made like nine prototypes. ## Scoring block (paste at the top of the deliverable)
```text
CLAIM AUDIT: <asset name> | draft source: <model/project> | truth sheet: <file/date>
Claims extracted: __   SOURCED: __   FIXED: __   CUT: __   OPEN [VERIFY]: __
⛔ C1 expert __  C2 testimonials __  C3 stats/studies __  C4 ingredient→product __  C5 counts __
⛔ C6 scarcity __  C7 disease/drug __  C8 cosmetic-as-drug __  C9 income __  C10 endorsement __
⛔ C11 invented story __  C13 reference price __  C14 subscription __  C15 format/pretext __  C16 AI persona __
⚠  C12 suppression __  C17 citation __  C18 legal __  C19 mismatch __  C20 over-scare __
Verdict: PASS | FIX | BLOCK      Human/legal review needed: Y/N (health, finance, biz-opp, weight loss = Y)
```
Each check is `pass` / `fail (row #s)` / `n.a. (reason)`. Any open `[VERIFY]` in a ⛔ category = BLOCK until filled with a sourced fact or cut.

## Audit prompt (derived, not a course asset)
Run in a fresh chat or a second model, with the draft and the truth sheet attached. It mirrors the course's second-model review step ([07-01 v2 00:00–06:38](../lessons/07-01-long-form-vsl.md)) applied to claims instead of style.
```text
You are a compliance editor for direct-response copy. Attached: (1) DRAFT, (2) TRUTH SHEET (the only allowed source of facts).
1. List every sentence in DRAFT that asserts a fact, number, person, credential, study, rating, review count, customer count, result, comparison, cause, deadline, stock level, price history or billing term.
2. For each, quote it, tag it with the check IDs C1-C20 below, and say whether the TRUTH SHEET supports it (quote the supporting line) or not.
3. For every unsupported claim, give a compliant rewrite that uses only TRUTH SHEET facts or a bracketed placeholder [VERIFY: fact needed, source needed]. Do not invent replacement facts.
4. Flag any disease, treatment, drug-comparison, "reverse", "clinically proven", income, earnings or ROI language, any named institution, celebrity or expert, any scarcity or deadline, and any subscription term as high-risk regardless of support.
5. Output: the claim register table, then the verdict (PASS / FIX / BLOCK) with reasons.
Checks: C1 invented expert; C2 invented testimonial; C3 invented stat/study; C4 ingredient study as product result; C5 counts/ratings; C6 fake scarcity; C7 disease/drug claim; C8 cosmetic-as-drug; C9 income claim; C10 fabricated endorsement/likeness; C11 invented story as true; C12 suppression framing; C13 fake reference price; C14 hidden subscription/add-on; C15 deceptive format/pretext; C16 AI persona as customer; C17 unverified citation; C18 legal assertion; C19 mismatch with offer; C20 over-scare.
```
