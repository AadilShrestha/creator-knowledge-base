# Sarah's Secret Book Stash
> A toolkit download: a 3-page PDF of "11 Rare & Underrated Books on Behavioral Science and Psychology." The full list, with authors and why each matters, is on **[Books](../../../resources/books.md)**.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** you need background theory or a citable source for a psychological mechanism behind an angle.
- **Inputs → outputs:** topic → book(s) on [Books](../../../resources/books.md).
- **Rules:** DO use the books as background, not as proof of product claims. DON'T invent quotes or stats "from" these books.
- **See also:** [Books](../../../resources/books.md) · [Ad Lab Toolkit](README.md)

## Page text (verbatim)
"**11 Rare & Underrated Books on Behavioral Science and Psychology.** Unlock a deeper level of strategy with 11 rare and underrated books that reveal how consumers *really* think, feel, and buy. Perfect for marketers, creatives, and brand owners who want an edge most of their competitors will never even discover."

## The PDF
- Title page: "RARE & UNDERRATED BOOKS ON BEHAVIORAL SCIENCE AND PSYCHOLOGY · BY SARAH LEVINGER", with a large "11".
- Pages 2–3: numbered list (title, authors, one-line description). Three clusters *(wiki grouping)*: **behavioral science & economics** (#1 Ledoux, #2 *Secrets of the Moneylab*, #11 *Behavioral Economics for Leaders*), **consumer behavior** (#3 *Consumerology*, #4 Engel/Blackwell/Kollat 1978, #5 Peter & Olson, #6 *Handbook of Research Methods*), **emotion & motivation** (#7 Lieberman, #8 Reeve, #9 Fang Xiang Ping, #10 *The Dictionary of Obscure Sorrows*).
- In the [July 9 live session](../05-live-recordings/2025-07-09-live-with-sarah.md) Sarah calls this the "very hidden" list in the community ([0:16:33]). Her top three public picks (*Marketing to Mindstates*, *Predictably Irrational*, *The Elements of Choice*) are **not** on it. See [Books → Mentioned in live sessions](../../../resources/books.md#mentioned-in-live-sessions).
