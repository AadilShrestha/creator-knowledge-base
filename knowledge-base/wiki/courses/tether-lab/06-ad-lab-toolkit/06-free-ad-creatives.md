# FREE Ad Creatives 🤩
> Despite the title, this toolkit item contains **no ad creatives**. It's only the CreativeOS offer: "Get 1 month FREE with CreativeOS by using the discount code: Tether", plus the CreativeOS logo. The "free ad creatives" are CreativeOS's template library during the free month.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** you need static ad templates or proven competitor references (CreativeOS Evergreen ads = live 90+ days) to remix a mechanism for our product.
- **Inputs → outputs:** chosen lever/angle → CreativeOS template or reference (by tag) → rebranded static.
- **Rules:** DO use code `TETHER` (written "Tether" here) for 1 month free. DO copy the layout/mechanism, not the competitor's assets. DON'T expect downloadable creatives on this page.
- **See also:** [CreativeOS (tool page)](../../../tools/creativeos.md) · [Ad Lab Toolkit](README.md)

## Page text (verbatim)
"**Get 1 month FREE with [CreativeOS](https://www.creativeos.com/) by using the discount code: Tether**"

Item 7, "Creative OS: 1 Month FREE 🤩", repeats the same offer with Sarah's pitch: "We've partnered with Creative OS because we genuinely believe static ad templates are the quickest, easiest way to put the ideas we teach into action… Creative OS gives you instant access to thousands of proven templates, no creative team or design skill required. You just copy to Figma, tweak the branding, and you're ready to launch." Benefits: "Test concepts quickly and consistently · Validate ideas without long production cycles · Learn what works through real-world data, not guesswork." Code: `TETHER`.
