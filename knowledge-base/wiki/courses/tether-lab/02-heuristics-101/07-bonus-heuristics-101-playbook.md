# Bonus: Heuristics 101 Playbook

> The download page for the 18-page **Heuristics 101** e-book by Sarah Levinger: "9 proven shortcuts that make your customer's brain say 'yes.'"

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** you need a one-page reference for all nine heuristics, or a pre-launch checklist for a script or static.
- **Inputs → outputs:** a draft ad → a pass or fail on the e-book's four mistakes, plus suggested heuristics to layer.
- **Rules:** DO layer 2–3 shortcuts. DON'T overcomplicate, manipulate, use one shortcut only, or argue rationally (e-book p.17).
- **See also:** [Heuristics 101 e-book (full summary)](../../../resources/heuristics-101-ebook.md), [Heuristics hub](../../../concepts/heuristics.md), [Heuristic stacking](../../../concepts/heuristic-stacking.md).

![Heuristics 101 e-book cover](../../../../raw/assets/tether-lab/pdf-pages/7-Heuristics-101-e-book/p01.png)

## Page text (verbatim)
> ## 9 proven shortcuts that make your customer's brain say "yes."
>
> Most ads fail because they talk to the wrong part of the brain.
>
> In this ebook, you'll learn how to trigger the *real* decision-maker — the subconscious — using nine proven heuristics.
>
> Master these mental shortcuts and you'll create ads that convert faster, feel easier, and lower your costs without relying on gimmicks or manipulation.

## What's inside
The full page-by-page summary, with page images, is at **[Heuristics 101 e-book](../../../resources/heuristics-101-ebook.md)**. In brief:
- pp.2–5: welcome; how the brain works (subconscious 11M bits/s vs conscious 40 bits/s); the pizza example; why we use heuristics.
- pp.7–15: one page per heuristic (definition, why, "Use it by…", Pro Tip, and a mock static-ad example).
- p.16: how to combine shortcuts (the Pela example).
- p.17: mistakes to avoid. p.18: final pep talk.

This is very likely the "worksheet... cheat sheet" Sarah tells you to download at the end of [lesson 5](05-stacking-psychology-for-growth.md) [08:06]. The Ad Lab Toolkit also lists it as "Heuristics 101 Playbook" (see [Welcome tour](../01-start-here/01-welcome-to-tether-lab.md)).

## How to use it
- Keep it open while you brief or design statics. Pick 2–3 shortcuts and layer them (p.16–17).
- Run your draft against the "Mistakes to avoid" page before launch.
