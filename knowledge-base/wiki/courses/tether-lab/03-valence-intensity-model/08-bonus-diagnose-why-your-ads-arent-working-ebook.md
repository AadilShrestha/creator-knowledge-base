# Bonus: Diagnose Why Your Ads Aren't Working (e-book)
> An 11-page PDF by Sarah Levinger that puts the whole module in writing: one chapter per lesson, plus visual cues per zone and short prompt starters.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** you need the condensed written rules, especially per-zone **visual cues** and one-line **prompt starters** for briefs.
- **Inputs → outputs:** target zone → visual and copy cues to put in a brief. Full extraction: [e-book summary](../../../resources/diagnose-why-your-ads-arent-working-ebook.md).

  - DO apply the visual equations: zoom in = more intensity, bold colors = more urgency, morning light = calm, desaturated tones = trust.
  - DON'T trust the e-book's Chapter 3 quadrant graphic labels. Use the video map.

## What the Skool page says
**"Fix the emotional tone of your ads and turn 'meh' into money."**
> Most ads don't fail because of bad hooks or offers — they fail because they miss the emotional tone entirely. This ebook will teach you how to spot emotional misfires, map your creative with the Valence & Intensity Model, and transform underperforming ads into psychology-driven winners that actually resonate and convert.

## What it adds beyond the videos
- **Visual cues per zone** ("Think:" lines). Zone 1: morning light, soft pastels, gentle copy. Zone 2: bold type, energy visuals, verbs that punch. Zone 3: sighs, subtle sarcasm, the tension before the solution. Zone 4: harsh lighting, high contrast, jarring visuals.
- Zone nicknames: "feel-good zone", "hype zone", "pain-point whisper", "danger zone".
- Zone 3 is "best in industries with thoughtful buyers". Zone 4 suits "high-stakes industries (safety, financial risk)" and "can backfire".
- Verb pair **"find" vs "seize"**. Visual rules: zoom in = more intensity, bold colors = more urgency, morning light = calm, desaturated tones = **trust**.
- Emotional-promise examples: **indulgence, safety, control**.
- One-line **prompt starters** per zone, and a skincare before/after example.
- Closing line: "The best ads aren't persuasive. They're emotionally calibrated."
