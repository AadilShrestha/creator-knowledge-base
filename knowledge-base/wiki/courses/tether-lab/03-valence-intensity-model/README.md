# Module 3: The Valence & Intensity Model
> **Diagnose why your ads aren't working (and how to fix them).** When an ad has a good offer, a strong headline and good-looking creative but still doesn't convert, the cause is usually the *emotional tone*. This module gives you a 2-axis map (valence × intensity) for finding where an ad sits emotionally, deciding where it should sit, and moving it there without rebuilding it.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** any step of the ad pipeline where emotional tone matters: tagging a competitor ad's emotional zone during teardown, choosing the zone for a new UGC script or brief, fixing a losing ad, or generating zone-specific variants.
- **Inputs → outputs:** ad or script + product + customer context → zone diagnosis ([diagnostic](../../../frameworks/valence-intensity-diagnostic.md)) → lever + rewrites ([tone-shifting](../../../frameworks/tone-shifting.md), [prompts](../../../resources/valence-intensity-prompts.md)).
- **Rules:**
  - DO diagnose emotional tone before touching hooks, price, targeting or creators. [L1 01:08]
  - DO score valence (what emotion) and intensity (how hard) separately, then read the zone. [L2 01:21; L3 00:32]
  - DO choose the zone that matches the buyer's state at exposure and their purchase motive. [L3 04:31; L4 05:04]
  - DO fix with the lightest lever (verbs → visuals → emotional promise), one per variant. [L5; L6]
  - DON'T default to Zone 3 in saturated categories. DON'T use Zone 4 to shame or trigger trauma. [L3 03:10, 03:48]
- **See also:** [Valence-Intensity diagnostic](../../../frameworks/valence-intensity-diagnostic.md) · [Tone-shifting](../../../frameworks/tone-shifting.md) · [Prompts](../../../resources/valence-intensity-prompts.md) · [Emotional zones](../../../concepts/emotional-zones.md)

## Learning goals
After this module you should be able to:
1. Explain why an ad that "should be working" fails: it's an **emotional mismatch**, not a creative, hook or price problem ([lesson 1](01-the-real-reason-your-ad-isnt-working.md)).
2. Define **[valence](../../../concepts/valence.md)** (*what* emotion: positive vs negative) and **[intensity](../../../concepts/intensity.md)** (*how hard* you hit it: whisper vs scream) ([lesson 2](02-understanding-valence-and-intensity.md)).
3. Place any ad in one of the **[4 emotional zones](../../../concepts/emotional-zones.md)** and know when each zone fits ([lesson 3](03-the-4-emotional-zones.md)).
4. Diagnose an **[emotional misfire](../../../concepts/emotional-misfire.md)** by comparing the ad's zone with the customer's emotional state, using four questions or a one-line AI prompt ([lesson 4](04-diagnosing-emotional-misfires.md)).
5. Shift an ad's tone with three levers (**verbs, visuals, [emotional promise](../../../concepts/emotional-promise.md)**) and four zone-rewrite prompts ([lesson 5](05-fixing-the-tone-without-rebuilding-the-ad.md)).
6. Run the 4-step "Why didn't my ad work?" loop on a real ad from your account ([lesson 6](06-map-and-move-your-messaging.md)).

## The model at a glance

| | **Low intensity** (whisper) | **High intensity** (scream) |
|---|---|---|
| **High / positive valence** | **Zone 1: Feel-good.** Cozy, calm, supportive, reassuring. For wellness, luxury, intimacy, safety and belonging. | **Zone 2: Hype & anticipation.** Joy, thrill, excitement, pride. For launches, performance, achievement and momentum. |
| **Low / negative valence** | **Zone 3: Pain-point whisper.** Annoyance, disappointment, fatigue, irritation. For soft problem awareness, before/after and setting up relief. | **Zone 4: Danger zone.** Panic, sadness, loss, anxiety. For limited-time offers, health/safety and financial risk. Use with care. |

## Module-wide takeaways
- **"Most marketers are going to optimize words… the winners are going to optimize for feel."** [L1 02:01]
- **"It's not your hook, it's not your price, it's your vibe."** [L1 01:25]
- **Valence = what you're saying, intensity = how you're saying it.** [L2 01:21]
- **No zone is wrong.** A zone works when it matches the emotional context your audience is in when they see the ad. [L3 04:13–04:34]
- **You can be ~10% off on the map and the ad will feel completely wrong.** [L5 05:17]
- **Don't scrap the ad, tune it:** change the verbs, the visuals, or (most important) the emotion you're selling. [L5 00:25, 03:42]
- **"Don't run static emotions in a dynamic feed."** Design emotional arcs. [L5 09:18]
- **"Every ad is an emotion… it's not just a message."** [L6 02:36]
- **Ethics:** it is "not our job to make people feel bad". Elicit the emotion attached to their real experience and don't trigger trauma. [L3 03:48–04:10]

## Running example: Copper Cow Coffee
The whole module works on one static Meta ad from **Copper Cow Coffee**. The brand name appears on screen only and is never spoken. The ad is a pink-background testimonial: *"So…I bought these for a gift. Then I drank them myself and had to re-order!" – Lee O.*
- L1: the ad "should work" but doesn't make money.
- L4: ChatGPT maps it to **Zone 1** (positive, low intensity, cozy). That's a likely misfire for coffee, which people buy "for a boost".
- L5: it gets rewritten into all four zones.

See [lesson 4](04-diagnosing-emotional-misfires.md) and [lesson 5](05-fixing-the-tone-without-rebuilding-the-ad.md).

## Quiz
The quiz page reads: "🧠 Quiz Time: How well do you understand Valence, Intensity & The 4 Emotional Zones?" It links to an external Typeform: <https://mqnukk249sy.typeform.com/to/cuU3mxpF>. It also asks students to "share your score or biggest takeaway in the group."

**The questions and answers are not in the course files.** They live on Typeform, which wasn't captured. The self-check below was **written for this wiki (not the official quiz)** and is based only on module content:

1. *What does valence describe?* The emotional **direction** of a message: positive (feels good) or negative (feels bad). In other words, *which* emotion. [L2]
2. *What does intensity describe?* The emotional **energy level**, i.e. how strongly the feeling is experienced (irritation vs rage). [L2]
3. *Which zone is "high valence, low intensity"?* Zone 1, the feel-good zone (cozy, calm, supportive, reassuring). [L3]
4. *Which zone suits product launches and performance products?* Zone 2, high valence + high intensity (hype/anticipation). [L3]
5. *Which zone does Sarah warn against if you're in a saturated industry, and why?* Zone 3 (low/low), because "a lot of marketers live" there. [L3 03:10]
6. *Which zone needs the most care, and why?* Zone 4 (low valence, high intensity: fear, panic, loss). It can backfire, and you must not trigger trauma. [L3]
7. *What are the four diagnostic questions?* (1) Is the emotion positive or negative? (2) Is it high or low energy? (3) What state is the customer in when they see it? (4) What do I want them to feel instead? [L4]
8. *Why might a calm Zone 1 ad fail with a stressed buyer on a deadline?* Their brain is attuned to *quick* solutions, not calm ones, so the ad gets ignored. [L4 04:13]
9. *What are the three levers for shifting tone?* Verbs/words, visuals, and the emotional promise (the emotion you're selling). The third matters most. [L5]
10. *Name two ways to raise visual intensity and two ways to lower it.* Raise: zoom in, use bolder or more saturated colour. Lower: zoom out, desaturate, use soft or morning light. [L5]

## Source notes and discrepancies
- **Two different 2×2 graphics.** The videos use valence on the vertical axis (high at top) and intensity on the horizontal axis (low on the left). The e-book's graphics put **intensity on the vertical axis and valence on the horizontal axis (positive → negative)**. The e-book's Chapter 3 graphic also labels its quadrants "Frustration / Inspiration / Indifference / Satisfaction", which doesn't match the zone definitions in its own text. Treat the **video map** as canonical. Details on the [e-book page](../../../resources/diagnose-why-your-ads-arent-working-ebook.md).
- Sarah uses "high/low valence" and "positive/negative valence" interchangeably.
- L6 mentions a "worksheet" and "cheat sheet" to download. The only downloadable files in this module are the e-book and the prompts PDF.
- Whisper transcription errors corrected in these notes: "valence identity map" → valence intensity map; "save for labor later" → save for later; "shift the emotional problem" → emotional promise (by context).
