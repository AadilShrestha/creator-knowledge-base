# Bonus: Valence + Intensity Prompts
> A 3-page PDF with four ChatGPT **"alteration prompts"**, one per emotional zone. Each rewrites an existing ad's copy *and* suggests an image direction for that zone.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** generating copy and image direction for a specific emotional zone (remix, variant, fix).
- **Inputs → outputs:** zone prompt + original ad → rewritten copy + image concept. Verbatim prompts: [resources/valence-intensity-prompts](../../../resources/valence-intensity-prompts.md).
- **Rules:** DO attach the original ad. DO pick the zone with the [diagnostic](../../../frameworks/valence-intensity-diagnostic.md) first. DO generate image direction as well as copy.
- **See also:** [Tone-shifting](../../../frameworks/tone-shifting.md)

![Prompts PDF cover](../../../../raw/assets/tether-lab/pdf-pages/9-Valence-+-Intensity---Prompts/p01.png)
*Cover: "Valence + Intensity Prompts" by Sarah Levinger (ChatGPT logo).*

## What the Skool page says
**"Quick prompts to rewrite any ad and instantly shift its emotional impact."**
> Once you've diagnosed your ad's emotional tone, it's time to reshape it. Use these targeted prompts to quickly rewrite any ad for each Valence & Intensity zone — so you can shift the message, the mood, and the visuals without starting from scratch.

## The four prompts at a glance
| Zone | Opening line |
|---|---|
| High valence, high intensity (Zone 2) | "Rewrite this ad as if the reader is about to have their breakthrough moment — and your product is the catalyst." |
| High valence, low intensity (Zone 1) | "Rewrite this ad as if your product is a sigh of relief they didn't know they needed." |
| Low valence, high intensity (Zone 4) | "Rewrite this ad as if the reader is about to make a big mistake — and your product is the only thing that stops it." |
| Low valence, low intensity (Zone 3) | "Rewrite this ad as if your product is quietly calling out a truth they've been avoiding." |

**Full verbatim prompts, usage notes and the diagnostic prompt:** [resources/valence-intensity-prompts](../../../resources/valence-intensity-prompts.md)

How Sarah uses them ([lesson 5](05-fixing-the-tone-without-rebuilding-the-ad.md) [06:03–08:31]): paste the prompt into ChatGPT **together with the underperforming ad**, then use the copy and image ideas it returns to build the new variant.
