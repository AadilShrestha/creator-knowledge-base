# Bonus: 3-Selves Analysis Prompts
> The downloadable "3-Selves Course – Prompts" doc: three ChatGPT prompts that score ads by self, link the scores to performance, and rewrite one ad for the other two selves.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** you need ready prompts (or the scoring logic to apply yourself) for self-scoring ads, linking self to performance, or rewriting a winner for the missing selves.
- **Inputs → outputs:** ads, optional metrics, or one winning ad → score tables, ratio and test directions, or two self rewrites.
- **Rules:** DO use the prompts verbatim, then adapt the output line (e.g. "generate an ad script" for UGC). DO keep numeric scoring. DON'T accept invented social proof.
- **See also:** [Three selves prompts](../../../resources/three-selves-prompts.md) · [Rewriting ads for new selves](../../../frameworks/rewriting-ads-for-new-selves.md)
