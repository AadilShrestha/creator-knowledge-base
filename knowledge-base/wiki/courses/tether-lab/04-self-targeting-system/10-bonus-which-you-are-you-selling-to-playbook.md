# Bonus: Which 'You' Are You Selling To? (Playbook)
> The module's companion e-book: a 10-page written version of the 3-selves system, plus worksheets, rewrite formulas, short prompts and a self-to-funnel guide.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** you need the compact written rules: spotting the self in customer language (reviews, DMs), scoring ads 0–10, the rewrite formula, one-line self prompts, or matching self to funnel stage.
- **Inputs → outputs:** reviews or ads → self tags and scores. Target self + funnel stage → copy direction.
- **Rules:** DO mine reviews, surveys and DMs for self language. DO use Awareness = Ideal/Ought, Consideration = Actual/Ideal, Conversion = Actual/Ought. DO score 10–20 recent ads to find the brand's "self signature".
- **See also:** [E-book resource page](../../../resources/which-you-are-you-selling-to-ebook.md) · [Three selves diagnostic](../../../frameworks/three-selves-diagnostic.md)

## What the bonus page says
**"How to Write Ads That Speak to Identity — Not Just Intention."**
"Most marketers sell benefits. But today's buyers want something deeper: to feel seen. This playbook shows you how to tap into the *Actual*, *Ideal*, and *Ought* selves of your audience using Self-Discrepancy Theory — so every ad hits like it was written just for them."

## TL;DR: what the e-book adds beyond the videos
- **Self-Spotting Worksheet** (ch. 2): the language, emotion and trigger for each self, to spot in **reviews, surveys and DMs**.
- **Ad Self-Score Worksheet** (ch. 3): score each self 0–10. Worked example: "No more skipping workouts. Your future self will thank you." scores Actual 1 / Ideal 7 / Ought 9.
- **Rewrite formula** (ch. 4): Headline 4–7 words, Subhead 1–2 lines, Social Proof if needed, Bullets or Badge. Skincare example.
- **One-line prompts per self** (ch. 5).
- **Self-to-Funnel Match Guide** (ch. 6): Awareness = Ideal/Ought · Consideration = Actual/Ideal · Conversion = Actual/Ought. *This isn't in the videos.*
- **Brand Self Signature** (ch. 7): score 10–20 recent ads and find your highest average self. *This isn't in the videos.*
