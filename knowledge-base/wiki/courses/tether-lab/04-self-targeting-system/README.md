# Module 4: The Self Targeting System
> Break the "angle rut" by targeting the right **buyer identity**. Every customer has three selves (**Actual, Ideal, Ought**). Most brands only talk to one. Diagnose which one, rewrite your winners for the other two, and test them side by side, all without changing the offer.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** any ad-creation task where you need *new angles from a proven ad*. Tag the self of a competitor's winner, then re-target its mechanism to the selves it ignores, for our product.
- **Inputs → outputs:** competitor or own ads (+ metrics) → self scores → self-shifted UGC scripts/statics → a one-ad-set test plan with per-self read-outs.
- **Rules:** DO diagnose → rewrite → test, in that order. DO keep the offer fixed. DO read CTR/AOV/LTV per self. DON'T default to aspirational Ideal-Self copy.
- **See also:** [Three selves diagnostic](../../../frameworks/three-selves-diagnostic.md) → [Rewriting ads for new selves](../../../frameworks/rewriting-ads-for-new-selves.md) → [Testing ads by self type](../../../frameworks/testing-ads-by-self-type.md) · [Prompts](../../../resources/three-selves-prompts.md)

## The module in one paragraph
Top ads fatigue because brands repeat the same few angles ([angle rut](../../../concepts/angle-rut.md)), and those angles almost always speak to the customer's **[Ideal Self](../../../concepts/ideal-self.md)**: aspiration, transformation, "next level". The Ideal-Self buying pool dries up. Using E. Tory Higgins' **Self-Discrepancy Theory** (1987), Sarah treats each customer as three selves: **[Actual](../../../concepts/actual-self.md)** (who I am now: validation, consistency), **Ideal** (who I want to be: hope, progress) and **[Ought](../../../concepts/ought-self.md)** (who I should be: duty, responsibility). Only one is "in the driver's seat" when an ad appears. The system has three steps: **diagnose** which self a sample of 9 ads targets (ChatGPT prompts), **rewrite** winners for the missing selves with the offer unchanged, and **test** all three in one ad set, reading results as identity signals (Actual: lower CTR, higher AOV · Ideal: high CTR, low AOV · Ought: slow start, high LTV).

## Learning goals
After this module you should be able to:
1. Explain **why ads stop working over time** (angle rut) and why waiting for fatigue is too late.
2. Describe the **three selves** from Self-Discrepancy Theory and the language, emotion and triggers of each.
3. Explain why a single self (usually Ideal) **can't scale**: the customer is a system of identities built by an energy-saving subconscious brain.
4. Run a **nine-ad identity scan** with the [three selves diagnostic](../../../frameworks/three-selves-diagnostic.md) and Sarah's ChatGPT prompts.
5. **Rewrite a winning ad** for the two missing selves without changing the offer ([framework](../../../frameworks/rewriting-ads-for-new-selves.md)).
6. **Set up and read a self-type test** ([framework](../../../frameworks/testing-ads-by-self-type.md)).

## Cheat sheet
| | Actual Self | Ideal Self | Ought Self |
|---|---|---|---|
| Question | Who am I right now? | Who do I want to be? | Who should I be? |
| Orientation | Present | Future | Social / duty |
| Seeks | Validation, consistency, identity alignment | Growth, pride, potential, emotional reward | Meeting a standard, safety, doing it right |
| Sell | Reassurance: "you're already this person" | Hope, aspiration, thrill, progress | Relief and reassurance: "you have a standard to uphold and this is how you meet it" |
| Language | "This is so me." "Exactly what I needed." "Made for people like you." | "One day I'll…" "Finally becoming…" "Unlock your next level." | "I should really…" "It's about time I…" "People are counting on you." |
| Emotion (e-book) | Comfort, pride | Hope, inspiration | Guilt, pressure |
| Test signature (L8) | Lower CTR, higher AOV, stable returns | High CTR, low AOV, faster clicks | Slow starts, strong intent, high LTV |
| Funnel fit (e-book) | Consideration, Conversion | Awareness, Consideration | Awareness, Conversion |
