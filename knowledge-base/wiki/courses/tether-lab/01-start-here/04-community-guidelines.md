# Community Guidelines

> The rules of the Tether Lab Skool group. There's a zero-tolerance policy: break a rule and you're permanently banned.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** not relevant to ad creation. These are community rules.
- **Inputs → outputs:** none.
- **Rules:** none for creative work.
- **See also:** [Module 1 overview](README.md).

**Intent:** "to create a **safe and positive** group and community of like-minded individuals who want to learn together, support each other, and create meaningful connections."

## Not allowed 🚫
- Spam
- Self-promotion
- Job promotion
- Advertisement
- Affiliate links
- Links to PDF documents
- Mention of your services
- Mention of other similar marketing services or agencies
- Hate/disrespect speech tone

## Expected
- 🔍 Search the category before you post.
- 💬 Have meaningful conversations.
- 📝 Put effort into writing valuable posts.

## Consequences
- ❌ Your posts/comments will be deleted.
- ⛔️ Your account will be banned permanently ("No second chances").
- ⚠️ Use "Report to Admin" for suspected spam or suspicious behaviour.
- P.S. The admins "reserve the right to remove any post that does not align with our theme," so they can curate a quality group.
