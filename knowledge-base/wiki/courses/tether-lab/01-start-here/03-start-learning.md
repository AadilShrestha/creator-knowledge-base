# Start learning!

> The recommended learning path through Tether Lab, and how Skool points work. Text page, no video.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** not relevant to ad creation, apart from the learning order (Heuristics 101 first).
- **Inputs → outputs:** none.
- **Rules:** none for creative work.
- **See also:** [Heuristics 101](../02-heuristics-101/README.md).

## TL;DR
- After introducing yourself, go to the Classroom and start with [Heuristics 101](../02-heuristics-101/README.md).
- Comment on what you learn in each video. Commenting "will accelerate your path to unlocking extras and bonuses in the group."
- **1 like = 1 point.** Be generous with likes. Points take you toward **Level 9**.

## The 4-step path (from the page)

| Step | Instruction |
|---|---|
| **Step 1: Watch [Heuristics 101](../02-heuristics-101/README.md) 🎓** | The foundational course on mental shortcuts in ads |
| **Step 2: Join our LIVE calls every week 📅** | The weekly calls (the community tab shows a "Weekly Q&A" countdown). Recordings live in the Recordings course. |
| **Step 3: Execute what you have learnt 🏆** | Apply it to real ads |
| **Step 4: Submit your wins in the community 🥳** | Post results in the Wins category |

(Each step has a banner image on the page: "HEURISTICS 101", a calendar graphic, "Execute what you've learnt", "Submit Your Wins!". The images are hosted on Skool and weren't saved locally.)

## Points and likes (verbatim)
> **P.S Like (👍) other members' comments to earn points and help each other grow!**
>
> **Remember, 1 Like = 1 point → The more "👍" the better :)**
>
> By "liking" other posts and comments, you are helping other members advance in their educational journey. Therefore, please be generous with your "Likes" 🙏 Click away! 👍
>
> P.P.S. - Learning comes from doing. By leaving a comment and liking other, you transform watching these videos from entertainment into education. In the process, you'll retain more information, make our community stronger, and earn more points on your way to Level 9 :)

## How to apply
- [ ] Watch Heuristics 101 and leave a comment under each lesson with your takeaway.
- [ ] Put the weekly live call in your calendar.
- [ ] Test one heuristic in an ad, then post the result in **Wins**.
