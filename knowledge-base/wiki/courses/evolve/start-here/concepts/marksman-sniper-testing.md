# Marksman, Sniper and Shotgun testing
> **Marksman** = several angles in one concept, one execution each, to find "direction" (the angle Meta spends on); **Sniper** = one angle, several executions, to go deep once direction exists; return to Marksman at a plateau; **Shotgun** (random volume) is only for creator content, never for the creative-strategy team.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** choosing the testing method for the next batch; reading a multi-angle test result; planning follow-ups after an angle gets spend; deciding whether an angle is dead or badly executed; setting weekly concept volume.
- **Inputs → outputs:** angle list with sub-avatars, account state (any angle getting spend? plateau?), format (image/video), team size → method per concept, angles per concept, variations per angle, the hypothesis to test, and the write-off rule.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** run Marksman first to find **direction** when you lack data or have plateaued. [L81 04:15–05:12; 10:58](../lessons/081-the-3-testing-methods-part-1.md)
  - **DO** switch to Sniper once an angle gets spend ("what angle Meta is liking"). [L81 08:37](../lessons/081-the-3-testing-methods-part-1.md)
  - **DO** go back to Marksman "when performance plateaus on an angle". [L81 10:58](../lessons/081-the-3-testing-methods-part-1.md)
  - **DO** write a hypothesis on why the angle wins and "why the customer cares", then test it: "You don't blindly test things." [L81 12:53](../lessons/081-the-3-testing-methods-part-1.md); [L82 07:55–08:51](../lessons/082-the-3-testing-methods-part-2.md)
  - **DO** in a Marksman video make "the hooks more specific and the holds more generic". [L83 17:19](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md)
  - **DO** in a Sniper video make the hold specific to that one sub-avatar; "if your hold… could be used on any angle, I don't really think it's that good of a hold". [L83 09:49–11:23](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md)
  - **DON'T** run "half Sniper, half Marksman". [L83 20:37–21:11](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md)
  - **DON'T** shotgun with the creative team: "Do not use shotguns in 2025 when it comes to creative strategy." [L81 17:03–18:55](../lessons/081-the-3-testing-methods-part-1.md)
  - **IF** an angle failed in Marksman and you were confident **THEN** run a Sniper batch 2; batch 3 only if a feedback loop shows an execution flaw; "anything after three" → move on. [L88 33:16–34:04](../lessons/088-the-execution-problem.md)
  - **IF** all three formats of an angle fail **THEN** suspect strategy; **IF** one or two give direction **THEN** it is execution. [L88 27:24–28:36](../lessons/088-the-execution-problem.md)
  - **IF** the team is small **THEN** one concept a day (5-7 a week); one Marksman concept counts as "almost three concepts". [L81 18:55–19:53](../lessons/081-the-3-testing-methods-part-1.md)
  - **DO** make the best ad possible regardless of method: "the customer doesn't care about this". [L81 11:43](../lessons/081-the-3-testing-methods-part-1.md); [L83 22:04–23:49](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md)
- **See also:** [ad-definitions](ad-definitions.md) · [core-and-sub-avatars](core-and-sub-avatars.md) · [winning-ad-labels](winning-ad-labels.md) · [evolve-growth-loop](evolve-growth-loop.md) · [Campaign structure 3:2:2](../frameworks/campaign-structure-322.md) · [Learnings review](../frameworks/learnings-review.md) · [Rewrite losers and iterate](../frameworks/rewrite-losers-and-iterate.md) · Copywriting: [Iteration playbook](../../copywriting/frameworks/iteration-playbook.md)

## Definition
| | Marksman | Sniper | Shotgun |
|---|---|---|---|
| Definition | "Test a lot of things at once to see what sticks, but… in a controlled environment" | "Test within a strict environment to get closer to significant learnings as fast as possible" | "Test a bunch of random things at once and see what sticks" |
| Inside one 3:2:2 | 3 different angles, usually 1 execution each | 1 angle, 3 executions (styles, headlines, hooks, formats, deeper sub-avatar cut) | Many creator videos with light guidance |
| Use when | New account/concept, no significant data, or plateau | An angle is getting spend ("direction") | TikTok Shop / Insense product seeding at scale |
| Pros | Fluid; learns direction fast; more angles per test | Clear learnings; validates the angle; rules out the "execution problem" | Raw material volume, little strategy time |
| Cons | Cannot validate any one angle; hard to say "this angle won't work" | Slow, restrictive, "iteration overload", misses breakthroughs elsewhere | Meta ignores tiny variations; not a creative-team strategy |
| Source | [L81 02:18–06:48](../lessons/081-the-3-testing-methods-part-1.md) | [L81 06:48–10:14](../lessons/081-the-3-testing-methods-part-1.md) | [L81 15:29–18:55](../lessons/081-the-3-testing-methods-part-1.md) |

- **Direction** = "identifying the specific angle that you should be going deeper on, aka doing more research and iterating on, to create more scalable ads". [L81 04:15–05:12](../lessons/081-the-3-testing-methods-part-1.md)
- Rule of thumb (doc): "When you have ONE Angle = you're taking more of a 'Sniper Approach'… When you have MULTIPLE Angles = you're taking more of a 'Marksman Approach'." [L81 10:58](../lessons/081-the-3-testing-methods-part-1.md)
- Agency sequence: Marksman 1st to find direction → Sniper 2nd to go deeper → Marksman again at plateau. [L81 10:58](../lessons/081-the-3-testing-methods-part-1.md)

## Why it matters
- "It is okay to test multiple angles, but only if you are aware of them": awareness of which sub-avatar each angle targets lets you refine and scale the winner. [L82 19:48](../lessons/082-the-3-testing-methods-part-2.md) "Being aware of this stuff is 99% of the battle." [L81 00:46](../lessons/081-the-3-testing-methods-part-1.md)
- Perfect single-variable testing is "nearly impossible… as a result of Meta's updates"; "teeny tiny swings… are just not significant enough for Meta to actually care". [L81 12:17](../lessons/081-the-3-testing-methods-part-1.md)
- The humidifier case went from about $10K/week to about $80K/week of spend capacity "with the same return on ad spend" via Marksman → Sniper → bigger Sniper swing (instructor claim; frames approximate). [L82 17:59–19:48](../lessons/082-the-3-testing-methods-part-2.md)
- "Most people NEVER know why something is winning" (doc); cracking it is how accounts scale "from like 5K a day to like 80K a day" (instructor claim). [L81 12:53](../lessons/081-the-3-testing-methods-part-1.md)

## How to use (steps)
1. **Marksman batch:** group ~3 angles (one sub-avatar with 3 angles, or 3 sub-avatars with one angle each) into one concept, one variation each, as a 3:2:2. [L81 06:06–06:48](../lessons/081-the-3-testing-methods-part-1.md)
   - Images: "three different angles and you test them congruently" (headline, image, style say the same thing). [L83 11:23; 13:43](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md)
   - Video: vary the first 3-5 seconds (up to 10); hold stays the same and must be "universal enough to support all of the angles", i.e. shows the shared desire/mechanism. [L83 17:19–19:15](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md)
2. **Read direction** after the test window: which variation took the spend? (Label it with [winning-ad-labels](winning-ad-labels.md).) [L82 13:50; 17:12–17:59](../lessons/082-the-3-testing-methods-part-2.md)
3. **Hypothesis:** name the surface desire, the behavior or product experience, then ask "why" to the core desire; verify in research. [L82 07:06–11:10](../lessons/082-the-3-testing-methods-part-2.md)
4. **Sniper round 1 (small swing):** same angle, different images, subtext, hooks. [L82 18:53–19:48](../lessons/082-the-3-testing-methods-part-2.md)
5. **Sniper round 2 (bigger swing):** more research, new format (e.g. storytelling AI voiceover), narrower sub-avatar (e.g. parents). "This is where most people stop." [L82 17:59–19:48](../lessons/082-the-3-testing-methods-part-2.md)
6. **Losers too:** extract behaviors/desires to go deeper on, "rather than just 'this headline sucks'". [L82 13:50](../lessons/082-the-3-testing-methods-part-2.md)
7. **Plateau:** queue the next Marksman with new angles. [L81 10:58](../lessons/081-the-3-testing-methods-part-1.md)

## Examples (from the lessons)
- **Humidifier superlatives (Marksman → Sniper → Sniper):** phase 1, three statics; the stainless-steel row took about $9.95K of about $10.0K (frame, ROAS about 2.79); phase 2, different images/subtext, about $18K/week at 2.8x; phase 3, storytelling AI voiceover for parents, about $80K/week at about the same ROAS (instructor claim; frames low-resolution). Doctor-designed lost: "people don't care if the humidifier has been designed by a doctor." [L82 03:46–21:39](../lessons/082-the-3-testing-methods-part-2.md)
- **POV: Label (Marksman video) → night-shift Sniper:** four labels; night shift took direction; the Sniper follow-up failed on a generic hold ("Fix your sleep with a hundred percent darkness… 30 day in home trial"). Should have shown "someone coming home from work at 7 a.m., sun rising… closing the blinds". [L83 06:25–11:23](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md)
- **Dog harness "Nutella" statics (Marksman, 2 angles):** if "This is a harness / This is confidence" wins, follow with a Sniper video on "zero confidence in your ability as a dog owner to walk your dog". [L83 00:39–06:25](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md)
- **Rabbit R1 kids angle (Sniper):** three headlines over one product photo for one sub-avatar. [L83 27:11–28:57](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md)
- **Shotgun that works:** creator content blasted into the account; agency used it "recently to scale a brand to $40,000 a day" (instructor claim). [L81 15:29–17:03](../lessons/081-the-3-testing-methods-part-1.md)

## Common mistakes
- **Shotgun inside creative strategy:** "dozens of iterations or dozens of videos with teeny tiny little hook changes." [L81 17:03–18:55](../lessons/081-the-3-testing-methods-part-1.md)
- **Too-specific hold in a Marksman video** (night-shift hold under "light sleeper" hook). [L83 19:57–20:37](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md)
- **Generic hold in a Sniper test** (career-ending-injury ad: no behaviors, experiences, emotions). [L83 24:48–27:11](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md)
- **Killing an angle for an execution error:** "most people suck at execution and can get turned off from an angle because they screwed up execution". [L83 13:43–14:42](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md)
- **Separating image and video campaigns because images don't spend:** "your image ads aren't getting spent because they suck." [L83 14:42–15:40](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md)
- **Stopping at a small iteration** after direction instead of a research-based bigger swing. [L82 19:48](../lessons/082-the-3-testing-methods-part-2.md)
- **Iteration overload / sniping forever:** take a break to reset. [L81 08:37–10:14](../lessons/081-the-3-testing-methods-part-1.md); [L88 34:48–35:34](../lessons/088-the-execution-problem.md)
- **Freezing over method choice:** "just pick one and go and be aware of it." [L81 00:46](../lessons/081-the-3-testing-methods-part-1.md)
