# Revenue-stage focus (what to do at each revenue and spend level)
> Evolve gates most decisions by stage: **$50K-300K/month = volume** (quality reps, tracked), **$300K-3M = intent** (winning-ad rate, congruent pages, free UGC, feedback loops), **$3M-10M = systems** (leverage, A players, AI); avatar breadth, testing method, campaign complexity, attribution and KPIs all scale with revenue or ad spend, and the product caps every stage.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** planning what a brand should work on next; scoping a creative or media plan to the account's size; deciding how many core avatars, which test method, which campaign add-ons and which KPI to use; pushing back on a tactic copied from a much bigger brand.
- **Inputs → outputs:** monthly revenue, monthly ad spend, daily ad spend, creative hit rate, team size → the stage, its 5 focus items, the gated settings (avatars, tests, campaigns, attribution, KPI) and a stop-doing list.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** state which axis a rule uses: monthly **revenue** (L71, L175), monthly **ad spend** (L112 tiers) or **daily** ad spend (L93, L97, L115, L116, L164). Ask for all three before planning. (derived; see conflicts)
  - **DO** match work to the bucket: "$50k-$300k/mth = volume, $300k-$3M/mth = intent, $3M-$10M/mth = systems", and revisit the plan every time the brand enters a new bucket. [L175 00:00; 21:12](../lessons/175-what-to-focus-on-to-scale-to-10m-mth-by-rev.md)
  - **DO** earn breadth: "As a brand, you earn the right to go broader." Under ~$100K/month run one desire-based core avatar. [L71 2:23:52–2:28:55](../lessons/071-the-evolve-avatar-training.md)
  - **DON'T** copy what a big brand does today; copy "what they did when they were at your size". [L71 2:10:47–2:13:25](../lessons/071-the-evolve-avatar-training.md)
  - **IF** under $1K/day **THEN** focus on winning ads, not ABO add-ons; keep to ~5 new test ad sets; use 7DC+1DV if data-starved. [L97 page text](../lessons/097-how-to-set-up-abo-scaling-campaign.md); [L93 00:58](../lessons/093-full-media-buying-strategy-outline-2026.md); [L116 11:50–12:11](../lessons/116-how-to-understand-facebook-ads-attribution.md)
  - **IF** under $2K/day **THEN** test one landing page only (no 3:2:2:2) and put whitelisted ads in the main campaign. [L93 00:58](../lessons/093-full-media-buying-strategy-outline-2026.md); [L164 00:00–00:48](../lessons/164-how-to-test-whitelisted-ads.md)
  - **IF** hit rate drops as volume rises ($300K+/month) **THEN** cut volume: "Winning ads per month is all we care about." [L175 06:47–07:46](../lessons/175-what-to-focus-on-to-scale-to-10m-mth-by-rev.md)
  - **IF** results are stuck at any stage **THEN** audit the product before the ads: "your product is what will create the majority of your success." [L173 00:58](../lessons/173-how-brands-actually-scale-to-8-figures.md)
  - **DON'T** read volume as ripping: "rip creatives, use AI to copy-paste scripts. That's not how it works." [L175 01:50](../lessons/175-what-to-focus-on-to-scale-to-10m-mth-by-rev.md)
- **See also:** [core-and-sub-avatars](core-and-sub-avatars.md) · [marksman-sniper-testing](marksman-sniper-testing.md) · [winning-ad-labels](winning-ad-labels.md) · [click-based-attribution](click-based-attribution.md) · [two-ways-to-win](two-ways-to-win.md) · [mindset-operating-system](mindset-operating-system.md) · [content-engine](content-engine.md) · [Scaling rules](../frameworks/scaling-rules.md) · [Campaign structure 3:2:2](../frameworks/campaign-structure-322.md) · [Launch a new product end to end](../playbooks/launch-a-new-product-end-to-end.md)

## Definition
**Revenue-stage focus** = choosing the few levers that move a brand at its current size and ignoring the rest. Two instructors define the stages differently:

| Source | Stages (monthly revenue) | Theme |
|---|---|---|
| L175 (Shaun Eng) | $50K-300K · $300K-3M · $3M-10M | Volume · Intent · Systems [L175 00:00](../lessons/175-what-to-focus-on-to-scale-to-10m-mth-by-rev.md) |
| L71 (Spencer) | $0-100K · $100K-1M · $1M-10M · $10M+ | Avatar count and breadth [L71 2:23:52–2:34:39](../lessons/071-the-evolve-avatar-training.md) |
| L112 (Spencer) | $0-100K · $100K-250K · $250K-500K · $500K+ **ad spend** | Winning-ad spend-share bar [L112 00:00–04:03](../lessons/112-winning-ad-definitions-0-mo-1m-mo.md) |

## Why it matters
- Finite time: "Success comes down to doubling down on what matters and not doing the things [that don't]." [L175 00:51–01:50](../lessons/175-what-to-focus-on-to-scale-to-10m-mth-by-rev.md)
- A beginner at 100K/month competing on a broad avatar with a 5M+/month operator "will lose". [L71 36:24–36:56](../lessons/071-the-evolve-avatar-training.md)
- Volume without hit rate wastes reps: "20% hit rate on 30 creatives a month, that's 6 winning ads a month… 100 creatives per month, 1% hit rate… only 1." [L175 06:47](../lessons/175-what-to-focus-on-to-scale-to-10m-mth-by-rev.md)
- Complexity at low spend spreads budget "too thin". [L93 00:58](../lessons/093-full-media-buying-strategy-outline-2026.md)
- Product ceiling: same strategy, new product, $6,940 → $38,960 monthly spend and ROAS 3.39 → 4.44 (slide screenshots). [L173 01:55–02:48](../lessons/173-how-brands-actually-scale-to-8-figures.md)

## How to use (steps)
1. Collect monthly revenue, monthly ad spend, current daily spend, last 30 days' hit rate, team roles. (derived)
2. Place the brand in the L175 bucket and the L71 avatar stage; note the L112 ad-spend tier for labelling.
3. Pull the bucket's five focus items (table A) and the gated settings (table B).
4. Write a stop-doing list from the "don't" column.
5. Re-run when revenue crosses a boundary, and reset the winning-ad bar when ad spend changes tier. [L175 21:12](../lessons/175-what-to-focus-on-to-scale-to-10m-mth-by-rev.md); [L112 16:51–18:15](../lessons/112-winning-ad-definitions-0-mo-1m-mo.md)

## Common mistakes
- Mixing axes (calling a $250K ad-spend account "$250K/month brand"). [L112 01:27](../lessons/112-winning-ad-definitions-0-mo-1m-mo.md)
- Broad avatars before the brand has the product/offer/brand/skill for them. [L71 29:11–31:01](../lessons/071-the-evolve-avatar-training.md)
- Building the ABO add-ons to "fix" bad ads: "THIS WILL NOT FIX YOUR BAD ADS." [L97 page text](../lessons/097-how-to-set-up-abo-scaling-campaign.md)
- Pushing creative volume while hit rate falls. [L175 07:46](../lessons/175-what-to-focus-on-to-scale-to-10m-mth-by-rev.md)
- Blaming the agency or the program when the product is not superior in a way "the market cares about". [L173 04:36; 08:14–09:04](../lessons/173-how-brands-actually-scale-to-8-figures.md)
- Thinking the next stage needs a big team ("Not true"). [L175 05:49](../lessons/175-what-to-focus-on-to-scale-to-10m-mth-by-rev.md)

- **Tether Lab (agrees on precision below 8 figures):** [Creative testing system](../../../../frameworks/creative-testing-system.md): "Volume is a crutch"; below 8 figures use a precision cadence (3-5 new ads/week); sub-$5M statics only (Chase); sub-$10M don't copy 9-figure playbooks.
