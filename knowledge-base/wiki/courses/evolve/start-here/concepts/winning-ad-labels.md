# Winning-ad labels (Breakthrough / Spend Winner / KPI Winner / Loser) with tier thresholds
> "There is no such thing as just a 'winning ad'": after 7 days in a CBO, every test ad is labelled from its **share of spend**, its **ROAS versus the initial campaign ROAS**, and whether **campaign (or account) budget rose because of it**; the spend-share bar depends on the account's monthly **ad spend** tier, labels are re-checked in weeks 2-3, and **breakthrough hit rate** is the team's north star.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** labelling a test ad after week 1; deciding keep / kill / move to ABO / iterate; setting the "winner" bar for a brand's spend tier; reporting hit rate; choosing which iteration list to brief.
- **Inputs → outputs:** account monthly ad spend at launch, test structure (CBO), ad spend and initial-campaign (or account) spend for weeks 0-3, ad ROAS vs campaign ROAS on click-based attribution, whether daily budget rose with no other cause → one label + the week assigned + routing (ABO ad set / zombie / iterate) + the fix list.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** label only CBO tests: "these percentages are for CBO, not for ABO." [L112 04:03–05:13](../lessons/112-winning-ad-definitions-0-mo-1m-mo.md)
  - **DO** tier by **ad spend per month, not revenue**, measured at the account level when the ad launched. [L112 01:27; 10:19–11:24](../lessons/112-winning-ad-definitions-0-mo-1m-mo.md)
  - **DO** judge on click-based data (7-day or 1-day click) and net-new customers. [L110 09:38–12:01](../lessons/110-winning-ads-definition-part-1.md) See [click-based-attribution](click-based-attribution.md).
  - **DO** call it a Breakthrough only if it holds the spend share **and** the budget rose week over week because of it (10% → 100%+). "A spend winner that does not raise the budget… is a spend winner. It is not a breakthrough." [L112 06:39](../lessons/112-winning-ad-definitions-0-mo-1m-mo.md)
  - **DON'T** call an ad a winner from a dollar threshold: "just because an ad spends $2,000 doesn't make it a winner." [L110 02:28](../lessons/110-winning-ads-definition-part-1.md)
  - **DO** relabel in weeks 2 and 3; at higher tiers "the scale usually doesn't happen until week 2 or week 3". [L111 00:47](../lessons/111-winning-ads-definition-part-2.md)
  - **DO** leave Spend Winners and KPI Winners on; "if you have a loser, you're more than likely good to turn it off." [L112 20:48](../lessons/112-winning-ad-definitions-0-mo-1m-mo.md)
  - **IF** a KPI Winner has very high ROAS on low spend **THEN** let it run (sheet: 1.0% → 37.3% → 63.8% of spend over weeks 1-3). [L112 24:16–25:30](../lessons/112-winning-ad-definitions-0-mo-1m-mo.md)
  - **DO** track **breakthrough hit rate**: "what really matters is breakthrough hit rate". [L110 17:43](../lessons/110-winning-ads-definition-part-1.md)
  - **DON'T** treat a Spend Winner as scale: "Spend winners usually don't allow you to scale." [L85 21:18](../lessons/085-the-3-0-evolve-growth-guide.md)
  - **DON'T** label or claim spend/ROAS outcomes for competitor ads; you can only infer "probably winning" from signals (longevity, shares). (derived; see Copywriting [Iteration outcomes](../../copywriting/concepts/iteration-outcomes.md))
- **See also:** [marksman-sniper-testing](marksman-sniper-testing.md) · [click-based-attribution](click-based-attribution.md) · [revenue-stage-focus](revenue-stage-focus.md) · [Scaling rules](../frameworks/scaling-rules.md) · [Campaign structure 3:2:2](../frameworks/campaign-structure-322.md) · [Learnings review](../frameworks/learnings-review.md) · [Rewrite losers and iterate](../frameworks/rewrite-losers-and-iterate.md) · [KPI thresholds](../libraries/kpi-thresholds.md) · Copywriting: [Iteration outcomes](../../copywriting/concepts/iteration-outcomes.md), [Iteration playbook](../../copywriting/frameworks/iteration-playbook.md)

## Definition
**Start with the Copywriting page** [Iteration outcomes](../../copywriting/concepts/iteration-outcomes.md) for the four classes and the per-label iteration lists. Start Here adds the data-driven, tiered version below ("IDGAF" definitions, doc last updated May 13th, 2026).

### The four variables (winning is a spectrum)
| Variable | Losing end | Winning end |
|---|---|---|
| KPI (CAC/ROAS) | Low/no | Super high |
| Spend | Low/no | "All of the spend" |
| Time | Runs 1 day | Runs 30+ days |
| Scale | Increases spend 0% | Increases spend 100%+ |
Source: [L110 05:33–09:38](../lessons/110-winning-ads-definition-part-1.md); [L112 doc "Mission"](../lessons/112-winning-ad-definitions-0-mo-1m-mo.md). L85 states three variables (spend, KPI, time). [L85 20:31](../lessons/085-the-3-0-evolve-growth-guide.md)

### Label definitions (week 1, CBO, initial campaign)

| 🎯 KPI Winner | < threshold | ≥ campaign ROAS | n/a | Leave on; own ABO ad set at what it was getting (e.g. $200-300/day); fix spend | [L112 06:39; 20:48](../lessons/112-winning-ad-definitions-0-mo-1m-mo.md); [L97 04:39–05:17](../lessons/097-how-to-set-up-abo-scaling-campaign.md) |

### Tier thresholds (sheet "Master Winning Ads Analysis"; small samples)
| Monthly ad spend | Measure share against | Breakthrough spend share, W1 (median; range) | Spoken guidance | Typical cumulative growth W0→W3 (median) | Reliability | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $0-100K | Initial campaign | 67.9% (28.5-92.2%) | "around 60 to 70%"; "30%… that's fine" on the low end; breakthrough should "basically double your account spend" | 199.6% | n=6 | [L112 00:00; 22:08–23:11](../lessons/112-winning-ad-definitions-0-mo-1m-mo.md) |
| $100K-250K | Initial campaign | 21.5% (1.0-71.2%) | "20% of spend… spend winner… grows my account by 13% or more… breakthrough" | 77.8% | n=3, "grain of salt" | [L112 01:27; 23:11–26:37](../lessons/112-winning-ad-definitions-0-mo-1m-mo.md) |
| $250K-500K | Initial campaign / account | 32.6% IC / 12.2% account | guesses "30 to 40%" and "15 to 30%" (conflict) | 39.5% IC | n=1, unusable | [L112 02:35; 26:37](../lessons/112-winning-ad-definitions-0-mo-1m-mo.md) |
| $500K+ | **Account** | 7.6% (1.0-59.8%) | "7.6% of total account spend"; "greater than 0% growth" | 78.3% | n=7 | [L112 02:35–04:03; 27:46–33:41](../lessons/112-winning-ad-definitions-0-mo-1m-mo.md) |

Rules for reading tiers: near the bottom of a tier expect values near the max, near the top expect the min [L112 16:51]; use medians, not averages [L112 18:15–19:24]; after a breakthrough moves you up a tier, reset the bar (e.g. expect 30-40% instead of 60%) [L112 16:51–18:15].

## Why it matters
- "Nobody has a clear answer on what makes a winning ad"; replies like "beats your KPI at scale" have no number. [L110 00:58–04:54](../lessons/110-winning-ads-definition-part-1.md)
- A 10-20% "hit rate" of spend/KPI winners with no scaling means you "need breakthroughs". [L110 17:43–18:34](../lessons/110-winning-ads-definition-part-1.md)
- CBO-only data avoids "collecting" purchases: of 176 ads that got no CBO spend and were forced into ABO, "I think one of them actually ended up quote unquote scaling" (instructor's sheet). [L110 15:13–16:59](../lessons/110-winning-ads-definition-part-1.md)
- The label picks the fix list and the budget route (ABO ad set vs zombie). [L110 20:09–22:26](../lessons/110-winning-ads-definition-part-1.md); [L97 02:53–05:17](../lessons/097-how-to-set-up-abo-scaling-campaign.md)
- "Winning ads per month is all we care about" (Shaun Eng, $300K-3M bucket). [L175 07:46](../lessons/175-what-to-focus-on-to-scale-to-10m-mth-by-rev.md)

## How to use (steps)
1. Confirm the ad ran 7 days in a CBO; pull click-based data in USD. [L112 04:03](../lessons/112-winning-ad-definitions-0-mo-1m-mo.md); [L113 01:21](../lessons/113-how-to-generate-submit-winning-ad-report.md)
2. Find the tier from **account** monthly ad spend at launch (weekly × 4.4). [L112 10:19–11:24](../lessons/112-winning-ad-definitions-0-mo-1m-mo.md)
3. Compute spend share: ad spend ÷ initial-campaign spend (tiers < $500K) or ÷ account spend ($500K+). [L112 05:13](../lessons/112-winning-ad-definitions-0-mo-1m-mo.md)
4. Compare ad ROAS with initial campaign ROAS. [L112 06:39; 13:33](../lessons/112-winning-ad-definitions-0-mo-1m-mo.md)
5. Check whether daily budget rose because of this ad, with no other outside event (promo, other winners). [L111 page "Mission" note](../lessons/111-winning-ads-definition-part-2.md)
6. Assign the label; write it as a sentence: "This is 100% a Breakthrough ad because in my spend category of $0-$100k/mo, after Week 1 the ad spent 50% of my campaign's budget and increased my total daily spend by 70%." [L110 18:34](../lessons/110-winning-ads-definition-part-1.md)
7. Route: Breakthrough/Spend/KPI Winner → own ABO ad set; Loser → zombie. [L97](../lessons/097-how-to-set-up-abo-scaling-campaign.md); [L93 02:47](../lessons/093-full-media-buying-strategy-outline-2026.md)
8. Pick the fix list (below); log in the Growth Guide Results column; do not mark Done until learnings are processed. [L85 21:54–22:52](../lessons/085-the-3-0-evolve-growth-guide.md)
9. Re-pull weeks 2 and 3; relabel. [L111 00:47–01:43](../lessons/111-winning-ads-definition-part-2.md)

### Fix list by label (doc section 4)
| Label | Likely cause | Fix | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🏆 Breakthrough | Crushing it | Iteration playbook: dissect components, aggressive iteration and variation | [L110 20:09](../lessons/110-winning-ads-definition-part-1.md) |
| 🎯 KPI Winner (spend issue) | Hook not stopping scroll; weak visuals/messaging; bad bridge (high hook, low hold); angle/labels too niche | More engaging hook; proper visuals; fix hook-to-hold bridge; broaden the label | [L110 20:09–21:01](../lessons/110-winning-ads-definition-part-1.md) |
| 💸 Spend Winner (KPI issue) | Misleading hook; colder audience without conversion factor; unclear CTA; claims not believed; price-value off; offer logistics; open loops not closed | Study price objections; clarify CTAs; build belief and urgency; close loops | [L110 21:01–21:44](../lessons/110-winning-ads-definition-part-1.md) |
| Any winner dying fast (time issue) | Too bottom-funnel; low awareness level; no emotion; fake-feeling AI story; competitor offer; seasonality | Expand avatar; raise awareness level; add emotion; refresh seasonal assets | [L110 21:44–22:26](../lessons/110-winning-ads-definition-part-1.md) |

## Examples (from the lessons)
- **KPI Winner:** campaign at 2x; ad pulls 5% of spend at 3x after 7 days. [L112 06:39](../lessons/112-winning-ad-definitions-0-mo-1m-mo.md)
- **Loser:** 3% of spend at 1.5x in a 2x campaign; or 1.4x at about 10% of spend in a 1.89x campaign. [L112 06:39; 19:24–20:48](../lessons/112-winning-ad-definitions-0-mo-1m-mo.md)
- **Week-1 Breakthrough:** launched Wednesday at $3K/day; 7 days later $5K/day, ad "sucked up like 50% of spend". [L111 00:47–01:43](../lessons/111-winning-ads-definition-part-2.md)
- **Loser → Breakthrough:** $500K+ Ad #4 at 2.6% of spend in week 1, 55.8% of initial-campaign spend in week 2 (doc says "rare"). [L111](../lessons/111-winning-ads-definition-part-2.md); [L112](../lessons/112-winning-ad-definitions-0-mo-1m-mo.md)
- **Mega-breakthrough:** $500K+ Ad #3 took 59.8% of account spend in week 1, account +265% WoW. [L112 27:46; 33:41](../lessons/112-winning-ad-definitions-0-mo-1m-mo.md)
- **Loser by share (L124):** a static concept at 2.17% of spend over 7 days: "the result for this ad is a loser." [L124 06:09–09:34](../lessons/124-how-to-do-learnings-part-3-losers.md)
- **"Super winner" = breakthrough** (older term): pulls spend, allows budget increase, raises ROAS. [L82 01:58](../lessons/082-the-3-testing-methods-part-2.md); the L130 learnings-call prompt uses "loser / winner / super winner". [L130 prompt doc](../lessons/130-how-to-rewrite-losing-ads-into-winners.md)

## Common mistakes
- **Labelling ABO or minimum-budget tests** with these percentages; forced spend "collects" rather than "generates" purchases. [L110 12:01–13:45](../lessons/110-winning-ads-definition-part-1.md)
- **Using revenue instead of ad spend** to pick the tier. [L112 01:27](../lessons/112-winning-ad-definitions-0-mo-1m-mo.md)
- **Judging a $500K+ ad on initial-campaign share** (Ad #6: 93% of its campaign, 7.4% of the account). [L112 27:46](../lessons/112-winning-ad-definitions-0-mo-1m-mo.md)
- **Locking the week-1 label.** [L111 00:00–01:43](../lessons/111-winning-ads-definition-part-2.md)
- **Turning off a winner after a bad couple of days** (logged in the daily Log). [L85 02:25–03:19](../lessons/085-the-3-0-evolve-growth-guide.md)
- **Forcing spend on other variants by switching off the top spender:** treat the concept as a loser and make a better one. [L124 10:48–11:41](../lessons/124-how-to-do-learnings-part-3-losers.md)
- **Trusting the thin tiers** ($100-250K n=3, $250-500K n=1) as hard benchmarks. [L112 01:27; 23:11](../lessons/112-winning-ad-definitions-0-mo-1m-mo.md)

## Cross-course notes
- **Evolve Copywriting (agrees, less precise):** same four labels, 7-day wait (2-3 days if plainly scaling), "majority of spend" bar from ~10% (big accounts) to 60-70% (small); per-label iteration lists. [Iteration outcomes](../../copywriting/concepts/iteration-outcomes.md). Start Here adds the CBO-only rule, ad-spend tiers with medians, account-level measurement at $500K+, the budget-rise test for breakthroughs and week 2-3 relabelling.
- **Evolve Copywriting (differs on hit rate):** the Growth Guide formula in Copywriting L22 counts **Breakthrough + Spend Winner** as hits ([Mini breakthrough memo and growth guide](../../copywriting/frameworks/mini-breakthrough-memo-and-growth-guide.md)); Start Here L110 makes **breakthrough hit rate** the north star and L85 says spend winners "usually don't allow you to scale". Default: report both; optimise breakthrough hit rate.
- **Evolve Copywriting (spend-winner kill):** Copywriting L50 turns off a spend winner at 0.2-0.3x ROAS; Start Here L112 says leave spend winners on, while the L110 doc allows turning one off when "KPI is much further below… breakthrough data". Default: keep unless click-based ROAS is far below breakeven.
- **Internal conflict:** spend-share denominator. L112 uses initial-campaign spend (account at $500K+); L124 uses total account spend with 10% / 30-50% bars. Default: use L112 for labels; L124's account share is a quick sanity check.
- **Tether Lab:** [Creative testing system](../../../../frameworks/creative-testing-system.md) judges winners on CPA/CVR and scales +10-15%/day; it has no spend-share labels. Use Evolve labels for Meta CBO tests.
