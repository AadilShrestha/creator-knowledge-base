# Core avatars and sub-avatars (the Core 5 method)
> An avatar is "a group of specific people that are most likely to buy your product"; Evolve builds one **core avatar** from a single category (99% of the time a surface-level desire), then many **sub-avatars** by stacking experience, emotion, behavior and (last) demographic on top, and each sub-avatar becomes an angle.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** defining who an ad, script or brief is for; converting research quotes (reviews, Reddit, comments) into targetable people; auditing an "avatar" that is really a demographic; deciding how many core avatars a brand should run at its revenue stage; reverse-engineering the avatar behind a competitor's winning angle.
- **Inputs → outputs:** product overview (features → benefits → assumed desires), research statements in customer words, brand monthly revenue → tagged atoms (desire / experience / emotion / behavior / demographic), one chosen core avatar line, 5-10 sub-avatar records (desire + 1-3 fields + label + draft headline), handed to [ad-definitions](ad-definitions.md) for angles.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** start from a desire, not a demographic: "You should always start your avatar creation process with the desire over a demographic." [L71 37:36](../lessons/071-the-evolve-avatar-training.md)
  - **DO** give every avatar a desire: "you should never have desireless avatars". [L76 00:42](../lessons/076-how-to-build-sub-avatars.md)
  - **DO** build the core avatar from exactly one category; add categories to make sub-avatars. "More categories = more specific." [L71 22:41–24:33; 30:05](../lessons/071-the-evolve-avatar-training.md)
  - **DO** use a surface-level desire ("I want my dog to stop pulling"), slightly broad, never a core desire (health, sex, status, belonging, control, comfort). [L71 34:38–37:36](../lessons/071-the-evolve-avatar-training.md); [L76 07:33–09:58](../lessons/076-how-to-build-sub-avatars.md)
  - **DO** pick a desire the product can deliver with a believable mechanism: "if your product can't help with sleep, it's not going to be a good desire". [L76 05:12–07:33](../lessons/076-how-to-build-sub-avatars.md)
  - **DON'T** combine more than one surface desire in one avatar. [L71 29:11](../lessons/071-the-evolve-avatar-training.md)
  - **DON'T** add demographics unless the product works better for that group. [L71 2:08:54–2:15:15](../lessons/071-the-evolve-avatar-training.md)
  - **IF** revenue is $0-100K/month **THEN** run one desire-based core avatar and "sub avatar, sub avatar, sub avatar" for 2-4 weeks before changing the desire. [L71 2:23:52–2:28:55](../lessons/071-the-evolve-avatar-training.md)
  - **IF** a commenter has no problem (sleeps great already) **THEN** log them as information, not an avatar. [L76 23:52–24:49](../lessons/076-how-to-build-sub-avatars.md)
  - **IF** a statement mixes categories ("hates living in a noisy apartment") **THEN** split it (experience + emotion) before using it. [L71 1:10:26–1:12:17](../lessons/071-the-evolve-avatar-training.md)
  - **DON'T** research for days: "you can maybe spend an hour going through all these threads". [L76 15:05](../lessons/076-how-to-build-sub-avatars.md)
  - **DON'T** invent avatar quotes. Every sub-avatar field must trace to a real research line or be marked "(assumed)", as the instructor does with "Not true, but I'm assuming…" [L76 25:41–27:35](../lessons/076-how-to-build-sub-avatars.md) (derived rule from repo policy).

## Definition
- **Avatar:** "a group of specific people that are most likely to buy your product." ICP, persona, target market: "it's the same shit". Goal: relatability, so the viewer thinks "yeah, that's me". [L71 01:32–03:31; 06:20](../lessons/071-the-evolve-avatar-training.md)
- **Core avatar:** the starting point, built from **one** of the five categories. **Sub-avatar:** the core avatar narrowed by one or more other categories. [L71 22:41–24:33](../lessons/071-the-evolve-avatar-training.md)
- **The Core 5 categories** (order matters; 3 and 4 can flip; demographics last) [L71 25:32–27:42; Doc](../lessons/071-the-evolve-avatar-training.md):

| # | Category | Definition (Doc) | Check | Example (pure) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Desire | "I want / I need" statements | "If you can't say that in a sentence, it's not a desire." | "I want to brush my teeth faster" [39:59–41:40] |
| 2 | Experience | Situations gone through (situational: active or past; product-based: tried X, outcome Y) | Circumstance or event? Emotion and desire removed? | "Just had a baby", "Tried Ozempic and it worked" [1:10:26] |
| 3 | Emotion | How people feel about what is happening | "I feel" test; if not one of joy, sadness, fear, disgust, anger, surprise, ask why and what | "Feels embarrassed walking the dog" [1:40:53] |
| 4 | Behavior | Regular action patterns that respond to the other categories | What the person does (can be mental)? How often? | "Meal preps every Sunday" [2:04:34] |
| 5 | Demographic | Static factual traits | Is it on the demographics list? | "60+", "works a night shift job" [2:20:02] |

- **Beliefs are not a sixth category.** Unpack a belief into experience (what happened), emotion (how it feels), behavior (what they do); keep the belief for hooks. [L71 2:21:00–2:23:52](../lessons/071-the-evolve-avatar-training.md); [L72 14:17–15:16](../lessons/072-evolve-avatar-training-ai-podcast-version.md)
- **The avatar scale:** "One Pepe" (a 34-year-old dentist in Tampa with a red Tesla) to "All Pepes" ("You have a heartbeat"). Both ends lose; aim "specific, but not alienating". [L71 18:48–22:41; 31:01](../lessons/071-the-evolve-avatar-training.md)

## Why it matters
- Meta targeting is "99 percent" creative (the instructor calls this an exaggeration); relevance to a specific avatar lowers costs (instructor claim). [L71 14:47–15:47](../lessons/071-the-evolve-avatar-training.md)
- Broad avatars are already "called out and sold to"; they only win with a new mechanism, new information or the best offer. Otherwise "you're in a race to the bottom with your offer". [L71 11:48–14:47](../lessons/071-the-evolve-avatar-training.md)
- "The more broad your avatar is, the better your product, offer, brand, and marketing skillset have to be." [L71 30:05–31:01](../lessons/071-the-evolve-avatar-training.md)
- "Your sub avatars create the angles… if the angles are relatable, you're going to have better angles." [L76 31:41](../lessons/076-how-to-build-sub-avatars.md) "When you're testing your angle, you're testing your sub avatar." [L79 13:43–14:32](../lessons/079-how-to-come-up-with-ad-angles.md)
- Stage-1 brands should "out-avatar" the biggest competitor, not "out-market" them: "you're not that good at marketing yet". [L71 2:23:52–2:28:55](../lessons/071-the-evolve-avatar-training.md) See [two-ways-to-win](two-ways-to-win.md).

## How to use (steps)
1. **List assumed desires** from the product overview: for each benefit ask "Why does someone want/need that benefit?" Rewrite each as "Wants X" / "Person wants to …". [L74 00:57; 03:11–04:10](../lessons/074-how-to-build-core-avatars.md)
2. **Prune.** Park desires that are mega-broad or not tied to what the product does ("confidence in their purchase", "flexibility and freedom"). Reclassify behaviors ("makes smart investments" → behavior field of a sub-avatar). Merge near-duplicates, but note wording reaches different people ("restorative sleep" for an exhausted mom vs "optimize your sleep routine" for a biohacker). [L74 02:33–09:48](../lessons/074-how-to-build-core-avatars.md)
3. **Score the 3 survivors** on scope, urgency, staying power and place them on the avatar scale. [L74 12:01–19:14](../lessons/074-how-to-build-core-avatars.md)
4. **Pick one by revenue stage** (table below) and check the ad library for desires competitors are not pushing. [L74 19:14–19:55](../lessons/074-how-to-build-core-avatars.md)
5. **Search each category separately** on Google/Reddit: "want more restorative sleep reddit" (desire), "how lack of sleep is making me feel Reddit" (emotion), "At what age does sleep start getting worse?" (demographic), "blackout room sleep Reddit" (product experience); read behaviors out of comments. About an hour. [L76 10:47–15:05](../lessons/076-how-to-build-sub-avatars.md)
6. **Write sub-avatar records** (template below): Desire + the 1-2 fields the quote supports + a label in customer lingo ("night shifters", "graveyard shift") + an arrow-prefixed headline draft. [L76 17:31–31:41](../lessons/076-how-to-build-sub-avatars.md) Desire + one field is enough. [L83 03:14–04:09](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md)
7. **Turn vivid customer lines into headlines now** ("9pm looks like it's the middle of the night" → "Turn 9 p.m. into 2 a.m."). [L74 45:45–46:39](../lessons/074-how-to-build-core-avatars.md)
8. **Hand off to angles**: desire → current behavior → gap → reason to buy. [L79 24:25–25:50](../lessons/079-how-to-come-up-with-ad-angles.md) See [ad-definitions](ad-definitions.md).
9. **Test sub-avatars 2-4 weeks** before switching the core desire; log which sub-avatar each winner belongs to. [L71 2:26:35](../lessons/071-the-evolve-avatar-training.md); [L81 00:00–00:46](../lessons/081-the-3-testing-methods-part-1.md)

### Revenue stage → avatar count and desire breadth
| Stage (monthly revenue) | Core avatars | Desire breadth (L74 blackout-blinds pick) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| $0-100K (up to ~250K) | 1, desire-based; many sub-avatars | Smallest, most specific ("sleep in a quiet environment") | [L71 2:23:52](../lessons/071-the-evolve-avatar-training.md); [L74 19:14](../lessons/074-how-to-build-core-avatars.md) |
| $100K-1M | 1 + specific sub-avatars; 2-3 total above ~$500K (L71) vs "a few" (L76) vs "1-2 avatars" to reach $1M/month (L171, Shaun Eng) vs "one and two" (L88) | Middle ("restorative sleep") | [L71 2:28:55–2:31:33](../lessons/071-the-evolve-avatar-training.md); [L76 13:14–14:08](../lessons/076-how-to-build-sub-avatars.md); [L171 35:45–36:16](../lessons/171-how-to-make-any-product-work-brand-building-pt-1.md); [L88 34:04](../lessons/088-the-execution-problem.md) |
| $1M-10M | Broader and/or 3-10 core avatars; needs volume plus new mechanism, information, identity | Broad ("enhance health") | [L71 2:31:33–2:34:09](../lessons/071-the-evolve-avatar-training.md); [L74 19:55](../lessons/074-how-to-build-core-avatars.md) |
| $10M+ | Everything in the Doc plus volume | n/a | [L71 2:34:09](../lessons/071-the-evolve-avatar-training.md) |

Agent default for the $100K-1M conflict: one core avatar until ~$500K/month, then at most two below $1M/month; add sub-avatars before adding core avatars (see [revenue-stage-focus](revenue-stage-focus.md)).

### Sub-avatar record template (from screen, L76 [31:40])
```
Desire-Based Core Avatar #1: <Wants X>
Note: ALL avatars need desires.

<Category>-Based
Sub Avatar #<n>: <short label>
Desire: <Wants X>
<Product Experience | Experience | Emotion | Behavior | Demographic>: <customer-worded fact>
Label: <customer lingo, optional>
→ <draft headline, optional>
Source: <Reddit/review link or "(assumed)">   ← added for this KB (derived)
```

### Call-out headline patterns (derived from L71 examples)
| Category called out | Pattern | Lesson example |
|---|---|---|
| Experience | "Do you [experience]?" | "Do your kids throw temper tantrums in public?" [L71 1:55:31] |
| Behavior | "If you [behavior], then…" / "Are you seriously still [behavior]?" | "Are you seriously still meal prepping every Sunday?" [L71 1:59:21] |
| Desire | "Do you want to [surface desire]?" (broad) | "Do you want to lose 20 pounds by summer?" [L71 1:58:32] |
| Behavior + emotion | "If you're [behavior] because you feel [emotion]…" | "If you're constantly ordering new skincare products because you feel insecure…" [L71 2:01:45] |
| Demographic + experience | "If you're [demo] and [experience], here's why…" | "if you're 60+ and you only sleep six hours, here's why…" (sketch) [L76 27:35] |

## Common mistakes
- **Demographic as avatar** ("Susan, 47, corporate, two kids"; "woman 35+ with knee pain"): "not actionable". [L71 06:20–08:42](../lessons/071-the-evolve-avatar-training.md); [L14 02:01](../lessons/014-rule-of-1-1-desire-avatar-message.md)
- **Core desire as core avatar** ("wants to be healthy"): too broad for a stage-1 brand. [L76 07:33–09:00](../lessons/076-how-to-build-sub-avatars.md)
- **Impure atoms**: experience lines carrying desire or emotion ("hates living in a noisy apartment"). [L71 1:10:26](../lessons/071-the-evolve-avatar-training.md)
- **Stopping at a secondary emotion** ("anxious", "motivated") without asking why and what. [L71 1:19:50; 1:41:54](../lessons/071-the-evolve-avatar-training.md)
- **Copying a big brand's current avatar** (Norse Organics "moms of 15-year-olds"): "do what they did when they were at your size". [L71 2:10:47–2:13:25](../lessons/071-the-evolve-avatar-training.md)
- **Avatar hopping**: "you get rewarded for going deeper"; one, maybe two avatars. [L88 34:04–34:48](../lessons/088-the-execution-problem.md)
- **Mixing avatars in one ad** (post-surgery woman vs golfer vs gardener). [L14 04:09–05:10](../lessons/014-rule-of-1-1-desire-avatar-message.md)
- **Trusting AI stats** about the avatar (Claude's REM-awareness percentages): "take AI with a grain of salt". [L74 29:47–31:38](../lessons/074-how-to-build-core-avatars.md)

## Cross-course notes
- **Evolve Copywriting (agrees):** desire-first avatars, demographics only if needed. [Avatars and angles](../../copywriting/concepts/avatars-and-angles.md). **Differs:** Copywriting's avatar card ends with **belief** ("They want [desire]. But they have the belief [belief]"); Start Here rejects belief as a category and unpacks it into experience/emotion/behavior [L71 2:21:00]. Default: keep belief out of the avatar record, put it in the hook brief ([Beliefs](../../copywriting/concepts/beliefs.md)).
- **Evolve Copywriting (adds a threshold):** go narrower if under ~$100-200K/month spend; widen a too-niche angle only at ~$3-10K/day [Copywriting L48 06:43–09:17](../../copywriting/lessons/48-how-to-iterate-on-losing-ads.md). Compatible with the Start Here stage table.
- **Tether Lab (differs):** [Self-targeting](../../../../concepts/self-targeting.md) targets the Actual/Ideal/Ought self "not avatars", arguing one customer is "hundreds of different avatars". Use both: Evolve picks *who* (desire + experience/behavior); Tether picks *which self* the ad speaks to. See [three selves](../../../../concepts/three-selves.md) and [angle rut](../../../../concepts/angle-rut.md) for when to rotate.
- **Tether Lab (agrees):** research-first customer language; see [Customer research to angles](../../../../playbooks/customer-research-to-angles.md).
- **Internal conflicts** (logged in contradictions notes): stage-2 core-avatar count (L71 vs L76 vs L88 vs L171); core desire as broad pick (L74 vs L76); desireless experience-only core avatars (L71 Disneyland vs L76 "never desireless"). Defaults given above.
