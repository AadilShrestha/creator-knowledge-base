# Market awareness levels
> The five Schwartz levels are covered in full on the [Evolve Copywriting market awareness page](../../copywriting/concepts/market-awareness.md); this page adds only what Start Here adds: the per-level goal/action sheet, the "change what you compare against to change the level" rule, the unaware test that catches AI mislabels, the humidifier awareness ladder, the awareness × landing-page matrix, and which level to start at by stage.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** labelling an ad (ours or a competitor's) by level; choosing where an ad enters and which page it sends to; writing the same idea at another level; diagnosing roller-coaster performance or a winner that died fast.
- **Inputs → outputs:** audience knowledge (problem? solution types? our product?), product, current ads and their pages → a level label per ad, the job and proof for that level, the page type to send traffic to, and sibling ideas one level up/down.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** pick the entry level before writing and carry the viewer through every later level to the offer. [L10 00:00; 16:54–17:52](../lessons/010-market-awareness.md)
  - **IF** you run an us-vs-them **THEN** set the comparison target to set the level: same product type = product aware; a different type of solution = solution aware. "You can actually climb a level of market awareness by changing what you're comparing to." [L10 07:16–08:48](../lessons/010-market-awareness.md)
  - **DON'T** label a line unaware if it names the problem ("The hidden reason you're exhausted" is problem aware). [L11 01:08–01:57](../lessons/011-understanding-unaware-awareness-levels.md)
  - **DO** audit every AI awareness label yourself: "You also can't blindly trust AI." [L11 00:00–00:37](../lessons/011-understanding-unaware-awareness-levels.md)
  - **DO** at problem aware, name the problem so specifically they say "YES THAT'S THE PROBLEM I'M HAVING", then test how long you stay in it. [L10 09:35–11:49](../lessons/010-market-awareness.md)
  - **DO** send unaware/problem-aware/education ads to an advertorial or listicle, and test at least two pages per batch (control LP + listicle or advertorial). [L121 23:20–25:50](../lessons/121-how-to-deal-with-roller-coaster-performance.md)
  - **DO** match each winning ad with a landing page at the same stage of awareness and angle. [L175 08:46–09:44](../lessons/175-what-to-focus-on-to-scale-to-10m-mth-by-rev.md)
  - **IF** most live ads are product/most aware in a sophisticated market **THEN** add unaware and problem-aware siblings that educate or entertain. [L121 12:50–15:36](../lessons/121-how-to-deal-with-roller-coaster-performance.md)
  - **IF** a winner dies fast **THEN** one listed fix is "try a higher awareness level (problem aware/unaware)". [L110 21:44–22:26](../lessons/110-winning-ads-definition-part-1.md)
  - **IF** the market already knows the category (self-cleaning litter boxes) **THEN** skip "why you need one" education and run product-aware comparison/price/ease ads. [L172 24:04–26:18](../lessons/172-live-product-research-w-kalodata-pt-2.md)
  - **DO** record the awareness level on every Growth Guide row. [L85 11:28](../lessons/085-the-3-0-evolve-growth-guide.md)
- **See also:** [Evolve Copywriting: market awareness](../../copywriting/concepts/market-awareness.md) · [Evolve Copywriting: unaware ads](../../copywriting/concepts/unaware-ads.md) · [Market sophistication levers](market-sophistication-levers.md) · [Campaign structure 3:2:2](../frameworks/campaign-structure-322.md) · [Tether Lab: landing page psychology audit](../../../../frameworks/landing-page-psychology-audit.md)

## Definition (Start Here wording)
"The level of understanding your customer has on your product" (or "on the product in general"); "market awareness is the target market you are going after within a specific desire." [L10 00:36–01:10; 17:52](../lessons/010-market-awareness.md)

### Per-level sheet (L10 slides; verbatim fields, condensed)
| Level | Running example (knee pain on stairs) | Goal | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Most aware | Waiting for Black Friday | "It's time to sell" | "Get out of the way, make a great offer, reduce risk & make it easy for them to purchase" |
| Product aware | Comparing knee braces | "Make your product shine & show more of how it solves the desire" | Build trust, benefits & features, future pacing (easy, effective, complete) |
| Solution aware | Knows surgery and braces exist | "Introduce your solution… as the superior solution" | Compare against *other solution types*; success stories, influencer reviews |
| Problem aware | "My knee really hurts on the stairs" | Show you understand the problem, then answer | Articulate the problem until "YES THAT'S THE PROBLEM", then the solution |
| Unaware | Stairs feel "kind of annoying" | "Educate the unaware prospect to become problem aware" | Call out the core emotion → the problem causing it → solution; or talk about people "like" the prospect |

[L10 01:10–16:54](../lessons/010-market-awareness.md). Shaun Eng's shorter version: unaware = stories, secrets, infotainment; problem aware = pain points, benefits of solving; solution aware = intro the product that offers the solution; product aware = proof it works; most aware = offers, promos, pricing. [L171 16:40 slide](../lessons/171-how-to-make-any-product-work-brand-building-pt-1.md)

### The unaware test (L11)
Ask of every "unaware" line: does it name or imply a problem the reader already feels? If yes, it is at least problem aware. True unaware lines sell curiosity, aspiration or "interesting information" anyone can watch. [L11 01:08–06:27](../lessons/011-understanding-unaware-awareness-levels.md)

## Why it matters
- Spencer uses awareness to judge every new idea before building it, and calls it "the cheat code to being able to scale a lot further" (instructor claim). [L10 00:00–00:36](../lessons/010-market-awareness.md)
- Roller-coaster formula: "product-aware ads + sophisticated market + trending desire = roller coaster"; product/most aware is the most competitive level. [L121 07:31](../lessons/121-how-to-deal-with-roller-coaster-performance.md)
- Page mismatch kills education ads: a viewer who clicks after 20-30 seconds lands on a PDP that "feels like just an ad". [L121 24:34](../lessons/121-how-to-deal-with-roller-coaster-performance.md)

## How to use it (steps)
1. Write the audience's knowledge in one line (problem? solution types? our product?). [L10 procedure](../lessons/010-market-awareness.md)
2. Choose the entry level (see the stage defaults and conflict below).
3. Look up the level's goal/action; draft one beat per later level up to the offer. [L10 16:54](../lessons/010-market-awareness.md)
4. For comparisons, pick the comparison target that matches the level. [L10 07:16](../lessons/010-market-awareness.md)
5. Run the unaware test on any top-of-funnel line, by hand. [L11](../lessons/011-understanding-unaware-awareness-levels.md)
6. Pick the page with the matrix below; queue a listicle/advertorial test next to the control LP for unaware/problem-aware ads. [L121 24:34–25:50](../lessons/121-how-to-deal-with-roller-coaster-performance.md)
7. Log the level in the Growth Guide; when a winner fatigues, write its sibling one level higher. [L85 11:28](../lessons/085-the-3-0-evolve-growth-guide.md); [L110 21:44](../lessons/110-winning-ads-definition-part-1.md)

### Humidifier ladder (one product, five levels; L121 slide 45)
| Level | Line |
|---|---|
| Most aware | "Save 20% On Humidifiers" |
| Product aware | "The world first stainless steel humidifier" |
| Solution aware | "Traditional Humidifier bread mold, ours eliminates it." |
| Problem aware | "Waking Up Tired? It's Not Your Mattress..." |
| Unaware | "You'll Never Guess What's Floating in Your Air Right Now" |

In summer (off season) the unaware line is the one "anybody can stop" for; "are you struggling to sleep?" gets scrolled by "90, 99% of people" (instructor claim). [L121 15:36–16:57](../lessons/121-how-to-deal-with-roller-coaster-performance.md)

## Common mistakes
- Two seconds of an "unaware-style hook" then straight into product/brand: the ad is product aware in practice. [L121 06:22–07:31](../lessons/121-how-to-deal-with-roller-coaster-performance.md)
- Dwelling in the problem: "I don't want to relive my problem." [L10 09:35–10:14](../lessons/010-market-awareness.md)
- Trusting the model's level labels (it mislabelled unaware twice in one demo). [L11 01:08–01:57](../lessons/011-understanding-unaware-awareness-levels.md)
- Sending education ads to a PDP or collection page. [L121 24:34](../lessons/121-how-to-deal-with-roller-coaster-performance.md)
- Using "market awareness" loosely for "who we sell to" (price tiers) as the older research doc does; that is not the Schwartz level. [L28 03:23](../lessons/028-amazon-research-part-1.md)
- Confusing awareness with sophistication; sophistication is not a test variable ("This is not market awareness"). See [market sophistication levers](market-sophistication-levers.md).

### Course conflicts and agent defaults
- **Where to start / how much unaware.** L10: unaware is hardest; most brands don't need it "until you're at some serious scale" [L10 13:28](../lessons/010-market-awareness.md). L11: "If you can create true unaware ads, you will be able to scale infinitely"; the unaware market is "the biggest piece of the pie" [L11 06:27–07:16](../lessons/011-understanding-unaware-awareness-levels.md). L121: climb up-market to unaware/problem aware to stop volatility [L121 12:50–15:36](../lessons/121-how-to-deal-with-roller-coaster-performance.md). L171 (Shaun Eng): beginners should use solution- or product-aware; "if you… feel like you need to do unaware to scale, I wouldn't do it at all"; "$800k day with product aware ads" (claim) [L171 15:40–16:42](../lessons/171-how-to-make-any-product-work-brand-building-pt-1.md). **Default:** new brands and products start at problem/solution/product aware; add unaware/problem-aware education as separate concepts (with an advertorial/listicle) once a product-aware winner exists or performance swings.
- **What "solution aware" means.** L10: compare against a different *type* of solution [L10 06:23–08:48](../lessons/010-market-awareness.md); L11: Spencer often places it "on the mechanism / angle" (ways to get energy) [L11 02:48–03:28](../lessons/011-understanding-unaware-awareness-levels.md). **Default:** label solution aware when the ad contrasts solution types or mechanisms, product aware when it contrasts brands of the same product type.
