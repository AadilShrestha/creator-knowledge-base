# The Evolve growth loop
> Every Evolve account runs one loop: Study → Research → Create → Test → Feedback Loop, then repeat; when winners stop appearing you escalate in a fixed order (more feedback loops → back to research → back to study) instead of touching media buying.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** an account or brand has no winners, a fatiguing winner, rising CPA, or a vague request ("get more winners"); before deciding what work to do next on any brand.
- **Inputs → outputs:** recent test history (what was made, what got spend, what learnings were written), research doc status, current offer/page → the loop stage that is failing + the next action (write learnings, re-research, re-study, or create more from a winner).
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** place every problem on the loop first: Study → Research → Create → Test → Feedback Loop. [L174 01:46](../lessons/174-im-overwhelmed-stuck-wat-do.md); [L1 15:33–16:27](../lessons/001-start-here-overview-of-evolve.md)
  - **DO** judge a test by spend: "Launch tests in 1 campaign. If it gets spend = good. If it does not get spend = not good." [L174 02:42](../lessons/174-im-overwhelmed-stuck-wat-do.md)
  - **IF** no winners (ads, pages, offers, products) **THEN** spend more time on feedback loops to find execution flaws. [L174 02:42–03:30](../lessons/174-im-overwhelmed-stuck-wat-do.md)
  - **IF** still no winners **THEN** go back to research: who the buyer is, what they want, whether better products or channels beat yours. [L174 03:30–05:07](../lessons/174-im-overwhelmed-stuck-wat-do.md)
  - **IF** still none "no matter what you do" **THEN** go back and study to find "the unknown" (offer, copywriting, awareness). [L174 05:07–06:51](../lessons/174-im-overwhelmed-stuck-wat-do.md)
  - **DON'T** run create → test → create → test with no written learnings: "A lot of you guys aren't doing feedback loops… That's your problem." [L174 04:17](../lessons/174-im-overwhelmed-stuck-wat-do.md)
  - **DON'T** blame media buying first: "for 99% of you guys, it's not media buying" (instructor claim). [L174 07:53](../lessons/174-im-overwhelmed-stuck-wat-do.md); [L1 11:46–12:37](../lessons/001-start-here-overview-of-evolve.md)
  - **IF** CPA rises and a winner fatigues **THEN** build three more winners from the same strategy instead of changing media buying. [L1 01:39–02:22](../lessons/001-start-here-overview-of-evolve.md)
  - **DO** log every creative with why you made it and who it targets; untracked volume is not a rep. [L175 03:48–04:49](../lessons/175-what-to-focus-on-to-scale-to-10m-mth-by-rev.md); [L171 06:17](../lessons/171-how-to-make-any-product-work-brand-building-pt-1.md)
  - **DO** prepare before creating: "The effort that you put in prior to making the ad is the difference between the ad usually winning and the ad flopping." [L56 01:18](../lessons/056-organizing-insights-overview-must-watch.md)
- **See also:** [Learnings review](../frameworks/learnings-review.md) · [Rewrite losers and iterate](../frameworks/rewrite-losers-and-iterate.md) · [Revenue-stage focus](revenue-stage-focus.md) · [Research doc](research-doc.md) · [Evolve Copywriting: ten-step iteration learnings](../../copywriting/frameworks/ten-step-iteration-learnings.md) · [Tether Lab: test and iterate](../../../../playbooks/test-and-iterate.md)

## Definition
Five steps, defined verbatim on the course's "Watch If Overwhelmed/Stuck" page and read out the same way in two lessons ([L1 frame 16:10](../lessons/001-start-here-overview-of-evolve.md); [L174 01:46–02:42](../lessons/174-im-overwhelmed-stuck-wat-do.md)):

| Step | Definition (verbatim) | What it produces in this repo |
|---|---|---|
| Study | "Learning new information to improve understanding & identify the unknown" | Re-read concept pages; outside sources allowed ([L174 07:53](../lessons/174-im-overwhelmed-stuck-wat-do.md)) |
| Research | "Figure out what people want and why they want it." | [Research doc](research-doc.md), [product overview](product-overview-and-mechanism.md), [desires](market-desire.md), avatars |
| Create | "Communicate via ads, pages, offers how you can help those people get what they want" (products also fit here, [L174 02:42](../lessons/174-im-overwhelmed-stuck-wat-do.md)) | Ads, landing pages, offers |
| Test | "Launch tests in 1 campaign. If it gets spend = good. If it does not get spend = not good." | Spend / no spend per concept ([campaign structure](../frameworks/campaign-structure-322.md)) |
| Feedback Loop | "Make educated guesses on why something is working or not working and keep track of those learnings." | Written learnings per batch ([learnings review](../frameworks/learnings-review.md)) |

"I could honestly summarize the entire program with just these five steps." [L174 01:46](../lessons/174-im-overwhelmed-stuck-wat-do.md)

## Why it matters
- Feedback loops are named as the scaling lever: "if you don't understand how to properly close feedback loops that is the reason why you cannot scale" (instructor claim). [L1 12:37–13:32](../lessons/001-start-here-overview-of-evolve.md)
- Hit-rate logic (Shaun Eng): start at maybe a 1% product hit rate; repeating the same thing keeps it at 1%; reviewing results lifts it to 2-4%, which is what makes testing affordable. [L171 06:17–08:13](../lessons/171-how-to-make-any-product-work-brand-building-pt-1.md)
- "Creating a winning ad just comes down to understanding why the previous winners did work and why your previous losers didn't work… Almost no one does it." [L175 11:39–12:37](../lessons/175-what-to-focus-on-to-scale-to-10m-mth-by-rev.md)
- Real case: during a roller-coaster month the team found 3 new winning ads, 1 offer and 1 landing page, and "spent extra time processing learnings"; an hour spent reviewing one losing ad produced ad #22, the new top spender (instructor claim). [L121 34:14](../lessons/121-how-to-deal-with-roller-coaster-performance.md)

## How to use it (steps)
1. **Name the stage.** Write one line: which step is the account in, and which step last produced a result? [L1 How to apply](../lessons/001-start-here-overview-of-evolve.md)
2. **Check the feedback loop exists.** For each tested batch: is there a written "why it worked / why it failed"? If not, write them before any new creative. [L174 04:17](../lessons/174-im-overwhelmed-stuck-wat-do.md); [L88 00:00–00:56](../lessons/088-the-execution-problem.md)
3. **Escalate in order** using the table below. Do not skip a rung.
4. **Feed learnings back.** Put learnings into the brand's AI project/knowledge base so the next scripts use them (the master project prompt asks for "actionable takeaways from winning and losing ads"). [L7](../lessons/007-evolve-master-prompt-doc.md)
5. **Repeat.** The loop's answer is "usually create more", sometimes research or study. [L174 01:46](../lessons/174-im-overwhelmed-stuck-wat-do.md)

### Escalation table
| Symptom | Likely failing step | Action | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Creating and testing, no logged learnings | Feedback loop skipped | Write learnings for every test; track them | [L174 04:17](../lessons/174-im-overwhelmed-stuck-wat-do.md) |
| Loops complete, still no winner | Research | Re-research buyer, desire, awareness, competing products, channel | [L174 03:30–05:07](../lessons/174-im-overwhelmed-stuck-wat-do.md) |
| Research and loops done, still stuck | Study (knowledge gap) | Re-study; test parts you never questioned (offer, copy, awareness) | [L174 05:07–08:49](../lessons/174-im-overwhelmed-stuck-wat-do.md) |
| Winner fatigues, CPA rising | Create | Three more winners from the same strategy | [L1 01:39–02:22](../lessons/001-start-here-overview-of-evolve.md) |
| Plateau at 5K–10K/day | Everything except media buying | Research, avatars, creative, editors | [L1 11:46–12:37](../lessons/001-start-here-overview-of-evolve.md) |
| Daily ROAS swings | Strategy (only controllable cause) | Permanent desires, higher awareness ads, congruent pages, better offers | [L121 06:22–15:36](../lessons/121-how-to-deal-with-roller-coaster-performance.md) |
| Hit rate falls as volume rises | Create (junk volume) | Cut volume to protect hit rate | [L175 06:47–07:46](../lessons/175-what-to-focus-on-to-scale-to-10m-mth-by-rev.md) |

## Examples
- **Account B (Spencer's team):** $4.9K/day at $600 CPA → batch 47 winner → $7.2K/day → batches 62-64 → $16.7K/day, lowest CPA in account history; fix was more winners, not media buying (instructor claim). [L1 00:59–02:22](../lessons/001-start-here-overview-of-evolve.md)
- **Broken offer:** the instructor could not get pages to convert, assumed pages/ads were at fault, then tested the offer, which "was completely broken"; fixing it worked. That is the "study" rung: a problem he did not know was a problem. [L174 06:00–06:51](../lessons/174-im-overwhelmed-stuck-wat-do.md)
- **Shaun's humidifier learnings:** "If you close this feedback loop, you can understand what to avoid next and you're going to save a lot more money and time." [L133 06:25](../lessons/133-winning-humidifier-ad-learnings-shaun.md)
- **Content engine:** when the creator system produces a winner, learn from it, do creative strategy off it, pass it back to creators: "that positive feedback loop… gets you to the million dollar plus months" (instructor claim). [L135 07:00](../lessons/135-why-content-is-so-important-part-2.md)

## Common mistakes
- Create → test → create → test with no learnings. [L174 04:17](../lessons/174-im-overwhelmed-stuck-wat-do.md)
- Treating a plateau as a media-buying problem, testing a new buying strategy, product or testing process mid-dip. [L121 35:19](../lessons/121-how-to-deal-with-roller-coaster-performance.md)
- A shallow research pass ("a couple of angles, then forget it") followed by "rinse and repeat" ad pumping. [L56 01:18](../lessons/056-organizing-insights-overview-must-watch.md)
- Ripping creatives or copy-pasting AI scripts as the "marketing": "That's not how business works." [L175 01:50](../lessons/175-what-to-focus-on-to-scale-to-10m-mth-by-rev.md)
- Arguing about method names instead of closing loops: customers "care about the ad that's in front of them". [L83 22:04–23:49](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md)
- Learning without ego removed: ask "if this ad didn't work, why does it suck?… where is that gap?" [L88 00:00–00:56](../lessons/088-the-execution-problem.md)
