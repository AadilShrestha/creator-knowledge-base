# Content engine
> A repeatable creator pipeline (framework → casting → seeding → production → cut three ways → performance review → contract and pay → profit review → feedback loop) that supplies a steady stream of raw ads to test. Evolve's claim: "If you nail this content engine and you know how to complete feedback loops, you can scale to millions" ([L135 02:41](../lessons/135-why-content-is-so-important-part-2.md)).

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** sizing how much raw content a brand needs; designing its creator supply; choosing content sources per clip type; reporting whether creator spend pays back.
- **Inputs → outputs:** weekly creative-test target, current content library, budget, revenue stage → creators needed, sources per clip type, a weekly flow of raw videos (each cut three ways), a monthly engine report (UGC spend → winners → spend on winners).
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** count **content** (raw files: a photo, a clip) separately from **creative** (the edited ad: photo + headline + logo). [L134 03:08; 04:52](../lessons/134-why-content-is-so-important-part-1.md)
  - **DO** size content from the test target: each piece "is only going to get you so many creatives before you run out of ways to manipulate it". [L134 03:08](../lessons/134-why-content-is-so-important-part-1.md)
  - **DO** run the engine for paid ads: "we're not doing this for organic. We're doing this for paid ads." [L135 06:23](../lessons/135-why-content-is-so-important-part-2.md)
  - **DO** cut every returned creator video **three ways** (three start points, no new text or music). [L135 01:44; 04:51](../lessons/135-why-content-is-so-important-part-2.md); [L98 02:44–03:36](../lessons/098-how-to-set-up-raw-content-campaign.md)
  - **DO** test raw creator content in the separate **raw content campaign**, not the main 3:2:2. [L98 00:00–01:46](../lessons/098-how-to-set-up-raw-content-campaign.md)
  - **DO** judge creators by spend, not taste: "good is something that gets spent. Bad is something that doesn't get spent." [L154 00:38](../lessons/154-how-to-contact-winning-creators-affiliate.md)
  - **DO** review the engine monthly or quarterly: "we spent this amount on UGC; how many winning ads did we get and how much were we able to spend on that?" [L135 05:26](../lessons/135-why-content-is-so-important-part-2.md)
  - **DO** feed winners back to creators: learn from the winning ad, then share what works. [L135 07:00](../lessons/135-why-content-is-so-important-part-2.md); [L160 00:46](../lessons/160-feedback-loops-with-your-brand-ambassadors.md)
  - **IF** revenue is $300k-$3M/month **THEN** optimise winning-ad rate over volume: "Winning ads per month is all we care about." [L175 06:47–07:46](../lessons/175-what-to-focus-on-to-scale-to-10m-mth-by-rev.md)
  - **DON'T** make paid-per-video UGC the main model; seed product and turn the best seeders into "mini creative strategists". [L175 09:44–11:39](../lessons/175-what-to-focus-on-to-scale-to-10m-mth-by-rev.md) (See Conflicts.)
  - **DON'T** rip other people's videos. The course places ripped ads in the raw campaign [L98 01:46](../lessons/098-how-to-set-up-raw-content-campaign.md): **course content, don't execute.** Compliant alternative: seed or pay creators, film yourself, or generate AI B-roll; copy the mechanism, not the asset.
- **See also:** [Insense creator pipeline](../frameworks/insense-creator-pipeline.md) · [Brand ambassador program](../frameworks/brand-ambassador-program.md) · [Creator frameworks vs scripts](creator-frameworks-vs-scripts.md) · [Whitelisting and partnership ads](whitelisting-and-partnership-ads.md) · [Campaign structure 3:2:2](../frameworks/campaign-structure-322.md) · [Revenue stage focus](revenue-stage-focus.md) · [Creator program setup](../playbooks/creator-program-setup.md)

## Why it matters
- **Buyers only see content.** "Your product is looked at through pixels"; people "cannot touch, cannot feel, cannot measure" it. [L134 04:00](../lessons/134-why-content-is-so-important-part-1.md)
- **Content serves every channel** [L134 04:52–06:48](../lessons/134-why-content-is-so-important-part-1.md):

| Job | Without it |
|---|---|
| Ads: capture attention, drive traffic | No ads, no traffic |
| Website: showcase the product | Low conversion rate |
| Emails: retain, build relationships | Low lifetime value |
| Organic: build an audience | No trust |
| Brand: resonate | No real business |

- **Volume claim.** From *$100M Leads* via slide: the top 0.1% of advertisers "test eleven times more creative than everyone else" (book claim, not verified). Instructor's reading: test 1 ad/week → aim for 11. [L134 02:11–03:08](../lessons/134-why-content-is-so-important-part-1.md)
- **Cost.** Strategist + copywriter + editor + filmer would cost "definitely more than 10K per month"; creators do it for "$100, $150, and a lot of them for free". [L135 00:53](../lessons/135-why-content-is-so-important-part-2.md)
- **Instructor claims (unverified):** a $25,000/day client runs 10 creators × 1-2 videos/week × 3 cuts ≈ "100 extra ads per month" [L135 01:44]; a brand at $150,000/day, 3x+ ROAS, "does zero creative strategy" and has thousands of incentivised creators (secondhand) [L135 02:41–03:19]; raw content hit rate 15% vs 5% for images and videos [L175 11:39](../lessons/175-what-to-focus-on-to-scale-to-10m-mth-by-rev.md); raw campaign spent $13.0K at 5.17 ROAS beside a $16.8K acquisition campaign at 6.01 over 14 days [L98 00:10 frame](../lessons/098-how-to-set-up-raw-content-campaign.md).

## How to use (steps)
1. **Pick the stage goal.** Under $300k/month: volume. $300k-$3M: intent and hit rate. $3M-$10M: systems. [L175 00:00](../lessons/175-what-to-focus-on-to-scale-to-10m-mth-by-rev.md) See [revenue stage focus](revenue-stage-focus.md).
2. **Size the engine** (derived arithmetic from [L135 01:44](../lessons/135-why-content-is-so-important-part-2.md)):
   - raw videos needed per week = target new raw ads per week ÷ 3 (cuts per video)
   - creators needed = raw videos per week ÷ 1.5 (1-2 videos per creator per week)
   - seeded creators deliver unevenly ("IT IS NOT GUARANTEED THAT YOU WILL RECEIVE CONTENT", [L138 page text](../lessons/138-ugc-content-overview-part-1.md)): hire more than the formula says (derived).
3. **Pick a source per clip type** (table below).
4. **Write the framework** and run casting through the [Insense creator pipeline](../frameworks/insense-creator-pipeline.md).
5. **Triage returns:** folder "✅ <first name> - DCT" if the video can be chopped into three variations as-is; X if not (upload anyway, tell the team). [L152 00:00–01:38](../lessons/152-how-to-follow-up-upload-content-part-2.md)
6. **Launch in the raw content campaign:** three cuts per ad set, or three different videos when content is plentiful [L98 02:44](../lessons/098-how-to-set-up-raw-content-campaign.md); the 2026 outline prefers flexible ads (3-6 visuals, 2 copies, 2 headlines, 1 landing page) [L93 02:47–03:29](../lessons/093-full-media-buying-strategy-outline-2026.md).
7. **Rank creators by spend** after the test window; contract winners ([brand ambassador program](../frameworks/brand-ambassador-program.md)).
8. **Report monthly** (template below); cut volume if the hit rate drops [L175 07:46](../lessons/175-what-to-focus-on-to-scale-to-10m-mth-by-rev.md).

## Examples
- **$25K/day client:** 10 creators, 1-2 videos/week each, cut three ways ≈ 100 extra ads/month, with only seeding, onboarding and feedback as overhead (instructor claim). Arithmetic check (derived): 40-80 videos × 3 = 120-240 cuts, so "100" is conservative. [L135 01:44](../lessons/135-why-content-is-so-important-part-2.md)
- **Seeding volume:** 74+ applications in about 48 hours for a client whose product was "worthy of filming for free". [L138 00:40](../lessons/138-ugc-content-overview-part-1.md)
- **Insense cost per video** (3-month maths, page): $500/month ÷ 10 videos = $50; ÷ 15 = $33.33; ÷ 20 = $25 (excludes product and shipping). [L140](../lessons/140-action-item-sign-up-for-insense.md)
- **Engine → ambassador:** one seeded creator's ad took $30,000 at 2x; once contracted, $40,000 at 3.36x in December (instructor claim). [L154 00:00; 06:52](../lessons/154-how-to-contact-winning-creators-affiliate.md)
- **Counter-example:** a humidifier brand "tested a bunch of different UGC but nothing really converted" for six months because "we didn't understand our customer well enough". [L133 00:51](../lessons/133-winning-humidifier-ad-learnings-shaun.md) Volume without research does not fix a strategy gap.

## Conflicts (logged in contradictions notes)
- **Volume vs win rate.** L134-135 push volume (11x; 100 extra ads/month). L133 [03:40] says don't spam test; L175 [06:47–07:46] says reduce volume if hit rate drops. **Agent default:** volume of *raw content* under $300k/month; from $300k/month track hit rate and cut volume when it falls.
- **Paying creators per video.** L135 [05:26] and L139: pay winners or paid-campaign creators $100-$150/video. L175 [10:41]: paying about $100/video is "not going to be sustainable" as the main model. **Agent default:** seeding is the base; per-video pay only for paid campaigns with budget, or for proven winners.
- **Creative strategy.** L135 [02:41]: install the engine before hiring creative strategists; a $150K/day brand does "zero creative strategy". L133/L175: creative strategy is research and customer understanding, not volume or ripping. **Agent default:** the engine supplies volume; research and learnings still write the framework and the feedback.

## Cross-course notes
- **Tether Lab (disagreement):** the [creative testing system](../../../../frameworks/creative-testing-system.md) is "precision over volume" (3-5 new ads a week; "Volume is evil until you have data"; volume works for 8-figure brands with production and analytics staff). Evolve's engine targets far more raw ads. Agent default: follow Evolve's stage logic (L175). Below $300k/month, high volume of raw creator cuts in the raw campaign is fine. Strategised ads in the main campaign stay within Tether-style batch sizes (derived).
- **Tether Lab (agreement):** [iteration vs variation](../../../../concepts/iteration-vs-variation.md) favours varying expression of a fixed message; "cut three ways" is that idea applied to raw footage.
- **Evolve Copywriting:** [iteration outcomes](../../copywriting/concepts/iteration-outcomes.md) gives the loser / spend winner / KPI winner / breakthrough labels used in the performance review.
