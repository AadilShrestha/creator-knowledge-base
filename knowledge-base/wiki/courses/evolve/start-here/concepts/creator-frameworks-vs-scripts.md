# Creator frameworks vs scripts
> Evolve gives UGC creators a **framework** (why it matters, do's and don'ts, specs, 3-9 concept ideas with one sample hook each, B-roll references, brand links), not a script and not a blank page: "Creators Get Frameworks / Actors Get Scripts" ([L148 page text](../lessons/148-sending-frameworks-to-creators-part-2.md)).

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** briefing a UGC creator (seeded, paid or ambassador); deciding how much to dictate; turning research, angles or viral references into creator concepts.
- **Inputs → outputs:** angles and avatar facts, product truth (what the product can be shown doing), the creator's existing content → a master framework (≤9 concepts), a per-creator copy (3-6 concepts), and a locked-claims list (derived).
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** sit in the middle of the scale: freestyle - **frameworks** - fully scripted. "Here you kind of want to be in the middle." [L137 03:34](../lessons/137-does-insense-still-work.md)
  - **DO** write each concept as a name, one sample hook ("literally just the hook") and a short paragraph on what to *show*, not what to say. [L148 05:07–05:59](../lessons/148-sending-frameworks-to-creators-part-2.md)
  - **DON'T** put more than nine concepts in a framework: "If you have more than nine total concepts, you have too many." [L148 06:43–07:29](../lessons/148-sending-frameworks-to-creators-part-2.md)
  - **DO** make a copy per creator and keep only the 3-6 concepts that fit that creator's existing content, because of "first pick bias": creators pick "the easiest to do or... one of the first two". [L137 00:58–02:10](../lessons/137-does-insense-still-work.md)
  - **DO** keep the mission to 2-4 lines: "Do not go in and write five paragraphs about your brand. Nobody cares." [L148 02:45–03:34](../lessons/148-sending-frameworks-to-creators-part-2.md)
  - **IF** a creator asks "what do I do?" **THEN** a script is allowed, but only if you understand the creator's style and can write for them. [L137 02:56](../lessons/137-does-insense-still-work.md)
  - **IF** scripted creator videos have not been working **THEN** "let the creators do some of their own ideas". [L137 03:34](../lessons/137-does-insense-still-work.md)
  - **DON'T** allow full freestyle "unless they're just a sensational creator". [L137 03:34–04:18](../lessons/137-does-insense-still-work.md)
  - **IF** the creator is an onboarded ambassador **THEN** send ideas in chat (concept title, framing question, avatar facts, objection to own), not scripts: "You didn't have to write a script." [L156 04:11](../lessons/156-how-to-onboard-brand-ambassadors.md); [L158 00:45](../lessons/158-how-to-communicate-with-brand-ambassadors.md)
  - **IF** a creator is less natural on camera **THEN** give "a lot more guidance". [L158 01:26](../lessons/158-how-to-communicate-with-brand-ambassadors.md)
  - **DO** lock every claim, number and result: the creator words the video, but may only state what is true for them and the product (derived from repo rules 2-3; same rule in Tether [creator brief](../../../../playbooks/creator-brief.md)).
- **See also:** [Insense creator pipeline](../frameworks/insense-creator-pipeline.md) · [Brand ambassador program](../frameworks/brand-ambassador-program.md) · [Content engine](content-engine.md) · [Creator program setup](../playbooks/creator-program-setup.md) · Copywriting [video ad script workflow](../../copywriting/frameworks/video-ad-script-workflow.md) · Tether [creator brief](../../../../playbooks/creator-brief.md) · [Templates library](../libraries/templates.md)

## Definition
| Mode | What the creator gets | When Evolve uses it | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Freestyle | Product only, "do your thing" | "Very rare"; only for a sensational creator | [L137 03:34–04:18](../lessons/137-does-insense-still-work.md) |
| **Framework** (default) | Six-section doc: mission + what's in it for you; do's and don'ts; video specs; hooks and ideas (≤9 concepts: name, sample hook, what to show); B-roll GIF references; brand links | Every hired creator, seeded or paid; sent after hiring | [L148 02:45–08:55](../lessons/148-sending-frameworks-to-creators-part-2.md) |
| Ideas in chat | Concept title, framing question, avatar facts, objection to own, sale dates | Onboarded ambassadors | [L156 03:21–04:11](../lessons/156-how-to-onboard-brand-ambassadors.md); [L158 00:45](../lessons/158-how-to-communicate-with-brand-ambassadors.md) |
| Fully scripted | Word-for-word lines | Only when the creator asks and you can write in their voice; "we haven't seen it work as much" | [L137 02:56–04:18](../lessons/137-does-insense-still-work.md) |

Not to be confused with "pulling frameworks from ads" ([L126](../lessons/126-how-to-pull-frameworks-from-ads.md)), which means abstracting a winning ad's structure "one level" up from its words. That output can feed a creator concept, but it is a different object.

## Why it matters
- **Authenticity.** "We are not telling them exactly what to do because we want the content creator to be authentic." [L148 00:00](../lessons/148-sending-frameworks-to-creators-part-2.md) Sunglasses analogy: offer three reasons to buy (logo flex, look, photo feature); the creator picks the one that resonates, or finds their own ("they're comfortable"). [L148 00:00–01:48](../lessons/148-sending-frameworks-to-creators-part-2.md)
- **Creators are the creative team.** A creator is "the strategist, the copywriter, the video editor, the person that films the content". [L135 00:53](../lessons/135-why-content-is-so-important-part-2.md) "The idea that you're going to come up with is not always the idea that the creator is going to come up with." [L148 01:48](../lessons/148-sending-frameworks-to-creators-part-2.md)
- **Scripts misfire.** Many scripts "don't make any sense for the creator". [L137 02:56](../lessons/137-does-insense-still-work.md)
- **Hit rate (instructor claim).** Raw creator content hit 15% vs 5% for images and videos "last year", because the UGC "was actually very authentic". [L175 11:39](../lessons/175-what-to-focus-on-to-scale-to-10m-mth-by-rev.md)

## How to use (steps)
1. **Collect concept inputs.** Angles and avatar facts from research; viral references from The Daily Virals (transcript + AI breakdown) turned into "concept lines", never copied lines. [L136 02:06; 05:07](../lessons/136-how-to-get-viral-ugc-inspo-for-creators-ads.md)
2. **Write the master framework** (template in [Insense creator pipeline](../frameworks/insense-creator-pipeline.md#creator-framework-template)): mission 2-4 lines; "what's in it for you" (only offers the brand will honour); standard do's/don'ts and specs (9:16, 30-60 s, vibe line); up to 9 concepts; 5-7 B-roll rows; product page and Instagram links. [L148 02:45–08:15](../lessons/148-sending-frameworks-to-creators-part-2.md)
3. **Write concepts as moments to show.** Each concept = name → sample hook → "Concept: show [moment], then reveal [product proof]". Two concepts may overlap in benefit; the count may not exceed nine. [L148 05:07–07:29](../lessons/148-sending-frameworks-to-creators-part-2.md)
4. **Split by audience** if needed: "[Brand] [Gender] UGC creator framework". Change the scenarios, keep the structure. [L148 09:46–10:24](../lessons/148-sending-frameworks-to-creators-part-2.md)
5. **Curate per creator**: look at their content, keep 3-6 concepts, delete the rest. [L137 01:36–02:10](../lessons/137-does-insense-still-work.md)
6. **Add the locked list** (derived): claims allowed, numbers allowed, words never to say, "only show what really happens on camera".
7. **Send after hiring.** [L148 08:15–08:55](../lessons/148-sending-frameworks-to-creators-part-2.md) If a creator asks what to make, reply with framework concepts or examples that "could do well". [L151 05:28](../lessons/151-how-to-follow-up-upload-content-part-1.md)
8. **Graduate to chat ideas** once the creator is an ambassador; feed each idea from performance data and ad comments. [L156 03:21–04:11](../lessons/156-how-to-onboard-brand-ambassadors.md)

## Examples
- **"Lost on the Roof"** (insulated cup, female version). Sample hook: "I literally left my cup on the roof and only noticed halfway through my drive-and it was still perfect!" Concept: hectic moment, cup on the car roof, it flies off, reveal it is unscathed and the coffee still hot. [L148 05:07–05:59](../lessons/148-sending-frameworks-to-creators-part-2.md) It was the first concept, so the one most creators picked: the first-pick-bias case. [L137 00:58](../lessons/137-does-insense-still-work.md) Mechanism to reuse: a relatable mishap that becomes a live product demo. Don't reuse the line.
- **Audience swap.** The male version keeps the structure and changes scenarios: "lost it on my truck", "I always forget about my coffee when I'm working construction", "I have to commute so far to the job site". [L148 10:24](../lessons/148-sending-frameworks-to-creators-part-2.md)
- **Ambassador ideas without a script** (hockey-gear brand): a sale video, a closeout-sale video, a reaction video to the founder's winning ad, all taken from the 30-day top-spend report. [L156 03:21–04:11](../lessons/156-how-to-onboard-brand-ambassadors.md)
- **Chat concept brief:** "is it actually worth it?" framing for an expensive product, plus avatar facts (family expenses add up, kids outgrow gear). The creator added her own supporting news headlines and chose to own the price objection. [L158 00:45–01:26](../lessons/158-how-to-communicate-with-brand-ambassadors.md)
- **B-roll references:** GIFs captured from reference videos, each with a one-line action ("Aesthetic shot of the coffee"), at least two rows. [L149 02:08–02:44](../lessons/149-how-to-make-b-rolls-for-frameworks.md) They are shot types to film, not footage to reuse.

## Common mistakes
| Mistake | Fix | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Same full framework to every creator | Per-creator copy, 3-6 concepts | [L137 00:58–02:10](../lessons/137-does-insense-still-work.md) |
| Ten or more concepts | Cap at nine | [L148 06:43](../lessons/148-sending-frameworks-to-creators-part-2.md) |
| Five-paragraph brand story | 2-4 line mission + "what's in it for you" | [L148 02:45–04:23](../lessons/148-sending-frameworks-to-creators-part-2.md) |
| Product-only "basic testimonial" | Show real daily challenges and the product in use | [L148 04:23–05:07](../lessons/148-sending-frameworks-to-creators-part-2.md) |
| Scripting a creator you don't know | Framework; script only on request and in their voice | [L137 02:56](../lessons/137-does-insense-still-work.md) |
| Sample hook read as a claim to repeat | Sample hooks are first-person scenarios; the creator must actually do the thing on camera. No invented results (derived) | repo rule 2 |
| Treating B-roll GIFs from other brands as assets | Shot types only; the creator films their own | [L149](../lessons/149-how-to-make-b-rolls-for-frameworks.md); repo rule 3 |
| Promising earnings the brand won't pay | "Change this however you want"; only real offers | [L148 03:34–04:23](../lessons/148-sending-frameworks-to-creators-part-2.md) |

## Cross-course notes
- **Evolve Copywriting (difference in object):** the [video ad script workflow](../../copywriting/frameworks/video-ad-script-workflow.md) writes full scripts (3 hooks/bridges, 1 hold, 1 CTA) for voiceover + B-roll ads built by editors. Its derived UGC adaptation locks hook words and claims and lets the creator say the rest in their own words. No conflict: scripts for editor-built ads, frameworks for creators (derived).
- **Tether Lab (partial disagreement):** the [creator brief](../../../../playbooks/creator-brief.md) starts from a finished script plus a shot list and tells the creator to say it "in their own words". Evolve sends no script, only concepts with a sample hook. Both forbid creators adding claims or results that are not true. Agent default: Evolve framework for creator sourcing and ambassadors; Tether script-in-own-words brief when one specific tested script must be re-shot (derived).
- **Tether Lab (agreement):** casting matters ([messenger effect](../../../../concepts/heuristics/messenger-effect.md)); Evolve's equivalent is curating concepts to the creator's existing content [L137 01:36](../lessons/137-does-insense-still-work.md).
