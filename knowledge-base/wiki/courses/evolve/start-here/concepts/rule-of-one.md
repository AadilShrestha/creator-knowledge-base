# Rule of one
> Every ad and landing page speaks to one avatar (defined by emotional pain, failed attempts, goals and daily habits, not demographics), one deep desire (found by asking "why"), and one message (an angle: emotional trigger + daily struggle or ideal outcome), kept congruent from hook to page, because specificity creates belief and belief converts.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** before writing any ad, script, brief or landing page; auditing an ad that "targets everyone"; checking ad-to-page congruence; deciding what to change after a test.
- **Inputs → outputs:** research on one customer type + product + landing page → one named avatar line, one deep desire, one angle, a hook that calls out avatar/problem/solution, and a congruence check (ad vs page).
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** make each ad or page appeal to "one avatar, one desire, and one message". "If you try to target everyone, you try to get no one." [L14 00:00–00:59](../lessons/014-rule-of-1-1-desire-avatar-message.md)
  - **DON'T** call a demographic an avatar ("Woman 35 plus who struggles with knee pain… is a demographic"). [L14 02:01](../lessons/014-rule-of-1-1-desire-avatar-message.md)
  - **DO** define the avatar by "emotional pains, past failed attempts, specific desired goals, and day-to-day habits". [L14 02:01–03:07](../lessons/014-rule-of-1-1-desire-avatar-message.md)
  - **DON'T** mix avatars in one video (post-surgery woman vs golfer vs gardener). [L14 04:09–05:10](../lessons/014-rule-of-1-1-desire-avatar-message.md); don't combine more than one surface desire in one avatar [L71 29:11](../lessons/071-the-evolve-avatar-training.md)
  - **DON'T** go too specific either (exact age, kids, degree): "We care about commonalities." [L14 05:10–06:07](../lessons/014-rule-of-1-1-desire-avatar-message.md)
  - **DO** make the hook call out the avatar, the problem, or the solution. [L14 07:09; 22:31](../lessons/014-rule-of-1-1-desire-avatar-message.md)
  - **DO** write the angle as "Emotional Trigger + [Daily Struggle OR Ideal Outcome]" (Skool correction). [L14 page text](../lessons/014-rule-of-1-1-desire-avatar-message.md)
  - **DON'T** treat a format (image, video, UGC, us-vs-them, before/after) as an angle. [L14 21:27](../lessons/014-rule-of-1-1-desire-avatar-message.md); [L79 02:00–05:27](../lessons/079-how-to-come-up-with-ad-angles.md)
  - **DO** show the same avatar and desire in every segment (True Classic shows women throughout, not only in the hook). [L14 14:41–15:48](../lessons/014-rule-of-1-1-desire-avatar-message.md)
  - **DO** send the ad to a page with the same avatar, desire, emotion, angle and stage of awareness: "Congruence Across Funnel = Zero Friction." [L14 30:09–32:17](../lessons/014-rule-of-1-1-desire-avatar-message.md); [L175 08:46–09:44](../lessons/175-what-to-focus-on-to-scale-to-10m-mth-by-rev.md)
  - **IF** avatar and desire win **THEN** test other angles around them; **IF** avatar works but desire doesn't **THEN** test desires; **IF** desire works but avatar doesn't **THEN** test avatars. [L14 29:03; 33:16](../lessons/014-rule-of-1-1-desire-avatar-message.md)
- **See also:** [Core and sub avatars](core-and-sub-avatars.md) · [Ad definitions](ad-definitions.md) · [Market desire](market-desire.md) · [Evolve Copywriting: avatars and angles](../../copywriting/concepts/avatars-and-angles.md) · [Tether Lab: one core emotion per ad](../../../../concepts/one-core-emotion-per-ad.md)

## Definition
Slide "The Rule of One" (verbatim): "One avatar, one desire and most importantly one message. Trying to speak to everyone by cramming in multiple features and promises only weakens your message. Confusion kills conversions… The more specific you can be to that one person's wants, the more it resonates with them. Which generates belief, and the stronger the belief, the higher the likelihood they CONVERT." [L14 00:00–00:59](../lessons/014-rule-of-1-1-desire-avatar-message.md)

| Element | Definition in the course | Test |
|---|---|---|
| Avatar | One person: emotional pain, failed attempts, desired goal, daily habits; identity lost (e.g. can't ice skate with friends) | Could two different people both see themselves? Then it's two avatars |
| Desire | The deep desire at the end of the Why Train (look better → … → feel desired and wanted again → to be loved) | Does every segment show it? |
| Message (angle) | "Emotional Trigger + [Daily Struggle OR Ideal Outcome]… your 'meeting point'"; for non-problem products (fashion) show what the avatar aspires to | Does the hook call out avatar, problem or solution? |
| Congruence | Same avatar, desire and emotion on the landing page | Would the avatar "see herself" on the page? |

[L14 02:01–32:17; page text](../lessons/014-rule-of-1-1-desire-avatar-message.md)

## Why it matters
- Goal: relate so well that the product "feels bespoke… tailor-made". [L14 00:59–02:01](../lessons/014-rule-of-1-1-desire-avatar-message.md)
- Broad targeting means low intent: "tens or hundreds of millions of people with the same problem" and "billions of different products"; "You stand out by being more specific." [L14 02:01–03:07](../lessons/014-rule-of-1-1-desire-avatar-message.md)
- Planning the variables first "makes learnings on the ads much easier since the variables are stated beforehand." [L14 32:17–33:16](../lessons/014-rule-of-1-1-desire-avatar-message.md)
- Shaun Eng says this concept "has made the most winners in Evolve" (instructor claim). [L14 33:16](../lessons/014-rule-of-1-1-desire-avatar-message.md)
- "The larger the desire, the more specific you will likely need to go on an avatar." [L61 01:22–02:40](../lessons/061-understanding-applying-desire-part-1.md)

## How to use it (steps)
1. **Avatar line:** "[Name/label], who [emotional pain], [failed attempt], and now wants [goal] that fits [daily habit]." Use commonalities from research (many Reddit threads about waking at 3 am), not one-off details. [L14 03:07–06:07](../lessons/014-rule-of-1-1-desire-avatar-message.md)
2. **Desire:** write the surface want, ask "why" until a feeling. [L14 10:28–11:35](../lessons/014-rule-of-1-1-desire-avatar-message.md)
3. **Angle:** emotional trigger + daily struggle (problem products) or ideal outcome (aspirational products). [L14 page text](../lessons/014-rule-of-1-1-desire-avatar-message.md)
4. **Hook:** call out the avatar, problem or solution (pick one). [L14 22:31](../lessons/014-rule-of-1-1-desire-avatar-message.md)
5. **Body:** show the avatar's scenarios and the same desire in every beat; meet them at their current emotional state, then present the product as the new belief (Hawkins map logic). [L14 22:31–28:00](../lessons/014-rule-of-1-1-desire-avatar-message.md)
6. **Page:** same people, scenarios and language as the ad; for unaware/problem-aware ads, an advertorial or listicle. [L14 30:09](../lessons/014-rule-of-1-1-desire-avatar-message.md); [L121 24:34](../lessons/121-how-to-deal-with-roller-coaster-performance.md)
7. **Within an ad's hooks:** every hook variant must be congruent with its own visual ("Kids are unpredictable" needs kids on screen). [L67 07:27–08:07](../lessons/067-action-item-find-new-mechanisms.md)
8. **Test logic:** keep winning avatar + desire, vary the angle; then desire; then avatar. [L14 29:03–33:16](../lessons/014-rule-of-1-1-desire-avatar-message.md)

### Template (derived from the slides)
```
Avatar: <who>, <emotional pain>, <past failed attempt>, wants <goal> that fits <daily habit>
Deep desire: <surface want> → why → … → <feeling>
Angle: <emotional trigger> + <daily struggle | ideal outcome>
Hook calls out: <avatar | problem | solution>
Page: <page type>, same avatar / desire / emotion / awareness level
```

## Common mistakes
- Niching on paper only: "Don't tell me you've niched down until you've done this level of work." [L14 32:17](../lessons/014-rule-of-1-1-desire-avatar-message.md)
- Clickbait hooks that stop the wrong people: a stop at the hook "won't convert". [L14 28:00](../lessons/014-rule-of-1-1-desire-avatar-message.md)
- Cramming many features and promises into one ad (see promise piling in [identification and belief](market-identification-and-belief.md)).
- Calling a concept an angle ("problem-aware ads" — "of what problem?"). [L79 03:46](../lessons/079-how-to-come-up-with-ad-angles.md)
- Testing several angles without knowing which avatar each came from: "It is okay to test multiple angles, but only if you are aware of them." [L82 19:48](../lessons/082-the-3-testing-methods-part-2.md)
- Pivoting off a working core avatar: "you probably lost your focus on the avatar that was working". [L71 2:28:55–2:31:33](../lessons/071-the-evolve-avatar-training.md)

### Course conflict and agent default
- **Angle definition.** Spoken/slide: angle = "dominant emotion + daily struggle" [L14 22:31](../lessons/014-rule-of-1-1-desire-avatar-message.md); the Skool correction replaces it with "Emotional Trigger + [Daily Struggle OR Ideal Outcome]" so aspirational products (fashion) fit [L14 page text](../lessons/014-rule-of-1-1-desire-avatar-message.md). L79 adds the planning definition "how you choose to sell the product and it must always be defined" [L79 00:31](../lessons/079-how-to-come-up-with-ad-angles.md). **Default:** use the corrected formula to *write* the angle and the L79 sentence to *name* it in the tracker.
