# Market sophistication and its three levers
> Sophistication is how many similar claims and products the market has already seen and tried; almost every e-commerce market is stage 3-5, so a plain or bigger claim no longer lands and you must pick a lever: a **new mechanism** (new hope), **new information** (authority), or a **new identity** (relatability, which for most brands means an underserved avatar).

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** a product has a real desire and a clear avatar but ads won't scale; before writing any claim; labelling a competitor ad; choosing the strategic lever for a batch; reading a category's competitor board.
- **Inputs → outputs:** product mechanisms, problems solved, every competitor and substitute-solution claim (ideally an ad board) → a band (1-2 or 3-5), the claims already used, and one chosen lever with its finder prompt and verification plan.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** assume stage 3, 4 or 5: "Just know that you're in a sophisticated market and that's the TLDR." [L65 10:43](../lessons/065-understanding-market-sophistication-part-1.md)
  - **DON'T** launch a claim without checking who else makes it: seven competitors all claimed "fast hair drying without heat damage", so the ads "just failed". [L12 17:57–18:47](../lessons/012-market-sophistication.md)
  - **DON'T** rely on "bigger, better, faster" claims; they "don't really resonate like they used to". [L65 12:20](../lessons/065-understanding-market-sophistication-part-1.md)
  - **DON'T** A/B test sophistication stages: "You do not test different levels of sophistication. Your sophistication just exists." [L65 17:47](../lessons/065-understanding-market-sophistication-part-1.md)
  - **DO** pick at least one lever; default to new information or new mechanism: "99% of you should only be focusing on new information and or… new mechanisms." [L70 00:00](../lessons/070-how-to-find-apply-new-identities-part-4.md)
  - **DO** explain a mechanism in plain words ("a thousand songs in your pocket"). [L65 22:38–24:29](../lessons/065-understanding-market-sophistication-part-1.md)
  - **DO** fact-check every piece of new information at its source; "Go and double check that Claude did not hallucinate." [L68 01:30; 21:00](../lessons/068-how-to-find-apply-new-information-part-3.md)
  - **DON'T** ship low-valence, high-intensity fear hooks ("brain health emergency"); rewrite them lower-intensity. [L68 13:00–17:54](../lessons/068-how-to-find-apply-new-information-part-3.md)
  - **DO** test a new lever as a new concept with three fully congruent hooks (bigger swings), not one-variable tweaks. [L67 05:49–08:50](../lessons/067-action-item-find-new-mechanisms.md)
  - **IF** you cannot find a new mechanism or an underserved avatar for a product **THEN** don't run it. [L171 17:13](../lessons/171-how-to-make-any-product-work-brand-building-pt-1.md); [L172 23:00](../lessons/172-live-product-research-w-kalodata-pt-2.md)
- **See also:** [Product overview and mechanism](product-overview-and-mechanism.md) · [Two ways to win](two-ways-to-win.md) · [Market identification and belief](market-identification-and-belief.md) · [Evolve Copywriting: unique mechanism](../../copywriting/concepts/unique-mechanism.md) · [Evolve Copywriting: positioning](../../copywriting/concepts/positioning.md)

## Definition
- "How many similar products has the market been told about before? How many claims have they been told? And how many of them have actually tried those products." [L12 04:13–04:45](../lessons/012-market-sophistication.md) TL;DR slide: "Market Sophistication is the market's overall experience & exposure to other solutions & products before you." [L12 17:20](../lessons/012-market-sophistication.md)
- "If you've ever heard the word saturated, that's essentially what market sophistication is." [L65 00:00](../lessons/065-understanding-market-sophistication-part-1.md)
- It is not awareness: "This is not market awareness." [L65 17:47](../lessons/065-understanding-market-sophistication-part-1.md)

### The five stages (two course versions)
| Stage | L12 slide version (move) | L65 doc version (state) | Example used |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | State the claim simply; prove it | First to Market: "simple benefit statements suffice" | BlackBerry pager; first gas station |
| 2 | Enlarge the claim; one-up competitors | Competition Emerges: "benefit amplification" | Nokia "Connecting People"; second gas station |
| 3 | New mechanism: same claim, new HOW | Market Saturation: skeptical; need a new mechanism | iPhone touchscreen; Purple; Ozempic |
| 4 | Enlarge the mechanism: easier, faster, better | Mechanism Competition: "easier, faster, surer" | iPhone 3G "twice as fast, half the price"; CarePod "zero filters" |
| 5 | Identification: show who they become | Complete Sophistication: "actively resist all claims" → identity, emotion, brand | Nike, Rolex |

[L12 05:16–13:23](../lessons/012-market-sophistication.md); [L65 05:39–10:43](../lessons/065-understanding-market-sophistication-part-1.md). Circle-back rule: a new mechanism that really works returns a mature market to stage 3 (Ozempic in weight loss). [L65 08:13–09:01](../lessons/065-understanding-market-sophistication-part-1.md)

### The three levers (L65 "Big 3 Recapped", verbatim headings)
| Lever | What it does | Delivers | Who can use it | Finder | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| New Mechanism | "Provides new HOPE… the claim will be delivered through new technology"; "Not another supplement, but the first one that…"; technology, process or ingredient angle | Hope | Needs a real product difference (ingredient, process, delivery form, or a feature you never talked about) | [L66 New Mechanism Finder Prompt](../lessons/066-how-to-find-apply-new-mechanisms-part-2.md) | [L65 18:39–25:00](../lessons/065-understanding-market-sophistication-part-1.md) |
| New Information | "Makes users aware of problems that they were previously unaware of and/or helps them better understand why previous mechanisms failed"; reframe the problem; reveal hidden obstacles | Authority, reciprocity, belief | "Anybody can do" it | [L68 New Information Finder Prompt](../lessons/068-how-to-find-apply-new-information-part-3.md) | [L65 25:00–31:18](../lessons/065-understanding-market-sophistication-part-1.md) |
| New Identity | "Builds relatability… buy-in for what the product represents"; "Not another [category], but for people who…" | Relatability, premium | Brand-level: experienced builders only; course version = underserved avatar | [L71 avatar training](../lessons/071-the-evolve-avatar-training.md) | [L65 31:18–34:08](../lessons/065-understanding-market-sophistication-part-1.md); [L70 04:31](../lessons/070-how-to-find-apply-new-identities-part-4.md) |

"New Mechanisms = build belief; New Information = build belief; New Identities (avatars) = build belief." [L56 doc section 4](../lessons/056-organizing-insights-overview-must-watch.md)

## Why it matters
- Even with a perfect avatar, clear desire and a product that works, ads fail when the market has heard the claim "like a hundred times": "Come and get it. No one believes you." [L65 13:47–14:32](../lessons/065-understanding-market-sophistication-part-1.md)
- Markets now sophisticate in months, not years, because reviews, Reddit and TikTok spread experiences; early winners decay when word spreads (instructor claim). [L65 01:19–04:54](../lessons/065-understanding-market-sophistication-part-1.md)
- Bad marketers can "get rich quick" with an early market or new mechanism; that is not durable skill. [L65 16:55](../lessons/065-understanding-market-sophistication-part-1.md)
- A found mechanism changed a brand's results: the agency took two years to crack CarePod's stainless-steel mechanism, which then "led to multiple winners" (instructor claim). [L66 03:13–04:55](../lessons/066-how-to-find-apply-new-mechanisms-part-2.md)

## How to use it (steps)
1. **List** your product's mechanisms and the problems each solves. [L12 13:23–14:16](../lessons/012-market-sophistication.md)
2. **Board** every competitor *and* every substitute solution (gym, trainer, liposuction for weight loss) in one ad board; look for the "black shirt" that stands out. [L12 14:16–17:20](../lessons/012-market-sophistication.md)
3. **Tally claims** on the board; mark any claim you planned that already appears. [L12 17:57](../lessons/012-market-sophistication.md)
4. **Band it:** 1-2 (rare) or 3-5 (default). Don't spend time on 3 vs 4. [L65 10:43](../lessons/065-understanding-market-sophistication-part-1.md)
5. **Pick the lever** in this order (Spencer's accessibility order): new information (anyone) → new mechanism (needs a product difference, can be an ingredient, process or unspoken feature) → identity via a specific underserved avatar. [L65 35:44](../lessons/065-understanding-market-sophistication-part-1.md); [L70 00:00–04:31](../lessons/070-how-to-find-apply-new-identities-part-4.md)
6. **Run the finder prompt** in a clean chat with the Market Sophistication doc attached; treat output as candidates. [L66 00:00–01:39; 07:10](../lessons/066-how-to-find-apply-new-mechanisms-part-2.md); [L69](../lessons/069-action-item-find-new-information.md)
7. **Verify and screen:** source and date for every finding; valence/intensity check; the "would you run this to your mom or your sister?" test. [L68 18:38](../lessons/068-how-to-find-apply-new-information-part-3.md)
8. **Stack** where possible: mechanisms usually need new information to explain "why", then an identity to attach to (Liquid Death, Purple, HexClad). [L65 36:32–37:11](../lessons/065-understanding-market-sophistication-part-1.md)
9. **Test** as a new concept with three congruent hooks; iterate single variables only on the winner. [L67 04:13–09:35](../lessons/067-action-item-find-new-mechanisms.md)

## Common mistakes
- Over-technical mechanisms, false uniqueness for industry-standard processes, mechanisms not tied to customer value (doc list). [L65 doc](../lessons/065-understanding-market-sophistication-part-1.md)
- Surface-level education ("your shoulder hurts because you're old"). [L65 29:30](../lessons/065-understanding-market-sophistication-part-1.md)
- Testing one variable (messaging only, imagery only) on an unproven mechanism concept: incongruent ads, too-granular data. [L67 01:56–03:23](../lessons/067-action-item-find-new-mechanisms.md)
- Copying an identity brand's tone without the brand-building skill. [L70 03:39](../lessons/070-how-to-find-apply-new-identities-part-4.md)
- Picking a "control" desire in a market that has heard "take control" endlessly. [L63 40:52](../lessons/063-how-to-find-desires-part-3.md)
- Quoting AI-found statistics without reading the study (149% vs a 1.49 hazard ratio). [L68 13:00](../lessons/068-how-to-find-apply-new-information-part-3.md)

### Course conflicts and agent defaults
- **Does a new mechanism reset the market to stage 1?** L171 video says the Shilajit gummies, Stanley and True Classic moves reset sophistication "all the way to level one" [L171 21:22; 28:36](../lessons/171-how-to-make-any-product-work-brand-building-pt-1.md); the L171 page correction and L65 say a new mechanism for the same product sits at stage 3 unless framed as something entirely different (or aimed at an avatar nobody addresses) [L171 page text](../lessons/171-how-to-make-any-product-work-brand-building-pt-1.md); [L65 08:13](../lessons/065-understanding-market-sophistication-part-1.md). **Default:** treat it as stage 3; still prove it and explain why it beats old mechanisms.
- **Stage mapping inside the new-information prompt.** The L68 prompt says stage 4 needs "new discoveries, breakthrough research" and stage 5 "complete repositioning or identification of new markets" [L68 prompt](../lessons/068-how-to-find-apply-new-information-part-3.md), while L12/L65 put mechanism enlargement at stage 4 and identity at stage 5. **Default:** use the L65 stage table for labelling; read the prompt's stage lines only as instructions to the model.
- **How precisely to label the stage.** L13 drills choosing stage 3 vs 4 per ad [L13 03:10–04:45](../lessons/013-action-item-practicing-the-core-3.md); L65 says don't bother [L65 10:43](../lessons/065-understanding-market-sophistication-part-1.md). **Default:** in teardowns record the band and the claim/mechanism the ad uses; skip exact-stage debates.
- **Identity: lever for all, or skip?** See [market identification and belief](market-identification-and-belief.md).
