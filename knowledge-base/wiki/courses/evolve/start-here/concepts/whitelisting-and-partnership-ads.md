# Whitelisting and partnership ads
> Two ways to run a creator's video under the creator's name on Meta: **whitelisting** (the creator's Facebook Page and Instagram account are the ad identity) and **partnership ads** (brand page + creator, "Partnership ad" toggle). As of January 2026 Evolve runs partnership ads "more than whitelisting" and asks new creators for 30-day access up front ([L165](../lessons/165-partnership-ads-updated-strategy.md)).

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** deciding how to run creator content on Meta; drafting the access ask or agreement clause; choosing the campaign for creator-identity ads; planning the risk if a creator revokes access.
- **Inputs → outputs:** the creator's status (new / winning / on retainer), account daily spend, existing campaigns → mode (partnership or whitelisting), access ask, fee, campaign placement, risk controls.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** test partnership ads first, especially with "high intent creators" and ambassadors' top creatives; leave identity on **Dynamic identity**. [L165 00:00–00:32; 02:10](../lessons/165-partnership-ads-updated-strategy.md)
  - **DO** ask new Insense creators for **30-day partnership access** at the start, promise removal after 30 days, frame it as raising their odds of a retainer, and send a screenshot of how it looks. [L165 01:29–02:10](../lessons/165-partnership-ads-updated-strategy.md)
  - **DO** whitelist only creators "we bring onto retainer who have WINNING ads". [L162 03:11; page text #1](../lessons/162-whitelisting-overview-proof-it-works.md)
  - **DO** pay $100-$250+ per month for whitelisting access ("anything more - not worth it"); refuse large asks such as $1,200 unless it is "an insane creator". [L162 03:46; page text #2](../lessons/162-whitelisting-overview-proof-it-works.md)
  - **DO** put access in a written agreement; consider a non-compete clause. [L162 06:57–07:34](../lessons/162-whitelisting-overview-proof-it-works.md) The "% Ad Spend + Whitelisting" contract variant adds Page and Instagram access. [L156 contract](../lessons/156-how-to-onboard-brand-ambassadors.md)
  - **DON'T** rely on whitelisting alone: "you can get cooked if the creator removes access". [L162 05:58; page text #3](../lessons/162-whitelisting-overview-proof-it-works.md)
  - **DON'T** run ads on a creator's page that the creator did not make: "super shady". "We only run the creator's specific ads on the creator's profile." [L162 05:22](../lessons/162-whitelisting-overview-proof-it-works.md)
  - **DON'T** use whitelisting to find winners: "we are not trying to find winning ads by whitelisting creatives". [L162 04:24](../lessons/162-whitelisting-overview-proof-it-works.md) (Partnership access is asked from new creators too, [L165 02:10](../lessons/165-partnership-ads-updated-strategy.md); see Conflicts.)
  - **IF** account spend is **under $2k/day** **THEN** put creator-identity ads in the main 3:2:2 or the raw content campaign; **IF over $2k/day** **THEN** a separate creator campaign, CBO preferred, ABO acceptable. [L164 page text; 00:00–01:33](../lessons/164-how-to-test-whitelisted-ads.md)
  - **DO** ask the creator to write the primary text in their own voice, using your top copy as a reference. [L163 04:08](../lessons/163-how-to-setup-whitelisting-creators.md) It must describe their real experience (derived, repo rule 2).
  - **DO** warn creators that comments land on their page; "if you are running some sus shit, they will see it and likely revoke access". [L162 07:34](../lessons/162-whitelisting-overview-proof-it-works.md)
- **See also:** [Brand ambassador program](../frameworks/brand-ambassador-program.md) · [Insense creator pipeline](../frameworks/insense-creator-pipeline.md) · [Campaign structure 3:2:2](../frameworks/campaign-structure-322.md) · [Content engine](content-engine.md) · [Creator program setup](../playbooks/creator-program-setup.md) · [KPI thresholds](../libraries/kpi-thresholds.md)

## Definition
| | Whitelisting | Partnership ad |
|---|---|---|
| What the viewer sees | The creator's Page/Instagram as sponsor; brand shop link below | Brand page + creator together |
| Meta setting (ad level) | Identity → creator's Facebook Page + Instagram account; "Partnership ad" toggle **Off** | "Partnership ad" toggle **On**, select brand page and creator; identity **Dynamic** |
| Access | Creator grants Page + IG access to your business portfolio (Leadsie custom request "Whitelisting") | Creator grants partnership access; Evolve asks for 30 days |
| Who gets asked | Winning creators on retainer | New Insense creators at the start, plus ambassadors |
| Fee | $0-$250/month access | Not stated |
| Evolve stance | Earlier: "we only run whitelisted ads now and we don't run partnership ads" | Jan 2026: "that has actually flipped"; partnership "more than whitelisting" |
| Source | [L162 00:00–01:28](../lessons/162-whitelisting-overview-proof-it-works.md); [L163 03:30](../lessons/163-how-to-setup-whitelisting-creators.md) | [L165 00:00–02:10](../lessons/165-partnership-ads-updated-strategy.md) |

Whitelisting can also use theme pages or other brands' pages; the course teases this but does not cover it. [L162 00:40; 08:22](../lessons/162-whitelisting-overview-proof-it-works.md)

## Why it matters
- **Reads as endorsement.** It is "camouflaged ... to the ultimate extent": a real person, then "Sponsored". "Clearly this person is willing to run ads from their own profile." [L162 00:40](../lessons/162-whitelisting-overview-proof-it-works.md)
- **Evidence shown (instructor data, one hockey-gear account, no controlled test):**

| Comparison | Spend | Purchases | CPA | ROAS | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Whitelisted ad set, 7 days (Mar 20-26, 2025) | $8,030.24 | 340 | $23.62 | "a lot higher" (spoken, not on screen) | [L162 01:40 frame](../lessons/162-whitelisting-overview-proof-it-works.md) |
| Non-whitelisted batch, same window | $12,377.25 | 463 | $26.73 | — | [L162 01:40 frame](../lessons/162-whitelisting-overview-proof-it-works.md) |
| Whitelisting campaign (ABO), 7 days (Mar 21-27, 2025) | $9,157.07 | 442 | $20.72 | 4.04 | [L164 04:30 frame](../lessons/164-how-to-test-whitelisted-ads.md) |
| Raw content campaign (CBO), same window | $27,824.10 | 949 | $29.32 | 2.39 | [L164 04:30 frame](../lessons/164-how-to-test-whitelisted-ads.md) |
| Counter-case: hoodie video from brand page vs whitelisted | brand page $5,461.41 purchase value at 3.74 ROAS; whitelisted $0.00 | — | — | — | [L162 02:12–02:30](../lessons/162-whitelisting-overview-proof-it-works.md) |

  The L164 campaigns hold different ads, so the ROAS gap is not a clean test (derived). The instructor: ABO is not the cause, "the whitelisting is working well" [L164 01:33](../lessons/164-how-to-test-whitelisted-ads.md); "this isn't ... going to save your ad account, but this actually can help nudge performance" [L162 02:12](../lessons/162-whitelisting-overview-proof-it-works.md).
- **Partnership ads:** "We are essentially seeing Meta favor the partnership ads way more" (instructor claim, no data shown). [L165 00:00–00:32](../lessons/165-partnership-ads-updated-strategy.md)
- **Costs:** fewer clicks to your own Instagram; comments, follows and profile visits go to the creator. [L162 00:40](../lessons/162-whitelisting-overview-proof-it-works.md); [L164 03:13](../lessons/164-how-to-test-whitelisted-ads.md)

## How to use (steps)
1. **Ask early for partnership access.** Add a whitelisting yes/no screening question to the Insense campaign (preferred answer Yes) [L143 07:36–08:19](../lessons/143-how-to-create-an-insense-campaign.md), and ask hired creators for 30-day partnership access with a screenshot [L165 01:29–02:10](../lessons/165-partnership-ads-updated-strategy.md).
2. **Run new creator ads as partnership ads** in the raw content campaign (segmented) or the main campaign, depending on volume; identity Dynamic. [L165 00:32; 02:10](../lessons/165-partnership-ads-updated-strategy.md)
3. **Remove access at day 30** unless the creator moves to a retainer. [L165 01:29](../lessons/165-partnership-ads-updated-strategy.md)
4. **For winning creators on retainer, add whitelisting.** Three ways to ask [L162 04:24](../lessons/162-whitelisting-overview-proof-it-works.md): in the original agreement ("we would love you to create ads for us. And we would also love to run ads from your profile"); a small raise if they say they charge extra; or after a good month ("your ads are doing really well. If you want better performance, would you be open to doing whitelisting with us?"). Reported fees: $0 or $100-$150/month.
5. **Get access with Leadsie:** sign up (14-day trial, then $99/month per page text), connect Meta, create a Custom Request with access level "Whitelisting", send the link; the creator clicks Connect Facebook → Confirm Access → Connected. If the creator has no Facebook Page, they create one first. [L163 00:59–02:44](../lessons/163-how-to-setup-whitelisting-creators.md)
6. **Build the ad:** swap identity to the creator's Page + Instagram; paste creator-written primary text. [L163 03:30–04:08](../lessons/163-how-to-setup-whitelisting-creators.md)
7. **Place by spend** (table below); keep a native-page twin of your best creator ad. [L162 05:58](../lessons/162-whitelisting-overview-proof-it-works.md)
8. **Log the fee** in the retainer sheet's "Whitelisting Fee" column. [L154 sheet](../lessons/154-how-to-contact-winning-creators-affiliate.md)

### Placement table
| Account daily spend | Where creator-identity ads go | Budget | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| < $2k/day | Main 3:2:2 or raw content campaign; optionally A/B native vs whitelisted | CBO as in that campaign | [L164 00:00–00:48](../lessons/164-how-to-test-whitelisted-ads.md) |
| > $2k/day (spoken: "two or three K") | Separate whitelisting/creator campaign, "almost like a second raw content campaign" | CBO preferred, ABO fine | [L164 01:33–03:59](../lessons/164-how-to-test-whitelisted-ads.md) |

| Partnership ads | Segmented raw content campaign or main campaign, by volume | — | [L165 00:32; 02:10](../lessons/165-partnership-ads-updated-strategy.md) |

## Examples
- **Creator-voiced copy** (hockey-gear brand): first-person story opening "Hockey moms, I don't have to describe that pit in your stomach feeling..." and continuing "My kids aren't in contact age yet but I can only imagine...". [L163 04:40 frame](../lessons/163-how-to-setup-whitelisting-creators.md) Mechanism: honest first-person framing that admits what the creator hasn't lived.
- **Pricing refusal:** a creator who had agreed later asked $1,200 after "research"; the instructor declined. [L162 03:46](../lessons/162-whitelisting-overview-proof-it-works.md)
- **Whitelisting campaign ad sets:** four ad sets at $1,152, $200, $200 and $50 daily; ROAS 3.70, 6.83, 5.48, 4.33 (instructor data). [L164 01:50 frame](../lessons/164-how-to-test-whitelisted-ads.md)

| Mistake | Fix | Source |

| Running another creator's or a brand ad on a creator's page | Only that creator's own ads | [L162 05:22](../lessons/162-whitelisting-overview-proof-it-works.md) |

| Ghost-writing first-person claims into creator copy | Creator writes it; claims must be true for them (derived) | repo rule 2 |

## Conflicts (logged in contradictions notes)
- **Whitelisting vs partnership.** L162-164 teach whitelisting as the method; L165 (Jan 2026) says the earlier "only whitelisted" stance "has actually flipped". **Agent default:** partnership ads first; whitelisting for trusted winning creators; A/B when spend allows.
- **Who to ask.** L162: whitelist only winning creators on retainer. L165: ask partnership access from new creators "not just with winning creators". **Agent default:** partnership (30-day) from everyone; whitelisting (ongoing Page access, fee) only for winners on retainer.
- **Top ad placement.** L162 [05:58]: run whitelisted ads in a separate campaign so a top CBO ad is not whitelisted. L164 [00:00–00:48]: under $2k/day put them in the main 3:2:2. **Agent default:** under $2k/day follow L164, but keep a native twin of each top ad.
- **Threshold.** L164 page $2k/day; audio "two or three K". **Agent default:** $2k.
