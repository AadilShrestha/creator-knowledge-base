# Insense creator pipeline
> Evolve's SOP for sourcing UGC on Insense: set up the brand, launch a product-seeding campaign, vet and shortlist applicants, confirm, hire, ship, send the framework, chase content on a fixed cadence, file it, mark it DCT-ready, close the deal. The goal is "to meet as many content creators as possible" and find one brand ambassador; a winning ad "is a bonus" ([L138 01:36–02:27](../lessons/138-ugc-content-overview-part-1.md)).

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** running creator sourcing on Insense (or any seeding marketplace) end to end; drafting campaign copy, screening questions or creator messages; scheduling follow-ups.
- **Inputs → outputs:** brand facts, product photos/URL/price, avatar demographics, master creator framework, budget → live campaign, vetted shortlist, hired creators with confirmed product-only terms, per-creator Drive folders marked ✅ DCT or X, completed deals, candidates for the [brand ambassador program](brand-ambassador-program.md).
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** check fit first. Insense "will absolutely crush" with a broad product-aware market, "even more if you have some kind of unique mechanism". [L137 04:18](../lessons/137-does-insense-still-work.md) With nothing unique, expect it to be harder. [L137 07:15](../lessons/137-does-insense-still-work.md)
  - **DO** buy the **brand plan**: the trial plan dropped product seeding. [L138 page text](../lessons/138-ugc-content-overview-part-1.md)
  - **DO** run it like a client job, sheet and all, even for your own brand. [L138 00:00–00:40](../lessons/138-ugc-content-overview-part-1.md)
  - **DO** set the seeding goal to **Awareness & Reach**, creators wanted "maybe 90 ... unlimited really". [L143 00:56](../lessons/143-how-to-create-an-insense-campaign.md)
  - **DO** keep seeded product value "probably at least $30" (Insense UI says $20+). [L143 03:37](../lessons/143-how-to-create-an-insense-campaign.md)
  - **DON'T** filter by followers/engagement when you don't require posting: "You really just need the content." [L143 04:32](../lessons/143-how-to-create-an-insense-campaign.md)
  - **DO** write screening questions with a preferred yes/no answer so you can filter later. [L143 05:29–07:36](../lessons/143-how-to-create-an-insense-campaign.md)
  - **DON'T** hire at shortlist stage: "You don't hire them ... you just let them know that you're interested." [L145 01:40](../lessons/145-how-to-process-creators-add-to-shortlist.md)
  - **DO** make every hired creator confirm it is a "FREE CAMPAIGN" with "NO PAYMENT. ONLY PRODUCT" before shipping. [L147 page text](../lessons/147-how-to-hire-approved-creators-part-1.md)
  - **DO** let creators pick the product when there are several SKUs; forced products lead to "poor and unauthentic content". [L147 02:22–03:16](../lessons/147-how-to-hire-approved-creators-part-1.md)
  - **DO** send the framework after hiring, curated to 3-6 concepts per creator. [L148 08:15](../lessons/148-sending-frameworks-to-creators-part-2.md); [L137 01:36–02:10](../lessons/137-does-insense-still-work.md)
  - **DO** a daily Insense check (5-20 min) and follow up non-responders every 2-3 days. [L138 page text Rules #1-#2](../lessons/138-ugc-content-overview-part-1.md)
  - **DON'T** go "longer than seven days really without touching back with the creator". [L151 05:28](../lessons/151-how-to-follow-up-upload-content-part-1.md)
  - **DO** press Complete only after content is downloaded and uploaded. [L151 04:47](../lessons/151-how-to-follow-up-upload-content-part-1.md)
  - **DON'T** expect polish from free content: "this is free content, so you can't really expect it to be amazing". [L151 04:47](../lessons/151-how-to-follow-up-upload-content-part-1.md)
  - **DON'T** store creator names, emails, handles, phone numbers or addresses in the knowledge base; keep them in the private client sheet (repo rule).

## Procedure

|---|---|---|
| Market | Broad, product-aware; mechanism helps | [L137 04:18; 07:15](../lessons/137-does-insense-still-work.md) |

| Very niche product | Expect few applicants even when paying | [L139 03:18](../lessons/139-ugc-content-overview-part-2.md) |

### Step 1: Add the brand
1. Check the active-brand limit (demo: "5 of 6 active brands"); ask Insense to raise it if needed. [L141 00:00](../lessons/141-how-to-add-a-new-brand-to-insense.md)
2. Fields: logo (clean), brand name, site URL with tracking "filler" removed, closest category, description. [L141 00:00–00:57](../lessons/141-how-to-add-a-new-brand-to-insense.md)
3. Description: start from the brand's best existing copy, rewrite until it reads well, check spelling: "we're trying to sell ourselves here to the creators". [L141 00:57–02:40](../lessons/141-how-to-add-a-new-brand-to-insense.md) Only claims the brand can substantiate (derived).

Demo filters (postpartum/menopause collagen): US only ("tariffs" made Canada expensive), female, 25-65, Beauty and Care / Fashion and Style / Health and Wellness / Lifestyle, English. [L143 03:37–04:32](../lessons/143-how-to-create-an-insense-campaign.md)

**Screening-question template** (from the demo; replace topics):
```text
1. What specific issue would you be talking about in your video?            [Text]
2. Are you cool with talking about <SENSITIVE TOPIC> if needed?             [Yes/No, preferred: Yes]
3. Whitelisting may be required of you when we run your ad. Are you OK with this?   [Yes/No, preferred: Yes]  (on-screen wording cut off; completion derived)
4. Are you able to provide a selfie of you with the product that we can feature on our website?   [Yes/No, preferred: Yes]
```
Seeding cannot require deliverables such as a selfie, so ask for them as yes/no questions. [L143 10:10–11:14](../lessons/143-how-to-create-an-insense-campaign.md) In 2026, also ask for 30-day partnership access ([L165 01:29](../lessons/165-partnership-ads-updated-strategy.md); see [whitelisting and partnership ads](../concepts/whitelisting-and-partnership-ads.md)).

### Step 3: Vet and shortlist
1. Open the brand's Instagram and site first, to judge fit. [L145 00:00](../lessons/145-how-to-process-creators-add-to-shortlist.md)
2. For each applicant, read the pitch, then their social profile, then their Insense profile (content, reviews). [L145 00:00–06:11](../lessons/145-how-to-process-creators-add-to-shortlist.md)

| Signal | Decision | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Customised pitch with a concrete idea | Strong yes | [L145 01:40–03:24](../lessons/145-how-to-process-creators-add-to-shortlist.md) |
| Content and life stage fit the product | Yes | [L145 02:35–03:24](../lessons/145-how-to-process-creators-add-to-shortlist.md) |
| Unsure | Pass: "the creator's not going to go anywhere" | [L145 02:35](../lessons/145-how-to-process-creators-add-to-shortlist.md) |
| Profile won't load / thin content | Ask for their Instagram or TikTok link | [L145 04:11–05:17](../lessons/145-how-to-process-creators-add-to-shortlist.md) |
| Low engagement, relevant content, good reviews | Can be fine | [L145 05:17–06:11](../lessons/145-how-to-process-creators-add-to-shortlist.md) |
| Screening answers vs preferred | Filter on them | [L143 07:03–07:36](../lessons/143-how-to-create-an-insense-campaign.md) |

3. Send the **Shortlisted Creator** message, then thumbs-up (= shortlist). [L145 01:40; 04:11](../lessons/145-how-to-process-creators-add-to-shortlist.md)

### Step 4: Sheet and confirmation
1. Copy the template "Evolve | CLIENT - Insense Sheet": tabs **All Creators**, **Selected Creators**, **Hired Creators**. [L146 page text](../lessons/146-how-to-download-creators-upload-to-sheet.md)
2. Campaign → download creator list (xlsx) → convert to csv → File > Import > **Insert new sheet** → filter Status to "Onboarding" → keep template columns → paste into All Creators. [L146 00:38–03:10](../lessons/146-how-to-download-creators-upload-to-sheet.md)
3. Conditional-format Shortlisted = TRUE; the client (or you) ticks Client Confirmed. [L146 04:07–04:56](../lessons/146-how-to-download-creators-upload-to-sheet.md)

Columns: All Creators `Platform | Gender | Profile URL | Creator | Followers | Average ER, % | Status | Date added | Price | Shortlisted | Client Confirmed | Agency Confirmed`; Hired Creators `nickname | name | surname | phone number | email | address | Product To Send | Product Sent | Tracking Number | Content Received`. [L146 sheet](../lessons/146-how-to-download-creators-upload-to-sheet.md)

### Step 5: Hire and ship
1. Copy confirmed rows to Selected Creators. [L147 00:00](../lessons/147-how-to-hire-approved-creators-part-1.md)
2. Chats → search the creator → **Hire**; shipping info appears. [L147 00:51](../lessons/147-how-to-hire-approved-creators-part-1.md)
3. Send **Hired Creator** (email, address confirmation, product-only agreement, product choice). [L147 01:34–02:22](../lessons/147-how-to-hire-approved-creators-part-1.md)

| Catalogue | Ask | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Many SKUs | Pick favourites from a collection link (size, colour) | [L147 02:22–03:16](../lessons/147-how-to-hire-approved-creators-part-1.md) |

4. After the reply: **Product Sent** message (keep text in the centre; 9:16 is cropped to 4:5). [L145 template](../lessons/145-how-to-process-creators-add-to-shortlist.md)
5. Send the curated framework ([template below](#creator-framework-template)). [L148 08:15](../lessons/148-sending-frameworks-to-creators-part-2.md)

### Step 7: Collect, triage, close
1. Ask for everything: "The more content, the merrier", including no-audio versions and post links. [L151 03:52](../lessons/151-how-to-follow-up-upload-content-part-1.md)
2. Download originals → Drive: client → "Insense content" → one folder per creator. [L151 00:52](../lessons/151-how-to-follow-up-upload-content-part-1.md)
3. Name the folder `✅ <first name> - DCT` if it can be chopped into three variations as-is; mark X if not (no talking, nothing happening, ring light in shot), upload anyway, tell the team. The check mark "will notify us ... and the clients". [L152 00:00–01:38](../lessons/152-how-to-follow-up-upload-content-part-2.md)
4. Press **Complete** in Insense; tick Content Received. [L151 04:47; 06:23](../lessons/151-how-to-follow-up-upload-content-part-1.md)
5. Launch ✅ folders in the raw content campaign ([campaign structure 3:2:2](campaign-structure-322.md)); after spend data, go to the [brand ambassador program](brand-ambassador-program.md).

## Creator framework template
Structure of the "[Brand] [Gender] UGC Creator Framework" ([L148](../lessons/148-sending-frameworks-to-creators-part-2.md); example text is the course's insulated-cup doc, shortened; replace everything in brackets):
```text
1️⃣ Your Mission
📌 About the Brand: [2-4 lines: product + who it serves]
🤩 What's in it for you: [only offers the brand will honour, e.g. retainer if the first video wins, affiliate commission, % of ad spend]
2️⃣ Do's & Don'ts (same for every brand, tweak lightly)
✅ Be authentic and speak from personal experience · Show real-life scenarios of use · Natural, well-lit settings · Fun, relatable, unscripted
❌ Over-scripting or polished language · Irrelevant/staged settings · Focusing only on the product without your real daily challenges
3️⃣ Video Specs: Vertical 9:16 · 30-60 sec · Vibe: [one line]
4️⃣ Hooks & Ideas: Pick one or more ideas that fit your style best!
  Concept #1: "[Name]" → "[sample hook]"
    Concept: Show [moment from the avatar's day], then reveal [what the product visibly does].
  ... (max 9 in the master; 3-6 in each creator's copy)
5️⃣ Film Your B-Rolls: # | Action  (GIF reference + one-line shot description, 2+ rows)
6️⃣ Learn More About Our Brand: Product page · Instagram
```
B-roll GIFs: record a reference video with Giphy Capture, cut the clips, paste each into a numbered row with a description ("Aesthetic shot of the coffee"). [L149 00:00–02:44](../lessons/149-how-to-make-b-rolls-for-frameworks.md) These show shot types for creators to film themselves; never use another brand's footage in an ad (repo rule 3).

## Conflicts (logged in contradictions notes)
- **Insense price:** $500/month or "$1,200 for the quarter" ([L138 01:36](../lessons/138-ugc-content-overview-part-1.md)) vs $1,500 over three months ([L140](../lessons/140-action-item-sign-up-for-insense.md)); L90 page: $500/month paid quarterly. **Default:** verify on Insense; model $500/month.
- **Price floor:** instructor ~$30 vs Insense UI $20+ ([L143 03:37](../lessons/143-how-to-create-an-insense-campaign.md)). **Default:** $30.
- **Paid budget:** page $1,000 min, ideally $2k-$5k vs spoken "ideally like a thousand bucks", $5-6K/month ([L139](../lessons/139-ugc-content-overview-part-2.md)). **Default:** page values.
- **Follow-up interval:** 2-3 days ([L138](../lessons/138-ugc-content-overview-part-1.md)) vs every 3 days, never > 3-7 days ([L151](../lessons/151-how-to-follow-up-upload-content-part-1.md)). **Default:** every 2-3 days, hard ceiling 7.
- **Hired message:** video says "2 things"; current doc says 3, adding the product-only agreement ([L147](../lessons/147-how-to-hire-approved-creators-part-1.md)). **Default:** the 3-item doc.
