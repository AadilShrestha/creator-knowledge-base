# Concept planning + Growth Guide 3.0
> Turn angles into 5-10 concept cards (concept, explanation, angle(s) with sub-avatar, variations, format, testing method, explanation), choose Marksman or Sniper per card, then log each as an Ad Roadmap row in the Growth Guide 3.0 with a Why/What/How memo, and run the sheet weekly: upvote → filter → batch → launch → wait 7 days → label → learnings → Done.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** angles exist and you need the first test backlog; planning a weekly batch; checking a teammate's test plan for "format with no angle", "generic hold" or "half Sniper, half Marksman"; closing the loop on a launched ad.
- **Inputs → outputs:** desire/core avatar, sub-avatars, ranked angles + hooks ([angle prompt](angle-generation-prompt.md)), revenue stage, team size → 5-10 concept cards, one Growth Guide Ad Roadmap row per card (status, batch #, desire, sub-avatar, angle(s), memo, awareness, file type, ad type), and after 7 days a result label + learnings.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** fill the same fields on every card: concept, concept explanation, angle(s) + sub-avatar, variations, format, testing method, explanation. [L83 00:00–00:39](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md)
  - **DO** write 5-10 concepts before building any. [L84 page text](../lessons/084-action-item-plan-your-5-10-first-concepts.md)
  - **DO** keep 2-4 creatives per round: "don't do more than four"; with two ideas "do a two, two, two". Default 3:2:2. [L83 00:39](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md); [L78 01:28–02:10](../lessons/078-understanding-ad-definitions.md)
  - **DO** on a brand-new concept test only fully congruent hooks: "The only thing that we are testing for brand new concepts is hooks. We don't test holds anymore." [L67 05:49–06:47](../lessons/067-action-item-find-new-mechanisms.md)
  - **DO** take "bigger swings" on first concepts; single-variable tests (messaging, imagery, hold) are "for winning ads only". [L67 08:50; 09:35](../lessons/067-action-item-find-new-mechanisms.md)
  - **DO** in a Marksman video make hooks specific and the hold generic (shared desire/mechanism). [L83 17:19–19:15](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md)
  - **DO** in a Sniper video make the hold specific to the one sub-avatar (behaviors, emotions, day). [L83 09:49–11:23; 21:11](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md)
  - **DON'T** run "half Sniper, half Marksman". [L83 20:37–21:11](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md)
  - **DO** write the memo with at least WHAT and WHY first; "don't really start with the how first". [L85 14:14](../lessons/085-the-3-0-evolve-growth-guide.md)
  - **DO** label every ad set with its batch number. [L85 22:52–23:47](../lessons/085-the-3-0-evolve-growth-guide.md)
  - **DO** wait 7 days after launch before marking a result; don't mark Done until learnings are written. [L85 21:54–22:52](../lessons/085-the-3-0-evolve-growth-guide.md)
  - **DON'T** call spend winners scale: "Spend winners usually don't allow you to scale." [L85 21:18](../lessons/085-the-3-0-evolve-growth-guide.md)
  - **DON'T** read an angle as dead after one poorly executed ad. [L83 13:43–14:42](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md); [L88 03:34](../lessons/088-the-execution-problem.md)
  - **IF** the team is more than 2-3 people **THEN** use the upvote column; otherwise hide it. [L85 04:57; 17:09](../lessons/085-the-3-0-evolve-growth-guide.md)
  - **IF** you are short of ideas **THEN** filter Results = Breakthrough and iterate those. [L85 17:52](../lessons/085-the-3-0-evolve-growth-guide.md)
- **See also:** Copywriting: [mini breakthrough memo + Growth Guide](../../copywriting/frameworks/mini-breakthrough-memo-and-growth-guide.md) (full column list, formulas, memo quality gate) · [marksman/sniper testing](../concepts/marksman-sniper-testing.md) · [winning ad labels](../concepts/winning-ad-labels.md) · [ad definitions](../concepts/ad-definitions.md) · [campaign structure 3:2:2](campaign-structure-322.md) · [learnings review](learnings-review.md) · [execution checklist](../rubrics/execution-checklist.md)

## Definition
**Concept planning** = converting angles into test-ready cards that state what is tested, for whom, with which variations and method. **Growth Guide 3.0** = the 2026 Google Sheet ("the #1 system" behind "43+ brands" reaching $100k/day, instructor claim; audio says 34+) holding every ad idea (Ad Roadmap), page test (Page Roadmap), account change (Log) and hit rate. Planning ≠ making: "there's a big difference between planning ads and making ads"; making moved to the Copywriting program. [L85 page text; 00:58–02:25](../lessons/085-the-3-0-evolve-growth-guide.md)

The Copywriting page already documents the sheet's columns, dropdown values, hit-rate formulas, memo template and quality gate. This page adds Start Here's concept card, the Marksman/Sniper choice rules, the video hook/hold rule and the weekly routine.

## Why it matters
- Batch numbers show "how many ads it took you to kind of unlock that winner". [L78 02:50](../lessons/078-understanding-ad-definitions.md)
- The Log prevents repeat damage: a team turned off a high-spend ad after a bad few days and the account declined for weeks. [L85 02:25–03:19](../lessons/085-the-3-0-evolve-growth-guide.md)
- Hypotheses, not blind tests: "That is how you make progress in your ad account. You don't blindly test things." [L82 07:55–08:51](../lessons/082-the-3-testing-methods-part-2.md)

## How to use it (steps)

### 1. Write the concept cards
```text
Batch #:
Concept:                 (the idea/format: "Nutella ad", "POV: Label videos")
Concept Explanation:     (one sentence: what we're testing)
Angle(s):                (reason to buy; repeat even if concept = angle)
  Sub avatar:            Desire - ..., Product Experience - ..., Emotion - ..., Behavior - ...
Variations (2-4):        V1 / V2 / V3
Format:                  (static graphic | subtitles + B-roll | meme | product photo + headline | UGC ...)
File type:               Static | Video
Testing Method:          Marksman | Sniper
Explanation:             (why this structure; what we'll do with each result)
Headlines (2) / Body copies (2):
Awareness level:         Unaware | Problem | Solution | Product | Most aware
Ad type:                 Ideation (own research) | Iteration (own ads) | Imitation (others' ads)
```
Fields from [L83 prompts](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md), [L78 record](../lessons/078-understanding-ad-definitions.md), [L85 columns](../lessons/085-the-3-0-evolve-growth-guide.md). A sub-avatar needs only desire + one more field. [L83 03:14–04:09](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md)

### 2. Choose the method
| Situation | Method | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Initial image test, several angles ready | Marksman: 2-4 images, one angle each, each congruent | [L83 12:22–13:43](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md) |
| Initial video test, several sub-avatars | Marksman video: specific hooks, one generic hold | [L83 17:19–19:15](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md) |
| Initial video and Marksman feels confusing | Sniper | [L83 13:13](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md) |
| Follow-up after an angle got spend | Sniper ("probably always") | [L83 12:22–13:43](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md); [L81 08:37](../lessons/081-the-3-testing-methods-part-1.md) |
| Angle plateaued | Back to Marksman with new angles | [L81 10:58](../lessons/081-the-3-testing-methods-part-1.md) |
| Under $100K/month, "easy mode" | "Marksman means images and sniper means videos" | [L83 12:22; 23:01](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md) |
| Creator raw content at volume | Shotgun (creator content only, never creative strategy) | [L81 16:28–17:57](../lessons/081-the-3-testing-methods-part-1.md) |
Method theory: [marksman/sniper testing](../concepts/marksman-sniper-testing.md).

### 3. Quality checks per card (before it gets a batch #)
| Check | Pass | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Angle present | Card states a reason to buy, not only a format | [L83 24:48](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md) |
| Congruence | Headline, image/clip and voiceover say the same thing | [L67 05:00; 06:47](../lessons/067-action-item-find-new-mechanisms.md) |
| Marksman hold | Hold still makes sense under every hook | [L83 19:57–20:37](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md) |
| Sniper hold | Hold could NOT run under another angle (swap test) | [L83 09:49–10:36; 26:17](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md) |
| Variation spread | Hooks differ in idea, not wording ("all say the same thing") | [L83 25:21](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md) |
| Memo | WHY has evidence; WHAT names angle + sub-avatar | [L85 13:20–16:22](../lessons/085-the-3-0-evolve-growth-guide.md) |
| Truth | Every claim exists in product facts | repo rule |
| Page | Landing page named and congruent with the ad | [L85 19:46](../lessons/085-the-3-0-evolve-growth-guide.md) |

### 4. Log in the Growth Guide (make a copy; never edit the master)
1. Fill Desires/Core Avatar ("I want...") and Sub Avatars/Angles tabs from avatar work. [L86](../lessons/086-action-item-make-a-copy-add-your-concepts.md)
2. One Ad Roadmap row per card; delete the demo rows. [L86](../lessons/086-action-item-make-a-copy-add-your-concepts.md)
3. Memo in WHY / WHAT / HOW. For ideation the WHY can be research ("they all mentioned this specific pain point"). [L85 14:14–15:04](../lessons/085-the-3-0-evolve-growth-guide.md)

### 5. Weekly routine
| Step | Action | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Everyone adds ideas (Author, Date added, Concept, Desire, Sub-avatar optional, Angle(s), memo, awareness, file type, ad type) | [L85 04:08–12:23](../lessons/085-the-3-0-evolve-growth-guide.md) |
| 2 | Before the strategy call: upvote (+1 each); filter Date added = this week, Upvote ≥ +3 | [L85 04:57–06:24; 16:22–17:09](../lessons/085-the-3-0-evolve-growth-guide.md) |
| 3 | Chosen ideas: Batch #, Status = Working, link to brief | [L85 21:54; 25:26](../lessons/085-the-3-0-evolve-growth-guide.md) |
| 4 | Launch: Status = Learning; media buyer adds Link to Ad | [L85 21:54–22:52; 25:26](../lessons/085-the-3-0-evolve-growth-guide.md) |
| 5 | Day 7: Result = Breakthrough / Spend Winner / KPI Winner / Loser | [L85 19:46–22:52](../lessons/085-the-3-0-evolve-growth-guide.md) |
| 6 | Write learnings ([learnings review](learnings-review.md)), then Done | [L85 22:52](../lessons/085-the-3-0-evolve-growth-guide.md) |
| 7 | Every account change → daily Log; weekly recap row | [L85 02:25–03:19](../lessons/085-the-3-0-evolve-growth-guide.md) |
| 8 | Pages: Page Roadmap, about 1 page per week vs 15-20 ads; each page names the ad it serves | [L85 18:47–19:46](../lessons/085-the-3-0-evolve-growth-guide.md) |
Pace for small teams: about one concept a day, not volume for its own sake. [L81 18:55–19:53](../lessons/081-the-3-testing-methods-part-1.md)

## Examples

### Concept card, filled (L83 Example 2, POV labels)
```text
Concept: POV: Label videos
Concept Explanation: call out sub-avatars directly with labels to find which avatar "total darkness" resonates with most
Angle(s): Sleep better during the day with total darkness (night shift worker) | Sleep more regularly (insomniac) | Avoid premature wake-ups (light sleeper) | Stay asleep through sunrise (early riser)
Variations: "POV: You work night shift" | "POV: You have insomnia" | "POV: You're a light sleeper" | "POV: You hate being an early riser"
Format: subtitles + B-roll; File type: Video
Testing Method: Marksman (hooks specific, hold = product working / total darkness)
Explanation: find direction; follow up with Sniper on the winning sub-avatar
```
[L83 06:25–08:57](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md). The Sniper follow-up (night shift) used a generic hold ("Fix your sleep with a hundred percent darkness", "30 day in home trial"), which the instructor called an execution problem; it should show someone coming home at 7 a.m., sunrise, kids going to school, blinds closing. [L83 08:57–11:23](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md)

### Ad teardowns
| Brand / product (niche) | Format | Hook / opening | What works | What fails | Fix suggested | Principle | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Total-darkness blinds, shift worker (sleep) | Subtitles + B-roll, Sniper | "Night shift is killing me" | Three hooks, one sub-avatar; "Join 10,000 shift workers" speaks to the person | Hold generic, fits any angle | Lived 7 a.m. scene; behaviors, emotions, work life | Sniper hold must be sub-avatar specific | [L83 08:57–11:23](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md) |
| Hockey neck/wrist guard (sports) | Subtitles + B-roll, Sniper | "Don't risk a career-ending injury" | Strong stakes opening | Hooks near-identical; body = features ("15 times stronger") with no injury story | Near-miss story or stat after the hook | Hook and body carry the same angle | [L83 24:48–27:11](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md) |
| Coffee tumbler (drinkware) | Meme static, Marksman | "When your coffee still tastes perfect 6 hours later" | Clear claim in a humour format | No sub-avatar; "not as relatable" | Go deeper on the sub-avatar | Format concept still needs an angle | [L83 23:49–24:48](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md) |

### Breakthrough memo (WHY / WHAT / HOW) from the sheet
WHY: an existing "Negative Framing" ad "spent close to $280k" (instructor figure) + a new winner that also worked as a UGC iteration → blend them. WHAT: lead with what the product prevents: "If you are [SUB AVATAR], This [UNIQUE MECHANISM] won't Do X everytime Y Happens which will not cause [THING SUB AVATAR IS SCARED OF]". HOW: a video like a linked ad. Solution aware; Video; Iteration; Result Breakthrough. [L85 13:20–16:22](../lessons/085-the-3-0-evolve-growth-guide.md) Verbatim: [L85 templates](../lessons/085-the-3-0-evolve-growth-guide.md).

## Common mistakes
| Mistake | Fix | Source |
|---|---|---|
| "UGC test" / "meme statics" with no angle | Add the angle: "testing UGC formats of what?" | [L78 06:39–07:21](../lessons/078-understanding-ad-definitions.md) |
| Single-variable test on an unproven concept | Test congruent hooks; isolate variables only on winners | [L67 01:56–03:23; 09:35](../lessons/067-action-item-find-new-mechanisms.md) |
| Headline on an incongruent image ("World's first stainless steel humidifier" over a plastic one) | Make visual and text one idea | [L67 05:00](../lessons/067-action-item-find-new-mechanisms.md) |
| Images and videos in separate campaigns because images don't spend | "Your image ads aren't getting spent because they suck"; improve them | [L83 14:42–15:40](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md) |
| Learnings about structure ("good hook rate") | Learnings about the customer: sub-avatar, angle, why they care | [L81 13:46–14:35](../lessons/081-the-3-testing-methods-part-1.md) |
| Getting stuck in the angle matrix | "Just make the best answer you can" | [L83 28:02](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md) |
| Marking Done on a Breakthrough with no learnings | "NO LEARNINGS + NOT DONE" | [L85 Examples](../lessons/085-the-3-0-evolve-growth-guide.md) |
