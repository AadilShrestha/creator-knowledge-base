# Desire hunt
> Find the mass desires a product can credibly channel: mine Reddit, YouTube comments and reviews by hand for "I want" statements in the customers' own words, run the Desire AI deep-research prompt in parallel, verify every quote, tag each desire to an instinct or technological problem plus a force of change, score Scope / Urgency / Staying Power, and hand the winner to avatar building.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** after the [research stack](research-stack-workflow.md) and product overview, before core avatars, angles or scripts; when a brand needs fresh desire-led angles; when choosing which desire to lead with this month.
- **Inputs → outputs:** product URL (not a landing page), 1-2 angles already running, the Product Overview doc (L57-60), research doc → a Desire Hunting sheet (Desires as "I want..." lines / Customer Language Snippets with source / Ad Ideas / New Information) + a ranked desire table + 3-5 channel lines per top desire + the chosen lead desire for avatar work.
- **Rules:**
  - **DON'T** create desires. "You cannot CREATE desires. You can only CHANNEL existing desires onto your product." "Never Fight Desires." [L64 Desire Document](../lessons/064-action-item-start-your-desire-hunt.md); [L61 02:40–04:01; 57:02](../lessons/061-understanding-applying-desire-part-1.md)
  - **DO** run the manual hunt alongside any AI run: the "golden nuggets... deep in Reddit threads, deep in reviews... is where a lot of the winning ads are from." [L63 00:54](../lessons/063-how-to-find-desires-part-3.md)
  - **DO** write desires in the customer's words and confirm the word exists in the source (Ctrl/Cmd+F). "Hydrated" was wrong; buyers said "smoother and less dry skin". [L63 04:00–04:53](../lessons/063-how-to-find-desires-part-3.md)
  - **DO** ask "why?" until you reach an instinct (Why Train). [L56 02:44](../lessons/056-organizing-insights-overview-must-watch.md); [L63 13:14–14:13](../lessons/063-how-to-find-desires-part-3.md)
  - **DO** score every desire on Scope, Urgency, Staying Power; "scope more than anything". [L61 36:31–40:39](../lessons/061-understanding-applying-desire-part-1.md)
  - **DON'T** target a desire the product "cannot live up to". [L57 03:47–04:42](../lessons/057-understanding-your-product-part-1.md); [L76 05:12](../lessons/076-how-to-build-sub-avatars.md)
  - **DON'T** use AI-returned quotes, stats or Reddit claims as truth: verify at source; "just add it as an insight for you to test." [L63 16:14](../lessons/063-how-to-find-desires-part-3.md); [L64 rules](../lessons/064-action-item-start-your-desire-hunt.md)
  - **DON'T** move a product down the instinct ranking: "Moving down = less emotional urgency = lower prices = weaker positioning." [L61 45:14–46:42](../lessons/061-understanding-applying-desire-part-1.md) (see the health exception below)
  - **DON'T** fake an association to climb to status ("celebrity trainers use it") if untrue. [L61 53:15](../lessons/061-understanding-applying-desire-part-1.md)
  - **IF** the desire is a heavily marketed one ("take control") **THEN** check sophistication first: menopause buyers have heard it "a zillion" times. [L63 40:52](../lessons/063-how-to-find-desires-part-3.md)
  - **IF** the market is product-aware **THEN** lean on mass technological problems with existing products. [L61 24:22–25:37](../lessons/061-understanding-applying-desire-part-1.md)
- **See also:** [market desire](../concepts/market-desire.md) · [desire calendar](../concepts/desire-calendar.md) · [core and sub-avatars](../concepts/core-and-sub-avatars.md) · [angle generation prompt](angle-generation-prompt.md) · Copywriting: [labels and customer language](../../copywriting/concepts/labels-and-customer-language.md) · Tether: [customer research to angles](../../../../playbooks/customer-research-to-angles.md)

## Definition
A desire is what a mass of people already want ("Mass Desire = 'The public spread of a private want'"). The hunt is the procedure that finds which existing desires the product can channel, with evidence. Theory (instincts, technological problems, forces of change, elevation) lives on [market desire](../concepts/market-desire.md); this page is the procedure. [L64 Desire Document](../lessons/064-action-item-start-your-desire-hunt.md)

## Why it matters
- Avatars start from desire, not demographics: "A 22-year-old and a 45-year-old can share the same desire." [L61 01:22–02:40](../lessons/061-understanding-applying-desire-part-1.md)
- "All avatars need to have desires because that is what actually pushes them to buy." [L76 00:42](../lessons/076-how-to-build-sub-avatars.md)
- Desire alone does not convince: "Desires don't mean anything unless I can build belief." The hunt feeds the sophistication levers next. [L56 06:45](../lessons/056-organizing-insights-overview-must-watch.md)

## How to use it (steps)
1. **Open the sheet.** Copy the Desire Hunting Template (or add these sections to the research doc): Your Brand / Desires (Wants/Needs) / Customer Language Snippets / Ad Ideas / New Information. [L64 template](../lessons/064-action-item-start-your-desire-hunt.md)
2. **Seed from the product.** Copy the Product Overview's "assumed desires" (feature → benefit → "why does someone want that?"), labelled *assumed*. [L58 14:24–18:52](../lessons/058-understanding-your-product-part-2.md)
3. **Start the AI run** (it works while you hunt by hand): the Desire AI Deep Research Prompt with product URL, 1-2 current angles, the Product Overview doc and the "How To Find Desires" guide attached. Verbatim prompt and guide: [L64 prompt](../lessons/064-action-item-start-your-desire-hunt.md#desire-ai-deep-research-prompt). [L63 28:04–30:59](../lessons/063-how-to-find-desires-part-3.md)
4. **Manual Reddit pass (~30 min, derived from L63 page).** Google "[category] reddit"; read by recency and comment count; prefer upvoted, long comments ("that's how you know you're dialed in"); copy verbatim snippets with upvotes and thread date. [L63 02:49–03:00; 14:13](../lessons/063-how-to-find-desires-part-3.md) Guide search patterns: "[your product category] sucks", "[problem] solution", upvoted complaints with 100+ comments, "I love this BUT..." [L64 guide](../lessons/064-action-item-start-your-desire-hunt.md)
5. **Write each desire** as "I want..." in the customer's words; Ctrl+F the word in the source. [L63 04:53](../lessons/063-how-to-find-desires-part-3.md)
6. **Why Train each complaint** to an instinct: joint pain from pull-ups → "work out without joint pain" → health; "hit PRs" → status. [L63 13:14–14:13](../lessons/063-how-to-find-desires-part-3.md)
7. **YouTube/TikTok pass.** Search the avatar's state (e.g. "menopause"), open top-view videos, read comments; optionally paste comment batches into an LLM with the desire definition and the permanent-forces list. Watch at least one explainer for "new information". [L63 18:18–24:57](../lessons/063-how-to-find-desires-part-3.md)
8. **Log competitors** named positively in comments (for competitor research). [L63 15:10](../lessons/063-how-to-find-desires-part-3.md)
9. **Read the AI report**, then open every cited source and confirm each quote and number; delete what you cannot find. (derived; the prompt's "Your life depends on" line does not prevent invention) [L64](../lessons/064-action-item-start-your-desire-hunt.md)
10. **Tag** each desire: instinct (Health, Sex, Status, Belonging, Control, Comfort) or technological problem (Complexity, Overwhelm, Fragility, Maintenance, Incompatibility, Obsolescence), plus a force of change (style trend = decade-scale shift, not a fad; mass education = something people recently learned). [L61 05:20–20:23](../lessons/061-understanding-applying-desire-part-1.md)
11. **Score and rank** (table below). Break ties with the instinct power ranking. [L61 36:31–44:05](../lessons/061-understanding-applying-desire-part-1.md)
12. **Fit check** against the product: can it credibly deliver; is the mechanism believable (sunglasses vs a sleep machine for sleep)? [L57 03:47–06:31](../lessons/057-understanding-your-product-part-1.md); [L76 05:12–06:34](../lessons/076-how-to-build-sub-avatars.md)
13. **Write 3-5 channel lines** per top desire: one instinct/problem + one force of change → a headline. Don't attempt all 24 combinations. [L61 20:23–30:23](../lessons/061-understanding-applying-desire-part-1.md)
14. **Check timing** in the [desire calendar](../concepts/desire-calendar.md) for the launch month; plan seasonal lines a month ahead. [L62 07:32; 14:55](../lessons/062-the-desire-calendar-part-2.md)
15. **Hand off**: chosen lead desire (sized to revenue stage) → core avatar ([L74](../lessons/074-how-to-build-core-avatars.md)); snippets → hooks; New Information → [sophistication levers](../concepts/market-sophistication-levers.md). Return the next day for a second read: "always be looking for desires." [L63 39:08–39:54](../lessons/063-how-to-find-desires-part-3.md)

## Scoring table
Score 1-5 each (the 1-5 scale is derived; the three dimensions are the course's).

| Dimension | Question | Course contrast | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scope | How many people share it? | "fine antiques VS making money" | [L64 Desire Document](../lessons/064-action-item-start-your-desire-hunt.md) |
| Urgency | How desperately do they want relief? | "broken bone VS cut" | same |
| Staying Power | Does it renew continuously? | "hunger VS Going to paris" | same |

Instinct power ranking (tie-breaker): 1 Health · 2 Status · 3 Sex · 4 Comfort · 5 Control · 6 Belonging. [L64 Desire Document](../lessons/064-action-item-start-your-desire-hunt.md)

### Desire breadth by revenue stage
| Monthly revenue | Pick | Example (blackout blinds) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| $0-100K | Smallest, most specific desire | "sleeps in a quiet environment" | [L74 19:14–19:55](../lessons/074-how-to-build-core-avatars.md) |
| $100K-$1M | Middle desire | restorative sleep | same |
| $1M-$10M | Broad desire | enhance health | same |
Never pick a core instinct itself (health, status...) as the avatar desire: "slightly more broad... not too broad where it's a core desire". [L76 05:12; 07:33–09:58](../lessons/076-how-to-build-sub-avatars.md)

## Templates

### Desire Hunting sheet (L64, extended with evidence fields)
```yaml
brand: ""
desires:
  - want: "I want ..."            # customer words; Ctrl+F confirmed
    evidence: [{quote: "", source_url: "", upvotes: 0, date: ""}]
    instinct_or_tech_problem: ""  # Health|Sex|Status|Belonging|Control|Comfort|Complexity|Overwhelm|Fragility|Maintenance|Incompatibility|Obsolescence
    force_of_change: ""           # style trend | mass education | none
    why_train: "surface want -> ... -> deep desire"
    scope: 0; urgency: 0; staying_power: 0   # 1-5 (derived scale)
    product_can_deliver: yes|no|partly
    sophistication_flag: ""       # e.g. "heavily marketed claim"
    status: assumed|verified
customer_language_snippets: [{quote: "", source_url: ""}]
ad_ideas: [""]
new_information: [{finding: "", source: "", verified: false}]
lead_desire: ""                   # sized to revenue stage
```

### Channel line formula
`[instinct or technological problem] + [style trend or mass education] → headline`

| Product | Force | Force of change | Channel line (Desire Document) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dog harness | Status | Style trend: "Dogs are family members" | "Be the dog parent everyone admires" |
| Dog harness | Health | Mass education: collar pulling damages the trachea | "Why are you still choking your dog in 2025?" |
| Dog harness | Fragility | Style trend: built-to-last | "The last harness you'll ever buy" |
| Dog harness | Incompatibility | Mass education: dogs slip out of regular harnesses | "The harness Houdini can't escape" |
| Blackout blinds | Comfort | Style trend: "Locking in" | "Time to lock in perfect sleep" |
| Blackout blinds | Complexity (install) | Mass education: installation usually sucks | "No tools. No drilling. No BS." |
| Blackout blinds | Complexity (sleep optimisation) | Style trend: biohacking | "Biohack your bedroom in 5 minutes" |
Source: [L64 Examples](../lessons/064-action-item-start-your-desire-hunt.md). Reuse the structure, not the lines. "Light is slowly killing you." is in the doc but is low-valence/high-intensity; screen it per [L68](../lessons/068-how-to-find-apply-new-information-part-3.md).

### Customer-interview probes (from the guide; for a human interviewer)
"What keeps you up at night about [problem area]?" · "When you imagine the perfect solution, what would your life look like?" · "What have you tried before that disappointed you?" · "If money wasn't an issue, what would you do about this?" · "When friends complain about [problem], what do they say exactly?" Listen for emotional words, extreme language (always, never, hate), time pressure (finally, constantly), social references. [L64 guide](../lessons/064-action-item-start-your-desire-hunt.md)

## Examples
- **Collagen (live hunt):** "hydrated" replaced by "smoother and less dry skin"; nail growth used as a visible proxy so viewers can self-test, in "might" wording, because the product can't be shown working on camera. [L63 04:00–10:20](../lessons/063-how-to-find-desires-part-3.md)
- **Why Train (shirt):** new shirt → improve my style → look more attractive → be in a relationship, fall in love. [L56 02:44](../lessons/056-organizing-insights-overview-must-watch.md)
- **Elevation ladder (dog harness, weakest → strongest):** Belonging "Join the responsible dog owner community" → Comfort "Finally, stress-free dog walks" → Control "Take control of every walk" → Sex "Turn heads on every walk" → Status → Health "Why vets are begging owners to switch". The Status line's "3%" has no source; never use such a figure. [L64 Elevation Strategy](../lessons/064-action-item-start-your-desire-hunt.md)
- **Customer line → headline:** "9pm looks like it's the middle of the night" → "Turn 9pm into 2am". [L74 45:45–46:39](../lessons/074-how-to-build-core-avatars.md)

## Common mistakes
| Mistake | Fix | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Demographic "avatar" before desire | Desire first | [L61 01:22–02:40](../lessons/061-understanding-applying-desire-part-1.md) |
| Fully automating with AI | Manual pass + AI, compare | [L63 00:54](../lessons/063-how-to-find-desires-part-3.md) |
| Marketer words ("hydrated") | Customer words, Ctrl+F checked | [L63 04:00–04:53](../lessons/063-how-to-find-desires-part-3.md) |
| Fads treated as style trends | Decade-scale shifts only | [L61 17:47–20:23](../lessons/061-understanding-applying-desire-part-1.md) |
| Testing every instinct × force combination | Rank, then write the top few | [L61 27:59](../lessons/061-understanding-applying-desire-part-1.md) |
| Mechanical checklist writing ("analysis paralysis") | Write, then analyse why it works | [L61 57:02–1:01:14](../lessons/061-understanding-applying-desire-part-1.md) |
| Dropping core angles for the seasonal desire | Calendar = diagnostic and extra tests | [L62 04:11; 15:42](../lessons/062-the-desire-calendar-part-2.md) |
| Mega-broad desires under $1M/month | Pick by revenue stage | [L74 09:06](../lessons/074-how-to-build-core-avatars.md) |

## Disagreement inside the course
- **Elevation vs health downshift.** The Desire Document and L61 say never move down the ranking [L61 45:14–46:42](../lessons/061-understanding-applying-desire-part-1.md); in the same lesson the instructor suggests health brands consider a lower desire (comfort, control, sex) because "everybody in health is selling health" [L61 45:14](../lessons/061-understanding-applying-desire-part-1.md). **Agent default:** elevate when authentic; in a health category saturated with health claims, add one lower-desire angle to a Marksman batch next to the health angle and let spend decide.
