# Learnings review
> Close the feedback loop on every ad: after ~7 days, judge each concept by its share of account spend, find the variant Meta preferred and why, compare it to known winners, write "educated guess" learnings and end with one action. Run the same breakdown on winning ads (yours and other brands', found by sorting a spy tool by shares), "chunk up" their lines into reusable frameworks, and QA every video brief with the 14 questions.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** a test batch has ~7 days of data; writing the "Learnings" entry for any ad (loser, near-winner, winner); tearing down a competitor or cross-niche winner; QA of a video brief before launch or after an ad "didn't scale or didn't convert"; preparing the weekly learnings call.
- **Inputs → outputs:** Ads Manager data per variant (spend, ROAS/CPA, CVR, AOV, frequency, CTR; video: hook, hold, avg play time), account spend for the same 7 days, the creatives and scripts, 2-3 known winning ads, customer language (Reddit, reviews, comments) → a learnings card per ad (metrics, % of spend, verdict, top-spend variant and its differences, guesses, one action), a line-by-line teardown for winners, 1-3 framework statements with new lines, and a 14-question pass/fail for each video brief.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** review losers, not only winners: "you got to know where you're fucking up... and why your ads aren't working." [L122 01:28](../lessons/122-how-to-do-learnings-part-1-losers.md)
  - **DO** wait at least 7 days before judging ("Meta is trying to figure itself out"); a kill after 3-4 days is possible but not recommended. [L124 07:54](../lessons/124-how-to-do-learnings-part-3-losers.md)
  - **DO** judge by % of spend = concept spend ÷ total account spend over the same 7 days; ~2% = loser, ≥10% = early signal, 30-50% = likely winner. [L124 05:13-07:54](../lessons/124-how-to-do-learnings-part-3-losers.md)
  - **DO** record metrics in this order: amount spent → ROAS or CPA → CVR, AOV, frequency, CTR → (video) hook rate, hold rate, average play time. [L124 01:15-02:53](../lessons/124-how-to-do-learnings-part-3-losers.md)
  - **IF** an ad got no CBO spend **THEN** read hook rate and hold rate before judging the concept. [L122 02:32](../lessons/122-how-to-do-learnings-part-1-losers.md)
  - **DON'T** turn off the top-spend variant to force spend onto the others: "If there was a winner in here, Meta would have spent on it." Use the top spender as the direction. [L124 10:48-11:41](../lessons/124-how-to-do-learnings-part-3-losers.md)
  - **DO** list what the top-spend variant does that the others don't (claim type, specificity, Big Four frame, product name, congruence, avatar callout, credibility). [L124 11:41-19:46](../lessons/124-how-to-do-learnings-part-3-losers.md)
  - **IF** you can't see why an ad failed **THEN** compare it line by line with known winning ads (the winning-static deck). [L124 19:46-23:12](../lessons/124-how-to-do-learnings-part-3-losers.md)
  - **DON'T** write broad learnings ("this image sucks"); look at messaging, framing, what you say, whether you explain the mechanism. [L124 24:51-25:41](../lessons/124-how-to-do-learnings-part-3-losers.md)
  - **DO** end every review with one action: "what are we going to do as a result of this new information?" [L124 23:12](../lessons/124-how-to-do-learnings-part-3-losers.md)
  - **DO** label learnings "educated guesses. They are not facts"; a guess becomes "slightly more of a fact" only when a re-test built on it wins. [L124 25:41-27:31](../lessons/124-how-to-do-learnings-part-3-losers.md)
  - **IF** an ad got $0 spend **THEN** skip the data review, note why customers didn't care, keep "don't do A, B, C, D" and move on. [L124 28:20-29:59](../lessons/124-how-to-do-learnings-part-3-losers.md)
  - **DO** find winners to study by sorting a brand's ads by **shares** in a recent "seen between" window (3-6 months), not by "longest running". [L123 01:16-03:34](../lessons/123-how-to-do-learnings-part-2-winning-ads.md); [L127 00:00](../lessons/127-how-to-do-learnings-part-5-winning-ads.md)
  - **DO** ask "how is this ad making the customer feel?" phrase by phrase; separate what the copy says from what it communicates. [L125 07:38; 11:31-12:30](../lessons/125-how-to-do-learnings-part-4-winning-ads.md); [L127 00:53](../lessons/127-how-to-do-learnings-part-5-winning-ads.md)
  - **DO** "chunk up one level" from the words to the framework and the feeling; write 1 control + 2 framework variants in your own words. [L126 07:46-13:45](../lessons/126-how-to-pull-frameworks-from-ads.md)
  - **DON'T** copy a hook one-to-one inside your niche: "You're just copying what someone else has copied." [L126 13:45](../lessons/126-how-to-pull-frameworks-from-ads.md)
  - **DO** run the 14 questions on every video brief before submission and again after a test that "just didn't scale or didn't convert"; an ad does not have to pass all of them. [L128 00:00-00:33; 04:43](../lessons/128-how-to-do-learnings-questions.md)
  - **DO** keep a sheet of every ad with awareness stage, desire, persona, messaging and why it won or lost, so you don't retest the same angle. [L133 05:29-06:25](../lessons/133-winning-humidifier-ad-learnings-shaun.md)
- **See also:** [Rewrite losers and iterate](rewrite-losers-and-iterate.md) · [KPIs and columns](kpis-and-columns.md) · [Winning ad labels](../concepts/winning-ad-labels.md) · [Execution checklist](../rubrics/execution-checklist.md) · [Weekly media-buying routine](../playbooks/weekly-media-buying-routine.md) · Copywriting: [Ten-step iteration learnings](../../copywriting/frameworks/ten-step-iteration-learnings.md), [Iteration outcomes](../../copywriting/concepts/iteration-outcomes.md) · Tether Lab: [Competitor ad teardown](../../../../playbooks/competitor-ad-teardown.md)

## Definition
A **learnings review** is the "Feedback" step of the Evolve loop: a written, per-ad diagnosis of why Meta spent (or didn't) and why customers bought (or didn't), ending in an action. Evolve runs it as a weekly team "learnings call" [L122 00:00](../lessons/122-how-to-do-learnings-part-1-losers.md); the agency's report prompt describes the cadence: ads run 4-7 days, then each is reviewed and labelled "loser", "winner" or "super winner", tracked in ClickUp, and only ads in the "learnings" phase are discussed [L130 prompt A](../lessons/130-how-to-rewrite-losing-ads-into-winners.md).

Three review types:

| Type | Input | Main question | Lessons |
|---|---|---|---|
| Loser review | Your ad that got little or no spend / poor KPI | Why didn't Meta or people care? What did the top-spend variant do differently? | [L122](../lessons/122-how-to-do-learnings-part-1-losers.md), [L124](../lessons/124-how-to-do-learnings-part-3-losers.md) |
| Near-winner review | Spent, decent soft metrics, then faded | What showed potential, what was shallow? | [L130](../lessons/130-how-to-rewrite-losing-ads-into-winners.md) → [rewrite loop](rewrite-losers-and-iterate.md) |
| Winner teardown | Your winner or a high-share ad from any brand | How does each line make the customer feel, and which moves transfer? | [L123](../lessons/123-how-to-do-learnings-part-2-winning-ads.md), [L125](../lessons/125-how-to-do-learnings-part-4-winning-ads.md), [L126](../lessons/126-how-to-pull-frameworks-from-ads.md), [L127](../lessons/127-how-to-do-learnings-part-5-winning-ads.md), [L133](../lessons/133-winning-humidifier-ad-learnings-shaun.md) |

## Why it matters
- "Feedback loops are so crucial." [L122 01:28](../lessons/122-how-to-do-learnings-part-1-losers.md) Without them you can't tell strategy failure from execution failure ([L88 00:00-01:49](../lessons/088-the-execution-problem.md)).
- "If you close this feedback loop, you can understand what to avoid next and you're going to save a lot more money and time." Many teams "have been testing the same angle over and over again" because they don't track. [L133 05:29-06:25](../lessons/133-winning-humidifier-ad-learnings-shaun.md)
- How to judge your skill: "when you do learnings, can you make a winning ad?" Spencer claims a ~60-70% chance for himself (instructor claim). [L124 28:20](../lessons/124-how-to-do-learnings-part-3-losers.md)
- "That's how learnings compound." [L124 29:59](../lessons/124-how-to-do-learnings-part-3-losers.md)

## Procedure A: loser review (L124 method, with L122 checks)

| # | Step | Do exactly this | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pull the concept | In Ads Manager filter by run dates; find every variant; sort by spend | [L124 00:00-00:30](../lessons/124-how-to-do-learnings-part-3-losers.md) |
| 2 | Record metrics | Fill the learnings card (template below), in priority order | [L124 01:15-04:27](../lessons/124-how-to-do-learnings-part-3-losers.md) |
| 3 | % of spend | Concept spend ÷ account spend, 7 days. Use Breakdown > By day at **ad-set** level to see the daily curve | [L124 05:13-07:04](../lessons/124-how-to-do-learnings-part-3-losers.md) |
| 4 | Verdict | Apply the % of spend table below | [L124 07:04-09:34](../lessons/124-how-to-do-learnings-part-3-losers.md) |
| 5 | Did Meta test it? | No spend → hook rate and hold rate first (L122 example: hook ~16%, hold "insanely low") | [L122 02:32](../lessons/122-how-to-do-learnings-part-1-losers.md) |
| 6 | Top-spend variant | Open Preview; name the variant with the most spend; treat it as the direction | [L124 09:34-11:41](../lessons/124-how-to-do-learnings-part-3-losers.md) |
| 7 | Differences | Write what the top variant does that others don't (see checklist below) | [L124 13:26-19:46](../lessons/124-how-to-do-learnings-part-3-losers.md) |
| 8 | Compare to the account's top performer | Hook wording, first-clip length, angle, callout | [L122 08:45-09:54](../lessons/122-how-to-do-learnings-part-1-losers.md) |
| 9 | Compare to known winners | Avatar callout, real-review claim, bold numbers, stealing credibility, wordplay, specific pain | [L124 19:46-23:12](../lessons/124-how-to-do-learnings-part-3-losers.md) |
| 10 | Reality check | Even the best variant of a 2% concept is "still not a winning ad"; don't "crack the code" from it | [L124 16:29-17:24](../lessons/124-how-to-do-learnings-part-3-losers.md) |
| 11 | Action plan | One named next ad (double down on the top-spend direction) or "don't repeat A, B, C, D" | [L124 23:12-24:51](../lessons/124-how-to-do-learnings-part-3-losers.md) |
| 12 | Log as guesses | Tag each learning "guess"; record whether the re-test validates it | [L124 25:41-28:20](../lessons/124-how-to-do-learnings-part-3-losers.md) |

## Procedure B: winning-ad teardown (yours or other brands')

| 11 | Compare with our ad | "Damn, I'm not even tapping into any of those desires…" | [L123 16:26-17:38](../lessons/123-how-to-do-learnings-part-2-winning-ads.md) |

Rules from the teardowns:
- **DON'T** read shares as conversion proof: "shares are not everything", but strong engagement means the ad is "likely converting". [L123 15:23](../lessons/123-how-to-do-learnings-part-2-winning-ads.md)
- **DON'T** define a hook as only a scroll-stopper: "Hooks are about keeping people hooked in, not just hooking them." [L125 09:39-10:37](../lessons/125-how-to-do-learnings-part-4-winning-ads.md); "Some of you have really high hook rates, but it doesn't actually keep them engaged." [L127 05:13](../lessons/127-how-to-do-learnings-part-5-winning-ads.md)
- **DO** create curiosity, not confusion: "Confusion is going to make someone scroll." [L127 04:25](../lessons/127-how-to-do-learnings-part-5-winning-ads.md)
- **DO** "turn a matter of opinion into a matter of fact" (only real, checkable facts). [L127 06:53](../lessons/127-how-to-do-learnings-part-5-winning-ads.md) See [Facts over opinions](../../copywriting/concepts/facts-over-opinions.md).
- **IF** add-to-carts happen but not purchases **THEN** suspect missing urgency; build it from worsening symptoms, not "your dog is at risk". [L127 15:38-17:14](../lessons/127-how-to-do-learnings-part-5-winning-ads.md)
- **DO** note desire, awareness stage, persona and messaging of every new winner. [L133 board, step 4](../lessons/133-winning-humidifier-ad-learnings-shaun.md)

## Course content to describe, not execute
- **"From this video only" / "first come, first served"** (Everyday Dose teardown): the instructor notes the exclusivity line is "mostly untrue". **Don't execute.** Compliant alternative: use urgency only from a real, dated offer or from real consequences of waiting (symptom urgency, [L127 15:38-17:14](../lessons/127-how-to-do-learnings-part-5-winning-ads.md)). [L126 04:48-05:29; 07:11-07:46](../lessons/126-how-to-pull-frameworks-from-ads.md)
- **"One control if we just want to take this one-to-one"** [L126 13:45](../lessons/126-how-to-pull-frameworks-from-ads.md): **don't execute** a word-for-word copy. Compliant control = the closest version of the *framework* in our own words, with our claims.
- **Feeding a competitor's winning image to ChatGPT to "create an ad… similar to that"** [L123 04:55](../lessons/123-how-to-do-learnings-part-2-winning-ads.md): only extract the mechanism (structure, framing, emotional move); never reproduce the visual, lines or claims.
- **Health claims in teardown ads** (GLP-1 "56%", "100,000 five star reviews", "96% of dog owners"): they are other brands' claims. Never reuse; source or bracket every number for our own product.

## Common mistakes
- Judging by taste instead of spend: Spencer was "pretty pumped" about statics that took 2% of spend. [L124 23:12](../lessons/124-how-to-do-learnings-part-3-losers.md)
- Killing an angle after one weak execution ("probably it means the execution... was just not good"). [L124 17:24-18:00](../lessons/124-how-to-do-learnings-part-3-losers.md); [L88 03:34](../lessons/088-the-execution-problem.md)
- Turning learnings into laws ("two things that don't go together needs to be on every single ad"). [L124 25:41-27:31](../lessons/124-how-to-do-learnings-part-3-losers.md)
- Copying a winner's surface ("three reasons why" as every hook) instead of the underlying learning. [L128 05:32-06:22](../lessons/128-how-to-do-learnings-questions.md)
- Spam testing (20 creatives a day) instead of thinking and research. [L133 03:40](../lessons/133-winning-humidifier-ad-learnings-shaun.md)
- Studying only your own ads: "You need to stop looking at just your ads." [L88 01:49-02:43](../lessons/088-the-execution-problem.md)
- Stretching a gag past the payoff ("you kind of just wasted someone's time"). [L126 02:42-03:29](../lessons/126-how-to-pull-frameworks-from-ads.md)
