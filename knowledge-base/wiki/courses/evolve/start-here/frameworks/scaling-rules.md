# Scaling rules
> Once a day, per campaign, run one flowchart: at target KPI for 48-72 h (or a new winning ad, offer or page) with ≥ 60% click-based purchases → +20%; below breakeven 24-48 h or below target 48-72 h → −20% unless at the minimum daily spend; otherwise wait 24 h. In promos, surf-scale intra-day on blended return and reset before account midnight. Kill only at ad-set level, by the table in §5. When scaling stalls, the fix is outside the ad account: more and better ads, offers and pages.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** deciding a budget change for a campaign or ABO ad set; planning a sale's intra-day budget moves; deciding whether to turn off a test, a spend hog or an old top spender; diagnosing a sudden drop or day-to-day whiplash.
- **Inputs → outputs:** target and breakeven KPI ([KPIs and columns](kpis-and-columns.md)), campaign and account ROAS/CPA on 7-day click per day, hours of consistency, click share of purchases, current and minimum daily spend, promo end date, new winning assets, per-ad spend share and launch date → one action (+20% / +X% / double / hold / wait 24 h / −20% / −50% / off / cap) with its rule ID and citation, plus the out-of-account task list when cutting or holding.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** start with "are we hitting target KPI?" for the campaign **and** the whole account, on 7-day-click data. [L115 00:00; 09:41](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md)
  - **DO** require 48-72 h consistently above target (or a "new reason to be scaling") and > 60% click-based purchases (or click data alone meeting KPI) before any evergreen increase; else wait 24 h. [L115 01:01–05:51](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md)
  - **DO** raise budget 20% flat ("For most people, you can actually scale 20%"); raise by the % you are above KPI only when consistently 50-100% above, "at your own risk". [L115 05:51–07:13](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md)
  - **DO** cut 20% (not 10%) when below breakeven 24-48 h, or below target 48-72 h, unless already at the minimum daily spend. [L115 08:23–09:41](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md)
  - **IF** you are at the minimum daily spend or cutting **THEN** work outside the account: new DCTs, funnel bug check, learnings, deep research, offer testing. "What is actually going to fix the inside of your ad account is everything outside." [L115 11:04–15:42](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md)
  - **DON'T** turn off ads that are working, or a proven older top spender because a tiny new ad shows higher ROAS. [L094 page](../lessons/094-how-to-set-up-a-3-2-2-campaign.md) [L120 09:11](../lessons/120-how-to-see-if-you-should-turn-off-high-spend-ads.md)
  - **DO** kill a new ad that hogs spend at a bad KPI within 24-48 h ("a day or two" below $10-20k/day; rules above that). [L120 02:18; 09:11](../lessons/120-how-to-see-if-you-should-turn-off-high-spend-ads.md)
  - **DO** surf-scale intra-day only in promos (or one evergreen campaign already spending "few thousand a day"), on blended return, and reset budgets before ad-account midnight to ~half the day's actual spend. [L118 page; 04:52–07:47](../lessons/118-advanced-surf-scaling-method.md)
  - **DON'T** answer a drop with a new media-buying strategy, product or testing process; "Make better ads. Make better offers. Make better pages." [L121 34:14–35:19](../lessons/121-how-to-deal-with-roller-coaster-performance.md)
  - **IF** you can't scale **THEN** "Make a more Ads!" [L119 page image](../lessons/119-action-item-scale-your-ads.md)

## 1. Definition
The "EAM scaling process" is a yes/no board ("Evolve - Protocols", Figma, not in the course files; read from frames) applied to "the campaigns that are in your account and what you currently have an active budget on", once every 24 hours. "Scaling protocol" = scaling the account every 24 hours after "two to three days of consistent performance above your target KPI". [L115 00:00–02:19; page](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md) Three add-ons extend it: ABO ad-set doubling ([L097](../lessons/097-how-to-set-up-abo-scaling-campaign.md)), promo self-scaling ([L108](../lessons/108-how-to-set-up-a-promo-campaign.md)) and surf scaling ([L118](../lessons/118-advanced-surf-scaling-method.md)). Turn-off rules come from [L094](../lessons/094-how-to-set-up-a-3-2-2-campaign.md), [L096](../lessons/096-how-to-set-up-zombie-graveyard-campaign.md), [L107](../lessons/107-how-to-protect-against-bad-ads-over-spending.md) and [L120](../lessons/120-how-to-see-if-you-should-turn-off-high-spend-ads.md).

## 2. The daily scaling flowchart (evergreen campaigns)
Run once per day per campaign with a budget. KPI = the target and breakeven on the brand's card, read on 7-day click.

| # | Question | Yes → | No → | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Is the campaign **and** the whole account hitting target KPI? | 2 | 6 | [L115 00:00](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md) |
| 2 | Limited-time promo with a hard end date? | 4 (skip the wait) | 3 | [L115 01:01](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md) |
| 3 | 48-72 h consistently above target (scaling protocol), **or** a new reason to scale (new winning ad / offer / landing page, or "something... leading to dramatically better performance")? | 4 | Wait 24 h | [L115 01:01–03:23](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md) |
| 4 | > 60% of purchases click-based, **or** click data alone meets KPI? | 5 | Wait 24 h; consider 7-day click only | [L115 03:23–05:51](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md) |
| 5 | Consistently 50-100% above target? | Increase by that % ("at your own risk") | Increase 20% (smaller steps at very large budgets) | [L115 05:51–07:13](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md) |
| 6 | Below **breakeven** KPI for 24-48 h? | 7 | 8 | [L115 08:23](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md) |
| 7 | At the minimum daily spend? | Hold budget + out-of-account list | Decrease 20% + out-of-account list | [L115 08:23–11:04](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md) |
| 8 | 48-72 h consistently below **target** (7-day-click data)? | 7 | Wait 24 h | [L115 09:41](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md) |

**Counting hours:** Monday above target = first 24 h → wait; Tuesday above again = 48 h → qualifies. [L115 02:19](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md) With a new winning ad the team still often waits "36 hours" or "48 hours". [L115 07:13](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md)

**Board text (verbatim, frames):**
```text
Has there been 48-72 hours of performance consistently above target KPI? (aka Scaling Protocol)
Or is there a new reason to be scaling?
Increase Budget 20%
Or increase by 50-100% IF 50-100% above target KPI
(For Example - target is 2.5x and performance is at 3.75x aka 50% above target, you can increase budget 50%)
Wait Another 24 Hours
Is more than 60% of purchases from click based attribution data?
Or is Click Based data alone meeting KPI?
Are You Below Breakeven KPI for 24-48 hours?
Has there been 48-72 hours of performance consistently below target KPI (On 7dc Click Based data)?
Are we at the minimum daily spend?
Decrease Budget 20%
Do not decrease spend / And Also:
- Launch New DCT's in queue
- Double check entire funnel for bugs or errors & report to strategist
- Conduct data analysis on ads getting spend and process learnings
- Conduct deep research for new avatars & new mechanisms
- Start Offer Testing
```
Source: [L115 frames 02:10–12:00](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md).

**Minimum daily spend** = "how much you're willing to spend no matter what", set from cash flow and reserves: early $50-$100/day "for 30 days"; most viewers $100-$200/day; a $20k/day brand might set $10k ("we better figure it out"). [L115 09:41–11:04](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md)

**Out-of-account work** (both "hold" and "cut" branches): check the funnel daily after any big change (removed cart code, changed product page, stockouts tanked accounts) [L115 11:04–12:13](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md); offer testing as new ads with a new offer inside the existing campaign, because "the offer that you ran in January is probably not as powerful as it is now in June" [L115 14:32–15:42](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md).

| ABO spend winner | Throttled start $100-$200 ($400-$500 for $4-5k/day ads) | Run 7 days and see | [L097 03:41](../lessons/097-how-to-set-up-abo-scaling-campaign.md) |

| Promo (Shaun) | 50-100%+ above KPI in the window / 20-50% above / at KPI / below | Double / +20% / hold / −20% | [L108 05:57; frame 06:00](../lessons/108-how-to-set-up-a-promo-campaign.md) |
| Promo surf sheet | Blended 4.5+ / 4.0 / 3.75 / 3.5 / 3.0 / 2.0 / 1.5 (target 3.5, breakeven 2.0) | +100% / +50% / +20% / hold / −20% / −50%+ / rework, maybe turn off | [L118 sheet](../lessons/118-advanced-surf-scaling-method.md) |

## 4. Levels of scaling and when budget isn't the lever
| Level (board) | Daily spend | Structure | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | $0-$50k+ | 1 campaign; under $3k/day "lock in on your creatives, lock in on your landing pages" | [L115 15:42–17:57](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md) |
| 2 | $3k-$10k+ | CBOs + raw content (low-intent testing) campaign | [L115 17:57–19:06](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md) |
| 3 | $10k-$50k+ | Named, not explained ("Don't click off if you want to hit $100k/days") | [L115 board](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md) |
| 4 | $50k-$100k+ | Named, not explained | [L115 board](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md) |

Campaign build per level: [campaign structure §2](campaign-structure-322.md#2-why-it-matters-and-the-spend-gates).

- **Scale comes from new winners inside the same campaign.** "We test and scale within the same campaign... scale to a point where your returns are where you desire. Chill there. Test new creatives." (~$20k/week at 4x → ~$60k after one winner, instructor claim.) [L094 30:06–32:06](../lessons/094-how-to-set-up-a-3-2-2-campaign.md)
- **Why budget increases fail:** more budget forces Meta to "expand its reach"; if the creatives only convert the first slice, ROAS falls: "the creatives are just not good enough". [L115 08:23](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md)
- **When stuck:** "If you can't scale your ads... Make a more Ads!" [L119 page](../lessons/119-action-item-scale-your-ads.md); the roller-coaster case scaled back by finding "3 new winning ads... 1 new winning offer... 1 new winning LP". [L121 34:14](../lessons/121-how-to-deal-with-roller-coaster-performance.md)
- **Seasonal lever:** old winners can return in their season (Nov 2023 $45k at 2.36x; reactivated next November → $13k in December, instructor claim). "This is NOT an excuse to stop making new ads." [L106 01:41–04:58; page](../lessons/106-turning-on-seasonal-ads-for-quick-performance.md)

## 5. Kill / keep / scale table
One table for ads, ad sets and campaigns. Kill = turn off at **ad-set level**, never ad level. [L094 page](../lessons/094-how-to-set-up-a-3-2-2-campaign.md) Labels per [winning-ad labels](../concepts/winning-ad-labels.md). Check in this order: spend share → KPI (7-day click) → soft metrics.

| # | Situation | Signal | Decision | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | New test, day 1 | Little spend or weak return | **Keep.** "Don't panic"; day 1 may not convert | [L094 27:02; page](../lessons/094-how-to-set-up-a-3-2-2-campaign.md) |
| 2 | New test, days 1-3 | Spend rising day over day, returns OK | **Keep**, extend to 7 days | [L094 27:02–28:31](../lessons/094-how-to-set-up-a-3-2-2-campaign.md) |
| 3 | New test, day 3 | No traction (no spend) | **Keep to day 7** (default, C2); if ad-set slots are full and a batch is waiting, it goes first (derived) | [L094 27:32; page](../lessons/094-how-to-set-up-a-3-2-2-campaign.md) [L120 page](../lessons/120-how-to-see-if-you-should-turn-off-high-spend-ads.md) |
| 4 | New ad hogs spend at a bad KPI, account still at KPI | e.g. $1.6k of $2.4k at 0.97 on day 2 | **Keep one more day**; Meta often pulls spend itself | [L120 00:41–01:26](../lessons/120-how-to-see-if-you-should-turn-off-high-spend-ads.md) |

**Housekeeping capacity rule:** each ad set should get ~3× CPA per day; when slots run out, kill "not taking spend AND not at KPI" first. [L120 page](../lessons/120-how-to-see-if-you-should-turn-off-high-spend-ads.md)

### High-spender check (run before turning off any top spender)
1. **Frequency** on 7 days: ≤ 1.5 = prospecting; higher = retargeting-leaning, so its ROAS isn't a scaling signal. [L120 07:47](../lessons/120-how-to-see-if-you-should-turn-off-high-spend-ads.md)
2. **Replacement test:** "if this ad is off and its spend is forced to my other top 2-3 ads, would they perform better than the top spender?" [L120 04:25–05:46; page](../lessons/120-how-to-see-if-you-should-turn-off-high-spend-ads.md)
3. **Multiplication test:** if the challenger got 3× its budget ($4k → $12k/day), would it still beat the top spender's 2.49x? "Probably not." [L120 04:25–05:15](../lessons/120-how-to-see-if-you-should-turn-off-high-spend-ads.md)
4. **Account check:** "If the overall account performance is good, then we're good." [L120 06:37](../lessons/120-how-to-see-if-you-should-turn-off-high-spend-ads.md)
5. **Better fix:** make a better ad; old top spenders fade when one arrives ("we just made a better ad"). [L120 10:02](../lessons/120-how-to-see-if-you-should-turn-off-high-spend-ads.md)

## 6. Promo and surf scaling (intra-day)
**Gates:** real sale with a hard end date (skips the 48-72 h wait) [L115 01:01](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md); third-party attribution (Hyros, Triple Whale, Northbeam) or period Shopify revenue, because "Facebook doesn't update their data that fast" [L108 05:57](../lessons/108-how-to-set-up-a-promo-campaign.md); above ~$3,000/day you "can scale more aggressively and self-scale" [L108 05:03](../lessons/108-how-to-set-up-a-promo-campaign.md); evergreen surf only if one campaign already spends "few thousand a day" [L118 page](../lessons/118-advanced-surf-scaling-method.md).

### Check interval (agent default merges both lessons)
| Daily spend | L118 | L108 | Agent default |
|---|---|---|---|
| Few hundred $ | Every 6 h | — | 6 h, ±20% moves only (derived) |
| ~$5k | Every 4 h | < $50k/day: every 4 h (6-8 h fine) | 4 h |
| ~$20k | "You can experiment 4 hours" | Hourly possible (spoken); thread: hourly at 100k+ days | 4 h |
| ~$1M/month (~$33k/day, derived) | "Potentially every 2 hours" | 4 h | 2-4 h |
| ~$100k | "Definitely can do 1 hour" | Hourly | 1 h |

Sources: [L118 02:56–03:55](../lessons/118-advanced-surf-scaling-method.md) [L108 05:57; frame 06:00](../lessons/108-how-to-set-up-a-promo-campaign.md). Don't check too often at low volume: with about one purchase an hour the algorithm can't optimise between edits. [L118 02:56](../lessons/118-advanced-surf-scaling-method.md)

### Period loop (surf sheet)
1. Sheet header: brand, **ad-account time zone** (scale on its clock, not yours), your time zone, breakeven and target blended return. [L118 00:59–01:55](../lessons/118-advanced-surf-scaling-method.md)
2. At account midnight, reset budgets. [L118 03:55](../lessons/118-advanced-surf-scaling-method.md)
3. Each interval, log the **period's** Shopify revenue, Meta revenue, Meta spend, other-channel spend; ROAS = Meta revenue ÷ Meta spend; BROAS = Shopify revenue ÷ total ad spend (derived from sheet rows). [L118 03:55–04:52; sheet](../lessons/118-advanced-surf-scaling-method.md)
4. Decide on blended ("the most accurate one"): Meta good but blended weak → don't scale. [L118 04:52–05:48](../lessons/118-advanced-surf-scaling-method.md)
5. Apply the ladder; write the action in Notes: `<Person> - <campaign> Scaled <+x%> to $<new daily budget>` (e.g. "BFCM US Scaled 50% to $22.3k"). [L118 sheet Notes](../lessons/118-advanced-surf-scaling-method.md)
6. Before account midnight: set tomorrow's start to **half of what was actually spent** (24k → 12k), or leave it if the day was strong. A mid-day 100k budget that only spent 50k will try to spend 100k tomorrow: "you blew your whole ad account". [L118 06:48–07:47](../lessons/118-advanced-surf-scaling-method.md)
7. After the promo, back to a normal budget (sheet: reset to $10k/day the day after BFCM). [L118 sheet](../lessons/118-advanced-surf-scaling-method.md)

### Ladders
| Sheet (target 3.5, breakeven 2.0, blended) | Rescaled to any target T and breakeven B (derived) | Shaun's promo bands (KPI, third-party data) |
|---|---|---|
| 4.5+ → +100% | ≥ 1.29 × T → +100% | 50-100%+ above KPI → double |
| 4.0 → +50% | ≥ 1.14 × T → +50% | 20-50% above → +20% |
| 3.75 → +20% | ≥ 1.07 × T → +20% | — |
| 3.5 → do nothing | = T → hold | At KPI → let it sit |
| 3.0 → 20% pull back | ≈ 0.86 × T → −20% | Below KPI → −20% |
| 2.0 → 50% or more pull back | ≤ B → −50% or more | — |
| 1.5 → "rework overall offer + creatives... may be best to turn ads off" | ≤ 0.75 × B → off / rework | — |

Sheet note: "During hourly checkups at 8am and 4pm, only scale down by 20% if below target. 50-100% if performance is terrible and losing lots of money." [L118 sheet](../lessons/118-advanced-surf-scaling-method.md) Shaun's bands: [L108 05:57; frame 06:00](../lessons/108-how-to-set-up-a-promo-campaign.md). Shaun's reset example: start $1k, scale to $15k, reset to $5k "depending on how well the performance was". [L108 06:47–07:44](../lessons/108-how-to-set-up-a-promo-campaign.md)

## 7. Roller-coaster handling
Example: $1,100 at 3.61 → $1,169 at 2.23 → $1,313 at 0.99 on three straight days. [L121 slides 3-5](../lessons/121-how-to-deal-with-roller-coaster-performance.md)

1. **Check attribution first:** roller-coaster days → look at view-through share. [L116 10:50](../lessons/116-how-to-understand-facebook-ads-attribution.md)
2. **Zoom out:** judge 7-day averages, not single days; daily whiplash between ad sets is normal (batches that "got nothing" for days were 5x and 8x on the days they spent). [L094 page](../lessons/094-how-to-set-up-a-3-2-2-campaign.md) [L120 03:08](../lessons/120-how-to-see-if-you-should-turn-off-high-spend-ads.md)
3. **Name the cause:** real people ("we don't sell to robots"), the market (policy, competitors, tariffs), Meta (unexplained CPM/CPC swings), or your strategy. Act only on strategy. [L121 01:26–07:31](../lessons/121-how-to-deal-with-roller-coaster-performance.md)
4. **Meta anomaly:** CPM/CPC collapse ($35 → $8 CPM; $6 → $0.50 CPC) and an ad suddenly takes spend → pause what hogs spend at a bad KPI, keep launching. [L121 05:12–06:22; 37:47](../lessons/121-how-to-deal-with-roller-coaster-performance.md)
5. **Strategy fix:** "product-aware ads + sophisticated market + trending desire = roller coaster". Add permanent-desire angles, unaware/problem-aware education or entertainment ads, an advertorial or listicle next to the control LP, and stronger real offers. [L121 07:31–30:41](../lessons/121-how-to-deal-with-roller-coaster-performance.md)
6. **Budget during the dip:** follow §2 (−20% unless at minimum spend); "scaled back down to about 6K/day... now we've restable". [L121 32:58–34:14](../lessons/121-how-to-deal-with-roller-coaster-performance.md)
7. **Don't:** test a new media-buying strategy, a new product, change the testing process, or complain ("pure copium"). [L121 00:00–01:26; 35:19](../lessons/121-how-to-deal-with-roller-coaster-performance.md)
8. **Cadence:** Friday review of every winner plus one loser. [L121 50:53–52:19](../lessons/121-how-to-deal-with-roller-coaster-performance.md) → [learnings review](learnings-review.md)

## 12. Cross-course notes
- **Tether Lab (shared):** [creative testing system](../../../../frameworks/creative-testing-system.md) scales a winner at **+10% every other day**, rising to **+10-15% a day** while CPA and CVR hold, and says a frequency of 2 is not fatigue. **Difference:** Evolve's default is +20% per 24 h after 48-72 h above target, with a click-share gate and 20% cuts. **Agreement:** don't kill winners on boredom or frequency; scale only while KPI holds. Default for our accounts: Evolve's flowchart; use Tether's +10% steps for accounts above $10k/day where Evolve also allows 5-15%.
- **Tether Lab [test-and-iterate](../../../../playbooks/test-and-iterate.md):** don't kill a slow-start "Ought" variant in its first ~2 weeks. Evolve's closest rule is "re-check week-1 losers in week 2" and the KPI-winner weeks 2-3 ramp; both argue against day-3 kills.
- **Evolve Copywriting:** [iteration outcomes](../../copywriting/concepts/iteration-outcomes.md) labels spend winners and turns them off at 0.2-0.3x ROAS (S10); the [iteration playbook](../../copywriting/frameworks/iteration-playbook.md) says what to build next for each label.
