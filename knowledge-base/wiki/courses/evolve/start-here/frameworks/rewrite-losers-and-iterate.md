# Rewrite losers and iterate
> Turn a learnings card into the next ad. Route by what the ad showed: a near-winner (spent, then faded) gets a full script rewrite where the mechanism is taught before the product; a concept that spends at soft ROAS climbs a spend-led ladder (claim → reason → research-led new format); a working concept gets three variations changed one element at a time, in priority order hook/imagery → headline → hold/benefits → persona.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** you have a finished [learnings review](learnings-review.md) and must write the next script, static set or iteration batch; deciding how many more tries an angle gets; choosing which element to vary.
- **Inputs → outputs:** learnings card (verdict, top-spend variant, winning hook, what was weak), the original script/creative, account spend by concept, product facts and substantiated claims, customer language (Reddit, reviews) → a routing decision, a rewritten script or 3 variations with one changed element each, named ad sets, and a "why we expect it to win" line.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** let spend pick what to iterate: the stainless-steel static was "the one that we didn't even think would get any spend" and got the most. [L131 03:26-04:10](../lessons/131-how-to-do-iterations-variations-live-example.md)
  - **DON'T** kill a spending concept on one ROAS number (~1.9 here): "That's where you can make the mistake of not going deeper." [L131 04:10](../lessons/131-how-to-do-iterations-variations-live-example.md)
  - **DO** run a rewrite on any ad that "shows something" (spend, decent hook/hold, then dropped): "That ad is telling you something." [L130 00:48](../lessons/130-how-to-rewrite-losing-ads-into-winners.md)
  - **DON'T** introduce the product before the viewer believes the problem; the losing ad "forced the product too soon". Teach the mechanism first. [L130 22:36-26:23](../lessons/130-how-to-rewrite-losing-ads-into-winners.md)
  - **DO** shift the blame from the viewer to a named external cause, then offer the solution to that new problem. [L130 12:16-13:08](../lessons/130-how-to-rewrite-losing-ads-into-winners.md)
  - **DO** write your own diagnosis and instruction (15-45 min) before asking an AI to write; "here's the previous script, here's the learnings, write me a better script" gives "shitty results". [L130 20:22-32:21](../lessons/130-how-to-rewrite-losing-ads-into-winners.md)
  - **DON'T** one-shot a script with AI: "really really hard to one-shot prompt a script for a video ad." Read the draft line by line as the customer and finish it yourself. [L130 32:21-37:41](../lessons/130-how-to-rewrite-losing-ads-into-winners.md)
  - **DO** check the final script in Hemingway: grade 6 or lower. [L130 52:30](../lessons/130-how-to-rewrite-losing-ads-into-winners.md)
  - **DO** keep researching while a concept spends ("the research process never stops"); search Reddit for the exact winning element. [L131 06:07-07:40](../lessons/131-how-to-do-iterations-variations-live-example.md)
  - **DO** iterate on the *reason* something works, not "this image works, let me use it with another headline". [L131 13:41](../lessons/131-how-to-do-iterations-variations-live-example.md)
  - **DO** test three variations per concept: "sometimes it's just your headline that sucks. It could have been a winning ad." [L133 07:24-08:21](../lessons/133-winning-humidifier-ad-learnings-shaun.md)
  - **IF** varying **THEN** change in this order: 1 video hook/imagery, 2 headline/hook text, 3 video hold/benefits, 4 persona. [L133 08:21](../lessons/133-winning-humidifier-ad-learnings-shaun.md)
  - **DO** keep iterating a winner while you still have ideas; if a test doesn't improve it, "reset your lens". [L131 11:30-12:14](../lessons/131-how-to-do-iterations-variations-live-example.md)
  - **IF** an angle lost after a Marksman test and a Sniper batch, and a third execution also fails **THEN** move to a different angle. [L88 33:16-34:04](../lessons/088-the-execution-problem.md)
  - **DON'T** reuse any claim from the course examples (GLP-1 "56%", "only collagen with Dyglomera", "99.99% sterilizable"); substantiate or bracket every claim for our product.

## Loop 1: near-winner rewrite (L130)
Taught by Chef Karlo on a client supplement ad for postpartum women. The losing ad was "a top spender in the account for like seven days" then "dropped off" [L130 02:38-03:35](../lessons/130-how-to-rewrite-losing-ads-into-winners.md).

| # | Step | Do exactly this | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Context | Pull spend and metric context; put the video and its script side by side; set a 20-30 min timer | [L130 16:41; 22:06](../lessons/130-how-to-rewrite-losing-ads-into-winners.md) |
| 2 | Learnings card | Winning hook; why the audience reacted (new mechanism, guilt removed, scientific authority, emotional arc); what was weak ("almost a bit shallow with no narrative") | [L130 07:04-15:46](../lessons/130-how-to-rewrite-losing-ads-into-winners.md) |
| 3 | Avatar state | Record the psychological state, not only the problem ("frustrated", doing everything right, still retaining weight) | [L130 08:02](../lessons/130-how-to-rewrite-losing-ads-into-winners.md) |
| 4 | Diagnose | Check the three failure points below | [L130 22:06-27:18](../lessons/130-how-to-rewrite-losing-ads-into-winners.md) |
| 5 | Write the brief | In your own words: what happened, the transcript, the learnings block, your diagnosis, the instruction (keep / add / format). Budget 30-45 min | [L130 20:22-27:48](../lessons/130-how-to-rewrite-losing-ads-into-winners.md) |
| 6 | Run the script prompt | In a custom AI project loaded with brand info and copywriting resources; a fresh chat "will not work well"; answer its "core idea" question with the brief | [L130 19:23-20:22](../lessons/130-how-to-rewrite-losing-ads-into-winners.md) |
| 7 | Warm up with research | Tell the AI to research the avatar on Reddit before writing; ask for source links; read 5-15 min of threads yourself | [L130 27:48-35:52](../lessons/130-how-to-rewrite-losing-ads-into-winners.md) |
| 8 | State the format | e.g. "execute this new script as an organic text on screen concept", or the AI asks | [L130 29:29-30:23](../lessons/130-how-to-rewrite-losing-ads-into-winners.md) |
| 9 | Human edit | Move the draft into a doc; read line by line as the customer (problem explained? belief shift done? bridge good? emotional journey?); send targeted fixes; finish by hand | [L130 35:52-37:41](../lessons/130-how-to-rewrite-losing-ads-into-winners.md) |
| 10 | Readability | Hemingway grade 6 or lower ("we usually aim for even lower") | [L130 52:30-53:17](../lessons/130-how-to-rewrite-losing-ads-into-winners.md) |
| 11 | Ship and log | New learnings card for the new ad on the same board | [L130 48:54-54:05](../lessons/130-how-to-rewrite-losing-ads-into-winners.md) |

**The three failure points (diagnosis checklist):**

| Failure | Signal in the script | Fix | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product before belief | One line on the cause, then "That's where [product] comes in" around the halfway mark: a "huge disconnect" | Several lines of causal explanation first; product late (new ad: ~1:09-1:20 of ~1:54) | [L130 22:06-27:18; 40:46-41:39](../lessons/130-how-to-rewrite-losing-ads-into-winners.md) |
| Thin or missing mechanism | No mechanism for *why the solution works*; unique ingredient not named | Problem mechanism → solution mechanism → simple analogy → "most alternatives don't do X" → "one ingredient does" → "only we have it" | [L130 45:17-48:04](../lessons/130-how-to-rewrite-losing-ads-into-winners.md) |
| No narrative | Claims in a list | Agree first, educate, then a "gentle" product intro | [L130 53:17-54:05](../lessons/130-how-to-rewrite-losing-ads-into-winners.md) |

**Brief skeleton** (generalised from the brief on screen [L130 21:00-29:30](../lessons/130-how-to-rewrite-losing-ads-into-winners.md); fill with our facts, keep brackets for anything unverified):
```text
We made an ad that had [result: e.g. decent initial performance but died out after a week]. It was a [format] targeting [avatar].
This is the transcript: <script>
<learnings block from the call>
We think this ad got attention because [hook element], positioned as [villain/cause], so the viewer believes the problem is not THEM but [cause].
The biggest issue: [e.g. we introduce the product too quickly and viewers aren't convinced].
So: [be more persuasive about the cause, believable, customer language, mechanism before product, name the unique mechanism earlier].
First, research [avatar] on Reddit: problems, feelings, words they use. Give me source links.
Then write a better script with a similar structure (it showed potential), more persuasive, with narrative, funnelling gradually to the product.
Execute it as [format].
```

**What changed in the winning rewrite** (mechanism patterns; the lines themselves are the client's and must not be reused):

| Before | After | What changed | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook states the mechanism ("10 times the normal levels of the fat-storing hormone") | Counter-market reassurance hook ("Here's why GAINING weight postpartum is NORMAL") | Agree with the viewer and put the market's promise in question; "once they're emotionally invested… we can guide their logic" | [L130 41:39-43:30](../lessons/130-how-to-rewrite-losing-ads-into-winners.md) |
| Blame-shift near the end | Blame-shift at line 2 ("nothing to do with willpower") | Agree early | [L130 43:30](../lessons/130-how-to-rewrite-losing-ads-into-winners.md) |
| One line on the cause | Four causal lines + a second contributing cause found in Reddit research | Specificity, belief | [L130 43:30-45:17](../lessons/130-how-to-rewrite-losing-ads-into-winners.md) |
| No solution mechanism | "Here's what changes everything" → solution mechanism → analogy | Mechanism before product | [L130 45:17-46:15](../lessons/130-how-to-rewrite-losing-ads-into-winners.md) |
| "That's where [product] comes in" | "Most supplements don't… one ingredient does… [product] is the ONLY…" | Bridge via unique mechanism | [L130 46:15-48:04](../lessons/130-how-to-rewrite-losing-ads-into-winners.md) |
| AI voiceover, ~1 min, normal pace | Text on screen + music, no VO, ~1:54, slower | Format/pace; viewers read "with their inner voice" (instructor hypothesis) | [L130 40:46; 48:54-51:36](../lessons/130-how-to-rewrite-losing-ads-into-winners.md) |
| Hype CTA ("lock in your transformation") | Honest CTA ("real results take time") | Anti-hype for a sensitive, saturated market | [L130 48:04-48:54; 53:17-54:05](../lessons/130-how-to-rewrite-losing-ads-into-winners.md) |

Caveat from the instructor: the angle "doesn't mean it's going to do great for every single product and every single market" [L130 54:05-54:59](../lessons/130-how-to-rewrite-losing-ads-into-winners.md). No spend or ROAS is shown for either ad; "winning" rests on the team's classification [L130 43:30](../lessons/130-how-to-rewrite-losing-ads-into-winners.md).

## Loop 2: spend-led iteration ladder (L131)
Stainless-steel humidifier, account went from "about 4.5K a day" to $10K+/day "over the last basically two weeks" [L131 15:16](../lessons/131-how-to-do-iterations-variations-live-example.md) (instructor claim).

| Round | Move | Variable changed | Result (as stated / read from frames) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Big swing: six "superlative" statics, headline = visual (congruence) | Claim/angle | Stainless-steel static got the most spend at ~1.9 ROAS; DCT #133 ~$7.5K at ~1.95 vs Champions ~2.7 (low-res frame) | [L131 01:40-04:10](../lessons/131-how-to-do-iterations-variations-live-example.md) |
| 2 | Iterate the spender: claim → reason ("could we make this ad better? …yes") | Image + benefit sub-line; headline fixed | DCT #137 top spender within ~2 days (round 1 took ~4); frame ~$4.2K at ~3.6 vs Champions ~4.8 | [L131 04:10-05:23](../lessons/131-how-to-do-iterations-variations-live-example.md) |
| 3 | Research (Reddit: people don't know non-plastic humidifiers exist) → "discovery" video, AI voiceover, overlay borrowed from a year-old own UGC winner | Format, hook text | DCT #138: ~$64 → ~$100 (3 purchases) → ~$5K-5.7K on days 1-3; account ~$10.2-10.8K/day | [L131 06:07-13:41](../lessons/131-how-to-do-iterations-variations-live-example.md) |
| 4 | Planned: "did you know there are humidifiers that aren't plastic" video; "world's first" in video | Hook | Not run | [L131 11:30; 14:21](../lessons/131-how-to-do-iterations-variations-live-example.md) |

Rules from the ladder:
- **DO** read spend before ROAS when choosing what to iterate. [L131 03:26](../lessons/131-how-to-do-iterations-variations-live-example.md)
- **IF** you have more than three ideas under one angle **THEN** split them into two ad sets by theme. [L131 02:31-03:26](../lessons/131-how-to-do-iterations-variations-live-example.md)
- **DO** port a small proven element from an older winner in your own account (here an on-screen overlay). [L131 08:31-09:24](../lessons/131-how-to-do-iterations-variations-live-example.md)
- **DO** write voiceovers as a skeptical, relatable peer ("someone finally made one out of stainless steel") rather than a brand claim ("this humidifier is the best"). [L131 10:17-10:55](../lessons/131-how-to-do-iterations-variations-live-example.md)
- **DON'T** expect a minimum budget or slow ramp. [L131 12:14-13:41](../lessons/131-how-to-do-iterations-variations-live-example.md)
- **DO** move from big swings to medium swings once a direction appears. [L131 13:41-14:21](../lessons/131-how-to-do-iterations-variations-live-example.md)

## Loop 3: three variations in priority order (L133)
Shaun's comparison static ("Them vs [brand]") differed across three versions only in the competitor-side callouts; the winner used the ownership pains found in reviews and comments ("constant filter changes", "impossible to clean") and "made over 200,000" (instructor claim; spend or revenue not specified) [L133 00:00; 02:42](../lessons/133-winning-humidifier-ad-learnings-shaun.md).

| Priority | Element | Static | Video | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook / imagery | The image | First 3 seconds | [L133 08:21](../lessons/133-winning-humidifier-ad-learnings-shaun.md) |
| 2 | Headline / hook text | Headline | Text in the first 3 seconds | [L133 08:21](../lessons/133-winning-humidifier-ad-learnings-shaun.md) |
| 3 | Hold / benefits | Benefit callouts | Everything after the hook | [L133 08:21](../lessons/133-winning-humidifier-ad-learnings-shaun.md) |
| 4 | Persona | Different person | Different person | [L133 08:21](../lessons/133-winning-humidifier-ad-learnings-shaun.md) |

Variation grid shown on screen [L133 08:20](../lessons/133-winning-humidifier-ad-learnings-shaun.md): `V1: Headline 1 + Image 1 · V2: Headline 1 + Image 2 · V3: Headline 1 + Image 3`, then `Headline 2 + Image 1`, `Headline 3 + Image 1`.

Rules:
- **DO** iterate only on a concept that is already doing well (Shaun). [L133 board step 5](../lessons/133-winning-humidifier-ad-learnings-shaun.md) (See conflict below.)
- **DO** pick benefit/callout text from customer complaints (Amazon review cons, ad comments, TikTok/YouTube comments): "that's what people actually want". [L133 01:48-02:42](../lessons/133-winning-humidifier-ad-learnings-shaun.md)
- **DO** write "why I think it will win" in the planning sheet before building. [L133 04:31-05:29](../lessons/133-winning-humidifier-ad-learnings-shaun.md)
- **DON'T** copy other brands' styles: "the marketing behind it matters more than how you show it." [L133 05:29](../lessons/133-winning-humidifier-ad-learnings-shaun.md)

## Loop 4: hook fix on a good concept (L122)
- **IF** the concept is good but hook/hold are poor **THEN** "We put too much work into this to just throw it away": write new hooks, quick cut, relaunch. [L122 03:39-05:06](../lessons/122-how-to-do-learnings-part-1-losers.md)
- Hook fixes demonstrated: lead with the answer ("Here's how to enter the flow state"); bridge about the viewer ("here's why you should care" instead of "here's what they found out"); smaller, sharper question ("Is nicotine actually healthy?"); shorter hook and a first clip cut at ~3 s; add the missing avatar callout. [L122 03:39-12:19](../lessons/122-how-to-do-learnings-part-1-losers.md)
- Test shape: three new hooks over the same visual, or three visuals for one hook. [L122 07:36](../lessons/122-how-to-do-learnings-part-1-losers.md)
- **IF** the hook works but hold is poor **THEN** change the body format (storytelling or explanatory text on screen), not the hook. [L122 05:06-06:31](../lessons/122-how-to-do-learnings-part-1-losers.md)

## Output template: iteration plan

```text
Source ad: [batch / DCT #, concept]   Label: [ ]   Loop: [1 | 2 | 3 | 4]
Learning (guess): [ ]
Keep (proven): [hook element / mechanism / format]
Change (one variable per variation unless Loop 1 rewrite): [ ]
Variations:
  V1: [element changed → new value]
  V2: [ ]
  V3: [ ]
Why we expect it to win: [ ]
Claims used + source for each: [ ] (bracket anything unverified)
Readability: Hemingway grade [ ] (target ≤6)
Naming: [DCT/batch number + concept + ITER#[N]_BATCH#[ORIG#]]
Try count on this angle: [1 | 2 | 3] (stop after 3 failed executions)
```
Naming: test numbers by DCT/batch per [Campaign structure 3:2:2](campaign-structure-322.md); iteration token `ITER#[N]_BATCH#[ORIG#]` per [Ten-step iteration learnings](../../copywriting/frameworks/ten-step-iteration-learnings.md).

## Course content to describe, not execute
- The L130 winning ad's claims (GLP-1 boost, "56% in clinical studies", "ONLY collagen supplement that contains…") are a client's claims with FDA-style disclaimers. **Don't reuse.** Use the bridge *structure* only with our own substantiated mechanism. [L130 46:15-48:04](../lessons/130-how-to-rewrite-losing-ads-into-winners.md)
- The L131 overlay reuse is from the team's **own** older winner, which is fine; never port overlays, footage or lines from competitor ads.
- The L131 AI-voiceover script uses first-person experience lines ("this is perfect for my baby girl's nursery", "after using it for a month"). For an AI narrator, **don't execute** human-experience claims; keep it third person or use a real creator who actually used the product (Copywriting gate G6 in the [copy checklist scorecard](../../copywriting/rubrics/copy-checklist-scorecard.md)). [L131 09:24-10:17](../lessons/131-how-to-do-iterations-variations-live-example.md)

## Common mistakes
- Changing many elements at once and learning nothing; L131 round 2 changed only imagery and sub-line. [L131 14:21](../lessons/131-how-to-do-iterations-variations-live-example.md)
- Asking AI for "a better script" with no diagnosis. [L130 31:21-32:21](../lessons/130-how-to-rewrite-losing-ads-into-winners.md)
- Stopping after a successful iteration instead of researching again ("This is where most people stop" [L82 19:48](../lessons/082-the-3-testing-methods-part-2.md); [L131 06:07](../lessons/131-how-to-do-iterations-variations-live-example.md)).
- Re-testing the same angle because nothing was logged. [L133 05:29](../lessons/133-winning-humidifier-ad-learnings-shaun.md)
- Spamming hook iterations instead of real feedback loops ("rather than just being like, let me test new hooks"). [L88 29:15-30:10](../lessons/088-the-execution-problem.md)
- Iterating ads that keep you at current spend rather than studying ads "that are actually scaling further". [L88 29:15-30:10](../lessons/088-the-execution-problem.md)
