# Brand ambassador program
> Turn creators whose ads actually got spend into a standing content team: offer a $500/month retainer (1 video/week) or a tiered performance program (2-3 videos/week), onboard with a deck, agreement, welcome message and three ready ideas, brief them casually in a shared channel, and send each one a monthly report of their own ads. "You process learnings for the creator" ([L156 04:55](../lessons/156-how-to-onboard-brand-ambassadors.md)).

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** a seeding/paid campaign has spend data per creator; deciding whom to keep and what to pay; writing offer emails, onboarding messages, idea briefs or per-creator reports; computing payouts.
- **Inputs → outputs:** per-creator spend/ROAS/purchases (creator name in every ad name), offer choice, top-spend report, ad comments → winner list, offer email, agreement variant, welcome message + 3 ideas, channel briefs, per-creator report with repeat/fix notes, payout rows.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** define a winning creator as one who "actually makes an ad that can scale"; "good is something that gets spent. Bad is something that doesn't get spent." [L154 00:00–00:38](../lessons/154-how-to-contact-winning-creators-affiliate.md)
  - **DON'T** hire creators who are "absolute trash" because they were cheap. [L154 00:00](../lessons/154-how-to-contact-winning-creators-affiliate.md)
  - **DO** send the retainer-only email ($500/month, 1 video/week) when you don't want an affiliate structure; leave room to negotiate ("find some middle ground"). [L154 03:03](../lessons/154-how-to-contact-winning-creators-affiliate.md)
  - **DO** offer the performance program only to creators who will make 2-3 videos/week: "I don't want to pay people that I could just pay 500 bucks a month for one video per week." [L154 03:55–04:40](../lessons/154-how-to-contact-winning-creators-affiliate.md)
  - **DO** keep the agreement **congruent with the email** (revenue vs ad-spend version). [L156 page note](../lessons/156-how-to-onboard-brand-ambassadors.md)
  - **DON'T** treat the course contracts as legal protection: "We are not lawyers ... use it at your own risk." [L156 page text](../lessons/156-how-to-onboard-brand-ambassadors.md) Have a lawyer review (derived).
  - **DO** give three ready ideas from the 30-day top-spend report instead of a script. [L156 02:22–04:11](../lessons/156-how-to-onboard-brand-ambassadors.md)
  - **DO** message ambassadors casually in one channel each (Discord/Slack), "as simple as like sending a Slack to your team"; share sale dates early. [L158 00:00–01:26](../lessons/158-how-to-communicate-with-brand-ambassadors.md)
  - **DO** put the creator's name in every ad name and build one report per creator. [L160 01:27; 08:05](../lessons/160-feedback-loops-with-your-brand-ambassadors.md)
  - **IF** the creator is not on revenue or ad-spend share **THEN** hide spend, ROAS and purchase value: "they're probably going to ask more". [L160 03:15](../lessons/160-feedback-loops-with-your-brand-ambassadors.md) (L156 hides only purchase value; see Conflicts.)
  - **DON'T** assume organic winners are ad winners: "most of the time it's actually not". [L160 00:46](../lessons/160-feedback-loops-with-your-brand-ambassadors.md)
  - **DO** mine the ad's comments for new video ideas. [L160 06:30–08:05](../lessons/160-feedback-loops-with-your-brand-ambassadors.md)
  - **DO** pay on a fixed schedule (1st and 15th) and log every payout. [L154 02:14](../lessons/154-how-to-contact-winning-creators-affiliate.md)
  - **DON'T** let the "what's in it for you" earnings line or report numbers become ad claims; creators' scripts claim only their own experience (repo rule 2).
- **See also:** [Insense creator pipeline](insense-creator-pipeline.md) · [Content engine](../concepts/content-engine.md) · [Creator frameworks vs scripts](../concepts/creator-frameworks-vs-scripts.md) · [Whitelisting and partnership ads](../concepts/whitelisting-and-partnership-ads.md) · [Learnings review](learnings-review.md) · [KPIs and columns](kpis-and-columns.md) · [Creator program setup](../playbooks/creator-program-setup.md)

## Procedure

### Stage 1: Identify winning creators
1. Pull per-creator spend, ROAS and purchases from the raw content campaign ([L98 00:00–00:50](../lessons/098-how-to-set-up-raw-content-campaign.md); columns in [KPIs and columns](kpis-and-columns.md)).
2. Classify:

| Evidence | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Ad took real spend at acceptable ROAS (e.g. $30,000 at 2x) | Winning creator → offer | [L154 00:00](../lessons/154-how-to-contact-winning-creators-affiliate.md) |
| Likeable content, negligible spend (e.g. $66, one likely view-through purchase) | Don't offer | [L154 00:38](../lessons/154-how-to-contact-winning-creators-affiliate.md) |
| A good but not "super winner" first video | Hire, give feedback; the second video "made a super winner" (instructor claim) | [L137 05:42](../lessons/137-does-insense-still-work.md) |
| No winning ad, but content that "impressed you enough to hire them" | Eligible per page text; retainer only, no whitelisting until a winning ad (derived) | [L157 page text](../lessons/157-action-item-onboard-brand-ambassador.md) |

### Stage 2: Make the offer
| Offer | Volume | Pay | When | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Retainer only | 1 video/week | $500/month (some accept $400; some counter $750) + product + channel access | Default; simple | [L154 03:03](../lessons/154-how-to-contact-winning-creators-affiliate.md) |
| Performance program | 2-3 videos/week | $500 base + tiered % of revenue *or* ad spend from their videos, cap $10,000/month | To "double and triple their output" | [L154 05:53](../lessons/154-how-to-contact-winning-creators-affiliate.md) |
| Whitelisting add-on | — | $100-$250+/month access | Winning creators on retainer | [L162 03:46](../lessons/162-whitelisting-overview-proof-it-works.md) |
| Framework teaser (pre-hire) | first video | $500/month retainer if first video wins + affiliate + % of ad spend | In the creator framework's mission | [L148 03:34–04:23](../lessons/148-sending-frameworks-to-creators-part-2.md) |

**Performance tiers** (calendar month, resets monthly) [L154 email](../lessons/154-how-to-contact-winning-creators-affiliate.md); [L156 contract](../lessons/156-how-to-onboard-brand-ambassadors.md):

| Slice of monthly revenue / ad spend from the creator's videos | Rate | Slice payout | Cumulative performance pay |
|---|---|---|---|
| First $10,000 | 10% | $1,000 | $1,000 |
| $10,000-$30,000 | 5% | $1,000 | $2,000 |
| $30,000-$70,000 | 2.5% | $1,000 | $3,000 |
| Above $70,000 | 1% | varies | total payout capped at $10,000/month |

Payout calculator (derived from the tiers): `perf = 0.10·min(G,10k) + 0.05·clamp(G−10k,0,20k) + 0.025·clamp(G−30k,0,40k) + 0.01·max(G−70k,0)`; `total = min(500 + perf, 10,000)`. Course examples: G = $70,000 → $3,500; G = $100,000 → $3,800. To pay on ad spend, change "your video generates" to "your video spends". [L154 page note](../lessons/154-how-to-contact-winning-creators-affiliate.md) Payout calculations are done in Atria. [L154 06:52](../lessons/154-how-to-contact-winning-creators-affiliate.md)

Email framing: show the tiers and the two worked examples, don't lead with "scary" numbers, and say creators don't have to post on their own profile ("film the videos, upload them to the drive, and we'll take it from there"). [L154 05:14](../lessons/154-how-to-contact-winning-creators-affiliate.md) The email's line "We already have creators making videos that are GENERATING/SPENDING $100,000's of dollars per month" is a factual claim. Send it only if true for your brand; otherwise delete it (repo rule 2).

### Stage 3: Onboard
| # | Send | Content | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Onboarding deck | Welcome; content submission (videos per week, upload to Drive, submit via the Discord form); payment and monthly performance report; review timeline; who to contact; "winning video formula"; "Let's start creating" (join Discord, review framework concepts, submit first video on time) | [L156 00:00–00:40](../lessons/156-how-to-onboard-brand-ambassadors.md) |
| 2 | Agreement | Variant matching the email: % revenue, % ad spend, or % ad spend + whitelisting | [L156 01:33; PDFs](../lessons/156-how-to-onboard-brand-ambassadors.md) |
| 3 | Welcome message | Template below | [L156 page text](../lessons/156-how-to-onboard-brand-ambassadors.md) |
| 4 | First Loom + report | Atria "Top spend ads", last 30 days, shared link (check in incognito); three ideas | [L156 02:22–04:11](../lessons/156-how-to-onboard-brand-ambassadors.md) |
| 5 | Framework | Current framework or a new one for that creator | [L156 04:11](../lessons/156-how-to-onboard-brand-ambassadors.md) |
| 6 | Payout sheet row | Pay 1st and 15th | [L154 01:25–02:14](../lessons/154-how-to-contact-winning-creators-affiliate.md) |

Agreement clauses (all three variants): ≥2 videos/week; $500 base in two payments (1st, 15th); tiers above; $10,000 monthly cap; return products within 14 days or reimburse if deliverables aren't met; confidentiality; client owns all content; independent contractor; mutual indemnification; month-to-month, 14 days' written notice. The whitelisting variant adds Facebook Page and Instagram access. Full text: [L156](../lessons/156-how-to-onboard-brand-ambassadors.md).

### Stage 4: Brief continuously
| Brief type | Contents | Example | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sale heads-up | Date (even before it's live), offer PDF, tag the brand contact | "we're going live in 7-10 days" | [L158 00:00](../lessons/158-how-to-communicate-with-brand-ambassadors.md) |
| Concept idea | Working title, framing question, avatar facts, objection to own, "from your POV" | "is it actually worth it?" for an expensive product | [L158 00:45](../lessons/158-how-to-communicate-with-brand-ambassadors.md) |
| Report-driven ideas | What is spending now → sale video, closeout video, reaction to the founder's winning ad | hockey-gear brand | [L156 03:21–04:11](../lessons/156-how-to-onboard-brand-ambassadors.md) |
| Comment-driven idea | Theme from the ad's comments → new video | gift comments → "gifts my husband got me that I actually love" | [L160 07:15–08:05](../lessons/160-feedback-loops-with-your-brand-ambassadors.md) |

Accept the creator's additions (in the example she found real news headlines) and let them run: "you want someone who can execute things without you having to tell them every single thing". Give more guidance only to creators who need it. [L158 00:45–01:26](../lessons/158-how-to-communicate-with-brand-ambassadors.md)

### Stage 5: Feedback loop (monthly per creator)
1. Creator name in every ad name. [L160 01:27](../lessons/160-feedback-loops-with-your-brand-ambassadors.md)
2. Atria → Custom report → filter "Ad name contains <creator>" → group by ad name → 30 or 90 days. [L160 01:27–02:20](../lessons/160-feedback-loops-with-your-brand-ambassadors.md) Atria is optional; Google Slides, a Loom or notes also work. [L161 page text](../lessons/161-action-item-complete-your-feedback-loops.md); [L160 05:09](../lessons/160-feedback-loops-with-your-brand-ambassadors.md)
3. Choose metrics by pay type:

| Creator pay type | Show | Hide | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue or ad-spend share | Spend, ROAS, purchase value, plus soft metrics | — | [L160 03:15](../lessons/160-feedback-loops-with-your-brand-ambassadors.md); [L156 02:22](../lessons/156-how-to-onboard-brand-ambassadors.md) |
| Flat retainer (agent default) | Thumbstop, hold rate, click to purchase, CTR (link click), 15s/3s retention, shares, average play time | Spend, ROAS, purchase value | [L160 03:15; 05:09–06:30](../lessons/160-feedback-loops-with-your-brand-ambassadors.md) |

4. Diagnose and write notes:

| Pattern | Note to creator | Source |
|---|---|---|
| High thumbstop, weak hold | "Keep the suspense going"; same product, new video | [L156 05:37](../lessons/156-how-to-onboard-brand-ambassadors.md) |
| Similar thumbstop, one hook converts ~2x | Keep the hook that attracts buyers (mom tying skates vs kid skating) | [L160 03:15](../lessons/160-feedback-loops-with-your-brand-ambassadors.md) |
| Low thumbstop but big spend and ROAS | Thumbstop alone doesn't decide; repeat the winner's format | (derived) [L160 02:30 frame](../lessons/160-feedback-loops-with-your-brand-ambassadors.md) |
| High shares | "People are really relating with this content": make more of it | [L160 05:09](../lessons/160-feedback-loops-with-your-brand-ambassadors.md) |
| Video is working | "Can you do another one of these?" | [L156 04:55](../lessons/156-how-to-onboard-brand-ambassadors.md) |

   Note format (derived from L160): best ad and why; weakest metric and the fix; one new idea from comments.
5. Share the report link with a Loom or notes; repeat monthly (the deck promises a report on the last day of the month). [L156 00:00](../lessons/156-how-to-onboard-brand-ambassadors.md)

### Stage 6: Extend
- Whitelisting or partnership ads for ambassadors' top creatives: [whitelisting and partnership ads](../concepts/whitelisting-and-partnership-ads.md).
- At $300k-$3M/month, turn the best seeders into "mini creative strategists" and retain winning creators as ambassadors. [L175 09:44–11:39](../lessons/175-what-to-focus-on-to-scale-to-10m-mth-by-rev.md)

## Templates (verbatim)
Retainer-only email [L154 page text](../lessons/154-how-to-contact-winning-creators-affiliate.md):
```text
Hi [CREATORS NAME],
I'm [NAME], from [YOUR COMPANY], [DESCRIPTION OF YOUR COMPANY]. We loved your work from Insense and would like to discuss a potential partnership with you!
Here's what we're thinking:
* 1 Video Per Week: You'd create 1 video per week featuring our products.
* Product: We'll send you all the products you need to continue to make your videos
* Payment: We'd like to offer you a $500 monthly payment for your work.
We'd also invite you into our DISCORD/SLACK with the rest of our team for fast communication, brainstorming new ideas, and providing you real-time feedback on how your videos perform.
I look forward to hearing back from you and hopefully partnering together!
```
Performance email: verbatim in [L154](../lessons/154-how-to-contact-winning-creators-affiliate.md#prompts--templates-verbatim) (tiers and examples as above; ends "Let me know if you're game to get started on either the retainer only or the performance program, and I'll send you the agreement.").

Welcome message [L156 page text](../lessons/156-how-to-onboard-brand-ambassadors.md):
```text
Hey NAME welcome in! We're excited to start creating more winning ads with you.
Here's our latest breakdown of our winning ads to help you strategize and ideate for your next vids: LOOM LINK & CANVA LINK
Here's our latest framework where you can find new angles & talking points: LINK
You can upload all videos here so it wont take space on your Drive: LINK
We generally expect 1 video a week as a normal setup, but feel free to send through whenever you have them ready. We will setup a bi-weekly payment schedule as well, and a monthly report on how your ads performed.
Lmk if you have any questions!
```
Concept brief in channel (structure from [L158 01:20 frame](../lessons/158-how-to-communicate-with-brand-ambassadors.md)):
```text
for another video idea @CREATOR (after the sale video)
we'd like to explore "[concept title]" style video
And I think for you, you could do a video around - "[framing question]"
The key to this will be making sure you point out [avatar facts]...
But, ---> then go into [core message / objection to own].
I'll let @[BRAND CONTACT] jam in on this as well ... coming from your POV (and talking about whatever feels natural) would be awesome
```
Payout sheet columns [L154 xlsx](../lessons/154-how-to-contact-winning-creators-affiliate.md): `Creator Name | Payout # | Payout Date | Monthly Retainer | Retainer Pay | Performance Pay | Whitelisting Fee | Total Pay | PayPal Email | Status | Notes` ($500 retainer = $250 per payout).

## Conflicts (logged in contradictions notes)
- **Videos per week for the $500 base.** L154 email: 1/week; L156 welcome message: "1 video a week as a normal setup"; L156 contract: "minimum of two (2) videos per week"; L162 [03:46]: agreement "$500/month for four videos" plus whitelisting. **Default:** retainer = 1/week; performance = 2-3/week; edit the contract to match the email sent.
- **Eligibility.** L154/L155: only creators whose ads scaled. L157 page: a winning ad *or* content that impressed you. **Default:** winning ad for performance offers and whitelisting; "impressive content" only for a retainer trial.
- **Metrics shown to flat-retainer creators.** L156 [02:22]: remove purchase value, show spend, thumbstop, hold. L160 [03:15] / L161: hide spend, ROAS and purchase value. **Default:** hide all money metrics.
- **Paying per video.** L135 [05:26]: pay winners $100-$150/video. L175 [10:41]: per-video pay isn't sustainable as the main model. **Default:** monthly retainer or performance program for winners; per-video pay only in paid Insense campaigns.
