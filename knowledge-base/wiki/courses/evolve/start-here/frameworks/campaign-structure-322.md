# Campaign structure 3:2:2 (Evolve Meta account map)
> One broad Sales CBO ("acquisition") is the whole account at low spend; every new concept enters it as one ad set of 3 manual ads × 2 copies × 2 headlines, and support campaigns (3:2:2:2 page tests, zombie cost-cap, ABO testing/scaling, raw content, promo) are added only when daily spend and content justify them.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** building or auditing a Meta account, writing a launch spec for a media buyer, deciding which campaign a new ad, a winner, a loser, a raw creator video, a new landing page or a sale goes into, or naming anything in Ads Manager.
- **Inputs → outputs:** daily spend, target CPA (from [KPIs and columns](kpis-and-columns.md)), country/age/gender, repeat-customer rate, the creative batch (concept, format, 3 visuals, 2 copies, 2 headlines), landing pages, labelled winners/losers ([winning-ad labels](../concepts/winning-ad-labels.md)), raw/seeded creator content, sale dates → a campaign roster for that spend tier, exact settings per level, ad-set layout, starting budgets, names and UTMs, and a launch checklist.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** run one fully broad acquisition CBO and test every new ad inside it: "You do not have to have separate campaigns... a testing campaign or a scaling campaign or audience this or audience that." [L092 00:00–00:51](../lessons/092-evolve-facebook-ads-philosophy.md) [L093 00:00](../lessons/093-full-media-buying-strategy-outline-2026.md)
  - **DO** build it as Sales, manual, CBO on, ≥ $100/day, website only, maximize number of conversions, Dynamic Creative/flexible off, start at midnight account time. [L094 00:00–02:47](../lessons/094-how-to-set-up-a-3-2-2-campaign.md)
  - **DO** put exactly one concept per ad set: 3 manual ads (same format), the same 2 primary texts and 2 headlines. "DCTs are gone", so this is 3 unique ads. [L094 11:55](../lessons/094-how-to-set-up-a-3-2-2-campaign.md) [L093 00:58](../lessons/093-full-media-buying-strategy-outline-2026.md)
  - **DON'T** force spend on ads Meta ignored by moving them to an ABO: forced ads ran at about 0.5x vs 3-4x in the main campaign (instructor claim). [L092 02:29–04:05](../lessons/092-evolve-facebook-ads-philosophy.md)
  - **DON'T** add support campaigns below their spend gate (table in §2): under $1k/day max 5 new test ad sets; under $2k/day no 3:2:2:2; ABO scaling needs a media buyer. [L093 sheet](../lessons/093-full-media-buying-strategy-outline-2026.md) [L097 page text](../lessons/097-how-to-set-up-abo-scaling-campaign.md)
  - **DON'T** purge running campaigns when launching this structure; add it "alongside whatever campaigns you already have". [L099 page text](../lessons/099-action-item-launch-your-campaigns.md)
  - **DON'T** set ad-set min/max spend limits in normal testing ("it just spends more at lower KPI"); the only exception is the $10k+/day overspend cap (§5.7). [L094 22:10–23:06](../lessons/094-how-to-set-up-a-3-2-2-campaign.md) [L107 page text](../lessons/107-how-to-protect-against-bad-ads-over-spending.md)
  - **IF** a test loses after 7 days (no spend, misses KPI) **THEN** turn its ad set off and duplicate its best ad into the zombie campaign. [L096 02:19](../lessons/096-how-to-set-up-zombie-graveyard-campaign.md)
  - **IF** an ad wins (breakthrough / spend winner / KPI winner) and spend is ≥ $2k/day with a media buyer **THEN** give it its own ABO ad set and keep the original on in the CBO. [L097 02:53–05:17](../lessons/097-how-to-set-up-abo-scaling-campaign.md)
  - **DO** reuse any winner by native duplication with "show existing reactions" ticked; post IDs only as fallback. [L103 01:13](../lessons/103-how-to-duplicate-ads-into-champions-new-way.md) [L104 page text](../lessons/104-how-to-find-any-post-ids-for-winning-ads.md)
  - **DON'T** run ripped or downloaded third-party videos in the raw campaign (course tactic, ⛔ don't execute); run only content our creators made under agreement. [L098 01:46](../lessons/098-how-to-set-up-raw-content-campaign.md) → [rules X01](../libraries/rules.md)

## 1. Definition
Evolve's media buying is "the one campaign strategy with the CBO", which "has evolved quite a lot over the last two years" ([L093 00:00](../lessons/093-full-media-buying-strategy-outline-2026.md)):

- **Core:** one broad **acquisition CBO**. It prospects, retargets and covers mid-funnel on its own; the campaign level only controls budget: "this is my budget, Facebook. Here it is and I want you to spend it." [L092 00:00–00:51](../lessons/092-evolve-facebook-ads-philosophy.md)
- **Test unit (3:2:2):** one concept = one ad set = 3 creatives × 2 body copies × 2 headlines. Spend inside the CBO is the verdict: "Spend = Good Ad, No Spend = Bad Ad" (slide). [L092 05:00; slide 02:00–05:50](../lessons/092-evolve-facebook-ads-philosophy.md)
- **Variants and support campaigns (2026 sheet):** 3:2:2:2 (adds a 2nd landing page), Page # (winners on a new page), ABO testing/scaling, zombie cost-cap, raw content (flexible ads), promo. [L093 sheet](../lessons/093-full-media-buying-strategy-outline-2026.md)

The same idea in the ad-definition vocabulary: concept, angle, variation and format are defined in [ad definitions](../concepts/ad-definitions.md); how to fill the 3 slots (3 angles vs 1 angle × 3 executions) is [Marksman / Sniper testing](../concepts/marksman-sniper-testing.md).

## 2. Why it matters, and the spend gates
New ads face "the best of the best. There is no warmup"; no budget goes to playground pre-tests, and "you don't have to have massive budgets to test a lot of creatives". [L092 00:51–01:48; 06:37](../lessons/092-evolve-facebook-ads-philosophy.md) The support campaigns add complexity that "for a lot of you guys [spending] less than two or three K a day, you don't really need to worry too much about". [L093 00:00](../lessons/093-full-media-buying-strategy-outline-2026.md)

**Capacity (how many test ad sets):**
- Few hundred dollars a day: max 5 ad sets at once (champions counted when it held ads); "$50,000 a day, you can definitely have more." [L094 23:36–25:29](../lessons/094-how-to-set-up-a-3-2-2-campaign.md)
- Concepts per week = budget ÷ target CPA ($100/day ÷ $20 = 5), "considering 100% of your budget is testing". [L094 page FAQ](../lessons/094-how-to-set-up-a-3-2-2-campaign.md)
- Each ad set should get about 3× CPA per day: $30 CPA → $90/ad set → $900/day → 10 ad sets; when full, kill the worst (no spend and off KPI) first. [L120 page](../lessons/120-how-to-see-if-you-should-turn-off-high-spend-ads.md)
- **Agent default:** active test ad sets = daily budget ÷ (3 × target CPA), capped at 5 under $1k/day (derived; conflict C3 in [kpi-thresholds](../libraries/kpi-thresholds.md#conflicting-values-side-by-side)).

### 5.3 Landing-page tests (3:2:2:2 and Page #)
1. Gate: ≥ $2k/day for 3:2:2:2; below that, one page per batch. [L093 sheet](../lessons/093-full-media-buying-strategy-outline-2026.md)
2. **3:2:2:2 (new batch):** build the 3 ads on page A; duplicate the 3 and change only the URL to page B; 6 ads in one ad set; put the page in the ad name (`LP: <name>`). [L095 02:16–04:46](../lessons/095-how-to-test-landing-pages-3-2-2-2.md)
3. **Page # (existing winners):** duplicate the top 3-5 ads (or the 2-4 that fit the page's angle) into a `Page #n` ad set, change the URL, launch in the main campaign. [L095 page](../lessons/095-how-to-test-landing-pages-3-2-2-2.md) [L093 01:45–02:47](../lessons/093-full-media-buying-strategy-outline-2026.md)
4. Pair choices: listicle or advertorial vs control, product page vs homepage, collection vs product page. Unaware and problem-aware ads should get a control LP plus a listicle or advertorial. [L095 04:46](../lessons/095-how-to-test-landing-pages-3-2-2-2.md) [L121 23:20–25:50](../lessons/121-how-to-deal-with-roller-coaster-performance.md)
5. Read spend per page after the normal test window, then returns. If you still test pages in ABO, drip the winner into the main campaign after 7 days and watch it ("it might suck up spending"). [L095 06:15](../lessons/095-how-to-test-landing-pages-3-2-2-2.md)

### 5.5 ABO testing/scaling campaign
**Gate:** "THIS WILL NOT FIX YOUR BAD ADS. IF YOU ARE LESS THAN $1K/DAY IN SPEND, FOCUS MORE ON WINNING ADS. ALSO, IF YOU DO NOT HAVE A MEDIA BUYER, WE RECOMMEND GETTING ONE BEFORE DOING THIS." [L097 page text](../lessons/097-how-to-set-up-abo-scaling-campaign.md) Agent default: ≥ $2k/day + media buyer (C4).

1. Create → Sales; CBO toggle **off**; untick "Share some of your budget with other ad sets" (optional per the speaker). [L097 15:35](../lessons/097-how-to-set-up-abo-scaling-campaign.md)
2. One ad set per winner, duplicated with the same setup as the CBO ad sets; name it after the actual ad, not "spend winner". [L097 06:14; 15:47](../lessons/097-how-to-set-up-abo-scaling-campaign.md)
3. Budgets per §4 (5-20% of total daily budget; "remember you can always scale up"). $100-$200/day starts are fine even on a $10-15k/day account. [L097 06:14–07:02](../lessons/097-how-to-set-up-abo-scaling-campaign.md)
4. Keep the original live in the CBO: "NEVER TURN OFF ANY OF THESE ADS IN THE MAIN CBO AFTER YOU DUPLICATE TO ABO (unless obviously performance is terrible)". [L097 page text](../lessons/097-how-to-set-up-abo-scaling-campaign.md)
5. Optional test ad sets with the top proven ads (main CBO assumed on 7-day click):

| Ad set | Exclusions | Attribution | Purpose | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hard Exclusions | Website visitors, add-to-carts, page engagers (365 days), past customers: "every possible interaction" | 7-day click | Force net-new reach | [L097 09:50; page](../lessons/097-how-to-set-up-abo-scaling-campaign.md) |
| 1-Day Click | Purchasers only | 1-day click | "More conservative", limits over-attribution | [L097 10:34; page](../lessons/097-how-to-set-up-abo-scaling-campaign.md) |
| Incremental | "Very strong exclusions" | Meta incremental attribution | Net-new acquisition; judge in Northbeam/Triple Whale, not Meta | [L097 10:34–11:33](../lessons/097-how-to-set-up-abo-scaling-campaign.md) |

6. Judge with blended sales: "are we seeing more total Shopify sales or are we just spending more?" [L097 12:17](../lessons/097-how-to-set-up-abo-scaling-campaign.md) Scaling the ABO ad sets: [scaling rules §3](scaling-rules.md#3-increment-table-how-much-to-move).
7. Expect overlap: scaling an ABO ad set hard can stop the same ad spending in the CBO. [L097 14:48](../lessons/097-how-to-set-up-abo-scaling-campaign.md)

- **Purpose:** more volume, forced spend on creator ads to find the winning creator (a hiring lead for the ambassador program), and keeping low-intent ads from competing with "high intent ads" so learnings stay clean. [L098 00:00–01:46](../lessons/098-how-to-set-up-raw-content-campaign.md)

[L098 page](../lessons/098-how-to-set-up-raw-content-campaign.md) [L115 17:57](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md) (conflict C5)
- **Settings:** identical to the acquisition CBO: "budget setup, UTM setup, ad set targeting exactly the same". [L098 03:36](../lessons/098-how-to-set-up-raw-content-campaign.md)
- **Layout:** 2026: flexible ads with 3-6 visuals, 2 copies, 2 headlines, 1 landing page, one ad set; breakdowns don't work, so read the winning asset from the Meta post view. [L093 02:47–05:16](../lessons/093-full-media-buying-strategy-outline-2026.md) 2025: one video cut 3 ways (same video, 3 start points, no new text or music), or 3 different videos per ad set. [L098 02:44–03:36](../lessons/098-how-to-set-up-raw-content-campaign.md)
- ⛔ The course also puts "ripped ads" here. [L098 01:46](../lessons/098-how-to-set-up-raw-content-campaign.md) Don't execute. Compliant alternative: log the ripped ad's hook, demo and structure, then brief our own creators.

### 5.7 Overspend protection (≥ $10k/day, or repeat overspenders)
1. Each new test ad set: Edit → Spending limits → Add set spending limit → daily maximum you can lose "no matter what" (example $500/day, "even if it's a new winner"; $500 suits $15-30k/day accounts). [L107 05:02; page](../lessons/107-how-to-protect-against-bad-ads-over-spending.md)
2. Next day: remove the cap if performance is strong, or if the ad got no spend in the first 1-2 days. Editing may re-enter learning. [L107 06:00–06:57](../lessons/107-how-to-protect-against-bad-ads-over-spending.md)
3. Meta rejects performance-based auto turn-off rules on CBO ad sets ("Auto Kill": spent > $500, ROAS < 1.2, < 24 h fails). [L107 00:51–02:48](../lessons/107-how-to-protect-against-bad-ads-over-spending.md)

### 5.9 Champions, duplication and post IDs
- **Status:** the 2025 method kept a `Champions` ad set of proven winners as consolidation and benchmark ("If my creatives can't outspend the champions at KPI, they are not new winners"). Agree: niche tests stay in the main CBO below that.
- **Tether Lab (shared):** [creative testing system](../../../../frameworks/creative-testing-system.md) runs 3-5 new ads a week, "launch 3 → keep 1 → add 3", and one learning goal per campaign; the [test-and-iterate playbook](../../../../playbooks/test-and-iterate.md) structures campaigns by TEEP stage with ad sets per emotion. **Difference:** Evolve puts every test in one broad CBO, one concept per ad set, and lets spend share decide. **Agreement:** both use 3 creatives per test unit and keep one variable when iterating a proven winner. Default for our brands: Evolve's account structure; Tether's one-learning-goal rule when choosing what the 3 creatives vary.
