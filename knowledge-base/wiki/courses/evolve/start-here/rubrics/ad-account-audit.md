# Ad account audit
> Two-part audit for any Meta account with spend history: **Part 1** is Evolve's "Media Buyer: Creative Audit" (top prospecting and retargeting ads over 30 days and 6 months, hook and hold leaders, winners to carry over); **Part 2** scores the account against the Evolve media-buying system (structure, measurement, test hygiene, scaling discipline) with spend gates, so an agent can say exactly what to keep, fix, add or skip.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** onboarding a new or inherited ad account; a brand asks "why won't my account scale?"; before launching the first Evolve 3:2:2 campaign into an existing account; a quarterly health check.
- **Inputs → outputs:** Ads Manager access or exports (ad-level, last 30 days and last 6 months, with spend, purchases, CPA/ROAS, frequency, hook, hold, CTR, LPV), daily spend, gross margin inputs, attribution setting, campaign list → (1) a filled creative-audit block with share links, (2) a scored check sheet (Pass / Fail / N/A per check, ⛔ gates first), (3) a prioritised fix list routed to the right page, (4) the "winners to carry over" list for the new campaign.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** rank winners by "highest spend and lowest CPA... or best ROAS", filtered by spend so the data has volume. [L46 01:36](../lessons/046-ad-account-research.md)
  - **DO** run both windows (last 30 days, last 6 months) for prospecting and retargeting. [L46 00:00-00:55](../lessons/046-ad-account-research.md) [L46 05:02](../lessons/046-ad-account-research.md)
  - **DO** judge on money first: "We only care about ads that are getting spend and ads that are getting results." [L100 00:00](../lessons/100-how-to-see-ads-performance-our-columns.md)
  - **DON'T** trust hook or hold without purchases: "We've seen ads with insane hook and hold rates just not perform at all." [L100 13:08](../lessons/100-how-to-see-ads-performance-our-columns.md)
  - **DON'T** recommend purging old campaigns: "We do NOT recommend purging and turning off all of your existing campaigns." Add the new campaign alongside. [L99 page](../lessons/099-action-item-launch-your-campaigns.md)
  - **DON'T** trust the previous agency's structure; check duplicates and missing post IDs. [L46 01:36-02:18](../lessons/046-ad-account-research.md)
  - **DON'T** score an account Fail for missing advanced structures it is too small for: "less than two or three K a day, you don't really need to worry too much about this." [L93 00:00](../lessons/093-full-media-buying-strategy-outline-2026.md)
  - **IF** the account cannot scale **THEN** count new ads launched in the last 7 days before proposing any media-buying change ("Make a more Ads"). [L119 page](../lessons/119-action-item-scale-your-ads.md)
  - **IF** a check depends on spend **THEN** apply the gate column; below the gate the check is N/A.
  - **DON'T** copy a competitor's or another brand's assets into the account; audit findings feed new briefs (copy the mechanism, not the asset).
- **See also:** [Execution checklist](execution-checklist.md) · [Weekly media buying routine](../playbooks/weekly-media-buying-routine.md) · [Campaign structure 3:2:2](../frameworks/campaign-structure-322.md) · [KPIs and columns](../frameworks/kpis-and-columns.md) · [Scaling rules](../frameworks/scaling-rules.md) · [KPI thresholds](../libraries/kpi-thresholds.md) · [Rules library](../libraries/rules.md) · [Winning-ad labels](../concepts/winning-ad-labels.md) · [Click-based attribution](../concepts/click-based-attribution.md) · Tether Lab: [creative testing system](../../../../frameworks/creative-testing-system.md), [landing page scorecard](../../../../rubrics/landing-page-scorecard.md)

## How to score
1. **Part 1 first** (creative audit). It produces facts, not a score; Part 2 uses them.
2. **Part 2: ⛔ gates G1-G4.** Any gate Fail = "measurement not trustworthy": fix it before reading performance or giving scaling advice.
3. Score each check S/M/P/H row **Pass (1) / Fail (0) / N/A** (N/A when below its spend gate or the item doesn't exist, e.g. no promo running). Quote the evidence (setting, number, ad name) for every Fail.
4. **Bands (derived; the course gives none):** ≥ 85% of applicable checks = healthy, run the [weekly routine](../playbooks/weekly-media-buying-routine.md); 65-84% = fix the Fails this week in the order of the [fix routing table](#fix-routing); < 65% = rebuild per [campaign structure 3:2:2](../frameworks/campaign-structure-322.md), launched beside the old campaigns.
5. Finish with the [output template](#output-template).
