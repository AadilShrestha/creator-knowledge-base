# 50. Action Item/ Objections & Other Desires
> A checkpoint page: fill in alternative solutions, beliefs and objections, and "problems we have to solve" in your research doc before cleaning it up.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** lessons 48-49 are done and the research doc's "Other solutions", "Day To Day Struggles", "Beliefs & Objections" and "Problems we have to solve while marketing" sections are empty.
- **Inputs → outputs:** research already collected (reviews, Reddit, YouTube, Ad Library, Foreplay, TikTok, comments) → those four sections filled (derived from lessons 48-49).
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** treat this as a gate: "Take Action... Don't just consume" (page text).
  - **DO** write every objection as a "How do we..." problem with a candidate answer (from [49](049-understanding-objections.md) at 00:00-00:52).
  - **DON'T** begin [51. Cleaning Up The Research Doc](051-cleaning-up-the-research-doc.md) with those sections empty. (derived)
- **See also:** [48. Understanding Other Desires](048-understanding-other-desires.md) · [49. Understanding Objections](049-understanding-objections.md)

## What the page contains
- "Take Action... Don't just consume" and a hot-linked "Futurama Fry" meme image. No video, attachment or task list.

## What to do (derived from lessons 48-49)
- [ ] List alternative solutions with "helps with / falls short" and the customer's day-to-day struggles. [48 at 00:00-02:23]
- [ ] List beliefs and objections from low-quality reviews, Reddit and YouTube. [49 at 00:00]
- [ ] Rewrite each as a "How do we ..." problem with a solution, analogy or proof idea. [49 at 00:52-04:58]
- [ ] Draft at least one ad idea from the flaws of the alternatives. [48 at 00:57-05:11]
