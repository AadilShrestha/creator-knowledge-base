# 159. Action Item/ Communicate With Your Ambassadors!
> Open a shared chat channel with each brand ambassador and start giving them concrete video ideas, as modelled in lesson 158.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** ambassadors are onboarded and there is no ongoing channel to brief them in.
- **Inputs → outputs:** onboarded ambassadors, upcoming sales, winning-ad ideas → a Discord/Slack channel per ambassador and a first idea message.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** message ambassadors casually and often, "as simple as like sending a Slack to your team". [158 01:26]
  - **DO** send an upcoming-sale heads-up and a next-video idea (templates in [158](158-how-to-communicate-with-brand-ambassadors.md#prompts--templates-verbatim)).
  - **DON'T** look for more instructions on this page. Its only content is an image reading "If not NOW When?".
- **See also:** [158](158-how-to-communicate-with-brand-ambassadors.md) · [160. Feedback Loops](160-feedback-loops-with-your-brand-ambassadors.md) · [156](156-how-to-onboard-brand-ambassadors.md)

## Steps / framework
(derived from 158)
1. Create one channel per ambassador (Discord or Slack).
2. Post the next sale date and any offer PDF.
3. Post one new video idea with the angle and the avatar details.
4. Reply to the creator's suggestions the same day.

## How to apply
- [ ] Create the channel and invite the ambassador.
- [ ] Send the first message: sale heads-up plus one idea.
- [ ] Put the next sale dates in the channel as soon as they are known.
