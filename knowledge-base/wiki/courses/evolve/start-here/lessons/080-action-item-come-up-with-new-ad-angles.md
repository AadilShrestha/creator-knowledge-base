## Steps / framework
Derived from lessons 78 and 79, not stated on this page:
1. Take every sub-avatar from [77](077-action-item-create-your-sub-avatars.md). For each, write desire, behavior and gap, then 1-3 angles by hand. [79: 24:25]
2. Run the prompt in [79](079-how-to-come-up-with-ad-angles.md) with your product information and sub-avatars; compare to your manual angles. [79: 26:41–33:30]
3. Rank the angles; pick the top one, and its best hook, as the first test. [79: 28:32; 32:51]
4. Record each test as batch number + concept + angle + variations + format. [78]

## How to apply
- [ ] List all sub-avatars with desire and behavior.
- [ ] Write at least one angle per sub-avatar as a reason to buy (not a format, not "us vs them").
- [ ] Generate direct hooks for the top angles.
- [ ] Log them in the test sheet; choose what goes into the first 3:2:2.
