# 157. Action Item/ Onboard Brand Ambassador
> Onboard a brand ambassador, but only a creator who has either made a winning ad for your brand or impressed you enough with their content to hire.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** deciding whether a creator may be offered the ambassador deal and onboarding flow from lesson 156.
- **Inputs → outputs:** a creator plus their ad/content history → eligible yes/no → onboarding package sent ([156](156-how-to-onboard-brand-ambassadors.md)).
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** require one of two entry conditions. Page text: "REMEMBER these creators NEED to have made either: 1. A Winning ad for your brand 2. Made content that impressed you enough to hire them".
  - **DON'T** onboard a creator who meets neither condition.
  - **IF** the creator is eligible **THEN** run the lesson 156 steps: deck, agreement, welcome message, Atria report, ideas.
- **See also:** [154](154-how-to-contact-winning-creators-affiliate.md) · [156](156-how-to-onboard-brand-ambassadors.md) · [158](158-how-to-communicate-with-brand-ambassadors.md) · [162. Whitelisting Overview](162-whitelisting-overview-proof-it-works.md)

## Key ideas
- The page contains one reminder, verbatim: "REMEMBER these creators NEED to have made either: 1. A Winning ad for your brand 2. Made content that impressed you enough to hire them".
- Lesson 154 gives the stricter practical test (a creator whose ad was actually spent on). Lesson 162 repeats it for whitelisting: "We only do this with creators we bring onto retainer who have WINNING ads". The page's second condition (content that impressed you) is the looser one. (derived) Treat it as a route to a retainer only, and apply the winning-ad condition before you ask for whitelisting.

## How to apply
- [ ] For each candidate, record which condition (1 or 2) they meet and the evidence (ad name, spend, ROAS, or the specific content that impressed you).
- [ ] Send the lesson 156 onboarding package only to eligible creators.
