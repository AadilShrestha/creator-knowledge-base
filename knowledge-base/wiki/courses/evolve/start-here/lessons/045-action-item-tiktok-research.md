# 45. Action Item/ TikTok Research
> A checkpoint page: do the TikTok desire and creator research for your own brand before moving to the ad account audit.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** lesson 44 has been watched and the research doc has no TikTok findings or creator list.
- **Inputs → outputs:** the desires and do-not-wants in the research doc → TikTok formats, hooks, comment insights and a creator list in the doc (derived from lesson 44).
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** treat this as a gate: "Take Action... Don't just consume" (page text).
  - **DO** keep creators in the research doc, not the growth guide (the instruction on [44. TikTok Research](044-tiktok-research.md)'s page).
  - **DON'T** start [46. Ad Account Research](046-ad-account-research.md) until the TikTok section has entries. (derived)
- **See also:** [44. TikTok Research](044-tiktok-research.md) · [46. Ad Account Research](046-ad-account-research.md)

## What the page contains
- "Take Action... Don't just consume" and a hot-linked "Futurama Fry" meme image. No video, attachment or task list.

## What to do (derived from lesson 44)
- [ ] Search TikTok for each want, do-not-want and niche term from the doc. [44 at 00:00]
- [ ] For top videos, record format, hook, view count and 3+ comments. [44 at 00:00-04:33]
- [ ] Add niche creators (audience size, TikTok/YouTube/Instagram links, content trends) to the research doc. [44 at 02:46-03:35]
- [ ] Save videos to the board or keep the link list. [44 at 01:47-02:46]
