# 18. Action Item: Self Onboarding
> Copy and fill the 10-question brand information form before any market research, so you can compare your starting beliefs with what the research later shows.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** intake for a new brand or product, before research or ad writing. Also the checklist of facts an agent must ask for instead of guessing.
- **Inputs → outputs:** founder or brand-owner answers, store data (sales by product), claims and guarantees → a completed form that feeds the "Product" and "Competitive advantages" sections of the research doc ([21](021-action-item-copy-research-document.md)).
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** fill the form BEFORE moving to the next modules. [page text]
  - **DO** answer question 1 (business differentiator) first and most carefully: the form calls it "the most important piece of finding your place in the market." [doc Q1]
  - **DO** list every enticing claim in Q9; the form marks it "(IMPORTANT)". [doc Q9]
  - **IF** you do not know your best seller **THEN** check store sales by product and paste that product's URL. [doc Q3]
  - **DON'T** invent influencers, claims or guarantees. If the brand has none, write "none" (derived).
- **See also:** [17. Overview & Self Onboarding](017-overview-self-onboarding.md) · [22. Understanding Your Product - Part 1](022-understanding-your-product-part-1.md) · [Customer research stack](../../../../frameworks/customer-research-stack.md)

## Key ideas
- The page tells students to make a copy of a Google doc and fill it "BEFORE moving on to the next modules." [page text]
- Reason given: filling it before the research "will help you compare your current knowledge VS the knowledge you gain after this full research process (It's awesome)." [page text]
- The doc is titled "Information Form" / "Onboarding & Information Form" (Easy A Media branding, the instructor's agency). [doc]

## Steps / framework
1. Make a copy of the Google doc.
2. Answer all 10 questions in the copy (use the verbatim form below).
3. Attach the copy's link to the research doc overview (done in [19](019-getting-ready-to-do-research-part-1.md) and [21](021-action-item-copy-research-document.md)).
4. After the full research process, re-read your answers and compare.

## Prompts / templates (verbatim)
The form as exported from the linked Google doc and the attached DOCX (the two are identical in content):

```text
Information Form

Onboarding & Information Form

1. What is your business differentiator?
How do you stand out from the rest? The products that you have, the mission of the business, etc.
This is the most important piece of finding your place in the market.
ANSWER

2. What are 2-3 things you wish everyone knew about your product(s)?
ANSWER

3. What is your best-selling product?
If you aren't sure, check your store sales by product and then provide the URL to that product
URL

4. How do you want your brand to be perceived by visitors?
E.g. Luxury, premium, wholesome, witty, etc.
ANSWER

5. What stops your customers from purchasing?
Complaints, comments, and feedback that you have received from those who don't buy your product after scrolling through your website.
ANSWER

6. Do you have any influencers or authorities that you work with/have used your products?
Social media influencers, actors, artists, celebrities, doctors, etc. Please list their names and their social media handles below.
ANSWER

7. Are there seasonal buying patterns?
What are typically your best months? What are your worst months?
ANSWER

8. What are some of your most frequently asked questions?
ANSWER

9. List an enticing claims that you offer. (IMPORTANT)
Offer 100% Money Back Guarantee, Satisfaction Guarantee, Lifetime Warranty, Made in USA/Canada, Clinically proven/tested, etc.
ANSWER

10. Attach your main Google Drive or Dropbox Link for ALL content
URL
```

## How to apply
- [ ] Copy the form and fill all 10 answers; mark unknowns as "unknown" rather than guessing.
- [ ] Pull Q3 from store data, not memory.
- [ ] Copy Q5 and Q8 answers into the research doc's objections and FAQ sections later.
- [ ] Copy Q9 claims into "Potential Competitive Advantages & Claims" ([22](022-understanding-your-product-part-1.md)).
- [ ] Keep the form; compare it with the finished research doc.
