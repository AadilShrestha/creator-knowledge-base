# 24. Action Item: Understanding Product
> Checkpoint page: stop watching and fill in the Product part of your research doc before starting Google research.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** gating the research pipeline: confirm the product block is filled before moving to market sources.
- **Inputs → outputs:** [22](022-understanding-your-product-part-1.md) cold-read notes + [23](023-understanding-the-product-part-2.md) example + self-onboarding answers → a completed Product / Core Research top section.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** act on the lesson. The only instruction on the page is: "Take Action ... Don't just Consume." [page text]
  - **DON'T** advance to [25. Google Research](025-google-research-part-1.md) with empty product fields (derived from the module loop in [17](017-overview-self-onboarding.md)).
- **See also:** [22](022-understanding-your-product-part-1.md) · [23](023-understanding-the-product-part-2.md) · [21. Action Item: Copy Research Document](021-action-item-copy-research-document.md)

## Key ideas
- The page contains a one-line prompt and a stock "Take Action" image. No tasks, links, files or video are included in the course files.
- The task is implied by the preceding modules: complete the research-doc Product fields (who is the company, product physical/functional, features, benefits, competitive advantages and claims, use cases).

## How to apply (derived from lessons 22-23)
- [ ] Self-onboarding answers copied into the research doc.
- [ ] Website cold-read done in an incognito window; features and benefits logged separately.
- [ ] Claims and guarantees listed under "Potential Competitive Advantages & Claims."
- [ ] At least the obvious objections noted (e.g. brand-size/trust, price vs the market).
- [ ] Features with unclear benefits flagged for later explanation.
