# 153. Action Item/ Collect Content
> A checkpoint page: follow up with hired creators, confirm delivery, and collect their raw content into your Drive, as taught in lessons 151-152.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** hired creators have been sent product and the next job is chasing and filing their content.
- **Inputs → outputs:** list of hired creators with ship dates → follow-up messages sent, content downloaded, per-creator Drive folders, deals marked complete.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** follow the cadence and message templates in [151](151-how-to-follow-up-upload-content-part-1.md): ask about arrival, then about content, and do not go more than about 3-7 days without contact. [151 05:28]
  - **DO** name each creator folder with the DCT check or X from [152](152-how-to-follow-up-upload-content-part-2.md).
  - **DON'T** invent extra requirements. The page text is only "Don't just consume..." and the same meme image as lesson 150.
- **See also:** [151](151-how-to-follow-up-upload-content-part-1.md) · [152](152-how-to-follow-up-upload-content-part-2.md) · [146. How to Download Creators + Upload to Sheet](146-how-to-download-creators-upload-to-sheet.md) · [98. How To Set Up Raw Content Campaign](098-how-to-set-up-raw-content-campaign.md)

## Key ideas
- Page text: "Don't just consume..." plus an image. The image is the same stock meme as lesson 150 ("IT IS TIME TO TAKE ACTION !", byte-identical file). No process content.

## Steps / framework
(derived from 151-152)
1. Confirm each creator received the product.
2. Ask for the content and files ("The more content, the merrier" [151 03:52]).
3. Download to the client Drive, one folder per creator.
4. Mark the folder DCT-ready (check) or not (X).
5. Press Complete in Insense.
6. Send raw content to the testing campaigns ([98](098-how-to-set-up-raw-content-campaign.md)).

## How to apply
- [ ] Message every creator whose product has shipped but who has not confirmed receipt.
- [ ] Ask every creator who confirmed receipt for a content ETA, then follow up on that date.
- [ ] Upload received content and press Complete in Insense.
