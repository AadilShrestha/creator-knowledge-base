# 27. Action Item: Google Research
> Checkpoint page: do the Google research for your own brand before moving to Amazon.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** gating the research pipeline after the Google modules.
- **Inputs → outputs:** [25](025-google-research-part-1.md) and [26](026-google-research-part-2.md) methods → research-doc entries from Google (objections, buzz words with tallies, segments, draft personas, ad ideas).
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** act: "Take Action ... Don't just Consume." [page text]
  - **DON'T** start Amazon research until the Google findings are in the doc (derived from the module loop in [17](017-overview-self-onboarding.md)).
- **See also:** [25](025-google-research-part-1.md) · [26](026-google-research-part-2.md) · [28. Amazon Research - Part 1](028-amazon-research-part-1.md)

## Key ideas
- The page holds only the prompt "Take Action ... Don't just Consume." and a stock image. No assignment text, links, files or video are in the course files.

## How to apply (derived from lessons 25-26)
- [ ] Searched with a VPN to the target market in incognito.
- [ ] Brand + product search checked: what shows, which articles mention you.
- [ ] "Best [category]" lists read; notes and +1 tallies added to features, benefits and buzz words.
- [ ] Trust/competitor-size objection logged.
- [ ] Segments (budget / mid / luxury / use cases) written, with 2-3 draft personas.
- [ ] Ad ideas from customer quotes added to the brain dump.
