# 64. Action Item: Start Your Desire Hunt
> A no-video action item that hands you four documents (a fill-in Desire Hunting template, an AI deep-research prompt, the Desire Calendar and the Desire Document) so you can find the mass desires your product can channel, with verbatim customer language, before building avatars and angles.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** starting research for a brand or product (before avatars, angles or scripts), or when a brand needs fresh desire-led angles, or when picking which desire to lead with in a given month.
- **Inputs → outputs:** product URL + brand, 1-2 angles already being run, the Product Overview doc (from the product modules) → a five-part desire report (wants/needs with exact quotes, mapping of existing angles, new desire opportunities, 10-15 verbatim customer snippets, desire power ranking) → the filled Desire Hunting Template (Desires / Customer Language Snippets / Ad Ideas / New Information).
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** copy the Desire Hunting Template and fill it from your Market Research doc from the previous modules; use the blank template only if you have no research doc yet. (Page text, "Desire Hunting Template")
  - **DO** run the Desire AI Deep Research Prompt with the product URL, 1-2 current angles, the Product Overview doc and the "How To Find Desires" guide attached. (Prompt doc, "Context 1/2")
  - **DO** require exact customer quotes with sources. The prompt says "Do not paraphrase or summarize customer statements - provide exact quotes with their sources." (Prompt doc)
  - **DON'T** try to create a desire. "You cannot CREATE desires. You can only CHANNEL existing desires onto your product." and "Never Fight Desires: You will fail if you go after nonexistent desires." (Desire Document, "The Foundation" and "Final Words")
  - **DO** score every desire on Scope, Urgency and Staying Power, then pick the strongest combination. (Desire Document, "How to Find & Evaluate Desires")
  - **DON'T** move a product down the desire hierarchy (e.g. position a luxury watch as "convenient timekeeping"): "Moving down = less emotional urgency = lower prices = weaker positioning". Elevation must feel authentic. (Desire Document, "The Elevation Strategy")
  - **IF** you are choosing a seasonal lead desire **THEN** check the month in the Desire Calendar for its top 3 mass instincts and the spend data behind them. (Desire Calendar)
  - **DON'T** treat AI-returned "customer quotes" as verified; open the source and confirm the quote exists before using it in copy (derived; the prompt itself threatens the model with "Your life depends on" finding verbatim quotes, which does not prevent invention).

## Key ideas
### The four documents and what each is for
- **Desire Hunting Template** (fill-in): five headings, "Your Brand", "Desires (Wants/Needs)" (stub "I want X"), "Customer Language Snippets", "Ad Ideas", "New Information". Use your Market Research doc from earlier modules first; the blank template is the fallback "if you want a fresh, simple one". (Page text; template doc)
- **Desire AI Deep Research Prompts** (make a copy): a long prompt that makes an AI act as a US consumer-psychology researcher and return a five-part report. Reproduced in full below.
- **The Desire Calendar**: month-by-month top-3 instincts with US spend data, 2021-2025. Video walkthrough is lesson 62.
- **The Desire Document**: the theory (mass desire, instincts, technological problems, forces of change, power ranking, elevation strategy). Video walkthrough is lessons 61 and 63.

### Mass desire and why it comes before the avatar (Desire Document, "The Foundation")
- "Mass Desire = 'The public spread of a private want'". When millions privately want the same thing, advertising can harness it.
- "A 22-year-old and 45-year-old can share the same desire... it's about understanding what a group of people WANT before narrowing down the avatar. The larger the desire, the more specific you can go on the avatar."
- Key rule: channel, never create.

### The forces that create desire (Desire Document, "How Desires Are Born: The 2 Forces (Spencer's Edition)")
The doc's headings number these oddly ("1. Permanent Forces", "2. Permanent Forces", "3. Forces of Change") under a title that says "2 Forces". Read them as: permanent forces (instincts, technological problems) plus forces of change.

| Force | Items | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Mass Instincts (permanent) | Health, Sex, Status, Belonging, Control, Comfort | "Work on anyone, anywhere, anytime. Caveman or CEO." The way they are satisfied changes; the desires stay. |
| Mass Technological Problems (permanent) | Complexity, Overwhelm, Fragility, Maintenance, Incompatibility, Obsolescence | "The technology changes, but the problems persist." |
| Forces of Change | Style Trends, Mass Education | Change how permanent desires get fulfilled. Style trend = what becomes fashionable; mass education = what society learns. |

### Evaluate desires on three dimensions (Desire Document, "How to Find & Evaluate Desires")
- Step 1 Spot the desire: watch complaint patterns, look for "wants" and "needs", follow cultural trends, look at where people spend money.
- Step 2 Test power: **Scope** (how many people share it; doc contrasts "fine antiques VS making money"), **Urgency** (how desperately; "broken bone VS cut"), **Staying Power** (does it renew continuously; "hunger VS Going to paris").
- Step 3 Choose the strongest combination. Doc's own power ranking of the instincts:

| Rank | Instinct | Doc's reason |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Health | "Still ultimate. Rich or poor, health scares move everyone" |
| 2 | Status | "Amplified massively by social media. More visible competition than ever" |
| 3 | Sex | "Fundamental but more subtle in modern marketing (regulations/platforms)" |
| 4 | Comfort | "Elevated because technology made it possible + expected" |
| 5 | Control | "Boosted by modern complexity. People want control over chaotic systems" |
| 6 | Belonging | "Important but less survival-urgent (don't need tribe to literally survive)" |

### The Elevation Strategy (Desire Document)
- "Moving products within the hierarchy can lead to more scale. However, moving products down can lower scale."
- Dog harness ladder, weakest to strongest: Belonging ("Join the responsible dog owner community.") → Comfort ("Finally, stress-free dog walks") → Control ("Take control of every walk") → Sex ("Turn heads on every walk") → Status ("The harness only 3% of dog owners know about") → Health ("Why vets are begging owners to switch"). Note: the doc also labels the ladder levels 6 (weakest) to 1 (strongest) and the "3%" figure is an example line with no source (derived: do not treat it as data).

### Final words from the Desire Document ("Final Words From Schwartz's + Spencer")
- **Never fight desires.** **Education is powerful** (more in lessons 65 and 68). **Timing is important**: the same product can succeed or fail depending on which desires are rising/falling and what mass education or trends are happening. **Analysis paralysis**: "Don't write like a mechanical robot"; learn to identify desires from headlines and go deeper on why specific ones work. **Execution above all**: "you need to understand your execution (how you communicate the desire) is what will lead to real success".

## Steps / framework
1. Make a copy of the Desire Hunting Template (or use your Market Research doc).
2. Open the Desire AI Deep Research Prompt, replace `INSERT PRODUCT URL`, `ANGLE 1`, `ANGLE 2`.
3. Attach (a) your Product Overview doc and (b) the "How To Find Desires" guide (the Context 1 text in the prompt doc). The prompt's "Context 2" placeholder is "THIS WILL BE YOUR UNDERSTANDING YOUR PRODUCT DOCUMENT FROM THE RESEARCH MODULES".
4. Run it in a deep-research-capable AI. Open each cited source and verify each quote.
5. Move findings into the template: Desires ("I want X" statements), Customer Language Snippets, Ad Ideas, New Information.
6. Score each desire for Scope / Urgency / Staying Power; check the Desire Calendar for the launch month; pick the strongest authentic desire to lead with.
7. Hand the winning desire(s) to avatar building ([71. The Evolve Avatar Training](071-the-evolve-avatar-training.md)) and angles ([79. How to come up with ad angles](079-how-to-come-up-with-ad-angles.md)).

### Desire AI Deep Research Prompt
Source: the Desire Prompts doc, also embedded in "64. Action Item: Start Your Desire Hunt.docx". Placeholders `INSERT PRODUCT URL`, `ANGLE 1`, `ANGLE 2` are the user's to fill.

```
You are a specialized market research analyst with expertise in consumer psychology and desire-based marketing in the United States. Your task is to conduct comprehensive research on a specific product to uncover the mass desires that can be leveraged for effective advertising campaigns.

Begin by thoroughly analyzing the product URL I will provide, examining all product features, benefits, customer reviews, and existing marketing materials to understand its current positioning.

Product + Brand:

INSERT PRODUCT URL

Some potential angles that we have been targeting are around:

ANGLE 1

ANGLE 2

Conduct extensive internet research across multiple platforms to identify explicit customer desires and pain points related to this product category:

1. Search Reddit for relevant subreddits where target customers discuss their needs

2. Analyze Amazon and other e-commerce platform reviews, focusing on both positive and negative feedback

3. Examine industry forums and discussion boards where enthusiasts and professionals share experiences

4. Review social media conversations on Twitter, Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok using relevant hashtags and keywords

5. Analyze YouTube comments on product reviews and tutorials

6. Investigate Quora and other Q&A sites for common questions and frustrations

7. Review customer service complaints and feedback on public platforms

When conducting this research, specifically look for:

- Direct "I want" and "I need" statements from customers

- Recurring complaints and frustrations that reveal unmet desires

- Emotional language indicating strong desires or pain points

- Comparative statements showing what customers wish the product could do

- Evidence of customers creating workarounds for limitations

Frame all findings within this desire framework:

1. Mass Instincts (universal human drives):

- Health: Survival, safety, staying healthy

- Sex: Attractiveness, finding mates, feeling desired

- Status: Importance, respect, superiority

- Belonging: Fitting in, friendship, avoiding rejection

- Control: Power, predictability, being in charge

- Comfort: Ease, avoiding pain and work

2. Mass Technological Problems in this product category:

- Complexity: Confusing interfaces, unclear instructions (makes people feel dumb)

- Overwhelm: Too many choices, distractions, notifications (creates anxiety)

- Fragility: Breaking when needed most (causes frustration)

- Maintenance: Constant updates, rising costs, repairs (creates fatigue)

- Incompatibility: Won't work with existing systems (feeling trapped)

- Obsolescence: Quickly becomes outdated (feeling cheated)

3. Forces of Change affecting desire fulfillment:

- Style Trends: What becomes fashionable

- Mass Education: What society learns

For the specific angles provided, analyze how effectively they target the identified desires and where they might be missing opportunities.

Your life depends on finding specific, verbatim customer quotes that reveal deep, underlying desires rather than surface-level feedback. Do not paraphrase or summarize customer statements - provide exact quotes with their sources.

Deliver your findings in this exact structure:

**1. WANTS AND NEEDS ANALYSIS**

- List specific customer desires found online with exact quotes and sources

- Categorize by the permanent forces they relate to

**2. EXISTING ANGLES DESIRE MAPPING**

- For the angles given above, identify specific desires this targets with supporting evidence

**3. NEW DESIRE OPPORTUNITIES**

- Identify untapped desires your product can address

- Provide customer quotes and sources as evidence

**4. CUSTOMER VOICE EVIDENCE**

- Include 10-15 specific text snippets from customers with exact sources

- Focus on complaint patterns, explicit wants/needs statements

**5. DESIRE POWER RANKING**

- Rank all identified desires from most to least powerful using:

* Scope: How many people share this desire?

* Urgency: How desperately do they want relief?

* Staying Power: Does this desire renew continuously?

- Provide reasoning for each ranking

For each desire identified, answer these critical questions:

- Is this desire widespread enough to target in mass marketing?

- Is this desire strong enough to motivate purchase decisions?

- Is this desire persistent or merely temporary?

- How directly can the product address this desire?

- What emotional triggers connect to this desire?

Ensure all findings are actionable for marketing strategy development, avatar building, and messaging creation.

For additional context, I have attached:

1. Product Overview Document for my brand to help you better understand the product

2. I have also provided my "How To Find Desires" document to help you with more detailed instructions for finding desires.
```

### Context 1: "How To Find Desires" guide (attached to the prompt)
```
How To Find Desires Guide:

Knowledge

Where to Find Desires Online:

Reddit Research Protocol:
- What everyday annoyances could your product solve?
- Subreddit search: "[your product category] sucks" or "[problem] solution"
- Look for upvoted complaints with 100+ comments

Review Section Mining:
- Search negative reviews for your category
- Look for repeated frustrations across brands
- Pay attention to "I love this BUT..." statements
- Track seasonal complaint patterns

Google Trends Desire Tracking
Rising Desires Indicators:
- Search volume increasing 50%+ year-over-year
- Related queries shifting from "what is" to "how to get"
- Geographic spread from urban centers outward
- Cross-seasonal sustained growth

Other Big Platforms For Desire Tracking
- YouTube: Focus on comment patterns, creator pain points, and engagement signals
- Amazon: Q&A mining, review analysis, and wishlist trend tracking
- Facebook: Group discussions, marketplace needs, and social proof validation
- TikTok: Trending desires, comment-to-view ratios, and cultural shift detection
- Answer The Public: Question clustering and search intent evolution
- Scientific Studies/Blogs: Evidence-based validation and expert predictions

Enhanced Existing Sections:
- Added advanced pattern recognition techniques
- Included seasonal and geographic desire mapping
- Expanded the customer interview framework with deeper psychological questions
- Enhanced Google Trends with predictive indicators

New Universal Framework:
- Cross-platform validation methods
- Language pattern recognition guide
- Red flag indicators to avoid wasting time
- Success metrics for research quality

Customer Interviews

The Desire Excavation Questions:
- "What keeps you up at night about [problem area]?"
- "When you imagine the perfect solution, what would your life look like?"
- "What have you tried before that disappointed you?"
- "If money wasn't an issue, what would you do about this?"
- "When friends complain about [problem], what do they say exactly?"

Listen For:
- Emotional words (frustrated, excited, worried, proud)
- Extreme language (always, never, hate, love)
- Time pressure indicators (finally, immediately, constantly)
- Social proof references (everyone's doing, nobody has)

Context 2
THIS WILL BE YOUR UNDERSTANDING YOUR PRODUCT DOCUMENT FROM THE RESEARCH MODULES
```
Note: the "Enhanced Existing Sections" and "New Universal Framework" lists are headings only in the source; the detailed content they promise (pattern-recognition techniques, red-flag indicators, success metrics, language-pattern guide) is not included in the course files.

## How to apply
- [ ] Copy the Desire Hunting Template; fill "Your Brand" and keep one section per desire.
- [ ] Run the Desire AI Deep Research Prompt with the product URL, 1-2 current angles, the Product Overview doc and the guide above.
- [ ] Verify every returned quote against its source; discard any quote you cannot find.
- [ ] Tag each desire with its instinct or technological problem and any force of change (style trend or mass education).
- [ ] Score each desire 1-5 on Scope, Urgency, Staying Power; rank; choose the top one that is authentic to the product.
- [ ] Check the Desire Calendar for the planned launch/test month and note the matching instinct.
- [ ] Write 3-5 channel lines ("instinct/problem + force of change") per top desire.
- [ ] Pass the ranked desires and the verbatim snippets into avatar building and angle generation.
