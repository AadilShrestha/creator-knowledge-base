# 86. Action Item / Make a Copy + Add Your Concepts
> Make your own copy of the 3.0 Growth Guide sheet and add your planned concepts to it; it becomes the ad planner and creative roadmap for every concept you strategise.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** setting up a brand's planning sheet, or entering the 5-10 concepts from [84](084-action-item-plan-your-5-10-first-concepts.md) into the Ad Roadmap.
- **Inputs → outputs:** the master Growth Guide link + your concept list → a private copy of the workbook with one Ad Roadmap row per concept (status, batch, author, desire, sub-avatar, angle, memo, awareness, file type, ad type).
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** make a copy of the Google Sheet (File → Make a copy); do not edit the master. (Page: "3.0 Growth Guide Link"; the "make a copy" step is in the title.)
  - **DO** use it for "EVERY SINGLE concept that you strategize." (Page text.)
  - **DON'T** skip planning: "You can't make great ads without great planning." (Page text.)
  - **DO** (derived) fill the rows exactly as taught in [85](085-the-3-0-evolve-growth-guide.md): memo as WHY/WHAT/HOW, awareness level, file type, ad type, batch number.
  - **DO** (derived) replace the demo rows (Night shift / Negative Framing / PAGE #1) in your copy with your own.
- **See also:** [85. The 3.0 Evolve Growth Guide](085-the-3-0-evolve-growth-guide.md) · [84. Plan Your 5-10 First Concepts](084-action-item-plan-your-5-10-first-concepts.md) · [Mini breakthrough memo + Growth Guide](../../copywriting/frameworks/mini-breakthrough-memo-and-growth-guide.md)

## Steps / framework
Steps are derived from lessons 84 and 85; the page itself only says make a copy and add concepts.
1. Open the 3.0 Growth Guide link and make a copy into your own Drive.
2. Fill the Desires/Core Avatar and Sub Avatars/Angles tabs from your avatar work ([75](075-action-item-identify-your-core-avatars.md), [77](077-action-item-create-your-sub-avatars.md)).
3. Add each of the 5-10 concepts from [84](084-action-item-plan-your-5-10-first-concepts.md) as an Ad Roadmap row.
4. Write a Why/What/How memo per row and set awareness level, file type, ad type.
5. Assign a Batch # to the group you will build first.

## How to apply
- [ ] Copy the sheet; confirm you are editing your copy.
- [ ] Delete or overwrite the sample rows.
- [ ] Enter every planned concept; no concept gets briefed without a row.
- [ ] Share the copy with your team (and media buyer for the Log tab).
