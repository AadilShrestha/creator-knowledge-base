# 21. Action Item: Copy Research Document
> Make a copy of the updated research document (and start an Atria trial) before the product and market research modules; this page holds the full document template.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** creating the output skeleton for a research run; deciding what to collect per source; briefing a human or agent on what "done research" looks like.
- **Inputs → outputs:** brand + product + competitor list + ad account data → a research doc with these headings filled (ad copy bank, product, core research, avatars, FAQ, competitors, ad-account audit).
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** copy the UPDATED research doc before the next modules, and "grab your free trial for Atria." [page text]
  - **DO** write ad copy only after asking "who you are writing this for" (see avatars). The doc header says: "*IMPORTANT: Ask yourself who you are writing this for before writing." [doc, Ad Copy]
  - **DON'T** treat the older doc shown in the videos as required; it is "(Optional)". [page text] The video lessons 22-36 use it, so some headings differ from the updated one (mapping below).
  - **DON'T** treat the brand guidelines template as mandatory: "(NOT MANDATORY)". [page text]
  - **DO** audit the ad account by spend + efficiency: top creatives by highest spend and lowest CPA or best ROAS, for the last 30 days and last 6 months. [doc, Creative Audit Part 1]

## Key ideas
- The page lists four links: the UPDATED Research Doc (make a copy), the Research Doc shown in the video (optional, an older V2.0 version), a Canva brand-guidelines template (not mandatory) and the Atria referral link. [page text]
- The updated doc adds an **Avatars** section and a **Phrase Bank**, and drops the "Marketing Brain Dump / Market Awareness" headings of the older doc. The avatar block points to the newer avatar training: "You can find how to create these avatars by watching the new training here." [doc, Avatars]
- The competitor block is limited to 3-5 competitors with: website URL, Atria profile/ad library, product price range, active offers, notes. [doc, Competitor Research]

## Steps / framework
1. Make a copy of the updated doc; start the Atria free trial.
2. Fill the Overview links ([19](019-getting-ready-to-do-research-part-1.md), [20](020-getting-ready-to-do-research-part-2.md)).
3. Work module by module: Product ([22](022-understanding-your-product-part-1.md)), Google ([25](025-google-research-part-1.md)), Amazon ([28](028-amazon-research-part-1.md)), Reddit ([31](031-reddit-research.md)), YouTube ([33](033-youtube-research.md)), Competitors ([35](035-competitor-research-part-1.md)), Facebook/creative/TikTok/ad account later.

## How to apply
- [ ] Copy the updated doc, rename for the brand, start the Atria trial.
- [ ] Keep the old headings in mind when watching videos 22-36; file findings under the V4 equivalent.
- [ ] Rank possible desires "In Order Of Power" (older doc) when you get to desire research.
- [ ] Do the ad-account audit with real numbers (hook rate, hold rate/CTR, spend, CPA/ROAS); do not invent them.
