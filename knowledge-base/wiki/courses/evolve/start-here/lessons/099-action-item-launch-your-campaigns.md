# 99. Action Item - Launch Your Campaigns
> Launch the new Evolve campaign structure next to your existing campaigns; do not purge or switch off what already runs.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** an account already runs ads and the user asks to "launch the Evolve campaigns" or "clean up" old campaigns.
- **Inputs → outputs:** list of existing campaigns plus the campaign structures from lessons 94-98 → a launch plan that adds new campaigns alongside the old ones.
- **Rules:**
  - **DON'T** recommend purging or turning off all existing campaigns. "We do NOT recommend purging and turning off all of your existing campaigns." (page text)
  - **DO** add the new campaign "alongside whatever campaigns you already have" as the NEW campaign for testing and scaling. (page text)
  - **IF** old campaigns "begin to die out over a few weeks or months" **THEN** optimise them and turn them off. (page text)
- **See also:** [94. 3/2/2 Campaign](094-how-to-set-up-a-3-2-2-campaign.md) · [98. Raw Content Campaign](098-how-to-set-up-raw-content-campaign.md) · [100. Columns](100-how-to-see-ads-performance-our-columns.md)

## Key ideas
- This page is a reminder only: no video, no attachments. All text is the three-paragraph note quoted above.
- Order of operations implied by the section: set up campaigns (94-98), launch them (this item), then read performance with the Evolve columns ([100](100-how-to-see-ads-performance-our-columns.md)).

## How to apply
- [ ] Export or list every active campaign first. Leave them untouched.
- [ ] Create the new 3/2/2 (and, if relevant, raw content) campaigns beside them.
- [ ] Revisit old campaigns only after they decay over weeks or months; optimise, then switch off.
