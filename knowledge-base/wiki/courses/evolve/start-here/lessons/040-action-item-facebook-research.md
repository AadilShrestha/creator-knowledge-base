# 40. Action Item/ Facebook Research
> A checkpoint page: do the Facebook Ad Library and ad-comment research on your own brand before moving to creative research.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** the Facebook research lessons (38-39) have been watched and the research doc still lacks Ad Library findings.
- **Inputs → outputs:** research doc + competitor list + (if available) the ad account → saved competitor ads, ideas in the doc, and a comment-mining list (derived from lessons 38-39).
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** treat this as a gate: "Take Action ... Don't just Consume." (page text)
  - **DO NOT** start [41. Creative Research - Part 1 (Atria)](041-creative-research-part-1-atria.md) until the doc has Ad Library notes for your competitors (derived from the course loop "Watch Module -> Take Action -> Finish Research Step -> Watch Next Module", printed on lessons 38-39).

## What to do (derived from lessons 38-39)
- [ ] Keyword-search the niche in the Meta Ad Library; filter English, one country, Active; save useful ads to the client board. [38 at 01:47-02:23]
- [ ] Search each of the 5 competitors; note longest-running ads, messaging, objections, landing page. [38 at 04:19-05:56]
