# 129. Action Item - Review Your Ads
> A checkpoint after the learnings lessons (122–128): stop watching and run the learnings process on your own ads.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** a brand has ads with a few days of data and nobody has yet written down why they won or lost.
- **Inputs → outputs:** your ads plus their spend and metrics → one written learnings card per reviewed ad (derived from lessons 122–128 and 130).
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** treat this as the "do it now" step after lessons 122–128. The page itself says only "Don't just consume... Take action!".
  - **DON'T** invent a method beyond the lessons; the page has no instructions, rubric or template.

## How to apply (derived from lessons 122–130; not stated on the page)
- [ ] Pick the ads that got spend in the last test window, plus any winner.
- [ ] For each: note spend context, the hook, why the audience reacted, what was weak ([130](130-how-to-rewrite-losing-ads-into-winners.md) learnings card).
- [ ] Run the 14 video-brief questions from [128](128-how-to-do-learnings-questions.md) on each video ad.
- [ ] For winners, break the ad down line by line ([125](125-how-to-do-learnings-part-4-winning-ads.md), [127](127-how-to-do-learnings-part-5-winning-ads.md)).
- [ ] Turn each learning into one concrete next test (see [131](131-how-to-do-iterations-variations-live-example.md)).
