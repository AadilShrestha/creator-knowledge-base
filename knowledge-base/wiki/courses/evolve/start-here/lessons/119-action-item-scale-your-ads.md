# 119. Action Item - Scale Your Ads
> The whole page is one line and one image: "If you can't scale your ads..." followed by a speech bubble reading "Make a more Ads!". The scaling lever the course points to is more ad creative, not another budget or targeting tweak.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** an account is stuck and an agent is tempted to propose media-buying changes (new structure, new bid settings, new budget rules) to unlock scale.
- **Inputs → outputs:** current ad count and creative pipeline → a plan for more new ads (new concepts and iterations of winners and losers).
- **Rules:**
  - **IF** you cannot scale **THEN** "Make a more Ads" (verbatim image text). Source: page image.
  - **DON'T** treat budget moves alone as the fix. The page offers no media-buying instruction at all. (derived from the page content)
