# 151. How to Follow Up & Upload Content - Part 1
> After product ships to Insense creators, chase every creator on a fixed rhythm (arrival, then content), download what they send into a per-creator Drive folder, and close the deal in Insense. It keeps free-product seeding from stalling.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** running a product-seeding / Insense campaign and deciding what to message each hired creator, when, and where to file their content.
- **Inputs → outputs:** list of hired creators with ship dates and chat history → follow-up messages sent on schedule, content saved to a Drive folder per creator, each deal marked Complete.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** tell the creator when the product is shipped and say it should arrive "within the next three to seven days". [00:00]
  - **DO** follow up "every three days" if the creator has not replied after receiving the product. [01:30]
  - **DO** follow up "about two to three days after" a creator says they will shoot over the weekend, allowing "a little bit more wiggle room" when weekends are included. [02:06]
  - **DON'T** go longer than "three or longer than seven days really without touching back with the creator". [05:28]
  - **DO** ask for everything: "The more content, the merrier", including a version without audio, and any post links. [03:52]
  - **DON'T** treat creators like robots: "they're real people". [05:28]
  - **IF** the video looks bad (for example a ring light visible in the camera) **THEN** ask politely for a fix, but remember "this is free content, so you can't really expect it to be amazing". [04:47]
  - **IF** a creator is fast and on top of it **THEN** add them to the Insense favorites list "if that is still an option". [00:00]
  - **DO** press Complete in Insense only after the content is received and uploaded. [04:47]

## Key ideas

### Confirm product arrival [00:00–02:53]
- Trigger: the client has uploaded the product and ticked the "Product Sent" box in the tracking sheet (frame shows a hired-creators sheet with columns Product to Send, Product Sent, Tracking Number, Content Received). [00:00]
- Tell the creator the product is sent. One creator replied before the message landed ("the product arrived today") and had sent a full video within a day. [00:00]
- Use the Insense Chats tab with the filter set to **Content creation** to see only hired creators. [01:30]
- Messages seen on screen (read from chat, wording approximate): "Product should be sent tomorrow", then "product has been shipped & should be arriving soon. Please let us know once you receive it. Excited to see your content!" sent about three days later. [02:06]
- Rhythm shown across examples: product shipped on the 5th, creator confirms; follow up on the next Monday or Tuesday. [02:53]

### Chase the content [03:52–06:23]
- If a creator says they "already posted everything", ask for the files so you can use them, and ask whether they have a version without audio. Creators sent a link to the Instagram post plus the video files. [03:52]
- A creator who got the product and "will shoot over the weekend" is followed up two to three days after (more if weekends fall in between). [02:06]
- Example of a stalled creator: product received on the 2nd, shooting "tomorrow", still no content five days later → follow up Monday morning with "do you have the content or how's the content coming along?". [05:28]
- A creator who asks "what kind of content you would like" gets examples of content that "could do well" (the framework, see [148](148-sending-frameworks-to-creators-part-2.md)). [05:28]
- A creator returning from travel on a given date is followed up once she is back. [06:23]

### Upload to the client Drive [00:52–01:30; 03:52–04:47]
- Path used: Drive → client assets → the client → **Insense content** folder → create a new folder named for the creator → copy the downloaded files in. [00:52]
- Download content from the Insense chat ("Download original"). [00:52]

### Close the deal [04:47–05:28; 06:23–07:11]
- Press the **Complete** button, confirm "Are you sure you want to complete the deal?", and the creator moves into the Completed section. [04:47]
- Completed vs "still in content creation" is how you track what is finished. [05:28]
- Mark creators as content received once files are uploaded. [06:23]

## Examples
- **Fast creator:** received product, replied the same day, planned a video "over the weekend" and delivered within a day. Lesson: add to favorites. [00:00]
- **Slow creator:** "I'm going to start shooting tomorrow" on the 2nd, nothing by the 7th → nudge on Monday. [05:28]
- **Needs direction:** creator says the package arrived and asks what content to make → reply with example content that could do well. [05:28]
- **Poor video:** ring light visible in the camera → ask politely, but accept that free product content varies. [04:47]

## How to apply
- [ ] Keep a sheet with Product Sent, Tracking Number and Content Received columns.
- [ ] Send the Product Sent message with the 4:5 crop rule, so creators keep text and edits in the middle of the frame.
- [ ] Re-ping on the 3-day rhythm and log each touch date.
- [ ] Create a Drive folder per creator under Insense content; upload all files, with and without audio.
- [ ] Press Complete only after files are in the Drive.
- [ ] Hand strong creators to [154](154-how-to-contact-winning-creators-affiliate.md) once their ads have spend data.
