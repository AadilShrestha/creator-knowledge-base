# 155. Action Item/ Contact Top Performing Creators
> Send the retainer-only or performance-program email from lesson 154 to the creators whose ads actually scaled. The page's own verdict: "This is WORTH IT".

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** a seeding or Insense campaign has produced ad data and you are choosing whom to offer an ongoing deal.
- **Inputs → outputs:** per-creator ad spend and results → shortlist of "winning creators" → outreach emails sent (templates in [154](154-how-to-contact-winning-creators-affiliate.md#prompts--templates-verbatim)).
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** contact only creators whose ad scaled, not creators you merely liked. Lesson 154 defines a winning creator as one who "actually makes an ad that can scale". [154 00:00]
  - **DO** pick the email version first: retainer only, or performance program (see [154](154-how-to-contact-winning-creators-affiliate.md)).
  - **DON'T** expect a checklist here. The page text is: "This is WORTH IT".
- **See also:** [154](154-how-to-contact-winning-creators-affiliate.md) · [156. How To Onboard Brand Ambassadors](156-how-to-onboard-brand-ambassadors.md) · [157](157-action-item-onboard-brand-ambassador.md) · [98. Raw Content Campaign](098-how-to-set-up-raw-content-campaign.md)

## Key ideas
- Entire page text: "This is WORTH IT". No image, attachment or link.

## Steps / framework
(derived from 154)
1. Rank creators by ad spend and results in your raw-content/seeding campaign.
2. Exclude creators whose ads got little or no spend.
3. Send the matching email.
4. Log accepted creators in the retainer tracking sheet.
5. Send the agreement and onboard ([156](156-how-to-onboard-brand-ambassadors.md)).

## How to apply
- [ ] Pull per-creator spend, ROAS and purchases from Ads Manager (columns: [100](100-how-to-see-ads-performance-our-columns.md)).
- [ ] Mark creators with a scaled ad as "winning".
- [ ] Send each winner the lesson 154 email, edited to your company and offer.
