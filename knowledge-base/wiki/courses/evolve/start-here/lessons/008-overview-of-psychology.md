# 8. Overview Of Psychology
> Opens the "Why Ads Work" section by naming the five forces that determine whether an ad works (identity, belief, desire, awareness, sophistication) and crediting them for the instructor's client results.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** deciding what to analyse in an ad or a market before writing: what the buyer wants, what they know, how many promises they've heard, whom they want to be, and whether they believe you.
- **Inputs → outputs:** a product + market → a five-part psychological read (identity, belief, desire, awareness level, sophistication) that feeds angle and script choices.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** check all five before making ads: identities the buyer wants to resonate with; whether they "actually believe us"; their desires; their awareness levels; how sophisticated the market is. [00:40]
  - **DO** re-watch and go deeper: "if you circle back on these and you dive deeper and listen to some of the recommendations that I provide, you will definitely be far ahead of a lot of other marketers." [00:40–01:31]
  - **DON'T** copy creatives without understanding what drives the buyer: "We used to make ads and just copy creatives from what everybody else was talking about, but until we actually understood what makes people tick…". [00:00–00:40]
  - **IF** the material feels odd at first **THEN** stay with it; "it could be a little bit weird to understand at first". [00:40–01:31]

## Steps / framework
Five-part read (order as in the video; derived checklist):
1. Identity: who does the buyer want to be seen as?
2. Belief: do they believe the promise, the mechanism, the brand?
3. Desire: what do they want, how intense and how widely shared?
4. Awareness: how much do they know about the problem, the solutions and our product?
5. Sophistication: how many similar claims have they already heard?
