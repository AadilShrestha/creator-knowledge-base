# 47. Action Item/ Ad Account Research
> A checkpoint page: complete the media buyer creative audit on your ad account before moving to desires and objections.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** lesson 46 has been watched and the research doc's "Media Buyer: Creative Audit" is blank, and an ad account with history exists.
- **Inputs → outputs:** ad account access + research doc → completed audit lists with links and metrics (derived from lesson 46).
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** treat this as a gate: "Take Action... Don't just consume" (page text).
  - **IF** the account has no spend history **THEN** write that in the audit and skip to [48. Understanding Other Desires](048-understanding-other-desires.md). (derived)
  - **DON'T** mark the audit done without both the 30-day and 6-month passes and the hook/hold lists (derived from [46](046-ad-account-research.md) at 00:00-00:55).
- **See also:** [46. Ad Account Research](046-ad-account-research.md) · [100. How To See Ads Performance + Our Columns](100-how-to-see-ads-performance-our-columns.md)

## What the page contains
- "Take Action... Don't just consume" and a hot-linked "Futurama Fry" meme image. No video, attachment or task list.

## What to do (derived from lesson 46)
- [ ] Top prospecting and retargeting ads, last 30 days: links, spend, purchases, CPA/ROAS. [46 at 01:36-05:02]
- [ ] Same for the last 6 months. [46 at 05:02-06:00]
- [ ] Save a column preset with Video Hook, Video Hold, average play time and CTR; list top hook-rate and hold/CTR ads. [46 at 06:00-07:29]
- [ ] Add the agency's own top performers from the last 30 days. [46 at 00:55]
- [ ] Send the winners to the media buyer / strategist for the champions ad set. [46 at 07:29-08:06]
