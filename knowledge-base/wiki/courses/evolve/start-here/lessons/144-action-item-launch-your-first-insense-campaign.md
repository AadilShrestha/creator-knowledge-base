# 144. Action Item: Launch Your First Insense Campaign
> Do not just watch lesson 143; build and submit your first product-seeding campaign so applications start arriving.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** the brand is on Insense and no seeding campaign exists.
- **Inputs → outputs:** brand, product assets, filters, screening questions, creative idea → a campaign in moderation, then live applications.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** launch the campaign now: the page text is only "Don't just consume..." plus the "It is time to take action!" meme. 
  - **DO** follow the settings and checklists in [143](143-how-to-create-an-insense-campaign.md) (derived; the page gives no further instruction).
  - **DO** expect volume if the product is "worthy of filming for free": a client campaign got 74+ applications in about 48 hours ([138](138-ugc-content-overview-part-1.md) [00:40]).
- **See also:** [143](143-how-to-create-an-insense-campaign.md) · [145. How To Process Creators & Add To Shortlist](145-how-to-process-creators-add-to-shortlist.md) · [139](139-ugc-content-overview-part-2.md) for the paid variant

## Page content
- "Don't just consume..." and the same meme image as lesson 142 ("IT IS TIME TO TAKE ACTION !"). No other text, links or attachments.

## How to apply (derived from 143)
- [ ] Goal: Awareness & Reach; product photos and a 2-3 line description; product price above about $30.
- [ ] Creator filters from the avatar; followers/ER blank.
- [ ] Screening questions: topic fit, sensitive topics, whitelisting, selfie.
- [ ] One clear creative idea; posting choice; send to review.
- [ ] Once live: run the daily check and 2-3-day follow-ups ([138](138-ugc-content-overview-part-1.md)); process applicants ([145](145-how-to-process-creators-add-to-shortlist.md)).
