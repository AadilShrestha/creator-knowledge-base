# 30. Action Item: Amazon Research
> Checkpoint page: finish the Amazon research for your own market before moving to Reddit.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** gating the research pipeline after the two Amazon modules.
- **Inputs → outputs:** [28](028-amazon-research-part-1.md) and [29](029-amazon-research-part-2.md) methods → Amazon-derived entries in the research doc (price tiers, buzz words, use cases, bad reviews, questions, desires).
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** act: "Take Action ... Don't just Consume." [page text]
  - **DON'T** begin [31. Reddit Research](031-reddit-research.md) before the Amazon findings are written down (derived from the module loop in [17](017-overview-self-onboarding.md)).
- **See also:** [28](028-amazon-research-part-1.md) · [29](029-amazon-research-part-2.md) · [31. Reddit Research](031-reddit-research.md)

## Key ideas
- The page holds only the prompt "Take Action ... Don't just Consume." and a stock image. No assignment text, links, files or video exist for this lesson in the course files. The Shulex link lives on [29](029-amazon-research-part-2.md).

## How to apply (derived from lessons 28-29)
- [ ] Installed a review-analysis extension and ran it on the top listing in your category and on mid-priced and budget listings.
- [ ] Price tiers written (budget / similar / premium) with pros, cons and purchase motivations.
- [ ] Claims buyers are used to (and any your product lacks) listed.
- [ ] Use-case percentages turned into filming environments.
- [ ] Bad-review failure modes added to "Bad Competitor Product Reviews."
- [ ] Customer questions (specs, compatibility) added to the doc and the product page.
- [ ] Your own Amazon listing compared against competitors' claims.
