# Evolve Start Here: Spencer Pawliw and Shaun Eng
> Evolve's main DTC growth program: 175 lessons in 12 sections, about 1,996 video minutes, plus prompts, sheets and templates. It runs one loop (Study → Research → Create → Test → Feedback): market psychology, a 14-source research doc, desires, avatars and angles, concept planning, the 3:2:2 Meta account, winning-ad labels, scaling, learnings and iteration, and a creator program. It is the **strategy, media-buying and creator-ops** layer of this knowledge base. [Evolve Copywriting](../copywriting/README.md) writes the ads; [Tether Lab](../../tether-lab/README.md) is the psychology layer.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** researching a market or product, finding desires, avatars and angles, planning concepts and tests, setting up or auditing a Meta account, deciding to scale, kill or iterate an ad, or building a creator / ambassador program.
- **Inputs → outputs:** onboarding facts, product facts, reviews, Reddit and comment language, competitor ads, account data (daily spend, margin, CPA, 7-day-click results) → research doc, desire and avatar records, angles, concept cards, campaign plan, labels, scaling moves, learnings cards, creator briefs.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** run the loop in order and finish the research gates before making ads: "The effort that you put in prior to making the ad is the difference between the ad usually winning and the ad flopping." [L56 01:18](lessons/056-organizing-insights-overview-must-watch.md) See the [growth loop](concepts/evolve-growth-loop.md).
  - **DO** set targets from margin: Breakeven ROAS = 1 ÷ gross margin; Scaling ROAS = breakeven + 1. [L101 01:40](lessons/101-how-to-set-targets-kpis.md)
  - **DO** label every test after 7 days in the CBO by spend share and KPI ([winning-ad labels](concepts/winning-ad-labels.md)), and judge on 7-day-click purchases ([click-based attribution](concepts/click-based-attribution.md)).
  - **DO** write the scripts and statics with the Copywriting course. Start Here hands ad-making over to it. [L91](lessons/091-action-item-go-to-copywriting-program.md)
  - **DON'T** quote instructor results (revenue, ROAS, "$100k/day") as evidence; they are instructor claims.
  - **DON'T** execute the ⛔ tactics listed in [don't execute](#dont-execute). Copy the mechanism, not the asset.
  - **IF** two lessons give different numbers **THEN** use the [contradictions](contradictions.md) default and say which row you applied.

## Sources and limitations

- **Blocked:** lesson 5's "100+ Hook Templates" Google Sheet needs a login (HTTP 401). Only rows visible in the recording are transcribed in [L5](lessons/005-100-hook-templates-from-ads-doc.md).

Most of the 498 are page, doc or on-screen text rather than speech, so a quote missing from the audio is not automatically wrong, but treat it as unconfirmed by audio.

- **Evidence:** `knowledge-base/raw/evolve/start-here/` (`_transcripts/`, `_extracted/`, `linked/`). Screenshots: `wiki/assets/evolve/start-here/`. Checkpoint: [PROGRESS](PROGRESS.md); history: [log](log.md).
