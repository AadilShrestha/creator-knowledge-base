# Playbook: research to first concepts
> Ten gated steps that take a brand from zero to a backlog of 5-10 test-ready concepts: intake, research doc, product overview, desire hunt, belief lever (mechanism / information / identity), one desire-based core avatar, sub-avatars, angles, concept cards, Growth Guide rows. Each step has an output and a gate; don't open the next step until the gate passes.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** a new brand, product or market needs its first ad tests; an account has run "rinse and repeat" ads on shallow research and stalled; you are asked for "angles" or "concepts" and no research exists yet.
- **Inputs → outputs:** brand + product URL + target country + any founder answers, reviews, order data and ad-account access → research doc, Product Overview doc, Desire Hunting sheet, belief-lever notes, core avatar + sub-avatars, ranked angles with direct hooks, 5-10 concept cards, Growth Guide rows; plus a "missing inputs" list.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** follow the order product → desire → sophistication → belief builders before writing ads. [L56 02:05–06:45](../lessons/056-organizing-insights-overview-must-watch.md)
  - **DON'T** pump ads off "a couple of angles" from a shallow pass: "you need to go deeper". [L56 01:18](../lessons/056-organizing-insights-overview-must-watch.md)
  - **DO** label every benefit, desire and mechanism "assumed" until research validates it. [L58 14:24](../lessons/058-understanding-your-product-part-2.md)
  - **DO** assume the market is sophisticated (stage 3-5) and don't spend time labelling the stage. [L65 10:43](../lessons/065-understanding-market-sophistication-part-1.md)
  - **DO** build ONE desire-based core avatar first, then many sub-avatars. [L74 00:57; 10:38; 46:39](../lessons/074-how-to-build-core-avatars.md)
  - **DO** time-box: research threads for sub-avatars about an hour, "not days"; clean-up 10-15 min. [L76 15:05](../lessons/076-how-to-build-sub-avatars.md); [L51 00:00–00:51](../lessons/051-cleaning-up-the-research-doc.md)
  - **DON'T** invent research outputs (calls, quotes, stats). List what's missing and continue with what's verifiable. (repo rule; [L55](../lessons/055-customer-phone-call-research.md))
  - **DO** fact-check every AI finding before it reaches copy: "Go and double check that Claude did not hallucinate." [L68 21:00](../lessons/068-how-to-find-apply-new-information-part-3.md)
  - **IF** you skipped avatars **THEN** angle work "may not make 100% sense"; go back. [L79 page text](../lessons/079-how-to-come-up-with-ad-angles.md)
- **See also:** [research stack workflow](../frameworks/research-stack-workflow.md) · [desire hunt](../frameworks/desire-hunt.md) · [angle generation prompt](../frameworks/angle-generation-prompt.md) · [concept planning + Growth Guide](../frameworks/concept-planning-and-growth-guide.md) · [launch a new product end to end](launch-a-new-product-end-to-end.md) · Copywriting: [ad idea to brief](../../copywriting/playbooks/ad-idea-to-brief.md) · Tether: [customer research to angles](../../../../playbooks/customer-research-to-angles.md)

## Step details that are easy to get wrong

### Step 3: product overview fast path
- Research exists → Claude project per brand with reviews, research doc and onboarding doc; paste the template: "Hey Claude, can you please fill out the following"; then audit "what of this is fluff". [L59 01:22–04:06](../lessons/059-understanding-your-product-part-3.md)
- No research → manual pass from the product page. [L58](../lessons/058-understanding-your-product-part-2.md)
- Hidden-mechanism prompts: what you don't advertise, what competitors can't claim, what "worked too well" (complaints), how a friend would explain it. Don't let AI answer "what do you not advertise?"; it "has no idea". [L58 24:59; 30:09–31:40](../lessons/058-understanding-your-product-part-2.md)

### Step 5: which lever
| Situation | Lever | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Product is ordinary, no real difference | New information (education, root causes the market doesn't know) | [L65 35:44](../lessons/065-understanding-market-sophistication-part-1.md); [L68 00:45](../lessons/068-how-to-find-apply-new-information-part-3.md) |
| Real ingredient/process/form difference | New mechanism, explained in plain words ("a thousand songs in your pocket") | [L65 22:38–24:29](../lessons/065-understanding-market-sophistication-part-1.md); [L66](../lessons/066-how-to-find-apply-new-mechanisms-part-2.md) |
| Market resists claims; you can't out-claim | Identity = underserved specific avatar (steps 6-7) | [L70 04:31–06:41](../lessons/070-how-to-find-apply-new-identities-part-4.md) |
| Best case | Mechanism + information that explains why it works + an identity to attach to | [L65 37:11](../lessons/065-understanding-market-sophistication-part-1.md); [L68 00:00](../lessons/068-how-to-find-apply-new-information-part-3.md) |
"99% of you should only be focusing on new information and or... new mechanisms." [L70 00:00](../lessons/070-how-to-find-apply-new-identities-part-4.md) Ethics gate for new information: "Would you run this message to your mom or would you run this to your sister?" [L68 18:38](../lessons/068-how-to-find-apply-new-information-part-3.md)

### Steps 6-7: avatar counts by revenue stage
| Revenue / month | Core avatars | Source |
|---|---|---|
| $0-100K | 1 desire-based core, many sub-avatars, 2-4 weeks before changing the desire | [L71 2:24:49–2:28:25](../lessons/071-the-evolve-avatar-training.md); [L76 13:14–14:08](../lessons/076-how-to-build-sub-avatars.md) |
| $100K-$1M | A few core avatars (L76) vs "1-2 avatars" can reach $1M/mth (L171) | [L76 13:14–14:08](../lessons/076-how-to-build-sub-avatars.md); [L171 35:45–36:16](../lessons/171-how-to-make-any-product-work-brand-building-pt-1.md) |
| $1M+ | A few more, depending on breadth | [L76 13:14–14:08](../lessons/076-how-to-build-sub-avatars.md) |
Agent default for the $100K-$1M conflict: at most 2 core avatars; add sub-avatars before adding cores. Avatar build details: [core and sub-avatars](../concepts/core-and-sub-avatars.md).

## Final deliverable (hand to the creative/brief stage)
```yaml
brand: ""
missing_inputs: []                 # e.g. phone calls not done, no ad-account access
research_doc: <link>
product_overview: <link>           # assumptions labelled
lead_desire: "I want ..."          # with evidence links
belief_lever: {type: new_information|new_mechanism|identity, findings: [{claim: "", source: "", verified: true}]}
core_avatar: "Wants ..."
sub_avatars:
  - label: ""                      # customer lingo
    desire: ""; experience: ""; emotion: ""; behavior: ""; quotes: [{text: "", source: ""}]
angles:
  - sub_avatar: ""; angle: ""; reason_to_buy: ""; hooks: ["", "", ""]; inspired_by_structure: ""; claims_to_verify: []
concept_cards: []                  # 5-10, fields per concept-planning page
growth_guide: <link>; first_batch: "BATCH #1"
```

## Common failure points
| Symptom | Cause | Fix | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ads have a real desire and product but don't scale | Writing "here we are, come and get it" in a stage 3-5 market | Add mechanism, new information or avatar-specific framing | [L56 02:44–03:30](../lessons/056-organizing-insights-overview-must-watch.md) |
| Angles feel generic | Sub-avatars lack behavior/experience | Go back to step 7 | [L79 30:03](../lessons/079-how-to-come-up-with-ad-angles.md) |
| Avatar is "Susan, 47, two kids" | Demographic-first | Desire first, demographics last | [L71 06:20–08:42](../lessons/071-the-evolve-avatar-training.md) |
| Concept list is formats ("UGC", "memes") | No angle attached | "About what?" | [L83 24:48](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md) |
| Ad wins/loses and nobody knows why | No belief-builder check | Record which builder (mechanism, information, identity) was present | [L56 05:05–05:48](../lessons/056-organizing-insights-overview-must-watch.md) |
