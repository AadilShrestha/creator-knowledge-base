# Playbook: Creator program setup
> End-to-end workflow for building a DTC brand's creator supply the Evolve way: fit check → creator framework → Insense seeding → vet, hire, ship → collect and triage → raw content campaign → spend-based creator verdict → ambassador offers and onboarding → channel briefs and monthly feedback → partnership/whitelisting → monthly engine review.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** a brand has no creator pipeline, or has one that produces content nobody reviews; a user asks to "set up UGC", "find creators", "run Insense" or "build ambassadors".
- **Inputs → outputs:** product facts and photos, price, offer, avatar(s), research doc (desires, angles, customer language), account daily spend, monthly revenue, budget → the artefacts in the output checklist below, phase by phase.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** collect real inputs first: product truth, offer, avatar, customer language. List what's missing; don't guess (repo rule 1).
  - **DO** set the objective in writing: find creators and one ambassador; a winning ad from seeding "is a bonus. It is not the objective." [L138 02:27](../lessons/138-ugc-content-overview-part-1.md)
  - **DO** brief creators with frameworks, not scripts. [L148 page text](../lessons/148-sending-frameworks-to-creators-part-2.md); [L137 03:34](../lessons/137-does-insense-still-work.md)
  - **DO** decide creators by spend. [L154 00:38](../lessons/154-how-to-contact-winning-creators-affiliate.md)
  - **DON'T** fabricate proof in frameworks, offers or creator copy. Sample hooks are scenarios the creator really films; earnings lines and "we already have creators generating..." lines go out only if true (repo rule 2).
  - **DON'T** rip or reuse other brands' or creators' footage; reference GIFs and viral transcripts are for mechanism only (repo rule 3). Course tactics that place ripped ads in the raw campaign [L98 01:46](../lessons/098-how-to-set-up-raw-content-campaign.md) are **course content, don't execute**.
  - **DON'T** write creator names, emails, handles, addresses or phone numbers into the knowledge base or shared outputs beyond the private client sheet.
  - **DO** hand contracts, payments and platform access to a human; the agent drafts, the human signs and pays (derived; contracts are "use at your own risk", [L156 page text](../lessons/156-how-to-onboard-brand-ambassadors.md)).
- **See also:** [Content engine](../concepts/content-engine.md) · [Creator frameworks vs scripts](../concepts/creator-frameworks-vs-scripts.md) · [Whitelisting and partnership ads](../concepts/whitelisting-and-partnership-ads.md) · [Insense creator pipeline](../frameworks/insense-creator-pipeline.md) · [Brand ambassador program](../frameworks/brand-ambassador-program.md) · [Campaign structure 3:2:2](../frameworks/campaign-structure-322.md) · [Revenue stage focus](../concepts/revenue-stage-focus.md) · Tether [creator brief](../../../../playbooks/creator-brief.md)

## Phase 0: Fit, stage, budget (day 0)
1. **Fit check:** broad product-aware market? mechanism? "worthy of filming for free"? retail value ≥ ~$30? [L137 04:18; 07:15](../lessons/137-does-insense-still-work.md); [L138 00:40](../lessons/138-ugc-content-overview-part-1.md); [L143 03:37](../lessons/143-how-to-create-an-insense-campaign.md) If not, flag it: "How do I make people want my stuff for free?" [L138 00:40–01:36](../lessons/138-ugc-content-overview-part-1.md)
2. **Stage goal:** < $300k/month volume; $300k-$3M intent and hit rate; $3M-$10M systems. [L175 00:00](../lessons/175-what-to-focus-on-to-scale-to-10m-mth-by-rev.md)
3. **Budget sheet** (prices as shown in the course; verify before quoting):

| Line | Amount | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Insense brand plan | $500/month (other figures: $1,200/quarter; $1,500/3 months) | [L140](../lessons/140-action-item-sign-up-for-insense.md); [L138 01:36](../lessons/138-ugc-content-overview-part-1.md) |
| Product + shipping per seeded creator | brand's COGS (not in course maths) | (derived) |
| Paid UGC (optional) | $100-$150/video, cap $200; $1,000+ credits + 10% fee | [L139](../lessons/139-ugc-content-overview-part-2.md) |
| Ambassador retainers | $500/month each (+ performance, cap $10,000) | [L154](../lessons/154-how-to-contact-winning-creators-affiliate.md) |
| Whitelisting access | $0-$250/month per creator | [L162 03:46](../lessons/162-whitelisting-overview-proof-it-works.md) |
| Leadsie (only when whitelisting) | 14-day trial, then $99/month | [L163 page text](../lessons/163-how-to-setup-whitelisting-creators.md) |
| Atria (optional, reporting) | not stated | [L161](../lessons/161-action-item-complete-your-feedback-loops.md) |
| The Daily Virals (optional, inspiration) | 30-day trial code; price unclear | [L136 06:41](../lessons/136-how-to-get-viral-ugc-inspo-for-creators-ads.md) |
| VA for daily checks and follow-ups | not stated | [L135 05:26–06:23](../lessons/135-why-content-is-so-important-part-2.md) |

4. **Size the roster** (derived): creators ≈ (target raw ads/week ÷ 3 cuts) ÷ 1.5 videos per creator per week; over-hire for seeding no-shows. [L135 01:44](../lessons/135-why-content-is-so-important-part-2.md)

## Phase 1: Concepts and framework (days 1-3)
1. Pull angles, avatar facts and customer language from the research doc ([research doc](../concepts/research-doc.md), [ad definitions](../concepts/ad-definitions.md)).
2. Optional inspiration: The Daily Virals feed for the category → comments, transcript, AI breakdown → tag the hook, the "switch" and the demo format → turn into concept lines. Don't refilm 1:1. [L136 00:54–05:07](../lessons/136-how-to-get-viral-ugc-inspo-for-creators-ads.md)
3. Write the master framework (≤9 concepts) with the [template](../frameworks/insense-creator-pipeline.md#creator-framework-template); add B-roll rows; make gender versions if needed. [L148](../lessons/148-sending-frameworks-to-creators-part-2.md)
4. QA each concept: is it a scene the creator can really film? does it imply a result or number? If it does, verify it or cut it (derived, repo rule 2).

## Phase 2: Insense setup and launch (days 3-5)
1. Brand plan with code → add brand → seeding campaign (Awareness & Reach, ~90 creators, filters from avatar, no follower/ER limits, 4 screening questions, one creative idea ≤500 chars, posting optional) → send to review. [L140](../lessons/140-action-item-sign-up-for-insense.md)–[L144](../lessons/144-action-item-launch-your-first-insense-campaign.md)
2. Add the 2026 ask for 30-day partnership access with a screenshot of the ad format. [L165 01:29–02:10](../lessons/165-partnership-ads-updated-strategy.md)
3. Copy the client Insense sheet template. [L146](../lessons/146-how-to-download-creators-upload-to-sheet.md)
4. If applications are thin after the first days and budget exists, add a paid campaign. [L139](../lessons/139-ugc-content-overview-part-2.md)

## Phase 3: Vet, hire, ship (week 1-2)
1. Daily: vet applicants (pitch → social → Insense profile/reviews → screening answers); Shortlisted message + thumbs-up. [L145](../lessons/145-how-to-process-creators-add-to-shortlist.md)
2. Export to sheet → highlight shortlisted → confirm. [L146](../lessons/146-how-to-download-creators-upload-to-sheet.md)
3. Hire → Hired Creator message (email, address, product-only agreement, product choice) → Product Sent message (4:5 crop rule) → curated framework (3-6 concepts). [L147](../lessons/147-how-to-hire-approved-creators-part-1.md); [L137 01:36–02:10](../lessons/137-does-insense-still-work.md)
4. Full settings, templates and close-out rules: [Insense creator pipeline](../frameworks/insense-creator-pipeline.md).

## Phase 4: Collect, triage, launch (weeks 2-4)
1. Follow up every 2-3 days; never more than 7 days without contact; 10-day / 14-day close-out rules. [L138 page text](../lessons/138-ugc-content-overview-part-1.md); [L151 05:28](../lessons/151-how-to-follow-up-upload-content-part-1.md)
2. Download all files (with and without audio) → one Drive folder per creator → `✅ <first name> - DCT` or X → Complete. [L151](../lessons/151-how-to-follow-up-upload-content-part-1.md); [L152](../lessons/152-how-to-follow-up-upload-content-part-2.md)
3. Launch ✅ content raw (no edits, no captions) in the **raw content campaign**: same budget logic, UTMs and targeting as the 3:2:2; three cuts per video, or three videos per ad set; 2026 outline uses flexible ads (3-6 visuals, 2 copies, 2 headlines, 1 landing page). [L98 00:00–03:36](../lessons/098-how-to-set-up-raw-content-campaign.md); [L93 02:47–03:29](../lessons/093-full-media-buying-strategy-outline-2026.md) Skip the raw campaign if there is no raw content. [L98 page text](../lessons/098-how-to-set-up-raw-content-campaign.md)
4. Run eligible creator ads as partnership ads (Dynamic identity). [L165 00:32](../lessons/165-partnership-ads-updated-strategy.md)
5. Ad names carry the creator's name (needed for Phase 6 reports). [L160 01:27](../lessons/160-feedback-loops-with-your-brand-ambassadors.md)

## Phase 5: Creator verdict and offers (after the test window)
1. Rank creators by spend and ROAS; label the ads with the account's winner tiers ([winning ad labels](../concepts/winning-ad-labels.md)).
2. Winners → retainer-only or performance email; agreement matching the email. [L154](../lessons/154-how-to-contact-winning-creators-affiliate.md); [L156](../lessons/156-how-to-onboard-brand-ambassadors.md)
3. Good-but-not-winning first video → hire and give feedback; the second video can win (instructor claim). [L137 05:42](../lessons/137-does-insense-still-work.md)
4. Details, tiers and calculator: [Brand ambassador program](../frameworks/brand-ambassador-program.md).

## Phase 7: Creator-identity ads (winning creators)
1. Partnership ads for ambassadors' top creatives (Jan 2026 default). [L165 02:10](../lessons/165-partnership-ads-updated-strategy.md)
2. Whitelisting for winning creators on retainer: agreement clause, $0-$250/month, Leadsie link, identity swap, creator-written copy. [L162](../lessons/162-whitelisting-overview-proof-it-works.md); [L163](../lessons/163-how-to-setup-whitelisting-creators.md)
3. Placement: < $2k/day inside main 3:2:2 or raw; > $2k/day separate creator campaign (CBO). Keep a native twin of top ads. [L164](../lessons/164-how-to-test-whitelisted-ads.md); [L162 05:58](../lessons/162-whitelisting-overview-proof-it-works.md)
4. Details: [Whitelisting and partnership ads](../concepts/whitelisting-and-partnership-ads.md).

## Output checklist
- [ ] Fit verdict + stage goal + budget sheet + roster size
- [ ] Master framework (≤9 concepts) + gender versions + per-creator copies (3-6 concepts)
- [ ] Insense brand profile, seeding campaign (and paid campaign if used), screening questions
- [ ] Client Insense sheet (All / Selected / Hired), kept private
- [ ] Message set: Shortlisted, Hired (3 asks), Product Sent, nudges, partnership-access ask
- [ ] Drive folders per creator, ✅ DCT / X marked; deals Completed
- [ ] Raw content campaign live with creator-named ads
- [ ] Creator verdict table (spend, ROAS, purchases → offer / no offer)
- [ ] Offer emails, matching agreement variant (lawyer-reviewed), onboarding deck, welcome message, first report + 3 ideas
- [ ] One channel per ambassador; payout sheet; monthly per-creator reports
- [ ] Monthly engine report

## Failure modes
| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Few applications | Cheap, niche or unappealing product; no pay | Raise the value of the seeded product; add a paid campaign | [L139 00:32; 03:18](../lessons/139-ugc-content-overview-part-2.md); [L143 03:37](../lessons/143-how-to-create-an-insense-campaign.md) |
| Everyone films concept #1 | First-pick bias | Per-creator curation | [L137 00:58–02:10](../lessons/137-does-insense-still-work.md) |
| Stiff, fake-looking videos | Over-scripting | Framework; "unscripted" do's | [L148 04:23–05:07](../lessons/148-sending-frameworks-to-creators-part-2.md) |
| Creators ghost after product ships | No cadence | 2-3 day follow-ups; 14-day support escalation | [L138 Rule #2](../lessons/138-ugc-content-overview-part-1.md) |
| Creator demands payment after shipping | Product-only terms not confirmed | Hired message item 3 before shipping | [L147 page text](../lessons/147-how-to-hire-approved-creators-part-1.md) |
| Raw ads confuse learnings | Mixed with strategised ads | Separate raw campaign | [L98 01:46](../lessons/098-how-to-set-up-raw-content-campaign.md) |
| Ambassadors make more of what flops | No feedback loop | Monthly per-creator report | [L160 00:46](../lessons/160-feedback-loops-with-your-brand-ambassadors.md) |
| Retainer creator asks for a raise after seeing ROAS | Money metrics shown | Hide spend/ROAS/purchase value | [L160 03:15](../lessons/160-feedback-loops-with-your-brand-ambassadors.md) |
| Main spend collapses when a creator revokes access | Over-leveraged on whitelisting | Native twins; separate campaign | [L162 05:58](../lessons/162-whitelisting-overview-proof-it-works.md); [L164 03:59](../lessons/164-how-to-test-whitelisted-ads.md) |
| Lots of content, few winners | Volume without research or intent | Hit-rate tracking; research-led concepts | [L175 06:47–07:46](../lessons/175-what-to-focus-on-to-scale-to-10m-mth-by-rev.md); [L133 00:51](../lessons/133-winning-humidifier-ad-learnings-shaun.md) |
