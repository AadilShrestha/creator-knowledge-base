# Playbook: launch a new product end to end
> The whole Evolve loop for one product, as 12 phases with a gate each: screen the product, research, desires and avatars, angles, concept cards, make the ads, set targets, launch the 3:2:2 CBO, label results after 7 days, scale or cut on rules, run learnings, iterate on what got spend, and build a creator pipeline for content volume. When nothing wins, escalate: more feedback loops → more research → study again.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** planning a launch for a new product or brand; auditing where a stalled brand broke the loop; writing a 30-60 day launch plan; deciding which framework page to open next.
- **Inputs → outputs:** product + economics (price, COGS, shipping) + brand access (store, ad account, reviews) + team size + daily budget → a phase-by-phase plan with owners, outputs and gates; per-phase artifacts (go/no-go card, research doc, avatars, concept cards, campaign spec, KPI targets, week-1 labels, learnings, iteration plan, creator framework).
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** run the system as Study → Research → Create → Test → Feedback Loop and place every problem on it. [L174 01:46](../lessons/174-im-overwhelmed-stuck-wat-do.md)
  - **DO** define a test result by spend: "Launch tests in 1 campaign. If it gets spend = good. If it does not get spend = not good." [L174 02:42](../lessons/174-im-overwhelmed-stuck-wat-do.md)
  - **DO** write how the product will stand out (new mechanism or underserved avatar) before spending on a test. [L171 19:15–19:46](../lessons/171-how-to-make-any-product-work-brand-building-pt-1.md)
  - **DO** prepare before making ads: "The effort that you put in prior to making the ad is the difference between the ad usually winning and the ad flopping." [L56 01:18](../lessons/056-organizing-insights-overview-must-watch.md)
  - **DO** test new ads inside the one broad CBO; don't force spend on ignored ads in a separate ABO. [L92 00:51–04:05](../lessons/092-evolve-facebook-ads-philosophy.md)
  - **DON'T** turn off ads that are working: "THE DUMBEST THING YOU CAN DO IS TURN OFF ADS THAT ARE WORKING". [L94 page text](../lessons/094-how-to-set-up-a-3-2-2-campaign.md)
  - **DON'T** run create → test → create → test without feedback loops: "That's your problem." [L174 04:17](../lessons/174-im-overwhelmed-stuck-wat-do.md)
  - **DON'T** blame media buying first: "for 99% of you guys, it's not media buying" (instructor claim). [L174 07:53](../lessons/174-im-overwhelmed-stuck-wat-do.md)
  - **DON'T** rip competitor or TikTok Shop videos into ads (taught in L172 and the raw-content campaign). **Don't execute.** Brief original creative from the teardown. [L172 54:30–59:30](../lessons/172-live-product-research-w-kalodata-pt-2.md)
  - **DO** prioritise winning-ad rate over raw volume once past the volume stage: "Winning ads per month is all we care about." [L175 06:47–07:46](../lessons/175-what-to-focus-on-to-scale-to-10m-mth-by-rev.md)
- **See also:** [Evolve growth loop](../concepts/evolve-growth-loop.md) · [research → first concepts](research-to-first-concepts.md) · [weekly media buying routine](weekly-media-buying-routine.md) · [creator program setup](creator-program-setup.md) · [KPI thresholds](../libraries/kpi-thresholds.md) · Copywriting: [iterate on test results](../../copywriting/playbooks/iterate-on-test-results.md)

## Phase notes (numbers and rules that the gate depends on)

### 0. Product screen
- AOV $60+ after bundles; ≥3x markup on COGS + shipping; trend not declining; differentiator or no-go. [L171 08:13–10:47; 17:13](../lessons/171-how-to-make-any-product-work-brand-building-pt-1.md)
- Prefer solution-/product-aware entry for a first product. [L171 16:11–16:42](../lessons/171-how-to-make-any-product-work-brand-building-pt-1.md)
- Product + store build capped at 8 hours, then launch. [L171 05:16–06:17](../lessons/171-how-to-make-any-product-work-brand-building-pt-1.md)

### 5-6. Concepts → ads
- New concepts: test congruent hooks only, bigger swings; single-variable tests only on winners. [L67 05:49–09:35](../lessons/067-action-item-find-new-mechanisms.md)
- Under $100K/month: Marksman for images, Sniper for videos. [L83 12:22; 23:01](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md)
- Planning is Start Here; making ads is the Copywriting program. [L87](../lessons/087-overview-of-how-to-make-ads.md); [L85 00:58–02:25](../lessons/085-the-3-0-evolve-growth-guide.md)
- Run the 13 execution checks on every draft; test one angle in multiple formats before judging it. [L88 10:30–28:21](../lessons/088-the-execution-problem.md)
- UGC: creators get frameworks, not scripts; ≤9 concepts per framework, 3-6 per creator (first-pick bias). [L148 00:00–07:29](../lessons/148-sending-frameworks-to-creators-part-2.md); [L137 00:58–02:10](../lessons/137-does-insense-still-work.md); [creator frameworks vs scripts](../concepts/creator-frameworks-vs-scripts.md)

### 7. Targets
- No flat "3x". Breakeven ROAS from gross margin; Scaling ROAS = Breakeven + 1. [L101 00:00–02:50](../lessons/101-how-to-set-targets-kpis.md)
- Under ~$50k/month spend: blended ROAS + in-platform ROAS; skip LTV/NCPA. [L101 07:38; 09:26](../lessons/101-how-to-set-targets-kpis.md)
- Judge on money first (spend, results); hook/hold without purchases mean little. [L100 00:00–00:56; 13:08](../lessons/100-how-to-see-ads-performance-our-columns.md)

### 8. Launch
| Setting | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Campaign | One Sales CBO, manual setup, ≥ $100/day, website only, maximize conversions | [L94 00:00–00:58](../lessons/094-how-to-set-up-a-3-2-2-campaign.md) |
| Targeting | Broad: age, gender, location only; Advantage+ placements | [L94 02:47–06:03](../lessons/094-how-to-set-up-a-3-2-2-campaign.md) |
| Ads | Manual single image/video, no Dynamic Creative/flexible; Meta AI enhancements off; 9:16 master | [L94 01:52; 06:33–07:34](../lessons/094-how-to-set-up-a-3-2-2-campaign.md) |
| Ad sets | One concept each: 3 creatives × 2 copies × 2 headlines; champions ad set of proven post IDs as benchmark | [L94 08:30–20:15](../lessons/094-how-to-set-up-a-3-2-2-campaign.md) |
| Volume | A few hundred $/day → ≤ 5 ad sets live | [L94 23:36–25:29](../lessons/094-how-to-set-up-a-3-2-2-campaign.md) |
| Test length | 3 days minimum, 7 if traction; turn off at ad-set level | [L94 26:29–28:31](../lessons/094-how-to-set-up-a-3-2-2-campaign.md) |

| Naming | Batch # at ad-set level | [L85 22:52–23:47](../lessons/085-the-3-0-evolve-growth-guide.md) |
Zombie, ABO scaling, raw content and promo campaigns, naming templates: [campaign structure 3:2:2](../frameworks/campaign-structure-322.md).

### 9. Read week 1
- Wait 7 days, label, write learnings, then Done. [L85 21:54–22:52](../lessons/085-the-3-0-evolve-growth-guide.md)
- Labels by share of campaign spend and ROAS vs campaign, tiered by monthly **spend**: $0-100k/mo ≈ 60-70% of campaign spend; $100-250k ≈ 20%; $500k+ ≈ 7.6% of account spend; Breakthrough also requires the budget to rise because of it. [L112 00:00–06:39](../lessons/112-winning-ad-definitions-0-mo-1m-mo.md)
- Labels are provisional for weeks 2-3. [L110 16:59](../lessons/110-winning-ads-definition-part-1.md)
- Fix by label: KPI Winner → hook, visuals/messaging, bridge, widen angle; Spend Winner → hook honesty, CTA, belief, price-value; Breakthrough → iterate aggressively. [L110 20:09–21:44](../lessons/110-winning-ads-definition-part-1.md)

### 10. Scale / cut (every 24 h)
- KPI hit for 48-72 h (or a new winning asset) and ≥60% click-based purchases → +20% budget. Below breakeven 24-48 h → −20% (if above minimum spend). At minimum spend → work outside the account (new tests, funnel, research, offers). [L115 01:01–15:42](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md)
- Turning off a top spender: only if the next 2-3 ads would do better with its spend; a new ad burning spend at a bad KPI can go in 24-48 h. [L120 02:18–09:11](../lessons/120-how-to-see-if-you-should-turn-off-high-spend-ads.md)
- Full table: [scaling rules](../frameworks/scaling-rules.md).

### 11-12. Learnings → iteration
- Review losers as well as winners; recorded team call on losers. [L122](../lessons/122-how-to-do-learnings-part-1-losers.md); [learnings review](../frameworks/learnings-review.md)
- Benchmarks are secondary: hook ≥25%, hold ≥30% of hook, CTR ≥1%; "focus on... ROAS and CPA". [L88 31:47–33:16](../lessons/088-the-execution-problem.md)
- Confident angle failed in Marksman → second batch as Sniper; confirmed execution error → a third; after three, change angle. [L88 33:16–34:04](../lessons/088-the-execution-problem.md)
- Let spend pick what to iterate; don't kill a spending concept over one soft ROAS; keep researching the winning element. [L131 03:26–07:40](../lessons/131-how-to-do-iterations-variations-live-example.md)

| Ops | Daily Insense check 5-20 min; follow up non-responders every 2-3 days | [L138 page text](../lessons/138-ugc-content-overview-part-1.md) |
| Paid campaigns | $100-150 per video, never above $200 | [L139 page text](../lessons/139-ugc-content-overview-part-2.md) |
| Keep | Only creators whose ads got spent on; $500/month retainer for 1 video/week, or performance program for 2-3/week | [L154 00:00–04:40](../lessons/154-how-to-contact-winning-creators-affiliate.md) |
| Whitelisting | Winning creators only; ~$100-250/month; agreement | [L162](../lessons/162-whitelisting-overview-proof-it-works.md) |
| Where to run | < $2k/day: in the main 3:2:2 or raw content campaign; above: separate campaign | [L164 00:00–01:33](../lessons/164-how-to-test-whitelisted-ads.md) |
