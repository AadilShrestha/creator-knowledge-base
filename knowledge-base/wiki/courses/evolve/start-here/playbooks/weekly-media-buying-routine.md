# Playbook: weekly media buying routine
> The operating rhythm for a running Evolve account: **every 24 h** run the scaling flowchart, guard new ads and swap one dead ad for a new one; **on day 3** read traction by spend; **on day 7** label every test, route it (keep, ABO, zombie, off), run learnings on winners and losers, plan iterations and launch the next batch; **in weeks 2-3** re-check labels; **monthly** refresh winners, tiers and attribution. Promo days switch to intra-day surf scaling.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** running or reviewing an account week to week; writing a daily budget recommendation; preparing the weekly learnings call; deciding what to do on a given day of a test; an account is "on a roller coaster".
- **Inputs → outputs:** targets (breakeven ROAS, scaling ROAS, target CPA, minimum daily spend), account monthly-spend tier, Ads Manager data on 7-day click (campaign, ad set, ad: spend, ROAS/CPA, share of spend, frequency, hook, hold, CTR, launch date), creative queue → a **daily log** (budget move per campaign + reason), a **weekly report** (labels, routing, learnings cards, iteration plan, next batch), and the checklist state for the month.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** make budget decisions once per 24 h per campaign with the L115 flowchart: +20% only after 48-72 h consistently above target with > 60% click-based purchases; −20% after 24-48 h below breakeven if above minimum spend. [L115 01:01-09:41](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md)
  - **DO** read tests by spend: "If it gets spend = good. If it does not get spend = not good." [L174 02:42](../lessons/174-im-overwhelmed-stuck-wat-do.md)
  - **DO** wait 7 days before labelling and mark nothing Done until learnings are written. [L85 21:54-22:52](../lessons/085-the-3-0-evolve-growth-guide.md) [L124 07:54](../lessons/124-how-to-do-learnings-part-3-losers.md)
  - **DON'T** turn off ads that are working: "THE DUMBEST THING YOU CAN DO IS TURN OFF ADS THAT ARE WORKING". [L94 page](../lessons/094-how-to-set-up-a-3-2-2-campaign.md)
  - **DON'T** force spend on ads Meta ignored; route them to zombie or learnings. [L92 02:29-04:05](../lessons/092-evolve-facebook-ads-philosophy.md)
  - **DON'T** change structure, bid strategy or testing process when results drop: "Make better ads. Make better offers. Make better pages." [L121 35:19](../lessons/121-how-to-deal-with-roller-coaster-performance.md)
  - **IF** the account can't scale **THEN** "Make a more Ads" before touching media buying. [L119 page](../lessons/119-action-item-scale-your-ads.md)
  - **IF** you sit at minimum daily spend **THEN** hold budget and work outside the account (new DCTs, funnel check, learnings, research, offers). [L115 11:04-15:42](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md)
  - **DO** end every learnings session with one action, even "don't repeat A, B, C". [L124 23:12-24:51](../lessons/124-how-to-do-learnings-part-3-losers.md)
  - **DON'T** quote instructor results ("10k to 70k per day in 24 hours") as expectations. [L108 04:05](../lessons/108-how-to-set-up-a-promo-campaign.md)
- **See also:** [Scaling rules](../frameworks/scaling-rules.md) · [KPIs and columns](../frameworks/kpis-and-columns.md) · [Campaign structure 3:2:2](../frameworks/campaign-structure-322.md) · [Winning-ad labels](../concepts/winning-ad-labels.md) · [Click-based attribution](../concepts/click-based-attribution.md) · [Learnings review](../frameworks/learnings-review.md) · [Rewrite losers and iterate](../frameworks/rewrite-losers-and-iterate.md) · [KPI thresholds](../libraries/kpi-thresholds.md) · [Rules library](../libraries/rules.md) · [Ad account audit](../rubrics/ad-account-audit.md) · Copywriting: [iterate on test results](../../copywriting/playbooks/iterate-on-test-results.md) · Tether Lab: [test and iterate](../../../../playbooks/test-and-iterate.md)

## Daily routine (every 24 h)
### D1. Budget move per campaign (L115 flowchart, condensed)
Read 7-day-click data at the same time each day. Count hours like the lesson: Monday above target = first 24 h; Tuesday above again = 48 h. [L115 02:19](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md)

| State (campaign and account) | Move | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Above target, hard-end-date promo | Scale now (promo ladder below) | [L115 01:01](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md) |
| Above target 48-72 h, or a new winning ad / offer / page, AND > 60% click-based | +20% (15/10/5% at very large budgets) | [L115 01:01-05:51](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md) |
| Consistently 50-100% above target | Raise by that %, "at your own risk" (target 2.5x, actual 3.75x → +50%) | [L115 05:51](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md) |
| Above target but < 48 h, or click-based share ≤ 60% | Wait 24 h | [L115 02:19-04:36](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md) |
| Below breakeven 24-48 h, above minimum spend | −20% + out-of-account list | [L115 08:23](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md) |
| Between breakeven and target 48-72 h, above minimum spend | −20% + out-of-account list; under 48 h wait 24 h | [L115 09:41](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md) |
| Any losing state at minimum spend | Hold budget; out-of-account list | [L115 11:04](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md) |

Out-of-account list: launch queued DCTs · check the whole funnel for bugs · analyse ads getting spend and process learnings · deep research for new avatars and mechanisms · start offer testing (as new ads in the same CBO). [L115 11:04-15:42](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md) ABO scaling ad sets use their own rule (double after 2-3 solid days). [L97 07:02](../lessons/097-how-to-set-up-abo-scaling-campaign.md) Full table and conflicts: [scaling rules](../frameworks/scaling-rules.md).

### D2. New-ad guard
| Situation | Action | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1-2, little spend or weak first day | Leave it; day 1 may not convert | [L94 page](../lessons/094-how-to-set-up-a-3-2-2-campaign.md) [L131 12:14-13:41](../lessons/131-how-to-do-iterations-variations-live-example.md) |
| New ad (< 48 h) takes most spend at a bad KPI, account < $10-20k/day | Give it "a day or two", then turn off the ad set if still bad; "you can always turn an ad back on" | [L120 09:11](../lessons/120-how-to-see-if-you-should-turn-off-high-spend-ads.md) |
| Same, account ≥ $10k/day | Daily spending limit on new test ad sets at launch (e.g. $500); lift when strong or if no spend in 1-2 days. Meta won't auto-kill inside a CBO | [L107 02:15-06:00](../lessons/107-how-to-protect-against-bad-ads-over-spending.md) |
| An older proven top spender shows lower ROAS than a tiny new ad | Keep it unless the next 2-3 ads would beat it with its spend ("Most of the time... NO") | [L120 04:25-09:11](../lessons/120-how-to-see-if-you-should-turn-off-high-spend-ads.md) |
| A spike: one ad, many clicks, poor ROAS | Check LPV ÷ link clicks; low ratio = clickbait, turn off | [L107 03:34](../lessons/107-how-to-protect-against-bad-ads-over-spending.md) |
| CPM/CPC fall sharply and odd ads suddenly take spend | Treat as a Meta-side anomaly; pause what hogs spend at a bad KPI; keep launching | [L121 05:12-06:22](../lessons/121-how-to-deal-with-roller-coaster-performance.md) |

### D3. One out, one in
Each day: find the ad set from the last 7 days with no spend or poor performance, turn it off (duplicate its best-spending ad into zombie), launch one new ad set. "One ad per day" ≈ seven a week. [L124 07:54-08:37](../lessons/124-how-to-do-learnings-part-3-losers.md) [L96 02:19-03:10](../lessons/096-how-to-set-up-zombie-graveyard-campaign.md) Keep live test ad sets ≤ daily budget ÷ (3 × target CPA), and ≤ 5 under $1k/day. [L120 page](../lessons/120-how-to-see-if-you-should-turn-off-high-spend-ads.md) [L93 sheet](../lessons/093-full-media-buying-strategy-outline-2026.md) Teams that batch weekly instead (3-4 concepts per brand per week in the L121 agency example) do D3 on launch day. [L121 50:53-52:19](../lessons/121-how-to-deal-with-roller-coaster-performance.md)

## Day 3: traction read
- Use the 3-day average and "judge traction based on the spend": is its spend rising each day and relative to the campaign? [L94 27:02-27:32](../lessons/094-how-to-set-up-a-3-2-2-campaign.md)
- No spend by day 3 is a note, not a verdict; the label waits for day 7 (agent default; see [conflicts](#conflicts-side-by-side-with-agent-default)).
- Don't kill on one number: DCT #138 went $64 → $100 → ~$5.7k on days 1-3; a ~1.9 ROAS new concept is not a kill. [L131 04:10; 12:14](../lessons/131-how-to-do-iterations-variations-live-example.md)

## Day 7: label and route
1. Pull each test ad set: share of initial-campaign spend (account spend at $500k+/month), ROAS vs the campaign's ROAS, and whether campaign budget rose because of it. [L112 06:39](../lessons/112-winning-ad-definitions-0-mo-1m-mo.md)
2. Label with the tier bar from [winning-ad labels](../concepts/winning-ad-labels.md) ($0-100k/mo ≈ 60-70% of campaign spend; $100-250k ≈ 20%; $500k+ ≈ 7.6% of account spend). [L112 00:00-02:35](../lessons/112-winning-ad-definitions-0-mo-1m-mo.md)
3. Route:

| Label | Stays in CBO? | Also | Learnings | Iteration | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Breakthrough | Yes | ≥ $2k/day: own ABO ad set at 10-20% of daily budget | Winner teardown, 30-40 min | Iterate aggressively; 3 variations in priority order | [L97 02:53-06:14](../lessons/097-how-to-set-up-abo-scaling-campaign.md) [L110 20:09-21:44](../lessons/110-winning-ads-definition-part-1.md) [L133 08:21](../lessons/133-winning-humidifier-ad-learnings-shaun.md) |
| Spend Winner | Yes | ≥ $2k/day: ABO at $100-$200/day to throttle | Why spend but weak KPI (hook honesty, CTA, belief, price-value) | Fix KPI | [L97 03:41](../lessons/097-how-to-set-up-abo-scaling-campaign.md) [L110 20:09-21:44](../lessons/110-winning-ads-definition-part-1.md) |
| KPI Winner | Yes | ≥ $2k/day: ABO at what it was getting | Why Meta didn't push it (hook, visuals, bridge, angle width) | Fix spend; watch W2-W3 | [L97 04:39](../lessons/097-how-to-set-up-abo-scaling-campaign.md) [L112 24:16](../lessons/112-winning-ad-definitions-0-mo-1m-mo.md) |
| Loser | Off at ad-set level | Best-spending ad → zombie | 10-15 min; find the variant Meta preferred | Hook-only fix if the concept was good; else new angle or format | [L94 page](../lessons/094-how-to-set-up-a-3-2-2-campaign.md) [L96 02:19](../lessons/096-how-to-set-up-zombie-graveyard-campaign.md) [L122 03:39-07:36](../lessons/122-how-to-do-learnings-part-1-losers.md) |
| $0 spend | Off | — | No data: ask why customers didn't care | "Avoid A, B, C" applied to the next angles | [L124 28:20-29:59](../lessons/124-how-to-do-learnings-part-3-losers.md) |

4. Proven winners (Breakthrough/Spend Winner) that should run long go into the champions/winners ad set by native duplicate with "show existing reactions", no edits; post ID if that fails. [L103 01:13-02:50](../lessons/103-how-to-duplicate-ads-into-champions-new-way.md) (L97 drops the champions ad set: see [campaign structure 3:2:2](../frameworks/campaign-structure-322.md).)
5. Write the label sentence the doc models, e.g. "in my spend category of $0-$100k/mo, after Week 1 the ad spent 50% of my campaign's budget and increased my total daily spend by 70%." [L110 18:34](../lessons/110-winning-ads-definition-part-1.md)
6. Update the Growth Guide status from "Learning" to the result; don't mark Done until learnings exist. [L85 21:54-22:52](../lessons/085-the-3-0-evolve-growth-guide.md)

## Weekly learnings and iteration
Run the full method in [learnings review](../frameworks/learnings-review.md) and route outputs through [rewrite losers and iterate](../frameworks/rewrite-losers-and-iterate.md). The routine only fixes the order and the time boxes:

| Step | Do | Source |
|---|---|---|
| W1 | Record per concept, in order: amount spent, ROAS/CPA, CVR, AOV, frequency, CTR; video: hook, hold, average play time | [L124 00:30-02:53](../lessons/124-how-to-do-learnings-part-3-losers.md) |
| W2 | Losers (10-15 min each): spend share over 7 days (~2% = loser; ≥ 10% early signal), top-spend variant and what differs, compare to known winners; skip data review on $0 spend | [L124 05:13-13:26; 29:59](../lessons/124-how-to-do-learnings-part-3-losers.md) |
| W3 | Winners (30-40 min each): play and pause the hook, "how is this ad making the customer feel and why?", says vs communicates, check bridge pain against Reddit | [L125 03:11-16:47](../lessons/125-how-to-do-learnings-part-4-winning-ads.md) |

## When performance drops
1. Check attribution first on roller-coaster days (view-through share). [L116 10:50](../lessons/116-how-to-understand-facebook-ads-attribution.md)
2. Zoom out to 7 days: daily whiplash between batches is normal. [L120 03:08](../lessons/120-how-to-see-if-you-should-turn-off-high-spend-ads.md)
3. Classify the cause: people / market / Meta / strategy; only strategy gets action. Don't change media-buying strategy, product or testing process. [L121 35:19](../lessons/121-how-to-deal-with-roller-coaster-performance.md)
4. Apply the L115 cut path (−20% unless at minimum spend) and the out-of-account list. [L115 08:23-15:42](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md)
5. Strategy fixes: fewer trending-desire ads, more permanent-desire angles; if most ads are product-aware in a sophisticated market, add unaware and problem-aware story ads to an advertorial or listicle; test new pages and offers as new ads in the CBO. [L121 09:00-15:36; 23:20-25:50; 42:22](../lessons/121-how-to-deal-with-roller-coaster-performance.md)
6. Plateau with fatigued winners: build three more winning ads with the same strategies (not a media-buying change). [L1 01:39](../lessons/001-start-here-overview-of-evolve.md)
7. Still flat after several cycles: escalate per [launch a new product end to end](launch-a-new-product-end-to-end.md#when-nothing-wins-escalation).

- **Evolve Copywriting:** the [iterate on test results](../../copywriting/playbooks/iterate-on-test-results.md) playbook and [iteration outcomes](../../copywriting/concepts/iteration-outcomes.md) use the same four labels; Copywriting adds a ~$1k spend floor before reading hook/hold, which Start Here lacks (use it in W1-W3). - **Tether Lab:** [test and iterate](../../../../playbooks/test-and-iterate.md) launches 3-5 new ads a week, changes one variable on winners, scales +10-15%/day and waits ~2 weeks before killing slow "Ought" ads. Evolve launches daily or weekly batches into one CBO, takes bigger swings, scales +20% after 48-72 h and labels at day 7. Default for our accounts: the Evolve cadence and gates; use Tether's slow-start exception only for ads with rising repeat purchases.
