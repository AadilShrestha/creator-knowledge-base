# Ad teardowns library (Evolve Start Here)
> Every real ad, static, landing page and campaign critiqued in the Start Here lessons (80 ad rows + 7 positioning moves, merged from 108 harvested rows), grouped by niche with what works, what fails, the fix and the principle, each cited to lesson and timestamp.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** tearing down a competitor ad, finding a precedent in our niche, naming why an ad works (awareness level, desire, avatar call-out, mechanism), or QA-ing our draft against failures Evolve has already called out.
- **Inputs → outputs:** competitor ad or our draft + niche → the matching niche section and the [format index](#format-and-principle-index) → 2–4 precedent rows → the **Principle** column applied to our product, with our own words, visuals and real proof.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** label the awareness level before judging the ad: offer-first for most aware, reasons and trust for product aware, cross-category comparison for solution aware, problem scenes for problem aware, emotion → problem → solution for unaware. [L010 04:34–16:54](../lessons/010-market-awareness.md)
  - **DO** check that hook, hold and body carry the same angle and the same sub-avatar; generic holds and feature-list bodies are the most common failure in the student ads reviewed. [L083 08:57–11:23](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md) [L083 24:48–27:11](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md)
  - **DO** pick comparison callouts from real reviews and comments (filters, cleaning, mold), not aesthetics. [L133 00:00–07:24](../lessons/133-winning-humidifier-ad-learnings-shaun.md)
  - **DON'T** read a teardown as proof it works: most results are instructor claims or old library ads ("Old ad (133 weeks)"). [L004 02:18–04:14](../lessons/004-100-winning-ads-document.md)
  - **DON'T** copy wording the course itself calls used up ("I think I just got scammed"): "Reframe, don't copy". [L126 00:53–07:46](../lessons/126-how-to-pull-frameworks-from-ads.md)
  - **DON'T** reuse stats, counts or social proof from a teardown ("10,000 shift workers", "56.4%", "$2.65 per meal"): they belong to that brand and the course doesn't verify them.
  - **IF** a format "didn't work" for one team but scaled elsewhere **THEN** fix execution (words, images) before dropping the format. [L088 07:23–08:11](../lessons/088-the-execution-problem.md)
  - **IF** the market is sophisticated (e.g. "cortisol" weight claims) **THEN** a reason alone isn't enough; lead with a new mechanism. [L124 21:27–22:17](../lessons/124-how-to-do-learnings-part-3-losers.md)
  - **IF** you are tempted by fear framing **THEN** ground it in a real, checkable problem; the course both says "fear motivates more than positive framing" [L123 08:41–15:23](../lessons/123-how-to-do-learnings-part-2-winning-ads.md) and "I am not recommending fear-mongering at all" [L079 card 16](../lessons/079-how-to-come-up-with-ad-angles.md), and dials down threat stats [L068 13:00–17:54](../lessons/068-how-to-find-apply-new-information-part-3.md).
- **See also:** [Hooks](hooks.md) · [Angles](angles.md) · [Rewrites](rewrites.md) · [Templates](templates.md) · [Rules](rules.md) · [Copywriting ad teardowns](../../copywriting/libraries/ad-teardowns.md) (Spencer's 186 rows with issue codes) · [Tether Lab ad teardowns](../../../../libraries/ad-teardowns.md) (psychology-tagged) · [Market awareness levels](../concepts/market-awareness-levels.md) · [Learnings review](../frameworks/learnings-review.md)

## ⚠️ Copy the mechanism, not the asset
Every row is a precedent, not a script. Keep the structure (bold number + problem, "This is X. This is Y.", Them vs Us callouts, discovery story) and rebuild it with our product, our customer language and our proof.

**⛔ Don't execute: lesson 172 ripping.** Shaun teaches downloading top TikTok Shop videos from Kalodata (top 10–20 by revenue, picking ones that don't mention the brand), clearing their metadata with a tool or by re-exporting in Premiere Pro, CapCut or Canva, and running them as ads beside your own creatives. [L172 57:34–59:30](../lessons/172-live-product-research-w-kalodata-pt-2.md) He also calls ripped creatives "borrowed time" with takedown risk. [L172 34:07–36:39](../lessons/172-live-product-research-w-kalodata-pt-2.md) This repo forbids re-uploading others' videos. **Compliant alternative:** use Kalodata, TikTok Shop and Ad Library ads as research (angle, hook type, format, length), then brief original creators or write AI-VO scripts on that mechanism. L172 rows below are flagged ⛔ and kept for research only.

## Format and principle index
| IF the ad you're studying is… | THEN look at | Principle to extract |
|---|---|---|
| A bold-number or bold-claim static | TD049 (14+ hours), TD007 (accidentally 12 lb), TD009 (double chin in 7 days) | Number or claim against a named problem; body copy completes it |
| "This is X. This is Y." / "This is NOT a…" | TD025 (Huel), TD028 (Nutella), TD003 (dog harness), TD076 (phone case) | Reframe the category; it must resolve and be true |
| Them vs Us comparison | TD060 (Carepod), TD072 (Brrrn) | Same category = product aware; different category = solution aware |
| Avatar call-out static | TD034 (moms of 15-year-olds), TD017 (teen grumpy), TD051 (shift shoes) | Call out who, then show matching proof |
| Offer-led | TD035 (Solawave), TD022 (Everyday Dose), TD058 (watch), TD008 (free gifts) | Offer + risk reversal for most-aware buyers |
| Educational or fear video | TD002 (tick), TD018 (kids vitamins), TD045 (shampoo watch-outs), TD059 (wallet) | Educate on a real hidden problem; no fear-mongering |
| New-mechanism VSL | TD050 (hollow fiber socks), TD023 (Flow Pouch), TD015 and TD016 (MetaGlow) | Mechanism + authority + open loops; problem believed before product |
| Sniper (one sub-avatar) | TD068 and TD069 (blinds), TD075 (hockey), TD078 (Rabbit R1), TD033 (Stoke) | Hook, hold and body all specific to one sub-avatar |
| Unaware | TD052 (Billy Footwear), TD024 (NeoCell, ⛔ research only) | Emotion → problem → solution → product |
| Low budget, high specificity | TD053 vs TD054 (kids' AFO shoes) | A Canva collage with one avatar beat a vague video |
