# Hooks library (Evolve Start Here)
> Every hook, headline and opening line harvested from the 175 Start Here lesson pages, merged across lessons, grouped by hook type and tagged by niche, with test outcomes where the course shows them.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** you need to name the hook type of a competitor ad, write the first line or first 3 s of a UGC script, write a static headline, or build a set of hooks for a 3:2:2 test (3 creatives × 2 copy × 2 headlines).
- **Inputs → outputs:** the avatar / sub-avatar, the awareness level, the angle and the niche → filter by section (below) and the [niche index](#quick-index-by-niche) → 3–5 precedent rows → the **mechanism** in "Why it works" rewritten with our avatar's words, our mechanism and our real proof. Fill-in slot versions: [templates](templates.md). Angles behind the hooks: [angles](angles.md).
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** give the viewer a reason to stop instead of asking them to: "If you have X thing, give me five seconds and I'll show you how to get rid of it" beats "stop scrolling". [L088 20:36](../lessons/088-the-execution-problem.md)
  - **DO** make the hook specific and visual: "Lose the muffin top for good" over "lose weight"; "Teen always grumpy" creates "a visual and a memory". [L088 11:24](../lessons/088-the-execution-problem.md) [L088 12:22](../lessons/088-the-execution-problem.md)
  - **DO** call out the sub-avatar in the first line (experience, behavior, desire, emotion, belief); combine two for a sub-avatar ("desire + behavior"). [L071 1:55:31](../lessons/071-the-evolve-avatar-training.md) [L071 2:00:09](../lessons/071-the-evolve-avatar-training.md) [L083 07:01](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md)
  - **DO** answer the hook's question fast; the winning question hooks "answer the question right away". [L122 06:31](../lessons/122-how-to-do-learnings-part-1-losers.md)
  - **DO** keep hook, visual and VO congruent; "kids are unpredictable" fails without kids on screen. [L067 08:07](../lessons/067-action-item-find-new-mechanisms.md)
  - **DO** mine hooks from comments, reviews, Reddit and search queries; a commenter's "Have you seen these new shoes?" "could even be a headline right there". [L039 03:01](../lessons/039-facebook-research-part-2.md) [L054 02:50](../lessons/054-answer-the-public-research.md) [L074 45:45](../lessons/074-how-to-build-core-avatars.md)
  - **DON'T** lead with an authority the avatar doesn't care about (NASA failed for the flow-state product; "doctor designed" humidifier: "people don't care"). [L122 02:32](../lessons/122-how-to-do-learnings-part-1-losers.md) [L082 14:36](../lessons/082-the-3-testing-methods-part-2.md)
  - **DON'T** let a loss/fear hook run at too high intensity; "Light is slowly killing you" is flagged low valence, and the course has a fear-intensity re-prompt. [L061 31:34–32:56](../lessons/061-understanding-applying-desire-part-1.md) [L068](../lessons/068-how-to-find-apply-new-information-part-3.md) — see [prompts](prompts.md).
  - **DON'T** reuse a statistic, study, "scientists discovered", percentage or medical claim from these rows unless it is true for our product and sourced; bracket it until verified (repo rule). Rows with that risk carry ⚠️.
  - **IF** the market is unaware **THEN** open on curiosity with no problem named (section B). [L011 04:29–05:54](../lessons/011-understanding-unaware-awareness-levels.md) [L121 16:57](../lessons/121-how-to-deal-with-roller-coaster-performance.md)
  - **IF** a hook wins **THEN** iterate it into the next batch (new desire, new avatar, same structure) rather than retire it; see [rewrites](rewrites.md) and [rules §14 (LR)](rules.md).
- **See also:** [Angles](angles.md) · [Templates](templates.md) · [Rewrites](rewrites.md) · [Ad teardowns](ad-teardowns.md) · [Prompts](prompts.md) (avatar → headlines, Angle Identifier, fear re-prompt) · [Rules](rules.md) · [Market awareness levels](../concepts/market-awareness-levels.md) · [Core and sub-avatars](../concepts/core-and-sub-avatars.md) · Copywriting [hooks library](../../copywriting/libraries/hooks.md) (229 rows, 18 hook types) and [hooks concept](../../copywriting/concepts/hooks.md) · Tether Lab [shared hooks](../../../../libraries/hooks.md)

## ⚠️ Copy the mechanism, not the asset
Every row is a precedent. Rows from competitors, the 100-hook sheet (L005) and the static-template doc (L079) are other brands' lines: keep the structure ("This is X. This is Y", "If you [behavior], …", "World's first [material] [product]"), change the words, the visual and the proof. Rows labelled *prompt-output* are Claude drafts from live demos, not tested ads. Results ("won most spend", "worked well for us") are instructor claims unless the lesson shows the data.
