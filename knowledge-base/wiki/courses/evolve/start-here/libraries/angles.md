# Angles library (Evolve Start Here)
> Every ad angle harvested from the Start Here lesson pages, as a plain reason to buy tied to a desire or pain, merged across lessons and grouped by desire family, with the niche, best format and an example line.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** you are naming the angle of a competitor ad, choosing angles for a test batch or creator brief, or turning a desire hunt and sub-avatar list into concepts.
- **Inputs → outputs:** the research doc (desires, sub-avatars, objections, other desires), the product mechanism and the awareness level → filter by section and the [niche index](#quick-index-by-niche) → 3–5 analogous angles → our angle stated as "emotion + struggle or outcome" for one sub-avatar, with a hook from [hooks](hooks.md) and a format.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** state an angle as a reason to buy for one sub-avatar; an angle is the desire, struggle or outcome you lead with, not the format or the hook. See the [ad definitions](../concepts/ad-definitions.md) and the definition conflict in [rules §8 (AD)](rules.md). [L078](../lessons/078-understanding-ad-definitions.md) [L079](../lessons/079-how-to-come-up-with-ad-angles.md)
  - **DO** build angles from real research: comments, reviews, Reddit, search questions and product features each gave angles in the course (back-to-school, braces, tennis players, women's paddle). [L039 02:12–03:01](../lessons/039-facebook-research-part-2.md) [L054 04:42–08:34](../lessons/054-answer-the-public-research.md) [L058 11:08–31:40](../lessons/058-understanding-your-product-part-2.md)
  - **DO** pair a mass desire with a trend or new information (health instinct + trachea education → "Why are you still choking your dog in 2025?"). [L061 25:37](../lessons/061-understanding-applying-desire-part-1.md)
  - **DO** use a new mechanism when the market is sophisticated: the stainless-steel angle beat "easiest to clean" and "doctor designed" in the Marksman test (instructor claim). [L082 06:13–17:12](../lessons/082-the-3-testing-methods-part-2.md)
  - **DO** ask customers before building a story angle on a real event (the name-tag-saved-a-dog story needs a real customer). [L058 20:40](../lessons/058-understanding-your-product-part-2.md)
  - **DON'T** pick an authority angle the avatar doesn't care about ("doctor designed": "people don't care"). [L082 14:36](../lessons/082-the-3-testing-methods-part-2.md)
  - **DON'T** copy a competitor's angle 1:1; find an underserved avatar or a new mechanism instead ([two ways to win](../concepts/two-ways-to-win.md)).
  - **DON'T** reuse the health claims in L130 (prolactin, GLP-1) or any unverified "first / cleanest / 100%" claim; bracket and verify (repo rule).
  - **IF** you have a validated angle **THEN** test it across sub-avatars (the POV night-shift / insomnia / light-sleeper / early-riser set) before inventing a new angle. [L083 07:01](../lessons/083-putting-all-the-planning-together-examples.md)
  - **IF** you need angles fast **THEN** run the Angle Identifier prompt ([prompts](prompts.md)) on the research doc, then rank them as L079 did. [L079 20:19–31:58](../lessons/079-how-to-come-up-with-ad-angles.md)
- **See also:** [Hooks](hooks.md) · [Templates](templates.md) · [Ad teardowns](ad-teardowns.md) · [Prompts](prompts.md) · [Rules](rules.md) · [Angle generation prompt](../frameworks/angle-generation-prompt.md) · [Desire hunt](../frameworks/desire-hunt.md) · [Market desire](../concepts/market-desire.md) · [Desire calendar](../concepts/desire-calendar.md) · [Core and sub-avatars](../concepts/core-and-sub-avatars.md) · [Market sophistication levers](../concepts/market-sophistication-levers.md) · Copywriting [angles library](../../copywriting/libraries/angles.md) and [avatars and angles](../../copywriting/concepts/avatars-and-angles.md) · Tether Lab [shared angles](../../../../libraries/angles.md)
