# Libraries hub (Evolve Start Here)
> The fast-lookup layer for the Evolve Start Here program: every hook, angle, ad teardown, rewrite, template, prompt, rule and numeric threshold from the 175 lessons. Rows are de-duplicated, grouped for grep, and linked back to the lesson and timestamp they came from.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** you need a precedent, a rule, a number or a prompt inside a pipeline step. Examples: "what spend share makes a breakthrough at $100–250k/mo?", "how did Evolve split blackout blinds into sub-avatars?", "what is the 3:2:2 naming template?", or the verbatim angle prompt.
- **Inputs → outputs:** the job you're on ([table below](#which-library-for-which-job)) → the right library → filter rows by section, niche, ID prefix or keyword → a **mechanism** to remix, a rule to apply, a number to use, a template to fill or a prompt to paste. Then check the output against [rules §19](rules.md) and the repo rules in [AGENTS.md](../../../../../../AGENTS.md).
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** start full workflows from the course playbooks and frameworks ([launch a new product](../playbooks/launch-a-new-product-end-to-end.md), [research to first concepts](../playbooks/research-to-first-concepts.md), [weekly media buying](../playbooks/weekly-media-buying-routine.md), [creator program setup](../playbooks/creator-program-setup.md)). Come here for lookups inside a step.
  - **DO** take numbers only from [kpi-thresholds](kpi-thresholds.md), which lists conflicting values side by side with an agent default.
  - **DO** copy the mechanism, not the asset (see the warning below).
  - **DO** open the lesson link and check the timestamp before quoting an instructor or citing a result.
  - **DON'T** execute anything marked ⛔ (ripping and re-uploading TikTok Shop videos, stripping metadata, copying ads 1:1, unverified health claims, fake limits). The [⛔ index in rules](rules.md) lists the compliant alternatives.
  - **DON'T** treat *prompt-output*, *hypothetical*, "(derived)" or "(instructor claim)" rows as evidence that something works.
- **See also:** [Course README](../README.md) · [Course index](../index.md) · Evolve Copywriting [libraries hub](../../copywriting/libraries/README.md) (the writing-craft companion: 229 hooks, 209 templates, 305 rules, word lists) · Tether Lab [shared libraries hub](../../../../libraries/README.md) (psychology-tagged)

## ⚠️ Copy the mechanism, not the asset
Every library row is a precedent, not a line to reuse. Competitor ads, the 100 winning ads document (L004), the 100-hook sheet (L005), the 13 static templates (L006) and the L079 static-template cards belong to other brands. Keep the structure, change the words, visuals and proof. Lesson 172 teaches ripping other sellers' TikTok Shop videos, clearing the metadata and running them as ads. That is course content only: ⛔ don't execute. Study the hook, demo and structure, then brief our own creators. [rules X01](rules.md) · [ad teardowns](ad-teardowns.md)

Health, statistical and superlative claims in the rows ("10 times the normal levels of prolactin", "96% of dog owners", "world's first", "100% of light") are the source's claims. Bracket them and use them only with substantiation for our product.

## Conventions
- **IDs:** hooks A01–I19; angles A01–J08 (cite as "angles B03" to avoid clashes with hook IDs); teardowns TD001…; rewrites W001…; templates T001…; prompts P01–P28; rules by prefix (GN01, MB23, X01…); thresholds K001–K264. Cite IDs in briefs and reviews.
- **Citations:** `[L094 03:15](../lessons/094-how-to-set-up-a-3-2-2-campaign.md)` = lesson 94 at 03:15. Non-video sources are labelled "page", "doc", "docx", "frame", "slide", "sheet row" (L005 hook sheet) or "prompt card" (L079 static-template doc).
- **Provenance tags:** "(derived)" = an inference from the lesson page; "(instructor claim)" = an unverified result; *prompt-output* = a Claude draft from a live demo; ⚠️ = a claim that needs substantiation; ⛔ = don't execute (conflicts with repo rules).
- **Attribution:** Shaun Eng for lessons 14, 108, 133, 166, 169, 172 and 175; Chef Karlo (guest) for L130; otherwise "Evolve" (Spencer Pawliw where named on screen or self-named).
- **Relationship to Evolve Copywriting:** the Copywriting libraries hold the writing-craft detail (hook types, bridges, word lists). These libraries hold Start Here's additions: research, desire, avatars, angles, media buying, numbers and the creator program. Where both cover an idea, the row links the Copywriting page.

## Coverage and gaps
- **Sources:** all 175 lesson pages were harvested. Rules cite 163 lessons; the rest are action-item or housekeeping pages with no rule of their own.
- **Unavailable in the course files:** the full 100-hook sheet (L005, behind a login); most of the Elite Video Ad Script Creator (L007); the "CBO No Spend VS ABO Spend" sheet; several named prompts (listed in [prompts](prompts.md)).
- **Evidence limits:** outcomes are rarely shown with account data. Winner labels rest on 17 breakthrough ads in L112, and the $250k–500k tier has one data point. Treat results as instructor claims.
- **Open conflicts:** 18 rule conflicts and 19 numeric conflicts are listed with agent defaults in [rules](rules.md) and [kpi-thresholds](kpi-thresholds.md).
