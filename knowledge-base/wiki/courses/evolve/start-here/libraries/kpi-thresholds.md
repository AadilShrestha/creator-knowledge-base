# KPI thresholds library (Evolve Start Here)
> Every numeric rule in the Evolve Start Here course in one place: campaign counts, budgets and spend gates, ROAS/CPA target math, winning-ad label thresholds by revenue tier, creative benchmarks, kill/keep/scale timings, attribution settings, promo intervals, creative volume, creator pay and revenue-stage buckets. Each row cites the lesson and timestamp. Where lessons give different numbers, they sit side by side in the [conflicts table](#conflicting-values-side-by-side).

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** you are setting a budget, a target ROAS or CPA, a test length, a kill or scale decision, a winning-ad label, a creative-quality check, or a creator pay offer, and you need the course's actual number.
- **Inputs → outputs:** account monthly spend (tier), daily budget, gross margin, AOV, target CPA, the ad's 7-day spend share and ROAS vs campaign ROAS → the matching K rows → a decision with its citation. Run the [decision quick reference](#decision-quick-reference-derived) first, then confirm against the rows.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** set targets from margin, not a flat number: Breakeven ROAS = 1 ÷ gross margin; Scaling ROAS = Breakeven + 1. [L101 01:40](../lessons/101-how-to-set-targets-kpis.md) [L101 02:50](../lessons/101-how-to-set-targets-kpis.md)
  - **DO** pick the spend-share bar from the account's monthly tier at launch, not a fixed %: $0-100k ≈ 60-70% of campaign spend, $100-250k ≈ 20%, $500k+ ≈ 7.6% of account spend. [L112 00:00](../lessons/112-winning-ad-definitions-0-mo-1m-mo.md) [L112 01:27](../lessons/112-winning-ad-definitions-0-mo-1m-mo.md) [L112 02:35](../lessons/112-winning-ad-definitions-0-mo-1m-mo.md)
  - **DO** wait 7 days in a CBO before labelling an ad; earlier only if it is plainly scaling the account. [L110 doc](../lessons/110-winning-ads-definition-part-1.md) [L111 00:47](../lessons/111-winning-ads-definition-part-2.md)
  - **DO** scale +20% only after 48-72 h consistently above target KPI with ≥60% click-based purchases. [L115 01:01](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md) [L115 03:23](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md) [L115 05:51](../lessons/115-how-to-scale-facebook-ads-up-down.md)
  - **DON'T** judge on hook, hold, CTR or CPC alone: they are "soft metrics"; "focus on what actually matters, which is ROAS and CPA". [L088 32:17](../lessons/088-the-execution-problem.md) [L100 13:08](../lessons/100-how-to-see-ads-performance-our-columns.md)
  - **DON'T** copy a "3x ROAS" target: at 65% margin, 2x-2.5x is profitable and ~1.5x is break even. [L101 02:17](../lessons/101-how-to-set-targets-kpis.md)
  - **DON'T** quote instructor result figures ($100k/day, "10k to 70k per day in 24 hours") as evidence; they are instructor claims. [L108 04:05](../lessons/108-how-to-set-up-a-promo-campaign.md)
  - **IF** daily spend is under $1k **THEN** cap new test ad sets at 5 and skip 3:2:2:2, ABO scaling and separate whitelisting campaigns. [L093 sheet](../lessons/093-full-media-buying-strategy-outline-2026.md) [L097 page](../lessons/097-how-to-set-up-abo-scaling-campaign.md) [L164 page](../lessons/164-how-to-test-whitelisted-ads.md)
  - **IF** two rows disagree **THEN** use the "Agent default" column of the [conflicts table](#conflicting-values-side-by-side).
