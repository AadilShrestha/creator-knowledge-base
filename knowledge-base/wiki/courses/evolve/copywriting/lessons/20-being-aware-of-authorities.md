# 20. Being aware of authorities
> A mechanism alone may not build enough belief, so for each claim name the authority you can use and signal it before the pitch, not during; the lesson also adds volume of creators and virality as an authority type.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** after choosing an angle and mechanism, deciding what proof or credibility element goes in the hook or opening; also when auditing where a competitor puts its authority.
- **Inputs → outputs:** angle + mechanism pairs → for each, one named authority (who or what vouches), plus where it sits in the script (before the pitch).
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** signal authority **before** the pitch: "It is so much better to essentially have the authority or kind of talk about the authority before you say like, hey, this is why we're the best." [00:00–00:34]
  - **DO** pair every mechanism with an authority and ask "what authority do you have?" [00:34–01:18]
  - **DO** use a fact that makes the problem real before presenting the product (e.g. microplastic particle counts). [01:18]
  - **DO** count volume of people talking about you as authority: virality, large numbers of creators, word of mouth. [01:18–03:48]
  - **DON'T** treat a mechanism as enough proof on its own. [00:00]
  - **IF** an influencer or celebrity speaks in the ad **THEN** the authority is already signalled (e.g. David Beckham holding IM8). [00:00–00:34]
  - **IF** you cannot get a person or organisation **THEN** use research or word-of-mouth volume. [01:18–03:48]
- **See also:** [16. Why you need to understand authority](16-why-you-need-to-understand-authority.md) (the 10 types) · [19. Being aware of mechanisms](19-being-aware-of-mechanisms.md) · [15. ### 3. Volume and virality as authority (Comfrt) [02:12–03:48]
- "That authority and that virality... is not something that we actually talked about in the copywriting section" of [lesson 16](16-why-you-need-to-understand-authority.md), but "it has built authority for Comfrt. Everybody is talking about them." [02:12–02:53]
- Search proof: "When you go to Comfrt and search 'comfortable hoodie', guess what comes up? Comfrt." The volume they have done builds authority. "That's not easy for everybody to do." [02:12–02:53]
- Additional authority he suggests for Comfrt: a doctor explaining that heavier hoodies calm people down, "like weighted blankets kind of do". [02:53–03:48]
- General rule: when "so many people are talking about you... you then have word of mouth in your favor... that is how you can build authority, just kind of on an evergreen standpoint to support a lot of the different claims that you make." [02:53–03:48]
- The lesson ends by pointing to the final topic: market awareness ([21](21-being-aware-of-market-awareness.md)). [03:48]

## Steps / framework
1. Take each angle + mechanism pair ([18](18-being-aware-of-angles.md), [19](19-being-aware-of-mechanisms.md)).
2. Ask: "What authority do I have?" Choose from the ten types in [16](16-why-you-need-to-understand-authority.md) or from volume/virality.
3. Place it first: in the hook, opening line or first visual, before the "why we're the best" pitch.
4. Keep claim, mechanism, authority in one chain in the brief.

## How to apply
- [ ] Extend each claim-mechanism pair in the angle log with an "Authority" column and where it appears in the script.
- [ ] Put the authority line or visual in the first 3 seconds or first line before pitching.
- [ ] For brands with lots of creator content, treat creator volume (UGC count, searches, reviews) as an authority and show it.
- [ ] Verify any statistic used as authority (the particle count above is the instructor's, not sourced).
