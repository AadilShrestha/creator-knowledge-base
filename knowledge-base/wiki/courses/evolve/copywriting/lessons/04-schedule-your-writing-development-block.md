# 04. Schedule Your Writing Development Block
> An action item, not a video: put a recurring, protected block on your calendar for deliberate practice in studying writing, writing copy, and ideating, with intent.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** setting up a recurring job (for an agent) or a calendar habit (for a human) that runs the three-part daily practice from [lesson 3](03-how-to-guarantee-you-get-better-at-copywriting.md).
- **Inputs → outputs:** a fixed time slot and the daily practice steps → a recurring block that produces read-copy notes, one written ad, and one ad idea per session.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** schedule the block in advance and make it recurring: the page's instruction is "Schedule your time block to make sure you have time to focus on developing your writing further."
  - **DO** use the block for three things, "with intent": "studying writing", "actually copywriting", and "ideating". (Page text, verbatim in substance: "A dedicated time to studying writing with intent, actually copywriting with intent, and ideating, with intent.")
  - **DON'T** leave practice to spare time; the page treats the block as the mechanism that guarantees the work happens (derived from the lesson's placement right after lesson 3, where the instructor demonstrates a repeating one-hour calendar event).
  - **IF** you need the exact how-to for choosing the time, tools, and blockers **THEN** read lesson 3's section "Schedule it in" ([06:41–10:49] in that lesson), not this page.

## Steps / framework
The page gives no steps of its own. The three components of the block map onto the lesson-3 daily practice (derived mapping):

| Block component (page wording) | Lesson 3 daily-practice step |
|---|---|
| Studying writing with intent | Read a piece of copy actively and state what it communicates |
| Copywriting with intent | Write a page of copy / an ad with intent |
| Ideating with intent | Come up with an ad idea from one of five sources |

## How to apply
- [ ] Create a recurring calendar block (daily, same time) named for the writing practice.
- [ ] In the block do three things: study one piece of copy, write one piece of copy, log one ad idea.
- [ ] Remove distractions during the block (see the blocker tip in lesson 3).
- [ ] (derived, agents) Register the practice as a scheduled job and write its outputs to the ad-idea backlog.
