# 17. Being aware of avatars
> Before writing any headline, answer "Who am I writing this for?" (desire, experience, emotion, behaviour) and "Why do they care?" (desire plus the belief in the way), because the same product needs a different headline for a beginner than for a professional.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** starting any hook, headline, script or static ad; checking a finished draft; switching an existing concept to a new sub-avatar.
- **Inputs → outputs:** product + a chosen avatar/sub-avatar (start from a desire) → a four-line avatar card (desire, experience, emotion, behaviour), a "why do they care" line (desire + belief), and a headline written for that person only.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** build avatars that start with a desire, then add experiences, behaviours, emotions and (only if needed) demographics. Experiences and behaviours reveal beliefs and pain points. [00:00–00:58]
  - **DO** answer "Who am I writing this for?" before writing copy, and "Why do they care?" with the form *they want [desire] but they believe [obstacle]*. [00:00–02:20]
  - **DO** check the finished headline against the avatar again: "who did I write this for, and are they going to care?" [04:26]
  - **DON'T** reuse a headline across avatars. "Never overcook chicken again" works for a beginner at-home chef and "is not going to resonate at all" with a professional chef, who needs a flavour/status headline. [02:57–04:26]
  - **DON'T** rely on demographics alone: "there are better ways to make avatars than just using demographics." [00:00–00:58]
  - **IF** the audience is different **THEN** change the headline *and* the angle (see [18](18-being-aware-of-angles.md)), not just the visual. (derived from [02:57–04:26])

## Key ideas

### 1. Do the avatar work first [00:00–00:58]
- The instructor reminds students to go through the Evolve **avatar training** ("this two and a half hour long video") before writing ads. Its TL;DR as he gives it: build avatars from a **desire**, then add experiences, behaviours, specific emotions and demographics "if needed, but mainly the experiences and behaviors", "which are going to tell us the beliefs and the pain points that that person has as a result of what they've been through." - Before any ad: "who am I writing this for?" [00:00–00:58] The avatar training itself is not included in this lesson's files (the video shows the course nav with "How To Build Sub Avatars" at 00:30).

### 3. Same product, different avatar, different headline [02:57–04:26]
- **Beginner chef:** "Never overcook chicken again" (desire: impress; belief: can't cook it; promise: forgiving chicken). [02:57–03:45]
- **Professional chef:** "The wagyu of chicken" (page text). In the audio he starts "more flavor than..." then settles on the wagyu line; this fits a pro's desire to make "the best tasting meals... that overwhelms your taste buds". [03:45–04:26]
- Cross-check: the Wagyu headline "potentially might be scary for a beginner at-home chef", because they can only imagine burning an expensive cut. [04:26]
- "Even if you just start with a person, you're going to have headlines that come to you" (he is "just winging" these and says they are "obviously not the best headlines"). [04:26]

## Steps / framework
**Avatar-before-headline check (from [00:00–04:26], restated as steps)**
1. Choose the desire; name the avatar around it.
2. Fill: **Desire → Experience → Emotion → Behavior** (add demographics only if useful).
3. Write **Why do they care?** as: "They want [desire]. But they believe [belief]."
4. State what our product does *for this belief* (here: forgiving of overcooking).
5. Write the headline for this avatar only.
6. After writing, re-read: who is it for, will they care?

### Rules of thumb
- **DO** write "why do they care" as desire + belief. [01:42]
- **DON'T** write one headline for everyone. [02:57–04:26]

## How to apply
- [ ] For each concept, write the four-line avatar card and the "why do they care" line before the first draft.
- [ ] Write at least two headlines for two different sub-avatars of the same product and confirm they would not be interchangeable.
- [ ] After drafting, re-ask: who is this for; will they care?
- [ ] Record the avatar card in the `avatars` tab of the ad-idea workbook (see [22](22-how-to-finalize-your-ad-ideas.md)).
