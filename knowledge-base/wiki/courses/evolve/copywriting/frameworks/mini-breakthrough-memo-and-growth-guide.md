# Mini breakthrough memo + Growth Guide
> No ad idea gets written until it passes the three-question memo (**WHY** are we making this ad, **WHAT** is it about, **HOW** will we make it), sits on the avatar → angle → mechanism → authority chain, and is logged as a row in the Growth Guide's Ad Roadmap with its awareness level, file type, ad type, result and learnings.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** someone pitches an idea ("let's do an us-vs-them"), an agent proposes a concept from a competitor teardown, or you need the tracking table that the [ten-step learnings](ten-step-iteration-learnings.md) write back into.
- **Inputs → outputs:** raw idea + product facts + sub-avatar research (quotes) + reference ad(s) + account evidence → a filled persuasion-chain card, a memo (Why/What/How), and an Ad Roadmap row; later the result label and learnings.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** write the memo in three labelled parts: WHY, WHAT, HOW. "This is how you get ideas fully finalized and fully fleshed out. And it'll help you realize whether or not this is actually a good idea." [L22 00:50–01:21](../lessons/22-how-to-finalize-your-ad-ideas.md)
  - **DO** put evidence in the WHY (a past ad and its spend, a new winner, a reason to blend them). [L22 01:21–02:10](../lessons/22-how-to-finalize-your-ad-ideas.md)
  - **DON'T** accept "let's do an us versus them" without "an us versus them about what, and why should we do it?" [L22 01:21; 03:42–04:23](../lessons/22-how-to-finalize-your-ad-ideas.md)
  - **DO** write down even assumed reasons; once on the page "you might be like this idea is really weak". [L22 03:42–04:23](../lessons/22-how-to-finalize-your-ad-ideas.md)
  - **DO** name the angle (the customer's reason to buy) for every concept, even a format test: "If the concept doesn't give the customer a reason to buy, it's not an angle yet." [L67 angle-prompt doc](../lessons/67-how-to-write-image-ads-part-1.md); [L18 page text](../lessons/18-being-aware-of-angles.md)
  - **DO** pair every claim with a mechanism and every mechanism with an authority, and signal authority "BEFORE you make your pitch, not during". [L19 03:45–04:23](../lessons/19-being-aware-of-mechanisms.md); [L20 00:00–00:34](../lessons/20-being-aware-of-authorities.md)
  - **DO** remember "we can not create desires, we can only channel them" (Growth Guide desires tab). [L22 workbook](../lessons/22-how-to-finalize-your-ad-ideas.md)
  - **IF** you feel inspired and want to just write **THEN** write, "but ideally think through the memo first". [L22 04:23–05:05](../lessons/22-how-to-finalize-your-ad-ideas.md)
  - **DON'T** count a KPI winner as a hit: the hit-rate formula counts only Breakthrough and Spend Winner. [L22 Ad Roadmap P2/Q2](../lessons/22-how-to-finalize-your-ad-ideas.md)
- **See also:** [Ad idea to brief (playbook)](../playbooks/ad-idea-to-brief.md) · [Nine-component audit](nine-component-audit.md) · [Positioning exercise](positioning-exercise.md) · concepts: [avatars and angles](../concepts/avatars-and-angles.md), [unique mechanism](../concepts/unique-mechanism.md), [authority in ads](../concepts/authority-in-ads.md), [market awareness](../concepts/market-awareness.md) · Tether: [angle remix](../../../../playbooks/angle-remix.md), [customer research to angles](../../../../playbooks/customer-research-to-angles.md)

### Concept vs angle vs hook (angle-prompt document, L67)
| Term | Definition (doc) | Test |
|---|---|---|
| Concept | "The big idea (what you're testing)": formats, iterations, awareness levels, or angles. "About you/your test strategy (internally focused)" | "I want to test Us vs Them" is a concept |
| Angle | "How you choose to sell the product (must always be defined)"; "About the customer's reason to buy (externally focused)" | Gives a reason to buy? "Stops tossing and turning" yes; "Video version" no |
| Hook / headline | "how you communicate the angle"; until advanced, "direct instead of indirect" | Angle "Stops swelling" → "14+ Hours. No Swelling" |

"Us vs Them", "Before & After", "Problem-Aware Angle Ads" and "Post It Note Ads" are concepts or formats; "the 'What' is the angle" [L67 doc](../lessons/67-how-to-write-image-ads-part-1.md).

### Testing method
| Method | What | When | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Marksman | Several angles in one concept, one hook each | Sub-avatar or angle direction test | [L67 01:37; 12:27](../lessons/67-how-to-write-image-ads-part-1.md) |
| Sniper | One angle, several hooks | You believe in one angle and want the best execution | [L67 05:38](../lessons/67-how-to-write-image-ads-part-1.md) |
| "Double sniper" | After a little buzz, lead with the mechanism ("protect" vs everyone's "boost") | Spin-off of a promising script | [L71 26:43–27:33](../lessons/71-how-to-write-video-ads-part-2.md) |

## Part 2: the memo (Why / What / How)
| Part | Must contain | Model memo (Batch #2, verbatim from the sheet) |
|---|---|---|
| **WHY** are we making this ad? | Evidence: an ad of ours or a competitor's, its spend or traction, and the reason to blend/iterate | "We have this ad 'Negative Framing' that spent close to $280k. Now we have this new ad from [X] that's doing really well and then did well with the UGC iteration too. So I was thinking if we could blend two of these concepts together it could crush." |
| **WHAT** is this ad going to be about? | The angle and the fill-in line or structure | "Instead of talking about features, we lead with what our [PRODUCT] prevents: 'If you are [SUB AVATAR], This [UNIQUE MECHANISM] won't Do X everytime Y Happens which will not cause [THING SUB AVATAR IS SCARED OF]'. Basically bring in all the negative outcomes from each of the other SOLUTIONS mentioned and we say like this [PRODUCT] won't bring that outcome." |
| **HOW** are we going to make it? | Format, a reference ad link, who appears | "This will be a video that looks similar to this ad [LINKED HERE]." |

Instructor's reaction to a good WHY: "OK, I'm interested. That makes sense. It's logical." [L22 02:10](../lessons/22-how-to-finalize-your-ad-ideas.md). Ideal state: "here's why I want to do this idea. Here's what I want to do. And here's how I'm thinking we should do it", so that writing is "just ... execution" [L22 04:23–05:05](../lessons/22-how-to-finalize-your-ad-ideas.md).

A second model memo, written as "Growth Guide Notes" on the final video brief: "I'm recreating this ad because I saw that it did actually get traction, and I think this is a real pain point for this specific avatar around being in a deficit. ... This is a very skeptical market that's heard everything for cravings, and so I want to make sure that I really show my understanding of what they're doing rather than telling them something is easy with no additional context." [L71 PDF](../lessons/71-how-to-write-video-ads-part-2.md)

### Memo quality gate (derived from L22)
| Check | Pass | Fail → |
|---|---|---|
| WHY has evidence | Names an ad, a result (spend, traction, comments) or a research finding | Weak why → drop or research more [L22 03:42–04:23] |
| WHAT names the angle | A reason to buy, not only a concept/format | Write the angle ("the what") |
| WHAT names sub-avatar + mechanism | Both present | Fill the chain first |
| HOW has a reference | Link to the reference ad + format | Find one, or mark "ideation, no reference" |
| Truth | Every claim in the WHAT exists in the product facts | Remove or verify (see [static workflow QA](static-ad-workflow.md#product-truth-qa)) |
| Copy, not clone | If the reference is a competitor ad: you are copying the mechanism, not lines, footage or claims | Rewrite the WHAT around our product |

## Part 3: the Growth Guide (EVOLVE | BRAND - Growth Guide 3.0)
Ten tabs (four hidden in the XLSX). The Ad Roadmap is the idea tracker.

### Ad Roadmap columns and vocabularies (verbatim headers)
| Col | Header | Values |
|---|---|---|
| A | STATUS | `Working`, `Learning`, `Filming`, `Done` |
| B | UPVOTE | `+1` ... `+12` (team vote before production) |
| C | BATCH # | e.g. `BATCH #1` |
| D | DATE ADDED | date |
| E | AUTHOR | name |
| F | AD CONCEPT | concept name |
| G | DESIRE/CORE AVATAR | from the desires tab ("I want...") |
| H | SUB AVATAR | one or several |
| I | ANGLE(S) | reasons to buy |
| J | BREKATHROUGH MEMO (sic) | Why / What / How |
| K | AWARENESS LEVEL | `Unaware`, `Problem Aware`, `Solution Aware`, `Product Aware`, `Most Aware` |
| L | FILE TYPE | `🖼️ Static`, `🎬 Video` |
| M | AD TYPE | `🔄 Iteration`, `🎭 Imitation`, `💡 Ideation` |
| N | LINK TO BRIEF | URL |
| O | LINK TO AD | URL |
| P | RESULTS | `Breakthrough`, `Spend Winner`, `KPI Winner`, `Loser` |
| Q | LEARNINGS | free text |
| R | Ad Variable | `🪝Hook`, `🖼️ Visual` |
| S | Test Result | `Losing Ad`, `Winning Ad` |
| T | Learnings | variable-test notes |

Counters (verbatim): `P2 =COUNTIF(P3:P997, "*Breakthrough*") + COUNTIF(P3:P997, "*Spend Winner*")`; `Q2 =COUNTIF(P3:P997, "*Loser*") + COUNTIF(P3:P997, "*KPI Winner*")`; Overview "Ad Hit Rate Percentage" `=sum('Ad Roadmap'!P2)/('Ad Roadmap'!P2+'Ad Roadmap'!Q2)` [L22 workbook](../lessons/22-how-to-finalize-your-ad-ideas.md).

### Other tabs
| Tab | Purpose | Key fields |
|---|---|---|
| Overview | Ad and page hit-rate gauges | formulas above |
| Log | Blank daily Meta-ads diary with a "Weekly Recap" row every 7 days | Day, Notes |
| Page Roadmap | Same tracker for landing pages | PAGE FORMAT `PDP`, `Sales Page`, `Advertorial`, `Listicle`; same RESULTS vocabulary |
| CRO (hidden) | Landing-page test log | Concept, Test Start/End, Coupon, Explanation, URL, Offer, Selling Point, Avatar, Test Result, Learnings |
| Desires/Core Avatar | "Market's Desire - 'I want...'" list | "Remeber that we can not create desires, we can only channel them" |
| Sub Avatars/Angles | The chain in one row | Product Name, Feature (what it is), Benefit "so you can...", Desire, Sub Avatar, Angle(s) |
| Avatars (hidden) | Avatar names for dropdowns | — |
| Awareness (hidden) | One customer question per awareness level per product | e.g. Problem Aware: "I feel congested at home but not at work. What in the world is going on?" |
| Creators (hidden) | "Top Creator Anaylsis" (sic) | Audience Size, Creator, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, High Performing Content Trends, User Comment Notes |

Behavior: No screens 1 hour before Sleeping | Protect Your Melatonin Production` [L22](../lessons/22-how-to-finalize-your-ad-ideas.md).

## Cross-course notes
- **Tether Lab's** [angle remix](../../../../playbooks/angle-remix.md) produces `angle_brief`s scored and marked `ship_to_script`; Evolve's memo is the human-readable justification and the Roadmap row is the log. Use both (derived): the angle brief carries the psychology tags (self, zone, TEEP), the memo carries the evidence and the reference ad.
- **Agreement:** both require a reason to buy grounded in customer evidence and both forbid copying a competitor's asset. Evolve's "we can not create desires, we can only channel them" matches Tether's research-first [customer research to angles](../../../../playbooks/customer-research-to-angles.md).
- **Difference:** Tether's angle remix forces a change on at least two psychology axes from the source; Evolve's memo allows a straight iteration of our own winner (AD TYPE = Iteration) with one component changed.
