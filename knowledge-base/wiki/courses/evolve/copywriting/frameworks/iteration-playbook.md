# Iteration playbook
> What to make next once an ad has an outcome label: losers and KPI winners get **fixed** (issue codes), spend winners get their conversion gaps fixed **before** any format change, and spend winners and breakthroughs get **multiplied** through nine script iterations, fifteen formats/styles, and the lesson-47 DOCX list of 22 numbered iterations with priorities. Start with the components rewrite.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** step 9 of the [ten-step learnings](ten-step-iteration-learnings.md); planning the next batch after a winner; naming what a competitor's new version of an ad changed.
- **Inputs → outputs:** outcome label + ad type + [nine-component card](nine-component-audit.md) + comments under the ad + available resources (creators, founder/expert access, AI tools, editor, budget) → a ranked list of 3–6 iterations, each with: name, component(s) changed, what stays constant, priority, production note.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** run script iterations only on spend winners and breakthroughs; for losers and KPI winners "what you're trying to do is you're just trying to fix those ads". [L52 00:00](../lessons/52-the-iteration-playbook-script.md)
  - **DO** start with #1 Components rewrite: "the biggest one is this components rewrite, break down your components, look for where you're missing on things, add those in, and then... go through different types of executions." [L52 19:07](../lessons/52-the-iteration-playbook-script.md)
  - **DON'T** jump to a format change on a spend winner: moving it "into a native ad or ... an AI animated ad is not going to make it work all of the time" and "changing the script blindly is not going to make it work". Fix belief, urgency, stakes, funnel first. [L50 17:18](../lessons/50-how-to-iterate-on-spend-winners.md)
  - **DON'T** react to a dying breakthrough by "spamming hook iterations or format iterations that you think are better, but they don't convert". Dissect first. [L51 03:25](../lessons/51-how-to-iterate-on-breakthroughs.md)
  - **DO** keep facts true in every iteration ("always take the facts and tell the truth"; page on emotional hooks: "BE ETHICAL"). [L52 02:19; 05:51–06:39](../lessons/52-the-iteration-playbook-script.md)
  - **DO** keep AI UGC honest: the instructor prefers an AI narrator, "we are not pretending the UGC is a real person", and removes first-person claims an AI could not have ("you're an AI. We know you didn't see it"). [L53 08:51–09:37](../lessons/53-the-iteration-playbook-formats-styles.md)
  - **IF** you identified an authority in the script **THEN** have the real authority deliver it: "the authority is actually delivering it. Not someone who knows the authority". [L53 11:13–13:09](../lessons/53-the-iteration-playbook-formats-styles.md)
  - **IF** you move a script to a new sub-avatar or creator **THEN** rewrite the authority and setting ("if he was coming back from the nail salon, it wouldn't make sense"). [L52 17:32–18:32](../lessons/52-the-iteration-playbook-script.md)
- **See also:** [Iteration issue codes](iteration-issue-codes.md) · [Nine-component audit](nine-component-audit.md) · [Iterate on test results (playbook)](../playbooks/iterate-on-test-results.md) · [Iteration outcomes](../concepts/iteration-outcomes.md) · Tether: [iteration vs variation](../../../../concepts/iteration-vs-variation.md), [format variation ladder](../../../../frameworks/format-variation-ladder.md), [creative testing system](../../../../frameworks/creative-testing-system.md)

## Step 1: route by outcome
| Outcome | Job | Use | Don't | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Loser | Fix with evidence or move on | [Issue codes](iteration-issue-codes.md) #1–#8; ad-type rules (imitation → usually stop) | iterate without learnings | [L48](../lessons/48-how-to-iterate-on-losing-ads.md) |
| KPI winner | Tell a lucky loser from a working BOF/offer ad | Lucky loser → loser codes; BOF/offer ad doing its job → leave it, maybe test formats or make it more offer-focused | expect it to scale; "most of the time, we're not overly excited by KPI winners" | [L49 00:39–03:56](../lessons/49-how-to-iterate-on-kpi-winners.md) |
| Spend winner | Turn it into a breakthrough | Codes #7, #9–#12, #2.5; then script iterations; formats last | hold/format iteration mode | [L50](../lessons/50-how-to-iterate-on-spend-winners.md) |
| Breakthrough | Keep it alive; make many more | Keep it on through bad days; dissect; script + format iterations; plan the next breakthrough | turn it off after a few bad days: "your ads earn respect" | [L51 03:25–05:36](../lessons/51-how-to-iterate-on-breakthroughs.md) |

Breakthrough keep-on rule: an ad that has ripped "for a month plus" gets leeway; after three good weeks, one bad week → leave it on; "skyrockets for three days and then crashes for a week" → less leeway [L51 04:22–05:36](../lessons/51-how-to-iterate-on-breakthroughs.md). Lifespan risks to log: easy to copy, copied from a competitor, niche TAM, offer-only, seasonality/outdated visuals, no product differentiation [L51 05:36–10:28](../lessons/51-how-to-iterate-on-breakthroughs.md).

## Step 2: the nine script iterations (L52)
| # | Iteration | What changes | Trigger | Course example | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Components rewrite | Any missing or weak component; optimised hook, bridge, hold | Always first | Airport: "I saw a video where someone took a pen and popped open the zipper on a suitcase." → "Airport thieves are now stealing your stuff with just a pen." Washer: health → status desire, same grandpa authority | [L52 00:55–05:51](../lessons/52-the-iteration-playbook-script.md) |
| 2 | Emotional hook testing | Valence and intensity (often raise intensity, lower valence), often adding authority | Breakthrough needs more urgency/stakes | "If your dog eats grass, do this every day" → "Dog trainer says why your dog's grass eating isn't normal" | [L52 05:51–07:24](../lessons/52-the-iteration-playbook-script.md) |
| 3 | Bridge remake | Bridge: add authority, raise stakes, make it visual; brief audio | Hook stops people, they leave after it | "It's not dry skin, it's fungus. And although it sounds gross, it's pretty common." → "It's not dry scalp. That's a yeast problem on your head. And it's feeding on your scalp like a buffet." | [L52 07:24–11:25](../lessons/52-the-iteration-playbook-script.md) |
| 4 | In media res (credit Sarah Levinger) | Start the cut at the action or mechanism | Longer ads with several strong middle moments | "we literally had an ad with three different cuts. One of them was a breakthrough. The other two completely flopped." (instructor claim) | [L52 11:25–12:22](../lessons/52-the-iteration-playbook-script.md) |
| 5 | Extended problem section | Longer, compounding problem/agitation before the solution | "One of the first iterations that we do on any winning ads" | page example (spend-winner iteration that stays in the problem longer) | [L52 12:22–13:43](../lessons/52-the-iteration-playbook-script.md) |
| 6 | Awareness-level test | Starting awareness, up or down | Ad crushes at one level | "My grandpa used to fix washing machines for a living..." → "What's actually in the washing machine cleaner you're using? Let me show you." | [L52 13:01–14:30](../lessons/52-the-iteration-playbook-script.md) |
| 7 | Overcoming objections | Script lines or a comment-reply hook answer real comment objections | Ad has comments with repeated objections | "Does it work for X type of person, like me?" → add "it even works for X"; "I've tried X before..." → comment on screen + "I used to think X was the same as this, but here's how it's actually different." | [L52 14:30–16:05](../lessons/52-the-iteration-playbook-script.md) |
| 8 | Negative framing | Argue against the product, then disprove each point | Skeptical crowd | "Honestly, don't buy this. Here's why..."; "Why I regret buying this modern wallet" | [L52 16:05–17:32](../lessons/52-the-iteration-playbook-script.md) |
| 9 | Different sub-avatar | Avatar, authority, setting, creator | Winner exists; another sub-avatar is plausible | nail-salon story recast with a male creator and a new setting | [L52 17:32–18:32](../lessons/52-the-iteration-playbook-script.md) |

Awareness ladder written out for one idea (cat toy, [L52 13:43–14:30](../lessons/52-the-iteration-playbook-script.md)): Product aware "The most addicting cat toy" · Solution aware "I tried everything to keep my cat busy" · Problem aware "I didn't realize my cat being bored could take years off its life..." · Unaware "Who has a better life... Cats or dogs?"

## Step 3: the fifteen formats and styles (L53)
"In no particular order", harder ones pushed to the bottom [L53 13:47](../lessons/53-the-iteration-playbook-formats-styles.md). Break the ad into components first, then "optimize scripts for different formats. It just absolutely crushes." [L53 00:00](../lessons/53-the-iteration-playbook-formats-styles.md)

| # | Format / style | Use when | Needs | Note | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Voiceover ↔ text-on-screen | You have only one of the two | Editor | Different music changes valence/intensity | [L53 00:00–01:23] |
| 2 | Real UGC creators | Winning concept exists | 2–3 creators | Copy the winning hook word for word at least once; the 3-video creator brief below | [L53 01:23–03:58] |
| 3 | Animated ingredients / mechanisms | Mechanism or ingredients are complex | AI animation | "turning your mechanism or ingredient into a hero is what converts best"; instructor flags an over-intense example as "borderline being kind of a piece of shit" | [L53 03:58–04:48] |
| 4 | AI animated solutions | Other solutions are the enemy | AI animation | "villainizing the other solutions and then showing yours as the hero" ("Hi, I'm fiber...") | [L53 04:48–05:36] |
| 5 | AI animated body parts / hormones | You have new information on the cause | AI animation | Juxtaposition stops the scroll; "still relatively new" | [L53 05:36–06:32] |
| 6 | AI singing version | Story or vibe-friendly angle | Claude + Suno + animation | Script → rhyming song | [L53 06:32–08:03] |
| 7 | AI animated story, no human | Clearly-AI narrator | AI animation | Pixar, crochet, claymation, skeletons | [L53 08:03–08:51] |
| 8 | AI animated story, human | Founder won't go on camera | AI animation | Claymation founder story | [L53 08:51–09:37] |
| 9 | AI UGC narration | A face without pretending it's real | AI avatar | Remove false first-person experiences | [L53 08:51–09:37] |
| 10 | VSL expansion | Strong problem, objections, urgency material | Heavy copy | "not just making it longer for the sake of making it longer"; 2–5 minutes | [L53 10:17–11:13] |
| 11 | Native / camouflage static | Breakthrough exists; test a static | Designer/copy | Primary copy mirrors the winning script | [L53 11:13] |
| 12 | Founder / authority delivery | An authority was identified as working | Founder/expert access | "creates authority that no one else can copy" | [L53 11:59–13:47] |
| 13 | Street interview | Capital + agency | Agency or bold creator | "Today, I'm asking strangers to guess the ingredients..." | [L53 13:47–14:22] |
| 14 | Podcast style | Two people, a set | Set | "I asked a Vet WHY My Dog Eats Grass" | [L53 14:22–15:16] |
| 15 | Stack winners mega-ad | 2–4 breakthroughs on the same angle | Editor | "you gotta edit it and flow it to make sense"; no public example | [L53 15:16–15:49] |

**Three-video brief for new creators** (Lukas Pakter via the instructor, [L53 02:37–03:13](../lessons/53-the-iteration-playbook-formats-styles.md)): (1) copy the top winning script, word for word if possible; (2) iterate on the top winning ad (components → new script); (3) imitate top daily viral ads. For a competitor's script, (1) does not apply: "copy the mechanism, not the asset" (see Rules in the [ad-idea-to-brief playbook](../playbooks/ad-idea-to-brief.md)).

## Step 4: the 22 named iterations (L47 DOCX "Video Ad Winner - Iteration Playbook")
Fill the identify block first (desire, avatar, angle, mechanism, authority, awareness, emotion, positioning: the [nine-component audit](nine-component-audit.md) minus urgency). Then pick by priority. Text below is the DOCX's ([L47](../lessons/47-claude-code-10-step-iteration-process.md)); the DOCX numbers both "In media res" and "Overcoming Objections" as #3 and leaves two items unnumbered.

**Hook & script**
| # | Name | Description (DOCX) | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Winning Hook Remake | Remake with the winning hook in the ad account. Keep what worked, fix what was sloppy. | High |
| 2 | Extended Problem Section | Longer problem/agitation; more specific pain points; make the viewer feel it more before the solution. | High |
| — | Awareness levels test | Variations for higher/lower awareness levels | — |
| 3 | In media res | Start with the unique mechanism (show don't tell); straight to the point in the first 5 seconds; start with the ideal outcome, then explain how we got there | Medium |
| 3 | Overcoming Objections | Pull real objections from comments; weave them in, "don't make it obvious" | High |
| 4 | Comment Reply Hook | A real comment/objection as the hook (screenshot), then play the winning ad as the "reply" | High |
| 5 | Dumber Version | Natural spoken language, filler words, pauses; "it should feel too simple" | Medium |
| 6 | Add Soft CTAs | Soft CTAs throughout the ad | Medium |
| 7 | Negative Framing | "Honestly, don't buy this. Here's why..." then disprove each point | Medium |
| 8 | Turn Script Into Framework, Test diff sub avatars | Keep the winning script, rewrite for a different avatar (age, life stage, use case) | Medium |

**Format & style**
| # | Name | Description (DOCX) | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | VO ↔ Text-on-Screen | Test the other delivery | High |
| 10 | Organic/Ugly Edit | Ugly, simple editing like a normal person; raw, native, unpolished | High |
| 11 | VSL Expansion | Longer form; deeper on education, mechanism, proof | High |
| 12 | AI Animation Version | Same message, AI-animated style | Medium |
| 13 | Podcast Style | Conversation between two people | Medium |
| 14 | New Location/Setting/Vibe | Same script, different environment | Medium |

**Scaling & stacking**
| # | Name | Description (DOCX) | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| 15 | Stack Winners Mega-Ad | Combine 3–4 winning clips/hooks into one compilation | Medium |
| 16 | UGC Creators | 2–3 creators execute the same concept and script; vary age, gender, energy | High |
| 17 | Founder/Authority Delivery | Founder or authority (vet, doctor, expert) delivers the winning script | Medium |

**Emotional testing**
| # | Name | Description (DOCX) | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| 18 | Valence/Intensity Test | Positive → fear/guilt; negative → aspirational; intense → dial down; mild → crank up | High |
| — | Self discrepancy test | Variations for ideal, ought and actual self | — |

**Cross-format**
| # | Name | Description (DOCX) | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| 19 | Static Ad Extraction | Strongest moment/claim from the video → static | High |
| 20 | First Frame Static Ad | First frame + copy from the script → static | Medium |
| 21 | Native Ad | Static with primary copy mirroring the winning script; feels organic | Medium |
| 22 | Static Ad + Congruent LP | Static version + a landing page matching the angle | High |

## Decision table: diagnosis → first iterations to run

| Breakthrough, viewers drop after the hook | #3 bridge remake | #4 in media res | [L52 07:24–12:22](../lessons/52-the-iteration-playbook-script.md) |
| Breakthrough with many comments repeating one objection | #7 objections / DOCX #4 comment reply | #8 negative framing (skeptical crowd) | [L52 14:30–17:32](../lessons/52-the-iteration-playbook-script.md) |

## Cross-course notes
- **Tether Lab** ([iteration vs variation](../../../../concepts/iteration-vs-variation.md)) keeps the core message fixed and varies one primitive or format; the [format variation ladder](../../../../frameworks/format-variation-ladder.md) varies product treatment one rung at a time. Evolve's list is broader: it also changes desire, sub-avatar, awareness and framing (negative framing, extended problem) [L47 cross-notes](../lessons/47-claude-code-10-step-iteration-process.md).
- Both include cross-format moves (static ↔ video). Tether says statics first, video after a static wins ([static ad creation](../../../../playbooks/static-ad-creation.md)); Evolve's DOCX #19–#22 go video → static. Not a conflict: direction depends on which format won.
- Evolve's "self discrepancy test" names Tether's three selves ([three selves](../../../../concepts/three-selves.md)); use Tether's [rewriting for new selves](../../../../frameworks/rewriting-ads-for-new-selves.md) to execute it.
