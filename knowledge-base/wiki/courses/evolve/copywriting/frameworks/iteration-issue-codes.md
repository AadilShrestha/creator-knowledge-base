# Iteration issue codes (#1–#14)
> The numbered issues from Spencer Pawliw's "2026 Iteration Playbook Examples" deck: each names one reason an ad underperforms (hook, TAM, too broad, valence/intensity, audience signal, empty claims, bridge, belief, offer, urgency, stakes, funnel congruence, misleading, easily copied, no product differentiation) with the fix and a real before/after. Use the code as the label on every "Changes Made" line.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** after the [ten-step learnings](ten-step-iteration-learnings.md) have labelled an ad; when explaining why a competitor's "version 2" beats version 1; when QA-ing a draft hook, bridge or hold before launch.
- **Inputs → outputs:** outcome label + metrics (hook rate, hold rate, CTR, CPC, CVR, AOV, ROAS vs target, spend share) + the ad + the landing page + comments → 1–3 issue codes with evidence → one specific fix per code, written as a before → after line.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** suspect the hook first on a loser: "the majority of time when you create a losing ad, it's due to the fact that the hook just simply did not grab the person's attention." [L48 00:00–00:39](../lessons/48-how-to-iterate-on-losing-ads.md)
  - **DO** treat hook rate under 30% as a hook problem, but not as gospel: "we have had breakthroughs with 30% hook rates". [L48 00:39–01:27](../lessons/48-how-to-iterate-on-losing-ads.md)
  - **DO** start after the hook on a spend winner: Meta is already stopping people; "the majority of the time, the reason why spend winners aren't breakthroughs is because they lack conversion rate, not CTRs." [L50 12:15–13:14](../lessons/50-how-to-iterate-on-spend-winners.md)
  - **DON'T** fall into "hold iteration mode" on a spend winner: "just because your hook is stopping people ... doesn't mean that it's going to get them to purchase." [L50 03:29–04:46](../lessons/50-how-to-iterate-on-spend-winners.md)
  - **DO** label every change you make, especially when you change two things at once (format and awareness). [L54 08:49](../lessons/54-iteration-examples-breakdowns-walkthrough.md)
  - **DO** keep fixes truthful: the urgency and stakes fixes use facts, not fake scarcity; urgency is "WHY DOES THE CONSUMER NEED TO BUY THIS NOW", not "sale ends tonight". [L50 07:40–08:35](../lessons/50-how-to-iterate-on-spend-winners.md)
  - **IF** ROAS is 0.2–0.3x on a spend winner **THEN** "something is clearly wrong. We're misleading ... you could probably turn that off" (code #12). [L50 00:35](../lessons/50-how-to-iterate-on-spend-winners.md)
- **See also:** [Nine-component audit](nine-component-audit.md) · [Iteration playbook](iteration-playbook.md) · [Iterate on test results](../playbooks/iterate-on-test-results.md) · concepts: [hooks](../concepts/hooks.md), [bridges](../concepts/bridges.md), [raising the stakes](../concepts/raising-the-stakes.md), [beliefs](../concepts/beliefs.md), [positioning](../concepts/positioning.md) · Tether: [valence-intensity diagnostic](../../../../frameworks/valence-intensity-diagnostic.md), [test and iterate](../../../../playbooks/test-and-iterate.md)

| #3 | Valence & intensity | "Your ad could be saying the right thing, but it isn't converting because it just doesn't feel right" | Flip valence; boring → crank intensity and stakes; too intense → dial down; or stay in your winners' zone for that avatar | "Fixing the ugliest thing about my house" → "This is the coolest thing we've added to our house" | loser, KPI winner | [L48 10:49–12:57](../lessons/48-how-to-iterate-on-losing-ads.md) (credit Sarah Levinger) |
| #4 | Hook issue #2 (audience signal) | Visuals and messaging don't tell Meta which audience to serve; "much more common now with Andromeda" | Visual that makes the audience obvious | Mirror man "could be anything" → needle into a muscular man "is going to be about muscles and steroids" | loser, KPI winner | [L48 12:57–13:54](../lessons/48-how-to-iterate-on-losing-ads.md) |
| #5 | Empty claims | Decent hook rate but no conversion; a claim with no proof, facts or authority in a sophisticated market | Replace the claim with new information that redirects a prior belief; claims later | "Beat the dad bod with Mars Men" → "That belly isn't beer. It's cortisol killing your testosterone" | loser, KPI winner | [L48 13:54–14:51](../lessons/48-how-to-iterate-on-losing-ads.md) |
| #6 | Bridge issue | Great hook, attention lost right after; bridge is opinion | Fact-based or story bridge that sets up what follows | Chocolate "way too sweet... feels more sugary" → "This is a cacao pod... It started as medicine"; deck refresh "random clips" → "welcome to part three of our back deck refresh" | loser, KPI winner | [L48 14:51–16:50](../lessons/48-how-to-iterate-on-losing-ads.md); [L54 02:09–03:02](../lessons/54-iteration-examples-breakdowns-walkthrough.md) |
| #7 | Belief issue | "A majority of the time ads fail because they are lacking trust, aka belief"; spend winner: they like the claims but don't believe them | Add authority (third party), facts, or vulnerability; script lines that prevent objections | Korean cream: "...look like a 17yo girl again" → "When you're 35, but don't have a wrinkle in sight because a MILF was on here talking about how this $9 Korean cream is better than the Tox"; dental: "...can help" → "Depending how bad your dog's buildup is, this new powder ... may still be able to help." | loser, KPI winner, spend winner | [L48 16:50–18:22](../lessons/48-how-to-iterate-on-losing-ads.md); [L50 07:04–08:35](../lessons/50-how-to-iterate-on-spend-winners.md); [L54 03:53–04:46](../lessons/54-iteration-examples-breakdowns-walkthrough.md) |
| #8 | Offer issue | Lots of spend, very low conversion rate; price-to-value off; offer stated as an empty claim | Fix the offer (an iteration "outside of the ad"); in copy, frame the offer with vulnerability and urgency instead of a bare discount | "GET YOUR GLOW BACK, SAVE UP TO 25%" → "Too good not to share. Even if it hurts our margins." / "Our best bundle. Ever." | loser, KPI winner | [L48 18:22–19:35](../lessons/48-how-to-iterate-on-losing-ads.md); [L54 03:02–03:53](../lessons/54-iteration-examples-breakdowns-walkthrough.md) |
| #9 | Urgency issue | No reason to act now; no consequence of waiting | Fact → time-bound consequence → daily cost ("every day you delay...") | "Don't brush your dog's teeth. Food builds up quickly causing issues." → "Are you one of the 96% of dog owners who don't brush their dog's teeth daily? ... by age three ... a lifetime of dental issues" (96% is the slide's unverified stat) | spend winner | [L50 07:40–10:58](../lessons/50-how-to-iterate-on-spend-winners.md) |
| #10 | Stakes issue | Problem shown but low stakes or not explained | Name the concrete threat; "2 for 1": stakes raise urgency | "Here's why I regret buying this modern wallet" → "A thief can use a small device to secretly scan and steal your credit card information." | spend winner | [L50 10:58–12:15](../lessons/50-how-to-iterate-on-spend-winners.md) |
| #11 | Funnel / congruence | New spend winner with a new angle lands on a generic or mismatched page | Build or adapt the landing page (advertorial, listicle, founder page, offer) to the ad's angle and avatar | "Stomach issues? There's a soup for that." → homepage swapped for "Frustrated With Doctors and Digestive Issues? I was too." (instructor: spend winner → breakthrough, "3x'd the entire ad account spend") | spend winner | [L50 12:15–14:43](../lessons/50-how-to-iterate-on-spend-winners.md); [L54 05:32–06:23](../lessons/54-iteration-examples-breakdowns-walkthrough.md) |
| #12 | Misleading issue | High engagement, cheap clicks, poor conversion; the click bait is not the product | Make the visual star the product you sell; turn off clearly misleading ads yourself ("meta doesn't pull away fast enough") | "Five reasons why you need this dog leash" with a charm collar as the star → "5 reasons your dog needs these charms" (marked NOT A REAL AD) | spend winner | [L50 14:43–17:18](../lessons/50-how-to-iterate-on-spend-winners.md) |
| #13 | Easily replicable (time issue) | Breakthrough dies fast; native or AI UGC ad anyone can regenerate | Differentiate the product and the process; re-execute the components in harder-to-copy formats | Native "At 3:17 AM, my son Jake woke me up screaming..." story; "I could just take this ad and ... maybe I put this house on fire." | breakthrough | [L51 12:19–14:45](../lessons/51-how-to-iterate-on-breakthroughs.md); [L54 06:23](../lessons/54-iteration-examples-breakdowns-walkthrough.md) |
| #14 | Product differentiation | Same product as dozens of others; competitors copy ads and product | Add real product differentiation; own-made content; authority only you can use | Bad: cat fountain sold elsewhere at about a third of the price; good: CarePod humidifier ad that "lasted for over two years" | breakthrough | [L51 14:45–18:29](../lessons/51-how-to-iterate-on-breakthroughs.md) |

KPI winners use the loser list "literally the exact same" [L49 03:56](../lessons/49-how-to-iterate-on-kpi-winners.md). The lesson-54 slides mark the dog-dental spend winner as "#8 belief" while lesson 52's slide for the same ad says "#9 Urgency"; the code numbers on slides are not perfectly consistent, so use the **name**, not only the number.

## Triage decision table: metric pattern → codes to check (in order)
| Pattern | Check these codes first | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Hook rate < 30% (with meaningful spend) | #1, #4, then #2 / #2.5 | [L48 00:39–01:27; Steps](../lessons/48-how-to-iterate-on-losing-ads.md) |
| Decent hook rate, spend or conversion poor | #5, #6, #7, #8 | [L48 12:57–13:54; 18:56](../lessons/48-how-to-iterate-on-losing-ads.md) |
| High spend, very low conversion rate | #8 offer (outside the ad) | [L48 18:56](../lessons/48-how-to-iterate-on-losing-ads.md) |
| Spend winner, CTR/CPC fine, CVR or AOV low | #7 → #9 → #10 → #11 → #12 → #2.5, offer, open loops | [L50 01:15–02:38; Steps](../lessons/50-how-to-iterate-on-spend-winners.md) |
| Spend winner with a new angle unlike other winners | #11 (check the landing page first) | [L50 12:15](../lessons/50-how-to-iterate-on-spend-winners.md) |
| ROAS 0.2–0.3x while taking spend; high engagement | #12; consider turning off | [L50 00:35; 14:43](../lessons/50-how-to-iterate-on-spend-winners.md) |
| Spend winner ~1.8x vs 2.0x target | iterate #7/#9/#10/#11 to unlock efficiency | [L50 00:35](../lessons/50-how-to-iterate-on-spend-winners.md) |
| KPI winner at ~1% spend share | lucky loser → loser codes | [L49 00:00–00:39](../lessons/49-how-to-iterate-on-kpi-winners.md) |
| Breakthrough dropping after weeks at scale | not a code first: keep it on; check seasonality, offer-only, copied, #2, #13, #14 | [L51 03:25–10:28](../lessons/51-how-to-iterate-on-breakthroughs.md) |

The lesson-70 video prompt uses different "low" markers for its feedback questions: CTR < 1%, hook rate < 25%, hold rate < 5% [L70 docx](../lessons/70-how-to-write-video-ads-part-1.md). **Agent default:** flag hook rate < 25% as a definite hook problem and 25–30% as a probable one (see the contradiction log).

## Spend-winner metric checklist (pull before editing)
From [L50 01:15](../lessons/50-how-to-iterate-on-spend-winners.md): ad vs account **CPM, CPC/CTR, CVR, AOV**; hook rate, hold rate, hook-to-hold ("what % of people who got hooked watched 15 seconds or more"), average play time, frequency, % new visitors (Northbeam / Triple Whale), breakdowns by age, gender, placement, platform.

## Why ads lose, by awareness level (L48 page)
| Awareness of the ad | Usual reason it lost | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Unaware | "too much of a wild card ... a really big swing" with execution not good enough | [L48 01:27](../lessons/48-how-to-iterate-on-losing-ads.md) |
| Problem / solution aware | Hook not relatable, problem not painful enough, or copy "super basic" ("do you have low testosterone?") | [L48 01:27](../lessons/48-how-to-iterate-on-losing-ads.md) |
| Product / most aware | Offer not competitive, differentiation unclear | [L48 01:27](../lessons/48-how-to-iterate-on-losing-ads.md) |

## Why spend winners stall (L50 page list)
1. The hook is misleading (expectation vs page). 2. They like the claims but don't believe them. 3. Broader/colder audience but no conversion factor (funnel or hold). 4. No urgency or strong CTA. 5. Price-to-value off. 6. Open loops from earlier in the ad not closed. [L50 04:46–05:23](../lessons/50-how-to-iterate-on-spend-winners.md)

## Output template: diagnosis line
```yaml
diagnosis:
  ad: "<name>"
  outcome: "<label>"
  evidence: {hook_rate: "<%>", ctr: "<%>", cvr_vs_account: "<x>", roas_vs_target: "<x>", spend_share: "<%>"}
  codes:
    - code: "#7 Belief"
      evidence_line: "<quoted line or frame>"
      fix: "<specific change>"
      before: "<line>"
      after: "<line>"            # facts only from the product truth sheet
  changes_made: "#7 belief (third-party authority added); #1 hook (juxtaposition)"
  hypothesis_status: unproven
```

## Cross-course notes
- **Tether Lab** fixes failing video in the order speed → hook → story → creator ([test and iterate](../../../../playbooks/test-and-iterate.md)); Evolve's loser order also starts with the hook, but its spend-winner order skips the hook and starts at belief. Different problem states, not a conflict ([L50 cross-notes](../lessons/50-how-to-iterate-on-spend-winners.md)).
- **#3 Valence & intensity** is Sarah Levinger's zone model ([emotional zones](../../../../concepts/emotional-zones.md)); Tether adds "keep testing the losing zones after a winner appears", Evolve adds "stay in the winners' zone for that avatar" [L48 11:46–12:18](../lessons/48-how-to-iterate-on-losing-ads.md). Both are options; pick by learning goal.
- **One variable at a time (Tether) vs several labelled changes (Evolve):** see the contradiction log; default in the [iterate playbook](../playbooks/iterate-on-test-results.md).
