# Playbook: write static image ads
> From a concept brief to a batch of test-ready statics: pick the course route (angle-first, image-first or the Banger prompt), write headlines from verbatim customer quotes, borrow a proven layout's structure (never its words), write fact-led bullets with the model, build in the template, run the product-truth QA (the 68% vs 56% GLP-1 mismatch is the cautionary case), rest, revise, and ship one PNG per variation with the brief.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** a concept brief from [ad idea to brief](ad-idea-to-brief.md) says `format: static`; a sub-avatar or angle direction test; a video winner needs static extractions.
- **Inputs → outputs:** concept brief + Master Document (product doc, facts with sources, sub-avatars with verbatim quotes, new information, new mechanisms) + 1–3 reference ad screenshots + brand colours → `static_batch` (template below): per variation headline, sub-line, bullets, CTA box, image, truth log, PNG → gate result.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** name the angle for every concept and choose marksman (several angles) or sniper (one angle, several hooks). [L67 01:37; 05:38; 12:27](../lessons/67-how-to-write-image-ads-part-1.md)
  - **DO** write headlines from the customer's own words and behaviours ("my portion size will be meant for a toddler" → "Eating toddler-sized portions?"). [L68 26:18–27:09](../lessons/68-how-to-write-image-ads-part-2.md)
  - **DO** borrow a reference ad's structure from outside your niche and write it "EXACTLY like the ad itself. Just plugged in with our specific product + customer research", then make it ours. [L68 18:39–20:21; 11:55–12:34](../lessons/68-how-to-write-image-ads-part-2.md)
  - **DON'T** keep a reference ad's wording (copy the mechanism, not the asset); the Banger demo's "Zero stars. Would not recommend. - Mother Nature" kept the Bible App's line. [L65 Hooks](../lessons/65-banger-static-ads-prompt.md)
  - **DO** check every bullet and number against the product document and make numbers identical across variations (the course shipped 68% on one ad and 56% on the others; "this is meant to be 56%. I kind of screwed that up"). [L67 25:38–26:09](../lessons/67-how-to-write-image-ads-part-1.md); [L68 42:29](../lessons/68-how-to-write-image-ads-part-2.md)
  - **DON'T** let the model invent terms or benefits: "Be a marketer, don't be an AI regurgitator." [L68 24:27](../lessons/68-how-to-write-image-ads-part-2.md)
  - **DO** write inside the template so copy fits the layout. [L05 11:49–12:40](../lessons/05-why-you-need-to-write-in-the-environment.md)
  - **DON'T** wait for a designer; "It's okay to have ugly ads" when the information is good. [L67 22:02](../lessons/67-how-to-write-image-ads-part-1.md)
  - **DO** rest, then optimise the ads you made before making more. [L68 39:59–44:46](../lessons/68-how-to-write-image-ads-part-2.md)
- **See also:** [Static ad workflow](../frameworks/static-ad-workflow.md) · [Copy checklist scorecard](../rubrics/copy-checklist-scorecard.md) · [Memo and Growth Guide](../frameworks/mini-breakthrough-memo-and-growth-guide.md) · [Templates library](../libraries/templates.md) · [Hooks library](../libraries/hooks.md) · [Prompts library](../libraries/prompts.md) · [Word lists](../libraries/word-lists.md) · concepts: [three copy checks](../concepts/three-copy-checks.md), [labels and customer language](../concepts/labels-and-customer-language.md), [value words / Big 4](../concepts/value-words-big-4.md)

### Cautionary case: the 68% vs 56% GLP-1 mismatch (L67/L68)
| What happened | Where | Lesson for the agent |
|---|---|---|
| Instructor read "natural GLP-1 production by 68%" from the product doc and led a bullet with it ("now we're leading with a fact") | [L67 24:37–26:09](../lessons/67-how-to-write-image-ads-part-1.md) | A fact-led bullet is only as good as its source; check the primary source (the study chart said +56%) |
| The product page chart on screen read "+56% Participants on average increased GLP-1 by 56%" | [L67 23:13](../lessons/67-how-to-write-image-ads-part-1.md) | When two internal documents disagree, use the study/primary source and flag the other |
| Final ads 1 and 4 say 56%, final ad 3 says 68% | [L67 final ads](../lessons/67-how-to-write-image-ads-part-1.md) | Numbers must be identical across variations in one batch (T1) |
| Review after a break: "this is meant to be 56%. I kind of screwed that up." The Skool final image still shows 68% | [L68 42:29](../lessons/68-how-to-write-image-ads-part-2.md) | The break catches errors; fix before export, not after |
| The AI video draft repeated 68%; the final script corrected to 56% "because of what was on the website" | [L70 17:13–18:32](../lessons/70-how-to-write-video-ads-part-1.md); [L71 25:12](../lessons/71-how-to-write-video-ads-part-2.md) | Models propagate a wrong number from their context; recheck numbers in every new asset |

## Output: static batch
```yaml
static_batch:
  id: STA-<batch>-<nn>
  concept_ref: IDEA-<...>
  route: angle_first | image_first | banger_prompt | cross_format
  concept_swing: "<what we test>"
  testing_method: marksman | sniper
  awareness: "<level>"
  reference_structures:
    - {pattern: "<e.g. iPhone Notes>", source_ad: "<link>", niche: "<other niche>", copied: structure_only}
  variations:
    - id: V1
      sub_avatar: "<label>"
      angle: "<reason to buy>"
      headline: "<customer words; behaviour > state>"
      sub_line: "<...>"
      bullets: ["<fact-led, truth_ref>", "<...>", "<...>"]
      cta_box: "<closes the loop>"
      image: "<product photo | congruent generated | lifestyle>"
      quotes_used: ["<quote id>"]
      three_checks: {visual: pass, falsifiable: pass, nobody_else: pass, google_exact_match: not_found}
      truth_log: [{claim: "<...>", truth_ref: "<doc/page/study>", status: verified}]
  numbers_consistent_across_variations: true
  reviewed_after_break: {minutes: <n>, changes: ["<...>"]}
  sizes: {master: "1080x1920 (9:16)", safe_zones: ["1080x1080", "1080x1350"]}
  primary_text: ["<copy 1>", "<copy 2>"]
  headline_field: ["<headline 1>", "<headline 2>"]
  gate: {G1_G6: pass, copy_scorecard_pct: <n>, tether_ad_scorecard: <n>}
  export: ["STA-..._V1.png", "..."]
```

## Quality gates (all must pass)
1. **⛔ G1–G6** of the [copy checklist scorecard](../rubrics/copy-checklist-scorecard.md); G1 explicitly checks the same number appears on every variation.
2. **Copy checklist scorecard ≥ 80%** (D9 pitch timing is n.a. for headline statics); B1–B3 not all zero.
3. **Packaging:** headline, sub-line and bullets fit the template; bullet count as briefed; image matches the headline or is the plain product (congruence is a nice-to-have, [L68 44:46](../lessons/68-how-to-write-image-ads-part-2.md)).
4. **Tether [ad scorecard](../../../../rubrics/ad-scorecard.md) ≥ 80, no auto-fail**, criterion 9a visual hierarchy.

## (Derived) Reusing the statics for on-camera UGC
A winning static becomes the creator's frame 1: headline as the spoken first line and on-screen text, bullets as three proof beats, CTA box as the close. Behaviour headlines ("Chugging water instead of eating?") are filmable opening scenes. The truth log carries over unchanged.
