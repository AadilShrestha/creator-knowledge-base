# Unique mechanism (new, unspoken, renamed)
> The mechanism is the thing that makes a claim believable and the product different: its one job is to "inspire hope" (this will work, and it is not like everything else); every claim needs one ("what is the mechanism that can prove it?"), and there are three types to hunt for: **new**, **unspoken**, and **renamed**.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** tearing down why a competitor ad converts; choosing what our ad claims and how it proves it; a product has "nothing unique"; writing the claim/proof part of a hold; deciding what to lead with in a product-aware market.
- **Inputs → outputs:** product facts (components, ingredients, process, format, brand actions) + competitor ads + the desire/angle → candidate mechanisms classified new/unspoken/renamed with a usefulness check → for each claim: "[claim] because [mechanism]", where it sits in the script, and the visual that shows it.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** attach a mechanism to every claim: "What is the mechanism that can prove it?" [L19 03:45–04:23](../lessons/19-being-aware-of-mechanisms.md); checklist Q5 [L58](../lessons/58-copywriting-checklist-when-writing.md)
  - **DO** write the mechanism as the agent of the result: "this mechanism is what allows you to get X", and tie proof to it ("what allowed X person to get Y because they also used the mechanism"). [L14 05:08–05:59](../lessons/14-why-you-need-to-use-unique-mechanisms.md)
  - **DO** search in this order: (1) truly new mechanisms no other brand has, (2) new ones you could add, (3) unspoken ones (useful components nobody talks about), (4) worn-out but valued ones you can rename. [L14 15:36–16:31](../lessons/14-why-you-need-to-use-unique-mechanisms.md)
  - **DON'T** add a feature just to be new; it must be useful (stainless steel beats LED mood lighting). "Because it's new means that it will grab attention, but if it's not useful, it doesn't matter." [L14 06:49–09:26](../lessons/14-why-you-need-to-use-unique-mechanisms.md)
  - **DO** remember "It doesn't have to be better. It just has to be new" (but improve the product long-term). [L14 02:58–04:26](../lessons/14-why-you-need-to-use-unique-mechanisms.md)
  - **DO** prefer visual mechanisms the viewer can "put two and two together" on (gummy, can, weighted hoodie). [L19 00:00–02:15](../lessons/19-being-aware-of-mechanisms.md)
  - **DO** explain *how* it works in plain words without losing the desire: avoid getting so technical you forget "what you're actually trying to help this person accomplish". [L14 04:26–05:08](../lessons/14-why-you-need-to-use-unique-mechanisms.md); use comparisons for complex science [L33 12:44](../lessons/33-how-to-use-metaphors-similes-analogies.md)
  - **DON'T** rely on the mechanism alone for belief: "Your mechanism alone may NOT build enough belief"; pair it with an authority signalled before the pitch. [L20 00:00–00:34](../lessons/20-being-aware-of-authorities.md)
  - **IF** the market is product aware / sophisticated (stage 3-4: "how is yours different?") **THEN** lead with or bridge into a new mechanism and an authority that reframes why past solutions failed. [L21 13:35–14:32](../lessons/21-being-aware-of-market-awareness.md); [L28 04:18–05:15](../lessons/28-how-to-prevent-handle-objections-in-writing.md); [L27 02:15–03:41](../lessons/27-how-to-write-a-bridge.md)
  - **DO** (video prompt) use ONE central mechanism in the hold, describe what the product *does* rather than what category it is in, and give the mechanism a proprietary name if possible. [L72 Section C; Core Rule 2](../lessons/72-action-item-write-your-video-ad-briefs.md)
- **See also:** [Beliefs](beliefs.md) · [Authority in ads](authority-in-ads.md) · [Market awareness](market-awareness.md) · [Positioning](positioning.md) · [Bridges](bridges.md) · [Objection handling](objection-handling.md) · [Nine-component audit](../frameworks/nine-component-audit.md) · [Angles library](../libraries/angles.md)

## Definition
- "Unique mechanisms essentially serve one main job… they inspire hope." Hope = belief that "this will actually work, that this is not like everything else." [L14 00:00–00:49](../lessons/14-why-you-need-to-use-unique-mechanisms.md) Slide quote: "New and different is better than better." (credited to Kyle Milligan)
- The mechanism "is essentially what builds belief and essentially is your proof that what you're saying your product does, it actually does." [L19 01:43–02:15](../lessons/19-being-aware-of-mechanisms.md)
- "The mechanism does all of the work." It is why the product can make its claims. [L14 04:26–05:08](../lessons/14-why-you-need-to-use-unique-mechanisms.md)
- In the iteration audit, component 6 is "Is there a new mechanism communicated, i.e. how it's different?" [L46 10:58](../lessons/46-the-10-step-iteration-process.md)

## Why it matters
- **The lizard brain filters sameness.** The desire "wants to be served", but a message about HOW that is "the same as everything else" is categorised and ignored. [L14 01:41–03:37 (slide 02:20)](../lessons/14-why-you-need-to-use-unique-mechanisms.md)
- **It decides who wins the sale.** Positioned right, it feels like "the only place that has that mechanism" and "creates this bias that they have to now buy from you"; skip it and a competitor "is probably going to win the customer". [L14 00:49–01:41](../lessons/14-why-you-need-to-use-unique-mechanisms.md)
- **Product aware is where scale lives**, and people fail there because of "no positioning… [not knowing] how to write with new mechanisms the right way… how to build authority". [L21 13:35–14:32](../lessons/21-being-aware-of-market-awareness.md)
- **It is part of belief before the pitch**: "You've provided a new mechanism" is one of the belief checks before you pitch. [L29 06:08–07:43](../lessons/29-how-to-pitch-without-rejection.md)
- **Worn mechanisms need renewal**: where the market is sophisticated about a mechanism, "new mechanisms are created". [L19 02:15](../lessons/19-being-aware-of-mechanisms.md)

## The three types (decision table)
| Type | You have it when | Test | Course example | Belief it builds | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **New** | A truly unique element, ingredient, tech or formula no other brand has | Useful? Does the customer care? | CarePod stainless-steel humidifier ("The World's First Stainless Steel Humidifier"); Kizik heel that pops back up | "Easier to clean, less mould, no plastic" | [L14 05:59–09:26](../lessons/14-why-you-need-to-use-unique-mechanisms.md) |
| **Unspoken** | Nothing unique, but a component competitors don't talk about | Useful, makes sense, clear? ("toasted" explains taste; "made in Wyoming" doesn't) | Lucky Strike "It's toasted" (all brands toasted; one said it) | "That's the reason it has great taste" | [L14 09:26–13:10](../lessons/14-why-you-need-to-use-unique-mechanisms.md) |
| **Renamed** | Every component already has attention; the name is ordinary | Does the new name feel new and imply higher quality, and does the product deliver it? | Kettle & Fire "bone broth" vs chicken stock | "It's higher quality; it delivers the benefits" | [L14 12:20–15:36](../lessons/14-why-you-need-to-use-unique-mechanisms.md) |

Closing slide: "If it's presented differently it's new. And because it's new, its better." [L14 14:50–15:36](../lessons/14-why-you-need-to-use-unique-mechanisms.md)

## How to use it
1. **Inventory** every component, ingredient, process step, format and brand action (the course keeps a "New Mechanisms" tab in the master document). [L67 00:00](../lessons/67-how-to-write-image-ads-part-1.md); [L70 09:27](../lessons/70-how-to-write-video-ads-part-1.md)
2. **Classify** each candidate new / unspoken / renamed; drop decorative ones (LED-light test). [L14 06:49–09:26](../lessons/14-why-you-need-to-use-unique-mechanisms.md)
3. **Pair claim → mechanism → authority** in one chain (Claim: "You'll add more flavor"; Mechanism: higher % fat; Authority: used in Michelin-starred restaurants). [L19](../lessons/19-being-aware-of-mechanisms.md), [L20 page](../lessons/20-being-aware-of-authorities.md)
4. **Write the because-line**: "[claim] because [mechanism]" ("It's so easy to clean because it's made of stainless steel so you can just throw it right in the dishwasher"). [L28 14:38–15:48](../lessons/28-how-to-prevent-handle-objections-in-writing.md)
5. **Place it** by awareness: problem aware → angle in the hook, mechanism in the bridge/hold as THE solution; product aware → contrarian hook, mechanism early ("why YOURS is different"). [L72 awareness section](../lessons/72-action-item-write-your-video-ad-briefs.md); [L27 02:15–03:41](../lessons/27-how-to-write-a-bridge.md)
6. **Make it visible**: show it happening; for invisible mechanisms use a comparison or AI animation that makes the ingredient the hero and villainises other solutions. [L19 00:46](../lessons/19-being-aware-of-mechanisms.md); [L53 03:58–05:36](../lessons/53-the-iteration-playbook-formats-styles.md)
7. **De-categorise the claim**: "The first/only [thing] that [mechanism]" rather than "like X but natural". [L72 Core Rule 2](../lessons/72-action-item-write-your-video-ad-briefs.md)
8. **Consider a name or label** for the mechanism ("The Dyglomera Effect"; coin labels from your mechanism, which "requires adoption"). [L72 Section C](../lessons/72-action-item-write-your-video-ad-briefs.md); [L34 03:17–05:07](../lessons/34-how-to-write-with-labels-customer-language.md)
9. **Verify** every mechanism claim against the product doc (AI called the DPP-4 enzyme a hormone). [L71 03:40](../lessons/71-how-to-write-video-ads-part-2.md)

## Common mistakes
- **No mechanism at all** behind a claim ("Eating only 1,200 calories is easy" was "a pretty empty claim" that "was never explained"). [L70 03:09–04:07](../lessons/70-how-to-write-video-ads-part-1.md)
- **New but useless** features (LED lights). [L14 06:49–09:26](../lessons/14-why-you-need-to-use-unique-mechanisms.md)
- **Over-technical explanation** that forgets the desire. [L14 04:26–05:08](../lessons/14-why-you-need-to-use-unique-mechanisms.md)
- **Scattering several mechanisms** ("It has Dyglomera… plus Suntheanine… plus marine collagen…"). [L72 Section C](../lessons/72-action-item-write-your-video-ad-briefs.md)
- **Renaming without delivering**: only rename if the product genuinely delivers the implied benefit (derived caution, [L14](../lessons/14-why-you-need-to-use-unique-mechanisms.md)).
- **Trusting AI mechanism facts.** [L71 03:40](../lessons/71-how-to-write-video-ads-part-2.md)
