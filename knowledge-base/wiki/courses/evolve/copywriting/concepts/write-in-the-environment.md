# Write in the environment (copy laid out as it will appear)
> Write ad copy inside a visual brief that shows how each hook, bridge and headline will actually look in the feed (Milanote/Figma cards for video, a CreativeOS/Canva layout for statics), not in a Google Doc, because "copy is like food: how it looks matters" and seeing the layout changes what you write.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** drafting a video or static brief; writing hooks; handing a script to an editor or designer; outputting a script from an LLM (format it as cards, not a paragraph).
- **Inputs → outputs:** ad idea + research → a card-based brief: brief fields, one card per hook (with VISUALS line and the exact on-screen text and line breaks), one bridge card per hook, one hold card (or "editor's choice hold"), CTA → copy judged and shortened as it will appear.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** write in the environment the ad will appear in: "Copy is like food. How it looks matters." (quote attributed to Harry Dry) [L05 00:00–00:54](../lessons/05-why-you-need-to-write-in-the-environment.md)
  - **DON'T** write ads in Google Docs, Notion or ClickUp: "it's not the environment." [L05 00:54](../lessons/05-why-you-need-to-write-in-the-environment.md)
  - **DO** give every hook its own vertical card; for hooks "it's an absolute non-negotiable." [L05 04:42](../lessons/05-why-you-need-to-write-in-the-environment.md)
  - **DO** decide line breaks yourself (one line vs two) instead of leaving them to the editor. [L05 02:51–03:45](../lessons/05-why-you-need-to-write-in-the-environment.md)
  - **DO** label bridge cards by their hook ("hook one bridge") and say which lines share a visual and the pacing. [L05 05:35–06:54](../lessons/05-why-you-need-to-write-in-the-environment.md)
  - **IF** the video is a long VSL **THEN** use one "editor's choice hold" card and let the editor split slides; still write hooks card by card. [L05 06:54–07:49](../lessons/05-why-you-need-to-write-in-the-environment.md)
  - **IF** writing a static **THEN** draft inside a template that shows the final layout so the copy fits ("I've seen so many times our team take an ad like this and literally try to write copy that is way too long for it"). [L05 11:49–12:40](../lessons/05-why-you-need-to-write-in-the-environment.md)
  - **DO** (video) lock the script on the cards first; ask for visual instructions after the copy is final. [L70 22:12–23:07](../lessons/70-how-to-write-video-ads-part-1.md); [L71 25:12](../lessons/71-how-to-write-video-ads-part-2.md)
  - **DON'T** wait for a designer to test a static: "It's okay to have ugly ads"; the information is what is tested. [L67 22:02; 27:26](../lessons/67-how-to-write-image-ads-part-1.md)
  - **DO** remember fitting copy to a template "sometimes… forces you to make your copy sharper. Sometimes it can make your copy worse." [L05 12:40](../lessons/05-why-you-need-to-write-in-the-environment.md)
- **See also:** [Compression and plain language](compression-and-plain-language.md) · [Hooks](hooks.md) · [Bridges](bridges.md) · [Video ad script workflow](../frameworks/video-ad-script-workflow.md) · [Static ad workflow](../frameworks/static-ad-workflow.md) · [Ad idea to brief playbook](../playbooks/ad-idea-to-brief.md)

## Definition
Writing copy in the visual container it will live in (a 9:16 hook card, a scene card, a static layout), so length, line breaks, visual hierarchy and pacing are decided by the writer while writing. The page text: "Milanote/Figma with defined cards for how each scene should look - especially for hooks"; "Images can use CreativeOS specifically (writing with Images in mind first)"; "Images should be optimized ONCE they come back from the graphic designer". [L05 page text](../lessons/05-why-you-need-to-write-in-the-environment.md); [L06 page](../lessons/06-update-your-milanote-figma-for-writing.md) ("Remember, how your copy looks is CRUCIAL").

## Why it matters
- **People don't trust you, so presentation counts more than for food**: "the chef might tell you to just eat it. Trust me, it's good. Whereas most people don't trust you." [L05 00:00–00:54](../lessons/05-why-you-need-to-write-in-the-environment.md)
- **The old two-block brief ("copy" / "visuals") produced four-line hooks** because writers never saw the layout. [L05 01:53–02:51](../lessons/05-why-you-need-to-write-in-the-environment.md)
- **Seeing the card makes you cut**: "a great hook is just a good hook made shorter." [L05 03:45](../lessons/05-why-you-need-to-write-in-the-environment.md); see [compression](compression-and-plain-language.md).
- **The editor gets clear intent**, so you train editors instead of fixing their cuts ("You shouldn't be editing your own videos"). [L05 05:35–09:06](../lessons/05-why-you-need-to-write-in-the-environment.md)
- **Hooks must work without sound** (Adley's framework), which you can only judge on a card that shows the text. [L23 Skool page](../lessons/23-the-framework-behind-great-hooks.md) (derived link)

## How to use it
### Video brief (Milanote, Figma or FigJam)
1. Fill the brief fields: client name; DCT number "(Must match Creative Roadmap)"; type of ad; sizes needed; ad links to; variations needed ("3 HOOKS / 1 HOLD / 3 TOTAL ADS"); ideation concept + Growth Guide notes. [L71 final PDF](../lessons/71-how-to-write-video-ads-part-2.md); [L72 brief card set](../lessons/72-action-item-write-your-video-ad-briefs.md)
2. **Hook cards**: one vertical card per hook with a VISUALS line above; type the exact on-screen text; try one-line vs two-line. [L05 02:51–04:42](../lessons/05-why-you-need-to-write-in-the-environment.md)
3. **Bridge cards**: one per hook (the course writes a custom bridge for each hook in its 3-2-2 / 3-hook tests). [L27 23:06–23:52](../lessons/27-how-to-write-a-bridge.md); [L70 15:59–16:39](../lessons/70-how-to-write-video-ads-part-1.md)
4. **Hold card**: one universal hold (and CTA, inside or below it). Long VSL: "editor's choice hold". [L70 21:35–23:07](../lessons/70-how-to-write-video-ads-part-1.md); [L05 06:54–07:49](../lessons/05-why-you-need-to-write-in-the-environment.md)
5. Edit each line in place (cut, re-break), then run the readability check. [L71 23:47–24:24](../lessons/71-how-to-write-video-ads-part-2.md)
6. Only then add visual instructions per line (Spencer screenshots his hooks and hold and asks Claude for visuals). [L71 25:12](../lessons/71-how-to-write-video-ads-part-2.md)

### Static brief
7. Choose an order:
   - **Headline-first** (L67): angles and headlines from research → concept brief → pick a simple template → write bullets to fit. [L67 10:38–21:25](../lessons/67-how-to-write-image-ads-part-1.md)
   - **Image-first** (L68): pick a proven creative style (screenshot a winning ad from CreativeOS/Canva templates) → have Claude rewrite its headline/sub-head/bullets "exactly like the ad itself" for each sub-avatar → build. [L68 08:27–20:21](../lessons/68-how-to-write-image-ads-part-2.md)
   - Decision rule (derived): headline-first when you have a strong angle/fact; image-first when you are out of ideas or need many concepts fast (the instructor's framing of L68).
8. Write directly in the layout (Canva/CreativeOS/Figma); keep copy within the template's space; brand colours via a colour picker. [L67 13:59–17:06](../lessons/67-how-to-write-image-ads-part-1.md)
9. Take a break and review the built ads with fresh eyes before adding more. [L68 39:59–44:46](../lessons/68-how-to-write-image-ads-part-2.md)

### Agent output format (derived)
When an agent drafts a script, output it card by card: `HOOK n` (on-screen text with line breaks + VISUAL), `BRIDGE n`, `HOLD` (objection / claim / proof / benefit), `CTA`, then a separate visuals list. This mirrors the course's Milanote boards and the prompt's modular output. [L72 Output Structure](../lessons/72-action-item-write-your-video-ad-briefs.md)

## Common mistakes
- **Two-block briefs** ("copy" + "visuals") sent to editors. [L05 01:53–02:51](../lessons/05-why-you-need-to-write-in-the-environment.md)
- **Copy too long for the static template.** [L05 11:49–12:40](../lessons/05-why-you-need-to-write-in-the-environment.md)
- **Reusing one bridge for every hook.** [L27 23:06–23:52](../lessons/27-how-to-write-a-bridge.md)
- **Designing visuals before the words are locked** (video). [L70 22:12–23:07](../lessons/70-how-to-write-video-ads-part-1.md)
- **Copying a template's words** instead of its structure (derived; see [hooks](hooks.md) on templates).
