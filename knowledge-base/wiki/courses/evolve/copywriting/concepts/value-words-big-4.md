# Value words / the Big 4 (new, easy, safe, big)
> Four things every buyer wants to hear (NEW/ONLY, EASY/ANYBODY, SAFE/PREDICTABLE, BIG/FAST), each answering a silent doubt; hit the 2-3 that matter most for the angle, through subtext and proof, never as a feature list.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** choosing what a hook or headline should promise; diagnosing a flat claim; tearing down a competitor ad ("which of the Big 4 does it lean on, and how does it prove it?"); writing the "Big 4 Analysis" the course's video prompt demands after every script.
- **Inputs → outputs:** draft hook/script or competitor ad + product facts + real social-proof volume → each claim tagged NEW / EASY / SAFE / BIG, the 2-3 levers to lead with for this angle, and rewrites where each lever is shown or proven rather than asserted.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** treat the four as answers to reader doubts: "Is this new? Or is it the same old stuff I already tried?" / "Can I actually do this? Is it hard?" / "What if I try this and it doesn't work for me?" / "Will this actually change my life? Or is it just a small difference?" [L62 image](../lessons/62-value-words.md)
  - **DO** "Hit at least 2-3 of The Big 4 powerfully in every major section. Don't force all four if it makes the copy feel like a checklist." [L72 prompt, Core Rule 1](../lessons/72-action-item-write-your-video-ad-briefs.md)
  - **DO** ask "What 2-3 emotions hit hardest for THIS angle?" and let the rest live in subtext. [L72 prompt, Core Rule 1](../lessons/72-action-item-write-your-video-ad-briefs.md)
  - **DO** make UNIQUE / NEW the near-constant in hooks ("UNIQUE must almost always be present since it's the scroll-stopping element"). [L72 prompt, Four U's](../lessons/72-action-item-write-your-video-ad-briefs.md)
  - **DON'T** assert EASY without showing it: the course's own "Eating only 1,200 calories is easy with Dyglomera" static was logged a losing ad partly for "a pretty empty claim" that never explained why it was easy. [L70 03:09–04:07](../lessons/70-how-to-write-video-ads-part-1.md)
  - **DO** show ease through behaviour instead: "I had breakfast and I'm still not even hungry by dinner. Sometimes I just skip lunch." [L70 05:38](../lessons/70-how-to-write-video-ads-part-1.md)
  - **DON'T** fabricate SAFE: social-proof numbers must match real volume (tiers in [L72](../lessons/72-action-item-write-your-video-ad-briefs.md)); "Do not let it assume '12,000 women'." [L70 12:01–12:44](../lessons/70-how-to-write-video-ads-part-1.md)
  - **DON'T** express NEW by categorizing ("Like Ozempic but natural", "The best massage gun"); express it through the unique mechanism ("The first X that [mechanism]"). [L72 prompt, Core Rule 2](../lessons/72-action-item-write-your-video-ad-briefs.md)
  - **IF** you need more drama rather than more value **THEN** use power words / stakes, not value words (the course separates the two lists). [L01 04:08–04:39](../lessons/01-overview-of-copywriting-program.md)
- **See also:** [Unique mechanism](unique-mechanism.md) · [Show, don't tell](show-dont-tell.md) · [Raising the stakes](raising-the-stakes.md) · [Facts over opinions](facts-over-opinions.md) · [Objection handling](objection-handling.md) · [Hooks](hooks.md) · [Video ad script workflow](../frameworks/video-ad-script-workflow.md) · [Word lists](../libraries/word-lists.md) · [Copy checklist scorecard](../rubrics/copy-checklist-scorecard.md) · [Risk-reduction hooks (Tether Lab)](../../../../concepts/risk-reduction-hooks.md)

## Definition
"Value words" are, in the instructor's words, "new, easy, safe, big": words that "create extra value because they're what people want" [L01 04:08–04:39](../lessons/01-overview-of-copywriting-program.md). The reference table (lesson 62, credited to Kyle Milligan) pairs each group with what the copy must say and what the reader is thinking. The video-ad prompt (lessons 70-72) renames the same four groups **"The Big 4 Emotions"** and makes them a scored requirement.

The table (verbatim from the lesson 62 image):

| Value words | What You Need to Say | What They're Thinking |
|---|---|---|
| NEW / ONLY | This is something they've never seen before. It's different from everything else. | "Is this new? Or is it the same old stuff I already tried?" |
| EASY / ANYBODY | This is simple to do. It doesn't take a lot of work. Anyone can do this. | "Can I actually do this? Is it hard?" |
| SAFE / PREDICTABLE | This is safe. Other people tried it and it worked. You won't lose money if it doesn't work. | "What if I try this and it doesn't work for me?" |
| BIG / FAST | You'll see big results. It happens fast. You won't have to wait forever. | "Will this actually change my life? Or is it just a small difference?" |

Signals the video prompt lists for each (verbatim phrases from [L72](../lessons/72-action-item-write-your-video-ad-briefs.md), docx "Big 4"):

| Big 4 | Signal phrases and devices given in the prompt |
|---|---|
| NEW / ONLY | discovery language, "first time", contrarian angle, "viral", new mechanism, "first and only" |
| EASY / ANYBODY | "without trying", "automatically", "just X", "easiest way", "$1.57 per day", "Just tap below", "Starts working in 2 weeks" |
| SAFE / PREDICTABLE | social-proof numbers, "not your fault", scientific validation, "60-day money-back guarantee", "Keep the first jar anyway", no-questions-asked |
| BIG / FAST | specific timelines and percentages ("24% less, week one", "by day 10"), "Save 20%", bonus stacking, "Less than a coffee" |

## Why it matters
- Each group closes one specific doubt. A claim that does not answer any of the four reads as a feature, not a reason to buy [L62](../lessons/62-value-words.md).
- The prompt treats them as "emotional levers you pull strategically", not boxes: "Sometimes NEW + BIG carries more punch than trying to squeeze in all four." [L72 Core Rule 1](../lessons/72-action-item-write-your-video-ad-briefs.md)
- Value words without proof backfire. The course's own test showed this: the "easy" static got under ~10% of spend and was labelled a loser; the instructor's diagnosis included the unexplained "easy" claim and the need to "build trust with video" [L70 00:53–04:07](../lessons/70-how-to-write-video-ads-part-1.md).

## How to use it
1. **List every claim** in the draft or competitor ad and tag it NEW, EASY, SAFE or BIG (one claim can carry two). [L62](../lessons/62-value-words.md) (procedure derived)
2. **Pick the lead 2-3 for this angle.** Ask "What 2-3 emotions hit hardest for THIS angle?" [L72](../lessons/72-action-item-write-your-video-ad-briefs.md). Default pairings the prompt suggests for the three test hooks:

   | Hook type (prompt's suggested trio) | Dominant Big 4 |
   |---|---|
   | Discovery-led, from the New Mechanism doc | NEW/ONLY |
   | Contrarian / "what they're not telling you", from the New Information doc | NEW/ONLY + SAFE |
   | Emotional pain-point validation, from Customer Data | SAFE + BIG |

   (Source: [L72 prompt, Section A](../lessons/72-action-item-write-your-video-ad-briefs.md).)
3. **Express each lever through its device, not its adjective.** NEW → the unique mechanism, not "revolutionary" or a category comparison. EASY → a shown behaviour or "without trying". SAFE → real proof at your true volume, a guarantee, "not your fault". BIG/FAST → a specific number or timeline you can substantiate. [L72](../lessons/72-action-item-write-your-video-ad-briefs.md); [L70 05:38](../lessons/70-how-to-write-video-ads-part-1.md)
4. **Match SAFE to your real proof tier** (prompt's Social Proof Scaling System): 10,000+ customers → exact counts; 1,000-10,000 → "thousands"; 100-1,000 → "hundreds" or percentages; 10-100 reviews → rating and percentages, lead with testimonial content; under 10 / white-label → borrowed proof only if true and attributable, clinical data, or skip volume claims. [L72](../lessons/72-action-item-write-your-video-ad-briefs.md)
5. **Read it back for "listiness".** The prompt's human-writer check: "Which 2-3 emotions hit hardest for THIS angle? Does this read naturally or like a feature list? Am I communicating emotions or just stating facts?" [L72 "The Big 4 Checklist - Human Writer Version"](../lessons/72-action-item-write-your-video-ad-briefs.md)
6. **Write the Big 4 Analysis** after a script (how NEW, EASY, SAFE, BIG are each communicated per section), as the prompt's strategic-explanation block requires. [L72 Part 5](../lessons/72-action-item-write-your-video-ad-briefs.md); [L70 18:32–20:20](../lessons/70-how-to-write-video-ads-part-1.md)

**Product-type emphasis** (prompt's adaptations, [L72](../lessons/72-action-item-write-your-video-ad-briefs.md)): health/supplement/high-tech → SAFE via science and named ingredients; low-to-mid apparel → EASY = fits existing wardrobe, SAFE via user-volume stats if you have them; high perceived value/luxury → BIG transformation and status outcomes, guarantees and exclusive access.

## Common mistakes
- **Stacking all four in one line** ("fast and easy with this safe new..."): the prompt's named failure. [L72](../lessons/72-action-item-write-your-video-ad-briefs.md)
- **Saying "easy" instead of showing it**, leaving the viewer thinking "Sure it's easy". [L70 03:09–05:38](../lessons/70-how-to-write-video-ads-part-1.md)
- **Inflating SAFE**: letting the model invent review counts or customer numbers; claiming another brand's proof as yours. [L70 12:01–12:44](../lessons/70-how-to-write-video-ads-part-1.md); [L72 Borrowed Proof Protocol](../lessons/72-action-item-write-your-video-ad-briefs.md)
- **Expressing NEW as "better than X"**, which files the product into an existing category ("Categorization = Death"). [L72 Core Rule 2](../lessons/72-action-item-write-your-video-ad-briefs.md)
- **Vague BIG**: "Hopkins' Rule: Vague claims are ignored. Specific claims are believed." Use a substantiated number or timeline. [L72 Four U's](../lessons/72-action-item-write-your-video-ad-briefs.md)
- **Confusing value words with power words**: power words add drama/intensity ([L63](../lessons/63-power-words.md)); value words add perceived value. [L01 04:08–04:39](../lessons/01-overview-of-copywriting-program.md)

## Cross-course notes
- **Tether Lab (Sarah Levinger)** — [Risk-reduction hooks](../../../../concepts/risk-reduction-hooks.md): "Some people don't actually want to change their life. They want to reduce the risk of going through the process", and "We think aspirational everything… stop doing that." This maps onto Evolve's SAFE and EASY rows. **Tension** with Evolve's BIG/FAST row, which assumes the reader asks "Will this actually change my life? Or is it just a small difference?" [L62](../lessons/62-value-words.md). Agent default (derived): for skeptical, burned-before or anxious buyers lead with SAFE + EASY and keep BIG as a specific, modest, provable number; for curious or novelty-seeking buyers lead NEW + BIG. Logged in contradictions notes.
- **Tether Lab** — [Specificity](../../../../concepts/heuristics/specificity.md), [Social proof](../../../../concepts/heuristics/social-proof.md) and [Loss aversion](../../../../concepts/heuristics/loss-aversion.md) are the heuristic names for how BIG, SAFE and the stakes-side of copy work; Evolve's Big 4 is a claim checklist, Tether's heuristics are the psychological mechanism behind it. Use both: tag the Big 4 for "what is promised", the heuristic for "why it lands".
- **Tether Lab** — [Ad scorecard](../../../../rubrics/ad-scorecard.md) and the truth rules in the shared [Rules library](../../../../libraries/rules.md) agree with Evolve's proof-tier rule: never invent stats, testimonials or counts.
