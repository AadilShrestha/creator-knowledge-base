# Iteration outcomes (loser / KPI winner / spend winner / breakthrough)
> Every tested ad gets exactly one of four labels, set from two facts (did it hit the KPI? did it pull the majority of spend?), and the label decides what you iterate: losers and KPI winners get *fixed*, spend winners get their *conversion* repaired, breakthroughs get *kept alive and multiplied*.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** an ad (ours, or a competitor's we are modelling) has finished a test window and you must decide keep / kill / iterate and which iteration list to open; also when logging results in the Growth Guide or briefing the next batch.
- **Inputs → outputs:** ad spend and campaign spend over the same window, the account KPI (ROAS/CPA target) and the ad's KPI, days live, ad type (ideation / imitation / iteration), hook rate / hold rate / CTR / CVR (only if spend is significant), landing page → one label + a decision (leave running / treat as loser / fix conversion / protect and dissect) + the matching iteration list and the number of retries allowed.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** label before you iterate: "the most crucial thing that you need to do when it comes to iteration is you need to have a clear process on actually identifying what is the outcome of your ad." [L45 00:00](../lessons/45-overview-of-iteration-section.md)
  - **DO** wait **at least 7 days** before labelling; **IF** an ad is plainly scaling the account within **2-3 days** **THEN** you may start early. [L46 02:05-03:31](../lessons/46-the-10-step-iteration-process.md)
  - **DO** record ad-set spend and campaign spend for the same period so the label can be checked. [L46 03:31-04:14](../lessons/46-the-10-step-iteration-process.md)
  - **DON'T** read hook rate or hold rate on trivial spend ("$2 ... 70% hook rate ... needs more data"); the page's minimum is **$1k spend after 7 days**. [L46 07:18-08:00](../lessons/46-the-10-step-iteration-process.md)
  - **DO** set the "majority of spend" bar by account size: roughly **10%** of campaign spend for big accounts up to **60-70%** for small ones. [L45 01:29-02:24](../lessons/45-overview-of-iteration-section.md)
  - **DON'T** iterate without learnings: "If you didn't do learnings, don't do another iteration." [L48 04:59](../lessons/48-how-to-iterate-on-losing-ads.md)
  - **DON'T** iterate a losing or KPI-winning **imitation** (habit, not a ban). [L48 04:00-04:59](../lessons/48-how-to-iterate-on-losing-ads.md); [L49 03:06](../lessons/49-how-to-iterate-on-kpi-winners.md)
  - **IF** a spend winner's ROAS is **0.2-0.3x** **THEN** suspect a misleading ad and turn it off; **IF** it is near target (page: 1.8x vs 2.0x) **THEN** iterate for efficiency. [L50 00:35](../lessons/50-how-to-iterate-on-spend-winners.md)
  - **DON'T** turn a breakthrough off after a few bad days: "your ads earn respect." [L51 03:25-05:36](../lessons/51-how-to-iterate-on-breakthroughs.md)
  - **IF** you have run **30 iterations** without another winner **THEN** suspect shallow iterations, not the concept. [L46 19:46](../lessons/46-the-10-step-iteration-process.md)
  - **DO** name every iteration `ITER#[N]_BATCH#[ORIG#]` so you can count them. [L46 18:54-21:22](../lessons/46-the-10-step-iteration-process.md)
  - **DON'T** invent spend, ROAS or outcome labels for competitor ads; the instructor himself labels his deck examples on limited data (NDAs). [L54 02:09-03:02](../lessons/54-iteration-examples-breakdowns-walkthrough.md)

## Definition
Spencer's four outcome classes, defined on two axes [L45 01:29-02:24](../lessons/45-overview-of-iteration-section.md):

| Outcome | Hits KPI? | Pulls the majority of spend? | Instructor's wording | Core problem it signals |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Loser** | No | No | "ads that don't hit your KPI and don't pull a majority of spend"; "nobody cared about the ad" [L48 00:39](../lessons/48-how-to-iterate-on-losing-ads.md) | Attention: usually the hook [L48 00:00-00:39](../lessons/48-how-to-iterate-on-losing-ads.md) |
| **KPI winner** | Yes | No | "ads that hit your KPI, but they're just not pulling majority of spend" | Either a lucky loser or a bottom-of-funnel/offer ad doing its job [L49 00:00-01:27](../lessons/49-how-to-iterate-on-kpi-winners.md) |
| **Spend winner** | No (below account) | Yes | hits "the majority spend threshold, but they are not hitting your KPI and they are not allowing you to scale your account" | Conversion: belief, urgency, stakes, funnel [L50 02:02-02:38](../lessons/50-how-to-iterate-on-spend-winners.md) |
| **Breakthrough** | Usually ("most of the time they usually do") | Yes, at breakthrough levels | "allow you to scale and increase your ad spend" | Time: it dies too fast and is under-dissected [L51 00:00](../lessons/51-how-to-iterate-on-breakthroughs.md) |

Illustration of a breakthrough: "an ad that took us from $2,000 a day in spend to 2.5K per day ... or 4K ... or 5K per day" [L51 00:00-00:51](../lessons/51-how-to-iterate-on-breakthroughs.md). A breakthrough is "step one of scaling your brand" (page text, L51).

The labels are a fixed vocabulary in the Growth Guide (RESULTS column: `Breakthrough`, `Spend Winner`, `KPI Winner`, `Loser`). Its hit-rate formula counts **Breakthrough + Spend Winner as wins** and **Loser + KPI Winner as misses** ([L22](../lessons/22-how-to-finalize-your-ad-ideas.md), Ad Roadmap P2/Q2 formulas).

## Why it matters
- What you change depends on the label. Losers and KPI winners need fixing; spend winners and breakthroughs are the only ads the script/format iteration playbook targets, "because these have proven that, hey, these are getting spent. People give a shit about this." [L52 00:00](../lessons/52-the-iteration-playbook-script.md)
- Wrong labels produce wrong fixes. Example: a spend winner is already stopping people, so "hold iteration mode" (new formats, new holds) misses the real gap, which is "attention that will actually buy". [L50 03:29-04:46](../lessons/50-how-to-iterate-on-spend-winners.md)
- Turning off a breakthrough because of a dip "can be catastrophic to the account": with no other winners "you're just going to go back down to your pre-breakthrough levels". [L51 03:25; 05:36](../lessons/51-how-to-iterate-on-breakthroughs.md)
- Instructor claim (no data shown): when a breakthrough is iterated properly, "99.9% of the time ... it is almost guaranteed that we find another breakthrough within like one to two weeks". [L45 03:10-03:45](../lessons/45-overview-of-iteration-section.md)

## How to use it

### Step 1. Collect the inputs before labelling
1. Wait 7 days (or 2-3 days if the ad is obviously scaling the account). [L46 02:05-03:31](../lessons/46-the-10-step-iteration-process.md)
2. Copy ad-set spend and campaign spend for the same window. [L46 03:31-04:14](../lessons/46-the-10-step-iteration-process.md); demo values "$500" ad vs "$25,000" campaign. [L47 04:41-05:12](../lessons/47-claude-code-10-step-iteration-process.md)
3. Note the ad type by where the *idea* came from: ideation (research), imitation (another brand's ad), iteration (one of our ads). [L46 04:14-06:33](../lessons/46-the-10-step-iteration-process.md)
4. Write every hook verbatim (on-screen text and voiceover). Add hook/hold rate only if spend is significant. [L46 06:33-08:00](../lessons/46-the-10-step-iteration-process.md)

### Step 2. Apply the thresholds the course states

| Decision | Threshold / rule (as stated) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| When to label | ≥ 7 days live; 2-3 days if it "starts ripping and it starts scaling the entire account" | [L46 02:05-03:31](../lessons/46-the-10-step-iteration-process.md) |
| "Majority of spend" | ~10% of campaign spend (large accounts) up to 60-70% (small accounts); exact definitions exist for 0-100K and 500K+/month accounts, not yet for 100-500K/month | [L45 00:57; 01:29-02:24](../lessons/45-overview-of-iteration-section.md) |
| Significant spend for hook/hold metrics | $1k minimum after 7 days (page recommendation) | [L46 07:18-08:00](../lessons/46-the-10-step-iteration-process.md) |
| Hook failed to grab | Hook rate < 30% ("we have had breakthroughs with 30% hook rates", so not absolute) | [L48 00:39-01:27](../lessons/48-how-to-iterate-on-losing-ads.md) |
| "Low" metrics in the video-ad prompt | CTR < 1%, hook rate < 25%, hold rate < 5% | [L70 docx feedback questions](../lessons/70-how-to-write-video-ads-part-1.md) |
| KPI winner = lucky loser | Spend share around 1% (instructor: $300-400/day on a $15-30K/day account) | [L49 00:00-00:39](../lessons/49-how-to-iterate-on-kpi-winners.md) |
| KPI winner worth leaving on | ~10% share hitting KPI (instructor: $100-200 of a $2,000/day account); BOF ads hover "10%, 15%", or "maybe five" at scale | [L49 00:39-02:14](../lessons/49-how-to-iterate-on-kpi-winners.md) |
| Spend winner triage | 0.2-0.3x ROAS → misleading, turn off; "1.x ROAS when your target's a 2" (page 1.8x vs 2.00x) → iterate | [L50 00:35](../lessons/50-how-to-iterate-on-spend-winners.md) |
| Breakthrough leeway | Crushing 3 weeks then 1 bad week → leave it; "skyrockets for three days and then crashes for a week" → less leeway; "sometimes we let breakthrough ads run for a week" of bad results; a month+ at scale "earns respect" | [L51 03:25-05:36](../lessons/51-how-to-iterate-on-breakthroughs.md) |
| Niche angle in its own CBO/ABO | Only at scale: page "$3k/day" minimum ("likely cope" below), video "10k per day is probably a better benchmark" | [L48 06:43-08:31](../lessons/48-how-to-iterate-on-losing-ads.md) |
| Go broad? | Brands "sub 100K ... sub 200K per month" should go narrower unless DR copy is strong | [L48 08:31](../lessons/48-how-to-iterate-on-losing-ads.md) |
| Iteration ceiling | 30 iterations without a new winner → iterations "are not in depth and they're not with intent" | [L46 19:46](../lessons/46-the-10-step-iteration-process.md) |
| Pattern evidence | "After 10-15 batches, patterns show up ... iterations stop being guesses" (skill message on screen) | [L47 04:05](../lessons/47-claude-code-10-step-iteration-process.md) |

### Step 3. Route by label

| Label | First question | Retries allowed by ad type | What to change | Don't |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Loser** | Hook rate < 30%? Then the hook is the suspect [L48 00:39](../lessons/48-how-to-iterate-on-losing-ads.md) | Ideation: "2-3 more times", each with new evidence. Iteration of a winner: compare with the original ("you made the ad worse, or you didn't make it different enough for Meta to really care"). Imitation: usually stop [L48 02:18-04:59](../lessons/48-how-to-iterate-on-losing-ads.md) | Walk the loser issue list: #1 hook, #2 TAM, #2.5 broad, #3 valence/intensity, #4 audience signal to Meta, #5 empty claims, #6 bridge, #7 belief, #8 offer [L48 04:59-19:00](../lessons/48-how-to-iterate-on-losing-ads.md) | Re-make without a specific, evidenced fix; iterate an iteration-of-a-loser that lost again without new evidence [L48 04:00](../lessons/48-how-to-iterate-on-losing-ads.md) |
| **KPI winner** | Was it built as a BOF / offer / retargeting ad? [L49 00:39-01:27](../lessons/49-how-to-iterate-on-kpi-winners.md) | BOF/offer ideation doing its job: "mission accomplished". Non-offer ideation: 2-3 more tries with evidence. Imitation: rarely [L49 03:06](../lessons/49-how-to-iterate-on-kpi-winners.md) | If lucky loser: the same eight loser iterations; if BOF ad: maybe test formats or make it "more offer focused" [L49 03:06-03:56](../lessons/49-how-to-iterate-on-kpi-winners.md) | Expect it to scale the account; dismiss it either (KPI winners "can sometimes hold up the account") [L49 02:14; 03:56](../lessons/49-how-to-iterate-on-kpi-winners.md) |
| **Spend winner** | Which of CPM, CPC/CTR, CVR, AOV is weak vs the account? Usually CTR is fine and CVR/AOV is not [L50 01:15; 02:38](../lessons/50-how-to-iterate-on-spend-winners.md) | Ideation: "we always try the idea 3-5 more times" (with a direction). Imitation: 2-3 more (page) / "a few" (video). Iteration that stays a spend winner: diagnose again [L50 05:23](../lessons/50-how-to-iterate-on-spend-winners.md) | Fix in order: belief (#7: authority, facts, vulnerability), urgency (#9), stakes (#10), funnel congruence (#11), misleading (#12), broad (#2.5); keep format and hook unless the hook misleads [L50 06:17-17:18](../lessons/50-how-to-iterate-on-spend-winners.md) | Fall into "hold iteration mode"; change format "blindly" first [L50 03:29-04:46; 17:18](../lessons/50-how-to-iterate-on-spend-winners.md) |
| **Breakthrough** | How long has it been at scale and KPI? (leeway) [L51 04:22-05:36](../lessons/51-how-to-iterate-on-breakthroughs.md) | All three ad types: "Always" [L51 11:22](../lessons/51-how-to-iterate-on-breakthroughs.md) | Dissect into components; run script iterations (components rewrite first) and format iterations; score lifespan risks (#13 easily replicable, #14 product differentiation, TAM, offer-only, seasonality) [L51 05:36-18:29](../lessons/51-how-to-iterate-on-breakthroughs.md); [L52 19:07](../lessons/52-the-iteration-playbook-script.md); [L53](../lessons/53-the-iteration-playbook-formats-styles.md) | Turn off after a few bad days; spam "hook iterations or format iterations that you think are better, but they don't convert" [L51 03:25](../lessons/51-how-to-iterate-on-breakthroughs.md) |

### Step 4. Log and name
- Write the hypothesis as a hypothesis: "this is a hypothesis. This is not fact." It becomes a learning only when the iteration built on it "pops". [L46 14:17-15:40](../lessons/46-the-10-step-iteration-process.md)
- Name iterations `ITER#[N]_BATCH#[ORIG#]` at file, ad and ad-set level. [L46 18:54-21:22](../lessons/46-the-10-step-iteration-process.md)
- Label every change in a "Changes Made" line, especially when several components change at once. [L54 08:49](../lessons/54-iteration-examples-breakdowns-walkthrough.md)

## Common mistakes
- **Iterating before labelling**, or labelling on 2 days of data without a clear scaling signal. [L45 00:00](../lessons/45-overview-of-iteration-section.md); [L46 02:05](../lessons/46-the-10-step-iteration-process.md)
- **Throwing out an angle that was never executed**: the Growth Guide said problem aware, the finished ad read unaware. "This is where a lot of you guys are like throwing out angles ... when it just wasn't even executed properly." [L46 09:20](../lessons/46-the-10-step-iteration-process.md); [L47 05:12-05:55](../lessons/47-claude-code-10-step-iteration-process.md)
- **Calling "some purchases" a win**: "all I care about ... is an ad that scales." [L70 01:47](../lessons/70-how-to-write-video-ads-part-1.md)

## Cross-course notes
- **Evolve (Spencer Pawliw) vs Tether Lab, taxonomy:** Tether Lab sorts ads into three buckets (net-new experiment / scaling / variation) and says iterate the winner's *format*, not its core message ([Iteration vs variation](../../../../concepts/iteration-vs-variation.md)). Evolve adds a four-way *outcome* taxonomy that decides *what* to fix, and its script list also changes message structure (desire shift, negative framing, new sub-avatar). Use Evolve's label to pick the problem and Tether's bucket to plan the batch.
- **One variable vs several:** Tether Lab iterates "a proven winner one variable at a time" ([Creative testing system](../../../../frameworks/creative-testing-system.md)); Evolve's example pairs change several things at once (TRT: visual + sub-avatar + valence) and only ask you to label each change [L54 08:49](../lessons/54-iteration-examples-breakdowns-walkthrough.md). Agent default: one primary change per iteration; if you bundle changes, log each one in the ITER name or a "Changes Made" line.
- **Fatigue / keep a winner on:** agreement. Tether Lab: "frequency of two" is not fatigue, don't kill a format because *you* are bored. Evolve: don't switch off a breakthrough after a few bad days; leeway grows with weeks at scale [L51 04:22-05:36](../lessons/51-how-to-iterate-on-breakthroughs.md).
- **Budget scaling:** Tether Lab scales +10% every other day rising to +10-15%/day ([Creative testing system](../../../../frameworks/creative-testing-system.md)); Evolve illustrates a budget jump on a breakthrough (2K to 5K/day, then back to 2.5K as it dies) without stating a rule [L51 02:33-03:25](../lessons/51-how-to-iterate-on-breakthroughs.md). Agent default: Tether's gradual rule for budget, Evolve's leeway rule for keeping the creative on.
- **Diagnosis order:** Tether Lab fixes a failing video speed → hook → story → visuals; Evolve's loser order also starts with the hook, but its spend-winner order starts *after* the hook (belief, urgency, stakes, funnel). Different problem states, not a conflict.
- **Few purchases ≠ proof:** both. Tether: "volume is evil until you have data"; Evolve: ~1% spend share with purchases is a lucky loser [L49 00:00-00:39](../lessons/49-how-to-iterate-on-kpi-winners.md).
