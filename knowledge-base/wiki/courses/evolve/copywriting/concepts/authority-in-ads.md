# Authority in ads (3 levels, 10 types, 2 amplifiers)
> Authority has "one job in your ad… to build absolute conviction"; the course gives three levels (you are it / learned from it / know of it), ten types (credential, following, media, award/patent, organization, title, research, experience, borrowed, visual), two word-only amplifiers (relatability, transparency) and one extra (volume/virality), and says to signal it **before** the pitch and stack two or more types, without ever faking an endorsement.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** tearing down a competitor ad ("where is the authority and what type?"); writing any hook/script/static whose claim must be believed; briefing a founder or creator; fixing a loser or spend winner with a "belief issue".
- **Inputs → outputs:** our truthful assets (founder/head of product, creator experience, studies, awards/patents, partnerships, press, creator volume) + the claim + audience attitude to experts → 2-4 stacked authority types, the line or visual that carries each, and its position (hook, bridge, before the pitch).
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** put some form of authority in every ad; the instructor says the hardest-scaling brands all have it (instructor claim). [L16 00:00–01:47](../lessons/16-why-you-need-to-understand-authority.md); checklist Q6 [L58](../lessons/58-copywriting-checklist-when-writing.md)
  - **DO** "Signal authority BEFORE you make your pitch, not during." [L20 page; 00:00–00:34](../lessons/20-being-aware-of-authorities.md)
  - **DO** stack types: "You can manufacture authority through strategic stacking of principles." [L16 37:47](../lessons/16-why-you-need-to-understand-authority.md)
  - **DO** put the authority *in* the ad delivering the line: "It's one thing to say David Beckham uses it. It's another to literally have David Beckham in the ad." [L16 07:26–08:23](../lessons/16-why-you-need-to-understand-authority.md); "the authority is actually delivering it. Not someone who knows the authority." [L53 11:13–13:09](../lessons/53-the-iteration-playbook-formats-styles.md)
  - **DON'T** claim an endorsement you don't have. Borrowed authority says "we noticed X about Navy SEALs", not "Navy SEALs endorse us". [L16 21:13–23:09](../lessons/16-why-you-need-to-understand-authority.md)
  - **DON'T** name-drop experts without compliance review; "if you want to be completely safe, just say experts." [L16 23:09–23:57](../lessons/16-why-you-need-to-understand-authority.md)
  - **DON'T** rely on a doctor alone in a sophisticated market: "just having a doctor… is not always enough." [L16 05:53–06:44](../lessons/16-why-you-need-to-understand-authority.md)
  - **IF** you have no credentialed person **THEN** use types a brand controls: title (founder/head of product), experience, research, relatability, transparency, borrowed. [L16 15:39–16:13; 29:12](../lessons/16-why-you-need-to-understand-authority.md)
  - **DO** let authorities make the claims (repetition of proof: "These experts found…"); the narrator only relays. [L28 25:35–27:37](../lessons/28-how-to-prevent-handle-objections-in-writing.md)
  - **DO** state experience before the claim, and share what you learned: don't "jump straight to the claim". [L16 19:27–20:00](../lessons/16-why-you-need-to-understand-authority.md)
  - **IF** the hook is a dramatic action **THEN** introduce the authority right after it (choking clip, then "I'm a commercial technician… one type of call that still haunts me"). [L23 07:19–07:58](../lessons/23-the-framework-behind-great-hooks.md); [L35 14:25–14:59](../lessons/35-how-to-write-unaware-ads-hooks.md)
- **See also:** [Beliefs](beliefs.md) · [Unique mechanism](unique-mechanism.md) · [Objection handling](objection-handling.md) · [Facts over opinions](facts-over-opinions.md) · [Raising the stakes](raising-the-stakes.md) · [Nine-component audit](../frameworks/nine-component-audit.md) · [Templates library](../libraries/templates.md)

## Definition
- "Authority has one job in your ad, and that is to build absolute conviction in your potential customer" (attributed to Eugene Schwartz). [L16 00:56–01:47](../lessons/16-why-you-need-to-understand-authority.md)
- "Authority… is what accelerates trust." [L16 37:47](../lessons/16-why-you-need-to-understand-authority.md)
- In the iteration audit the instructor folds authority into the **belief** component ("What are we doing to build trust? Clear authority or strategic copy?"). [L46 10:58](../lessons/46-the-10-step-iteration-process.md)

## Why it matters
- **Belief needs a carrier.** Authority is "by far one of the best ways and clearest ways" to strengthen belief. [L16 00:00–00:56](../lessons/16-why-you-need-to-understand-authority.md)
- **Beliefs formed by authorities need authorities to shift** (the Reddit trainer example). [L15 14:03–14:53](../lessons/15-why-you-need-to-understand-beliefs.md)
- **Mechanisms are not always enough**: "Your mechanism alone may NOT build enough belief. Which where authority is even more crucial." [L20 page](../lessons/20-being-aware-of-authorities.md)
- **Missing authority is a top reason ads fail**; the instructor expects many students to find "absolutely no authority here at all". [L16 37:47–38:37](../lessons/16-why-you-need-to-understand-authority.md) Belief issues (#7) are fixed by "adding more authority and/or optimizing your script". [L48 16:50](../lessons/48-how-to-iterate-on-losing-ads.md); [L50 07:04](../lessons/50-how-to-iterate-on-spend-winners.md)
- "If you are not establishing yourself as an authority in your market, someone else will." (instructor's own line) [L16 00:00–00:56](../lessons/16-why-you-need-to-understand-authority.md)

## How to use it
1. **List truthful assets** (founder, head of product, creator experience, studies, awards, patents, partnerships, press, creator/UGC volume). [L16 how to apply](../lessons/16-why-you-need-to-understand-authority.md)
2. **Chain claim → mechanism → authority** for each angle (Grüns: "I'm a mom of 5 and all my kids look forward to taking them"). [L20 page](../lessons/20-being-aware-of-authorities.md)
3. **Pick a primary type** you can claim; add relatability to the pain; add one supporting type (research, borrowed, a third-party recommendation like "our speech therapist sent me this brand"); add a visual cue. [L16 30:44–33:36](../lessons/16-why-you-need-to-understand-authority.md) (stacking recipe derived from the lesson)
4. **Place it before the pitch** (hook, bridge, or first lines of the hold). [L20 00:00–00:34](../lessons/20-being-aware-of-authorities.md) Bridges #4 (reposition) and #5 (new information) work best "ideally with/from an authority". [L27 03:41–04:39](../lessons/27-how-to-write-a-bridge.md)
5. **Match voice to format**: in a third-person AI voiceover keep the authority frame without first person ("Scientists call it DPP-4" instead of "My doctor told me"). [L71 16:01](../lessons/71-how-to-write-video-ads-part-2.md)
6. **Use a witness** to raise stakes: an authority witness intensifies the moment ("Cop found us under a bridge again"). [L30 04:45–05:43; 12:15–13:22](../lessons/30-how-to-raise-the-stakes.md)
7. **Compliance pass**: no implied endorsements; "experts say" if unsure; real numbers only. [L16 23:09–23:57](../lessons/16-why-you-need-to-understand-authority.md)

## Common mistakes
- **No authority anywhere** in the ad. [L16 38:37](../lessons/16-why-you-need-to-understand-authority.md)
- **Caption-only authority** ("X recommends") instead of the authority speaking (the instructor calls the Brad Mondo "recommends" version almost level two to three). [L16 07:26–08:23](../lessons/16-why-you-need-to-understand-authority.md)
- **Invented "banned episode" style borrowing** ("They banned this episode…"; "I don't think they actually did"): "This is not something that we do." [L16 24:55–26:42](../lessons/16-why-you-need-to-understand-authority.md)
- **Lecturing with research** instead of educating lightly. [L16 17:12–18:07](../lessons/16-why-you-need-to-understand-authority.md)
- **Copying a script to a new sub-avatar without changing the authority** (a tattoo artist's authority won't land with people without tattoos). [L52 17:32](../lessons/52-the-iteration-playbook-script.md)
- **Founders too embarrassed to film**: "probably an answer to your question of why you can't scale" (instructor). [L16 14:41–15:39](../lessons/16-why-you-need-to-understand-authority.md)

## Cross-course notes
- **Agreement (Tether Lab):** [Authority bias](../../../../concepts/heuristics/authority-bias.md) ("We trust *perceived* experts", including well-read people, family and friends) matches Evolve's experience/relatability authority; [authority borrowing](../../../../concepts/heuristics/authority-borrowing.md) ("position the brand as the applier, not the owner"; don't imply endorsements) matches Evolve's borrowed-authority formulas. Tether's [messenger effect](../../../../concepts/heuristics/messenger-effect.md) ("who says the message is… more important than what the message is") matches L53's "the authority is actually delivering it".
- **Disagreement (authority by default vs validation first):** Evolve says every ad needs authority and recommends credential/visual signals such as a white coat or podcast set [L16](../lessons/16-why-you-need-to-understand-authority.md). Tether's [validation over authority](../../../../concepts/validation-over-authority.md) says audiences let down by experts (e.g. women 30+/50+ in hormone health) should get validation first ("your gut feeling is right"), with "no white coats unless the practitioner type is trusted". Agent default: keep some authority in every ad (Evolve), but choose the type this audience trusts (peer experience, relatability, the practitioner they now see) and lead with validation for expert-burned audiences (Tether). Logged in the contradictions notes.
- **Disagreement (AI presenters):** Evolve uses honest AI narrators and AI-animated characters as formats [L53 08:51–09:37](../lessons/53-the-iteration-playbook-formats-styles.md); Tether's messenger-effect page warns against AI UGC avatars "when brand trust and identity attachment matter". Default: never present an AI person as a real customer or expert; use AI characters as obvious characters, not as authority.
