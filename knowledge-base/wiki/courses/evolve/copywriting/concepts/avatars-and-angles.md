# Avatars and angles (who it's for, and the reason to buy)
> Before any headline, answer **who am I writing this for?** (an avatar built from a desire, then experiences, behaviours, emotions and beliefs) and **what is the angle?** ("the main reason you're giving someone to buy"); every ad needs exactly one main angle aimed at one avatar, because the same product needs a different headline for a beginner than for a pro.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** starting any concept, hook or static; naming the avatar and angle of a competitor ad; switching a winner to a new sub-avatar; logging ideas; diagnosing a loser as "TAM" (too niche) or "too broad".
- **Inputs → outputs:** product functions + research (reviews, Reddit quotes) → avatar cards (desire → experience → emotion → behaviour → belief, plus verbatim quotes) → angles written as reasons to buy ("You will…", "Your child will…"), tagged by type → one main angle per ad and a headline written for that avatar only → a logged row (desire, sub-avatar, angle, awareness).
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** build avatars starting from a **desire**, then experiences, behaviours, emotions; demographics only if needed. "There are better ways to make avatars than just using demographics." [L17 00:00–00:58](../lessons/17-being-aware-of-avatars.md)
  - **DO** answer "Who am I writing this for?" and "Why do they care?" as "They want [desire]. But they have the belief [belief]." [L17 00:58–02:20](../lessons/17-being-aware-of-avatars.md); checklist Q1-2 [L58](../lessons/58-copywriting-checklist-when-writing.md)
  - **DO** write the angle as a plain reason to buy ("Your child will enjoy taking their greens", "You won't be drinking microplastics"). [L18 00:00–03:00](../lessons/18-being-aware-of-angles.md)
  - **DO** focus each ad on one main angle: "Every ad needs an angle. But you must focus on one main angle per ad." [L18 03:42 page](../lessons/18-being-aware-of-angles.md)
  - **DON'T** reuse a headline across avatars ("Never overcook chicken again" vs "The wagyu of chicken"). [L17 02:57–04:26](../lessons/17-being-aware-of-avatars.md)
  - **DON'T** mistake a concept for an angle: "If the concept doesn't give the customer a reason to buy, it's not an angle yet." Us vs Them, Before & After, "video version" are concepts/formats. [L67 angle-prompt doc](../lessons/67-how-to-write-image-ads-part-1.md); "Even if your concept is 'testing formats,' you still need to define why someone is going to buy from you." [L18 page](../lessons/18-being-aware-of-angles.md)
  - **DO** narrow broad angles by naming who it's for ("easiest way to get your daily greens" → "your child will enjoy taking their greens"). [L18 00:43–01:26](../lessons/18-being-aware-of-angles.md)
  - **IF** your account spends under about $100-200K/month or your direct-response copy is weak **THEN** go narrower, not broad. [L48 08:31–09:17](../lessons/48-how-to-iterate-on-losing-ads.md)
  - **IF** an angle resonates but is too niche to scale next to your winners **THEN** widen it, or (only at scale, ~$3k-10k/day) run it in its own campaign. [L48 06:43–08:31](../lessons/48-how-to-iterate-on-losing-ads.md)
  - **DON'T** copy a winning script verbatim to a new sub-avatar; rewrite the authority, setting and props for that person. [L52 17:32–18:32](../lessons/52-the-iteration-playbook-script.md); [L53 03:13–03:58](../lessons/53-the-iteration-playbook-formats-styles.md)
- **See also:** [Beliefs](beliefs.md) · [Labels and customer language](labels-and-customer-language.md) · [Hooks](hooks.md) (targeted vs broad) · [Market awareness](market-awareness.md) · [Unique mechanism](unique-mechanism.md) · [Positioning](positioning.md) · [Mini breakthrough memo](../frameworks/mini-breakthrough-memo-and-growth-guide.md) · [Angles library](../libraries/angles.md)

## Definitions
| Term | Course definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Avatar | A person built from a desire, plus experiences, behaviours, emotions (and demographics if needed); experiences and behaviours "tell us the beliefs and the pain points" | [L17 00:00–00:58](../lessons/17-being-aware-of-avatars.md) |
| Sub-avatar | A specific slice of the core (desire) avatar, e.g. "Night shift worker / Insomniac / Light sleeper" under "I want restorative sleep"; "Stress Eater", "Addicted to Junk Food" under appetite control | [L22 Ad Roadmap row 3](../lessons/22-how-to-finalize-your-ad-ideas.md); [L67 01:37](../lessons/67-how-to-write-image-ads-part-1.md) |
| Desire | "Market's Desire - 'I want…'"; "we can not create desires, we can only channel them" | [L22 DesiresCore Avatar tab](../lessons/22-how-to-finalize-your-ad-ideas.md) |
| Angle | "The selling strategy - how you're choosing to sell the product"; 12-year-old version: "The main reason you're giving someone to buy" | [L18 page; 00:00–00:43](../lessons/18-being-aware-of-angles.md) |
| Concept | The big idea you test; about you / your test strategy (internally focused) | [L67 angle-prompt doc](../lessons/67-how-to-write-image-ads-part-1.md) |
| Hook / headline | How the angle is communicated; "direct instead of indirect" until advanced ("Stops swelling" → "14+ Hours. No Swelling") | [L67 angle-prompt doc](../lessons/67-how-to-write-image-ads-part-1.md) |

Chain used by the Growth Guide: **Feature (what it is) → Benefit ("so you can…") → Desire → Sub-avatar → Angle(s)** (Blackout Blind: edge-sealing track → total darkness → restorative sleep → "No Screener" → "Protect Your Melatonin Production"). [L22 Sub AvatarsAngles tab](../lessons/22-how-to-finalize-your-ad-ideas.md)

## Why it matters
- **You can't write without a person and a reason**: "It's very hard to write an ad for someone if you don't actually have an angle that you're writing it around." [L18 03:00–03:42](../lessons/18-being-aware-of-angles.md) "Even if you just start with a person, you're going to have headlines that come to you." [L17 04:26](../lessons/17-being-aware-of-avatars.md)
- **Many angles in one ad kill positioning and relatability**: catering to everyone makes it "very hard to establish any kind of position… and it's hard to be relatable." [L18 03:42–04:32](../lessons/18-being-aware-of-angles.md)
- **Breakthroughs depend on two variables**: "Is this angle scalable? And is the way that I'm communicating this angle right?" [L51 00:51–01:44](../lessons/51-how-to-iterate-on-breakthroughs.md)
- **Angle problems are often execution problems**: "a lot of the times it's not an angle issue. It's an execution issue." [L48 00:39–01:27](../lessons/48-how-to-iterate-on-losing-ads.md); planned awareness/angle not matching the finished copy is how teams "throw out angles" wrongly. [L46 09:20](../lessons/46-the-10-step-iteration-process.md)

## How to use it
1. **Choose the desire** (one per concept; Spencer picks the scalable desire, "My job is to scale brands"). [L71 25:52–26:43](../lessons/71-how-to-write-video-ads-part-2.md)
2. **Research sub-avatars** with verbatim quotes: Google the behaviour + "reddit" ("I eat when I'm bored and get cravings when I'm bored reddit"). [L68 00:00–03:32](../lessons/68-how-to-write-image-ads-part-2.md)
3. **Fill the avatar card**: Desire / Experience / Emotion / Behavior, then "Why do they care? They want [desire]. But they have the belief [belief]." [L17 page template](../lessons/17-being-aware-of-avatars.md); add beliefs as a field [L15 00:34–01:13](../lessons/15-why-you-need-to-understand-beliefs.md)
4. **List angles** from product functions as reasons to buy; tag type (benefit, avoidance/health, identity, status, ease). [L18 01:26–03:00](../lessons/18-being-aware-of-angles.md)
5. **Generate angles per sub-avatar** with the angle prompt: 3 angles × 3 hooks each, ranked; treat the ranking as input only. [L67 00:54–07:18](../lessons/67-how-to-write-image-ads-part-1.md)
6. **Pick the test design**: **marksman** = several angles in one concept (one per ad variation); **sniper** = one angle, several hooks. [L67 01:37; 05:38; 12:27](../lessons/67-how-to-write-image-ads-part-1.md)
7. **Write one headline per avatar**, then re-ask "who did I write this for, and are they going to care?" [L17 04:26](../lessons/17-being-aware-of-avatars.md)
8. **Signal the avatar** in copy and visual: a label or identity call-out in the hook [L34 02:21; 06:51–07:36](../lessons/34-how-to-write-with-labels-customer-language.md); visuals that "tell Meta's algorithm… which audience to serve" [L48 12:57](../lessons/48-how-to-iterate-on-losing-ads.md); implied demographics such as "After 40" when account data supports it [L71 04:13–07:13](../lessons/71-how-to-write-video-ads-part-2.md).
9. **Log it**: desire, sub-avatar, angle(s), memo, awareness, file type, ad type. [L22 Ad Roadmap](../lessons/22-how-to-finalize-your-ad-ideas.md)

## Common mistakes
- **Demographics-only avatars.** [L17 00:00–00:58](../lessons/17-being-aware-of-avatars.md)
- **No angle** ("testing formats" with no reason to buy). [L18 page](../lessons/18-being-aware-of-angles.md)
- **Several angles stuffed into one hook**; keep secondary angles for sub-headlines/body or other ads. [L18 03:00–04:32](../lessons/18-being-aware-of-angles.md)
- **Too niche** ("Men on TRT not happy") or **too broad with weak copy** ("For men who struggle with hair loss you need this!"). [L48 06:43–10:49](../lessons/48-how-to-iterate-on-losing-ads.md)
- **Half-baked ideas** ("let's do an us versus them") with no who/what/why. [L22 01:21; 03:42–04:23](../lessons/22-how-to-finalize-your-ad-ideas.md)
- **Writing for an avatar who already knows you**: the static prompt writes for someone who "has never heard of our brand and does not care who we are". [L65 prompt](../lessons/65-banger-static-ads-prompt.md)

## Cross-course notes
- **Disagreement (unit of targeting):** Tether's [self-targeting](../../../../concepts/self-targeting.md) says target "the actual internal self of your customer… not the angle, not the customer… not avatars" and rotate selves to escape the [angle rut](../../../../concepts/angle-rut.md); Evolve builds every ad from a desire-led avatar/sub-avatar and an angle. Agent default: use Evolve's avatar → angle as the brief's spine, then tag each variant with a Tether self (Actual/Ideal/Ought) and rotate selves within the angle; keep cold ads on Actual/Ideal ([three selves](../../../../concepts/three-selves.md)). Logged in the contradictions notes.
- **Agreement:** Tether's [angles library](../../../../libraries/angles.md) rule "name the angle by the desire or pain it serves, not by the product feature" matches Evolve's "reason to buy" definition; Tether's [specificity](../../../../concepts/heuristics/specificity.md) ("one hyper-specific situation… generic call-outs let Meta find the cheapest, not the right, buyer") matches Evolve's audience-signalling rule [L48 12:57](../lessons/48-how-to-iterate-on-losing-ads.md).
- **Agreement:** Tether's [customer research stack](../../../../frameworks/customer-research-stack.md) also verifies customer types on Reddit rather than inventing them with ChatGPT; Evolve's sub-avatar research uses Reddit quotes.
