# Show, don't tell
> If a claim can be demonstrated on screen, demonstrate it; if it can't, write the behaviour, experience or fact that implies it, so the prospect "tells it to themselves". It is "very hard to deny things that you can see with your own eyes."

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** turning claim lines ("easy to clean", "fits better", "boosts confidence") into shots and behaviour lines for a UGC script, creator brief or static; editing AI drafts that list benefits; tearing down which claim a competitor ad demonstrates instead of states.
- **Inputs → outputs:** the product's claims (the "tell" lines) + what can be filmed → for each claim either (a) a demonstration shot with minimal words, or (b) a concrete behaviour/fact line, plus a visual instruction for every voiceover line.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** ask of every claim "how can we show that?" and show it if you can. "When in doubt, show it out." [L31 06:10–08:14](../lessons/31-how-to-create-ads-that-show-dont-tell.md)
  - **DO** let the visual carry the claim; "you do not need to over explain when the visual can say it all" (the "Mona Lisa frame"). [L31 00:47–01:33](../lessons/31-how-to-create-ads-that-show-dont-tell.md)
  - **DO** prefer a contrast demonstration: the bad alternative, then ours (Carepod scrubbed vs boiled; drooping vs fitted tank top; dye test). [L31 02:11–04:45](../lessons/31-how-to-create-ads-that-show-dont-tell.md)
  - **DO** keep a few words of telling beside the show: "Yes, we are doing some telling, but most importantly, we are showing." [L31 02:11–03:04](../lessons/31-how-to-create-ads-that-show-dont-tell.md)
  - **IF** the benefit can't be filmed **THEN** "write the experience, write the behavior, write the new desire" ("This will have you running through a wall", not "feel energized"). [L32 00:00–01:45](../lessons/32-how-to-write-ads-that-show-and-dont-tell.md)
  - **DO** state the fact that implies the benefit ("made with titanium" → people assume high quality). [L32 01:45–02:35](../lessons/32-how-to-write-ads-that-show-and-dont-tell.md)
  - **IF** the benefit is invisible (gut, hormones) **THEN** stage a visible metaphor (Goli pour test) or personify the body part (Ryze AI animation). [L31 04:45–05:18](../lessons/31-how-to-create-ads-that-show-dont-tell.md); [L32 04:31–05:50](../lessons/32-how-to-write-ads-that-show-and-dont-tell.md)
  - **DO** delete explanations and give an example: "Every time you explain something, just delete it and give an example. The example will explain more than you can." [L41 06:40–07:32](../lessons/41-the-laws-of-story-telling.md)
  - **DO** use "half the words, double the examples" (James Clear, via Harry Dry) for copy-only ads. [L31 05:18–06:10](../lessons/31-how-to-create-ads-that-show-dont-tell.md)
  - **DO** give every voiceover line a visual instruction that shows behaviour change, not "just product shots or abstract concepts". [L72 docx Core Rule 7](../lessons/72-action-item-write-your-video-ad-briefs.md)
  - **DON'T** copy a demonstration a competitor already owns 1:1 (Goli "has already kind of got this"); find your own way to show your claim. [L31 04:45–06:10](../lessons/31-how-to-create-ads-that-show-dont-tell.md)
  - **IF** an outrageous outcome line makes the ad memorable **THEN** defuse it ("That would be an exaggeration, obviously") and keep the vivid scene (Dr. Squatch). [L32 03:33–04:31](../lessons/32-how-to-write-ads-that-show-and-dont-tell.md)
  - **DON'T** present a persuasive demo as proof it isn't: Spencer doubts the toothpaste dye test proves whitening. Keep demonstrations honest. [L31 03:04](../lessons/31-how-to-create-ads-that-show-dont-tell.md)
- **See also:** [three-copy-checks](three-copy-checks.md) · [facts-over-opinions](facts-over-opinions.md) · [comparisons](comparisons.md) · [compression-and-plain-language](compression-and-plain-language.md) · [raising-the-stakes](raising-the-stakes.md) · [unaware-ads](unaware-ads.md) · [word-lists library](../libraries/word-lists.md) (visual words) · [prompts library](../libraries/prompts.md) · [rewrites library](../libraries/rewrites.md) · Tether Lab: [sight smell](../../../../concepts/sight-smell.md), [skeptic format](../../../../frameworks/skeptic-format.md)

## Definition
Two related moves taught in back-to-back lessons:
1. **Show visually (L31):** replace a claim with a 1–3 second action that makes it self-evident. "This module is not as much on copywriting"; it is there to show that some things don't need to be written. [L31 00:00–00:47](../lessons/31-how-to-create-ads-that-show-dont-tell.md)
2. **Show with words (L32):** when you can't film it, stop writing abstract benefits and write concrete experiences, behaviours, desires and facts. Telling "triggers the logical brain (skepticism)"; showing lets the prospect "make the assumption". "Let your prospects tell it to themselves, not you." [L32 00:00–02:35](../lessons/32-how-to-write-ads-that-show-and-dont-tell.md)

Harry Dry's "write more concrete" and "write with the delete key" are the credited sources (page credits: Harry Dry, Kyle Milligan). [L31](../lessons/31-how-to-create-ads-that-show-dont-tell.md); [L32](../lessons/32-how-to-write-ads-that-show-and-dont-tell.md)

## Why it matters
- **Disarms objections:** "When you show rather than tell, you disarm objections, you build belief and you are essentially just letting your product do the selling for you." [L31 03:04–03:58](../lessons/31-how-to-create-ads-that-show-dont-tell.md)
- **Bypasses expected claims:** people "are always going to assume that you're going to tell them that your thing is better, faster, bigger and easier", so showing stands out and "triggers more of the emotional brain rather than the logical brain". [L32 05:50–06:16](../lessons/32-how-to-write-ads-that-show-and-dont-tell.md)
- **Lets the viewer categorize:** with the Ryze personification, "Objections become slightly less" because you are "forcing the person to categorize what this means rather than you categorizing it for them". [L32 05:01–05:50](../lessons/32-how-to-write-ads-that-show-and-dont-tell.md)
- **It is the first copy check:** "Can you visualize it?" If not, "the user won't clearly understand your message because they can't see it." [L08 01:29–05:34](../lessons/08-the-3-checks-for-great-copywriting.md)
- **It is how UGC is spoken:** "'this is 40 millimeters thin' versus 'let me just show you how thin it is'. It's a much more powerful way." [L12 02:38–03:24](../lessons/12-why-you-need-to-speak-copy-not-write-it.md)

## How to use it (steps)
1. **List every claim** in the draft as a "tell" sentence; underline benefit words (energized, confident, quality, easy, better). [L31 steps](../lessons/31-how-to-create-ads-that-show-dont-tell.md); [L32 steps](../lessons/32-how-to-write-ads-that-show-and-dont-tell.md)
2. **Route each claim** with the decision table below.
3. **Cut the words** to a caption or nothing where the shot carries it; check the video makes sense on mute. [L31 checklist](../lessons/31-how-to-create-ads-that-show-dont-tell.md)
4. **Run the visual-words prompt** (abstract verbs, nouns, adjectives, adverbs, clichés) and rewrite each flag "from ABSTRACT to concrete" ("productive routines" → "waking up at 6 am to write"). [L59 page](../lessons/59-visual-words.md)
5. **Write the shot into the brief** next to each voiceover line so the creator/editor films the demonstration. [L72 docx Core Rule 7](../lessons/72-action-item-write-your-video-ad-briefs.md); [L31 checklist](../lessons/31-how-to-create-ads-that-show-dont-tell.md)
6. **Check ownership:** if a competitor already runs the same demonstration, change the demonstration, not only the product. [L31 04:45–06:10](../lessons/31-how-to-create-ads-that-show-dont-tell.md)
7. **Check honesty:** the demonstration must correspond to the real mechanism and claims you can substantiate. [L31 03:04](../lessons/31-how-to-create-ads-that-show-dont-tell.md); [L72 docx Compliance](../lessons/72-action-item-write-your-video-ad-briefs.md)

|---|---|---|---|

| Easy to clean / maintain | Bad alternative, then ours | "Not Carepod" toothbrush scrub → Carepod tank on the stove | [L31 02:11–03:04](../lessons/31-how-to-create-ads-that-show-dont-tell.md) |
| Better than competitors | Side-by-side test | Toothpaste dye test; insoles egg drop vs Nike (inspired by Purple's egg test) | [L31 03:04–03:58](../lessons/31-how-to-create-ads-that-show-dont-tell.md); [L35 16:38–18:08](../lessons/35-how-to-write-unaware-ads-hooks.md) |
| Fit / look | Before/after on the body, labelled bad vs good | Haus tank top "ASS" / "GAS" | [L31 03:58–04:45](../lessons/31-how-to-create-ads-that-show-dont-tell.md) |
| Emotional result (calm, relief) | The result happening, no claim | Warmies: crying baby calms; words only "lavender scented" | [L31 01:33–02:11](../lessons/31-how-to-create-ads-that-show-dont-tell.md) |
| Invisible harm or benefit | Staged visual metaphor | Goli: junk food poured into a glass labelled "YOUR GUT" | [L31 04:45–06:10](../lessons/31-how-to-create-ads-that-show-dont-tell.md) |

| Security / risk | Footage of the threat | Pen popping a suitcase zipper instead of "zippers aren't secure enough" | [L41 06:40–07:32](../lessons/41-the-laws-of-story-telling.md) |
| Trustworthiness / transparency | Founder demonstrates in the warehouse | Strolee Carts: "this won't tip over regardless how much weight" | [L16 34:35–36:10](../lessons/16-why-you-need-to-understand-authority.md) |
| Can't be filmed at all | Behaviour, experience or fact line | "You'll forget you were ever nervous to go out"; "made with titanium" | [L32 01:45–02:35](../lessons/32-how-to-write-ads-that-show-and-dont-tell.md) |

## Common mistakes
- **"I can't show it."** Spencer calls it a limiting belief; creative advertisers find a way (Goli). [L32 00:00–00:53](../lessons/32-how-to-write-ads-that-show-and-dont-tell.md)
- **Benefit lists in the hold** ("more energy, better focus"). [L72 docx Core Rule 3](../lessons/72-action-item-write-your-video-ad-briefs.md)
- **Over-captioning a visual** that already says it (Mona Lisa frame). [L31 00:47–01:33](../lessons/31-how-to-create-ads-that-show-dont-tell.md)
- **Explaining instead of giving an example** ("if I didn't have these examples ... it just doesn't land the same"). [L41 06:40–07:32](../lessons/41-the-laws-of-story-telling.md)
- **Copying a demo a competitor owns.** [L31 04:45–06:10](../lessons/31-how-to-create-ads-that-show-dont-tell.md)
- **Demo that implies more than it proves** (dye test). [L31 03:04](../lessons/31-how-to-create-ads-that-show-dont-tell.md)
- **Shocking stakes shown but not explained** (wallet scanner shown, never said what it does). [L52 16:05–16:51](../lessons/52-the-iteration-playbook-script.md)

## Cross-course notes
- **Agreement (Tether Lab).** [Sight smell](../../../../concepts/sight-smell.md): "If you show me beef tallow caramel and bacon in an image, I'm gonna know automatically that that's a very unique flavor. You don't even need to say it." Same principle as Evolve's Mona Lisa frame. Tether's [skeptic format](../../../../frameworks/skeptic-format.md) (a creator tests the claim on camera) is a ready-made UGC container for Evolve's contrast demonstrations. Tether's [specificity](../../../../concepts/heuristics/specificity.md) matches "made with titanium".
- **Personification.** Evolve's Ryze example [L32](../lessons/32-how-to-write-ads-that-show-and-dont-tell.md) and Tether Lab's [anthropomorphism](../../../../concepts/heuristics/anthropomorphism.md) agree that personifying things makes the invisible visible; Tether adds: keep the zone/intensity that already works and keep it on-brand.
- **Within Evolve, a tension on animated mechanism visuals.** The video prompt labels "Animated enzymes and hormones with scientific diagrams" as TELLING and asks for behaviour footage instead [L72 docx Core Rule 3](../lessons/72-action-item-write-your-video-ad-briefs.md), while L32 praises AI-animated organs (Ryze) as "showing visually" and the model's own L70 draft used generated animation for the mechanism [L70 17:19](../lessons/70-how-to-write-video-ads-part-1.md). Agent default (derived): animation that shows a character or a behaviour counts as showing; a diagram explaining the mechanism is telling, so pair it with a behaviour shot. Logged in the contradictions notes.
