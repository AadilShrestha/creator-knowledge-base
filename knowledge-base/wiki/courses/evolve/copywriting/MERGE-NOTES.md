## How the two courses divide the work
| Job | Tether Lab (Sarah Levinger) | Evolve Copywriting (Spencer Pawliw) |
|---|---|---|
| Why an ad works | Heuristics, valence × intensity zones, three selves, TEEP, Fogg | Awareness level, belief, mechanism, authority, positioning |
| Writing the words | Rewrites by verb/visual/promise/self | Three checks, facts, plain spoken language, open loops, bridges, stakes, show don't tell, labels |
| Story | 15 story frameworks (doc) | 5 building blocks, ABC enemies, 9 plots (Booker + 2) |
| Video scripts | Derived from statics + story frameworks | Full video-script process, mega prompt, modular hooks/bridges, brief card |
| Statics | Visual weight, layout psychology | Static workflow with angle prompt and a 29k-character static-ads prompt |
| Testing | Creative testing system, TEEP account structure, one variable per test | 4 outcomes, 10-step learnings, issue codes, 22 named iterations |

## Proposed for a later shared synthesis (not done yet)
- A shared `concepts/market-awareness.md` combining Evolve's awareness levels with Tether's TEEP stages (they answer different questions: what the viewer knows vs where they are in the buying journey).
- A shared hooks page that merges Evolve's hook anatomy (sticky, targeted/broad, promise/open loop) with Tether's heuristic tagging of hooks.
- A shared iteration page that reconciles Evolve's outcome-based iteration with Tether's one-variable testing rules.
- Brand case files in `wiki/brands/` for brands that both courses discuss (e.g. Liquid Death, Grüns, Comfrt). Evolve examples currently live in the [course ad-teardowns library](libraries/ad-teardowns.md).
