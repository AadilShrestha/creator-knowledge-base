# Angles library (Evolve Copywriting)
> Every angle in the Evolve Copywriting course, written the way Spencer Pawliw defines an angle (a plain reason someone should buy), grouped by the core desire or pain it serves, with the mechanism and authority that make it believable and the lessons that teach it.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** naming the angle behind a competitor ad; choosing angles for a new batch or a creator brief; spinning one winning angle into new ones (other desire, sub-avatar or awareness level); checking that a concept has a defined angle at all.
- **Inputs → outputs:** product facts + avatar / sub-avatar (desire, experiences, behaviours, beliefs, quotes) → the matching desire family below → 3 angles per sub-avatar, each with a mechanism and an authority → one angle per ad, main angle in the hook.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** write every angle as a plain reason to buy: "Your child will enjoy...", "You won't be...", "It will be easier for you to...". [L18 00:00-03:42](../lessons/18-being-aware-of-angles.md)
  - **DO** define the angle even when the concept is a format or sub-avatar test: "you still need to define why someone is going to buy from you". [L18 page text](../lessons/18-being-aware-of-angles.md) [L67 Angle-prompt doc, Knowledge](../lessons/67-how-to-write-image-ads-part-1.md)
  - **DO** attach a mechanism to every angle ("What is the mechanism that can prove it?") and an authority to every mechanism ("what authority do you have?"). [L19 02:15-04:23](../lessons/19-being-aware-of-mechanisms.md) [L20 00:34-01:18](../lessons/20-being-aware-of-authorities.md)
  - **DON'T** pack several angles into one ad; keep the main angle in the hook and push secondary ones into sub-headlines or body. [L18 03:00-04:32](../lessons/18-being-aware-of-angles.md)
  - **DO** let the avatar shape the angle: the same product needs a different angle and headline for a beginner and a pro. [L17 02:57-04:26](../lessons/17-being-aware-of-avatars.md) [L18 00:43-01:26](../lessons/18-being-aware-of-angles.md)
  - **DO** remember "we can not create desires, we can only channel them". [L22 DesiresCore Avatar A2](../lessons/22-how-to-finalize-your-ad-ideas.md)
  - **IF** a good angle with a good claim isn't converting **THEN** suspect a violated or missing belief before changing the angle. [L15 01:13](../lessons/15-why-you-need-to-understand-beliefs.md) [L15 14:03](../lessons/15-why-you-need-to-understand-beliefs.md)
  - **IF** a breakthrough angle has a small TAM **THEN** keep it in its own campaign and scale the bigger-TAM angle. [L48 06:43-08:31](../lessons/48-how-to-iterate-on-losing-ads.md) [L51 11:22-12:19](../lessons/51-how-to-iterate-on-breakthroughs.md)
- **See also:** [Avatars and angles](../concepts/avatars-and-angles.md) · [Unique mechanism](../concepts/unique-mechanism.md) · [Authority in ads](../concepts/authority-in-ads.md) · [Market awareness](../concepts/market-awareness.md) · [Positioning exercise](../frameworks/positioning-exercise.md) · [Hooks](hooks.md) · [Prompts](prompts.md) (angle prompt) · Tether Lab's [angles library](../../../../libraries/angles.md) (zone- and self-tagged)

## How to read the tables
- **Angle:** the course's name for it (or the lesson page's when Spencer gives none).
- **Core desire / pain · Heuristic(s) · Best format · Example line:** as recorded on the lesson pages; "(derived)" = the page's inference. Heuristic labels here are Evolve's (authority type, awareness level, belief move), not Tether Lab's heuristic catalogue; "—" means none named.
- **Example line:** often a belief statement or an angle name, not a tested ad line; the cell says so when it matters.
