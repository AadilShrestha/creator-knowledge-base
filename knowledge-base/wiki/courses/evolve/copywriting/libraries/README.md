# Libraries hub (Evolve Copywriting)
> The fast-lookup layer for Spencer Pawliw's Evolve Copywriting course: every hook, template, rewrite, ad teardown, angle, rule, prompt and word list from the 72 lessons, de-duplicated, grouped for agent lookup and linked back to the lesson and timestamp it came from.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** you need a precedent fast ("how did Spencer judge a dental-powder spend winner?"), the rules for one part of an ad ("what goes in a bridge?"), a slot structure to fill, a minimal fix for a weak line, or the exact prompt for an LLM step.
- **Inputs → outputs:** the job you're on (table below) → the right library → filtered rows (by section, niche, lever, issue code) → a **mechanism** to remix, a fix to apply, a template to fill or a prompt to paste → our own line, checked against [rules §12](rules.md#12-ethics-and-compliance) and the [copy checklist](../rubrics/copy-checklist-scorecard.md).
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** start full workflows from the course playbooks ([video script](../playbooks/write-a-video-ad-script.md), [statics](../playbooks/write-static-image-ads.md), [iterate on results](../playbooks/iterate-on-test-results.md), [idea to brief](../playbooks/ad-idea-to-brief.md)); come here for lookups inside a step.
  - **DO** copy the mechanism, not the asset (see the warning below).
  - **DO** open the source link and check the timestamp before quoting Spencer or citing a result.
  - **DO** treat [rules X1–X11](rules.md#12-ethics-and-compliance) as hard constraints: no invented proof, stats, testimonials, review counts, endorsements or scarcity.
  - **DON'T** treat *prompt-output*, *hypothetical*, *derived*, "NOT A REAL AD" or instructor claims ("crushing", "3X'd the account", view counts) as evidence that something works.
- **See also:** [Course README](../README.md) · [Course index](../index.md) · [Contradictions](../contradictions.md) · Tether Lab's psychology-tagged companion set: [shared libraries hub](../../../../libraries/README.md)

## ⚠️ Copy the mechanism, not the asset
Every library row is a **precedent**, not a line to reuse. Spencer is "obviously not the biggest fan of templates" and doesn't "believe in copy and pasting"; his aim is to help you "take these specific hooks... and actually bring them over into your niche". [L57 00:00-00:54](../lessons/57-100-hook-templates-form-winning-ads-deck.md) When a competitor already owns a visual (Goli's pour test), "find your own" rather than copying 1:1. [L31 04:45-06:10](../lessons/31-how-to-create-ads-that-show-dont-tell.md)

Lesson 65 shows the risk in practice: the Banger Static Ads prompt makes Claude cite a reference ad for each headline, and several demo outputs follow their reference almost line for line ("Zero stars. Would not recommend. - Mother Nature" ← Bible App; "This is jewelry. This is survival gear." ← Nutella; "We're Bear Grylls' favorite necklace*" ← Surreal). Keep the twist (contrast, enemy's review, fine-print punchline), change the words, the picture and the proof. See [hooks §R](hooks.md#r-prompt-output-headlines-from-the-banger-static-ads-demo-lesson-65) and [rules SA17](rules.md#8-static-ads). Lesson 68's "write it EXACTLY like the ad itself" instruction is the same tension: fill the **structure** with our facts and customer words; don't keep the reference's distinctive wording. [L68 frames 19:47-20:07](../lessons/68-how-to-write-image-ads-part-2.md)

Remix recipe *(derived from the course)*: name the competitor ad's mechanism in course terms (hook type, angle, mechanism, authority, awareness, valence / intensity, stakes lever) → find 2–3 analogous rows → write our version with our avatar's words, our mechanism and our real proof → run the [3 checks](../concepts/three-copy-checks.md) (visual, falsifiable, nobody else) and the Google exact-match test. [L08 00:41-01:29](../lessons/08-the-3-checks-for-great-copywriting.md) [L58 page Q9](../lessons/58-copywriting-checklist-when-writing.md)

## Conventions
- **IDs:** hooks A01–R09; templates T001–T209; rewrites W001–W179; teardowns TD001–TD186; angles C01–P12 (recipes A1–A8); rules RS / WC / H / B / BD / PC / SY / SA / V / IT / AI / X; prompts P01–P26. Cite IDs in briefs and reviews.
- **Citations:** `[L26 03:15](../lessons/26-how-to-write-open-loops.md)` = lesson 26 at 03:15. Non-video sources: "page", "page text", "slide", "frame", "sheet row" (lesson 57 hook sheet), "Angle doc" (lesson 67 angle-prompt document), "docx" / "PDF" (lesson attachments).
- **Provenance tags:** "(derived)" = the lesson page's inference; *hypothetical*, *prompt-output*, *not used*, *untested*, "NOT A REAL AD"; instructor claims are labelled as such.
- **Relationship to Tether Lab:** these libraries hold Evolve's teaching only. The shared [Tether Lab libraries](../../../../libraries/README.md) are the psychology-tagged companion set (heuristics, emotional zones, three selves, failure patterns F1–F15). Where the two creators disagree, the course [contradictions page](../contradictions.md) states both positions.

## Coverage and gaps
- **Sources:** all 72 lesson pages were harvested; every library row keeps all its lesson citations after merging. Rules cite all 72 lessons.
- **Unavailable in the course files:** the full 100-hook sheet (lesson 57, Google login); the "2026 Iteration Playbook" doc behind the issue codes; Spencer's unexported prompts (Desires, New Information, New Mechanism, Ads Call Analysis; lesson 37); client ads and data under NDA (lesson 54).
- **Evidence limits:** outcomes are rarely shown. Most "winners" are labelled by Spencer from limited or NDA-restricted data ("I'm relatively confident"). [L54 02:09-03:02](../lessons/54-iteration-examples-breakdowns-walkthrough.md)
