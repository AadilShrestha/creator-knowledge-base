# Templates library (Evolve Copywriting)
> Every fill-in structure in the Evolve Copywriting course, grouped by the job it does in an ad: hook, bridge, pitch transition, stakes, claim and authority lines, objections and price, show-don't-tell, story, angle and positioning, briefs, and editing checks. Each row keeps the course's own example fill and the lesson it came from.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** you know which part of the ad you are writing (hook, bridge, hold, pitch, CTA, static headline, brief) and want a proven slot structure to fill with our avatar, mechanism and proof; or you want to name the structure a competitor used.
- **Inputs → outputs:** the job + our facts list, objections list, customer quotes → the matching section → 2–4 candidate templates → lines filled with **our** facts and customer words → checked with the [copy checklist](../rubrics/copy-checklist-scorecard.md).
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** reduce any borrowed hook to slots first ("use case, product category, made for specific avatar"; "can't + taboo problem"), then fill with our avatar and proof. [L57 00:54-01:39](../lessons/57-100-hook-templates-form-winning-ads-deck.md)
  - **DON'T** lift the example fill. Spencer's goal "is just to help you guys think about how you can take these specific hooks ... and actually bring them over into your niche". [L57 00:00](../lessons/57-100-hook-templates-form-winning-ads-deck.md)
  - **DON'T** fill a number, sell-out count, review count or "X people switched" slot with anything but a real, current figure. Spencer's "10,000" is a placeholder. [L29 10:49](../lessons/29-how-to-pitch-without-rejection.md) [L28 08:28-09:03](../lessons/28-how-to-prevent-handle-objections-in-writing.md)
  - **DO** write the filled line inside the final layout (hook card or static template), not in a doc. [L05 00:54-01:53](../lessons/05-why-you-need-to-write-in-the-environment.md) [L05 11:49-12:40](../lessons/05-why-you-need-to-write-in-the-environment.md)
  - **IF** you can't fill a template well **THEN** the gap is upstream (avatar, mechanism, belief, awareness), not the template: "you'll learn how to actually fill these in properly once you go through the entire copywriting program". [L57 01:39](../lessons/57-100-hook-templates-form-winning-ads-deck.md)
