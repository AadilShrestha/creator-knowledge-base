# Evolve Copywriting: Spencer Pawliw
> A 72-lesson DTC ad copywriting program (about 14.7 hours of video plus prompts, templates and swipe files) from the Evolve community. It covers how to write hooks, bridges, body copy, stories, static ads and video-ad scripts, and how to **iterate on ads after testing**. It is the **copy-craft and iteration** layer of this knowledge base; [Tether Lab](../../tether-lab/README.md) is the psychology layer.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** writing or fixing any ad copy (hook, bridge, body, pitch, CTA, static headline), writing a UGC/video script or a static brief, finding a new angle or mechanism for a product, or deciding what to change after a test.
- **Inputs → outputs:** product truth doc, customer research, competitor or own winning ads, test results → hooks, scripts, static briefs, iteration plans that pass the [copy checklist scorecard](rubrics/copy-checklist-scorecard.md).
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** start a new ad from the idea chain: avatar → angle → mechanism → authority → awareness level ([avatars and angles](concepts/avatars-and-angles.md), [unique mechanism](concepts/unique-mechanism.md), [authority](concepts/authority-in-ads.md), [market awareness](concepts/market-awareness.md)), and write the [mini breakthrough memo](frameworks/mini-breakthrough-memo-and-growth-guide.md) before writing copy.
  - **DO** run every line through the [three copy checks](concepts/three-copy-checks.md): can you visualise it, can you falsify it, could nobody else say it? [L08](lessons/08-the-3-checks-for-great-copywriting.md)
  - **DO** name the test outcome (loser / KPI winner / spend winner / breakthrough) before iterating, then follow the [10-step learnings](frameworks/ten-step-iteration-learnings.md) and the [iteration playbook](frameworks/iteration-playbook.md). [L45](lessons/45-overview-of-iteration-section.md)
  - **DO** treat AI output as a 70–80% draft and check every claim and number against the product document. [L70](lessons/70-how-to-write-video-ads-part-1.md), [L67](lessons/67-how-to-write-image-ads-part-1.md)
  - **DON'T** reuse a reference ad's wording near-verbatim; take its mechanism (structure, angle, hook move) and write new lines from our product's facts. See the caution on [L65](lessons/65-banger-static-ads-prompt.md).
  - **DON'T** invent proof, stats or authority. Borrowed authority says "we noticed…", never "X endorses us". [L16](lessons/16-why-you-need-to-understand-authority.md)
- **See also:** [playbooks](playbooks/README.md) · [libraries](libraries/README.md) · [course index](index.md) · [AGENT-GUIDE](../../../../AGENT-GUIDE.md)

## The Evolve model in one table
| Layer | Question for any ad | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Idea | Who is it for, what reason to buy, what mechanism proves it, what authority backs it? | [avatars and angles](concepts/avatars-and-angles.md), [mechanism](concepts/unique-mechanism.md), [authority](concepts/authority-in-ads.md) |
| Awareness | Which level is the viewer at, and does the ad walk them up? | [market awareness](concepts/market-awareness.md), [unaware ads](concepts/unaware-ads.md) |
| Hook | Does it stop *and* stick: emotion, gap, stakes, no backstory, works muted? | [hooks](concepts/hooks.md), [open loops](concepts/open-loops.md) |
| Bridge | Does the second beat keep them (mechanism, escalation, reposition, new info, timeline)? | [bridges](concepts/bridges.md) |
| Body | Visual, falsifiable, ownable, factual, spoken, grade 6, objections handled? | [three copy checks](concepts/three-copy-checks.md), [objections](concepts/objection-handling.md) |
| Story | Change, withheld info, stakes, but/so tension, cause and effect, an enemy? | [story building blocks](concepts/story-building-blocks.md), [enemies](concepts/strategic-enemies.md) |
| Pitch | Payoff delivered and belief built before the product appears? | [pitch transition](concepts/pitch-transition.md) |
| Position | Different, not better: what do we stand for and against? | [positioning](concepts/positioning.md) |
| Iterate | Which outcome, which component failed, which iteration next? | [iteration outcomes](concepts/iteration-outcomes.md), [iteration playbook](frameworks/iteration-playbook.md) |
