# Evolve Copywriting rubrics hub
> The course's quality gate is one scorecard built on Spencer Pawliw's 13-question checklist, the three copy checks and the video prompt's compliance checks. It judges **copy craft and product truth**; pair it with Tether Lab's shared rubrics, which judge **psychology** (zone, self, heuristics, visual hierarchy).

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** any Evolve-course playbook reaches its quality gate, or you need to score a competitor ad's copy.
- **Inputs → outputs:** asset + product truth sheet + sub-avatar card → filled scorecard (⛔ gates, % score, band, top fixes).
- **Rules:** run ⛔ gates first; quote evidence for every score; fix inputs before words; live results beat rubric scores ("let the data tell you what converts", [L03 01:01](../lessons/03-how-to-guarantee-you-get-better-at-copywriting.md)).
- **See also:** [Course playbooks](../playbooks/README.md) · Tether: [rubrics hub](../../../../rubrics/README.md)

| Rubric | Applies to | Output | Pass | Used in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Copy checklist scorecard](copy-checklist-scorecard.md) | any hook, static, video script or brief; ours or a competitor's | `copy_scorecard` (6 ⛔ gates, 24 criteria, 53 points) | no ⛔ fail; ≥ 80% | [write a video ad script](../playbooks/write-a-video-ad-script.md), [write static image ads](../playbooks/write-static-image-ads.md), [ad idea to brief](../playbooks/ad-idea-to-brief.md), [iterate on test results](../playbooks/iterate-on-test-results.md) |

## Use with the shared (Tether Lab) rubrics
| Order | Rubric | Course | Judges |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ⛔ gates of the [copy checklist scorecard](copy-checklist-scorecard.md) | Evolve | product truth, no fabrication, no fake urgency, copy the mechanism not the asset, honest first person |
| 2 | [Copy checklist scorecard](copy-checklist-scorecard.md) criteria | Evolve | who/why, hook, mechanism, authority, three checks, Big 4, stakes, urgency, show-don't-tell, objections, plain spoken language |
| 3 | [Ad scorecard](../../../../rubrics/ad-scorecard.md) | Tether Lab | hook frame 1, zone fit, self fit, heuristics, specificity, proof integrity, CTA, visual hierarchy or story structure, TEEP fit (≥80, no auto-fail) |
| 4 | [UGC script checklist](../../../../rubrics/ugc-script-checklist.md) (UGC scripts only) | Tether Lab | one core emotion, frame-1 message, setup share, realism beat, test packaging |

Disagreements between the two courses' gates (social-proof wording, test packaging, setup share) are listed in the scorecard's cross-course notes and the contradiction log; the playbooks state the default.
