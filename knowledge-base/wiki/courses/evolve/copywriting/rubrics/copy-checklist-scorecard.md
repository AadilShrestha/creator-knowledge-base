# Copy checklist scorecard
> Spencer Pawliw's 13-question "Copywriting Checklist (when writing)" turned into a scored rubric: each criterion has 0–2 or 0–3 anchors taken from the lessons, plus the three copy checks, the Big 4 value words, stakes, urgency, show-don't-tell, objections and pitch timing. Six ⛔ truth and originality gates block shipping regardless of score.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** QA on any hook, static, script or brief before it goes to a designer, editor or creator; post-mortem on a loser (lowest criteria = first fixes); scoring a competitor ad to see what carries it (score what is visible; never award truth points for their claims).
- **Inputs → outputs:** the draft (or competitor ad) + sub-avatar card + product truth sheet / product document + reference ad (if any) → filled scoring block: ⛔ gates, per-criterion score with one line of quoted evidence, % score, band, top 3 fixes in the order Inputs → Checks → Polish.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** run the ⛔ gates first; one fail = **block**, whatever the score.
  - **DO** quote the line (or describe the frame) behind every score; "n.a." needs a reason.
  - **DO** remember the instructor's intent: "if I'm missing a lot of these things, it's probably not a good script. If I have a lot of these things, it's probably a pretty good script", and "It doesn't have to go through each 13 of these things… it's just to kind of check yourself." [L01 03:37](../lessons/01-overview-of-copywriting-program.md) The weights and bands below are pipeline design, not the course's.
  - **DO** fix inputs before words: if Q1–Q6 fail, go back to research before editing lines ([L58 steps, derived](../lessons/58-copywriting-checklist-when-writing.md)).
  - **IF** all three copy checks score 0 **THEN** rewrite from scratch: "If you have three no's, you probably wrote something that is not that great." [L08 00:41–01:29](../lessons/08-the-3-checks-for-great-copywriting.md)
  - **DON'T** let the score replace test results: "Your job as a copywriter is to test, measure, and let the data tell you what converts." [L03 01:01](../lessons/03-how-to-guarantee-you-get-better-at-copywriting.md)

## The source checklist (verbatim, L58 page)
```text
1. Who am I writing this for? (Sub avatar + beliefs)
2. Why am I writing it for them?
3. Am I starting broad or targeted?
4. Does my hook have a gap, trigger emotion, and have high stakes?
5. What is the mechanism?
6. What is the authority?
7. Is the copy visual? (Proofing your writing with AI module)
8. Is the copy falsifiable (can somebody say it's true/false?)
9. Can nobody else write this? (Search your headline on Google with " ")
10. What is this copy actually communicating?
11. Is this writing at a 6th grade reading level?
12. Can this be condensed further without losing meaning?
13. Would you say this door-to-door? (spoken not written)
```
(Lesson 01's on-screen version words Q8 as "can somebody say it's not true" [L01 03:00](../lessons/01-overview-of-copywriting-program.md).)

## ⛔ Blocking gates (any "no" = block)
| Gate | Pass condition | Course basis |

| ⛔ G1 Product truth | Every claim, number and ingredient matches the product document / study, and numbers are identical across variations | 68% vs 56% GLP-1 mismatch shipped on one static ("this is meant to be 56%. | ⛔ G2 Terms and mechanism accurate | Scientific words used correctly; only benefits the product supports | "these aren't hormones"; "do not call out hormones that our product does not support" → "2 hormones & 1 enzyme" [L68 30:59–34:44](../lessons/68-how-to-write-image-ads-part-2.md); DPP-4 is an enzyme, not a hormone [L71 03:40](../lessons/71-how-to-write-video-ads-part-2.md) |
| ⛔ G3 No fabricated proof | No invented reviews, counts, testimonials, endorsements or results; social-proof tier matches real volume; borrowed proof attributed | "Are you fabricating social proof numbers? We know you didn't see it" [L53 08:51–09:37](../lessons/53-the-iteration-playbook-formats-styles.md); AI voiceover kept third person ("Scientists call it DPP-4") [L71 16:01](../lessons/71-how-to-write-video-ads-part-2.md) |

### E. Format packaging checks (yes / no, not scored)
- **Video (Evolve prompt):** 3 hooks, 3 bridges (one per hook), 1 universal hold, 1 universal CTA, hold and CTA identical, 30–45 s, Problem- or Product-Aware [L72 Modular Output Check](../lessons/72-action-item-write-your-video-ad-briefs.md); every line has a visual instruction with text overlay.
- **Static:** headline + sub-line + bullets fit the template's layout; bullet count as briefed; imagery congruent with the headline (nice-to-have) [L65 Final Rule 2](../lessons/65-banger-static-ads-prompt.md); [L68 44:46](../lessons/68-how-to-write-image-ads-part-2.md); reviewed after a break [L68 39:59–44:46](../lessons/68-how-to-write-image-ads-part-2.md).

## Scoring
- Max = A (17) + B (7) + C (8) + D (21) = **53**; subtract n.a. criteria from the max. Score % = points ÷ applicable max.
- Bands (pipeline design): **≥ 80%** ship to production · **65–79%** fix the lowest criteria, re-score · **< 65%** rewrite (start at section A) · **any ⛔** block · **B1 + B2 + B3 = 0** rewrite from scratch.
- Fix order: A (research/inputs) → B (three checks) → D (craft) → C (polish), mirroring the checklist's "inputs first, polish last" ([L58 steps, derived](../lessons/58-copywriting-checklist-when-writing.md)).

## Cross-course notes (how to combine with Tether Lab)
- **Run order (derived):** Evolve ⛔ gates → this scorecard (copy craft) → Tether [ad scorecard](../../../../rubrics/ad-scorecard.md) (psychology: zone fit, self fit, heuristics, visual hierarchy, TEEP; ≥80, no auto-fail) → for UGC scripts, Tether [UGC script checklist](../../../../rubrics/ugc-script-checklist.md).
- **Overlap:** Tether's specificity, proof integrity and CTA criteria overlap B2, A5/G3 and D9. Where both score the same thing, keep the stricter result.
- **Disagreements (logged in the contradiction notes):** Tether requires exact, typed numbers and treats "thousands love it" as weak proof; Evolve's prompt allows "thousands" when the real count is 1,000–10,000. Tether wants one core emotion; Evolve asks for 2–3 Big 4 values (different constructs; both can pass). Tether's checklist expects 3 hooks × 2 bodies and ~2/3 setup; Evolve's packaging expects 3 hook/bridge pairs × 1 hold with the claim at ~11–16 s.
