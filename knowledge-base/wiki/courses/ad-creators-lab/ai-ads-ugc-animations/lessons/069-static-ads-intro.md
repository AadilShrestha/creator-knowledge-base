# 69. Static Ads Intro
> A 36-second module opener: do not copy Meta competitor ads blindly; build statics from a single source of truth about what your customers want.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** starting any static-ad job; deciding whether to clone a competitor ad.
- **Inputs → outputs:** none (framing lesson). Sets the rule that the next lessons ([070](070-full-static-ads-creation-process.md) onward) implement.
- **Rules:**
  - **DON'T** copy competitor Meta ads blindly: "the fastest way for you to make zero sales and have high CPAs, even if they run for years" [00:00] (instructor claim).
  - **DO** use "a proven system that uses first principles and a singular source of truth as to what your customers actually want" and put that in the images [00:00].
  - **IF** an ad has no intention behind it **THEN** it is "copying random stuff" [00:00].
  - **IF** the account spends under about $100k/month **THEN** the instructor says this method is the one to stand out with [00:00] (instructor claim; no data shown).
- **See also:** [070 Full static ads creation process](070-full-static-ads-creation-process.md) · [Static ad creation playbook](../../../../playbooks/static-ad-creation.md) · [094 Research Protocol: start here](094-research-protocol-start-here.md)

## Key ideas
### Intention beats imitation [00:00–00:36]
- The only content: a talking-head statement. Competitor ads that run for years are not proof that cloning them will work for you.
- The replacement is a research-derived "singular source of truth" about the customer; every static must trace back to it. Lesson 070 shows how the instructor builds it (research document, one chosen avatar, angle bank).
