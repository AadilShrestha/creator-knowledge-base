# 108. Train Your Video Editor
> A one-minute promotion for a separate paid community, Direct Response Academy, where a hired editor can be trained in direct-response ads; contains no ad-making method.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** an editor you hired needs direct-response training (briefs, colour grading, editing tactics).
- **Rules:**
  - **IF** your editor lacks direct-response fundamentals **THEN** the lesson points to this paid community (vendor claim; not evaluated) [00:00].
  - **DON'T** treat the lesson as editing guidance; use the [103 editor brief](103-video-editors.md) for that.
- **See also:** [110 Hire from the community](110-hire-from-ad-creators-lab.md) · [Creator brief playbook](../../../../playbooks/creator-brief.md)

## Key ideas
- Direct Response Academy is described as a "training ground for direct response advertising" co-run by the speaker and Alex of Decimal Labs, who "has edited for brands like Dr Sasquatch and many many more" [00:00–00:54] (instructor claim).
- Contents listed: ad types (similar to the planning and scripting taught here), editing fundamentals (reading briefs, colour grading, editing tactics, speed), job shadows (watching ads edited live), project management, placement in full-time jobs, and bonus AI resources for making B-roll with AI [00:00].
- Page: yearly members get 6x 1:1 calls for advanced training (page text, no price in the files).
