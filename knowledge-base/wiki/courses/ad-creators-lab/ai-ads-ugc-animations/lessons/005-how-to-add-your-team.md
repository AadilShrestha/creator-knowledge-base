# 5. How To Add Your Team
> How to give an editor or teammate access: your own affiliate link (50% commission) or the VIP tier.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** staffing an editor or media buyer on this community.
- **Inputs → outputs:** teammate count → route (affiliate link for 1-2 people, VIP for a team).
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** use your Skool affiliate link for one or two people: Community tab, Settings (right sidebar), Invite; copy the link (page).
  - **DO** choose VIP for a whole team; it allows adding "an infinite number of members" [00:00].
  - **DON'T** ask the owner to add a teammate by hand: access must be "tied to something the system can actually see: a purchase, or a VIP seat" (page).
- **See also:** [Want to upgrade](006-want-to-upgrade.md) · [Hire from Ad Creators Lab](110-hire-from-ad-creators-lab.md)

## Key ideas
- Commission (instructor claim): 50% of the monthly subscription for each person who joins through your link, "every month, for as long as they stay" (page). On Standard this is $49.50 per person per month, on Premium $99.50 (page). Bring two members and "you've paid the price of what you're paying right now" [00:00].
- Why no manual adds: community is 1,100+ members (page) or "over a thousand" [00:00]; removing manually-added people later is a mess.
- VIP: the owner helps find editors and gives private Slack access; one year of everything (calls, modules, community) [00:00].

### FAQ from the page
| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| When is the money visible? | Commissions pending 14 days, then payable |
| Joined because of me but no commission? | Usually: didn't use your link, clicked more than 14 days before buying, or bought on a different device (cookie) |
| Editor leaves? | Their membership is theirs (tied to their payment) |
| Same plan as me? | No. Standard has the frameworks (fine for production); Premium is for the scaling and strategy person |

## Steps / framework
1. Community tab, Settings, Invite, copy link, send to teammate.
2. For a team: open the plans page and pick VIP.
