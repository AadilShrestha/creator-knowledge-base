# 52. Hall of Fame - Singing Ads
> A course-completion page that tells members to export their singing ad and post it in the "Ad Reviews" channel; the member example ads it displays are not in the extracted text.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** closing the singing-ads module; deciding how to get feedback on a finished song ad before spending.
- **Inputs → outputs:** exported ad → post in Ad Reviews with specific questions → feedback from Camilo, coaches or members.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** "Export your ad. Done beats perfect." (page)
  - **DO** ask specific questions: "Is my hook strong enough? Are my visuals easy to follow? How are my transitions?" (page)
  - **DON'T** treat the page as a source of example scripts: it contains none.
- **See also:** [49. The Ads We're Making](049-singing-animation-the-ads-we-re-making.md) · [51. AI songs](051-how-to-generate-ai-songs-for-your-brand.md)

## Key ideas
- Page text: "You just finished the Singing course, but it's not done." Post in "👀 Ad Reviews" to get "real feedback: Camilo, the coaches, or any member who has already nailed it will tell you what's broken (or great)."
- Community levels named on the page: The Arena (annual free credits, brand deals), The Test Lab (early access to private agency tests), The Inner Circle. No details given.
- Three steps: export, post in Ad Reviews with specific questions, learn from feedback on yours and others'.
