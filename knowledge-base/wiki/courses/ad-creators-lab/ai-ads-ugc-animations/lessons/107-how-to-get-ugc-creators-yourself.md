# 107. How to get UGC creators yourself
> DIY sourcing of UGC creators in priority order (social scouting, Fiverr, your own channels, Backstage, platforms), a DM template, red flags, and a tracking sheet; the alternative is the community pool.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** building an in-house roster of UGC creators, drafting outreach, or tracking creator status through a shoot.
- **Inputs → outputs:** brand, creator profile (gender, age, niche) → search keywords, DM, brief + budget message → a logged creator row.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** build your own roster "so you can have people that you can go back to" [00:00].
  - **DO** search "UGC creator" plus modifiers (male, female, senior male, senior female) on Instagram, TikTok and X/Twitter [00:53].
  - **DO** send the short DM, then the brief, budget and "are you ready to proceed?"; talk contracts after [01:45].
  - **DO** put at least one reply into a tracking sheet row with status, fee, concept allocated and review comments (sheet).
  - **DON'T** rely on Fiverr portfolios: "rarely find creators who have had good results" [03:07]. Reject bad portfolios, copy-pasted content, forced own style, little variety [03:37].
  - **DON'T** post "I need creators, please": good creators read it as "you're desperate" [04:28].
  - **IF** you want inbound creators **THEN** post about your brand (wins, failures, vision, behind the scenes) first [04:28–05:22].

## Steps / framework
1. Define profile (age, gender, niche).
2. Search platforms with modifiers; shortlist by portfolio.
3. DM; on reply send brief + budget.
4. Log in the sheet; set concept allocation and shipment.
5. Review footage ([104](104-bad-ugc.md)); mark status.
6. Approve captions and posting.

## How to apply
- [ ] Copy the sheet columns into your tracker (do not store creator contact details in this wiki).
- [ ] Shortlist several creators per concept; the lesson gives no outreach volume.
- [ ] Post one brand-story or behind-the-scenes piece per week before any "creators wanted" post (derived).
- [ ] Prefer a paid, written brief and contract; never use a stranger's photos as a "creator".
