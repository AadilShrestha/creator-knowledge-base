# 110. Hire from Ad Creators Lab
> Post a hiring request in the community's Hiring tab using a fixed template and state the budget; includes the editor price bands the coach quotes.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** drafting an editor job post, or setting an editor budget.
- **Inputs → outputs:** editor type, brand, videos per week, style references, budget, contact route → one post.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** open the community's Sections → More → Hiring tab and post there [00:00].
  - **DO** write a title naming the editor type and brand (example: animation editors for a beauty brand) [00:11].
  - **DO** state videos per week, add style examples (YouTube, Loom, Vimeo or direct links) and an approximate earnings figure [00:26–00:44].
  - **DON'T** post vaguely about the editor type; not every viewer is an editor (page cautions).
  - **IF** responses are low **THEN** ask the coach to make a mass post (he says he posts about one every two weeks) [01:52].
- **See also:** [109 Apply to other community](109-apply-to-other-community.md) · [108 Train your editor](108-train-your-video-editor.md) · [103 editor brief](103-video-editors.md)

## Structured units
### Editor price bands quoted [00:59]
| Editor type | Price | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Edits videos only | $20-$60 per video | [00:59] |
| Good animation videos | about $60-$80 per video, "higher margin" but better | [00:59] |
| AI video editor who pays own AI tool credits | about $100-$200 per video | [00:59] |
| Retainer | about 20 ads per month for $1,500-$2,000 | [00:59] |
Prices at recording (Aug-Sep 2026), instructor claims. The lesson page writes animation as "$60-$10", a typo for the spoken $60-80.

## Prompts / templates (verbatim)
```text
Title: Looking for [Type of editor] for [Brand/agency]
Body: How many videos per week
Style examples
Approximate earnings
Contact me via email or dm
```

## Key ideas
- The community had "629 people" at recording; some are editors, some creative strategists [01:52] (lesson 109 claims 734 editors in the separate pool).
- Page steps repeat the video: locate Hiring tab, clear title, scope and weekly output, style references, budget.
