# 17. AI Podcasts (that look real): What We Are Making
> Shows the target output of the module: a two-woman podcast-style ad, generated entirely with AI, that promotes the community itself. Build method is in [018](018-realistic-ai-podcasts-tutorial.md).

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** you need the format reference for an AI podcast ad (two speakers, shoulder-up shots, mic in frame, cut-aways) before building one.
- **Inputs → outputs:** none; this is the outcome spec for [018](018-realistic-ai-podcasts-tutorial.md).
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** copy the structure: hook line from speaker A, short challenge from speaker B ("really?"), proof beat, reveal, call to action [00:00–00:48].
  - **DO** cut away to B-roll that matches each line (blindfolded perfume test, ankle bruise, "beginner / intermediate / expert" sofa shot) with the podcasters in a small picture-in-picture corner (frames 00:05–00:12).
  - **DON'T** present the AI hosts as real members giving a real testimonial. The demo script does ("We've been making 10 to 20 new concepts every single week since we joined this group") while also saying "you probably didn't even realize that I'm not real" [00:00]. Disclose, or write a non-testimonial script (derived).
- **See also:** [018 tutorial](018-realistic-ai-podcasts-tutorial.md) · [AI UGC tools](../../../../tools/ai-ugc-tools.md) · [LLM output claim audit](../../../stefan-georgi/rmbc-ii/rubrics/llm-output-claim-audit.md)

## Key ideas
### The format [00:00–00:48]
- Vertical 9:16 podcast set: same blue wall and plant behind both hosts, a broadcast mic in frame, burned-in word-by-word captions, a bold on-screen hook ("The NEW WAY to scale on meta in 2026", frames 00:00–00:04).
- Dialogue is short alternating lines, each with a reaction ("Really?", "No way!") [00:00].
- The script sells the community in four beats: results claim, what you get (frameworks for animation, UGC, podcasts, claymation), creative strategy ("the words that your actual customers use and how to write scripts that actually convert"), access and CTA ("You just click the link below") [00:00] (instructor claims).
