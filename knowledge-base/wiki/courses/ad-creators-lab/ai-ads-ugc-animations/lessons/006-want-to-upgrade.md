# 6. Want to upgrade
> What Standard, Premium and VIP include and how upgrades are billed.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** deciding which tier covers the lessons you need.
- **Inputs → outputs:** goal (make ads vs scale vs team) → tier.
- **Rules:**
  - **IF** you make the ad (AI UGC, animation, statics) **THEN** Standard fits; **IF** you need conversion and scale (Meta scaling framework, creative strategy system) **THEN** Premium; **IF** you have a team **THEN** VIP (page).
  - **DO** expect to pay only the difference when upgrading mid-month [00:00] (page).
- **See also:** [How to add your team](005-how-to-add-your-team.md) · [How to get support](007-how-to-get-support.md)

## Key ideas
| Tier (at recording, page) | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | $99/month | The making side: AI UGC frameworks, AI animations framework, statics system |
| Premium | $199/month | Standard + Meta Scaling Framework + Creative Strategy System + 4 calls a week |
| VIP | $1,999/year | Premium for the year + private Slack with the owner + vetted AI ad editors matched to your brand + weekly group strategy call + bring your team |

- "Standard teaches you to make the ad. Premium teaches you to make it convert and scale. VIP is when you've got a team" (page TL;DR).
- How: big yellow Upgrade button, or right sidebar "Upgrade" / "Become a VIP" under the community description, then Plans page; new modules and calls open immediately (page).
- Billing: pay the difference between plans (checkout footnote) [00:00]; levels are unaffected by plan; downgrade any time; refund or fix by messaging the owner if it does not work out [00:00] (page).

## Steps / framework
1. Community page, Upgrade or Become a VIP, Plans page, pick tier.
