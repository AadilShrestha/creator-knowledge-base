# 3. Levels Unlocks
> What each community level unlocks (Arena, Test Lab, Inner Circle) and how to check your level.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** deciding which course material is accessible or what the Test Lab contains.
- **Inputs → outputs:** none; gives unlock thresholds.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** know which modules are gated: Level 2 = The Arena, Level 4 = The Test Lab, Level 8 = The Inner Circle [00:00-00:58] (page agrees).
  - **DON'T** treat Test Lab content as proven: "everything that has a full framework has been proven to work for all of our clients in different niches" [00:58] (instructor claim); Test Lab is for live trials and errors.
  - **IF** you want to check progress **THEN** Leaderboards, then your level; click the question mark for how points work; 7-day, 30-day and all-time boards [02:08].
- **See also:** [24H challenge](002-the-24h-challenge.md) · [Upgrade](006-want-to-upgrade.md)

## Key ideas
### What levels are [00:00-00:58]
- Points come from likes on your posts (video: likes and comments). "A fire emoji, nice video, bro" earns little; genuine feedback or questions earn replies, and replies earn points.
- Reasons to contribute: unlocks, and "the people that show up a lot make friends, make connections, find teammates, jobs, talent".

### Unlocks [00:00-02:08]
| Level | Unlock | What it is | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | The Arena | Monthly competition: live review, "deep dive breakdown", prizes of cash, AI credits, yearly subscription (instructor claim) | [00:00-00:58] |
| 4 | The Test Lab | "What we are testing live at the agency": trials, errors, formats, angles; not full frameworks | [00:58] |
| 8 | The Inner Circle | Free lifetime membership, private chat, a dedicated call | [00:58] |

- Three classroom modules are locked until these levels (see lesson 1).

## Steps / framework
1. Open Leaderboards; read your level.
2. Click the question mark for the points rules.
3. Study posts of top-ranked members (page: "Worth studying").
4. Leave one real piece of feedback on someone's ad.
