# 77. 7-Figure Vault: Watch First
> Intro to the Vault module: line-by-line breakdowns of winning ads from brands spending seven figures a year, shown so members study the strategy behind the creative, not just the tools.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** deciding how to read lessons 78-82 (each one pairs a video with a Google Doc holding the ad script, margin notes and metrics screenshot).
- **Inputs → outputs:** none. Output is a reading frame: study the *principles* in each breakdown (hook, belief shift, mechanism, emotional zone), not the lines.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** treat the Vault as advanced material on creative strategy, "more of an advanced thing" [00:00]; beginners should still watch it.
  - **DO** study how the strategists talk about each ad (research language, awareness level, emotional zone, iteration), not only the finished ad [00:00].
  - **DO** expect each lesson to come with a document [00:49]; more breakdowns are promised "as we unlock these from our brands and get the permissions to share them" [00:49].
  - **DON'T** read the spend and ROAS figures as benchmarks for your own account; they are instructor claims from agency client accounts (derived).
- **See also:** [078 Wine brand](078-wine-brand-125k-at-2-24-roas.md) · [079 Intimacy brand](079-intimacy-brand-200k-at-2-3-roas.md) · [080 Supplement brand](080-supplement-brand-70k-winning-ad-breakdown.md) · [081 Turning a $400 ad into a $13K spender](081-turning-400-ad-to-13k-spender.md) · [082 Men's skincare](082-mens-skincare-135k-spend-ad-breakdown.md)

## Key ideas
### What the Vault is [00:00–00:49]
- "Myself and the creative strategists at Epic Ads Lab are going to break down some of the best ads we have made for brands that are spending over a million dollars a year on ads. Some of them doing a million dollars a month on ads." (instructor claim) [00:00]
- Goal: show "more in-depth into the creative strategy aspects of making ads"; some videos run "like 24 minutes" and go "line by line through every ad" [00:00]. (In the course files the longest is 23:33.)

### Why it exists [00:49–01:36]
- Show "what is possible with some accounts at some scales" so members "think bigger" [00:49].
- The speaker frames $125,000 at a 2.24 ROAS as the scale to imagine for your own brand [00:49]; that is the wine ad in lesson 78.
- Each lesson comes with a document; the agency is "passionate about documenting these things" [00:49].
