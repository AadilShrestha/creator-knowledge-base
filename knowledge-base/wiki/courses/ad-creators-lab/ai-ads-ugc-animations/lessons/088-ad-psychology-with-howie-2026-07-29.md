# 88. Ad Reviews Call, 29 July 2026 (page summary only)
> The only content in the course files is Fathom's AI-written summary of a 77-minute ad-review call: static talking-head ads lose viewers, near-identical hooks get grouped by Meta, indirect desires beat obvious pain points, and open loops filter for the ideal customer.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** reviewing a talking-head UGC script, a set of hook variants, a mattress or skincare ad, or an AI voiceover.
- **Inputs → outputs:** draft ad or hook set → list of visual, script and CTA fixes from the summary below. Treat as (instructor-reported, machine-summarised) guidance, not verified quotes.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** add B-roll, split screens or small contextual graphics to a talking-head script, because a static talking head causes high drop-off (page: "Ad Review: Kegel Training Program").
  - **DO** make every hook variant visually distinct (speaker position, clothing, setting, green screen, split screen with different B-roll) so Meta's AI does not group them as duplicates and under-allocate budget (page: Key Takeaways).
  - **DO** frame ads around an indirect desire (the armchair not digging into your belly) rather than the obvious pain ("lose weight") (page: "Creative Strategy: Desire-Based Advertising").
  - **DO** put the visible pain of the indirect desire in the hook and bridge to the ideal outcome in the body with storytelling (page: Execution).
  - **DO** keep hook text clear (page: RV mattress: replace "throw a 2-inch slab of foam outside of the RV" with a simpler line such as "throw the mattress away").
  - **DO** make AI voices sound human: clone a voice from a high-quality podcast and turn off ElevenLabs' "cut out background noise" option to keep natural ambience (page: RV mattress). See [ElevenLabs](../../../../tools/elevenlabs.md).
  - **DO** add urgency and social proof to the CTA ("sale ends tonight", testimonials) and show the product mechanism (custom sizing, motion isolation) (page: RV mattress).
  - **DO** add an open loop (cliffhanger or question) after explaining a concept; open loops filter for the ICP because only the target audience understands the subtle references (page: Key Takeaways; family mattress).
  - **DON'T** mix AI ads with poor-performing UGC in an account without checking account history (page: Agency Operations).

### Meeting purpose
- "Review e-commerce ads and discuss creative strategy."

### Hooks must be visually distinct
- Problem: several hooks of the Kegel-training ad look identical, so Meta's AI groups them as duplicates and under-allocates budget.
- Fix: vary position, clothing or setting; use green screens or split screens with different B-roll. Compare [iteration versus variation](../../../../concepts/iteration-vs-variation.md) and the creative testing system in [creative testing system](../../../../frameworks/creative-testing-system.md).
- Idea: test an "ugly" AI avatar; its unexpected look can raise hook rate by breaking the pattern of attractive people in feeds (summary wording: "can increase hook rates"; unproven). Related: [pattern disruption](../../../../concepts/heuristics/pattern-disruption.md).

### Desire-based advertising (indirect desire)
- Obvious pain points are saturated. Mechanism per summary: frame the ad around an **ideal self**; the viewer's subconscious comparison with their current state creates an emotional gap the product fills ([ideal self](../../../../concepts/ideal-self.md), [actual self](../../../../concepts/actual-self.md)).
- Weight-loss example: instead of "lose weight", "the armchair not digging into your belly"; hook shows the pain visually, body bridges with story.
- The 12 August call gives the same idea as direct versus indirect benefits ([086](086-ad-psychology-with-howie-2026-08-12.md) [1:02:24]).

### Agency operations and client selection (summary)
- Vetting clients is critical: a previous client's media buyer mixed AI ads with poor UGC, the account underperformed and the client left.
- "An ad getting spend is a winner, even if it's not immediately at KPI" because it may be top-of-funnel driving later purchases (compare [086](086-ad-psychology-with-howie-2026-08-12.md) [54:22]).
- Scale small brands rather than taking large ones with established scale.
- Skincare and makeup are highly competitive and need expertise against major brands.
