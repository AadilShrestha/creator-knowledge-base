# 2. The 24H Challenge
> Onboarding task: reach Level 2 in 24 hours by finishing one ad format, posting it for feedback and giving real feedback, so you unlock the monthly ad competition (The Arena).

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** you need the course's feedback-loop mechanics or the definition of useful ad feedback.
- **Inputs → outputs:** one finished ad (AI UGC, AI animation or static) + a community post → points, feedback, Level 2.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** pick one format from AI UGC, AI Animated Ads or Static Ads and finish it [00:58].
  - **DO** post it and ask for feedback (page: tag the post "Ad Reviews") [00:58].
  - **DO** give feedback that says what you felt, what to improve, what you did not understand, or how a shot was made: not "fire emoji, great work dude" [00:58] (page list).
  - **DO** post an introduction first: "The fastest single win" (page); the instructor says it also gets welcome comments [00:58].
  - **DON'T** expect hacks: "Every like you get on a post = 1 point. That's it." (page)
- **See also:** [Levels & unlocks](003-levels-unlocks.md) · [Watch this if you want to win](001-watch-this-if-you-want-to-win.md) · [Diversified ads](008-how-to-make-diversified-ads.md)

## Key ideas
### Goal and prize [00:00-00:58]
- Get to Level 2 as fast as possible: it unlocks "the arena", where members "compete against other members" by submitting ads "rated by professionals in our monthly ad games" [00:00].
- Prizes (instructor claim, at recording): top three "get all their AI credits paid for"; number one champion "gets a full yearly subscription of the AI platform of their choice" [00:00]. He calls it "the singular most valuable aspect of this community" because experts review ads "from live accounts spending millions of dollars every single month" (instructor claim).

### Points [00:00-00:58]
- Skool gives a point for each like on a post (video says "for every like and comment"; the page says likes only: "Every like you get on a post = 1 point"). Use the page version.

## Steps / framework
1. Post your introduction (where you are, your story, what you want from the community) before watching anything else (page).
2. Pick a format, finish one ad, start the clock (page).
3. Post it with the ad-review tag and ask for feedback.
4. Leave genuine feedback on other members' ads (questions, improvements, confusion, how-did-they-do-it).
5. Check the leaderboard for your level.
