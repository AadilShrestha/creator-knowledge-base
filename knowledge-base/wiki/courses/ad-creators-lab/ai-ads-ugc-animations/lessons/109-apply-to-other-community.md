# 109. Apply to other community
> How to request editors from the agency's separate community of editors via a form, what to fill in, and the rate band the speaker advises.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** you want editors matched to you without posting yourself.
- **Inputs → outputs:** form answers (what you need, volume, job description, about you, budget) → editors DM or email you.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** state the role precisely, e.g. "UGC ads editor", and whether videos are "highly animated or not very animated" [00:00].
  - **DO** give monthly volume; for one-off work enter 1-3 [00:51].
  - **DO** describe the job: "taking videos from creators and editing them into ads" [00:51].
  - **DO** be honest about budget: "just think of what can you charge at this stage" [00:51].
  - **DON'T** answer yes to the screening question unless you want the coach to vet applicants first; "no" routes applicants straight to your inbox, "yes" is slower [00:51].
- **See also:** [110 Hire from the community](110-hire-from-ad-creators-lab.md) · [103 editor brief](103-video-editors.md)

## Key ideas
- Community claim: 734 video editors learning short-form ad content, affordable, across Europe, South America, South Africa and beyond Southeast Asia [00:00] (instructor claim; lesson 110 says 629 members in the Skool group, which includes non-editors).
- Rate guidance for ad editing: low end $30, high end $70 per video; different portfolios and skill levels [00:51] (at recording, Aug-Sep 2026; compare lesson 110's $20-60).
- The page offers a "Get VIP" option where the coach "personally finds you one" (paid upsell; price not in the files).
