# Ad roast scorecard
> The course's own scoring sheet (hook, clarity, sells, execution, 0–5 each, total /20) with its verbatim scoring key, Camilo's repeatable roast checklist from the August 2026 roast calls mapped onto the four criteria, and a pass/fail compliance line that blocks any ad regardless of score.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** before launching any AI UGC, animated or static ad; when reviewing an editor's or another agent's cut; when ranking several variants for a test.
- **Inputs → outputs:** the ad (video, static or script with shot list) + avatar, awareness stage, offer and landing page → four scores with quoted evidence, total /20, compliance line pass/fail, top three fixes.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** score hook, clarity, sells, execution 0–5 each, total /20 [L84 37:59](../lessons/084-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-21.md).
  - **DO** start with context: who is the avatar, awareness stage, market and offer; Camilo's first criticism of an unscored ad was that he couldn't tell who it was for [L83 11:18](../lessons/083-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-17.md).
  - **DO** quote the line, frame or timestamp that justifies each score; fix the lowest criterion first.
  - **DO** run the compliance line first; any fail blocks the ad whatever the total.
  - **DON'T** treat a high hook rate as proof the ad sells [L80 04:11](../lessons/080-supplement-brand-70k-winning-ad-breakdown.md); [L81 04:28](../lessons/081-turning-400-ad-to-13k-spender.md). Live results beat this score.
  - **IF** the ad is AI video **THEN** run the [AI video QA checklist](ai-video-qa-checklist.md) before scoring execution.
- **See also:** [Winning-ad checklist](../frameworks/winning-ad-checklist.md) · [AI video QA checklist](ai-video-qa-checklist.md) · [Shared ad scorecard (100 pt)](../../../../rubrics/ad-scorecard.md) · [Evolve copy checklist scorecard](../../../evolve/copywriting/rubrics/copy-checklist-scorecard.md) · [RMBC claim audit](../../../stefan-georgi/rmbc-ii/rubrics/llm-output-claim-audit.md) · [AI creator compliance](../concepts/ai-creator-compliance.md) · [Ad teardowns library](../libraries/ad-teardowns.md)

### Score anchors (derived; the course gives no 0–5 definitions)
| Score | Hook | Clarity | Sells | Execution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | No hook: slow intro, logo, nothing in 0–3 s | Can't say what the product is after the ad | No desire, no reason, no CTA | Broken: visible AI glitches, out of sync, unreadable |
| 1 | Obvious statement the viewer already knows ("duh, scroll") [L83 15:36](../lessons/083-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-17.md) | Product findable, benefit unclear; competing ideas | Feature list or vague claims ("better", "game changer") | Several AI tells; podcasty voice mismatch; text in the button zone |
| 2 | Relevant but generic; words the customer wouldn't say | Product clear after 5+ s | One reason, no pain build, weak CTA | Watchable; one obvious tell or pacing sag |
| 3 | Specific callout or claim in the customer's words | Product and why-want clear within a few seconds | Pain built, mechanism stated, CTA present | Clean; minor polish gaps |
| 4 | Open loop + matching first visual | Clear and one message only | Desire built, objection handled, believable speaker, CTA tied to hook | Every line shown; voice fits; safe zone clean; consistent motion |
| 5 | Open loop, customer language, visual pattern interrupt, earns the next second | Instantly clear to a cold scroller | All of 4 plus a reason only this product can give | Indistinguishable from the native format; polish serves the sell |

**Pass mark (derived default):** total ≥ 15/20, no criterion below 3, compliance line PASS. Below that, fix the lowest criterion and rescore. The course states no threshold.

## Output block
```yaml
roast_scorecard:
  ad_id: <id>
  context: {avatar: "<…>", awareness: <stage>, market: "<…>", offer: "<exact>", landing_page: <url|missing>}
  compliance_line: {result: PASS|FAIL, failed: []}   # e.g. [C2: "AI creator says 'I lost 12 kg'" at 0:07]
  scores:
    hook:      {score: 0-5, evidence: "<quote/frame + timestamp>"}
    clarity:   {score: 0-5, evidence: "<…>"}
    sells:     {score: 0-5, evidence: "<…>"}
    execution: {score: 0-5, evidence: "<…>"}   # attach ai_video_qa result for AI video
  total: <0-20>
  verdict: SHIP | FIX | BLOCK          # BLOCK if compliance FAIL; FIX if total <15 or any criterion <3
  roast_checklist_fails: [<item numbers with one-line note>]
  top_fixes:                            # lowest criterion first, one variable each
    - "<fix> (source: <lesson cite>)"
  funnel_fit_note: "<page matches / needs education page>"
```

## Conflicts and defaults
| Topic | Position A | Position B | Default |
|---|---|---|---|
| CTA wording | Sells key requires "a real reason + CTA to act" [L84 38:23 frame](../lessons/084-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-21.md); the supplement winner closes "Here, I'll leave a link below" [L80 16:58](../lessons/080-supplement-brand-70k-winning-ad-breakdown.md) | Don't say "buy it now / link below / try it now"; it "activates the natural defense mechanism" (instructor claim) [L82 11:09](../lessons/082-mens-skincare-135k-spend-ad-breakdown.md); the Gratsi brief also bans "link below" ([L20 page](../lessons/020-claude-skill-seedance-2-0-ai-ugc.md)) | A CTA must exist and be tied to the reason; score its presence, not its wording; test explicit vs implied CTA as a variable |
| Hook score vs results | High hook rate is attractive | 30% hook rate with buyers beat 50–60% without [L80 04:11](../lessons/080-supplement-brand-70k-winning-ad-breakdown.md) | The hook score predicts attention only; judge winners on spend and CPA |
