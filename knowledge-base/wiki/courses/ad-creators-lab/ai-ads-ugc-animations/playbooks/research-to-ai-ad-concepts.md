# Playbook: research to AI ad concepts
> The course's research protocol as gated steps: fill brand information, collect raw customer language (Reddit, reviews, TikTok comments), synthesise with an LLM and check every line against the raw data, pick one avatar, map awareness stages, find competitor and adjacent-niche ads by **spend** (not run time), tear them down in three layers, and write 5–10 diversified concept cards that route to an AI production playbook. Then close the loop with MORE / BETTER / NEW.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** starting a new brand or batch, ideas are exhausted, or a production playbook needs a concept card.
- **Inputs → outputs:** brand + product facts + access to reviews, Reddit, TikTok and an ad-spy tool → research doc (PDF), one avatar, awareness map, spend-ranked ad shortlist, teardown cards, 5–10 concept cards, `missing_inputs`.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** collect desires, pains, **objections**, **behaviors** (moments you can call out) and **terminology**, in the customer's exact words [L97 00:00](../lessons/097-customer-research-protocol-1.md).
  - **DO** run a source-truth check on the LLM report; the AI "does hallucinate"; re-prompt it to "use the actual data that's in the document" [L97 10:14–11:10](../lessons/097-customer-research-protocol-1.md).
  - **DO** write to one avatar [L98 00:00](../lessons/098-customer-research-protocol-2.md) and put avatar, awareness stage and market at the top of every copy doc [L83 11:18](../lessons/083-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-17.md).
  - **DON'T** pick competitor ads by days running: "that is the worst thing that you can do" [L100 00:00](../lessons/100-why-copying-longest-running-is-bad.md). Use spend [L100 02:08](../lessons/100-why-copying-longest-running-is-bad.md).
  - **DO** look at adjacent niches, not only your exact niche [L101 02:20](../lessons/101-how-to-find-ads-based-on-ad-spend.md); [L70 05:16–06:02](../lessons/070-full-static-ads-creation-process.md).
  - **DO** copy the mechanism, not the asset; identify the foundations that make the ad good and rebuild them for your product [L91 18:21–36:26](../lessons/091-special-guest-recordings-ai-office-hours-david.md).
  - **DON'T** invent quotes, personas or stats; the research prompt itself says "no invented quotes" ([L97 page prompt](../lessons/097-customer-research-protocol-1.md)); the L95/L96 example stats are illustrations [L95 01:38; 02:29](../lessons/095-market-awareness-explained-in-180s.md).
  - **IF** you need more creative **THEN** make variations (MORE); **IF** it isn't performing **THEN** rewrite hooks and brief (BETTER); **IF** ideas are exhausted **THEN** go back to research (NEW) [L94 01:30](../lessons/094-research-protocol-start-here.md).
- **See also:** [Research document protocol](../frameworks/research-document-protocol.md) · [Ad-spend signal](../concepts/ad-spend-signal.md) · [Winning-ad checklist](../frameworks/winning-ad-checklist.md) · [Hook schema and open loops](../concepts/hook-schema-and-open-loops.md) · [AI ad format catalog](../concepts/ai-ad-format-catalog.md) · [Diversification vs variation](../concepts/diversification-vs-variation.md) · [Shared: customer research to angles](../../../../playbooks/customer-research-to-angles.md) · [Shared: competitor ad teardown](../../../../playbooks/competitor-ad-teardown.md) · [Evolve: research to first concepts](../../../evolve/start-here/playbooks/research-to-first-concepts.md) · [Angles library](../libraries/angles.md) · [Hooks library](../libraries/hooks.md) · [Ad teardowns library](../libraries/ad-teardowns.md)

## Step details

### Step 7: awareness map (course version)
| Stage | Tactic | Format | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unaware | Statistics and questions that expose hidden pain; don't name the product | Stat or question video | [L95 00:43–01:38](../lessons/095-market-awareness-explained-in-180s.md); [L96 00:00](../lessons/096-market-awareness-ad-examples.md) |
| Problem aware | Call out the problem clearly; "you don't want to pitch your product" | Problem call-out | [L95 01:38](../lessons/095-market-awareness-explained-in-180s.md) |
| Solution aware | Introduce the unique mechanism | Mechanism explainer | [L95 01:38](../lessons/095-market-awareness-explained-in-180s.md) |
| Product aware | Compare with others, unique benefits | Us vs them | [L95 02:29](../lessons/095-market-awareness-explained-in-180s.md); [L96](../lessons/096-market-awareness-ad-examples.md) |
| Most aware | Social proof, FAQs, guarantees | Golden-nugget review (a real one) | [L95 02:29](../lessons/095-market-awareness-explained-in-180s.md); [L96](../lessons/096-market-awareness-ad-examples.md) |
Prefer unaware / problem-aware concepts when the goal is scale: "you can only scale ads that are bringing in new customers" (instructor claim) [L100 00:45](../lessons/100-why-copying-longest-running-is-bad.md).

| Layer / check | Question | Source |
|---|---|---|

| Hook | Juxtaposition ("how to [desire] without [pain]"), authority the target respects, desire made visual | [L80 07:28–08:22](../lessons/080-supplement-brand-70k-winning-ad-breakdown.md); [L78 10:38–20:55](../lessons/078-wine-brand-125k-at-2-24-roas.md) |

| Their risk | Fabricated proof, AI "customers", health claims in the ad: note and don't copy | Repo rule; [AI creator compliance](../concepts/ai-creator-compliance.md) |

### Step 10: concept diversity
- Diversified means different at a glance: different formats for statics (us-vs-them, product + text, news/unaware, problem-solution, notes app), different hook **and** B-roll for video, or 20–30 s hooks [L08 00:03–04:26](../lessons/008-how-to-make-diversified-ads.md); background in [diversification vs variation](../concepts/diversification-vs-variation.md) and [Meta Andromeda](../../../../tools/meta-andromeda.md).
- Concept types that need real people ("not really types of videos that AI can produce", instructor claim) go to human creators, not AI [L106 00:00](../lessons/106-ugc-concepts-that-print.md): challenge, real testimonial/case study. Don't rig challenges [L106 00:54](../lessons/106-ugc-concepts-that-print.md).
- Service businesses: objection Q&A concept from real review questions, answered by an AI or claymation character (instructor claim "always crushes") [L83 36:47](../lessons/083-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-17.md).

## Deliverables template
```markdown
## <Brand / product> — research → concepts, batch <n>
**missing_inputs:** [...] · **research time spent:** <min> · **sources:** <counts per source>

### 1. Research doc (PDF) outline
Brand information · Categorized insights (pains, desires, moments, objections, behaviors, terminology — each with raw-source link) · Strategy implications · ICP language (verbatim) · Key personas · Chosen avatar · Concepts

### 2. Awareness map
| Stage | What this avatar already believes | Tactic | Format | Evidence quote |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### 3. Ad shortlist (spend-ranked)
| Ad | Brand / niche (same / adjacent) | Spend (EU/UK) | Days running | Duplicates | Stage | Why shortlisted |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### 4. Teardown cards
| Ad | Mouth moving? | Visual layer | Audio layer | Script layer (loops, language, authority, mechanism) | Mechanism to borrow | Their risk (don't copy) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### 5. Concept cards (5–10)
| ID | Desire / pain (quote) | Avatar | Stage | Format (catalog) | Production playbook | Hook direction | Proof available | Source mix (borrowed / own / new) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### 6. Test plan + loop
| Concept | Variants | Metric | Read after | MORE / BETTER / NEW rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
```
