# AI creator compliance
> The gate every AI ad passes before it is generated and again before it is launched: this course teaches many tactics that break our rules (stranger faces, cloned voices, AI "customers", fake comments, invented experts and scarcity, unsupported health claims, 1:1 competitor clones); each is listed below with its lesson cite and the compliant way to get the same creative effect.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** before writing a brief for any AI-produced ad (AI UGC, podcast, street interview, animated, song, static), before uploading references or voices to a model, before the edit, and before launch. Run it on every course example you are about to reuse, because the examples carry the violations.
- **Inputs → outputs:** script or brief + reference set (faces, footage, audio) + voice plan + edit plan (overlays, footage sources) + claim list + truth sheet (real product facts, real reviews with source, real people with consent, real offer rules) → a per-row verdict (OK / FIX / BLOCK) from the [risk register](#risk-register-course-tactic--compliant-version), a cleaned brief, and a claim register from the [RMBC LLM output claim audit](../../../stefan-georgi/rmbc-ii/rubrics/llm-output-claim-audit.md) (checks C1–C20).
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** run the [claim audit](../../../stefan-georgi/rmbc-ii/rubrics/llm-output-claim-audit.md) on every script before generation: "the AI creator will say whatever the brief says" ([L20 How to apply](../lessons/020-claude-skill-seedance-2-0-ai-ugc.md)). An AI model renders invented proof as convincingly as real proof.
  - **DO** use only faces and voices you generated from a text description, licensed with an AI-use release, or own under a signed creator contract. The course's own podcast tutorial changes the face to "the opposite of what it is" so no real person is recognisable ([L18 02:24–03:16](../lessons/018-realistic-ai-podcasts-tutorial.md)); make that the minimum, not an option.
  - **DON'T** let an AI person give a first-person result, testimonial or expert opinion. Justin's own rule: use AI characters to "get across the information as long as they're not making any claims" ([L80 09:55](../lessons/080-supplement-brand-70k-winning-ad-breakdown.md)); his shipped script breaks it, so apply the rule more strictly than the course does.
  - **DON'T** clone or imitate a real person's voice or likeness: lesson 67 clones a named YouTube creator's voice from ripped audio ([L67 00:00–00:57](../lessons/067-zack-d-films-voice-clone-song.md)). Design the voice instead ([L28 00:00–01:43](../lessons/028-consistent-voices-method-2.md), [L57 00:00–01:58](../lessons/057-claymation-generating-voice-over.md)).
  - **DON'T** fake engagement or proof: no comment-generator overlays ([L31 02:44](../lessons/031-realistic-ai-ugc-rizz-model-editing-breakdown.md)), no invented testimonials, dentist quotes or "last 200 pairs" scarcity ([L74 00:25; 01:18; 09:05](../lessons/074-fixing-bad-static-ads-2.md)).
  - **DON'T** clone a competitor's ad scene by scene or recreate its static 1:1 ([L91 16:16–18:21](../lessons/091-special-guest-recordings-ai-office-hours-david.md), [L76 02:48](../lessons/076-new-make-static-ads-with-ai-poppy-method.md)); extract the mechanism into a brief and rebuild with our product, script, visuals and claims ([creator-swap-compliant](../frameworks/creator-swap-compliant.md)).
  - **DON'T** treat "Meta won't flag it" as permission. The guest who says Meta does not flag AI creative says in the same answer that disclosure and non-deception still apply ([L93 56:54–58:18](../lessons/093-special-guest-recordings-mr-paid-social-guest-talk.md)).
  - **IF** a claim is blocked as an explicit line **THEN** don't smuggle it in as a visual: the Gratsi script replaced "no hangover" with "wakes at 6 a.m." ([L78 09:43–10:38](../lessons/078-wine-brand-125k-at-2-24-roas.md)); a visual that implies the blocked claim is still that claim (derived).
  - **IF** a tool link in a lesson carries `?via=camilo`, `CAMILO20` or a referral code **THEN** treat the recommendation as sponsored and never paste the link into our outputs ([L20 Sources](../lessons/020-claude-skill-seedance-2-0-ai-ugc.md), [L101 Sources](../lessons/101-how-to-find-ads-based-on-ad-spend.md), [L102 Sources](../lessons/102-find-winning-concepts-with-adnova.md)).
  - **DO** upload every generated ad PAUSED, run a second QA pass, and keep a human final yes/no ([L93 50:59–52:27](../lessons/093-special-guest-recordings-mr-paid-social-guest-talk.md)).

## Definition
AI creator compliance = the set of checks that keep an AI-produced ad from (1) using a real person's face, body or voice without rights, (2) presenting an AI person, AI comment or AI story as a real customer, expert, founder or event, (3) carrying claims, proof, prices or urgency that are not true and documented, (4) copying another brand's or creator's asset, and (5) hiding that people on screen are synthetic where the platform or local law requires disclosure. It is a gate, not a style guide: a FAIL blocks generation or launch until fixed.

## Why it matters
- **The course's production methods make fabrication cheap and invisible.** Rizz can "make anybody speak anything" ([L29 04:07](../lessons/029-make-ai-creators-talk.md)); Seedance 2.0 "follows everything to the T" ([L20 11:28](../lessons/020-claude-skill-seedance-2-0-ai-ugc.md)); the agency's editing goal is to remove "subtle thoughts that this is an AI video" ([L31 03:48](../lessons/031-realistic-ai-ugc-rizz-model-editing-breakdown.md)). Every tactic that improves realism also raises the cost of a false statement.
- **The course's example scripts carry the violations.** Lesson pages flag invented stories, unsupported health claims and fake scarcity in 019, 024, 036, 037, 044, 049, 059, 063, 074, 075, 078, 080–084 and 090 (see [Where it's taught](#where-its-taught)). An agent that copies an example copies the violation.
- **The instructors flag some of it themselves.** Justin: AI characters should not make claims ([L80 09:55](../lessons/080-supplement-brand-70k-winning-ad-breakdown.md)). Hao on a guilt-trip hook: "not very ethical" ([L86 46:15](../lessons/086-ad-psychology-with-howie-2026-08-12.md)). The UGC concepts lesson: testimonial only after real use ([L106 05:00](../lessons/106-ugc-concepts-that-print.md)). Suno rejects named artists ([L51 01:39](../lessons/051-how-to-generate-ai-songs-for-your-brand.md)); Sora 2 Pro and Arcads reject face uploads ([L25 Agent use](../lessons/025-realistic-ai-ugc-rizz-model-generating-realistic-hook.md), [L33 01:23 frame](../lessons/033-sora-2-pro-hooks.md)). Use those as the floor.

## How to use (steps)
1. **Identity check (faces, bodies).** For every person on screen, record the source: generated from text / licensed with AI-use release / contracted creator with face consent / our own team with consent. Anything else (Pinterest stranger, competitor's creator, celebrity look-alike, real podcast host) = BLOCK. Rows 1–8.
2. **Voice check.** For every voice: designed (Voice Design), stock library voice, contracted voice actor, or our own team with consent. Any clone of a real person without written consent = BLOCK. Rows 9–14.
3. **Persona and proof check.** Who says each line, and is it presented as true? AI person + first-person result, testimonial, medical story or expert opinion = BLOCK (claim audit C1, C2, C11, C16). Comments, review counts, ratings and badges must exist and be checkable (C5). Rows 15–24.
4. **Claims check.** Run the full [claim audit](../../../stefan-georgi/rmbc-ii/rubrics/llm-output-claim-audit.md). Health, body, sexual-health, alcohol, weight-loss and earnings claims are BLOCK until substantiated (C3, C7, C8, C9). Scarcity, deadlines and reference prices only if real and enforced (C6, C13). Rows 25–30.
5. **Copying check.** List every reference ad, clip, song, SFX and garment used. Our product, our script, our visuals, licensed audio. Competitor brand names in scripts or on props need substantiation and legal review, else generic wording. Rows 31–36.
6. **Disclosure and edit check.** Check the current AI-content disclosure rules of the platform and market at launch; this course does not state them, so do not assume. Do not add edits whose purpose is to hide that people are synthetic. Rows 37–38.
7. **Launch check.** Upload PAUSED, second-agent QA, human yes/no ([L93 50:59–52:27](../lessons/093-special-guest-recordings-mr-paid-social-guest-talk.md)); then the [AI video QA checklist](../rubrics/ai-video-qa-checklist.md).

## Common mistakes
- Treating "the course did it and the ad spent $70K" as evidence the tactic is allowed. Spend says nothing about legality or platform policy; the 080 winner carries IGF-1 and WADA claims flagged as unsupported ([L80 Sources](../lessons/080-supplement-brand-70k-winning-ad-breakdown.md)).
- Fixing the script but not the references: a compliant script spoken by a stranger's Pinterest face still fails row 1.
- Running the claim audit on the brief but not on the model's output: Seedance and Sora add spoken words and on-screen text you did not write ([L20 06:41](../lessons/020-claude-skill-seedance-2-0-ai-ugc.md); [L25 01:32](../lessons/025-realistic-ai-ugc-rizz-model-generating-realistic-hook.md)). Re-check words in the final render.
- Copying Justin's or Hao's teardown scripts with new nouns: both say the principles are the asset, not the lines ([L78 20:55](../lessons/078-wine-brand-125k-at-2-24-roas.md); [L79 10:54–11:33](../lessons/079-intimacy-brand-200k-at-2-3-roas.md)); the lines also carry the claims.
- Assuming a style is safe because it is animated: claymation, crochet and skeleton scripts in this course carry the same health and price claims as the UGC scripts ([L44](../lessons/044-crochet-the-ad-we-are-making.md), [L59](../lessons/059-swipe-file-claymation-ad-scripts.md), [L37](../lessons/037-install-skill-generate-script.md)).
