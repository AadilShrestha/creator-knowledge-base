# Diversification vs variation
> Under Meta's Andromeda delivery (as the course describes it), ads that look alike at a glance get treated as one, so a "new ad" must differ in concept, format or visual world, not in headline wording or a 3–5 s hook swap; but the same course also shows a hook-only change on a proven body taking an ad from about $477 to about $13.6K spend, so hook iteration stays the right move when the body sells and the hook rate is low.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** planning a creative batch, deciding whether the next ad is a new concept, a new format of the same concept, or an iteration of a winner; briefing editors or AI tools for "more variants".
- **Inputs → outputs:** current live ads (concept, format, hook, visual world, spend, hook rate, hold rate, ROAS vs break-even) + research doc → a labelled batch plan: **diversify** (new concept or new format, looks different zoomed out), **iterate** (same body, new visual + copy hooks), or **restart at research** (MORE / BETTER / NEW, [L94 01:30](../lessons/094-research-protocol-start-here.md)).
- **Rules:**
  - **DON'T** count headline tweaks, text size or colour changes on the same layout as new ads: "No, they're not" different ([L8 00:03; 01:56](../lessons/008-how-to-make-diversified-ads.md), instructor claim); judge difference "when I zoom out and squint" ([L8 01:56](../lessons/008-how-to-make-diversified-ads.md)).
  - **DO** diversify statics by literal format: us-vs-them, product + text, news/unaware, problem-solution, notes app ([L8 02:43](../lessons/008-how-to-make-diversified-ads.md)); lazy fallback: three versions where headline concept, colours and copy all change ([L8 01:56–02:43](../lessons/008-how-to-make-diversified-ads.md)).
  - **DO** make each video variant differ after the hook too: new hook AND new B-roll set, optional new ending ([L8 03:33–04:26](../lessons/008-how-to-make-diversified-ads.md)); or a much longer 20–30 s hook ([L8 04:26](../lessons/008-how-to-make-diversified-ads.md), instructor claim).
  - **DO** make hook variants visually distinct (speaker position, clothing, setting, green screen, split screen with different B-roll) so Meta does not group them as duplicates (088 page summary, [L88 Agent use](../lessons/088-ad-psychology-with-howie-2026-07-29.md)).
  - **IF** the body sells but the hook rate is low **THEN** iterate new visual and copy hooks on the same body right away; the hook was the only difference between about $477 and about $13.6K spend ([L81 07:36; 08:30–09:24](../lessons/081-turning-400-ad-to-13k-spender.md), instructor-reported), and make the new hooks visually different too (derived reconciliation with [L8](../lessons/008-how-to-make-diversified-ads.md)).
  - **DO** test visuals in opposite emotional zones, not two versions of the same pleasantness ([L87 18:22](../lessons/087-ad-psychology-with-howie-2026-08-27.md)).
  - **DO** keep a funnel ladder in the account (top, middle, bottom, promo; statics included) instead of judging top-of-funnel creative on bottom-of-funnel ROAS ([L86 53:24; 1:05:17–1:06:15](../lessons/086-ad-psychology-with-howie-2026-08-12.md)).
  - **DON'T** read a high hook rate as a winner: a 30% hook rate with buyers outscales 50–60% with few buyers ([L80 04:11](../lessons/080-supplement-brand-70k-winning-ad-breakdown.md)); the 081 loser had the slightly higher hook rate ([L81 04:28](../lessons/081-turning-400-ad-to-13k-spender.md)).
  - **IF** ideas are exhausted **THEN** go back to research, not to more hook swaps ([L94 01:30](../lessons/094-research-protocol-start-here.md)).
- **See also:** [Meta Andromeda (shared)](../../../../tools/meta-andromeda.md) · [Iteration vs variation (shared)](../../../../concepts/iteration-vs-variation.md) · [Format variation ladder](../../../../frameworks/format-variation-ladder.md) · [Evolve iteration outcomes](../../../evolve/copywriting/concepts/iteration-outcomes.md) · [RMBC modular creative and hook swaps](../../../stefan-georgi/rmbc-ii/concepts/modular-creative-and-hook-swaps.md) · [AI ad format catalog](ai-ad-format-catalog.md)

## Definition
- **Diversification** (this course's word): ads that Meta's system and a scrolling viewer read as different, because concept, format or visual world differ. Lesson 8's "best way" is literal different creative formats; the "lazy way" restyles one concept in three clearly different treatments ([L8 01:56–02:43](../lessons/008-how-to-make-diversified-ads.md)).
- **Variation / iteration:** a new version of a working ad that keeps the concept and body and changes one block, usually the hook (visual and copy), as in Justin's feedback loop ([L81 08:30–09:24](../lessons/081-turning-400-ad-to-13k-spender.md)). Agency shorthand for cheap versions: "repurposed ads or sometimes we call them edit only ads" ([L102 00:57](../lessons/102-find-winning-concepts-with-adnova.md)).
- **Andromeda** (as taught here): "the Meta Andromeda algorithm that came out in July of this year" ([L8 00:03](../lessons/008-how-to-make-diversified-ads.md), instructor claim; year not stated). The instructor's model: it used to tag information groups in an ad and find audiences per tag; now ads that look alike are collapsed, "these all look the same. Let me just pick this one", before ranking ([L8 01:01–01:56](../lessons/008-how-to-make-diversified-ads.md), instructor claim, not Meta documentation).

## Why it matters
- Near-identical variants waste budget: they are grouped and under-allocated (088 page summary) and give the system nothing new to find buyers with ([L8 02:43](../lessons/008-how-to-make-diversified-ads.md)).
- Different formats may surface different winners: "may find that 'news angles' get served more" ([L8 02:43](../lessons/008-how-to-make-diversified-ads.md)).
- Who appears on screen may steer delivery: Justin says Meta tends to show a creative to the demographic that looks like the on-screen person ([L78 04:11–04:58](../lessons/078-wine-brand-125k-at-2-24-roas.md), instructor claim; the lesson page calls it unproven). Different creators are a diversity lever, not just a cosmetic one.
- Ads fatigue: at a frequency of about 2–3 a top-of-funnel ad's CPA spikes, so a pipeline of new top-of-funnel creative is needed ([L100 01:29](../lessons/100-why-copying-longest-running-is-bad.md), instructor claim).

## How to use (steps)
1. **Label the situation** with the research loop ([L94 01:30](../lessons/094-research-protocol-start-here.md)): need MORE ads → variations of what works; creative NOT performing → rewrite hooks and brief (new wording, different desires); ideas EXHAUSTED → back to research for a NEW concept.
2. **For a new concept or "more" batch: diversify.** For each concept, list the formats not yet made (statics: comparison, news, problem-solution, notes app; video: see [format catalog](ai-ad-format-catalog.md)) and make one of each ([L8 How to apply](../lessons/008-how-to-make-diversified-ads.md)). Check by zooming out: if two ads look alike, one is not new.
3. **For a winner whose body sells: iterate the hook.** Pair the winner with its loser siblings (same body, different hooks); write the learning (which words segment, which desire the avatar has); brief 3–5 new visual hooks and 3–5 new copy hooks on the same body; make hook words and hook visuals agree ([L81 04:58–09:24; How to apply](../lessons/081-turning-400-ad-to-13k-spender.md)).
4. **Make every hook variant look different.** Change speaker position, setting, outfit, green screen or split screen, and the B-roll after the hook ([L88 Agent use](../lessons/088-ad-psychology-with-howie-2026-07-29.md); [L8 03:33](../lessons/008-how-to-make-diversified-ads.md)). In AI production this is cheap: new creator reference, new location reference (see [character consistency](character-consistency.md) for keeping the body consistent while the hook changes).
5. **Vary emotion, not just look.** Put the new variant in an opposite valence-intensity zone ([L87 18:22](../lessons/087-ad-psychology-with-howie-2026-08-27.md)).
6. **Plan the ladder.** Top, middle, bottom and promo creative live together; judge each on its own job ([L86 1:05:17–1:06:15](../lessons/086-ad-psychology-with-howie-2026-08-12.md)).
7. **Batch size.** Caleb's operator setup fires batches of 10 after a one-image smoke test and uploads all ads PAUSED ([L93 36:11–38:40; 50:59](../lessons/093-special-guest-recordings-mr-paid-social-guest-talk.md)); our account structure stays [Evolve 3:2:2](../../../evolve/start-here/frameworks/campaign-structure-322.md) (see cross-course notes).

## Common mistakes
- Swapping only the first 3–5 seconds and calling it a new ad ([L8 03:33–04:26](../lessons/008-how-to-make-diversified-ads.md)).
- Over-correcting: abandoning a proven body because "hook swaps are dead" when the data says the hook is the weak block ([L81 08:30–09:24](../lessons/081-turning-400-ad-to-13k-spender.md)).
- Picking the next hook by hook rate alone ([L81 04:28](../lessons/081-turning-400-ad-to-13k-spender.md); [L80 04:11](../lessons/080-supplement-brand-70k-winning-ad-breakdown.md)).
- Diversifying format but keeping the same emotional zone everywhere ([L87 18:22](../lessons/087-ad-psychology-with-howie-2026-08-27.md); [emotional stagnation](../../../../concepts/emotional-stagnation.md)).
- Generating "diverse" ads from one stranger face, one stock voice and one kitchen: AI makes visual diversity cheap; use it.
- Making 30 near-duplicates because AI batch generation is cheap: volume without difference is the problem lesson 8 describes.

## Cross-course notes
- **Tether Lab (shared, agrees on diversity, differs on emphasis):** Sarah: five near-identical statics make the algorithm think "all of these ads are the same"; "One message with four formats, then test five messages" ([Meta Andromeda](../../../../tools/meta-andromeda.md)). Chase: "Variations is what variety actually should be mostly predicated on", most effort on variations of the core message ([iteration vs variation](../../../../concepts/iteration-vs-variation.md)). Agrees with lesson 8 that variations must differ in format or expression, not words.
- **Evolve (differs from lesson 8, agrees with lesson 81):** Evolve's iteration lists change the video hook and imagery first ([rewrite losers and iterate](../../../evolve/start-here/frameworks/rewrite-losers-and-iterate.md)) and its hooks page adds an Andromeda-era check: visuals must tell Meta which audience to serve ([Evolve hooks](../../../evolve/copywriting/concepts/hooks.md)). Label before you iterate and wait at least 7 days ([iteration outcomes](../../../evolve/copywriting/concepts/iteration-outcomes.md)).
- **RMBC II (differs from lesson 8):** hold the body, swap the hook, one variable per variant, track Script/Lead/Hook IDs ([modular creative and hook swaps](../../../stefan-georgi/rmbc-ii/concepts/modular-creative-and-hook-swaps.md)).
- **Agent default:** a hook swap counts as an iteration, not as diversification. Use it when a body has proven it sells; make the new hook visually distinct (new setting, creator angle, or B-roll). Fill diversity quotas with new formats and concepts. Logged in the course contradictions notes.
- **Frequency (differs):** lesson 100 reads frequency about 2–3 as fatigue and a cue to replace the ad ([L100 01:29](../lessons/100-why-copying-longest-running-is-bad.md)); Tether Lab says frequency ~2 is usually creator or format boredom, so change the product treatment first ([Meta Andromeda](../../../../tools/meta-andromeda.md)). Agent default: check the CPA trend; at ~2 ship a new treatment or format of the same message before killing the concept.
- **Volume (differs):** Caleb's 50 image ads from one prompt, each with 5 primary texts × 5 headlines ([L93 35:46–41:50; 49:31–50:59](../lessons/093-special-guest-recordings-mr-paid-social-guest-talk.md)) vs Evolve's [3:2:2](../../../evolve/start-here/frameworks/campaign-structure-322.md) ad-set unit; default: our account structure follows Evolve, the operator's batch volume feeds it.
