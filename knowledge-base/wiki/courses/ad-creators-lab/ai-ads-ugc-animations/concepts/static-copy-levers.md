# Static copy levers
> The copy moves the static lessons repeat when they rewrite weak Meta statics: climb the **benefit ladder** to the lived outcome, lead **customer-first** not "selfish", add a **"without"** line that removes the cost or pain, attach a **timeframe** to make the result feel likely, write the CTA as a **bridge** from desire to action, show proof instead of stating it, and use dense text (the **brochure effect**) only when the headline has already hooked a felt need.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** writing or rewriting static headlines, subheads, call-outs and CTAs; auditing a competitor static; turning a headline bank into finished ads.
- **Inputs → outputs:** research doc (customer language, desires, objections, moments) + truth sheet (real proof, real offer, real stock and dates) + the static (or template) → rewritten headline, subhead / "without" line, 3–5 benefit call-outs, one proof element, bridge CTA, with each claim mapped to the truth sheet.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** climb the benefit ladder and put level 4–5 in the static, not level 1: "You lose weight" → energy → look better → confident → "You can finally wear the dress that has hung in your wardrobe for months" ([L72 03:25–04:13](../lessons/072-static-ads-mistakes-to-avoid-4-17.md)).
  - **DO** lead customer-first: "Voted the best…" is a "selfish headline"; "nobody actually gives a damn"; say what you do for the buyer, then the credential ([L74 00:00–00:52](../lessons/074-fixing-bad-static-ads-2.md)); answer "What is in it for me?" instead of feature-bombing ([L73 06:25–07:19](../lessons/073-fixing-bad-static-ads-10-51.md)).
  - **DO** add a "without" line that removes the pain or cost: "Get your rent on time" + "Without doing the chasing yourself" ([L73 01:27–01:55](../lessons/073-fixing-bad-static-ads-10-51.md)); "Hear the birds outside without spending $5000" ([L74 00:52](../lessons/074-fixing-bad-static-ads-2.md)).
  - **DO** attach a timeframe when you can substantiate it: "the actual time scales give you a perceived likelihood of achievement" ([L75 01:10; 07:13–07:42](../lessons/075-fixing-bad-static-ads-3.md)); don't use vague dated milestones ("By July 14 you'll feel it") ([L75 00:00–00:44](../lessons/075-fixing-bad-static-ads-3.md)).
  - **DO** write the CTA as a bridge between desire and what the business does: "Skip the stress. Get the rent." not "Ask [brand]" ([L73 01:55](../lessons/073-fixing-bad-static-ads-10-51.md)).
  - **DO** say what nobody else can say: "what can I say that nobody else can say?" ([L74 00:25](../lessons/074-fixing-bad-static-ads-2.md)); a claim "worthy of a remark", e.g. a specific ranking or concentration, only if true ([L72 02:48–03:25](../lessons/072-static-ads-mistakes-to-avoid-4-17.md)).
  - **DO** show, not tell: display the ranking badges instead of "voted best" ([L74 01:18](../lessons/074-fixing-bad-static-ads-2.md)); show the shoe in a puddle and the sole for slip-resistance ([L73 04:35–05:01](../lessons/073-fixing-bad-static-ads-10-51.md)); don't state "sells out fast", show it ([L75 03:56–04:26](../lessons/075-fixing-bad-static-ads-3.md)).
  - **DO** open with what the product is; a product label alone ("Collagen peel off mask") "doesn't create curiosity, it doesn't state a benefit" ([L75 05:39–07:13](../lessons/075-fixing-bad-static-ads-3.md)).
  - **DON'T** pair a price with a pile of features (it associates "cheap") or run price-only ads to cold traffic; price-led statics suit only people who know the brand ([L73 05:56–06:53](../lessons/073-fixing-bad-static-ads-10-51.md); [L72 00:00–00:53](../lessons/072-static-ads-mistakes-to-avoid-4-17.md)).
  - **DON'T** use vague value words: "game changer" is banned at the agency ([L73 09:13–09:40](../lessons/073-fixing-bad-static-ads-10-51.md); [L83 41:20](../lessons/083-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-17.md)); drop "better" ([L83 24:36](../lessons/083-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-17.md); [L84 05:24](../lessons/084-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-21.md)).
  - **IF** the course's "after" example contains testimonials, an expert quote, scarcity or a struck-through price **THEN** copy the lever, not the asset: those are instructor illustrations, several invented ([L74 Sources](../lessons/074-fixing-bad-static-ads-2.md); [L75 Sources](../lessons/075-fixing-bad-static-ads-3.md)); see [AI creator compliance](ai-creator-compliance.md) rows 18, 22, 25.

## Definition
Static copy levers are the named, repeatable changes the course applies to a static's text (headline, subhead, call-outs, CTA, proof) to move it from a feature or price announcement to a desire the scroller already has. Each lever is a mechanism, transferable across products; the before/after ads in lessons 72–75 are teaching illustrations, not proof.

| Lever | What it does | Mechanism named in the lesson | Cite |
|---|---|---|---|
| Benefit ladder | Moves the headline from attribute to lived outcome | Five levels; use 4–5 | [L72 03:25–04:13](../lessons/072-static-ads-mistakes-to-avoid-4-17.md) |
| Customer-first | Puts the buyer's gain before the brand's credential | "Selfish headline" vs care first | [L74 00:00–00:52](../lessons/074-fixing-bad-static-ads-2.md); [L73 06:25–07:19](../lessons/073-fixing-bad-static-ads-10-51.md) |
| "Without" line | Removes the linked pain, effort or cost | Desire + reduced risk | [L73 01:27–01:55](../lessons/073-fixing-bad-static-ads-10-51.md); [L74 00:52](../lessons/074-fixing-bad-static-ads-2.md) |
| Timeframe | Makes the outcome feel achievable | "perceived likelihood of achievement" | [L75 01:10; 07:13–07:42](../lessons/075-fixing-bad-static-ads-3.md) |
| Bridge CTA | Connects desire to the action | "Skip the stress. Get the rent." | [L73 01:55](../lessons/073-fixing-bad-static-ads-10-51.md) |
| Remarkable claim | Something only we can say | "worthy of a remark" | [L72 02:48–03:25](../lessons/072-static-ads-mistakes-to-avoid-4-17.md); [L74 00:25](../lessons/074-fixing-bad-static-ads-2.md) |
| Shown proof | Badge, demo or real quote in the image | Show not tell | [L74 01:18](../lessons/074-fixing-bad-static-ads-2.md); [L73 04:35–05:01](../lessons/073-fixing-bad-static-ads-10-51.md) |
| Open-loop headline | Invites "what?" | "The only garden shoes that do all three" | [L73 04:09–04:35](../lessons/073-fixing-bad-static-ads-10-51.md) |
| Brochure effect | Dense benefit text gets read once the headline hooks a felt need | "If you were really sick and somebody gave you a brochure… would you not read it?" | [L74 07:23](../lessons/074-fixing-bad-static-ads-2.md); [L75 04:26–04:54](../lessons/075-fixing-bad-static-ads-3.md) |
| Relatable line | Target buyer thinks "yeah, I don't want to do that" | "You don't need to send legal bills at the beach" | [L75 02:24–03:27](../lessons/075-fixing-bad-static-ads-3.md) |
| Rule of one thing | One idea per static; a massive call-out needs nothing else | "Cortisol was found dead. Cause of death…" | [L84 01:34–02:04; 05:24](../lessons/084-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-21.md) |

## Why it matters
- Feed users are in "waste time mode", "zombies… not locked in": a static must win an impulsive decision on readability and desire, not design ([L72 01:49–02:48](../lessons/072-static-ads-mistakes-to-avoid-4-17.md)).
- Price- and sale-only statics don't "move the sale forward" to cold traffic ([L72 00:00–00:53](../lessons/072-static-ads-mistakes-to-avoid-4-17.md), instructor claim) and only work for people who already know you ([L73 05:56](../lessons/073-fixing-bad-static-ads-10-51.md)).
- Claims anyone can make ("everyone is going crazy for these") are invisible; specific, checkable ones are remarkable ([L72 02:48–03:25](../lessons/072-static-ads-mistakes-to-avoid-4-17.md)).
- Desire association: a headline must tie the viewer's desire to the offer ([L73 03:39–04:09](../lessons/073-fixing-bad-static-ads-10-51.md)).

## How to use (steps)
The rewrite sequence the instructor repeats ([L73 Key ideas](../lessons/073-fixing-bad-static-ads-10-51.md); [L74 Steps](../lessons/074-fixing-bad-static-ads-2.md)):
1. **Diagnose:** who does the ad talk about (us or them)? Which benefit level is the headline on? Readable in one second on a phone ([L72 Steps](../lessons/072-static-ads-mistakes-to-avoid-4-17.md))?
2. **Desire headline** from customer language, "rather than trying to be some kind of genius copywriter" ([L72 00:53–01:49](../lessons/072-static-ads-mistakes-to-avoid-4-17.md)); level 4–5 of the ladder; say what the product is ([L75 05:39–06:22](../lessons/075-fixing-bad-static-ads-3.md)). The course's headline-bank prompt asks for 6–10 words ([L70 headline-bank prompt](../lessons/070-full-static-ads-creation-process.md); [L76 prompt](../lessons/076-new-make-static-ads-with-ai-poppy-method.md)).
3. **Add the "without" line** (pain, effort, cost removed).
4. **Swap features for benefits** tied to research: answer the top objections buyers actually have ([L74 04:56](../lessons/074-fixing-bad-static-ads-2.md)).
5. **Add one proof element** that is real: shown badge, demo visual, verbatim review with source. Lead with social proof for unfamiliar products ([L74 08:28–08:57](../lessons/074-fixing-bad-static-ads-2.md)).
6. **Timeframe** only if substantiated; flow: desire → timeline → the first question the buyer will have → action ([L75 00:44–01:10](../lessons/075-fixing-bad-static-ads-3.md)).
7. **Bridge CTA**; for a very aware audience, real urgency gets them over the line ([L75 05:39](../lessons/075-fixing-bad-static-ads-3.md)); offer ads still need effort ([L74 02:13–02:42](../lessons/074-fixing-bad-static-ads-2.md)).
8. **Rebuild in a template** so text stays editable ([L71 00:00–00:53](../lessons/071-making-statics-with-ai.md)); one copy set per awareness level ([L70 08:52–09:46](../lessons/070-full-static-ads-creation-process.md)).
9. **Audit claims** with the [claim audit](../../../stefan-georgi/rmbc-ii/rubrics/llm-output-claim-audit.md) before export.

## Common mistakes
- Tiny text, busy backgrounds, low contrast (white on yellow is "a crazy combination") ([L72 00:00; 01:49–02:48](../lessons/072-static-ads-mistakes-to-avoid-4-17.md)).
- First-level benefits ("Bye bye baggy eyes") ([L72 00:00–00:53](../lessons/072-static-ads-mistakes-to-avoid-4-17.md)).
- "Cringy" sale headlines ("Destroy food cravings, destroy your savings") ([L72 00:53–01:49](../lessons/072-static-ads-mistakes-to-avoid-4-17.md)).
- Relying on a cultural reference alone ("Float like a butterfly"): only those who get it respond ([L74 03:58](../lessons/074-fixing-bad-static-ads-2.md)).
- Copying the back of the box ([L74 05:25–06:44](../lessons/074-fixing-bad-static-ads-2.md)).
- Treating the timeframe lever as licence for health promises: the course's own example "Melt your belly fat in four weeks" is an unsubstantiated weight-loss claim ([L75 Sources](../lessons/075-fixing-bad-static-ads-3.md)).
- Letting the GPT invent benefits or review counts for the call-outs ([L71 Agent use](../lessons/071-making-statics-with-ai.md)).
- Reusing one headline template across unrelated topics ([L83 49:16](../lessons/083-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-17.md)).
