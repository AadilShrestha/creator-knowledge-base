# Hook schema and open loops
> Hao's model: the hook is the ad's **title**; without it the viewer cannot file the rest of the ad into a mental folder (schema), so build it from the avatar's own words, experiences and visuals. Then keep the viewer with **open loops** (unresolved questions), make the pain concrete by **twisting the knife** on failed solutions, use **juxtaposition** ("how to [desire] without [pain]"), and lead with **indirect** benefits (what the result lets them do or feel) rather than the direct one.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** writing or auditing the first 3–11 seconds of a video ad, a static headline, a song hook; restructuring a flat problem-agitate-solution script; diagnosing "great hook rate, weak sales" or "hook attracts window shoppers".
- **Inputs → outputs:** avatar (age, identity, beliefs they repeat), raw customer language (Reddit, reviews, comments), the desire and its linked pain, failed solutions they tried, the mechanism → 3–5 hook candidates labelled by type (schema title, dissonance, juxtaposition, indirect desire, twist-the-knife), each with its visual, the loop it opens and the line where that loop closes.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** treat the hook as the title: "Every ad you write is that paragraph. The only question is whether the customer already has a title." (Hao, [L86 11:21](../lessons/086-ad-psychology-with-howie-2026-08-12.md)); build it from four elements, **avatar, angle, sound, visual**, because "Your entire hook will just determine your entire product positioning" ([L86 13:06–13:39](../lessons/086-ad-psychology-with-howie-2026-08-12.md)).
  - **DO** use the avatar's own words, experiences and stereotypes so the hook is easy to process; easy processing "feels true, more familiar, and more likable" ([L86 22:53](../lessons/086-ad-psychology-with-howie-2026-08-12.md)); lift Reddit lines 1:1 ([L80 09:06–10:43](../lessons/080-supplement-brand-70k-winning-ad-breakdown.md)); use customer words, not clinical terms ([L83 12:46](../lessons/083-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-17.md)).
  - **DON'T** argue against a belief the avatar holds as identity: "you never want to argue against a belief" ([L86 20:52–21:50](../lessons/086-ad-psychology-with-howie-2026-08-12.md)); reframe it or move the blame elsewhere ([L86 49:48–50:18](../lessons/086-ad-psychology-with-howie-2026-08-12.md)).
  - **DO** open a loop, not a statement: "saying you have a weak pelvic floor is not an open loop" ([L83 16:30](../lessons/083-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-17.md)); **IF** a hook is a statement **THEN** turn it into a curiosity question or a reveal ([L83 19:54](../lessons/083-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-17.md)).
  - **DO** stack loops and hold reveals: hook → clarify the emotion → loop → loop → payoff → CTA; withhold the product name and category until late ([L105 00:54–02:45](../lessons/105-make-ugc-scripts-that-scale.md), instructor claim); keep adding loops through the body ([L82 09:16](../lessons/082-mens-skincare-135k-spend-ad-breakdown.md)). Close every loop you open before the CTA (derived from the Evolve rule below).
  - **DO** twist the knife: several lines, each showing why a different substitute fails, before the product appears ([L79 09:27](../lessons/079-intimacy-brand-200k-at-2-3-roas.md); [L83 40:27](../lessons/083-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-17.md)).
  - **DO** write juxtaposition as "how to [desire] without [linked pain]" ("How to look juiced without prison time"); Justin says it "works almost all of the time" ([L80 07:28–08:22](../lessons/080-supplement-brand-70k-winning-ad-breakdown.md), instructor claim); or two things that do not go together (nightly wine + up at 6 a.m. making focaccia, [L78 11:37](../lessons/078-wine-brand-125k-at-2-24-roas.md)).
  - **DON'T** confuse relatable with desired: "What's relatable is pain and loss"; desire is the outcome ([L86 34:42–35:40](../lessons/086-ad-psychology-with-howie-2026-08-12.md)). Hook on the relatable, pay off on the desire.
  - **DO** prefer indirect benefits in the hook (confidence, looking good on the beach, the armchair not digging in) over the direct one ("lasting longer", "lose weight") ([L86 1:02:24–1:03:24](../lessons/086-ad-psychology-with-howie-2026-08-12.md); [L88 Agent use](../lessons/088-ad-psychology-with-howie-2026-07-29.md)).
  - **DO** make hook words and hook visuals agree ("compound" → show a compound, not a bottle) and segment with specific words ("growth factor", "after 40") ([L81 04:58–07:36](../lessons/081-turning-400-ad-to-13k-spender.md)).
  - **IF** a hook attracts window shoppers (high CPC, low buyer quality) **THEN** narrow it with a visual or verbal cue only the buyer recognises ([L86 1:04:21–1:05:17](../lessons/086-ad-psychology-with-howie-2026-08-12.md)).
  - **DON'T** fake danger or guilt to open a loop: Hao calls his own guilt-trip example "not very ethical" ([L86 46:15](../lessons/086-ad-psychology-with-howie-2026-08-12.md)); see [AI creator compliance](ai-creator-compliance.md) row 31.

## Definition
- **Schema** (Hao citing Bartlett, 1936): "a mental framework that organizes what you already know"; the brain files new information against old to save energy ([L86 19:54](../lessons/086-ad-psychology-with-howie-2026-08-12.md)). Schemas include language, experiences, senses, identity and stereotypes ([L86 38:41; 45:44; 52:19](../lessons/086-ad-psychology-with-howie-2026-08-12.md)).
- **Hook as title** (Hao citing Bransford and Johnson, 1972): with full context before a text, comprehension and recall "nearly tripled" in his reading of the study; context after barely helped ([L86 10:49–11:21](../lessons/086-ad-psychology-with-howie-2026-08-12.md)). The hook supplies that context.
- **Open loop:** an unresolved thing the viewer needs closed ([L83 16:30](../lessons/083-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-17.md)); in Howie's breakdown, nested dissonant statements each ending on a new question ([L82 03:29–04:25](../lessons/082-mens-skincare-135k-spend-ad-breakdown.md)).
- **Twisting the knife:** building pain before the product by naming why previous solutions failed in the viewer's situation ([L83 40:27](../lessons/083-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-17.md); [L79 09:27](../lessons/079-intimacy-brand-200k-at-2-3-roas.md)).
- **Juxtaposition:** two things that normally don't go together; the quick form is "desire without pain" ([L80 08:22](../lessons/080-supplement-brand-70k-winning-ad-breakdown.md); [L78 11:37](../lessons/078-wine-brand-125k-at-2-24-roas.md)).
- **Indirect vs direct benefit:** direct = the product's literal result; indirect = what that result lets the buyer be or do (ego, confidence, the beach, the photo) ([L86 1:02:24–1:03:24](../lessons/086-ad-psychology-with-howie-2026-08-12.md)).

## Why it matters
- A viewer without a title scrolls: in Hao's laundry exercise one attendee out of the group guessed the paragraph without its title ([L86 10:11](../lessons/086-ad-psychology-with-howie-2026-08-12.md)).
- The hook sets positioning for the whole ad: the EMF blocker reads as "protecting family from radiation" because the hook's angle is "Protect your family"; Hao's proposed swap to "protect yourself from EMF" would read as "another conspiracy. Scroll" ([L86 14:29–16:34; 22:21–22:53](../lessons/086-ad-psychology-with-howie-2026-08-12.md)).
- The hook alone moved spend from about $477 to about $13.6K on the same body ([L81 07:36](../lessons/081-turning-400-ad-to-13k-spender.md), instructor-reported).
- Loops filter for the buyer: a steep retention drop then a flat tail means the hook filtered; viewers past the elbow are the qualified ones ([L82 01:02–01:58](../lessons/082-mens-skincare-135k-spend-ad-breakdown.md)); open loops filter for the ICP because only they get the references (088 page summary).

## How to use (steps)
1. **Pick the schema.** From research, list the avatar's repeated beliefs, their words for the problem, the experiences they'd nod at ("I tried that", [L84 10:52](../lessons/084-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-21.md)), and the generation's format schema ([L86 31:10–33:12](../lessons/086-ad-psychology-with-howie-2026-08-12.md)).
2. **Write the title (four elements).** Avatar (who is called out), angle (the frame), sound (a recognisable sound or voice), visual (what the first frame shows). Check the angle, not the product, carries positioning ([L86 13:06–13:39](../lessons/086-ad-psychology-with-howie-2026-08-12.md)).
3. **Choose the opening move** (table below) to fit awareness: unaware → statistic or question exposing a hidden pain, no product; problem aware → name the problem clearly, no pitch; solution aware → unique mechanism ([L95 00:43–01:38](../lessons/095-market-awareness-explained-in-180s.md)).
4. **Open the first loop in the first seconds** and write where it closes. Add a second dissonant line or question if the viewer can already guess the topic: "human beings are really smart" ([L105 02:45](../lessons/105-make-ugc-scripts-that-scale.md)).
5. **Twist the knife** for 2–4 lines on failed substitutes in the customer's words ([L79 09:27](../lessons/079-intimacy-brand-200k-at-2-3-roas.md)).
6. **Bridge to the mechanism** with juxtaposition: "[Alternative] isn't worth it because there's a [risk-free adjective] [mechanism noun] that [delivers desire] without [pain 1] or [pain 2]" (template, [L80 Prompts](../lessons/080-supplement-brand-70k-winning-ad-breakdown.md)); substantiate every word of it.
7. **Pay off with the indirect desire in a visual story**: "she leaned in, grabbed my collar and goes, 'what are you wearing? You smell insane.'" ([L82 07:18–08:18](../lessons/082-mens-skincare-135k-spend-ad-breakdown.md)); then the product, then CTA.
8. **Audit:** every loop closed? hook words = hook visual? any claim in the hook on the truth sheet? Run the [claim audit](../../../stefan-georgi/rmbc-ii/rubrics/llm-output-claim-audit.md).

## Common mistakes
- Writing the hook as the editor's on-screen text only; for strategy it is the first sentence and how you call out the customer ([L86 12:35–13:06](../lessons/086-ad-psychology-with-howie-2026-08-12.md)).
- Opening loops you never close; viewers feel cheated and the CTA lands on confusion (derived; see Evolve rule below).
- Claiming neuroscience to justify loops: Howie's dopamine and "five to seven pieces of information" statements are his theories ("I haven't hooked someone up to a brain scan yet") ([L82 05:24–06:22](../lessons/082-mens-skincare-135k-spend-ad-breakdown.md)).
- Hook text that references a template headline from another video ("flat stomach" on a keto video) ([L83 49:16](../lessons/083-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-17.md)).
- A hook rate win that brings the wrong people ([L80 04:11](../lessons/080-supplement-brand-70k-winning-ad-breakdown.md); [L86 1:04:21](../lessons/086-ad-psychology-with-howie-2026-08-12.md)).
- Unrelatable stunt comparisons ("his electric toothbrush is lasting longer than him") ([L83 18:22](../lessons/083-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-17.md)).
- Copying the Vault scripts' lines: "You have to take the actual principles… the belief shifts" ([L79 10:54–11:33](../lessons/079-intimacy-brand-200k-at-2-3-roas.md)).
