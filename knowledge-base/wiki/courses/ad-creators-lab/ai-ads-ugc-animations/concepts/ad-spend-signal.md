# Ad spend signal
> To pick competitor ads worth tearing down, filter by how much money was put behind them (minimum spend, duplicates across campaigns), not by how long they have run: long-running ads are often bottom-of-funnel retargeting fed by other ads, or $1-a-day decoys. Run time becomes a fair signal only inside your own account's data, where you can see performance next to it.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** building a swipe list of competitor or adjacent-niche ads before a teardown; choosing reference ads for a static or video batch; deciding which of our own ads to iterate.
- **Inputs → outputs:** niche keyword + 2–3 adjacent niches + a spend-data tool (or our own ad account data) → a shortlist of ads with spend, days running, duplicate count, awareness stage and funnel position, each tagged with the mechanism to extract (never the asset).
- **Rules:**
  - **DON'T** pick ads by days running: "that is the worst thing that you can do" ([L100 00:00](../lessons/100-why-copying-longest-running-is-bad.md), instructor claim); a long-running ad "typically means that they are actually bottom of the funnel ads", fed by a top-of-funnel ad that brings the traffic ([L100 00:00–00:45](../lessons/100-why-copying-longest-running-is-bad.md)).
  - **DON'T** trust run time as proof: some brands run $1-a-day ads "to fool you into thinking that they are winning ads" ([L100 02:08](../lessons/100-why-copying-longest-running-is-bad.md), instructor claim).
  - **DO** use ad spend as "the best metric to know what a good ad is" ([L100 02:08](../lessons/100-why-copying-longest-running-is-bad.md)): Trendtrack → Trending Ads → Ad Spend minimum $10,000, Days running minimum 14, sort by Most Duplicates ([L101 01:34–02:20](../lessons/101-how-to-find-ads-based-on-ad-spend.md)).
  - **DO** prefer ads that bring in new customers (unaware or problem-aware): "you can only scale ads that are bringing in new customers" ([L100 00:45](../lessons/100-why-copying-longest-running-is-bad.md)).
  - **DO** search adjacent niches, not only your exact niche (travel supplements → other supplements, nootropics, fitness) ([L101 02:20](../lessons/101-how-to-find-ads-based-on-ad-spend.md); [L70 05:16–06:02](../lessons/070-full-static-ads-creation-process.md)).
  - **DON'T** expect US coverage from this tool: "it only works for EU and UK ads" ([L101 00:00](../lessons/101-how-to-find-ads-based-on-ad-spend.md), at recording).
  - **IF** you are reading your own account (or a client account in Adnova) **THEN** "lasted the longest and performed the best" is a valid filter because performance sits next to run time ([L102 00:57](../lessons/102-find-winning-concepts-with-adnova.md); derived reconciliation with [L100](../lessons/100-why-copying-longest-running-is-bad.md)).
  - **DO** start iteration from your own account's winners before copying the biggest competitors (David passing on Camilo's advice, [L91 46:51–47:52](../lessons/091-special-guest-recordings-ai-office-hours-david.md)).
  - **DON'T** copy what you find: "the fastest way for you to make zero sales and have high CPAs, even if they run for years" ([L69 00:00](../lessons/069-static-ads-intro.md), instructor claim); extract the mechanism ([AI creator compliance](ai-creator-compliance.md) rows 32–33).

## Definition
**Ad spend signal** = using the estimated money an advertiser has put behind a creative (total spend above a floor, daily spend trend, number of duplicates across campaigns) as the primary evidence that the ad works for them. It replaces **run time** (days live in an ad library) as the sort key for competitor research. Spend shows the advertiser's confidence with real money; duplicates show they scaled it into "many campaigns many many times" ([L101 02:20](../lessons/101-how-to-find-ads-based-on-ad-spend.md)).

## Why it matters
- **Run time measures the wrong thing.** A retargeting ad can run for months on traffic another ad brings in; copying it gives you "a bunch of bottom of funnel type of creative" with nothing that brings in new customers ([L100 00:45](../lessons/100-why-copying-longest-running-is-bad.md)).
- **Run time can be gamed.** $1-a-day decoys "since like 2015", used by big brands to hide what works ([L100 02:08](../lessons/100-why-copying-longest-running-is-bad.md), instructor claim based on "a lot of these ad accounts").
- **Scale comes from top-of-funnel.** A very-unaware or problem-aware ad grows purchases until it plateaus; at frequency about 2–3 fatigue hits, so brands need new top-of-funnel creative to "find a new peak and a new peak" ([L100 00:45–02:08](../lessons/100-why-copying-longest-running-is-bad.md), instructor claim). Those are the ads worth studying, and they are the ones that spend.

## How to use (steps)
1. **Set the search.** Trendtrack → Trending Ads ([L101 00:00](../lessons/101-how-to-find-ads-based-on-ad-spend.md)). Filters at recording: Ad Spend min $10,000; Days running min 14 (a later frame shows 30–180); Format Video for video research; CPM filter default $9 ([L101 01:34; frames 02:00, 02:49](../lessons/101-how-to-find-ads-based-on-ad-spend.md)).
2. **Sort by Most Duplicates** ("93 duplicates" on one ad) ([L101 02:20](../lessons/101-how-to-find-ads-based-on-ad-spend.md)).
3. **Open the EU and UK statistics** per ad for spend and daily spend over recent days ([L101 01:34](../lessons/101-how-to-find-ads-based-on-ad-spend.md)).
4. **Repeat with keywords and adjacent niches** (advanced ad search, e.g. "sleep") ([L101 02:20–03:29](../lessons/101-how-to-find-ads-based-on-ad-spend.md)); target about 10 competitor ideas + 10 adjacent-niche ideas and stop at one hour of research ([L70 06:02](../lessons/070-full-static-ads-creation-process.md)).
5. **Label each ad**: awareness stage ([L95 00:43–02:29](../lessons/095-market-awareness-explained-in-180s.md)), funnel position (does it bring new customers or convert warm ones?), format, hook, mechanism.
6. **Save** to a board, pin, follow the brand in Brand Tracker, or open the transcript ([L101 02:20](../lessons/101-how-to-find-ads-based-on-ad-spend.md)).
7. **Inside your own account** (or a client's in Adnova's Detective): read winning visual and audio hooks and recent angles by window (7d–365d), watch the best ads for the current angle, and remix ([L102 00:00–00:57](../lessons/102-find-winning-concepts-with-adnova.md)).
8. **Tear down and rebuild** with the [competitor ad teardown](../../../../playbooks/competitor-ad-teardown.md); write our own script and visuals.

## Common mistakes
- Sorting by "longest running" because the tool defaults to it ([L100 00:45](../lessons/100-why-copying-longest-running-is-bad.md)); the course's own Poppy statics lesson does exactly this for references ([L76 01:50–02:48](../lessons/076-new-make-static-ads-with-ai-poppy-method.md)); see cross-course notes.
- Reading spend as profitability. Spend shows the advertiser's confidence, not their ROAS or margin; big brands at the filter may be buying awareness (derived from [L101 02:20](../lessons/101-how-to-find-ads-based-on-ad-spend.md)).
- Ignoring the EU/UK limitation: the shortlist over-represents brands advertising there ([L101 00:00](../lessons/101-how-to-find-ads-based-on-ad-spend.md)).
- Copying the exact niche's top spender instead of looking at adjacent niches ([L101 02:20](../lessons/101-how-to-find-ads-based-on-ad-spend.md)).
- Copying the asset: run the [compliance gate](ai-creator-compliance.md) on every reference.
- Treating the 70/20/10 "steal" ratio ([L70 03:41](../lessons/070-full-static-ads-creation-process.md)) as licence to clone; "steal" means proven mechanisms, not lines or visuals.

## Cross-course notes
- **Tether Lab (shared, agrees):** "running for 195 days" isn't proof; run time scores +1 at most for large brands; combine with variant count, cross-platform presence and repeat spend ([competitor ad teardown](../../../../playbooks/competitor-ad-teardown.md); [KB contradictions row 9](../../../../contradictions.md)).
- **Evolve Start Here (differs):** sorts by longest running as the strongest signal for live-market discovery ([research stack workflow](../../../evolve/start-here/frameworks/research-stack-workflow.md)) but picks winners to tear down by shares in a recent 3–6 month window ([learnings review](../../../evolve/start-here/frameworks/learnings-review.md); [Evolve contradictions row 2](../../../evolve/start-here/contradictions.md)).
- **Within this course (differs):** lesson 100 bans run time; lesson 76 imports references sorted by "Longest running" ([L76 01:50–02:48](../lessons/076-new-make-static-ads-with-ai-poppy-method.md)); lesson 102 uses "lasted the longest and performed the best" inside own-account data ([L102 00:57](../lessons/102-find-winning-concepts-with-adnova.md)).
- **Agent default:** rank external ads by spend floor + duplicates where spend data exists (EU/UK); where it doesn't (US-only brands), use run time only combined with duplicates/variant count, cross-platform presence and a top-of-funnel check, and never as the sole criterion. Inside our own account, rank by performance (spend at or above break-even), then run time. Logged in the course contradictions notes.
- **Evolve Copywriting:** [market awareness](../../../evolve/copywriting/concepts/market-awareness.md) gives the stage labels used in step 5; lesson 95 assigns the unique mechanism to solution aware and comparison to product aware ([L95 01:38–02:29](../lessons/095-market-awareness-explained-in-180s.md)), while Evolve compares solution types at solution aware and puts positioning and new mechanisms at product aware. Agent default: follow Evolve's table for stage tactics; use lesson 95 only for its unaware tactic (statistics and questions that expose a hidden pain).
