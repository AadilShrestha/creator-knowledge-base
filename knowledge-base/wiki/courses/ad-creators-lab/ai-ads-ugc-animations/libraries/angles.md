# Angles library (Ad Creators Lab)
> Every angle in the Ad Creators Lab lesson pages: the 9 harvested angle rows plus angles that the winning-ad breakdowns, Hao's psychology calls and the AI UGC demos name or clearly use. Rows are grouped by angle family, tagged by format, niche and awareness, and cited.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** you are choosing what an ad argues before writing hooks, turning research into an angle bank, repositioning a product in a saturated niche, or naming the angle of a competitor ad.
- **Inputs → outputs:** product facts, real customer language, awareness stage → pick a family below → 2–4 precedent angles → our version, with the example line rewritten from our reviews and Reddit language → hooks from [hooks](hooks.md).
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** position the same product through the avatar's primal motive, not the feature: "protect your family" beat a direct "protect yourself from EMF", which reads as "another conspiracy. Scroll". [L86 14:29–16:34; 22:21–22:53](../lessons/086-ad-psychology-with-howie-2026-08-12.md)
  - **DO** in a saturated, sophisticated market, be the first to *show* the effect or say the process, even if competitors could too (instructor claims: 1983 battery ad, Camel "toasted"). [L87 01:30–06:32; 51:39–52:35](../lessons/087-ad-psychology-with-howie-2026-08-27.md)
  - **DO** shift the viewer's belief about their current fix in the first lines ("my towel doesn't do the job, it's gross"). [L79 10:24](../lessons/079-intimacy-brand-200k-at-2-3-roas.md)
  - **DO** move from the ingredient to "most brands do X wrong" before positioning the product, so the failed past solution handles the objection. [L91 33:57](../lessons/091-special-guest-recordings-ai-office-hours-david.md)
  - **DO** reuse a winning ad's foundations (wide hook, humour, style, couple dynamic) for the opposite demographic, but redo the Reddit research for the new avatar. [L91 34:57–37:51](../lessons/091-special-guest-recordings-ai-office-hours-david.md)
  - **DO** mix indirect benefits (confidence, ego, photos) into the hook instead of only the direct benefit. [L86 1:02:24](../lessons/086-ad-psychology-with-howie-2026-08-12.md)
  - **DON'T** build an angle on a drug comparison, a health or toxicity claim, or an invented personal story. Rows flagged ⛔/⚠️ keep their structure only. Run every draft through the [LLM claim audit](../../../stefan-georgi/rmbc-ii/rubrics/llm-output-claim-audit.md).
  - **IF** you find an angle in a spend-ranked competitor ad **THEN** keep the structure and change topic and visuals (warning-then-fix remix). [L102 00:57](../lessons/102-find-winning-concepts-with-adnova.md)
- **See also:** [Hooks](hooks.md) · [Ad teardowns](ad-teardowns.md) · [Rewrites](rewrites.md) · [Rules](rules.md) · [Research document protocol](../frameworks/research-document-protocol.md) · [Ad-spend signal](../concepts/ad-spend-signal.md) · [Static ads process](../frameworks/static-ads-process.md) · Evolve [avatars and angles](../../../evolve/copywriting/concepts/avatars-and-angles.md) and [unique mechanism](../../../evolve/copywriting/concepts/unique-mechanism.md) · Evolve Start Here [angles](../../../evolve/start-here/libraries/angles.md) · Shared [angles library](../../../../libraries/angles.md)

## ⚠️ Copy the mechanism, not the asset
Angles come from client ads (Gratsi wine, Love Blanket, Primal Muscle, Penrose), member ads and course demos. Keep the family and structure; write our own line from our customers' words. Spend, ROAS and "worked" notes are instructor or doc claims. The historical cases in §E are the instructor's retelling with no source.

## Tags
Flags: ⛔ don't execute (reuse structure only) · ⚠️ unsupported claim. Aware: **U** unaware, **PA** problem aware, **SA** solution aware, **PrA** product aware, **MA** most aware; "(d)" = derived; "—" = unclear.

## Cross-course notes
- **Evolve:** angle = desire × avatar; sub-avatar call-outs. Hao's repositioning (§D) is the same move expressed as "new title for the same product". See Evolve [avatars and angles](../../../evolve/copywriting/concepts/avatars-and-angles.md).
- **Evolve vs L087:** Hao says to "find the zone that works and stick with it"; Tether's emotional-stagnation rule says to rotate zones. Default: keep the winning zone for the core concept and test the opposite zone as a new concept (see [rules](rules.md) conflicts).
