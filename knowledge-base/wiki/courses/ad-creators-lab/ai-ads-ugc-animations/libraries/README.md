# Libraries hub (Ad Creators Lab)
> The lookup layer for the Ad Creators Lab course: hooks, angles, ad teardowns, rewrites, rules, an index of every prompt and Claude skill, and every tool setting stated in the 110 lessons. Rows are de-duplicated, tagged by format, niche and awareness stage, flagged for compliance, and cited to lesson and timestamp.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** you need a precedent, rule, prompt or setting inside a production step: "what hook types work for an unaware supplement crochet ad?", "how did the course fix a feature-list static?", "which Claude skill writes skeleton scripts?", "what duration and resolution did Seedance 2.0 AI UGC use?"
- **Inputs → outputs:** the job ([table below](#which-library-for-which-job)) → the library → filter by section, ID prefix, niche or format → a mechanism to remix, a rule to apply, a prompt location, or a dated setting. Then check the output against [rules §17 (compliance)](rules.md) and the repo rules in [AGENTS.md](../../../../../../AGENTS.md).
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** start full workflows from the course [playbooks](../playbooks/README.md) and frameworks ([produce an AI UGC ad](../playbooks/produce-an-ai-ugc-ad.md), [produce an animated AI ad](../playbooks/produce-an-animated-ai-ad.md), [make AI static ads](../playbooks/make-ai-static-ads.md), [research to AI ad concepts](../playbooks/research-to-ai-ad-concepts.md)). Come here for lookups inside a step.
  - **DO** take model names, durations, resolutions, credits and prices only from [tool-settings](tool-settings.md), which dates them "at recording (Aug–Sep 2026)" and lists conflicting values side by side. Re-check the live tool before budgeting.
  - **DO** open the lesson link and check the timestamp before quoting an instructor or citing a result.
  - **DON'T** execute anything marked ⛔: stranger photos as AI creators, cloning a real person's voice or likeness, face-swapping other brands' ads, AI people presented as real customers, doctors or founders, fake comments, invented urgency or testimonials, drug-equivalence and other unsupported health claims, competitor names, 1:1 competitor copies. [Rules ⛔ index](rules.md) gives each compliant alternative; see [ai-creator-compliance](../concepts/ai-creator-compliance.md).
  - **DON'T** treat spend, ROAS or "this worked" notes as evidence unless the row says "doc"; they are instructor claims from single accounts. [L081](../lessons/081-turning-400-ad-to-13k-spender.md) and [L079](../lessons/079-intimacy-brand-200k-at-2-3-roas.md) are the only lessons that show doc figures.
  - **IF** a row carries ⚠️ **THEN** keep the structure, bracket the claim, and run the [LLM claim audit](../../../stefan-georgi/rmbc-ii/rubrics/llm-output-claim-audit.md) before anything ships.
- **See also:** [Course concepts](../concepts/ai-ad-format-catalog.md) · [Frameworks](../frameworks/winning-ad-checklist.md) · [Rubrics](../rubrics/README.md) · Evolve Start Here [libraries hub](../../../evolve/start-here/libraries/README.md) · Evolve Copywriting [libraries hub](../../../evolve/copywriting/libraries/README.md) · Shared [libraries hub](../../../../libraries/README.md) · [write-ugc-script skill](../../../../../../.claude/skills/write-ugc-script/SKILL.md)

## ⚠️ Copy the mechanism, not the asset
The course teaches with client ads (Gratsi, Love Blanket, Primal Muscle, Penrose), member ads and its own demos, and many demos break repo rules: AI women voicing invented spouse stories, face-swapped competitor ads, a cloned YouTuber voice, invented dentist quotes and "last 200 pairs" scarcity, steroid comparisons for an IGF-1 spray. The libraries record these as course content with ⛔ and give the compliant version. Reuse structures, never lines, characters, claims or brand names.

## Flags used in every library
| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ⛔ | Course tactic that breaks a repo rule. Don't execute; the row gives the compliant alternative. |
| ⚠️ | The row rests on an unsupported claim, stat or number. Structure only; bracket and substantiate. |
| (instructor claim) | Stated by the instructor without data in the lesson. |
| (derived) / (d) | Inferred by the lesson writer or this page, not stated by the source. |
| U / PA / SA / PrA / MA | Awareness: unaware, problem, solution, product, most aware. |
| *demo* | Ad built by the course for teaching, not a market-tested ad. |
