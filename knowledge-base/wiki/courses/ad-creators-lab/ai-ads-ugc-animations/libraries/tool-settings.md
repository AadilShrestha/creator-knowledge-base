# Tool settings library (Ad Creators Lab)
> Every model, setting, duration, resolution, aspect ratio, credit cost, limit and price the course states, one row per fact, cited to lesson and timestamp, with conflicting values side by side and an agent default.

**All values are "at recording (Aug–Sep 2026)" unless the row says otherwise. Tool facts date fast: models, credit prices and limits change monthly. Several videos were recorded earlier than the course release (L018 screen clock "6 May"; L062 "as of today, March the 6th"; L071 "we're in 2025").** Check the tool's current UI before you quote a number in a brief.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** setting up a generation job (model, mode, duration, resolution, aspect ratio), estimating credits or cost for a batch, configuring a voice, song or caption tool, grading AI footage in an editor, or pricing editors and creators.
- **Inputs → outputs:** the job (AI UGC talking clip, B-roll, animated start/end clip, still, voice, song, edit) → the tool section → the course's setting with its cite → a job spec. Where values conflict, use the [agent default](#conflicts-and-agent-defaults).
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** test cheap and short first, then upgrade: Seedance 1.5 Pro before Kling 3.0 Pro ([L056 05:07](../lessons/056-claymation-generating-videos-transitions.md)), Seedream 4.5 for first stills ([L038 01:52](../lessons/038-skeleton-generating-images.md)), 5 s tests before full lines ([L018 05:08](../lessons/018-realistic-ai-podcasts-tutorial.md)).
  - **DO** split scripts into segments no longer than the model's maximum: Seedance 2.0 ≤15 s per clip ([L020 02:35](../lessons/020-claude-skill-seedance-2-0-ai-ugc.md)).
  - **DO** generate AI UGC at 720p and 9:16 ("We don't want it to look too high definition") and degrade the look in post ([L020 05:59](../lessons/020-claude-skill-seedance-2-0-ai-ugc.md); [L012](../lessons/012-make-ai-look-less-fake.md)).
  - **DO** estimate batch cost before running it and get approval ([L093 37:15](../lessons/093-special-guest-recordings-mr-paid-social-guest-talk.md)).
  - **DON'T** upload human faces as reference images to Sora 2 Pro or Arcads; they get flagged ([L025](../lessons/025-realistic-ai-ugc-rizz-model-generating-realistic-hook.md#important-rule-from-the-lesson-page); [L033](../lessons/033-sora-2-pro-hooks.md#lesson-page-rule); [L029 00:00](../lessons/029-make-ai-creators-talk.md)).
  - **DON'T** compare credit numbers across platforms or lessons; credit scales differ (see [Conflicts](#conflicts-and-agent-defaults)).
  - **DON'T** treat affiliate links or discount codes in the lessons (Max Fusion `?via=camilo`, Trendtrack and Adnova codes) as recommendations; they are sponsored ([L020](../lessons/020-claude-skill-seedance-2-0-ai-ugc.md), [L101](../lessons/101-how-to-find-ads-based-on-ad-spend.md), [L102](../lessons/102-find-winning-concepts-with-adnova.md)).
  - **IF** a quality claim ("Kling 3.0 Pro is the best", "Google Omni is taking the crown") drives your choice **THEN** treat it as one instructor test on one format and run your own 2–3 model comparison.
- **See also:** [prompts](prompts.md) · [README](README.md) · [rules](rules.md) · [ai-post-production](../frameworks/ai-post-production.md) · [start/end-frame video](../concepts/start-end-frame-video.md) · [ai-realism-tells](../concepts/ai-realism-tells.md) · shared tools: [AI UGC tools (Arcads)](../../../../tools/ai-ugc-tools.md) · [ElevenLabs](../../../../tools/elevenlabs.md) · [ChatGPT](../../../../tools/chatgpt.md) · [Claude](../../../../tools/claude-ai.md) · [Canva](../../../../tools/canva.md) · [Meta Andromeda](../../../../tools/meta-andromeda.md) · Evolve [KPI thresholds](../../../evolve/start-here/libraries/kpi-thresholds.md) (ad metrics; this page holds tool facts only)
