# Rules library (Ad Creators Lab)

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** you are making a decision inside an AI ad job (research, hook, script, character and reference setup, image and video generation, voice, edit, static build, test reading, creator brief, Claude skill or Claude Code run) and need the course's rule, or you are QA-ing a script, prompt, cut or plan against it.
- **Inputs → outputs:** the job you're on → the section in [sections and counts](#sections-and-counts) → grep a keyword (`Seedance`, `start frame`, `Voice Design`, `open loop`, `outbound CTR`, `Posterize`) → the rule and its citations → apply it after checking [conflicts](#conflicts-at-a-glance) and the [⛔ index](#-dont-execute-index).
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** cite the rule ID (e.g. "R157") in briefs and reviews, and open the lesson link before quoting an instructor or a result.
  - **DO** treat [§17 Compliance](#17-compliance-and-dont-execute-co) as hard constraints: they override any course tactic in §1–16.
  - **DO** take exact models, durations, resolutions and credit costs from [tool-settings](tool-settings.md); they are "at recording (Aug–Sep 2026)" and date fast. Rules here give the logic.
  - **DON'T** read "(instructor claim)" results, spend figures or ROAS as evidence. "(derived)" = the lesson page's inference, not the speaker's words. ⚠️ = the rule rests on an unsupported claim or number.
  - **IF** two rules disagree **THEN** use the agent default in [conflicts at a glance](#conflicts-at-a-glance).
  - **IF** a rule involves a script, claim or proof **THEN** run the draft through the [LLM output claim audit](../../../stefan-georgi/rmbc-ii/rubrics/llm-output-claim-audit.md) before it ships.

## ⚠️ Copy the mechanism, not the asset
Rules describe how the course makes ads. Its example ads, scripts, member ads, Vault breakdowns, competitor statics and swipe files belong to other brands and often carry invented proof (AI "customers", fake comments, invented stories and urgency) or unsupported health and results claims (GLP-1, IGF-1, testosterone, hangover, seed oils, "$1,000/day"). Reuse the structure (hook type, beat order, shot logic, prompt anatomy), never the lines, characters, faces, voices or claims. Where the course itself does the non-compliant thing, the rule is marked ⛔ and carries "→ compliant:".

### Brief and shoot rules
- **R326** **DO** brief framing: face or main action centred, no extreme close-ups ("this really limits our options"), product held near the face without covering it, whole action in frame (switch to 0.5x or wide if needed), test framing in selfie mode first.
