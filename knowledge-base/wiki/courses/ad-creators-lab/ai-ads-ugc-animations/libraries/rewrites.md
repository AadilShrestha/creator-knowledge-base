# Rewrites library (Ad Creators Lab)
> All 62 before → after rewrites in the course, grouped by what was rewritten (static, script and hook, video edit, funnel and offer), each tagged with the move made and a compliance flag. Several course "after" versions add invented urgency, testimonials or an expert quote; those rows show the compliant version.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** you are fixing a weak static headline or call-out set, tightening a UGC or AI script, revising an AI video edit after review, or explaining to a human why a revision is better.
- **Inputs → outputs:** our draft line, shot or layout → find the row with the same failure in "Before" → apply the move in "What changed" with our real benefits and proof → a revised line, plus the move name for the change log.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** climb the benefit ladder: attribute → lived, specific, timed outcome ("Enhanced energy" → "No longer tired after 2 pm"). [L72 03:25–04:13](../lessons/072-static-ads-mistakes-to-avoid-4-17.md)
  - **DO** write the headline as the buyer's desire, add a "without [pain]" sub-line and a CTA that bridges desire to outcome ("Get your rent on time" / "Without doing the chasing yourself" / "Skip the stress. Get the rent."). [L73 00:58–01:55](../lessons/073-fixing-bad-static-ads-10-51.md)
  - **DO** turn a flat statement into a question or open loop and third person into second person. [L83 16:30–19:54](../lessons/083-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-17.md)
  - **DO** show instead of tell: replace "I've tried everything" with the attempts on screen, a text claim with the visual, a talking expert with B-roll of the proof. [L84 10:52; 29:23; 46:27](../lessons/084-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-21.md)
  - **DO** when compliance blocks a direct benefit, express it as a scene ("wakes up at 6 a.m. and makes fresh focaccia"). [L78 09:43–10:38](../lessons/078-wine-brand-125k-at-2-24-roas.md)
  - **DON'T** copy the course's added proof into our ads: "last 200 pairs", the cosmetic-dentist quote, "Save 40 hours a month", "60% of the US population", "lasting over 12". Use only real stock facts, real reviews, consenting experts and sourced stats, or leave a bracketed slot. Check with the [LLM claim audit](../../../stefan-georgi/rmbc-ii/rubrics/llm-output-claim-audit.md).
  - **IF** you are making a variant of a video **THEN** change the hook *and* the B-roll after it, not only the first 3–5 s. [L8 03:33–04:26](../lessons/008-how-to-make-diversified-ads.md)
- **See also:** [Static rewrite moves](../frameworks/static-rewrite-moves.md) · [Static copy levers](../concepts/static-copy-levers.md) · [AI post-production](../frameworks/ai-post-production.md) · [AI realism tells](../concepts/ai-realism-tells.md) · [Ad teardowns](ad-teardowns.md) (the full critique for each row) · [Hooks](hooks.md) · [Rules](rules.md) · Evolve Copywriting [rewrites](../../../evolve/copywriting/libraries/rewrites.md) · Shared [rewrites library](../../../../libraries/rewrites.md)

## ⚠️ Copy the mechanism, not the asset
The "Before" lines are real brands' ads; the "After" lines are the instructor's rewrites for those brands. Reuse the move, not the words. Health and results lines in "After" (belly fat, DHT, detox, colon cancer, "lasting over 12") are unsubstantiated in the course.

## Flags
⛔ the course's "after" adds a non-compliant element (invented scarcity, invented expert or result, invented deadline); the compliant version follows "→ compliant". ⚠️ the "after" contains a claim that must be true and sourced for our product.
