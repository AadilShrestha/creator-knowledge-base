# Ad teardowns library (Ad Creators Lab)
> Every ad the course breaks down, roasts, fixes or builds as a demo: 7-Figure Vault winners, roast-call member ads, Hao's psychology reviews, the static "fix this ad" series, animated-format swipe files and hall-of-fame posts, and the AI UGC demo ads. Rows are grouped by source type, tagged by format, niche and awareness, and cited.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** you are tearing down a competitor or member ad and want a precedent with the same format or niche, roasting our own draft before launch, or explaining why an AI-made ad looks fake or fails to sell.
- **Inputs → outputs:** the ad (format, niche, hook, visuals, CTA) → find 2–3 rows with the same format or failure → apply the "Fix suggested" and "Principle" columns → a fix list for our ad. Score it with the [ad roast scorecard](../rubrics/ad-roast-scorecard.md) and check it with the [AI video QA checklist](../rubrics/ai-video-qa-checklist.md).
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** roast in this order: hook (visual, text, audio agree?), clarity (what is it, who is it for, in the first seconds), does it sell (desire, mechanism, objections, proof, CTA), execution (safe zone, realism, voice). [L83](../lessons/083-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-17.md) [L84](../lessons/084-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-21.md) · see [winning-ad checklist](../frameworks/winning-ad-checklist.md)
  - **DO** show what the script claims: arm shots for a sleeveless-tops hook, scent particles mixing instead of a text line, attempts instead of "I've tried everything". [L83 38:30–44:21](../lessons/083-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-17.md) [L84 10:52; 46:27](../lessons/084-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-21.md)
  - **DO** keep the product as an open loop in unaware ads; showing it early "filters out viewers who dislike being sold to". [L91 19:33–32:27](../lessons/091-special-guest-recordings-ai-office-hours-david.md) [L83 45:51–49:16](../lessons/083-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-17.md)
  - **DO** match funnel to traffic: a high-CTR, low-CVR static needs an education page (listicle or advertorial), not a thinner PDP. [L84 01:34–04:54](../lessons/084-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-21.md)
  - **DON'T** read a high hook rate as a winner, or a low one as a loser: the 35.93% hook ad and the 11.49% hook ad both scaled on body and offer. [L79](../lessons/079-intimacy-brand-200k-at-2-3-roas.md) [L81](../lessons/081-turning-400-ad-to-13k-spender.md)
  - **DON'T** copy rows marked ⛔ or ⚠️: AI "patients", doctors and customers presented as real, drug comparisons, toxicity and hormone claims, competitor brand names. Use the compliant alternative in the row and [ai-creator-compliance](../concepts/ai-creator-compliance.md).
  - **IF** a teardown row says "instructor claim" for spend or ROAS **THEN** cite it as such; only L079 and L081 show doc figures.
- **See also:** [Hooks](hooks.md) · [Angles](angles.md) · [Rewrites](rewrites.md) · [Rules](rules.md) · [Static rewrite moves](../frameworks/static-rewrite-moves.md) · [AI realism tells](../concepts/ai-realism-tells.md) · Shared [competitor ad teardown playbook](../../../../playbooks/competitor-ad-teardown.md) · Shared [ad scorecard](../../../../rubrics/ad-scorecard.md) · Shared [teardowns library](../../../../libraries/ad-teardowns.md) · Evolve Start Here [ad teardowns](../../../evolve/start-here/libraries/ad-teardowns.md)

## ⚠️ Copy the mechanism, not the asset
These are other brands' ads (Gratsi, Love Blanket, Primal Muscle, Penrose, Quinovic, Magic Spoon and many unnamed members). Study the structure, never the lines, characters, visuals or claims. Spend and ROAS are instructor claims unless the row says "doc". Many example ads carry unsubstantiated health, hormone or performance claims; the Flag column marks them.

## Tags
⛔ don't execute (non-compliant tactic; compliant version in "Fix suggested" or Flag) · ⚠️ unsupported claim in the ad · **Aware**: U / PA / SA / PrA / MA, "(d)" derived, "—" unclear. Rows from course demos are marked *demo*.
