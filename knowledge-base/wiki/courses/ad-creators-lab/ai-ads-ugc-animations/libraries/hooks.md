# Hooks library (Ad Creators Lab)
> Every hook and opening line in the 110 Ad Creators Lab lesson pages, from the 54 harvested rows plus hooks quoted inside teardowns, roasts and awareness examples. Rows are merged, grouped by hook type, tagged by format, niche and awareness stage, and cited to lesson and timestamp. Many lines carry unsupported claims, so read the Flag column before reusing anything.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** you are writing the first line or first 3 s of an AI UGC, animated, song or talking-object ad, writing a static headline, naming the hook type of a competitor ad, or building a hook set for a concept test.
- **Inputs → outputs:** avatar, awareness stage, angle, format and niche → filter by section and the [niche index](#quick-index-by-niche) → 3–5 precedent rows → the **mechanism** in "Why it works", rewritten with our avatar's words and our real proof. Angles behind the hooks: [angles](angles.md). Before/after fixes: [rewrites](rewrites.md).
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** use juxtaposition as a quick win: "how to [desire] without [linked pain]" (instructor: "works almost all of the time"). [L80 07:28–08:22](../lessons/080-supplement-brand-70k-winning-ad-breakdown.md)
  - **DO** make the hook name the avatar and the desire, not a metric: "restart your gains after 40" outspent "move your bench press PR" about $13.6K to $477 (doc figures, one account). [L81 04:58–06:43](../lessons/081-turning-400-ad-to-13k-spender.md)
  - **DO** show the outcome as a scene, not a claim, when compliance blocks the direct benefit ("wakes up at 6 a.m. and makes fresh focaccia" instead of "without a headache"). [L78 09:43–10:38](../lessons/078-wine-brand-125k-at-2-24-roas.md)
  - **DO** pick the hook from the creator's most appealing desire line, not a tutorial opener. [L13 01:02–02:22](../lessons/013-live-edit.md)
  - **DO** match hook text, voiceover and the shot on screen (product on the word "compound" read as steroids; sleeveless-tops hook with no arm shots failed). [L81 05:46–07:36](../lessons/081-turning-400-ad-to-13k-spender.md) [L83 38:30–44:21](../lessons/083-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-17.md)
  - **DON'T** use a statement the viewer already accepts; turn it into an open loop ("a muscle you've never trained"). [L83 12:16–17:24](../lessons/083-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-17.md)
  - **DON'T** reuse any statistic, "scientists", ingredient-performance, hormone, toxicity or results line from these rows. They are the source ads' claims (⚠️) and several are non-compliant (⛔). Bracket the slot and fill it with a claim our product can substantiate, then run the [LLM claim audit](../../../stefan-georgi/rmbc-ii/rubrics/llm-output-claim-audit.md).
  - **DON'T** put an invented first-person story ("my husband bet me", "my boyfriend spent $300", "I wasted $3,000") in an AI person's mouth as if it were a real customer's experience. Use a disclosed skit, a real creator's real experience, or second-person framing. See [ai-creator-compliance](../concepts/ai-creator-compliance.md).
  - **IF** the market is unaware **THEN** open on a relatable question or a surprising (true, sourced) fact, with no product. [L95 00:43–01:38](../lessons/095-market-awareness-explained-in-180s.md) [L96 00:00](../lessons/096-market-awareness-ad-examples.md)
  - **IF** the hook rate is low but the body converts **THEN** keep the body and test new visual and copy hooks (instructor target 20–30% hook rate). [L81 04:58–09:24](../lessons/081-turning-400-ad-to-13k-spender.md)

## ⚠️ Copy the mechanism, not the asset
Every row is a precedent from someone else's ad, a course demo or a Claude draft. Keep the structure ("how to [desire] without [pain]", "This [noun] will [desire] after [age]", "Stop pretending your [current fix] works"), change the words, visual and proof. The supplement, hormone, fragrance-performance, hangover, toxicity, anti-ageing and sexual-health lines are unsubstantiated in the course. Brand names in rows (Baccarat Rouge, Ad Creators Lab, Goli and others) are other companies' marks: never put them in our ads. Spend and ROAS figures are instructor or doc claims from single accounts.

## Flags and tags
| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ⛔ | Don't execute: the line or its execution breaks a repo rule (invented personal story or customer, fake doctor, invented deadline, drug or steroid comparison, competitor brand name). Reuse the structure only; the compliant version is in the row. |
| ⚠️ | Rests on an unsupported claim (stat, ingredient effect, health result, superlative). Bracket and substantiate before use. |
| Won / Lost | The lesson reports a result. Results are instructor or doc claims unless the lesson shows account data. |
| Aware | Awareness stage: **U** unaware, **PA** problem aware, **SA** solution aware, **PrA** product aware, **MA** most aware. "(d)" = derived from the hook's content, not stated by the lesson. "—" = unclear. |
| Format | AI UGC, AI podcast, street interview, skeleton, crochet, claymation, talking object, Zack D style (3D cartoon), song, static, UGC (human) |

## Decision table: which section to open
| IF the job is… | THEN open | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Unaware cold traffic, no felt problem | B, G, then D | Relatable question or story; no product in the first line (L091, L095) |
| Problem-aware buyer | C, E | Name the failure, or attack the current fix (L079) |
| Solution-aware buyer who tried things | D, E | "Without [pain]"; failed-solution talking object (see [angles](angles.md) A001) |
| Product comparison or taste/durability test | A | Real test with our product only; disclose AI people |
| Avatar segment by age or life stage | H | "after 40", "guy in his 40s", grandparents |
| Song or animated story ad | G | Story-song openers, skits, couple dynamics |
| Static headline | D, H, E | Also [rewrites](rewrites.md) §A for static fixes |
