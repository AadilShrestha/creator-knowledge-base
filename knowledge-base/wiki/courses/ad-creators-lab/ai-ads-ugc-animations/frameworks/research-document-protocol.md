# Research document protocol
> The course's research front end: build one research document from raw Reddit threads, own and competitor reviews and TikTok comments, have an LLM structure it with a fixed prompt, check every line against the raw paste, add brand facts and (hypothesis) personas, label the awareness stage of every concept, find reference ads by spend instead of run time, and after each test route back as MORE (create), BETTER (brief/hooks) or NEW (research). It is a fast first pass (about 30 minutes of Reddit, ≤1 hour of concept research); Evolve Start Here and the shared research stack are the deeper versions.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** starting creative for a brand or product; a batch needs fresh angles; deciding what to do after test results; collecting competitor and adjacent-niche ads to remix.
- **Inputs → outputs:** product category keyword, product page and brand facts (verified claims list), 10-30 own reviews, 10-30 competitor reviews, Reddit threads, TikTok comments, ad-library or spy-tool access → `Raw Data - [product]` doc → Research Document (categorized insights, strategy implications, ICP language, brand information, hypothesis personas, empty Concepts section) → awareness label per concept → spend-qualified reference ads → after testing, a MORE / BETTER / NEW decision.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** paste raw data unformatted first, structure second [L97 02:32](../lessons/097-customer-research-protocol-1.md); collect objections, behaviours and terminology, not only desires and pains [L97 00:00](../lessons/097-customer-research-protocol-1.md).
  - **DO** run a source-truth check on the LLM report: "it does hallucinate"; ask where a line came from and re-prompt "use the actual data that's in the document" [L97 10:14–11:10](../lessons/097-customer-research-protocol-1.md).
  - **DO** keep golden nuggets verbatim; the prompt's clause that rewrites "clunky" quotes in Reddit style produces paraphrases, which must be labelled paraphrase, never quoted (derived; repo rule "no invented quotes").
  - **DO** treat Buyer Avatar GPT personas ("Susan, 32, yoga instructor", "Alex, the fed up commuter") as hypotheses; every field must trace to a raw quote or be marked (assumed) [L98 01:54–02:27](../lessons/098-customer-research-protocol-2.md); Tether: never use ChatGPT as the source of customer types ([customer research stack](../../../../frameworks/customer-research-stack.md)).
  - **DO** label the awareness stage on every concept card before writing [L95 00:43](../lessons/095-market-awareness-explained-in-180s.md).
  - **DO** pick reference ads by spend, never by days running [L100 00:00–02:46](../lessons/100-why-copying-longest-running-is-bad.md); Trendtrack filter: spend ≥ $10,000, days running ≥ 14, sort Most Duplicates, EU/UK only [L101 01:34–02:20](../lessons/101-how-to-find-ads-based-on-ad-spend.md).
  - **DO** look in adjacent niches, not only your own [L101 02:20](../lessons/101-how-to-find-ads-based-on-ad-spend.md); [L70 05:16–06:02](../lessons/070-full-static-ads-creation-process.md).
  - **DON'T** reuse the course's illustrative stats or proof ("75% of people", "over 3,000 men and women… within three days") [L95 01:38; 02:29](../lessons/095-market-awareness-explained-in-180s.md).
  - **IF** results are weak **THEN** fix words and desire (BETTER → brief/hooks) before re-producing; **IF** ideas are exhausted **THEN** go back to research (NEW) [L94 01:30](../lessons/094-research-protocol-start-here.md).

## The loop (094)
| Stage | Question | Output | Go back here when | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Research | "What they want" | Research doc: desires, problems, words, objections | Need **NEW** creative (ideas exhausted) | [L94 00:00; 01:30](../lessons/094-research-protocol-start-here.md) |
| Concept | "What to show" | Format/concept (interview, us vs them…) | New research angle found | [L94 00:44](../lessons/094-research-protocol-start-here.md) |
| Brief / Hooks | "What to say" | Brief for video, hook selection for statics | Need **BETTER** creative (not performing: change wording, try other desires) | [L94 00:44; 01:30](../lessons/094-research-protocol-start-here.md) |
| Create | "Make it real" | Videos, statics | Need **MORE** creative (variations of what works) | [L94 01:30](../lessons/094-research-protocol-start-here.md) |
| Test → Feedback | "What do we need?" | MORE / BETTER / NEW decision | After every test | [L94 01:30](../lessons/094-research-protocol-start-here.md) |

The course gives no thresholds for the decision. Use Evolve's labels to choose (derived mapping):

| Evolve label ([iteration outcomes](../../../evolve/copywriting/concepts/iteration-outcomes.md), [winning-ad labels](../../../evolve/start-here/concepts/winning-ad-labels.md)) | Course route | Next action |
|---|---|---|
| Breakthrough / spend winner | MORE | Variations of the winner ([rewrite losers and iterate](../../../evolve/start-here/frameworks/rewrite-losers-and-iterate.md), hook swaps on the same body: [L81](../lessons/081-turning-400-ad-to-13k-spender.md)) |
| KPI winner without spend / near-winner | BETTER | Rewrite hook, headline, desire on the same concept |
| Loser after 3 tries on the angle | NEW | New research pass, new angle ([execution checklist](../../../evolve/start-here/rubrics/execution-checklist.md) three-tries limit) |

### Research document schema (what the finished doc holds)
`Categorized insights` (top pain points; failed solutions; desired outcomes; objections with real quote; misconceptions with clarification; golden nuggets verbatim) · `Strategy implications for [brand]` (opportunity | how brand can win) · `ICP language analysis` (tone, emotional style, vocabulary, copy tips) · `Brand information` · `Key personas` (hypotheses) · `Concepts` (empty). Source: prompt in [L97](../lessons/097-customer-research-protocol-1.md#prompts--templates-verbatim); brand fields [L98](../lessons/098-customer-research-protocol-2.md#prompts--templates-verbatim).

| # | Step | Setting | Source |
|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | Trendtrack → Trending Ads | EU/UK ads only | [L101 00:00](../lessons/101-how-to-find-ads-based-on-ad-spend.md) |
| 3 | Filters | Spend min $10,000; days running min 14 (frame later shows 30-180); format Video; CPM slider | [L101 01:34; frames 02:00, 02:49](../lessons/101-how-to-find-ads-based-on-ad-spend.md) |
| 4 | Sort | Most Duplicates (example: 93 duplicates) | [L101 02:20](../lessons/101-how-to-find-ads-based-on-ad-spend.md) |
| 5 | Keyword and adjacent niches | e.g. "sleep"; travel supplements → other supplements → nootropics → fitness | [L101 02:20–03:17](../lessons/101-how-to-find-ads-based-on-ad-spend.md) |
| 6 | Save | Board, pin, brand tracker, transcript | [L101 02:20](../lessons/101-how-to-find-ads-based-on-ad-spend.md) |
| 7 | Label stage | Prefer unaware / problem-aware ads for scale concepts ("you can only scale ads that are bringing in new customers") | [L100 00:45](../lessons/100-why-copying-longest-running-is-bad.md) |
| 8 | Inside your own account (Adnova or Ads Manager) | Here "lasted the longest and performed the best" is valid because you see performance; pull 7-day angles, then remix | [L102 00:00–01:38](../lessons/102-find-winning-concepts-with-adnova.md) |
| 9 | Tear down and remix | Copy the mechanism, not the asset: Q-tip earwax remix keeps the warning-then-fix structure with new topic and visuals | [L102 00:57](../lessons/102-find-winning-concepts-with-adnova.md); [teardown playbook](../../../../playbooks/competitor-ad-teardown.md) |

Notes: Trendtrack gives no US spend data; for US-only markets use another source (derived) [L101](../lessons/101-how-to-find-ads-based-on-ad-spend.md). Spend can also reflect brand-awareness budgets; pair it with duplicates and CPM (derived). For a new client, David's order: start from the client's own account winners and change the angle on the same avatar before copying big competitors [L91 46:51–47:52](../lessons/091-special-guest-recordings-ai-office-hours-david.md). Frequency of about 2-3 → fatigue and CPA spike, so keep feeding top-of-funnel concepts (instructor claim) [L100 01:29](../lessons/100-why-copying-longest-running-is-bad.md). Both Trendtrack and Adnova links carry the instructor's affiliate codes.

## Compliance gate
- [ ] No quote, statistic or result in the research doc that is not in the raw paste (source-truth check done).
- [ ] Personas marked as hypotheses; never presented as real customers in ads [L98 limitations](../lessons/098-customer-research-protocol-2.md).
- [ ] Third-party reviews and comments used as research language only; not republished as testimonials [L97 limitations](../lessons/097-customer-research-protocol-1.md).
- [ ] Brand "claims" section contains only claims the brand can substantiate; bracket the rest.
- [ ] Reference ads: mechanism only; no lines, characters, claims or layouts copied 1:1.

## Cross-course notes
- **Evolve Start Here** ([research stack workflow](../../../evolve/start-here/frameworks/research-stack-workflow.md), [research doc](../../../evolve/start-here/concepts/research-doc.md)): 14 sources in fixed order, phrase bank with "+1" tallies, objections, FAQ, competitor and ad-account audit, then avatars built desire-first ([core and sub-avatars](../../../evolve/start-here/concepts/core-and-sub-avatars.md)). Agrees on: comments are king, don't copy big competitors 1:1, never paste AI-returned quotes without opening the source. Differs on depth (this course: ~30 min Reddit + reviews + comments; Evolve: a full module per source) and on avatars (Buyer Avatar GPT demographic personas vs Evolve's "start with the desire over a demographic" and "never desireless avatars"). Default: this protocol is the minimum for a batch on a known brand; a new brand gets Evolve's full stack. When avatars disagree, Evolve's desire-first avatar wins.
- **Tether Lab** ([customer research stack](../../../../frameworks/customer-research-stack.md)): four layers (market monthly; consumer, CIM surveys and social trends weekly), "update, don't accumulate", ChatGPT only to brainstorm customer types that are then verified on Reddit (thread count) and TikTok. The Buyer Avatar GPT step violates "don't use ChatGPT as the source of customer types" unless each persona is verified that way.
- **RMBC II** ([deep research workflow](../../../stefan-georgi/rmbc-ii/frameworks/deep-research-workflow.md), [unified research document](../../../stefan-georgi/rmbc-ii/concepts/unified-research-document.md)): four LLM deep-research passes merged into a short brief attached to every later prompt. Same idea as "save the research doc as PDF and attach it everywhere"; RMBC adds ingredient research as a separate doc.
- **Shared spend vs run time:** the [competitor ad teardown](../../../../playbooks/competitor-ad-teardown.md) also says run time isn't proof; it scores several winner signals instead of spend alone.
