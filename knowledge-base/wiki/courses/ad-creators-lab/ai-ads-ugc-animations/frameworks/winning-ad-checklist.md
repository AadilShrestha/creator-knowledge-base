# Winning-ad checklist
> One pre-launch checklist built from five Vault winner breakdowns (077-082), Camilo's live roasts (083-084) and Hao's psychology calls (086-087): pick the belief-shift structure that fits the awareness stage, run the 20-item check, score hook / clarity / sells / execution out of 20, then read results in Evolve's order (spend and money first, soft metrics last). Every Vault script is structure only: their health, legal and comparison claims are not reusable.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** writing or reviewing a video script (AI UGC, podcast, animated, talking head) or a static before launch; roasting a member or competitor ad; diagnosing a winner/loser pair after launch.
- **Inputs → outputs:** draft ad or script + avatar, awareness stage, market, offer (written at the top) + research doc (customer language, objections, authority sources) + verified claims list → chosen structure, checklist pass/fail per item, /20 score with one fix per weak criterion, and after launch a MORE/BETTER/NEW decision with a written learning.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** write avatar, awareness stage, target market and offer at the top of every copy doc [L83 11:18](../lessons/083-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-17.md).
  - **DO** apply Justin's four-item core check to every script: open loops, customer language, authority, unique mechanism [L80 12:34–13:20](../lessons/080-supplement-brand-70k-winning-ad-breakdown.md).
  - **DO** build the hook as the ad's "title": avatar + angle + sound + visual, in the viewer's own words, so the rest of the ad can be filed into a schema they already hold [L86 11:21–13:39; 22:53](../lessons/086-ad-psychology-with-howie-2026-08-12.md).
  - **DO** make every script line show a matching visual: "association and showing not telling" is what the agency screens editors for [L83 47:25](../lessons/083-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-17.md); [L84 47:25](../lessons/084-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-21.md).
  - **DO** score hook, clarity, sells, execution 0-5 each before launch [L84 37:59](../lessons/084-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-21.md); the course gives no pass mark, so gate on the shared [ad scorecard](../../../../rubrics/ad-scorecard.md) (≥80, no auto-fail).
  - **DON'T** decide winners from hook rate or CTR: a 11.49% hook-rate ad scaled to about $13.6K while its 12.34% sibling stalled at $477 [L81 04:28](../lessons/081-turning-400-ad-to-13k-spender.md); read spend and money first ([KPIs and columns](../../../evolve/start-here/frameworks/kpis-and-columns.md)).
  - **DON'T** reuse any Vault line or claim: copy "the principles… the belief shifts", not the script [L79 10:54–11:33](../lessons/079-intimacy-brand-200k-at-2-3-roas.md).
  - **IF** a body sells but hook rate is low **THEN** iterate new visual and copy hooks on the same body within days, not months [L81 08:30–09:24](../lessons/081-turning-400-ad-to-13k-spender.md).
  - **IF** you use guilt **THEN** pivot to "it's not your fault" and move the cause to something unknown before the product [L86 49:48–50:18](../lessons/086-ad-psychology-with-howie-2026-08-12.md); Hao himself calls guilt-tripping "not very ethical" [L86 46:15](../lessons/086-ad-psychology-with-howie-2026-08-12.md).
- **See also:** [Ad roast scorecard](../rubrics/ad-roast-scorecard.md) · [AI video QA checklist](../rubrics/ai-video-qa-checklist.md) · [Hook schema and open loops](../concepts/hook-schema-and-open-loops.md) · [Diversification vs variation](../concepts/diversification-vs-variation.md) · [AI creator compliance](../concepts/ai-creator-compliance.md) · [Ad teardowns library](../libraries/ad-teardowns.md) · [Hooks library](../libraries/hooks.md) · Repo: [write-ugc-script skill](../../../../../../.claude/skills/write-ugc-script/SKILL.md) · Shared: [ad scorecard](../../../../rubrics/ad-scorecard.md), [UGC script checklist](../../../../rubrics/ugc-script-checklist.md) · Evolve: [KPIs and columns](../../../evolve/start-here/frameworks/kpis-and-columns.md), [winning-ad labels](../../../evolve/start-here/concepts/winning-ad-labels.md), [execution checklist](../../../evolve/start-here/rubrics/execution-checklist.md) · RMBC: [claim audit](../../../stefan-georgi/rmbc-ii/rubrics/llm-output-claim-audit.md)

## Step 1: pick the structure (Vault patterns)
All five are instructor-reported winners from client accounts (instructor claims; no isolating tests). Use the skeleton, never the lines.

| Pattern | When | Beats | Reported result | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Authority + juxtaposition + mechanism-through-story** | Solution aware, warm zone 1→2, known category and known pain | 1 authority the audience accepts → 2 desire as a visual scene → 3 juxtaposition (desire without the linked pain) → 4 mechanism told as a story ("When I asked how…") → 5 mechanism in known terms → 6 brand as the next research step ("from my friend") → 7 "so that" feature → lived scene → 8 emotion and identity close | $125,007 spend, ROAS 2.24, hook 46.55%, outbound CTR 2.12% | [L78 06:52–22:48](../lessons/078-wine-brand-125k-at-2-24-roas.md) |
| **B. Incumbent attack / belief shift** | Solution aware; buyers use a DIY or incumbent fix (towels, pads) | Pattern interrupt aimed at the incumbent ("Stop pretending your…") → twist the knife (why each substitute fails) → short product intro → benefit stack framed against the incumbent → lived-experience lines from Reddit → new-belief close; missing: guarantee, urgency, offer (test as variants) | $199,348 spend, ROAS 2.31, hook 35.93%, outbound CTR 0.91% | [L79 07:47–13:51](../lessons/079-intimacy-brand-200k-at-2-3-roas.md) |
| **C. DR podcast, zone 4** | Solution aware, high desire + high fear; conversational talking head | Reddit-language hook with juxtaposition ("how to [desire] without [pain]") → bridge with mechanism teaser → education that makes the speaker an authority → FOMO loop with third-party authority → mechanism → usage tied to desire → honest objection ("Is it as strong as…? No.") → recap sentence (problem + solution + desire in one line) → product, guarantee, CTA | $70,450 spend, hook 56.22% (no ROAS shown) | [L80 07:28–17:51](../lessons/080-supplement-brand-70k-winning-ad-breakdown.md) |
| **D. Same body, hook swap** | A body that sells; low hook rate | Change only the first 3 s: avatar-specific desire ("restart your gains after 40") over a metric ("bench press PR"); scientific noun the audience trusts ("growth factor") over one that triggers the feared alternative ("compound"); hook words and hook visuals must agree | V5 $13,608 at ROAS 1.08 vs V2 $477 at 0.58 | [L81 00:00–10:29](../lessons/081-turning-400-ad-to-13k-spender.md) |
| **E. Three-voice dissonance** | Sub-avatar holds a repeated belief ("best product" or standard routine) | Beginner states the common belief (no benefit, no pain) → Average adds the niche tip they know → Pro tells a lived story of the indirect desire → same product, different method (loop) → mechanism + an objection the viewer had not thought of → price + visual outcome CTA | £104,357 spend, ROAS 1.21, hook 41.73%, outbound CTR 1.53% | [L82 01:58–11:09](../lessons/082-mens-skincare-135k-spend-ad-breakdown.md) |

Emotional arc most winners share: open in a lower zone, close in zone 1 ("most ads end up closing in zone one… usually open in a lower zone", instructor claim) [L79 06:56](../lessons/079-intimacy-brand-200k-at-2-3-roas.md). ## Step 3: Camilo's roast order (how to give feedback)
Derived from the order Camilo works through ads in [L83](../lessons/083-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-17.md) and [L84](../lessons/084-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-21.md):
1. Context: who is it for, which stage? (Kegel ad had none) [L83 11:18]
2. Hook: open loop or obvious statement? [L83 16:30]
3. Language: would the customer say it? [L83 12:46]
4. Believability of speaker and comparison [L83 18:22]
5. Specificity: remove "better", "game changer", feature lists [L83 24:36; 41:20]
6. Pain build before the product [L83 40:27]
7. Visual match per line; show attempts, not a sad face ("I've tried everything" → show walking, steps) [L83 38:30–39:29]; [L84 10:52]
8. Reveal timing [L83 46:42]
9. Realism: "too brushed, too perfect" visuals; add "candid photo, TikTok style with uneven angles, as if a friend was recording it" [L83 42:50–44:21]
10. Voice match: "if I'd change one thing, redo the voice" [L84 27:34–28:27]
11. Safe zone: preview in Meta Advanced Preview; text 100% covered by overlays in one cut [L84 48:24–49:32]; [L83 34:24]
12. Retention: camera motion in every shot [L83 33:29]
13. Funnel fit: where does the click land; education-needing ads to education pages [L84 02:57–04:54]

## Step 4: the 4-part scorecard (/20)
On-screen key [L84 37:59; frame 38:23](../lessons/084-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-21.md); full rubric page: [ad roast scorecard](../rubrics/ad-roast-scorecard.md).

| Criterion (0-5) | Course key (verbatim) | Checklist rows feeding it |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | "Does it stop the scroll - pattern interrupt or a claim that makes the next second..." (truncated on screen) | 2, 3, 4, 7 |
| Clarity | "Within a few seconds do you know what the product is and why you'd want it" | 1, 5, 10, 16 |
| Sells | "Is desire built and is there a real reason + CTA to act - would it move units" | 8, 9, 11-14, 20 |
| Execution | "Clean craft - no AI jank, good pacing, sound, edit. Polish in service of the sell" | 17-19 |

Agent rule (derived): the course shows no pass mark and no weighting; report the four scores plus the lowest criterion's fix, then gate on the shared [ad scorecard](../../../../rubrics/ad-scorecard.md) and, for AI video, the [AI video QA checklist](../rubrics/ai-video-qa-checklist.md). "Ugly ads" can win: the $70K Vault ad "didn't have the most realistic looking AI UGC, didn't have the best editing" [L80 02:30](../lessons/080-supplement-brand-70k-winning-ad-breakdown.md); polish counts only "in service of the sell".

## Step 5: read results and close the loop
### Read order (Evolve default)
1. Spend and share of spend → 2. ROAS/CPA on click-based data against margin-based breakeven → 3. AOV/CVR → 4. soft metrics (hook, hold, CTR, CPC) as diagnostics ([KPIs and columns](../../../evolve/start-here/frameworks/kpis-and-columns.md)). The Vault agrees that soft metrics mislead: high hook rate "does not correlate" with winning [L80 04:11](../lessons/080-supplement-brand-70k-winning-ad-breakdown.md); intent of the stoppers matters more [L79 02:32](../lessons/079-intimacy-brand-200k-at-2-3-roas.md).
