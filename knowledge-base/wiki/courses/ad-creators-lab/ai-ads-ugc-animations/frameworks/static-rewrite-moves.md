# Static rewrite moves
> Twenty named moves, each with the course's own before → after, the principle behind it and, where the course's "after" invents proof, the compliant version to use instead. Apply them in the audit order below; one headline change is often the whole fix ("one headline change and the ad is going to do many times better", instructor claim [L75 07:55](../lessons/075-fixing-bad-static-ads-3.md)).

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** fixing a weak static (ours or a competitor's for teardown); writing a rewrite brief; checking a draft before launch; harvesting rewrite rows.
- **Inputs → outputs:** the static (image, headline, call-outs, offer, CTA) + research doc (desires, objections, real quotes) + verified facts sheet (claims, prices, stock, real reviews) → diagnosis per audit step → rewritten headline, ≤3 call-outs, one proof element, CTA, with every claim marked verified or bracketed.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** run the audit order once, top to bottom, and stop at the first failing step before adding more ([audit order](#audit-order)).
  - **DO** take the headline from what customers say "rather than trying to be some kind of genius copywriter" [L72 00:53–01:49](../lessons/072-static-ads-mistakes-to-avoid-4-17.md).
  - **DO** keep a hook that already works and fix its surroundings [L74 04:27](../lessons/074-fixing-bad-static-ads-2.md).
  - **DON'T** execute any course "after" element that invents proof, urgency or an expert (the [don't-execute table](#course-after-examples-that-invent-proof-dont-execute)).
  - **DON'T** use "game changer" (banned at the agency) [L73 09:13–09:40](../lessons/073-fixing-bad-static-ads-10-51.md), "better" [L83 24:36](../lessons/083-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-17.md), "best on the market" [L73 02:43](../lessons/073-fixing-bad-static-ads-10-51.md) or "everyone is going crazy for these" [L72 02:48](../lessons/072-static-ads-mistakes-to-avoid-4-17.md).
  - **IF** a rewrite needs more than 3 call-outs or dense copy **THEN** ship the ≤3 version as control and the dense version as a labelled test (shared playbook default; see [disagreements](#agreement-and-disagreement-with-the-shared-static-playbook)).
- **See also:** [Static ads process](static-ads-process.md) · [Static copy levers](../concepts/static-copy-levers.md) · [Rewrites library](../libraries/rewrites.md) · [Ad roast scorecard](../rubrics/ad-roast-scorecard.md) · Shared: [static ad creation](../../../../playbooks/static-ad-creation.md), [rewrites library](../../../../libraries/rewrites.md) · Evolve: [show, don't tell](../../../evolve/copywriting/concepts/show-dont-tell.md), [facts over opinions](../../../evolve/copywriting/concepts/facts-over-opinions.md), [bridges](../../../evolve/copywriting/concepts/bridges.md) · RMBC: [claim audit](../../../stefan-georgi/rmbc-ii/rubrics/llm-output-claim-audit.md), [compliance red flags](../../../stefan-georgi/rmbc-ii/concepts/compliance-red-flags.md)

## The moves

### M1. Desire headline, not a category or label
- **When:** headline is a service label, product name or vague promise.
- **Before → after:** "Expert care for your rental property starts here" → "Get your rent on time" [L73 00:58–01:55](../lessons/073-fixing-bad-static-ads-10-51.md) · "Collagen peel off mask" → "Reduce dry, flaky skin in just 15 minutes" [L75 06:51–07:55](../lessons/075-fixing-bad-static-ads-3.md) · "Hire a social media manager" → "Attract the right buyers" [L73 07:19](../lessons/073-fixing-bad-static-ads-10-51.md) · "Why it's a game changer" → "He finally stopped pulling" [L73 09:40–10:51](../lessons/073-fixing-bad-static-ads-10-51.md).
- **Principle:** desire association; "What is in it for me?" [L73 03:39–04:09; 07:19](../lessons/073-fixing-bad-static-ads-10-51.md).
- **Check:** the promise must be something the product actually delivers (the "15 minutes" and "rent on time" lines are claims; verify).

### M2. Add a "without" line
- **When:** the desire carries a known pain, cost or effort.
- **Before → after:** "Get your rent on time" + "Without doing the chasing yourself" [L73 01:27–01:55](../lessons/073-fixing-bad-static-ads-10-51.md) · "Voted the best low-cost hearing aid…" → "Hear the birds outside without spending $5000" [L74 00:52](../lessons/074-fixing-bad-static-ads-2.md) · candle bullets → "Turn any container into your candle without wax mess" [L74 05:58–07:23](../lessons/074-fixing-bad-static-ads-2.md) · cravings ad → "destroy cravings without spending months dieting" (suggested) [L72 00:53–01:49](../lessons/072-static-ads-mistakes-to-avoid-4-17.md).
- **Principle:** desire + reduced risk/cost; same juxtaposition as "how to [desire] without [pain]" in video ([winning-ad checklist](winning-ad-checklist.md)).
- **Check:** a price anchor ("$5000") must be a real market price you can cite.

### M3. Climb the benefit ladder to level 4-5
- **When:** call-outs read "Weight loss / Enhances energy / Boosts immunity".
- **Ladder (weight loss):** 1 you lose weight → 2 you have energy → 3 you look better → 4 you are confident → 5 you can finally wear the dress that has hung in your wardrobe for months [L72 03:25](../lessons/072-static-ads-mistakes-to-avoid-4-17.md).
- **Before → after:** "Enhanced energy" → "No longer tired after 2 pm"; "Boost immunity" → "You don't get sick for the fifth time this year" [L72 03:25–04:13](../lessons/072-static-ads-mistakes-to-avoid-4-17.md) · "Bye bye baggy eyes" → a headline about the routine or the event they want to look good for [L72 00:00–00:53](../lessons/072-static-ads-mistakes-to-avoid-4-17.md).
- **Principle:** sell the lived outcome; matches Vault "so that" chains ([L78 18:07](../lessons/078-wine-brand-125k-at-2-24-roas.md)).
- **Check:** immunity and illness lines are health claims; use only with substantiation.

### M4. Say what only we can say (worthy of remark)
- **When:** the line could run on any competitor's ad.
- **Before → after:** "Everyone is going crazy for these gummies" → "Top 30 in apple cider vinegar concentration" (only "if the gummy truly has an outrageous amount"); beauty clinic → "local magazine's number one choice" (only if true) [L72 02:48–03:25](../lessons/072-static-ads-mistakes-to-avoid-4-17.md).
- **Principle:** specificity; "what can I say that nobody else can say?" [L74 00:25](../lessons/074-fixing-bad-static-ads-2.md).
- **Compliant version:** a documented fact (lab-tested amount, named award with year, real ranking source). If none exists, use a specific product fact instead of a superlative.

### M5. Show, don't tell
- **When:** the ad states a quality ("voted best", "sells out fast", "non-slip").
- **Before → after:** "Voted the best…" → keep the 2022/2023 ranking badges visible and drop the boast [L74 01:18](../lessons/074-fixing-bad-static-ads-2.md) · "sells out fast… 2x sold out" → an on-image "2x SOLD OUT" badge + customer quote [L75 04:26–04:54](../lessons/075-fixing-bad-static-ads-3.md) · feature icons → shoe in a puddle, sole shown for slip resistance [L73 04:35–05:01](../lessons/073-fixing-bad-static-ads-10-51.md) · product-heavy static → mug with an ingredient list, product held back [L84 06:23](../lessons/084-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-21.md).
- **Check:** badges and sell-out counts must be true and repeated on the landing page (shared playbook rule).

### M6. Customer first, credential second
- **When:** headline is about the brand ("Voted the best…", an award, the price) — a "selfish headline".
- **Move:** lead with what you do for the buyer, then the credential: "nobody actually gives a damn… make them care about you first" [L74 00:00–00:52](../lessons/074-fixing-bad-static-ads-2.md).

### M7. Urgency with a number and a reason (only if true)
- **Before → after:** "Only $99 for a limited time" → "We're clearing out our last 200 pairs before summer ends. Grab your hearing aids while they're $99" with $239 struck through [L74 00:52–01:44](../lessons/074-fixing-bad-static-ads-2.md).
- **Principle:** "limited time" has zero urgency because anyone can say it [L74 00:25](../lessons/074-fixing-bad-static-ads-2.md).
- **Compliant version:** the course numbers are illustrations. **Don't execute** "last 200 pairs" or a struck-through reference price unless stock count, deadline and prior selling price are real and documented. Otherwise use a true reason (season end date of a real sale) or no urgency.

### M8. Bridge CTA (desire → what the business does)
- **Before → after:** "Ask Quinovic" → "Skip the stress. Get the rent." [L73 01:55](../lessons/073-fixing-bad-static-ads-10-51.md) · "For effortless gardening!" → "Get your pair now" [L73 04:09–05:29](../lessons/073-fixing-bad-static-ads-10-51.md) · "Summer clearance sale only $19.98" → "Grab your cooler for $19.98 before they're gone" [L74 02:42–03:58](../lessons/074-fixing-bad-static-ads-2.md).
- **Related test:** remove the CTA button from a strong static and test the clean version [L83 25:35](../lessons/083-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-17.md). Shared playbook adds: write a definitive CTA in primary text, not "Shop Now".

### M9. Open-loop headline
- **Before → after:** "Best waterproof barefoot shoes on the market" → "The only garden shoes that do all three" (viewer asks "all three what?") [L73 04:09–04:35](../lessons/073-fixing-bad-static-ads-10-51.md).
- **Check:** the three payoffs must be visible on the ad (comfy, prevents slips, dry) or the loop frustrates.

### M10. Features → benefits ("selfish features… confusion")
- **When:** back-of-box list, feature icons, or a pile of features next to a price.
- **Before → after:** "Eco Friendly / Reduces Waste / Endless style options / Self extinguishing" → "Help protect the planet from waste" / "Style your candle your way" / "Flame goes out if tipped over" / "Breathe in paraffin-free scents, not toxins" [L74 05:58–07:23](../lessons/074-fixing-bad-static-ads-2.md) · social media service feature list → "We post content they see", "We grow your pages", "We handle the stress while you grow" [L73 07:19–09:13](../lessons/073-fixing-bad-static-ads-10-51.md) · "No crashes, no jitters, no lock-in" → "No needle, no prescription, no crashes" (contrast set chosen against the real alternative, TRT) [L83 26:33–27:33](../lessons/083-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-17.md).
- **Rule:** DON'T pair a price with a pile of features; it anchors "cheap" [L73 06:53](../lessons/073-fixing-bad-static-ads-10-51.md).
- **Check:** "not toxins" and "no prescription" are claims; verify.

### M11. Call-outs answer researched objections
- **Before → after:** "Float like a butterfly.." (unreadable product) → same headline + "Stay focused while training hard", "Push harder with more endurance", "Gentle on your stomach", "Stay energized without crashing" [L74 04:27–05:25](../lessons/074-fixing-bad-static-ads-2.md) · leash ad → "Enjoy your walks", "Keep them focused", "No hand strain" ("the top three that we found that people want to fix") [L73 10:38](../lessons/073-fixing-bad-static-ads-10-51.md).
- **Check:** each call-out maps to an objection or desire row in the research doc.

### M12. Keep a working hook, fix the rest
- "Float like a butterfly.." stayed; setting, readable product and benefits were added [L74 04:27](../lessons/074-fixing-bad-static-ads-2.md). Same logic as hook-only iteration in video ([L81](../lessons/081-turning-400-ad-to-13k-spender.md)).

### M13. Timeframe for perceived likelihood
- **Before → after:** "By July 14 you'll feel it… July 28 slim and toned body" → "Melt your belly fat in four weeks. In 1 week you'll feel better. In 2 weeks you'll see a flatter belly. In 4 weeks you'll feel confident in your slimmer self." [L75 00:44–01:10](../lessons/075-fixing-bad-static-ads-3.md).
- **Principle:** "the actual time scales give you a perceived likelihood of achievement" [L75 01:10; 07:13–07:42](../lessons/075-fixing-bad-static-ads-3.md).
- **Compliant version:** **don't execute** the belly-fat line (unsubstantiated weight-loss claim; Meta health policy). Use a timeframe only for a documented, typical result ("[setup takes 10 minutes]", "[results seen in N weeks in study X]"), with "individual results may vary" where relevant [L75 07:13](../lessons/075-fixing-bad-static-ads-3.md).

### M14. Answer the first question before the CTA
- "Don't know how to fast? Take the test." added before the CTA [L75 00:44–01:10](../lessons/075-fixing-bad-static-ads-3.md). Flow: desire → timeline → first question → action.

### M15. Say what the product is, up front
- "This isn't a trend. It's science." + five mechanism call-outs → "Reduce thinning with science, not 'trends'" + "Improves hair health", "Helps block DHT", "Encourages blood flow", "Promotes new growth" [L75 05:39–06:51](../lessons/075-fixing-bad-static-ads-3.md). Scrollers just saw unrelated content, so vagueness fails at any awareness level [L75 05:39–06:22](../lessons/075-fixing-bad-static-ads-3.md). Ingredient statics: "one step back", say what the ingredient does before the emotional payoff ("L-citrulline makes her notice you") [L83 28:04](../lessons/083-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-17.md).
- **Check:** "Helps block DHT" and "Promotes new growth" need substantiation for a hair-loss product.

### M16. Social proof first for unfamiliar products (real proof only)
- **Course move:** "Multi-pack savings / Buy more save more" → authority quote "As a cosmetic dentist, I recommend [brand]…" + clear multi-pack price [L74 08:28–08:57](../lessons/074-fixing-bad-static-ads-2.md); legal software → customer quote added [L75 02:24–03:27](../lessons/075-fixing-bad-static-ads-3.md); property service → real website testimonials [L73 00:58–01:27](../lessons/073-fixing-bad-static-ads-10-51.md).
- **Compliant version:** only the 073 move (quotes lifted from the brand's own site) is executable, after verifying them. The dentist quote and the unsourced legal-software quote: **don't execute**. Use a named, consenting expert with a documented statement, or a verified customer review with permission.

### M17. Readability and contrast
- Tiny type and an over-designed flower background fail [L72 00:00](../lessons/072-static-ads-mistakes-to-avoid-4-17.md); white on yellow is "a crazy combination" [L72 01:49–02:48](../lessons/072-static-ads-mistakes-to-avoid-4-17.md); a "copy-pasted look like your cousin who did one Photoshop class" [L74 07:52](../lessons/074-fixing-bad-static-ads-2.md); pack blends into the background → regenerate with "more contrast… a backlight" [L83 25:35](../lessons/083-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-17.md); off-brand green sticker → brand gold with black text [L83 23:37](../lessons/083-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-17.md); illustrations of competitors in yarn style were hard to read: "choose images that are undoubtedly super clear" [L83 23:37](../lessons/083-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-17.md).

### M18. Replace vague and banned words with a specific contrast
- "No new habit. Just better coffee." → "No new habit. Just part of your routine." [L83 24:36](../lessons/083-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-17.md) · "nothing to plan, nothing to time" → "nothing to remember" [L84 05:24](../lessons/084-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-21.md) · "Fresh and healthy. Everyone is going crazy…" → M4 · "Best… on the market" → M9 (also an FTC/claims risk, flagged by the instructor) [L73 02:43–03:10](../lessons/073-fixing-bad-static-ads-10-51.md).

### M19. Pair each price with its product
- "Summer clearance sale only $19.98" over a shelf of coolers → the single exact cooler at that price [L74 02:13–03:12](../lessons/074-fixing-bad-static-ads-2.md). Bottom-of-funnel offer ads are "no excuse for zero effort": add the desire ("Keep your beer ice cold for days and save over $200") [L74 02:42](../lessons/074-fixing-bad-static-ads-2.md). Price-led statics only work on people who already know the brand [L73 05:56](../lessons/073-fixing-bad-static-ads-10-51.md).
- **Check:** "save over $200" and third-party marks (a cooler brand resold) need proof and permission [L74 limitations](../lessons/074-fixing-bad-static-ads-2.md).

### M20. Brochure effect: dense copy when the headline hooks a felt need
- "If you were really sick and somebody gave you a brochure… would you not read it? Even if it was very long and the text was tiny." [L74 07:23](../lessons/074-fixing-bad-static-ads-2.md); repeated [L75 04:26–04:54](../lessons/075-fixing-bad-static-ads-3.md). Used to justify the 5-call-out candle and honey rewrites.
- **Default:** conflicts with the shared ≤3 call-out limit; run dense layouts only as a labelled test against a ≤3 control. The shared playbook's own exception (all-text statics may break the word limit) is the closest agreement.

### M21. Match the click to the education the ad needs (roast move)
- "Cortisol was found dead. Cause of death…" is "a very good use of static ads… massive strong call out"; the failure was the PDP, which held the explanation below the fold. Fix: education-led page or listicle/advertorial; or rebuild PDP images so "each image is its own ad" (headline, ingredients with why each matters, offer, objection handling) [L84 01:34–04:54](../lessons/084-ad-roast-calls-2026-08-21.md). See the repo [write-landing-page skill](../../../../../../.claude/skills/write-landing-page/SKILL.md).

## Agreement and disagreement with the shared static playbook
| Topic | This course | [Shared static playbook](../../../../playbooks/static-ad-creation.md) | Default |

| Customer language headlines | Yes [L72 00:53](../lessons/072-static-ads-mistakes-to-avoid-4-17.md) | Yes | Same |

| True badges only | Course: "show the badge" ([L74 01:18](../lessons/074-fixing-bad-static-ads-2.md)) but invents scarcity and quotes in "afters" | Only true badges; repeat on landing page | Playbook |
