# UGC creator operations
> How the course runs human UGC: source creators in a fixed priority order, send one two-part brief (creator brief + editor brief) with 5 hooks, body script points and a 10-15 shot B-roll list, review raw footage on five axes and reshoot only for red flags, pick from eight concepts that "print", structure scripts as stacked curiosity loops, and hire editors at the quoted bands ($20-70 per video for plain edits). The same brief also feeds AI UGC: lesson 020 runs the identical Gratsi brief through a Claude skill.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** briefing a human creator or editor; drafting creator outreach; reviewing raw footage; choosing a UGC concept; setting an editor budget or job post; building a creator tracker.
- **Inputs → outputs:** product facts (verified claims list), research doc, chosen concept and script (from the [write-ugc-script skill](../../../../../../.claude/skills/write-ugc-script/SKILL.md)), creator profile (age, gender, niche), budget → outreach DM, filled two-part brief, tracker row, footage verdict note (accept / next-batch fix / reshoot), editor job post.
- **Rules:**
  - **DO** build your own creator roster "so you can have people that you can go back to" [L107 00:00](../lessons/107-how-to-get-ugc-creators-yourself.md).
  - **DO** brief body content as script points, not lines; ask the creator to speak "in your own storytelling voice" [L103 doc](../lessons/103-video-editors.md); the Gratsi brief also says benefits are personal experience, "not a brand claim" [L20 PDF p.3](../lessons/020-claude-skill-seedance-2-0-ai-ugc.md).
  - **DO** send pronunciation notes, outfit change and background instructions before the shoot; a mispronunciation after instructions = reshoot [L104 04:31](../lessons/104-bad-ugc.md); [L103 02:29](../lessons/103-video-editors.md).
  - **DO** review footage with the brief open beside it [L104 03:20–03:53](../lessons/104-bad-ugc.md).
  - **DO** expect raw files only: no captions, transitions, music or voiceover from the creator [L103 doc](../lessons/103-video-editors.md); [L104 00:01](../lessons/104-bad-ugc.md).
  - **DON'T** use testimonial concepts unless the creator genuinely used the product (product seeding, real case study) [L106 05:00](../lessons/106-ugc-concepts-that-print.md).
  - **DON'T** run "pretend to be the founder" [L106 05:56](../lessons/106-ugc-concepts-that-print.md): use a real founder or a clearly labelled actor.
  - **DON'T** post "I need creators, please"; good creators read it as desperate [L107 04:28](../lessons/107-how-to-get-ugc-creators-yourself.md).
  - **IF** the footage is technically flawed but fixable (background clutter) **THEN** flag for the next batch; **IF** performance is flat, instructions were ignored, or tone is wrong **THEN** reshoot [L104 01:20; 06:45–08:17](../lessons/104-bad-ugc.md).
- **See also:** Shared: [creator brief playbook](../../../../playbooks/creator-brief.md), [UGC script writing](../../../../playbooks/ugc-script-writing.md), [UGC script checklist](../../../../rubrics/ugc-script-checklist.md) · Evolve: [creator program setup](../../../evolve/start-here/playbooks/creator-program-setup.md), [Insense creator pipeline](../../../evolve/start-here/frameworks/insense-creator-pipeline.md), [brand ambassador program](../../../evolve/start-here/frameworks/brand-ambassador-program.md) · This course: [Claude Code ad operator](claude-code-ad-operator.md), [AI creator compliance](../concepts/ai-creator-compliance.md), [AI format catalog](../concepts/ai-ad-format-catalog.md), [produce an AI UGC ad](../playbooks/produce-an-ai-ugc-ad.md)

## 1. Sourcing ladder (107)
| Rank | Channel | How | Watch-outs | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Social scouting: Instagram, TikTok, X | Search "UGC creator" + modifier (male, female, senior male, senior female); creators put "UGC" in bios; TikTok talent better on camera, others more polished | No platform clearly better | [L107 00:00–01:45](../lessons/107-how-to-get-ugc-creators-yourself.md) |
| 2 | Fiverr | US/UK hub, pay per project, cheaper | "Rarely find creators who have had good results"; quality control is a "huge factor" | [L107 02:37–03:37](../lessons/107-how-to-get-ugc-creators-yourself.md) |
| 3 | Your own social media | Paid-opportunity post (age range, gender, "portfolio link + email below"); delete once filled | — | [L107 03:37–04:28](../lessons/107-how-to-get-ugc-creators-yourself.md) |
| 4 | Brand-building posts | Wins, failures, vision, behind the scenes; employee-generated content (EGC) "the best way" | EGC needs employee consent | [L107 04:28–05:22](../lessons/107-how-to-get-ugc-creators-yourself.md) |
| 5 | Backstage | Actors, good at emotion; give detailed persona instructions | Actors are not customers: no testimonial framing | [L107 05:22–06:19](../lessons/107-how-to-get-ugc-creators-yourself.md) |
| 6 | UGC platforms (Billo and similar) | Subscription + creator fee | "Pretty against it": creators "churn out creative" | [L107 06:19](../lessons/107-how-to-get-ugc-creators-yourself.md) |

**Red flags:** bad portfolio; copy-pasted content; forces own style on every video; little variety [L107 03:37](../lessons/107-how-to-get-ugc-creators-yourself.md).

**Outreach DM (verbatim):** "Hey [name], love your content on [topic]. We have a brand that does X, Y, Z. We're interested in having you do some juicy content for us. Worth a chat?" Then: brief + budget + "are you ready to proceed?", contracts after [L107 01:45–02:37](../lessons/107-how-to-get-ugc-creators-yourself.md).

**Tracker columns (sheet, per batch):** Name · Portfolio · Source · Age · Gender · Location | Status · Content expected · Last contact date | Shipment status · Fees agreed | Email · Phone · Shipment address · Tracking number · Arrival date | 1st concept + status · 2nd concept + status · Video review comments | Social media posting · Notes [L107 sheet](../lessons/107-how-to-get-ugc-creators-yourself.md). Sample row fee "$1,400 for 2 concepts ($700 each)" is a placeholder, not a benchmark. Keep contact details out of this wiki.

## 2. The two-part brief (103; same structure in the Gratsi brief, 020)
| Section | Content | Rules | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Header | Concept #, product + link, timeline "3 days to film and send… from the product delivery date" | — | [L103 doc](../lessons/103-video-editors.md) |
| 1 Concept | Explanation + idea link | One concept per brief | [L103 doc](../lessons/103-video-editors.md) |
| 2 Hooks | 5 hooks: script + additional (visual) instructions | Filmed separately so editors build variants; no product in hooks unless stated | [L103 doc](../lessons/103-video-editors.md); [L20 PDF p.1](../lessons/020-claude-skill-seedance-2-0-ai-ugc.md) |
| 3 Body | Script points (template example: Realization → Time Drain → Avoidance Pattern → Discovery → Time Reclaimed → Frequency Shift → Real Benefit → Invitation; rewrite for your concept) | Banned words: "game changer", "skyrocketed", "level up", "elevate", "boost", "synergy"; change outfit; articulate | [L103 doc](../lessons/103-video-editors.md) |
| 4 B-roll | Tick-off list (10 in template, 15 in Gratsi) | No voiceover; clean, well-lit, timeless; no seasonal decor; outfit change | [L103 doc](../lessons/103-video-editors.md); [L20](../lessons/020-claude-skill-seedance-2-0-ai-ugc.md) |
| 5 Filming | 9:16, eye level, eyes in line with camera, centre square, talking heads before B-roll, iPhone HDR off, no background noise, no face shadows, no branded clothes, product only as scripted | — | [L103 doc](../lessons/103-video-editors.md) |
| 6 Delivery | Raw only, Google Drive, three folders (Hooks / Talking Head / B-rolls); names "Hook 1", "B-roll 1_Description of action", "Talking-head video" | — | [L103 doc](../lessons/103-video-editors.md) |
| Editor brief | Show what is said; engage every 3-5 s; native social style (TikTok text, no huge graphics); simple text animation; max 2 lines of text; borders off frame edges; only this client's approved assets; table of audio hook / caption hook / B-roll guide | — | [L103 doc](../lessons/103-video-editors.md) |

**Filming mistakes to pre-empt (video):** extreme close-ups; product held far from the face (hold it near, not covering it); action leaving the frame (switch to 0.5x/wide); white-on-white clothing; dark or blown exposure (tap to expose on the phone, not in post) [L103 00:00–02:29](../lessons/103-video-editors.md).

## 3. Concepts that print (106)
| # | Concept | Execution tips (as taught) | Human-only? | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Challenge (try this vs this, taste/durability tests) | Look authentic; entertaining; contrast not trash talk; add stakes and doubt; don't rig it | Real reactions; results must be real | [L106 00:00–01:42](../lessons/106-ugc-concepts-that-print.md) |
| 2 | PAS | Build tension before product; show, not tell every problem | AI can't show real skin/digestion issues without stock | [L106 01:42](../lessons/106-ugc-concepts-that-print.md) |
| 3 | Educational | Explainer/scientist; breakthrough narrative; curiosity loop | — | [L106 01:42–02:38](../lessons/106-ugc-concepts-that-print.md) |
| 4 | Us vs them (video) | Bottom of funnel; educate, don't attack; show the failure ("I heard a crack") | — | [L106 02:38–03:33](../lessons/106-ugc-concepts-that-print.md) |
| 5 | Listicle | "3 lies / 3 mistakes / 3 signs"; act each out; spiciest reason first; personal, not generic | — | [L106 03:33–04:17](../lessons/106-ugc-concepts-that-print.md) |
| 6 | Story | FaceTime-to-a-friend tone; start mid-drama; transformation | — | [L106 04:17–05:00](../lessons/106-ugc-concepts-that-print.md) |
| 7 | Testimonial | Only from product seeding; real case study | Yes: real use required | [L106 05:00](../lessons/106-ugc-concepts-that-print.md) |
| 8 | Founder | Mission, reasons, culture; rally to a vision | Real founder only (course's "pretend" = **don't execute**) | [L106 05:56](../lessons/106-ugc-concepts-that-print.md) |

The coach claims these "are not really types of videos that AI can produce" (instructor claim) [L106 00:00](../lessons/106-ugc-concepts-that-print.md); the AI sections of the course produce several of them (taste test in 020, street interviews in 021). Default: real-reaction concepts (challenge, testimonial, founder) stay human; educational, listicle and story can be AI-produced with disclosure.

## 4. Script structure for creators (105)
| Standard (pink board) | Curiosity-loop version (yellow board) |
|---|---|
| Hook → Problem → (Solution) → Payoff → CTA | Hook → Clarify emotions → Curiosity loop ×3 → Payoff → CTA |

- Make the product the withheld answer: "all you need to do is take this [show powder]" before naming or explaining it; hold the category word ("fiber") until the payoff [L105 00:54–02:45](../lessons/105-make-ugc-scripts-that-scale.md).
- Before → after: "If your skin is breaking out, you need to try this." → "If your skin is breaking out, don't get upset" [L105 00:00–00:54](../lessons/105-make-ugc-scripts-that-scale.md).
- **Conflict:** the shared [UGC script writing playbook](../../../../playbooks/ugc-script-writing.md) runs one main loop from hook to payoff with small line-level curiosity; 105 stacks three or more. Default: one main loop, with delayed reveals inside the body (product shown before named, category word last); test the stacked version as a variant.
- **Don't execute:** "You have until the age of 40 to change this one habit if you want to live longer" (invented deadline), "60% of the US population is deficient", "colon cancer" risk lines (unsourced health claims) [L105 01:50–02:45](../lessons/105-make-ugc-scripts-that-scale.md).

## 5. Footage review rubric (104)
| Axis | Pass | Red flag | Action | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Technical | Even light (natural usually best), clean audio, correct orientation, framed, stable | Dark/blown, noise, shaky, clutter pulling focus | Flag (fixable) or reshoot | [L104 00:50–01:20](../lessons/104-bad-ugc.md) |
| Performance | Energy, believable, natural body language, "looks like a real recommendation" | Reading off a script, flat, monotone, stiff | Reshoot | [L104 02:19](../lessons/104-bad-ugc.md) |
| Script adherence | Strong hook in first seconds, talking points, product + benefits, pronunciation | Missing points; mispronunciation after notes were given | Reshoot | [L104 03:20–04:31](../lessons/104-bad-ugc.md) |
| Editability | Multiple takes, angles or movement, natural cut points | One long shot; takes unusable because of delivery | Ask for more | [L104 05:06](../lessons/104-bad-ugc.md) |
| B-roll | Usable variety | Missing or poor | Reshoot | [L104 06:45](../lessons/104-bad-ugc.md) |
| Claims (repo addition) | Only what the brief allowed | Results/health claims ad-libbed ("it cleared my skin… three days later") | Cut in edit or reshoot the line | [L104 05:36](../lessons/104-bad-ugc.md); [claim audit](../../../stefan-georgi/rmbc-ii/rubrics/llm-output-claim-audit.md) |

Feedback note (derived template in [L104](../lessons/104-bad-ugc.md#prompts--templates-verbatim)): concept · technical · performance · script/pronunciation · editability · B-roll · decision (accept / next-batch fix / reshoot by DATE).

## Compliance gate
- [ ] Testimonial and "my experience" lines only from creators who used the product; no scripted results claims (repo rule; [L106 05:00](../lessons/106-ugc-concepts-that-print.md)).
- [ ] No pretend founders, fake doctors or actors presented as customers.
- [ ] Paid content disclosed; contracts cover usage rights and whitelisting.
- [ ] EGC employees consent in writing [L107 limitations](../lessons/107-how-to-get-ugc-creators-yourself.md).
- [ ] Never use a stranger's photos or a cloned voice as a "creator" (the AI path's Pinterest-face method is not licensed likeness; see [AI creator compliance](../concepts/ai-creator-compliance.md)).
- [ ] Editors use only this client's approved assets [L103 editor brief](../lessons/103-video-editors.md).
