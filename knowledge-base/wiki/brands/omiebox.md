# OmieBox
> Premium kids' bento lunchbox. A **member's client brand: Bren Harper**, roasted in [Roast My Ads #3](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md). Teaches how to read what a winning headline reveals (parents care about their child's lunchroom popularity), how to stay positive when the brand forbids negative valence, and how to make "new colour" launches urgent without fake scarcity.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** tearing down kids/parenting or premium-utility product ads; mining a headline-test winner for the underlying desire; generating many positive phrasings; writing launch/new-colour creative.
- **Inputs → outputs:** a winning headline → 3 variants by desire *type* (peer / status / inner confidence) with subtext; a launch static with "drop" language, a reason/occasion, saturated colour.
- **Rules:**
  - DO read the winner for the sensitivity it reveals, then test the *type* of desire next ([0:39:56–0:46:24](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md)).
  - IF the brand won't go negative THEN find "a hundred different ways to say" the positive winner; text isn't the only lever ([0:42:03–0:43:07](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md)).
  - DO add subtext that pins the audience (e.g. "child's lunchbox") so a fun headline doesn't read as adult ([0:44:30–0:45:18](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md)).
  - DO use **drop** language and a reason for the sale ("Back to school is almost here"); never just "new colours" ([0:46:24–0:48:30](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md)).
  - DO push colour saturation up, not down (saturated = seems bigger = higher quality, per a study she discussed, uncited) ([0:48:30–0:50:35](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md)).
  - DON'T fake limited editions; give real time context instead.

## Transferable lessons (remix for our product)
1. **Headline tests are research**: hold everything constant, then ask *what desire did the winner expose?*
2. **Branch the winner by desire type** (peer / status / inner confidence), one static each, with clarifying subtext.
3. **Positive-only brands**: generate 10+ phrasings of the winner with ChatGPT; pick by desire type, check who each reference attracts.
4. **Launches**: "drop" + reason + time context; never imply forever availability.
5. **Saturate** product colour; pair launch colour with matching creator content.
6. **Justify premium price** with a customer comparison line ("BMW of lunch boxes").
