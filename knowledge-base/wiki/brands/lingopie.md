# Lingopie
> Language learning via TV (a Duolingo competitor) with a free trial that requires a credit card. Guest **Joanna Wallace** used it as her example of **calling out friction with honest humour**; Sarah named the move a "barrier buster". Proposed copy, not a shown ad.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** a product has an obvious friction point (card for a free trial, long shipping, price, weird ingredient) and you're writing ad copy, checkout copy or trial emails.
- **Inputs → outputs:** the friction + the reassurance you can honestly give → a line that names it first, then defuses it.
- **Rules:**
  - DO name the barrier in the customer's words before they do ("Yeah, we're sketched out about having to put in a credit card too") [0:38:48–0:39:50](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-23-guest-session-01-joanna-wallace.md).
  - DO follow with the concrete reassurance (notification before charge, no charge) [0:38:48–0:39:50](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-23-guest-session-01-joanna-wallace.md).
  - DO call out product problems or funnel bottlenecks rather than hiding them: people "respect you for being like 'yeah we know'" [0:39:50–0:40:56](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-23-guest-session-01-joanna-wallace.md).

## Ads and pages discussed

### 1. "Yeah, we're sketched out about having to put in a credit card too" (checkout copy + trial email, proposed)
| Brand / product (niche) | Format | Hook / opening | What works | What fails | Fix suggested | Principle | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lingopie, language learning via TV (subscription app) | Checkout / landing-page copy + trial emails (proposal) | "Yeah, we're sketched out about having to put in a credit card too." | Names the barrier, defuses it with honesty and humour, reassures (notification, no charge) | Card for a free trial is a barrier; people forget to use trials | Trial email: "We know you're super busy… you have three days left… here's a few quick ways you could use it, or let's be real, you've had a busy week, let's just extend it." | Call out friction instead of hiding it ("barrier buster") | [0:38:48–0:40:56](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-23-guest-session-01-joanna-wallace.md) |

## Transferable lessons (remix for our product)
- UGC hook pattern: "Okay, I know [the annoying thing]. I thought the same. Here's what actually happens…"
- Put the barrier buster where the friction happens (card field, shipping line, price block) and repeat it in the ad caption.
