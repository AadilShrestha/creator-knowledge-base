# FireClub Activity Club
> Social activity events for Gen Z (Nerf battles, campfires): "bring back fun". A **member's brand: Gavin S**, roasted in [Roast My Ads #1](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-28-roast-my-ads-1.md), where he tagged his own concepts by valence/intensity and TEEP stage. The course's examples of **one core emotion per ad**, nostalgia hooks, value stacking and short headlines.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** tearing down event / experience / community ads; writing nostalgia or anti-hustle-culture hooks; laying out call-outs on a static; picking one emotion per ad.
- **Inputs → outputs:** an experience offer → a ≤7-word "remember when…" headline, a 1-2-3 value stack of the event's real promise, one CTA, and a named enemy.
- **Rules:**
  - DO keep one headline, one CTA, **one core emotion** per ad ([Roast #1 0:40:44–0:43:52](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-28-roast-my-ads-1.md)).
  - DO cut headlines to 4–7 words; clarity first even in emotional work ([0:30:11–0:31:14](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-28-roast-my-ads-1.md)).
  - DO stack call-outs 1-2-3 on a grid with a bold top line; equal-weight blocks confuse the eye ([0:34:26–0:36:37](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-28-roast-my-ads-1.md)).
  - DO make the value stack the event's real promise ("learn to be a kid again", the campfire you never got) ([0:38:39–0:39:40](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-28-roast-my-ads-1.md)).
  - DO test emotions separately before blending (nostalgia first, then freedom) ([June 4 0:49:22–0:51:32](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-04-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DO qualify into a *situation*, not just a price ("now you have the time and the energy and the money to… just have fun") ([June 18 0:30:27–0:34:41](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-18-live-with-sarah.md)).

## Transferable lessons (remix for our product)
1. **"Remember when…" nostalgia hooks** work for experience products; keep them ≤7 words.
2. **Name the enemy** (hustle culture, LinkedIn small talk, workout plans) and contrast your fun against it.
3. **One emotion per ad**; blend only after each emotion wins alone.
4. **Value stack = the experience's real promise**, stacked 1-2-3 with a bold first line.
5. **Qualify by situation** ("now you have the time and money") to attract buyers who can pay.
