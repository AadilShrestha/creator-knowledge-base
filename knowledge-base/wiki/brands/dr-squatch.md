# Dr. Squatch
> Men's soap brand known for irreverent, relationship-based humour. Spoken example only: Sarah uses it for **owning one emotional territory**, and Chase Mohseni uses it in a hypothetical **ad-to-landing-page vibe mismatch**.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** defining a brand's humour/voice territory; checking that an ad's tone matches the page it sends traffic to.
- **Inputs → outputs:** brand + audience → one owned emotional territory and line; ad + landing page → vibe congruence check.
- **Rules:**
  - DO own one emotional territory and keep saying it (relationship humour + promise) [0:50:21–0:50:37 May 7](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-07-live-with-sarah.md).
  - DON'T send a fun, irreverent ad to a serious/luxury page; "you have under half a second before they bounce" [0:08:37–0:09:38 Chase](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-guest-session-02-chase-mohseni.md).

## Transferable lessons (remix for our product)
- Humour that names a social insecurity, then promises the social win, is a reusable script arc ("you [embarrassing problem] → [product] → people [like you again]").
- Before shipping a funny ad, open the destination page: same voice, same promise, or rewrite one of them.
