# Prose
> Custom haircare and skincare made to order ("personalized to 80+ factors from your in-depth consultation"); a **big-brand PDP Sarah audited** with the [Fogg Behavior Model](../frameworks/fogg-behavior-model.md) as the beauty example (not a member's brand).

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** building or auditing a personalized-product PDP; writing "this will work for you" ability copy; checking motivation intensity of PDP copy.
- **Inputs → outputs:** a PDP → which elements are motivation / ability / prompt → a fix list (list the buyer types, raise word intensity, add prompts).
- **Rules:**
  - DO finish the ability move: after "made for you", list the **types** the visitor can recognize themselves in (coarse, fine, curly, thick-dry-coarse…) "as many of them on a page as you can think of" ([0:11:28–0:17:47](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DON'T switch to ingredients right after a personalization claim; "they went back into look at us". Keep ingredients as a separate section ([0:17:15](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DO keep a risk-reversal guarantee near the CTA ("Love your first order (or it's on us)") = "way more motivating" ([0:14:50](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DON'T write neutral copy ("two gentles in one sentence"); raise word intensity without being harsh ([0:11:28; 0:17:47–0:19:56](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md)).
- **See also:** [Fogg Behavior Model](../frameworks/fogg-behavior-model.md) · [Intensity](../concepts/intensity.md) · [BJ Fogg](../people/bj-fogg.md)

## Transferable lessons (remix for our product)
- For any "made for you" claim, follow with a scannable list of buyer types; the viewer must find themselves.
- In UGC for personalized products, have the creator name her exact type ("I have fine, oily-root hair") so viewers self-identify.
- Pair the guarantee with the CTA; it moves motivation more than more feature copy.
