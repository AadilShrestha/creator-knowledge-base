# Barks & Brownies
> Aesthetic silicone slow-feeder dog bowls (e.g. "Tulip Slow Feeder Dog Bowl, Anti-Gulp", BPA-free, $24); **client of member [Darian James](../people/community-members.md#darian-james)**. The July 16 PDP is named on screen; the July 23 comparison statics are from "Darian's new client, likely Barks & Brownies" (the session page's identification, not confirmed in audio).

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** making ads or PDP visuals for a product whose benefit happens over time (slow eating); writing evaluate-stage comparison statics vs cheap competitors; tagging concepts by zone / intensity / self / need / TEEP stage.
- **Inputs → outputs:** product shot + insight → time-lapse ability visual, "four levels deep" situational copy, a desaturated competitor half.
- **Rules:**
  - DO show time-to-result on the product shot: bowl at start → 10 → 20 → 40 min → finished, as circles on the image ([July 16, 0:27:40–0:29:19](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DO go "four levels deep" on the insight (they feel *defeated*), not one level ("When nothing worked… we tried this!") ([July 23, 0:33:42–0:34:44](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md)).
  - DO use "every" to show repeated failure: "Every other bowl was a failure", "Every bowl we gave him, he destroyed" ([0:33:42](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md)).
  - IF the concept is evaluate-stage THEN be situational and specific; general language belongs to exploration ([0:34:44–0:35:44](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md)).
  - DON'T put the attention colour on the competitor; grey/desaturate their half so colour lands on ours ([0:35:44–0:37:49](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md)).
  - DON'T write quotes that read like news headlines ("Skeptical dog mom becomes believer"); write a real-sounding skeptic quote ([0:34:44](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md)).

## Transferable lessons (remix for our product)
- For "works over time" products, the hero visual should be a time-lapse grid; in UGC, film the bowl at timed intervals.
- Write evaluate-stage hooks as lived, repeated failure in the buyer's words ("every…"), then the switch.
- In us-vs-them visuals, the competitor half is grey; our half carries all colour.
- Tag every concept zone · intensity · self · need · TEEP stage before launch.
