# AMA Biolabs (Yerba Magic)
> "Yerba Magic" appetite-control tea for women ("The ritual that is helping thousands of women feel lighter"), Spanish-language site viewed auto-translated; a **competitor of member [Juan Ecom](../people/community-members.md#juan-ecom)**, audited by Sarah on July 16.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** auditing a supplement PDP that receives listicle/advertorial traffic; writing typed social proof; checking claim congruence across badges.
- **Inputs → outputs:** a mobile PDP → message-order map → fixes (typed proof, asterisk on results claims, remove competing badges).
- **Rules:**
  - DO type the social proof: "Thousands of **tired / frazzled / stressed-out** women", "**350,000 tea drinkers love us**" ([0:51:30–0:59:47](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DON'T show two numbers that tell different stories ("6 Day Results" + "30 Doses"): "either one, I don't have the ability… or two, is this not real" ([0:55:36–0:56:50](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - IF a results claim comes from a listicle THEN keep it only if 100% real and add a "*not typical results" asterisk ([0:55:50–0:56:36](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DON'T say "Discount applied automatically" before any discount is mentioned; order messages logically ([0:58:45–0:59:47](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DON'T overlay elements on top of body text (desktop "waterfall" layout) ([0:51:30](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md)).
- **See also:** [Social proof](../concepts/heuristics/social-proof.md) · [Fogg Behavior Model](../frameworks/fogg-behavior-model.md) · [Cognitive load](../concepts/heuristics/cognitive-load.md)

## Transferable lessons (remix for our product)
- Put a specific emotional state in front of the crowd number ("tired women", "tea drinkers") for both statics and UGC captions.
- Make every claim on the page agree with the advertorial/ad claim that brought the visitor.
- DON'T invent results timelines; only use real ones, with the legal asterisk.
