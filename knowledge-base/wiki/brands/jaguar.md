# Jaguar
> Luxury car maker. Sarah's cautionary example of an **emotional misfire**: a recent campaign went "ridiculously intense, lots of colors" when its luxury buyers wanted low intensity, high valence, and it felt cheap. Described, not shown.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** writing for luxury / high-trust products; checking that colour and energy match what premium buyers want to feel.
- **Inputs → outputs:** luxury brand brief → Zone 1 (high valence, low intensity) styling guardrails.
- **Rules:**
  - DON'T push high-energy, promotion-heavy, very colourful creative onto luxury buyers; it "feels cheap" [04:19–04:54 M3 L4](../courses/tether-lab/03-valence-intensity-model/04-diagnosing-emotional-misfires.md).
  - DO treat premium marketing as perception management; Jaguar "ruined their entire brand" when "they tried to change things" (Sarah's view) [0:29:31–0:30:28 May 21](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-21-live-with-sarah.md).
- **See also:** [Emotional misfire](../concepts/emotional-misfire.md) · [Intensity](../concepts/intensity.md) · [Maserati](maserati.md)
