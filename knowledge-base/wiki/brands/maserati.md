# Maserati (and Lamborghini)
> Luxury car maker. Sarah tells how Maserati showed cars at **boat and private-jet shows** so a $200–500k car looked "like a steal" next to a $2M jet, and how luxury brands qualify buyers by price rather than "limited" claims. Lamborghini is her counter-example of luxury turned "commoditized". Spoken examples; historical details are as Sarah tells them, not verified.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** positioning premium/high-ticket products; choosing comparison anchors; deciding between scarcity claims and price-based exclusivity.
- **Inputs → outputs:** premium product → a far-more-expensive comparison context + qualification framing.
- **Rules:**
  - DO compare your product to something far more expensive when going up-market [0:29:21–0:30:27](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-18-live-with-sarah.md).
  - DO qualify by environment/price: "Maserati doesn't do limited anything. They just jack that price up" [0:19:49](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-18-live-with-sarah.md).
  - DON'T raise prices without checking category perception; Lamborghini became "almost a commoditized luxury" [0:25:06–0:27:17](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-18-live-with-sarah.md).
  - DON'T bundle or discount luxury products [0:10:29–0:12:31](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-18-live-with-sarah.md).
- **See also:** [Anchoring](../concepts/heuristics/anchoring-effect.md) · [Status signaling](../concepts/heuristics/status-signaling.md) · [Jaguar](jaguar.md)

## Transferable lessons (remix for our product)
- Premium price anchors in scripts/statics: put our product beside a pricier alternative ("costs less than one [luxury thing]").
- Replace fake "limited spots" with real qualifiers (price, "see if you qualify", "opens once every six months" if true) [0:17:43–0:20:52](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-18-live-with-sarah.md).
