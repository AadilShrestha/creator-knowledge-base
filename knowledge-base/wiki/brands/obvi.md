# Obvi
> Weight-loss and fat-burner supplements; a brand that "do a lot of psychology-based tests"; **described example** from Sarah's longtime collaborator Ash (Obvi), cited in the July 2 bundling training. No ad was shown.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** designing upsells / bundle sizes for consumables; thinking about what frame of reference the buyer has for "how much do I need".
- **Inputs → outputs:** a consumable's offer ladder → whether buyers have a frame of reference → upsell size and how to frame it.
- **Rules:**
  - IF buyers have no frame of reference for how much they need THEN large quantities can be accepted (Obvi's ~$400 12-month upsell "was crushing") ([0:31:50–0:32:40](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-02-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DON'T oversupply carelessly: Sarah notes women "probably only need maybe like 30 to 60 days"; in the same session she sizes bundles to real consumption to avoid churn ([0:23:45–0:26:12](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-02-live-with-sarah.md)).
- **See also:** [Anchoring](../concepts/heuristics/anchoring-effect.md) · bundle psychology in [July 2](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-02-live-with-sarah.md)
