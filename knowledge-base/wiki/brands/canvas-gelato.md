# Canvas Gelato
> Artisan gelato known for unusual flavour combos (e.g. beef-tallow caramel, candied bacon), selling to consumers and wholesale to cafés/restaurants. It's **community member [Ahmad (Ahmed) Almarri](../people/community-members.md#ahmad-almarri)'s brand**, followed across five calls: roasted statics, a meme ad, a scaled "burned dinner" winner, bundle design and B2B payment terms.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** writing ads for food/dessert/treat brands; turning a generic "real ingredients" static into a specific, situational or funny one; designing food bundles.
- **Inputs → outputs:** a product-only food static → image that carries the emotional promise + ownable specifics + a situational or contrarian hook; a winner → two iteration threads that test *why* it won.
- **Rules:**
  - DO put the emotional promise in the image: if the text says "friends are coming", show friends [0:04:13 Roast 1](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-28-roast-my-ads-1.md).
  - DO show ingredients next to the product ("sight smell") instead of saying "unique" or "real ingredients" [0:05:15–0:07:43 Roast 1](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-28-roast-my-ads-1.md).
  - DO highlight a weird, specific situation ("You already burned dinner…") and then test whether it wins because it's lived or because it's funny [0:35:53–0:41:19 Jun 18](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-18-live-with-sarah.md).
  - IF a meme wins but dies fast THEN make the product the character (snarky gelato), so people attach to product + humour, not to "memes" [0:30:17–0:33:26 Jun 4](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-04-live-with-sarah.md).
  - DON'T bundle flavours (taste is subjective); bundle sizes/quantities in odd numbers (2, 3, 7) with a decoy [0:15:40–0:17:12 Jul 2](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-02-live-with-sarah.md).
- **See also:** [Specificity](../concepts/heuristics/specificity.md) · [Open loop](../concepts/heuristics/open-loop.md) · [Framing effect](../concepts/heuristics/framing-effect.md) · [Visual weight hierarchy](../frameworks/visual-weight-hierarchy.md) · [Creative testing system](../frameworks/creative-testing-system.md)

## Transferable lessons (remix for our product)
- Food creative: show the ingredients, the people and the moment; cut generic claims competitors could also make.
- Hook formula that scaled: **"You [specific mildly bad situation], but it's okay, because [product]."** Write 3 universal-moment and 3 lived-experience versions to learn which driver works.
- Contrarian open loop on the creative, payoff in the caption: "Most people think [category norm]…"
- Memes: make the product the recurring character so humour builds brand memory instead of a meme-lover audience.
- Bundles: sizes not flavours, odd quantities, a decoy, pairings that "make conceptual sense".
