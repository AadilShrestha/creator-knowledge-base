# Jaxxon
> Men's gold chains (Cuban link). The **category leader a member studies: Khalid Alameri** ("we just learn from these guys… the top one on the market"). Audited live in [July 16](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md) as a prompt-heavy page with almost no self activation; paired with Sarah's June 4 advice on men's chains ("quiet respect").

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** tearing down men's jewellery / status-accessory ads and pages; writing copy for male buyers of a "forever piece"; balancing urgency prompts with identity.
- **Inputs → outputs:** a men's jewellery product → an identity headline ("for those who have earned it"), a tone brief (quiet respect, subtle wealth), a cleaned layout with easy price math.
- **Rules:**
  - DO activate a self: Sarah's tested winner "**Jewelry for those who have earned it**" / "for those who have earned respect / finally made it" ([0:44:40–0:51:30](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DO write to respect, kept subtle: Gen Z avoids loud wealth; "we're going after **quiet respect**" ([June 4 0:05:04–0:07:10](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-04-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DO cover function first for men: lasts, water-safe, won't corrode with sweat; then what it signifies ([June 4 0:05:04](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-04-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DON'T lead a first-time visitor with a pop-up or "Best sellers / top picks"; that speaks to repeat buyers ([0:44:40–0:48:22](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DON'T use an image "sliver" layout that makes "your eyes… bounce all over the place" ([0:48:22](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DO keep price math easy ($249 → $199) ([0:50:27](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md)).

## Transferable lessons (remix for our product)
1. **Identity headline for male status buys**: "for those who have earned it" (earned, not bought).
2. **Quiet signals**: subtle lifestyle cues, no flexing.
3. **Prompts aren't enough**: urgency + discounts without self activation leaves motivation on the table.
4. **First-visit test**: would a stranger know what the product is and who it's for without clicking?
5. **Reviews as ability**: "are these dudes like me?"
