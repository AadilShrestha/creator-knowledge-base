# HexClad
> Hybrid cookware sold with Gordon Ramsay. Discussed by its former paid-creative lead **[Joanna Wallace](../people/joanna-wallace.md)** in [Guest Session 01](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-23-guest-session-01-joanna-wallace.md): the course's reference for **brand humor**: borrowed celebrity voice (cursing), customer-written jokes, puns with "meat information", and the recurring **skeptic creator** (Paige). No ads were screen-shared; all examples are described.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** writing humorous statics or UGC for kitchen/home or any "durable, confident" product; designing a recurring skeptic-creator series; turning review/comment language into ad copy.
- **Inputs → outputs:** product traits + reviews/comments + the voice the audience already follows → one humor type, "real user quote" statics, a skeptic-tests-the-claim script, cursing/pun lines that still state a benefit.
- **Rules:**
  - DO borrow the voice the audience already responds to (Gordon's brash cursing on a cocky-confident brand), check the demographics data before backing off after complaints, and don't overuse it ([0:03:04–0:06:10](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-23-guest-session-01-joanna-wallace.md)).
  - DON'T build the account on borrowed equity from another brand: "Rolls-Royce of pans" was 60% of the account until Rolls-Royce threatened to sue ([0:04:58–0:05:20](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-23-guest-session-01-joanna-wallace.md)).
  - DO let customers write the jokes ("a tank… a weapon") and label them "real user quote" ([0:07:12–0:08:17](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-23-guest-session-01-joanna-wallace.md)).
  - DON'T run puns that say nothing in acquisition ads: "to the point" → "sharp as f*ck"; puns need "meat information" ([0:33:34–0:34:35](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-23-guest-session-01-joanna-wallace.md)).
  - DO use a recurring skeptic who tests the claim on camera ("Don't believe us? Paige doesn't believe us either.") ([0:35:37–0:36:43](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-23-guest-session-01-joanna-wallace.md)).
  - DON'T copy HexClad-scale volume-testing playbooks below ~$10M revenue (Chase) ([0:40:00–0:41:01](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-guest-session-02-chase-mohseni.md)).

## Transferable lessons (remix for our product)
1. **One humor type, committed**: pick the register your audience already loves; scale what works without overusing it.
2. **Comments → copy**: the funniest lines are already in your reviews; label them as real quotes (only if real).
3. **Every joke states a benefit** in acquisition (sharp, durable, non-stick).
4. **Skeptic creator** = built-in objection handling and a recurring fluency character.
5. **Avoid borrowed trademarks** as your main claim.
6. **Scale-appropriate testing**: don't copy 9-figure volume playbooks.
