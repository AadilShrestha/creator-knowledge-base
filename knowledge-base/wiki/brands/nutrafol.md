# Nutrafol
> Hair-growth supplement; the **Women's Balance** PDP is "designed for women 45+ experiencing hair thinning"; a **big-brand page Sarah audited** live with the [Fogg Behavior Model](../frameworks/fogg-behavior-model.md) (not a member's brand).

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** auditing or building a supplement PDP / buy box; writing urgency without a countdown; pricing bundle tiers; qualifying the audience in one line.
- **Inputs → outputs:** a PDP screenshot → M / A / P classification of every element → fixes (qualifier line, typed stat, $-savings, easy-math tier prices, urgency icon).
- **Rules:**
  - DO put a qualifier line that ticks every box for the target ("Designed for women 45+ experiencing hair thinning") = ability ([0:29:19](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DO use a **plane** icon over a clock for limited-time offers: "it's leaving soon… very visceral reactions to missing out" ([0:29:19](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DO show dollars saved, not just %; "I can't do that math in my head. What is 10% of 224?" ([0:37:47](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DO make tier prices easy mental math (229, not 224) and make the bundle saving meaningful ($237 vs $224 = "like 10 bucks") ([0:36:44–0:44:07](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DON'T anchor the buy box on the cheapest option if you want bundles; "technically they should be doing this backwards" ([0:35:50–0:41:30](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DON'T use untyped stats ("100% of women…"); say which women ("women with hair loss / thin hair / alopecia") ([0:29:19–0:44:07](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md)).

## Transferable lessons (remix for our product)
- One qualifier sentence under the title ("Designed for [who] experiencing [what]") does ability work on any PDP or static.
- Swap clocks for a departure metaphor (plane) when the offer is genuinely time-limited.
- Price tiers so the saving is obvious in dollars and the math is round.
- In hair-loss, Sarah elsewhere says the industry is too aspirational (Zone 2) and should go Zone 3/4 "and always end it with hope" ([June 4, 0:28:12–0:30:17](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-04-live-with-sarah.md)).
