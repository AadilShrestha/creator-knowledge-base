# Clickmatix
> Australian digital-marketing agency ("Australia's #1 Marketing Agency: 1,100+ businesses"). Its long-form book-a-call lander was submitted by "socket" (probably **Sanket Patel**, wiki inference) and roasted in [Roast My Ads #2](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-25-roast-my-ads-2.md). The course's B2B example: long pages work if formatting carries them.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** auditing a lead-gen / service / B2B landing page; designing testimonial and proof sections; deciding what to animate or highlight.
- **Inputs → outputs:** a long lander → one dominant CTA, a highlight path for the eye, differentiated proof blocks, sharp testimonials.
- **Rules:**
  - DO keep one action and repeat it in every section ("Schedule a call") ([0:53:57; 0:58:14](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-25-roast-my-ads-2.md)).
  - DON'T run two pulsing elements in competing colours; highlight what to read first and animate the CTA in after 1–2 s ([0:51:32–0:52:55](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-25-roast-my-ads-2.md)).
  - DO colour-code repeated blocks (charts) by metric; identical blocks force cognitive work ([0:52:55–0:54:01](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-25-roast-my-ads-2.md)).
  - DON'T use fuzzy screenshot testimonials: "sharper imagery actually communicates quality" ([0:54:48–0:55:31](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-25-roast-my-ads-2.md)).
  - DO use arrows + very large numbers and authority logos ("even if you've only had one") ([0:55:46–0:57:13](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-25-roast-my-ads-2.md)).
  - DO add an ellipsis to a qualifier ("Businesses making over $25K a month…") to pull the reader on ([0:51:32–0:51:45](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-25-roast-my-ads-2.md)).

## Transferable lessons (remix for our product)
1. **One page, one action**, repeated per section.
2. **Qualifier + ellipsis** at the top pre-selects and pulls the reader into the headline.
3. **Risk reversal** ("or we work for free") is a strong B2B offer device.
4. **Proof must look high-quality**: sharp, varied, not duplicated.
5. **Guide the eye** with highlights, arrows and big numbers, not with competing animation.
6. **Long is fine** for considered/knowledge purchases if it's chunked.
