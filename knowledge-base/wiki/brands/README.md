# Brands hub: case files by niche
> The "find examples in my niche" entry point. Every brand whose real ad, landing page or campaign is discussed in Tether Lab has a case file here with the screenshots, teardown rows, Sarah's fixes and transferable lessons. Brands are grouped by niche, then followed by unnamed client/member cases and brands mentioned only in passing.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** researching a competitor or category before writing ads: open your niche below, read the case files, and take the *mechanism* (angle, hook structure, emotional move, proof type), not the asset.
- **Inputs → outputs:** our product's niche (or a competitor's name) → the relevant case files → patterns to remix (with zone, self and heuristic tags) and mistakes to avoid.
- **Rules:**
  - DO start with the closest niche, then scan the [mechanism index](#find-by-mechanism) for cross-niche patterns. Most lessons transfer (e.g. The Only Bean's gain framing applies to any craving product).
  - DO check the **Relationship** column. Member ads were roasted live (real, often small accounts); "Sarah's client" ads are her own work, shown as models; big-brand ads (AG1, Nutrafol, Prose, Jaxxon) were audited from the outside.
  - DO treat zone and self tags marked "(wiki tag)" in the case files as interpretation, not Sarah's verdict.
  - DON'T reuse invented proof seen in the cases (Net Positive's "Only 9 sets left", ChatGPT's "Featured in Outside and Men's Health" for High Camp). Use only true claims.
  - DON'T copy headlines verbatim into our ads. Rewrite them for our product, claims and customer language.
- **See also:** [Tools hub](../tools/README.md) · [Community members](../people/community-members.md) · [Sarah Levinger](../people/sarah-levinger.md) · [Valence–intensity diagnostic](../frameworks/valence-intensity-diagnostic.md) · [Three selves diagnostic](../frameworks/three-selves-diagnostic.md) · [TEEP journey](../frameworks/teep-journey.md)

**Zone key:** Zone 1 = positive/calm · Zone 2 = positive/intense · Zone 3 = negative/calm · Zone 4 = negative/intense ([emotional zones](../concepts/emotional-zones.md)). **Selves:** [Actual](../concepts/actual-self.md) · [Ideal](../concepts/ideal-self.md) · [Ought](../concepts/ought-self.md).
