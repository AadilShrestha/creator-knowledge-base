# Domino's
> Pizza chain. A sponsored Domino's Facebook ad is the course's illustration of **the subconscious stopping on an ad that matches a present physical state** (hunger). Shown, not critiqued.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** choosing the moment/state an ad or UGC hook speaks to (hungry, tired, cold, bored); explaining why a scroll-stop happens before conscious reading.
- **Inputs → outputs:** product + the customer's likely state at viewing time → a hook/visual that matches that state.
- **Rules:**
  - DO tie the ad to a need the viewer has right now (the pizza mechanism) [03:12–03:57 M2 L1](../courses/tether-lab/02-heuristics-101/01-targeting-the-decision-making-brain.md).
  - DO lean on what the viewer already associates with the category; the decision to stop uses stored "maps" [05:20 M4 L3](../courses/tether-lab/04-self-targeting-system/03-why-one-self-isnt-enough-to-scale.md).
  - DON'T rely on rational pros/cons to earn the stop [M2 L1](../courses/tether-lab/02-heuristics-101/01-targeting-the-decision-making-brain.md).
- **See also:** [Subconscious vs conscious mind](../concepts/subconscious-vs-conscious-mind.md)

## Transferable lessons (remix for our product)
- Open UGC scripts on the state the viewer is in when they'll see the ad ("It's 3pm and you're starving…"), then show the product close up.
- For food, a tight appetizing close-up does the stopping; the offer can be secondary.
