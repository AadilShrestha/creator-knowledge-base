# Liquid Death
> Canned water sold with skull-and-crossbones rebellion and the line "Murder your thirst". Never shown on screen; it's **Sarah's go-to spoken example** of picking a loud brand enemy, repeating one phrase forever, doubling down on bad reviews, and going "crazy wild the opposite direction" from a category.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** defining a brand's core message/enemy before writing ads; differentiating in a commodity category where every competitor sounds the same; deciding whether a humour/rebellion voice can carry a brand.
- **Inputs → outputs:** category + competitors' messaging → one enemy, one repeatable phrase, an "opposite direction" creative territory.
- **Rules:**
  - DO pick an enemy and say it "so definitively" you never deviate; that's how Liquid Death "took market share fast" [0:44:14–0:45:10 May 7](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-07-live-with-sarah.md).
  - DO repeat one phrase for years ("murder your thirst"); neutral or absent taglines (Dasani, Fiji, Aquafina) are forgettable [0:50:37–0:50:59 May 7](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-07-live-with-sarah.md).
  - DO go the opposite way from competitors instead of copying them ("if Liquid Death is any indicator…") [0:42:22 Jun 11](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-11-live-with-sarah.md).
  - IF negative reviews pile up THEN you can "double triple down" and use them as humour, as Liquid Death does [0:40:56 Joanna](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-23-guest-session-01-joanna-wallace.md).
  - DON'T assume a loud enemy is required: Apple's enemy was subtle and never stated [0:25:50–0:27:16 May 21](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-21-live-with-sarah.md).

## Transferable lessons (remix for our product)
- Write the brand's enemy in one sentence ("we're not for people who…") and one tagline; reuse it in every script's close.
- In a crowded category, map competitors' colours, words and tone, then pick the opposite on purpose.
- Mine negative reviews for self-deprecating hooks only if the brand voice can commit to it long term.
