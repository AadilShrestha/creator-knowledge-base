# Olivia Burton London
> British women's jewellery and watch brand. A **PDP Sarah audited live** with the Fogg Behavior Model in [Live With Sarah, July 16](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md), as the answer to "what would be a motivator for this model… for a jewelry brand?" The case for **reverse social proof (0 reviews)** and ability cues ("does it fit me?").

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** auditing a jewellery / accessories PDP or a product page an ad sends traffic to; writing "who is this for / what do I wear it with" copy; deciding whether to show review widgets.
- **Inputs → outputs:** a PDP → a Motivation / Ability / Prompt checklist with fixes (hide zero reviews, move upsells, you-language, scale shots).
- **Rules:**
  - DON'T show "0 Reviews": a nudge **away** from buying (social proof in reverse) ([0:19:56–0:27:14](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DO give ability cues: length/dimensions on a body, "what can I wear this with / who usually buys this" ([0:19:56–0:27:14](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DON'T split intent on the PDP with a gift-bag add-on; move it to cart (wiki summary of Sarah's critique).
  - DO size body text for the likely (older) buyer; tiny copy is "probably just skipping this entirely".
  - DO switch brand-talk sections ("Eyecatching essentials") to "you" language; slow image carousels and show scale.
- **See also:** expected [Fogg Behavior Model](../frameworks/fogg-behavior-model.md) · [Social proof](../concepts/heuristics/social-proof.md) · [Landing-page psychology audit](../frameworks/landing-page-psychology-audit.md) · [Jaxxon](jaxxon.md) (men's jewellery contrast)

| Olivia Burton London (jewellery/watches) | PDP | "Signature: Petite Honeycomb Silver Tone Necklace" | Slightly more intense language than Prose ("delicate", "understated elegant"); Affirm "4 interest-free payments of $20" = ability for budget shoppers; length/dimension info = ability | Body text too small for a likely older buyer; **0 Reviews** = nudge away; gift-bag add-on splits gift vs self buyers; "Eyecatching essentials" = brand talking about itself; fast images hide scale | Remove the review widget when there are no reviews; move gift bag to cart; add "what can I wear this with / who usually buys this"; "us" → "you"; slow images, show scale on a body | Ability = "does it fit me?"; social proof works in reverse at zero | [0:19:56–0:27:14](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md) |

- Sarah: jewellery is tricky "because people are buying for different reasons… the motivation, the prompts… and the ability is different for everyone" ([0:19:56](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md)). Women buy pieces to complete outfits; men buy one forever piece ([June 4 0:04:02–0:05:04](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-04-live-with-sarah.md)).
- Ability defined (to Gabriela): "Am I capable of getting this right now? Is it easy for me to do?" e.g. "it'll fit around your wrist at this length, it'll hang about this low" ([0:25:11–0:26:13](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md)).

## Transferable lessons (remix for our product)
1. **Zero proof is negative proof**: hide empty review widgets; show other proof instead.
2. **Answer "does it fit me?"** with dimensions on a body, styling pairings and "who usually buys this".
3. **One intent per page**: gift upsells belong in cart.
4. **Readability for the real buyer age**.
5. **BNPL line** = cheap ability boost for priced-up accessories.
