# Playbook: Creator / Production Brief
> Turn a finished [UGC script](ugc-script-writing.md) into a brief a creator (or an AI-production agent) can shoot without guessing: **who** to cast and why (messenger, in-group, face), the **tone and zone**, do/don't, a **shot list**, **props and context priming**, the **deliverables matrix** (hooks × bodies), and the **seed-asset approval** that lets variations ship fast. Includes when AI UGC is and isn't acceptable.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** a script has passed the [UGC script checklist](../rubrics/ugc-script-checklist.md) and needs to be produced.
- **Inputs → outputs:** `ugc_script` + angle brief + product truth sheet + landing page + budget/production mode (real creator / in-house / AI) → `creator_brief` (template below).
- **Rules:**
  - DO cast on purpose: "Who says the message is actually a little bit more important than what the message is." People close to the brand (employees) are especially credible [J 0:36:43–0:37:48] ([messenger effect](../concepts/heuristics/messenger-effect.md)).
  - DO cast a peer with the same problem when the angle is Actual-self ([in-group bias](../concepts/heuristics/in-group-bias.md)); for authority-sceptical audiences, use validation or the *type* of authority they trust [May21 0:42:33; Jul2 0:13:34–0:15:40] ([validation over authority](../concepts/validation-over-authority.md)).
  - DO brief the **face**: expression, brows, lids, smile vs smirk, posture must say the same thing as the line [R3 0:06:17–0:12:36] ([model image psychology](../concepts/model-image-psychology.md)).
  - DO tell creators to say the script **in their own words**; "it's the way Sarah would say it" is the failure mode [May14 0:22:33–0:22:44].
  - DO hire a performer for comedy; non-comedian comedy "is obvious" and copycats "fell flat" [J 0:43:04–0:44:08].
  - DO get one **seed asset** approved, then ship its variations without new sign-off: "permission equals speed" [Chase 0:48:08].
  - DON'T let a creator add claims, results, discount codes or "before/after" footage that isn't true for them and for our product.
  - DON'T use AI presenters for trust-dependent brands [May14 0:26:56–0:27:15] ([AI UGC tools](../tools/ai-ugc-tools.md)).

## Procedure

### Step 1: Persona and casting
Pick the messenger type from the angle, then write the casting spec.

| Angle signal | Cast | Why | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Actual self, "people like me" | Peer with the same problem and life stage | In-group bias | [in-group bias](../concepts/heuristics/in-group-bias.md) |
| Skeptical, hype-saturated category | Skeptic performer (deadpan / reluctant), ideally recurring | Voices the viewer's disbelief | [skeptic format](../frameworks/skeptic-format.md) [J 0:35:37–0:36:43] |
| Evaluation stage, process/quality story | Employee or founder close to the product | "Knows better than even our top customers" | [J 0:36:43–0:37:48] |
| Authority-sceptical audience (e.g. women 30+ in holistic health) | Validating peer, or practitioner type they trust (naturopath, nurse) — not a generic doctor | Right *type* of authority | [May21 0:42:33]; [Jul2 0:13:34] |
| Character around the customer (vet, trainer, partner) | Actor in that role, clearly a story | Narrator = avatar test | [May14 0:33:17; 0:40:09] |
| Humour angle | Comedian/actor-creator, not a strategist | Timing | [J 0:43:04–0:45:08] |

Casting spec (fill all): age range and life stage; body type (normal, un-airbrushed for millennial women [R3 0:10:32–0:11:33]); expression the hook needs (confident = brows centred, lowered lids, smirk; seductive = one brow higher — "two totally different faces" [R3 0:06:28–0:08:23]); posture (open, chest out for confidence [R3 0:11:33]); hair colour as a **test variable**, not a rule [R3 0:05:14–0:06:28]; speech style (pace, accent, vocabulary = the customer's words). Recurring character? If yes, name, persona and catchphrase ([fluency mechanism](../concepts/fluency-mechanism.md)).

### Step 2: Tone and zone
Translate the script's zone into delivery (derived from [tone shifting → UGC](../frameworks/tone-shifting.md#applying-this-to-ugc-video-wiki-extrapolation-not-from-course)):

| Zone | Delivery energy | Framing | Light / grade | Expression |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Z1 feel-good | calm, warm, unhurried | wider, more of the room | morning/window light, soft, desaturated | relaxed, eyes soft |
| Z2 hype | quick, definitive verbs, high energy | close-ups, punch-ins | saturated, bright | excited, wide eyes, smile |
| Z3 pain whisper | sighs, subtle sarcasm, "the tension before the solution" | medium, static | muted | tired, wry |
| Z4 danger | urgent, interruptive | tight, jarring | harsh, high contrast | intense |

Add: humour type (one only; periods-not-exclamation deadpan [J 0:19:44–0:20:49]) and the **pace rule**: slow down, pause on key lines; cuts every 2–4 s [May14 0:43:30–0:48:12].

### Step 3: Do / don't for the creator
**Do:** say it your way; keep the hook line's meaning exactly; show the real product and real use; mention honest doubts; look into the lens for "you" lines; keep the product label readable in the turn and CTA beats; film the hooks as separate takes.
**Don't:** promise results you haven't had; add numbers, discount codes, medical claims or comparisons not in the script; name or show competitor brands; use copyrighted music; read from the screen; open on skin with no context for women's products [R3 0:20:31–0:21:08].

### Step 4: Shot list
One row per beat of the script, plus coverage:

| # | Beat | Shot | Framing | Action / props | Line (gist) | Overlay | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook H1 | selfie to camera | close | <…> | <…> | "<≤7 words>" | 0–3 s |
| … | | | | | | | |
| B1 | b-roll | ingredient/texture macro | macro | pour, swirl, fabric movement ([sight smell](../concepts/sight-smell.md)) | — | — | 3–5 clips |
| B2 | b-roll | product in hand, label visible | close | — | — | — | 2 clips |
| B3 | b-roll | the problem scene | medium | the real mess / the situation | — | — | 2 clips |
| S | stills | 5–10 frames for statics | — | — | — | — | — |

Stills matter: screen grabs from videos become statics [Chase 0:53:38]; a strong frame can feed [static ad creation](static-ad-creation.md).

### Step 5: Props and context priming
- Every prop tells the viewer who this is for; visitors read scenes "based upon their own experiences" (basketball beside a nursing-dress model → "boy mom") [R2 0:25:31–0:27:32] ([hero-image ↔ copy congruence](../concepts/hero-image-copy-congruence.md)).
- List: required props (life-stage cues, the "old way" items for contrast, ingredients), banned props (competitor products with visible branding, status/price cues that mis-signal), setting (home room, time of day matching the zone's light).
- If the concept involves guilt or shame, stage it on a character, not the viewer ([mirror effect](../concepts/mirror-effect.md)).

### Step 6: Deliverables matrix (pipeline design)
| Item | Spec |
|---|---|
| Hooks | H1–H3, each a separate clean take, 2 takes each |
| Bodies | Body A + Body B (one difference) |
| Cuts | 6 assembled cuts (H × B) at master length + 30 s + 15 s if requested |
| Format | 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920, safe zones clear for overlays; 4:5 crop safe |
| Audio | clean lav/phone audio, no music bed (added in edit) |
| Raw | all raw clips + b-roll + 5–10 stills |
| Captions | burned-in versions + clean versions |
| Naming | `<STAGE>_<EMOTION>_<angle>_ugc_h<n>b<n>_<length>_v1` |
| Rights | paid-usage rights and whitelisting period agreed in writing (general practice, not from the course) |
| Disclosure | creator follows platform/ad disclosure rules (general practice) |

### Step 7: Approval via seed asset
1. Approve the **script skeleton + creator + first cut (H1×A)** as the seed [Chase 0:47:21–0:50:29].
2. Pre-approve the variation types that may ship without a new round: hook swaps from the approved list, b-roll swaps, caption/overlay style, length cuts, background music.
3. Anything that changes a claim, the offer, the CTA promise or the core message = **new approval**.
4. Before launch, re-check **vibe** against the landing page (tone, palette, promise) [Chase 0:08:26–0:09:38] ([3 Vs](../frameworks/three-vs-of-creative.md)).

## AI UGC: when it's acceptable
| Situation | Decision | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Trust- or identity-dependent brand (skincare, supplements, health, baby) | **Real creator.** No synthetic faces | "over time you're going to teach them that this is not trustworthy" [May14 0:26:56–0:27:15] |
| Budget for narration only | AI **voice** over real footage/b-roll ([ElevenLabs](../tools/elevenlabs.md)); don't slow it until robotic | [May14 0:22:33–0:24:30; 0:48:52] |
| Novelty / weird exploration test | AI visuals OK for clearly-not-real content: personified products, the AI cow, AI-generated model images for statics | "Weird works" [R3 0:56:53–0:58:30]; Darian's Midjourney images [R3 0:36:47]; "see if chat can generate some new… red hair models" [R3 0:08:23] |
| Volume test: 50–100 AI ads | **No.** "The best one might get $4 of spend" | [Chase 0:42:06–0:44:13] |
| AI creator persona | Only if it's built as an organic creator first; "people connect to people" | [May14 0:30:53–0:31:50] |
| Results so far | Members who tested Creatify: "none of the videos converted"; "some audiences find them really interesting and some really hate them" | [May14 0:30:53–0:31:50] |

If AI is used: never make a synthetic person deliver a testimonial or claim personal results; label it where the platform requires; keep it to clearly fictional/novelty roles (pipeline design).

## Output: creator brief
```markdown
### Creator brief: <SCR-id> · <product> · <angle>
**Why this ad exists (1 line):** <core message + target buyer>
**Casting:** messenger type <peer | skeptic | employee | founder | character | practitioner> · age/life stage <…> · body <…>
  · expression for hook <…> · posture <…> · voice/pace <…> · recurring character: <name, catchphrase | no>
**Tone & zone:** Zone <n> → energy <…>, framing <…>, light/grade <…>, expression <…> · humour type <one | none>
**Say it your way:** keep the meaning of every line; hook lines (H1–H3) keep their key words: "<…>"
**Do:** <list> · **Don't:** <list incl. no added claims, no competitor brands, no skin-first opening>
**Shot list:** <table from Step 4>
**Props / setting:** required <…> · banned <…> · location <…> · time of day <…>
**Proof on camera:** <demo / real review / time sequence> (truth_ref: <…>)
**Deliverables:** H1–H3 × A/B = 6 cuts · lengths <…> · 9:16 · raw + b-roll + stills · captions on/off · naming <…>
**Rights & disclosure:** <usage period, whitelisting, disclosure>
**Seed approval:** seed = H1×A first cut · pre-approved variations: <…> · needs new approval: claims, offer, CTA, core message
**Landing page:** <url> · vibe check: <pass/fail>
**Production mode:** real creator | in-house | AI voice over real footage | AI novelty visuals (justify)
```

## Quality gates
- [ ] Casting choice is justified by the angle's self and messenger logic.
- [ ] Expression, posture and props all repeat the core message.
- [ ] Every on-camera proof has a `truth_ref`; the creator is told not to add claims.
- [ ] Deliverables produce the full hooks × bodies matrix with one-variable differences.
- [ ] AI use, if any, passes the AI table above.
