# AI UGC tools (Creatify, Arcads, Icon)
> AI-generated "UGC" ads with synthetic presenters. Members asked about **Creatify** (creatify.ai) and **Icon**; Chase Mohseni named **Arcads** and Icon. Both Sarah and Chase are **sceptical**: Sarah for trust reasons, Chase because volume testing with AI ads doesn't produce signal. Members who tested Creatify reported no conversions.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** deciding whether to brief an AI presenter or a real creator; planning a high-volume test.
- **Inputs → outputs:** brand goal (trust/affinity vs cheap novelty test) → a production decision (real creator, voiceover, or AI presenter) with the course's caveats.
- **Rules:**
  - DON'T use AI UGC for brands that need trust or identity attachment: "over time you're going to teach them that this is not trustworthy, because they're using people who are not real" ([May 14 0:26:56–0:27:15](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-14-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - IF you test AI creators, THEN build an organic following for the AI creator first. That's what Sarah sees in the people succeeding: "People connect to people… you can't quite just post it in an ad and hope it works" ([May 14 0:30:53–0:31:50](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-14-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DON'T flood an account with 50–100 AI ads. "The best one might get $4 of spend" and never signal (Chase) ([Chase 0:42:06–0:44:13](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-guest-session-02-chase-mohseni.md)).
  - DO use an AI **voice** for narration on a budget instead ([ElevenLabs](elevenlabs.md)). It's the faces that trigger distrust.
