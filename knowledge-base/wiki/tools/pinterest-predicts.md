# Pinterest Predicts (and Pinterest Trends)
> Pinterest's yearly forecast of what will trend. Sarah describes it as "Pinterest's best guess as to what's gonna trend this year" and says it has "an 80% accuracy rate" ([Roast #3 0:26:19–0:27:22](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md)). In the course it's a source of **cheap visual "skins"** (colours, motifs) to put on a proven message. Pinterest Trends is also one of her monthly market-research sources.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** you have a winning static and want a low-cost variant (new colour/motif skin, same message); briefing visual direction for a launch; checking which aesthetics your audience is moving toward.
- **Inputs → outputs:** a proven ad + the current Pinterest Predicts trends → 1–2 "trend-coded" variants of that ad (same headline and model, new palette/motif).
- **Rules:**
  - DO keep the winning message and swap only the visual skin (Net Positive "cherry-coded" statics kept "Ditch Polyester") ([Roast #3 0:26:19–0:30:30](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md)).
  - DO change as few variables as possible. Sarah flagged that the cherry versions changed several things at once ([Roast #3 0:28:25](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md)).
  - DO push trend colours *more* saturated, not less (OmieBox "aura" pastels → Lisa Frank palette) ([Roast #3 0:48:30–0:50:35](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md)).
  - DON'T treat the "80% accuracy" figure as verified. It's Sarah's statement, not cited.

## Pinterest Trends and Pinterest behaviour
- **Market research (monthly):** Pinterest Trends sits with Google Trends, Keyword Planner, Reddit and AnswerThePublic in Sarah's first research layer ([June 4 0:25:00–0:28:12](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-04-live-with-sarah.md)) → [Customer research stack](../frameworks/customer-research-stack.md).
- **Fashion signal:** Pinterest users pin near-identical shirts that differ only by pants, background or model, which is why she tells a print-on-demand seller to "pulse your 3-2-2 with different backgrounds" ([June 11 0:10:29–0:17:00](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-11-live-with-sarah.md)).
