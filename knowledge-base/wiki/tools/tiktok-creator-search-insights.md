# TikTok Creator Search Insights
> TikTok's creator tool that shows search demand. Sarah's fourth research layer (**social trends**): "it'll show you the terms that are searched for the most but don't have enough content to serve" them. "Invaluable… especially when it comes to generating videos" ([June 4 0:25:00–0:28:12](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-04-live-with-sarah.md)).

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** choosing topics and hooks for UGC/video concepts, especially to find searched-for topics in the niche that few creators have covered.
- **Inputs → outputs:** category and problem keywords → high-search, low-content terms → video hooks and script topics.
- **Rules:**
  - DO check it about **weekly** (Sarah runs every research layer except market research weekly) ([June 4 0:25:00–0:28:12](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-04-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DO use TikTok to double-check that a customer type found elsewhere actually exists and talks about the problem ([May 7 0:31:08–0:32:06](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-07-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DO reverse-engineer viral posts' format structure (hook, scene, lighting, clothes) for organic tests ([June 11 0:33:57–0:36:06](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-11-live-with-sarah.md)).
- **See also:** [Customer research stack](../frameworks/customer-research-stack.md) · [AnswerThePublic](answerthepublic.md) · [Tools hub](README.md)

## Context in the course
- **Research stack position:** (1) market research monthly (Google Trends, Keyword Planner, Pinterest Trends, Reddit, AnswerThePublic) → (2) consumer research (NLP on reviews) → (3) behaviour/emotional research (picture-based CIM panels) → (4) **social trends: TikTok Creator Search Insights** ([June 4 0:25:00–0:28:12](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-04-live-with-sarah.md)).
- **TikTok format copying:** Faraz Khan had 66 TikTok videos flop (~200 views each), then copied a viral creator's whole script "my way" and got **5 million views** on the 67th ([June 11 0:35:00–0:36:06](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-11-live-with-sarah.md)). This was an organic result, not an ad test.
