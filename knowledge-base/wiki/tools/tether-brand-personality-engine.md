# Tether Brand Personality Engine (BPE)
> Sarah's own tool for brand-core work. It identifies **the core emotion a brand solves** across all customer data, plus **the identity/enemy to go after "to show people who you are not"** ([May 7 0:43:58–0:45:16](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-07-live-with-sarah.md)). She offered to build it for the community. It isn't in the saved course.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** before writing angles for a brand with no clear core message, or one that runs "millions of ads" to different use cases.
- **Inputs → outputs:** all customer data (reviews, surveys) → one core emotion + one brand enemy → a repeatable brand line (e.g. "a reliable piece of calm").
- **Rules (the method, since the tool itself isn't available):**
  - DO find the one emotion every use case shares, from repeated customer phrases ("I know I can go back to this over and over" → reliable; "quiet the noise" → calm) ([May 7 0:41:58–0:43:43](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-07-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DO pick an enemy and say it definitively, like Liquid Death ([May 7 0:44:14–0:45:10](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-07-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DO repeat the line until it's boring: "if you say it a lot it becomes yours" ([May 7 0:48:09–0:50:21](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-07-live-with-sarah.md)).
