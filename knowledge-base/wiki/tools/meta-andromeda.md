# Meta ads algorithm (Andromeda)
> How the course talks about Meta's ad delivery system. Sarah's model: the algorithm is a **library** that finds more readers for the "books" (ads and conversions) you put in it. **Andromeda** is Meta's 2025 delivery update; a member reported it had rolled out and made new pixels train fast ([May 21 0:28:26](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-21-live-with-sarah.md)). The practical upshot repeated across sessions: **the message and creative diversity do the targeting**, so creative strategy matters more than account hacks.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** planning a test batch, deciding how varied a creative set must be, or explaining why a new angle/format is needed to reach new people.
- **Inputs → outputs:** current ad set (messages × formats) → a plan that adds new messages and formats so the algorithm can find new buyers.
- **Rules:**
  - DO diversify **formats and messages**, not just one variable. "One message with four formats, then test five messages: 5 × 4 = 20 ads" ([June 11 0:51:49–0:52:52](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-11-live-with-sarah.md)). Five near-identical statics make the algorithm think "all of these ads are the same… I don't know who to send this to".
  - DO keep running proven ads but add ads that tell the algorithm to "go find people who you've never seen in this library before" ([May 7 0:39:20–0:40:48](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-07-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DO retrain with creative, not a new pixel: "you can retrain this algorithm within like a couple hours" by changing the message (a pet-supplement brand switched "50% off" → "You're the type of pet parent who cares about what's inside their pet supplements" and the account changed at once) ([May 21 0:19:49–0:21:20](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-21-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DO pick one account structure and stick with it: "you're continuously training the math on the back end" ([Roast #3 0:23:50–0:24:54](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md)).
  - DO decide what you're testing first, then the structure: heuristic tests in their own ad sets; beat-the-winner tests next to the winner ([Roast #3 0:24:54–0:26:19](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md)).
  - DON'T read a frequency of ~2 as audience fatigue. It's more often creator/format boredom; change the product treatment ([Roast #3 0:51:41](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md)).
