# Canva
> The design tool used across the course for static ads. Sarah's own client statics are Canva files (e.g. "HighCampFlasks-NLP-Ads-0225", "Maelove-CIM-Ads-0125"), members share roast boards in Canva, and [CreativeOS](creativeos.md) templates export to it.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** producing or briefing static variants: rounded text bubbles, sharp recreated testimonials, desaturating a competitor half, rebranding a template.
- **Inputs → outputs:** a static concept + brand assets → an editable static (or a design brief that names the Canva treatment).
- **Rules:**
  - DO put key text in **rounded bubbles/pills** with Canva's text "background" effect. Sarah calls rounded shapes her "best CRO hack" ("feel less scary") ([Roast #1 0:48:07–0:49:46](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-28-roast-my-ads-1.md)).
  - DO recreate fuzzy screenshot testimonials sharply (photo, name, stars). Fuzzy reads as low quality ([Roast #2 0:54:48–0:55:31](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-25-roast-my-ads-2.md)).
  - DO desaturate or grey out the competitor half of a comparison so colour lands on our product ([Roast #3 0:36:47](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md)).
  - DO name files/pages by the insight they test (Sarah's pages: "HCF-Competence-Details", "HCF-Esteem-Moments", "M-Ad-Switch") so the need/emotion travels with the asset ([Roast #1 0:37:26; 0:49:30](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-28-roast-my-ads-1.md)).
  - DON'T copy a template's assets. Rebrand the layout with our product and claims ([CreativeOS](creativeos.md)).
- **See also:** [Visual weight hierarchy](../frameworks/visual-weight-hierarchy.md) · [CreativeOS](creativeos.md) · [Tools hub](README.md)
