# ElevenLabs
> AI voice generator Sarah recommends for **low-budget voiceover ads**: "I think it's like five bucks a month", any country or accent, male/female/gender-neutral voices, drag-and-drop the script, "a lot of those voices too sound very very real" ([May 14 0:23:34–0:24:00](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-14-live-with-sarah.md)).

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** you have a story-structured script but no creator budget, or need a quick voiceover to test a script before paying for UGC.
- **Inputs → outputs:** a finished script (e.g. Pixar or Freytag structure) → a natural-sounding voice track (~30 s to render) → cut into video in an editor.
- **Rules:**
  - DO pick natural voices over robotic ones. Robots "pull out the emotion. You can't hear the inflection" ([May 14 0:24:00–0:24:30](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-14-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DO prefer a voiceover to music + text: "Voiceovers are fantastic. They convey emotion a whole lot better" ([May 14 0:23:05](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-14-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DON'T slow the speech down too far. It "gets robotic" ([May 14 0:48:52](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-14-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - IF you clone a voice (creator or your own content), THEN get the person's permission. *(Wiki addition; the course only says cloning is possible.)*
  - DON'T confuse a voiceover with AI UGC. Sarah backs AI voices for narration but warns against fake AI faces for trust brands ([AI UGC tools](ai-ugc-tools.md)).
