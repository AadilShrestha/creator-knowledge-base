# Actual Self
> **"Who I am right now."** The self that wants its current identity **validated**. Ads aimed at it say "*you're already this type of person*" and let the product slot into the story the customer already tells about themselves.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** classifying an ad or script as Actual-Self, or writing an Actual-Self variant (identity affirmation) of a winning ad. Useful for AOV-focused and conversion-stage creative.
- **Inputs → outputs:** copy or script → Actual score + cues found. Or: product + persona identity ("wine lovers", "the gear guy") → Actual hook and script lines.
- **Rules:**
  - DO look for or write: "you already…", "made for people like you", "for [identity] who know", "this is so me", "you earned this".
  - DO open UGC with the creator naming the identity they already hold ("As someone who always brings the wine…"), then show the product fitting their routine. *(Script pattern derived from the course lines. The course shows no UGC example.)*
  - DO use peer proof (reviews from "real [identity]").
  - DON'T promise transformation ("become", "unlock"). That moves the ad to Ideal.
  - DON'T judge Actual variants on CTR. Read AOV and stability [L8 02:12].
- **See also:** [3 selves](three-selves.md) · [Rewriting ads for new selves](../frameworks/rewriting-ads-for-new-selves.md)

One of the [3 selves](three-selves.md) in Self-Discrepancy Theory, alongside the [Ideal Self](ideal-self.md) and the [Ought Self](ought-self.md).

## Definition
- Lesson 4: when the brain is "switched into actual self mode, you're thinking a lot about who you currently believe you are. So this self is kind of aligned with **consistency of identity**." It isn't thinking about the future. It looks for "opportunities to validate what I already do, who I already am, lots of reassuring messages." [03:20]
- E-book: "Seeks validation, consistency, and identity alignment. Ads that reinforce this self feel comforting and familiar."
- Prompt doc: "Reinforces who the customer *already is*. Seeks identity validation, consistency, and alignment." / "Validates current identity (comfort, pride, belonging)."
- Orientation: **present**. "I just want my current sense of self to feel reassured." [L4 05:59]

## Why it matters
- It's the self most brands **neglect** in favour of Ideal, which leaves room for new buyers.
- Performance signature (lesson 8): **lower CTR, higher AOV, stable returns**. Present-moment people aren't "click happy". But when the ad fits their identity, "your identity gets activated after the ad", so they spend more [02:12].
- E-book funnel guide: good for **Consideration** ("build trust") and **Conversion** ("anchor in comfort"). "Calm their nerves with familiarity."
- In prompt 2's example table, the Actual-dominant ad has the **highest ROAS** ("High ROAS from validation-heavy copy").

## How to target it
- **Message:** "you're already this type of person", "and then the product is just going to fit… into that existent narrative in their head" [L4 04:25]. "You're not trying to change or transform anybody."
- **Lines from the course:**
  - "You already know this works" · "This is made for people like you" · "Stick to what feels right" · "You earned this" (L4)
  - "Finally, cookware that gets *you*." (e-book)
  - "Skin that feels like you again. / A routine that fits your life, not the other way around" (e-book rewrite)
  - "For Wine Lovers Who Know / You already bring the good stuff — now bring the gear to match." (L7)
  - "He put up the tent, but you brought the flask." (L5)
- **Customer language to look for** (reviews, surveys, DMs): "This is so me." "Exactly what I needed." **Emotion:** comfort, pride. **Trigger:** seeking validation or alignment.
- **E-book prompt:** "Write an ad that validates who the customer already is. Emphasize identity, comfort, and ease."
- **Social proof style:** peer proof from people like them, e.g. "★★★★★ 900+ 5-Star Reviews from real wine lovers" (L7).

*"He put up the tent, but you brought the flask." Sarah: it reinforces "who they already are… The language is really calm. It's proven. It's tested." [L5 01:30]*

- Identity callouts read as Actual. In the lesson 6 ChatGPT run, "Rosé All Day" scored **Actual 8 / Ideal 3 / Ought 2**: "strong identity validation for wine lovers" [L6 05:37]. High Camp's ads overall leaned Actual through "wine lovers, outdoorsmen, whiskey drinkers" callouts [L6 06:37].

## Common mistakes
- Turning an Actual ad into a transformation promise. Once you say "become", you're writing for Ideal.
- Judging Actual ads on CTR alone. Their strength is AOV and stability.
