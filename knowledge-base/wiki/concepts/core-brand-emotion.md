# Core Brand Emotion and Core Brand Message

> **"It's so critical you have a core emotion that applies to everyone… they're just using that core emotion for their specific problem."** Find the one emotion every use case shares, turn it into a short line ("a reliable piece of calm"), and **"never stop saying it. It's going to get boring as hell."** ([Live, May 7](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-07-live-with-sarah.md) [0:41:58–0:43:43; 0:48:09])

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** a brand has many use cases or avatars and its ads fatigue; writing the line every UGC script should land on; tearing down a competitor's brand position (what phrase do they own?).
- **Inputs → outputs:** reviews (repeated phrases across use cases) → one core emotion + a 3–5-word brand line + 3–4 core messages, then emotional "flavours" for individual ads.
- **Rules:**
  - DO find the emotion shared by **every** use case in review language ("I know I can go back to this over and over" → *reliable*; "quiet the noise" → *calm*) ([Live, May 7](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-07-live-with-sarah.md) [0:41:58–0:43:12]).
  - DO lead with the emotion and use features as the logical justification: "reliable source of calm in a delicious format" [0:45:54–0:48:09].
  - DO repeat the line everywhere for years: "if you say it a lot it becomes yours" [0:48:09–0:50:21].
  - DO vary emotions *around* the core message (risk reduction, aspirational, contrary): core message = **vanilla ice cream**, "we're just going to put a lot of flavoring on top of it" [0:52:00–0:53:39].
  - DO pick about **four core messages** for the brand and say each many ways, in many formats ([Live, June 18](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-18-live-with-sarah.md) [0:57:11–0:59:15]).
  - IF fashion or home decor THEN features first; ELSE emotion first ([Live, May 7](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-07-live-with-sarah.md) [0:46:46–0:47:20]).
  - DON'T try to be "everything to everyone… this is how we just stagnate" [0:44:14].
- **See also:** [Brand enemy](brand-enemy.md) · [One core emotion per ad](one-core-emotion-per-ad.md) · [Fluency mechanism](fluency-mechanism.md) · [Processing fluency](heuristics/processing-fluency.md) · [Tether Brand Personality Engine](../tools/tether-brand-personality-engine.md) · [Marketing to Mindstates](../people/will-leach.md)

## Definition
- **Core brand emotion:** the single emotion the product delivers across all buyers and use cases (calm, security, respect…). "Yes… you can use the mindstates to get to these" ([Live, May 7](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-07-live-with-sarah.md) [0:43:43]).
- **Core brand message:** that emotion compressed into a line the brand repeats forever ("a reliable piece of calm", Liquid Death's "murder your thirst", Sarah's "psychology-based creative").
- **Brand vs performance:** "Brand comes from the internal and performance comes from external. We get to decide what we want to say, but the customer is going to tell us how they want us to communicate it" [0:47:47–0:48:09].

## Why it matters
- Per-use-case ads "sometimes work and then sometimes fatigue… I can't keep running millions of ads to all these different use cases" [0:41:36–0:41:58]. One message "they can now broadcast to every use case on the planet" [0:43:12–0:43:43].
- Repetition builds ownership and fluency: Sarah repeated "psychology-based creative" for ~5 years until competitors who say it get told "that's Sarah" [0:48:27–0:49:37]; alternatives kept losing "because it's the easiest way for people to process it" [0:51:00] ([processing fluency](heuristics/processing-fluency.md)).
- Brands with no owned phrase (Dasani, Fiji, Aquafina, "never had a tagline" or only neutral ones) are forgettable [0:50:48–0:51:00].
- Very large brands get "familiarized in the culture" and can drift from their original message (Disney, Pepsi, Coca-Cola, Apple) ([Live, May 21](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-21-live-with-sarah.md) [0:40:56–0:41:44]). Smaller brands can't.

## How to use it
1. Collect reviews across every use case (~80 paragraph-length is Sarah's benchmark, [Roast My Ads #1](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-28-roast-my-ads-1.md) [0:47:07]).
2. Find the phrases that repeat regardless of use case. Map them to an emotion (optionally Leach's nine).
3. Compress to a 3–5-word line: emotion first, feature/format second ("delicious piece of calm").
4. Add a brand enemy if one fits ([brand enemy](brand-enemy.md)).
5. Write ~4 core messages under the brand line; each gets many phrasings and formats ([Live, June 18](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-18-live-with-sarah.md) [0:58:14]).
6. Every ad: one core message + one emotional flavour ([one core emotion per ad](one-core-emotion-per-ad.md)).

## Deploy in a UGC script
| Script slot | Pattern | Example line |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | Use-case specific (brain fog, ADHD, focus…) | "My brain fog was wrecking my afternoons." (illustrative, not from course) |
| Turn | The shared emotion in customer language | "I know I can go back to this over and over." (review language, [Live, May 7](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-07-live-with-sarah.md) [0:42:12]) |
| Landing line | The core brand message, verbatim, every time | "A reliable piece of calm." [0:43:12] |
| Justification | Feature second | "…in a delicious format." [0:47:20] |

**Visual cues:** the same end card / phrase / mascot across every video so the brand becomes recognisable ([fluency mechanism](fluency-mechanism.md)).

## Spot it in a competitor ad
- A phrase that recurs across all their ads, packaging and organic posts. That's their owned territory; don't copy it ([competitor research: go the opposite way](heuristics/von-restorff-effect.md)).
- No recurring phrase = an unowned brand you can out-position.
- Tag: `concept-core-brand-message` with the line verbatim.

## Rules of thumb
- **IF** a brand has many use cases **THEN** find the one shared emotion in reviews and make it the brand message [0:41:58–0:43:43].
- **DO** repeat the line until you're sick of it [0:48:09].
- **DO** keep the core message stable; vary emotion, format and heuristics around it [0:52:00; Live, May 7, 0:14:42].
- **DON'T** iterate the core message when iterating; iterate the format ([Live, May 7](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-07-live-with-sarah.md) [0:14:42–0:15:48]).

## Common mistakes
- Picking the founder's favourite message instead of the customer's repeated phrase.
- Switching the brand line every quarter.
- Leading with features outside fashion/home.
