# Messenger Effect

> **"Who says the message is actually a little bit more important than what the message is."** (Sarah, [Guest Session 01](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-23-guest-session-01-joanna-wallace.md) [0:37:21]). The same script lands differently depending on the face and voice delivering it, so casting is a strategic decision, not a production detail.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** deciding who delivers a UGC script (customer, employee, founder, expert, skeptic, a character around the buyer, an AI avatar); tearing down why a competitor's ad works (is it the line or the person?); writing creator briefs.
- **Inputs → outputs:** audience beliefs about who to trust + product type → a messenger choice (with the reason), the messenger's relationship to the brand stated on screen, and the script voice that fits that messenger.
- **Rules:**
  - DO pick the messenger before polishing the line. Employees close to the brand are strong messengers: "she's really close to us, she works for us" ([Guest 01](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-23-guest-session-01-joanna-wallace.md) [0:36:43–0:37:48]).
  - DO use a recurring **skeptic** messenger who voices the viewer's disbelief and tests the claim ("Don't believe us? Paige doesn't believe us either.") [0:35:37–0:36:43].
  - DO choose the *type* of authority this audience trusts: functional practitioners, acupuncturists or naturopaths instead of MDs for women 50+ let down by doctors ([Jul 2](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-02-live-with-sarah.md) [0:13:34–0:15:40]).
  - DO use a lived-experience messenger: "How I…" activates authority proof because "I actually went through this experience" ([May 7](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-07-live-with-sarah.md) [0:22:12–0:22:46]).
  - DO tell the story from any character around the buyer (the vet, the trainer, the pet sitter) and treat each as a new messenger test ([May 14](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-14-live-with-sarah.md) [0:33:17–0:34:21; 0:40:09–0:40:30]).
  - DON'T use AI UGC avatars when brand trust and identity attachment matter: "over time you're going to teach them that this is not trustworthy" ([May 14](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-14-live-with-sarah.md) [0:26:56–0:27:15]).

## Definition
Sarah names it during the HexClad discussion: "This is something that's called the messenger effect, where basically who says the message is actually a little bit more important than what the message is." ([Guest 01](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-23-guest-session-01-joanna-wallace.md) [0:36:43–0:37:21])

## From the course
- **Employees as messengers (Sarah, Guest 01):** "They've done studies on taking an employee of the business and putting them in an ad"; she is "so close to the brand that she probably knows better than even our top customers", so a receptionist, admin or PM sharing their experience "is critical for how the message gets interpreted." (Studies not cited.) [0:36:43–0:37:48]
- **Paige the skeptic (Joanna, HexClad):** an actress-comedian-creator hired as the recurring skeptic "putting herself in the viewer's shoes of 'I don't believe you, this is an ad'". Gordon says the egg won't stick; Paige: "All right, Gordy… whoa… go off, Gordy." [0:35:37–0:36:43] → [Skeptic format](../../frameworks/skeptic-format.md)
- **Celebrity voice (HexClad × Gordon Ramsay):** the brand borrowed Gordon's brash, cursing register; Sarah calls this "very halo effect" [0:42:01] → [Authority borrowing (halo)](authority-borrowing.md).
- **"How I" = lived-experience messenger (May 7):** "How I activates the authority proof heuristic… I actually went through this experience and I now know all of the speed bumps… somebody else went and did the hard work for me." [0:22:12–0:22:46] → [Risk-reduction hooks](../risk-reduction-hooks.md)
- **Right type of authority (Jul 2):** "It's not that authority bias won't work, it just means what type of authority do we need to use… What type of doctor you use, though: critical." [0:13:34–0:15:40]
- **Validation beats the expert (May 21):** 30+ women "sensitive to authority" respond to "your gut feeling is right… you know your body best" — the buyer herself becomes the trusted messenger [0:42:33–0:43:57] → [Validation over authority](../validation-over-authority.md)
- **Any character can narrate (May 14):** "You can do it for any character that's involved with your customer": the vet, "the downstairs neighbor", pet sitters. The vet Dr. Riley worked as "the trusted authority and emotional bridge", and a recurring character becomes a [fluency mechanism](../fluency-mechanism.md) [0:33:17–0:34:21; 0:36:54–0:37:48; 0:48:35]
- **Real people (Jun 4, chat):** a competitor crushing with an organic ad from a normal person — "Sometimes you could crush just putting real people in your ads." ([Jun 4](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-04-live-with-sarah.md) [0:22:56])
- **AI UGC (May 14):** "Humans are master pattern recognizers when it comes to faces and voices." Novelty can win short-term, but avoid for brand affinity; attendees' Creatify tests didn't convert; people succeeding build an organic following for the AI creator first [0:26:17–0:31:50].

## Deploy in a UGC script
| Script slot | Pattern | Example line |
|---|---|---|
| Hook (employee) | Name the relationship on screen | Lower-third: "Sam, our customer-support lead" + "I answer 200 emails a week about [problem]. Here's what actually works." (illustrative, not from course) |
| Hook (skeptic) | Viewer's disbelief voiced by a recurring character | "Don't believe us? [Name] doesn't believe us either." (HexClad pattern, [Guest 01 0:36:43](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-23-guest-session-01-joanna-wallace.md)) |
| Hook (lived experience) | "How I [outcome] in [time]" | "This is how I [achieved outcome] in [time frame]." ([May 7 0:21:48](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-07-live-with-sarah.md)) |
| Story frame | Third-party narrator close to the buyer | "Dr. Riley had seen it all… But the one thing she always dreaded? Dental checkups." ([May 14 0:36:30](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-14-live-with-sarah.md)) |
| Proof beat | Trusted-type expert for *this* audience | "My naturopath finally explained why…" (illustrative, built on [Jul 2 0:15:05](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-02-live-with-sarah.md)) |

**Visual cues:** name + role lower-third; workplace setting for employees (warehouse, kitchen, support desk); the skeptic's arms-crossed / side-eye opener; real, un-airbrushed faces; no uncanny AI faces for trust-led brands.

## Spot it in a competitor ad
- Who is speaking, and what is their stated relationship to the brand? (customer, employee, founder, celebrity, expert, skeptic, character)
- Is the same messenger recurring across ads (a series, a character)? That's a deliberate messenger strategy.
- Does the audience trust that messenger type? (doctor-led ads aimed at doctor-sceptical audiences = mismatch)
- Tag: `heur-messenger` + messenger type (`msgr-employee`, `msgr-skeptic`, `msgr-peer`, `msgr-expert`, `msgr-celebrity`, `msgr-ai`).

## Rules of thumb
- **IF** the audience distrusts conventional experts **THEN** switch the messenger type, don't drop authority ([Jul 2](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-02-live-with-sarah.md) [0:15:05]).
- **IF** a story character wins **THEN** keep using that character ([May 14](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-14-live-with-sarah.md) [0:48:35]).
- **DO** let UGC creators say the script in their own words ([May 14](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-14-live-with-sarah.md) [0:22:33]); a messenger reading Sarah's phrasing stops sounding like themselves.
- **DO** test the same script with two messengers before rewriting the script.

## Common mistakes
- Treating casting as cosmetic and only A/B testing words.
- Doctor/lab-coat messengers for audiences who've been dismissed by doctors.
- Hiding the messenger's link to the brand (employee) when the link is the point. *(Wiki caution: never pose an employee as an independent customer.)*
- AI avatars for brands whose growth depends on trust and identity attachment.

## Pairs with / distinctions
- **vs [Authority bias](authority-bias.md):** authority is one *kind* of messenger. Messenger effect is the general rule that the speaker shapes interpretation (peer, employee, skeptic, celebrity, expert).
- **vs [Authority borrowing (halo)](authority-borrowing.md):** borrowing projects an external name's trust onto the brand; messenger effect is about who is on camera.
- Pairs with [in-group bias](in-group-bias.md) (peer messenger), [validation over authority](../validation-over-authority.md), [risk-reduction hooks](../risk-reduction-hooks.md), [fluency mechanism](../fluency-mechanism.md) (recurring character).
