# Hidden Truth

> **"Hidden Truth taps into the sense that there's something important being overlooked or kept from the viewer. It scratches a deeper intellectual itch—delivering insight that feels both subversive and enlightening."** ([Prompt Library #6](../../courses/tether-lab/07-prompt-library/06-pattern-disruption-and-hidden-truth.md))

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** there's a real, specific fact about the category the buyer hasn't been told (how it's made, why the usual fix fails, what the label hides); skeptical audiences who reward brands that "tell the truth others avoid"; as the payoff after a pattern-break hook.
- **Inputs → outputs:** a verifiable overlooked fact + why it matters to the buyer → a reveal line, proof, and the product positioned as the honest alternative.
- **Rules:**
  - DO "Reveal something real, specific, and enlightening—something they've missed, misunderstood, or never been told." (#6 step 3)
  - DO make the payoff "satisfying and credibility-building. The viewer should feel smarter, more aware, and aligned with a brand that tells the truth others avoid." (step 4)
  - DO make it specific (numbers, names of ingredients/processes). See [specificity](specificity.md).
  - DO use it as a belief-changer: Sarah's way to shift the subconscious is to make people "second-guess" a belief, e.g. "people just say it's genetics. It's not. You can reverse it." ([2025-07-09](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-09-live-with-sarah.md) [0:54:54–0:55:50]).
  - DON'T fabricate or exaggerate a "they don't want you to know" claim. Sarah on data: "Don't fabricate data." ([2025-07-09](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-09-live-with-sarah.md) [0:15:04–0:15:58]).
  - DON'T slip into conspiracy tone. Subversive, yes; paranoid, no.
- **See also:** [Pattern disruption](pattern-disruption.md) · [Counterintuitive insight](counterintuitive-insight.md) · [First principles thinking](first-principles-thinking.md) · [Reframing](reframing.md) · [Data-driven insight](data-driven-insight.md)

## Definition
- Prompt Library #6 (verbatim above). Target reaction: "Why hasn't anyone else said this?"
- Module 3's Zone 3 alteration prompt uses the same move in a quiet register: "Rewrite this ad as if your product is quietly calling out a truth they've been avoiding." ([Valence + Intensity prompts](../../resources/valence-intensity-prompts.md))

## Deploy in a UGC script
*Derived guidance.*

| Script slot | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | Promise the reveal | "Nobody in [category] tells you this." (illustrative) |
| Reveal | The specific overlooked fact | "[N]% of [product type] contain [X]." (illustrative; must be true and sourced) |
| Implication | Why it matters to *them* | "That's why your [result] never lasted." (illustrative) |
| Product | The honest alternative | "So we [did the opposite]." (illustrative) |
| Zone 3 version | Quiet call-out | "It's 2025. Why are we still drinking stale beans?" (course example, [Fixing the Tone](../../courses/tether-lab/03-valence-intensity-model/05-fixing-the-tone-without-rebuilding-the-ad.md) [08:30]) |

**Visual cues:** label/ingredient close-ups, "what's actually inside" cutaways, creator reading the fine print, whispered or leaning-in delivery.

## Spot it in a competitor ad
- "What they don't tell you", "the dirty secret of…", "I worked in [industry] for 10 years…", "read the label", "here's why X doesn't work".
- Tag `lever-hidden-truth`; record the **truth claim** and its proof type (stat, insider, demo) since that's what makes it transferable.

## Common mistakes
- The "truth" is common knowledge, so no itch.
- Truth with no product link, i.e. an education ad that sells for the category, not for us.
- Unprovable claims that trigger comment-section fights or ad rejections.

## Pairs with
- **Prompt pairing:** [Pattern Disruption + Hidden Truth](../../courses/tether-lab/07-prompt-library/06-pattern-disruption-and-hidden-truth.md): jolt, then clarity.
- **Distinctions:** [counterintuitive insight](counterintuitive-insight.md) sounds *wrong* at first; a hidden truth is simply *unknown*. [First principles](first-principles-thinking.md) *derives* the truth; hidden truth *reveals* it. [Data-driven insight](data-driven-insight.md) is a hidden truth backed by a stat. Best-fit selves (wiki tag): [Actual](../actual-self.md) (validation: "I knew something was off") and [Ought](../ought-self.md) (doing it right).
