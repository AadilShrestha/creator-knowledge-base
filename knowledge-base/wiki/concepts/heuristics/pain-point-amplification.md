# Pain Point Amplification

> **"Pain Point Amplification deepens discomfort by shining a light on what isn't working. It makes the current reality feel *unbearably inefficient, slow, costly, or embarrassing*—turning discomfort into motivation."** ([Prompt Library #7](../../courses/tether-lab/07-prompt-library/07-what-if-scenario-and-pain-point-amplification.md))

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** the buyer tolerates a problem they've normalised and needs a push to act; problem-aware audiences; categories stuck in upbeat aspirational messaging that ignores the real feeling; tearing down "agitate" style ads.
- **Inputs → outputs:** the pain in the buyer's own words (reviews, Reddit) + its daily cost (time, money, confidence, opportunity) → a problem beat that turns intensity up, then the product as the way out.
- **Rules:**
  - DO make the current state "feel unsustainable" (#7 step 2) and "Amplify the frustration of inaction. Highlight how much time, money, confidence, or opportunity they're losing." (step 3)
  - DO use one specific, lived detail rather than a generic complaint: "I'm so done seeing more hair in my shower than on my brush." ([2025-07-09](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-09-live-with-sarah.md) [0:42:23]).
  - DO split one product into several pain-point narratives and test them (Joe Hides's founder stories: thinning hair / hair loss / itchy scalp, [2025-07-09](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-09-live-with-sarah.md) [0:48:04]).
  - DO "Position your offer as the bridge" (step 4) and end with hope ([2025-06-04](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-04-live-with-sarah.md) [0:29:15–0:30:17]).
  - DON'T overdo it in saturated categories. Module 3 says Zone 3 pain-point messaging is where "a lot of marketers live" ([The 4 Emotional Zones](../../courses/tether-lab/03-valence-intensity-model/03-the-4-emotional-zones.md) [02:59–03:14]).
  - DON'T shame. Amplify the *situation*, not the person's failure.
- **See also:** [What-if scenario](what-if-scenario.md) · [Loss aversion](loss-aversion.md) · [Vulnerability](vulnerability.md) · [Contrast](contrast.md) · [Emotional zones](../emotional-zones.md) · [Intensity](../intensity.md)

## Definition
- Prompt Library #7 (verbatim above). The pairing should make the audience "feel the gap between where they are and where they could be—and the frustration of staying stuck."
- In valence/intensity terms (wiki mapping): amplification = keep valence low and **raise intensity**, moving a Zone 3 "pain-point whisper" toward Zone 4 ([Intensity](../intensity.md), [Emotional zones](../emotional-zones.md)).

## Deploy in a UGC script
*Derived guidance; course lines cited.*

| Script slot | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | Lived, specific pain | "I'm so done seeing more hair in my shower than on my brush." ([2025-07-09](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-09-live-with-sarah.md) [0:42:23]) |
| Problem (amplify) | Stack the daily costs | "That's [N] minutes every morning, [N] products in the bin, and I still [failure]." (illustrative) |
| Cost of inaction | What staying stuck costs | "Every month I waited, [loss]." (illustrative) |
| Bridge | Product as the way out | "This is the first thing that [result]." (illustrative) |
| Hope | Close on relief | "You shouldn't have to experience this, but you are. There is hope." ([2025-06-04](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-04-live-with-sarah.md) angle line) |

**Visual cues:** the pain shown, not told (hair in the drain, overflowing drawer of failed products, the "my countertop" shot), harsher lighting and tighter framing in the problem beat, then a visible tonal lift at the bridge.

## Spot it in a competitor ad
- Problem beat longer than the product beat; lists of frustrations; "Tired of…?", "Still dealing with…?", "If you're sick of…".
- Tag `lever-pain-point-amplification`; note which pain and which costs (time/money/confidence/embarrassment) are stacked.

## Common mistakes
- Generic pain ("tired of bad skin?") instead of a lived detail.
- Amplifying with no bridge or hope, which leaves the viewer in Zone 4 with nowhere to go.
- Too many pains in one ad. One pain per ad, one ad per pain narrative.

## Pairs with
