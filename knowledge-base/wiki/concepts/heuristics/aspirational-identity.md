# Aspirational Identity

> **"It's not just about what they want to buy—it's about who they want to *become*."** (Prompt Library #8). Sell the upgraded self-image, and show that it's "already within reach."

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** writing the "after" state, the CTA or the identity line of a script, or tagging which self a competitor's transformation ad speaks to.
- **Inputs → outputs:** who the customer wants to become (from reviews, community language) → an identity-forward promise ("you become X") and a CTA framed as stepping into it.
- **Rules:**
  - DO "sell who they become when they use it," not the product (#8 step 4).
  - DO show the identity as reachable now ("already within reach"), not as a distant dream.
  - DO treat this as an **[Ideal Self](../ideal-self.md)** lever, and plan [Actual](../actual-self.md)/[Ought](../ought-self.md) variants so you don't fall into the Ideal-Self angle rut ([Module 4](../../courses/tether-lab/04-self-targeting-system/README.md)).
  - DON'T stop at a surface-level change. #8 asks for "an identity shift."
- **See also:** [Prompt #8](../../courses/tether-lab/07-prompt-library/08-contrast-and-aspirational-identity.md) · [Contrast](contrast.md) · [Status signaling](status-signaling.md) · [Ideal self](../ideal-self.md) · [Three selves](../three-selves.md)

## Definition
"**Aspirational Identity** taps into the audience's **ideal self-image**. It's not just about what they want to buy—it's about who they want to *become*. Great ads speak to this upgraded identity and show that it's already within reach." (Prompt Library #8; PDF p.16)

Target feel from the prompt: "**emotionally charged**, **inspiring**, and **identity-forward**—ideal for building long-term brand alignment and action," "as if the viewer is being called to step into a more powerful version of themselves."

## Deploy in a UGC script
*Derived guidance: slot mapping and lines adapted from #8. Example lines are illustrative, not from course.*

| Script slot | Pattern | Example line (illustrative) |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | Name the identity the viewer wants | "If you want to be the person who actually finishes the 6am run…" |
| Mechanism | The product as the vehicle of the shift | "This is what made 'morning person' stick for me." |
| Proof | Creator *living* the identity, not just holding the product | Creator lacing shoes at sunrise, unhurried |
| CTA | Join or become, not buy | "Be that person tomorrow. Link below." |

**Visual cues:** confident posture, the aspirational environment (clean kitchen, finish line), light that brightens from the "now" scenes, the creator looking at the camera as a peer who got there.

## Spot it in a competitor ad
- Copy in the "becoming" register: "step into", "unlock your next level", "finally becoming", "the version of you who…". These are the Ideal-Self signals from [Module 4](../../courses/tether-lab/04-self-targeting-system/04-the-3-selves-that-drive-every-buying-decision.md) [02:43], [03:15].
- The product is barely shown and the lifestyle or identity carries the ad.
- Tag: `lever-aspirational-identity`, and self = `ideal`.

## Common mistakes
- Making it the only angle. Most DTC brands already over-serve the Ideal Self, and that pool "dries up" ([Why One Self Isn't Enough to Scale](../../courses/tether-lab/04-self-targeting-system/03-why-one-self-isnt-enough-to-scale.md)).
- An identity too far from the viewer's reality, so it reads as fantasy, not "within reach."
- Generic aspiration ("be your best self") with no specific identity. See [specificity](specificity.md).

## Pairs with
- **[Contrast](contrast.md)** (Prompt #8): contrast shows the gap, aspiration names the destination.
- **vs [Ideal self](../ideal-self.md):** the Ideal Self is the *target* (who the buyer wants to be). Aspirational identity is the *lever* that addresses it.
- **vs [Status signaling](status-signaling.md):** status is about being *seen* by others as elite or in the know. Aspirational identity is about who *I* become. They often stack.
- **[Tribal belonging](tribal-belonging.md)**, so the identity becomes a group to join. Valence is positive: "Aspirational marketing is almost always positive valence" ([Understanding Valence and Intensity](../../courses/tether-lab/03-valence-intensity-model/02-understanding-valence-and-intensity.md)). Zone 2 is the natural home ([Emotional zones](../emotional-zones.md)).
