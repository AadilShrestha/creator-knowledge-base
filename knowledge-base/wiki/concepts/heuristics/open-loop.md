# Open Loop

> **"Open Loops are incomplete thoughts or stories that provoke the brain's need to resolve tension. Think cliffhangers, teaser lines, or insights that are *almost* fully revealed."** ([Prompt Library #2](../../courses/tether-lab/07-prompt-library/02-status-signaling-and-open-loop.md))

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** you need watch-time or a click (hook → payoff later in the video, or ad → landing page), or you're tearing down an ad whose hook promises something it delivers only at the end.
- **Inputs → outputs:** the payoff (insight, result, reveal) we can withhold → a teaser hook, the point in the script where the loop closes (or the CTA that closes it).
- **Rules:**
  - DO "Hint at something exclusive or transformational they could unlock—but don't give the full answer right away. Keep them leaning forward."
  - DO "End with a tease, not a resolution. The goal is to get the viewer to *click, watch, or scroll* to close the loop." (Prompt #2)
  - DO make sure the loop *can* be closed honestly: on the landing page, in the last 3 seconds, or in the product.
  - DON'T open more than one loop in a short ad. One question, one payoff.
  - DON'T bait. If the payoff is weaker than the tease, the viewer feels tricked and the brand pays for it.
- **See also:** [Curiosity gap](curiosity-gap.md) · [Suspense](suspense.md) · [Micro-story](micro-story.md) · [Status signaling](status-signaling.md)

## Definition
- Prompt Library #2 (verbatim above). The pairing's output must "Leave an unresolved curiosity gap that pulls the viewer deeper" and feel "cool, aspirational, and incomplete in the best way possible."

## Deploy in a UGC script
*Derived guidance.*

| Script slot | Pattern | Example line (illustrative, not from course) |
|---|---|---|
| Hook (open) | Tease the result, hide the how | "I stopped [annoying problem] in a week, and it wasn't [obvious fix]." |
| Middle (hold) | Delay with context, keep the promise alive | "Before I tell you what it was, here's what I'd tried…" |
| Close (pay off) | Reveal = product moment | "It was this." (product on screen) |
| Alt: CTA closes it | Withhold the last step | "The full routine's on the page." |

**Visual cues:** on-screen text "wait for it" / "#3 is the one", covered or blurred product until the reveal, cut away just before the answer.

## Spot it in a competitor ad
- Hooks with an unfilled slot: "The one thing…", "I didn't expect…", "Nobody told me this about…", "Here's what happened when…".
- Product hidden until late; a numbered list counting down to the "real" tip.
- Tag `lever-open-loop`. Note **where** the loop closes (in-video timestamp vs landing page), because that's the transferable structure.

## Common mistakes
- Loop never closed (rage-bait comments, wasted spend if the click lands on a generic page).
- Payoff unrelated to the product, so the reveal doesn't sell.
- Over-long hold in a short placement: viewers drop before the reveal.

## Pairs with
- **Prompt pairing:** [Status Signaling + Open Loop](../../courses/tether-lab/07-prompt-library/02-status-signaling-and-open-loop.md). The status world gives the *why I care*, the loop gives the *why I keep watching*.
- **Distinctions:**
  - [Curiosity gap](curiosity-gap.md) is the *state* (an information gap the brain itches to close). An open loop is the *device* that creates and holds it.
  - [Suspense](suspense.md) is an open loop inside a story: tension "through incompletion", "a moment before the moment" (#12).
  - [Pattern disruption](pattern-disruption.md) earns the first second; the open loop earns the next fifteen.
