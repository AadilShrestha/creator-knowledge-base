# Counterintuitive Insight

> **"A truth that sounds *wrong* at first, but clicks when explained."** (Prompt Library #13). Opening with "actually…" or flipping a cultural norm creates cognitive dissonance, "a kind of tension that demands resolution."

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** the category runs on a shared "best practice" or myth your product contradicts, you need a myth-vs-truth hook, or you're tearing down a contrarian competitor ad.
- **Inputs → outputs:** a belief the audience holds that's "flawed, outdated, or too simplistic" + the true mechanism + a specific number or example → a flip hook, a specific proof line, and the product as the way to act on it.
- **Rules:**
  - DO start from a belief the audience *actually* holds: "a best practice, a 'rule,' or a popular myth" (#13 step 1).
  - DO flip it "without being contrarian for its own sake" (#13 step 2).
  - DO anchor the flip in [specificity](specificity.md): "real numbers, comparisons, or examples" (#13 step 3).
  - DO frame the brand as "the enabler of this new way of thinking" (#13 step 4).
  - DON'T flip something true just for the shock. If the explanation doesn't "click", you've only made noise.
- **See also:** [Prompt #13: Counterintuitive Insight + Specificity](../../courses/tether-lab/07-prompt-library/13-counterintuitive-insight-and-specificity.md) · [Specificity](specificity.md) · [Hidden truth](hidden-truth.md) · [Reverse psychology](reverse-psychology.md) · [First principles thinking](first-principles-thinking.md)

## Definition
"**Counterintuitive Insights** are sticky because they challenge what people believe to be true. When an ad begins with 'actually…' or flips a cultural norm, it creates **cognitive dissonance**—a kind of tension that demands resolution." (Prompt Library #13; PDF p.26)

Target feel: "**surprising**, **immediately useful**, and **built to spread**—the kind of thing people say, 'I've never thought of it that way before.'"

## Deploy in a UGC script
*Derived guidance. Example lines are illustrative, not from course, except where a session is cited.*

| Script slot | Pattern | Example line |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | "Actually…" / myth → truth | "Washing your hair less isn't helping your scalp. Actually, it's the opposite." (illustrative) |
| Mechanism | Explain why the old belief fails, in one beat | "Oil builds up and clogs the follicle." (illustrative) |
| Proof | One specific number or comparison | "[N] washes a week cut my shedding in [X] weeks." (fill in real data) |
| CTA | The product as the way to act on the new model | "This one's gentle enough for daily use." |

Session example of the same move: "I know people just say it's **genetics**. It's not. You can reverse it." Sarah calls this a "belief changer" ([Live, July 9](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-09-live-with-sarah.md) [0:54:54–0:55:50]).

**Formats named in the source:** "tweet-style carousel, 'myth vs truth' video hook, stat + truth combo email, bold headline with micro-copy follow-up" (#13).
**Visual cues:** a "MYTH" stamp crossed out, a tweet-style text card, a split "what you think / what's true" frame, the creator shaking their head at the common practice.

## Spot it in a competitor ad
- Openers: "Actually…", "Stop doing X", "Everything you know about X is wrong", "Myth:", "Unpopular opinion".
- Check whether there's a specific proof after the flip. If not, it's a weak contrarian hook.
- Tag: `lever-counterintuitive`.

## Common mistakes
- Contrarian for its own sake (#13 warns against it).
- A flip with no specific evidence, which reads as an opinion.
- Flipping a belief the audience doesn't hold, so there's no dissonance.

## Pairs with
- **[Specificity](specificity.md)** (Prompt #13): specificity makes the flip believable.
- **vs [Hidden truth](hidden-truth.md):** hidden truth reveals something *withheld or overlooked*. A counterintuitive insight contradicts something *believed*. They often overlap.
- **vs [Reverse psychology](reverse-psychology.md):** reverse psychology tells them to *keep doing* the wrong thing. A counterintuitive insight just states the surprising truth.
- **[First principles thinking](first-principles-thinking.md)** (rebuild the logic) and **[Data-driven insight](data-driven-insight.md)** (a surprising stat).
- **[Hindsight bias](hindsight-bias.md)** ("of course the old way didn't work") makes the flip feel obvious in retrospect.
