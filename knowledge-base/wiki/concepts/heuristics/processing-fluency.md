# Processing Fluency

> **"If it's easy to understand, it feels more trustworthy. Simple = safe. Complicated = risky."** (e-book, p.11). Shortcut #5 in [Heuristics 101](../../courses/tether-lab/02-heuristics-101/README.md), and Sarah's default base layer: "I try and use processing fluency as much as I possibly can" ([L5 06:40]).

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** in **every** script and static, as the base layer and final QA pass. Use it to cut copy, simplify layouts, and score competitor ads for clarity.
- **Inputs → outputs:** a draft script or static → a simplified version (one message, short lines, plain words, clean frame) and a clarity pass/fail.
- **Rules:**
  - DO apply the e-book test: "If a 7-year-old can't understand your ad in 3 seconds, simplify it."
  - DO keep one clear message, short copy, familiar visuals, few colours and plain backgrounds.
  - DO let an expressive face or visual carry meaning instead of words.
  - DON'T add bullet points under a clear headline ("you don't need them").
  - DON'T "over design your way into trying to convince people."
  - DON'T stack heuristics at the cost of clarity. Simplicity comes first.
- **See also:** [Heuristic stacking](../heuristic-stacking.md), [Salience bias](salience-bias.md), [CreativeOS Copy Grader](../../tools/creativeos.md), [Subconscious vs conscious mind](../subconscious-vs-conscious-mind.md).

## Definition
"If it's easy to understand something, we believe it a whole lot more. We're... more interested in it because it's quick for us to understand" ([L3 02:37]–[02:48]).

## Why it works
- "The brain burns a lot less energy when something is fluent or if it's easy" ([L3 02:48]).
- **PB&J analogy:** once you've made peanut butter and jelly sandwiches a lot, you're "fluent" and don't think about it. "The same goes for ads." "That ease becomes trustworthy to the brain" ([L3 02:53]–[03:02]).
- The goal is for the brain to process the ad "within split seconds" ([L5 01:17]). "The simpler you can make the ad, the faster the brain is going to process it... And then they'll go and buy" ([L5 06:48]–[07:02]).

## How to use it in ads (from the course)
- Clean layouts, short copy, familiar visuals ([L3 04:01]–[04:13]). E-book: "Keeping layouts clean · Using short, clear headlines · Avoiding clutter and over-explaining."
- "We don't want a lot of colors. We don't want crazy backgrounds. We're not really trying to do a whole lot visually" ([L5 01:04]–[01:17]).
- Let one line plus an expressive visual carry it. The LEGENDS model's proud expression "does a lot... We don't actually have to say a whole lot" ([L3 03:14]–[03:30]).
- The CreativeOS Copy Grader yardstick: copy "at like first grade level so anyone can understand it" ([CreativeOS bonus](../../courses/tether-lab/02-heuristics-101/08-bonus-creativeos.md) [03:50]).

## Deploy in a UGC script
*Derived guidance: course principles applied to video. Not stated in the course.*

| Script slot | Pattern |
|---|---|
| Whole script | One promise per video. Each beat is one sentence a 7-year-old would understand. |
| Hook | Under ~8 words, everyday vocabulary, the product visible at once. |
| On-screen text | One short line per scene, not stacked bullet lists. |
| Visuals | Uncluttered background, single product, a clear facial expression that shows the feeling. |
| QA | Read the script aloud. Cut any sentence that needs a second sentence to explain it. |

## Spot it in a competitor ad
- **Fluent:** one headline, one product, a plain or textured single-colour background, short CTA, no bullet stack. These ads often look "too simple."
- **Disfluent (a weakness to exploit):** headline followed by 4–6 bullets, many colours, busy backgrounds, jargon-heavy copy. Note it as "low fluency" in the teardown and fix it in our remix.

## Rules of thumb
- **DO** use fluency "as much as I possibly can" ([L5 06:40]).
- **DON'T** use bullet points in statics: "I see bullet points everywhere in static ads these days, you don't need them" ([L3 03:41]).
- **DON'T** "over design your way into trying to convince people to buy from you" ([L3 03:50]).
- **IF** the headline needs explaining **THEN** rewrite the headline. Don't add sub-copy ([L3 03:30]–[03:45]).
- **DO** pass the 7-year-old / 3-second test (e-book p.11).
