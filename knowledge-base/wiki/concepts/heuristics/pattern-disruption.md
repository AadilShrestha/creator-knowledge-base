# Pattern Disruption

> **"Pattern Disruption interrupts mental autopilot. The brain is wired to filter out predictable stimuli—so when something unexpected appears (in format, tone, visuals, or narrative structure), attention snaps back into focus. It's the mechanism behind double-takes and 'Wait, what did I just see?' moments."** ([Prompt Library #6](../../courses/tether-lab/07-prompt-library/06-pattern-disruption-and-hidden-truth.md))

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** the first frame/second of any UGC video or static; the category's ads all look alike (same talking head, same product-on-white, same upbeat music); tearing down a competitor ad that earns a double-take.
- **Inputs → outputs:** the category's visual/verbal conventions (scan 10–20 competitor ads) → one deliberate break (format, tone, visual, pacing or structure) for the hook, plus the payoff it sets up.
- **Rules:**
  - DO "Disrupt the audience's expectations immediately… anything that breaks the predictable pattern of ads in your space." (#6 step 1). First list the pattern, then break it.
  - DO turn the disruption into a question: "Use that disruption to create a curiosity gap… 'Why is this different?'" (step 2)
  - DO pay it off with something worth the attention, e.g. a [hidden truth](hidden-truth.md) (steps 3–4).
  - DO use static tricks from the live calls: "break the square" (let objects bite out of or run off the 1080×1080 frame, "you're just telling the brain, stop on this, because this looks weird") ([2025-06-11](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-11-live-with-sarah.md) [0:54:59–0:56:04]); "Weird works… because it jumps out at you" ([2025-07-23](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md) [0:58:30]).
  - DON'T disrupt so hard the viewer can't read what the ad is about. Pair the break with [processing fluency](processing-fluency.md) in the rest of the frame.
- **See also:** [Salience bias](salience-bias.md) · [Shock factor](shock-factor.md) · [Curiosity gap](curiosity-gap.md) · [Hidden truth](hidden-truth.md)

## Definition
- Prompt Library #6 (verbatim above). The combined ad "should feel like a jolt followed by a moment of clarity. They challenge attention, then reward it with insight."
- Format ideas in the prompt: "lo-fi talking head with jarring intro, 'this might get me banned' carousel, animated scroll-breaker".

## Deploy in a UGC script
*Derived guidance.*

| Break type | How | Example (illustrative, not from course) |
|---|---|---|
| Format | Content that doesn't look like an ad | Notes-app screenshot, fake text thread, "this might get me banned" carousel (from prompt) |
| Tone | Opposite of category tone | Deadpan or whispered in a hype category; hype in a calm one |
| Visual | Something "visually wrong" | Product in an absurd place, personified product with "evil eyes" ([2025-07-23](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md) [0:57:59–0:58:30]) |
| Pacing | Hard cut, silence, freeze frame | Start mid-sentence, cut to black at 1s |
| Structure | Start with the ending | Open on the result, rewind |

**Where in the script:** the first 0–2 seconds (hook) and the first frame of a static. Can recur once mid-video to re-catch drifting viewers.

## Spot it in a competitor ad
- First frame unlike the rest of the category feed; a visual that makes you look twice; non-ad formats (screenshots, memes, green-screen of a headline).
- Tag `lever-pattern-disruption`; note the **break type** (format / tone / visual / pacing / structure) so it can be transferred without copying the asset.

## Common mistakes
- Disruption with no payoff (attention spike, no click).
- Disruption that becomes the new pattern. Once competitors copy it, it stops disrupting. Re-scan the category regularly.
- Confusing context, e.g. skin with no context in women's products reads as "weird" and gets scrolled ([2025-07-23](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md) [0:20:31–0:21:08]).

## Pairs with
- **Prompt pairing:** [Pattern Disruption + Hidden Truth](../../courses/tether-lab/07-prompt-library/06-pattern-disruption-and-hidden-truth.md).
- **Distinctions:** [salience bias](salience-bias.md) is the general "whatever stands out gets attention" shortcut (Heuristics 101). Pattern disruption is salience applied to *expectations of the feed*. [Shock factor](shock-factor.md) is disruption by *provocation*. Pattern disruption needn't provoke.
