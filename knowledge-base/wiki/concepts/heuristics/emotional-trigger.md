# Emotional Trigger

> **"Emotions drive memory and action. When people feel *seen and stirred*, they're more likely to click, engage, or buy."** (Prompt Library #14). Pick **one** core feeling and amplify it, so that a shift in perception becomes a decision.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** choosing the emotion a script runs on, adding a feeling beat after a reframe or insight, or labelling the emotion in a competitor ad.
- **Inputs → outputs:** reviews or Reddit language + the reframe or offer → one named, specific emotion + the line and visual that stir it + its [zone](../emotional-zones.md).
- **Rules:**
  - DO "tap into a core feeling: relief, shame, liberation, guilt, joy, regret, pride, resentment, etc." (#14 step 3). Pick one per ad.
  - DO name the *specific* emotion (disappointment, not "fear"). Specific emotions are cheaper to buy ([Live, June 4](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-04-live-with-sarah.md) [0:37:39–0:39:49]; [Specificity](specificity.md)).
  - DO map the emotion to [valence](../valence.md) × [intensity](../intensity.md) and check it fits the customer's context ([Valence–Intensity diagnostic](../../frameworks/valence-intensity-diagnostic.md)).
  - DO match visuals to the emotion: calm light for relief, harsh contrast for urgency ([Valence + Intensity prompts](../../resources/valence-intensity-prompts.md)).
  - DON'T trigger trauma. Negative emotions are for creating longing, not making people feel bad ([Fixing the Tone](../../courses/tether-lab/03-valence-intensity-model/05-fixing-the-tone-without-rebuilding-the-ad.md) [07:36–08:31]).
- **See also:** [Prompt #14](../../courses/tether-lab/07-prompt-library/14-reframing-perception-and-emotional-trigger.md) · [Reframing](reframing.md) · [Emotional zones](../emotional-zones.md) · [Affect heuristic](affect-heuristic.md) · [Emotional misfire](../emotional-misfire.md)

## Definition
"**Emotional Triggers** amplify that shift. Whether it's relief, pride, embarrassment, nostalgia, or even righteous anger, emotions drive memory and action. When people feel *seen and stirred*, they're more likely to click, engage, or buy." (Prompt Library #14; PDF p.28)

The objective lists: "frustration, guilt, pride, relief, shame, desire, etc." Target feel: "**emotionally intelligent**, **perspective-shifting**, and **deeply validating**."

## Emotion → zone quick map
*Derived: these emotions come from #14, and the zone placement uses the course's [Emotional zones](../emotional-zones.md) model.*

| Emotion (from #14) | Valence / intensity | Zone | Typical use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Relief, liberation | + / low | Zone 1 feel-good | "It wasn't your fault" reframes, wellness |
| Joy, pride, desire | + / high | Zone 2 hype | Wins, identity shifts, launches |
| Regret, nostalgia, quiet guilt, disappointment | − / low | Zone 3 pain-point whisper | Soft problem awareness, before states |
| Shame, embarrassment, frustration, resentment, righteous anger | − / high | Zone 4 danger | High-stakes categories, "stop making this mistake". Use with care |

## Deploy in a UGC script
*Derived guidance. Example lines are illustrative, not from course.*

| Script slot | Pattern | Example line (illustrative) |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | Name the feeling in the first line | "The most embarrassing part was the meetings." |
| After the reframe | Let the creator *show* the emotion shift | Pause, exhale on camera: "I actually cried a little." |
| Proof | An emotion-forward testimonial | A review read aloud with feeling words kept in |
| CTA | Promise the opposite emotion | "Feel that relief this week." |

**Formats named in the source:** "emotion-forward testimonial, confessional copy post, visual metaphor carousel" (#14).

## Spot it in a competitor ad
- Feeling words in hook or VO, a facial-expression close-up, music shifts, and the "emotional promise" of the end state.
- Label the specific emotion + zone (e.g. `emotion-relief` / `zone-1`). If the emotion doesn't match the product context, flag it as an [emotional misfire](../emotional-misfire.md).
- Tag: `lever-emotional-trigger`.

## Common mistakes
- An umbrella emotion ("confidence", "happiness") instead of a specific one.
- Several competing emotions in one ad. Blending comes *after* individual emotions win ([Live, June 4](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-04-live-with-sarah.md)).
- High-intensity negative emotion in a low-stakes category.
