# Tribal Belonging

> **"Tribal Belonging is one of the most primal drivers of behavior. People seek identity and safety through shared language, struggle, humor, or purpose. Great brands make people feel like insiders—part of a movement."** ([Prompt Library #5](../../courses/tether-lab/07-prompt-library/05-tribal-belonging-and-vulnerability.md))

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** the audience shares a struggle, hobby or identity with its own language (new moms, runners, people with thinning hair, chai lovers); when you want the CTA to feel like joining; when tearing down an ad that says "people like us".
- **Inputs → outputs:** the tribe's name, shared language, shared struggle and in-jokes (mine reviews/comments) → a "you're one of us" hook, tribe-language copy, and a join-not-buy CTA.
- **Rules:**
  - DO name the tribe with a noun the buyer would use about themselves. Course rewrite: "10,000+ customers" → "Why 10,000 **Chai Lovers** are Switching…" ([2025-07-09](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-09-live-with-sarah.md) [0:25:12]); "6,000 five-star reviews" → "6,000 sock lovers…" ([Using Biases That Sell](../../courses/tether-lab/02-heuristics-101/02-using-biases-that-sell.md) [03:33]–[03:51]).
  - DO "Highlight the community or shared identity—the tribe of people who have faced this and come out stronger" and "Invite the audience to join, not buy." (#5 steps 3–4)
  - DO cast creators who are visibly *in* the tribe. In-group bias: "we treat people who are like us a lot better" ([2025-07-09](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-09-live-with-sarah.md) [0:53:32–0:53:55]).
  - DON'T invent a tribe the audience doesn't recognise. Use their words, not brand words.
- **See also:** [Vulnerability](vulnerability.md) · [Social proof](social-proof.md) · [Actual Self](../actual-self.md) · [Status signaling](status-signaling.md) · [Anonymity](anonymity.md)

## Definition
- Prompt Library #5 (verbatim above). Paired with vulnerability, the aim is ads that make the audience "feel seen, safe, and understood—like they've finally found a brand that *gets them*", written "just for 'people like us.'"

## Deploy in a UGC script
*Derived structure; example lines from the course where cited.*

| Script slot | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | Call the tribe by name / shared detail | "I'm so done seeing more hair in my shower than on my brush." (situational specificity + in-group recognition, [2025-07-09](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-09-live-with-sarah.md) [0:42:23]) |
| Problem | Shared struggle, said vulnerably | "If you're [tribe], you know [struggle]." (illustrative) |
| Proof | Peer, not expert | Friend to a woman losing her hair: "I'm losing mine too. It's totally fine, because I found this shampoo… nobody will even know." [0:53:32–0:53:55] |
| CTA | Join | "Welcome to the club." / "Join [N] [tribe noun]." (illustrative) |

**Visual cues:** creator who looks like the buyer, tribe-specific settings and props, comment/stitch formats, group shots, insider language on screen.

## Spot it in a competitor ad
- "For [tribe] who…", "If you know, you know", "Us [tribe] have to stick together", community counts with a tribe noun, member/club language.
- Creators who mirror the buyer; comments full of "this is so me".
- Tag `lever-tribal-belonging`; co-tag `social-proof` if a crowd number is used.

## Common mistakes
- Faceless numbers ("10,000+ customers") where a tribe noun would work.
- A tribe so broad it means nothing ("women", "people who care").
- Exclusion that alienates the buyer's real identity.

## Pairs with
- **Prompt pairing:** [Tribal Belonging + Vulnerability](../../courses/tether-lab/07-prompt-library/05-tribal-belonging-and-vulnerability.md): the confession opens the door, the tribe catches them.
- **Distinctions:** [social proof](social-proof.md) = many people do it (volume). Tribal belonging = people *like me* do it (identity). [Status signaling](status-signaling.md) = rank above others; belonging = equality inside the group. Zone 1 lists "belonging brands" as a fit ([The 4 Emotional Zones](../../courses/tether-lab/03-valence-intensity-model/03-the-4-emotional-zones.md) [01:49]). Best fit self: [Actual](../actual-self.md) ("This is made for people like you", [The 3 Selves](../../courses/tether-lab/04-self-targeting-system/04-the-3-selves-that-drive-every-buying-decision.md) [03:57]).
