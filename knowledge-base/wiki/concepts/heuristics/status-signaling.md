# Status Signaling

> **"Status Signaling is the act of broadcasting identity through behavior, language, aesthetics, or association. It's how we subtly (or not so subtly) communicate where we belong in the social/aspirational hierarchy."** ([Prompt Library #2](../../courses/tether-lab/07-prompt-library/02-status-signaling-and-open-loop.md))

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** the product is bought partly to be *seen* (fashion, jewelry, gadgets, premium food, "insider" tools) or the buyer wants to be "successful, elite, informed, or 'in the know'"; when tearing down an ad that sells membership in a select group.
- **Inputs → outputs:** the status identity the audience aspires to (high-performers, trendsetters, early adopters, insiders, rebels) + the social cue the product gives off → an "insider invitation" hook, cues for wardrobe/setting/language, and a CTA framed as joining.
- **Rules:**
  - DO name the identity, not the product. The prompt: "Tap into a status-oriented identity your audience aspires to."
  - DO use "visual, tonal, or social cues to make the ad feel like an invitation to a world that's just out of reach."
  - DO decide which *kind* of status. In Roast My Ads #3 Sarah splits popularity into peer popularity, high-level status and inner confidence, and tests one static per type ([2025-07-23](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md) [0:45:18–0:46:24]).
  - DON'T flex where the audience wants quiet signals ("quiet respect" for men's chains, [2025-06-04](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-04-live-with-sarah.md) [0:05:04–0:07:10]).
  - DON'T live only here. Status is an [Ideal Self](../ideal-self.md) play, and Module 4 warns that Ideal-only accounts hit an [angle rut](../angle-rut.md).
- **See also:** [Open loop](open-loop.md) · [Aspirational identity](aspirational-identity.md) · [Tribal belonging](tribal-belonging.md) · [Social proof](social-proof.md) · [Ideal Self](../ideal-self.md)

## Definition
- Prompt Library #2 (verbatim above). Objective: leverage "people's desire to be seen as successful, elite, informed, or 'in the know'." Paired with an open loop, the viewer thinks: "People like me are already doing this… and I need to know what I'm missing."

## Deploy in a UGC script
*Derived guidance.*

| Script slot | Pattern | Example line (illustrative, not from course) |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | Insider reveal | "Every [high-status group] I know has switched to this, and nobody talks about it." |
| Problem | The gap between them and you | "I couldn't figure out how they [result] until…" |
| Proof | Association cue | Shot of the product in a high-status context (desk of a founder, gym of an athlete) |
| CTA | Join, don't buy | "Now you're in on it." |

**Visual cues:** setting and wardrobe that signal the tribe, "members only" or waitlist language, minimal branding, a creator who looks like the aspirational identity (not a mass-market spokesperson).

## Spot it in a competitor ad
- "For those who…", "Only [group] know…", "What [elite group] use", "the BMW of…", "for those who have earned it".
- Exclusive association (celebrity, elite profession), waitlists, invite codes.
- Tag `lever-status-signaling`. Note which status type (peer popularity / high status / earned achievement / insider knowledge).

## Common mistakes
- Status that doesn't match the buyer's real hierarchy (a celebrity reference that pulls the wrong demographic: "Beyoncé… beloved by Gen Xers and males usually", [2025-07-23](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md) [0:44:30–0:45:18]).
- Making the viewer feel excluded rather than invited. The world should be "just out of reach", not closed.

## Pairs with
- **Prompt pairing:** [Status Signaling + Open Loop](../../courses/tether-lab/07-prompt-library/02-status-signaling-and-open-loop.md): show the world, withhold the key.
- **Distinctions:** [aspirational identity](aspirational-identity.md) is who *I* want to become (internal); status signaling is how *others* see me (social). [Tribal belonging](tribal-belonging.md) is equality inside a group ("people like us"); status is rank. [Gamification](gamification.md) borrows status via leaderboards ("I'm in the top 1%").
