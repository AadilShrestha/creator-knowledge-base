# Reciprocity and the Ben Franklin Effect

> **"You can use this directly in your ads just by being kind."** ([Live, July 2](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-02-live-with-sarah.md) [0:13:00]). When a brand visibly does the buyer a favour, the buyer feels warmer toward it and wants to give something back (the purchase).

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** writing the offer/price beat of a UGC or founder script; writing bonus/gift lines; retention emails and trial reminders; tearing down a competitor offer framed as generosity.
- **Inputs → outputs:** real price, real bonus or real goodwill gesture → a "we wish we could give this free, but…" line, a bonus framed as a favour, or a goodwill retention move.
- **Rules:**
  - DO frame the price as the brand holding back from giving it away: "We want to give these fries to you for free but we can't, so we'll give them to you for a dollar" (KFC) ([Live, July 2](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-02-live-with-sarah.md) [0:12:37]).
  - DO add a bonus "just because we want to make sure that you get good results" ([Live, July 2](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-02-live-with-sarah.md) [0:13:00–0:13:34]).
  - DO make the kindness real. Any scarcity reason you give ("so we don't sell out") must be true.
  - DO surprise retained customers with something that isn't part of a points programme (Chewy flowers) ([Live, June 4](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-04-live-with-sarah.md) [0:09:15–0:10:19]).
- **See also:** [Barrier buster](../barrier-buster.md) · [Risk-reduction hooks](../risk-reduction-hooks.md) · [Commitment and consistency](commitment-and-consistency.md) · [Scarcity heuristic](scarcity-heuristic.md) · [The Decision Lab](../../tools/the-decision-lab.md)

## Definition
- **Ben Franklin effect** (The Decision Lab, on screen: "Why do we like someone more after doing them a favor?"): "doing a favor for someone can make you feel more positively to them" ([Live, July 2](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-02-live-with-sarah.md) [0:11:34; 0:13:30]).
- **Reciprocity**, "very similar": "if you do something for me… I will want to do something for you" ([Live, July 2](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-02-live-with-sarah.md) [0:12:21]).
- *Wiki note:* strictly, the Ben Franklin effect is about liking someone more after *you* did *them* a favour. Sarah uses the two together for "brand is nice to me → I reciprocate". This page follows her usage.

## From the course
- **KFC $1 fries** (from Sarah's own TikTok post, figure recalled): KFC lifted sales of the flagship $1 fries package "by I think it was like 56 [%]… something wild… by basically injecting psychology into their ads". Message: we'd give them free if we could ([Live, July 2](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-02-live-with-sarah.md) [0:11:51–0:13:00]).
- **Transfer template (Sarah):** "We really wish that we could give this serum away for free, but it's so powerful for wrinkles that… we have to make sure that we don't sell out so that everybody can get access to it. So we'll give it to you for X amount of dollars and we'll throw in Y… just because we want to make sure that you get good results" ([Live, July 2](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-02-live-with-sarah.md) [0:13:00–0:13:34]).
- **Trial extension (Joanna Wallace, wiki tag):** "We sign up for 10 different subscriptions and then immediately forget about them too… you have three days left… or let's be real, you've had a busy week, let's just extend it" ([Joanna Wallace](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-23-guest-session-01-joanna-wallace.md) [0:39:50]).
- **Retention goodwill (wiki tag):** Chewy sends flowers when a customer's pet dies and suggests donating leftover food — "not part of their retention program… just something nice", which (as Sarah tells it) drastically raised retention. Retained customers "want to feel special… want to be surprised"; points don't seem to help ([Live, June 4](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-04-live-with-sarah.md) [0:09:15–0:10:19]).

| Creator offer beat | Brand generosity reported by the creator | "They literally threw in [bonus] just so I'd get results faster." (illustrative, not from course; real bonus only) |
| Retention email / DM | Goodwill without asking | "Busy week? We extended your trial." ([Joanna Wallace](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-23-guest-session-01-joanna-wallace.md) [0:39:50]) |

**Visual cues:** founder to camera, warm tone; a gift being added to the box; handwritten note in the package.

## Spot it in a competitor ad
- "We wish we could…", "on us", "our gift to you", "just because", free extras framed as care rather than as a discount.
- Tag `heur-reciprocity`.

## Rules of thumb
- **DO** lead the price beat with care, then state the price.
- **IF** you give a bonus **THEN** say why it helps *them* ("so you get good results").
- **DON'T** pair a "we wish it were free" line with a fake sell-out reason.

## Common mistakes
- Framing a discount as generosity when it's obviously a permanent price.
- Loyalty points as the only retention tactic (Sarah: they don't seem to help, [Live, June 4](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-04-live-with-sarah.md) [0:09:15]).
