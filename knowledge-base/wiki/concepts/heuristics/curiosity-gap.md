# Curiosity Gap

> **"Curiosity is triggered when there's an *information gap*—something we *almost* understand but need closure to resolve. This creates a cognitive itch that must be scratched."** ([Prompt Library #3](../../courses/tether-lab/07-prompt-library/03-curiosity-and-reverse-psychology.md))

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** writing the first 1–3 seconds of a UGC hook or a static headline that must earn a "Wait, what?"; diagnosing why a competitor hook holds attention.
- **Inputs → outputs:** a belief the audience holds + a surprising fact/claim that contradicts or half-explains it → a hook that opens a gap, and the line that closes it.
- **Rules:**
  - DO make the gap *almost* closable. People need to "almost understand". Total mystery is ignored; full information kills the itch.
  - DO "Start with a statement or visual that contradicts what the audience believes… bold, confusing, or intentionally 'wrong-sounding' statements that prompt, 'That can't be right…'" (#3 step 1).
  - DO "land on an insight that reorients their thinking" (#3 step 4). The close must be smarter than the opener.
  - DON'T make the gap about something irrelevant to the product. The resolution should *be* the product's reason to exist.
- **See also:** [Open loop](open-loop.md) · [Reverse psychology](reverse-psychology.md) · [Counterintuitive insight](counterintuitive-insight.md) · [Pattern disruption](pattern-disruption.md) · [Salience bias](salience-bias.md)

## Definition
- Prompt Library #3 (verbatim above). The objective calls it "an irresistible sense of 'Wait, what?' curiosity", and the paired ads "should feel like a contradiction that *has to be resolved*."
- Pattern Disruption + Hidden Truth (#6) uses the same mechanism as step 2: "Use that disruption to create a curiosity gap. Make the viewer lean in by thinking, 'Why is this different?'"

## Spot it in a competitor ad
- "Wait, what?" openers: a claim that sounds wrong, an unfinished sentence, "the reason is not what you think", "nobody talks about…".
- Gap closed later in the video or on the landing page.
- Tag `lever-curiosity-gap`; add `lever-open-loop` if the gap is held across the ad.

## Common mistakes
- Clickbait gap that the product can't close.
- A gap the target doesn't care about. Curiosity follows relevance: the contradiction must hit a belief *they* hold.
- Explaining in the hook. If the first line answers the question, there's no gap.
