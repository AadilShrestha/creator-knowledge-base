# In-Group Bias

> **"In group bias has to do with like we treat people who are like us a lot better than people who are not like us."** ([Live, July 9](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-09-live-with-sarah.md) [0:53:32]). Put a messenger in the ad who is visibly *one of them* (same problem, same type, same tribe noun), and the message gets a warmer hearing.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** choosing the creator or on-screen character for a UGC script; writing the opening call-out ("[type] people, this is for you"); rewriting generic social proof into a typed crowd; tearing down a competitor ad whose creator is clearly "one of us".
- **Inputs → outputs:** the buyer's identity markers (problem, life stage, hobby, the word they call themselves) → a peer-messenger casting note, one "me too" line for the hook, one typed-crowd proof line.
- **Rules:**
  - DO cast a peer who shares the problem, and let them speak from inside it: "I'm losing mine too. It's totally fine…" ([Jul 9](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-09-live-with-sarah.md) [0:53:32–0:53:55]).
  - DO put the in-group bias in the **hook**: "mostly in hooks… because it's easier to put them in there real quick" ([Jul 9](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-09-live-with-sarah.md) [0:53:44]).
  - DO type the crowd with the noun buyers use for themselves: "350,000 tea drinkers love us", not "350,000 sold" ([Jul 16](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md) [0:54:50]); "10,000 chai lovers", not "10,000+ customers" ([Jul 9](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-09-live-with-sarah.md) [0:25:12]).
  - DO joke about the shared insecurity from inside ("I'm not the only one"), never at the viewer (Joanna, [Guest 01](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-23-guest-session-01-joanna-wallace.md) [0:01:01–0:03:04]).
  - DON'T insult the out-group by name. Sarah's enemy is framed so it "never insults anyone" ([May 21](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-21-live-with-sarah.md) [0:27:16–0:27:42]).

## Definition
The Decision Lab entry Sarah opened on screen: "Why do we treat our in-group better than we do our out-group?" ([Jul 9](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-09-live-with-sarah.md) [0:54:00]). Sarah's gloss: "we treat people who are like us a lot better than people who are not like us" [0:53:32].

## From the course
- **Hook demo (Jul 9):** "You might show a video of two women, one who's losing her hair, one who's not, and then a friend walks up and says to a woman who's losing her hair: I'm losing mine too. It's totally fine, because I found this shampoo… so nobody will even know." ([Jul 9](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-09-live-with-sarah.md) [0:53:32–0:53:55])
- **Typed social proof is in-group proof (Jul 16):** "That 'tea drinkers' language… is going to tell me… I'm a tea drinker, so yes I can do this." Generic "thousands of women" doesn't let her recognise herself; "tired / frazzled / stressed-out women" does ([Jul 16](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md) [0:53:50–0:54:50]).
- **Tribe noun + movement verb (Jul 9, member test):** "Why 10,000 Chai Lovers are Switching to This Golden Milk Tea." Aazar's doc: "'Chai lovers' made it tribal. 'Switching' made it a movement." It lost to scarcity but "held attention longer" ([Jul 9](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-09-live-with-sarah.md) [0:25:12–0:25:45]).
- **Shared insecurity in humor (Joanna):** brands that do humor well "talk about the insecurities… in a way that's not insulting… self-deprecating"; the viewer thinks "I'm not the only one who has that problem", their "guard is down" ([Guest 01](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-23-guest-session-01-joanna-wallace.md) [0:01:01–0:03:04]). Dentures example: someone who also has them jokes about the cup.
- **Us vs them via a named out-group (May 21, wiki tag):** "Introverts, we're calling you out. People who hate hustle culture, come hang out with us… if you hate XYZ, this is for you." Sarah frames it as [picking an enemy](../brand-enemy.md); the in-group is the introverts ([May 21](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-21-live-with-sarah.md) [0:25:23–0:25:50]).

**Visual cues:** two-person scene where the peer joins the one with the problem; creator who shows the same issue on camera (thinning part, the cluttered counter); insider props; on-screen text naming the group ("Introverts:", "Chai lovers:").

## Spot it in a competitor ad
- The creator says "me too", "us [type] people", "if you're like me"; a tribe noun in the headline ("dog moms", "tea drinkers", "chai lovers").
- An out-group is named or implied ("not for the gym bros", "unlike corporate water").
- Typed proof ("35,000+ dairy lovers", Milky, [Roast #3](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md) [0:51:00]).
- Tag: `heur-in-group`. Add `heur-social-proof` if a typed number is present, `brand-enemy` if an out-group is attacked.

## Rules of thumb
- **DO** let the peer carry the line; a brand voice saying "you're not alone" is weaker than a person with the same problem saying it.
- **DO** use the buyer's own self-label (from reviews/Reddit), not a marketing segment name.
- **IF** the problem is embarrassing **THEN** show the peer in the same situation first, then the fix (also see [mirror effect](../mirror-effect.md)).
- **DON'T** make the out-group a group of people the buyer loves (their friends, their doctor); keep it a behaviour or system ("hustle culture", "big water").

## Common mistakes
- Generic crowd nouns ("customers", "women", "people") that nobody identifies with.
- Casting a polished creator who visibly doesn't share the problem.
- Turning us-vs-them into mocking a group the audience partly belongs to.

## Pairs with / distinctions
- **vs [Tribal belonging](tribal-belonging.md)** (Prompt Library lever): tribal belonging *builds* a movement the buyer wants to join. In-group bias is the *existing* preference for people already like me. Use in-group for casting and peer lines, tribal belonging for brand-as-movement copy.
- **vs [Brand enemy](../brand-enemy.md):** the enemy defines the out-group at brand level; in-group bias is the per-ad casting/line mechanism.
- **vs [Social proof](social-proof.md):** social proof = "many people"; in-group = "people like me". Typed social proof combines both.
- Pairs with [messenger effect](messenger-effect.md) (who says it) and [specificity](specificity.md) (insider situations).
