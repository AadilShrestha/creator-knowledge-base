# Gambler's Fallacy ("It Won't Work for Me Either")

> **"We need to identify what have people experienced a lot… in my particular industry or with their problem so that I can call it out, because they're gonna think that it's gonna happen again."** ([Live, July 2](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-02-live-with-sarah.md) [0:07:17]). Buyers who have failed many times judge their odds of success by those past failures. Name the pattern out loud, then show why this time the pattern breaks.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** the category is full of repeat triers (weight loss, hair loss, skincare, supplements, slow feeders, pet chews); writing the problem beat or first objection of a UGC script; tearing down "I've tried everything" ads.
- **Inputs → outputs:** the list of things buyers have already tried (from reviews/Reddit) → a call-out line naming the repeated failures + a "why this is different" mechanism line.
- **Rules:**
  - DO name the repeated failure explicitly ("I've tried a lot of times to lose weight and it's never worked for me") ([Jul 2](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-02-live-with-sarah.md) [0:06:49–0:07:17]).
  - DO break the link with a concrete difference: "When the pattern changes… the link breaks" [0:07:17–0:08:20] (paraphrase).
  - DO use "every" to show how many times they've tried: "Every other bowl was a failure" ([Roast #3](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md) [0:33:42]) (wiki tag: same mechanism).
  - DON'T stop at "When nothing worked… we tried this!" That's "one level deep"; go "four levels deep" into the defeat [0:33:42–0:34:44].
  - DON'T blame the buyer for past failures; blame the products/options ([bounded rationality](bounded-rationality.md) framing).
- **See also:** [Bounded rationality](bounded-rationality.md) · [Hindsight bias](hindsight-bias.md) · [Pain-point amplification](pain-point-amplification.md) · [Specificity](specificity.md) · [Risk-reduction hooks](../risk-reduction-hooks.md)

## Definition
The Decision Lab definition Sarah read on screen: "The gambler's fallacy describes our belief that the probability of a random event occurring in the future is influenced by previous instances of that type of event." ([Jul 2](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-02-live-with-sarah.md) [0:06:31])

In Sarah's application, the "previous instances" are the buyer's own past failures: they expect the next product to fail the same way.

## Rules of thumb
- **DO** mine reviews for "I tried X, Y, Z" lists and reuse the buyer's own list.
- **DO** pair the call-out with a mechanism, not just a promise; a promise is what every failed product made.
- **IF** the category is full of repeat triers **THEN** put the call-out in the first 3 seconds.

## Common mistakes
- Generic "tried everything?" without naming what.
- Implying the buyer did it wrong.
- Calling out failure and never showing why the pattern breaks.

## Pairs with / distinctions
- **vs [Bounded rationality](bounded-rationality.md):** "you chose from bad options" excuses past failure; gambler's fallacy names the *expectation* that it'll fail again. Use both: excuse the past, break the expectation.
- **vs [Hindsight bias](hindsight-bias.md):** hindsight says "you should have seen it"; gambler's fallacy says "it will happen again".
- Pairs with [pain-point amplification](pain-point-amplification.md), [specificity](specificity.md), [social proof](social-proof.md) (skeptic testimonials).
