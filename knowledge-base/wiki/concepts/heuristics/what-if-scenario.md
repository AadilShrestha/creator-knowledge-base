# What If Scenario

> **"'What If' Scenarios activate the imagination. They open a new mental frame—helping the viewer visualize what's possible, sometimes utopian or dystopian. They make abstract benefits *feel real and emotionally close*."** ([Prompt Library #7](../../courses/tether-lab/07-prompt-library/07-what-if-scenario-and-pain-point-amplification.md))

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** the benefit is abstract or delayed (insurance, supplements, financial products, sleep, routines) and needs to *feel* close; story-led UGC that opens in an imagined world; high-commitment purchases ("perfect for high-commitment conversions", #7).
- **Inputs → outputs:** the dream state (gain) or the disaster state (loss) our product creates/prevents, made concrete and near → a "What if…" hook, a vivid scene, and a cut back to the present reality.
- **Rules:**
  - DO "Begin with a compelling 'What if…' scenario. Show them a dream state or dramatic alternate outcome (gain or loss). It should feel **specific, not vague**." (#7 step 1)
  - DO bring the future close in time. June 4 call: "protect your home… for the future you" is "too vague"; instead, "Next Tuesday your home is going to flood. Are you ready for that?" ([2025-06-04](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-04-live-with-sarah.md) [0:17:41–0:19:46]).
  - DO choose the direction deliberately: **utopian** (gain, [Ideal Self](../ideal-self.md), Zone 2) or **dystopian** (loss, [Ought Self](../ought-self.md), Zone 4).
  - DON'T leave the viewer in the imagined world. Step 2 is "Contrast it with a painful reality", and the offer is "the bridge".
- **See also:** [Pain-point amplification](pain-point-amplification.md) · [Temporal discounting](temporal-discounting.md) · [Contrast](contrast.md) · [Micro-story](micro-story.md) · [Loss aversion](loss-aversion.md)

## Definition
- Prompt Library #7 (verbatim above). Tone: "immersive tension + undeniable clarity—a moment of truth delivered with storytelling heat."

## Spot it in a competitor ad
- "What if…", "Imagine…", "Picture this…", "In 30 days you could…", future-dated scenarios, "a year from now".
- Tag `lever-what-if`; note **direction** (utopian/dystopian) and **time distance** (tomorrow vs "someday").

## Common mistakes
- Vague futures ("imagine a better life"). The prompt insists on "specific, not vague".
- Distant futures. Temporal discounting makes "someday" worthless; pull it to this week.
- No return to reality, so there's no felt gap and no urgency.

## Pairs with
- **Prompt pairing:** [What If Scenario + Pain Point Amplification](../../courses/tether-lab/07-prompt-library/07-what-if-scenario-and-pain-point-amplification.md): the dream world plus "the sting of the one they're still stuck in."
- **Distinctions:** [contrast](contrast.md) (#8) shows both states side by side; the what-if *imagines* one of them. [Temporal discounting](temporal-discounting.md) explains why the imagined future must be near. [Micro-story](micro-story.md) tells what happened; what-if tells what could happen.
