# Data-Driven Insight

> **"People are more likely to believe and act when they see a stat, number, or correlation that surprises them—especially when it challenges their assumptions."** (Prompt Library #11). Use a number as the hook, and turn it into a takeaway the viewer can act on.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** the audience is skeptical, analytical or "performance-minded", the product has real numbers (study results, usage data, survey stats), or a competitor ad leads with a stat.
- **Inputs → outputs:** a true, surprising number (own data, survey, cited study) → a stat hook + a one-line reframe + a product tie-in.
- **Rules:**
  - DO pick a stat that **surprises** or challenges an assumption. A confirming stat is wallpaper.
  - DO "tie it directly to a clear takeaway or reframed belief. Use the data to fuel insight—don't just display it." (#11 step 2)
  - DO use precise numbers. Odd, exact figures (53, 57) read as real ([Live, July 9](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-09-live-with-sarah.md) [0:47:00–0:48:04]). See [Specificity](specificity.md).
  - DON'T fabricate or round-up data. "Don't fabricate data." ([Live, July 9](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-09-live-with-sarah.md) [0:15:04–0:15:58])
  - DON'T stack several stats in the hook. Use one number per ad for [processing fluency](processing-fluency.md).
- **See also:** [Prompt #11](../../courses/tether-lab/07-prompt-library/11-authority-borrowing-and-data-driven-insight.md) · [Authority borrowing](authority-borrowing.md) · [Specificity](specificity.md) · [Counterintuitive insight](counterintuitive-insight.md)

## Definition
"**Data-Driven Insight** appeals to the **analytical mind**. People are more likely to believe and act when they see a stat, number, or correlation that surprises them—especially when it challenges their assumptions." (Prompt Library #11; PDF p.22)

Together with authority borrowing, "these tactics allow your brand to *educate while persuading*—making your point feel objective, logical, and too well-supported to ignore." Target feel: "**rational**, **persuasive**, and **calmly confident**—ideal for performance-minded audiences who value proof over hype."

## Deploy in a UGC script
*Derived guidance. Example lines are illustrative, not from course.*

| Script slot | Pattern | Example line (illustrative) |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | Stat first, as on-screen text + VO | "[N]% of people use their sunscreen wrong. I was one of them." (fill in a real, sourced number) |
| Mechanism | What the number means | "It's not the SPF, it's the amount. You need about a teaspoon for your face." |
| Proof | Source on screen, then the creator's own result | Citation crop → creator applying the right amount |
| CTA | The product makes acting on the insight easy | "This one has a dose pump, so you can't get it wrong." |

**Formats named in the source:** "minimalist stat-based carousel, '1 stat that changed how I market' short-form video, expert quote + real-world proof pairing" (#11).
**Visual cues:** one big number on screen, a simple chart (one bar or a before/after metric), a source citation in small type.

## Spot it in a competitor ad
- A number in the first line or frame, "X out of Y", "studies show", survey-result reveals, chart statics.
- Check whether the stat is translated into an insight (strong) or just shown (weak).
- Tag: `lever-data-insight`. Add `lever-authority-borrowing` if a source is named.

## Common mistakes
- A stat with no "so what." #11 says to use the data to *fuel insight*.
- A vague or round number ("thousands", "90%") where a precise one exists.
- Hype tone around data. #11: "Let the logic lead—don't oversell."
- Using it for impulse or hedonic buys, where [affect](affect-heuristic.md) beats analysis. *(Derived.)*

## Pairs with
- **[Authority borrowing](authority-borrowing.md)** (Prompt #11): data needs a credible source, and a source needs a usable number.
- **vs [Authority bias](authority-bias.md):** authority asks you to trust *who* says it. Data asks you to trust *what* the evidence says. They usually stack.
- **[Specificity](specificity.md)** and **[Counterintuitive insight](counterintuitive-insight.md)** (Prompt #13) are the same family. A surprising, specific number is the core of both.
- Self fit: [Ought Self](../ought-self.md) (doing it right, meeting a standard) and analytical buyers. Zone 1 or Zone 3 tone ([Emotional zones](../emotional-zones.md)). *(Derived.)*
