# Commitment and Consistency (Commitment Bias, Consistency Bias)

> **"Even a little bit of a monetary investment causes the brain to commit."** ([Live, July 9](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-09-live-with-sarah.md) [0:24:04]). Once people have taken a small step (paid, chosen, shown up), they keep acting in line with it. And buyers who already value consistency (dieters, routine-keepers) lean toward bigger commitments.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** designing the first ask in a funnel (trial, tripwire, quiz, application); writing offer lines for consistency-driven buyers (diets, routines, supplements); writing "already here" add-on prompts; tearing down a competitor that uses a $1 trial or a self-diagnosis checklist.
- **Inputs → outputs:** the big ask + the buyer's existing habits → a small first commitment (paid $0/$-amount trial, checklist, "see if you qualify"), a sequenced regimen, or a bigger-bundle recommendation for consistency-minded buyers.
- **Rules:**
  - DO make the first ask small but real: a small paid entry ("buy us a coffee", ~$8 to look around) commits the brain more than free ([Live, July 9](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-09-live-with-sarah.md) [0:23:53–0:24:24]).
  - DO write "$0 trial" instead of "free trial" ([Live, July 9](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-09-live-with-sarah.md) [0:21:49–0:22:03]).
  - DO fix the "break point" a trial user looks for before they hit it: "One snag in the process and that was all it took" ([Live, July 9](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-09-live-with-sarah.md) [0:22:03–0:23:53]).
  - IF the buyer must be consistent to get results (weight loss) THEN big bundles "are probably going to work incredibly well" because "consistency bias is already there" ([Live, July 2](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-02-live-with-sarah.md) [0:34:50–0:35:38]).
  - DO give in-store/on-site visitors add-ons once they've committed ("I'm already here") ([Live, July 9](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-09-live-with-sarah.md) [0:45:34]).
  - DON'T create the commitment through fake scarcity ("closing down" every month) ([Live, July 9](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-09-live-with-sarah.md) [0:43:49–0:45:34]).
- **See also:** [Sunk cost fallacy](sunk-cost-fallacy.md) · [Goal-gradient effect](goal-gradient-effect.md) · [IKEA effect](ikea-effect.md) · [Reciprocity](reciprocity.md) · [Fogg Behavior Model](../../frameworks/fogg-behavior-model.md) · [Bundle psychology](../../frameworks/offer-and-bundle-psychology.md)

## Definition
- **Commitment bias** (Sarah, on the lamp-store sale): shoppers who came for sale items also bought full-price items — "I'm already here… commitment bias is technically what they're activating", together with the sunk cost fallacy: "you already put in a little bit of effort, so you might as well just go all the way" ([Live, July 9](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-09-live-with-sarah.md) [0:45:34–0:45:58]).
- **Consistency bias** (Sarah, on low-carb pasta): "in order to lose weight you have to be consistent… that consistency bias is already there, it's predetermined for this customer type" ([Live, July 2](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-02-live-with-sarah.md) [0:34:50–0:35:38]).

## From the course
- **Small paid entry vs free:** "Anytime you put the word free on there the brain is automatically going to respond. Sometimes though it responds better if you put a dollar amount on it… $0 trial." Alternative: "buy us a coffee", pay ~$8 to look around ([Live, July 9](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-09-live-with-sarah.md) [0:21:49–0:24:24]).
- **Trial users look for an exit:** "I'm actually looking for one little piece of this that is just wrong enough so that I can get out before I have to pay." Sarah deleted an AI face-aging app after one choppy blend ([Live, July 9](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-09-live-with-sarah.md) [0:22:03–0:23:53]).
- **Sequenced regimen (wiki tag):** antibiotic study (recalled, "I need to go look at the study"): 15 white then 15 blue pills raised completion "by like 85 percent". Apply as "first 15 days / second 15 days" products → completion, AOV, LTV ([Live, July 2](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-02-live-with-sarah.md) [0:28:17–0:29:24]). Gabriela called it "chunking a task".
- **Tripwire (wiki tag):** for an info product, put a 1–5-page cheat sheet with a self-diagnosis checklist ("are you experiencing any of these issues? Yes? Then you need this book") in front of the book ([Live, June 4](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-04-live-with-sarah.md) [0:43:05–0:45:15]).
- **Qualification (wiki tag):** "see if you qualify" and an application ("if you're deemed fit… then these are our terms") turn the purchase into something the buyer has to step into ([Live, June 18](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-18-live-with-sarah.md) [0:19:49–0:20:52]; [Live, July 9](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-09-live-with-sarah.md) [0:36:59–0:37:41]).

## Spot it in a competitor ad
- $1/$0 trials, "quiz to find your…", "apply to join", step-1/step-2 kits, checklists, "you're already doing X, add Y".
- Tag `heur-commitment` or `heur-consistency`.

## Rules of thumb
- **DO** ask for one small, easy yes before the big one.
- **IF** the category requires routine (diet, supplements, skincare) **THEN** frame bigger packs as consistency support.
- **IF** a trial converts poorly **THEN** find the snag where users "break" the product and fix it first.
- **DON'T** manufacture commitment with dishonest urgency.

## Common mistakes
- Free trials with friction (credit card) and no reassurance — see [barrier buster](../barrier-buster.md).
- Offering a big bundle to a buyer with no routine; it becomes stacked bottles and churn ([Live, July 2](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-02-live-with-sarah.md) [0:53:21]).

## Pairs with / distinctions
- **[Sunk cost fallacy](sunk-cost-fallacy.md):** sunk cost looks back ("I already paid"); commitment looks forward ("I already started, so I'll continue"). Sarah names both for the lamp store.
- **[Goal-gradient effect](goal-gradient-effect.md):** once committed, show progress to keep them moving.
- **[IKEA effect](ikea-effect.md):** choosing/building is a micro-commitment.
- **[Reciprocity](reciprocity.md):** the brand gives first instead of asking first.
