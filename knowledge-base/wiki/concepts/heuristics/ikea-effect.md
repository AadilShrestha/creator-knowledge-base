# IKEA Effect (Co-creation)

> **"People kind of value objects more if they either make, assemble it, or… do something to plan it."** ([Live, July 2](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-02-live-with-sarah.md) [0:08:29]). Let the viewer take part in choosing or building, and they value the result (and your product) more.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** writing carousel or interactive ads ("swipe to choose"), quiz or build-your-bundle flows, comment-to-choose UGC, or anything customisable; tearing down a competitor's "design your own" ad.
- **Inputs → outputs:** product options (variants, add-ons, designs) → a guided 2–3-step participation mechanic (swipe, pick, comment, add a third) with low cognitive load.
- **Rules:**
  - DO involve the viewer in "the idea creation", even if all they can do is swipe ("swipe to choose your best shirt") ([Live, July 2](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-02-live-with-sarah.md) [0:08:52–0:09:55]).
  - DO pre-pick two well-matched products and let them **add a third** ([Live, July 2](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-02-live-with-sarah.md) [0:10:24]).
  - DO organise options logically (small / medium / large dog bundles, then "what do you want to add?") ([Live, July 2](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-02-live-with-sarah.md) [0:10:24–0:11:27]).
  - DON'T open the whole catalogue: "every time I have too many options I get overwhelmed and I quit" ([Live, July 2](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-02-live-with-sarah.md) [0:09:55]).
- **See also:** [Choice overload](choice-overload.md) · [Cognitive load](cognitive-load.md) · [Commitment and consistency](commitment-and-consistency.md) · [Gamification](gamification.md) · [The Decision Lab](../../tools/the-decision-lab.md)

## Definition
- The Decision Lab (on screen): "The IKEA effect… describes how people tend to value an object more if they make (or assemble) it themselves" ([Live, July 2](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-02-live-with-sarah.md) [0:09:00]).
- Named in Heuristics 101 as one of the heuristics still to cover ([Stacking Psychology for Growth](../../courses/tether-lab/02-heuristics-101/05-stacking-psychology-for-growth.md) [07:31]).

## From the course
- Works best for "customisable or personalisable products", but any product can use it through a process: "swipe to see what the story is" or "swipe to choose your best shirt… by just telling them 'what shirt do you want to create'… allowing them… a process of being involved in… the idea creation, that's how we use IKEA effect. Incredibly powerful" ([Live, July 2](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-02-live-with-sarah.md) [0:08:52–0:09:55]).
- **Bundle builders (Lesley Cramer):** yes, if cognitive load stays low → two pre-matched items + add a third ([Live, July 2](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-02-live-with-sarah.md) [0:09:55–0:11:27]).
- **Attendee idea, endorsed:** a t-shirt carousel — "choose the one you want, comment the reason and get a discount" — where the click opens WhatsApp for a conversation. "WhatsApp is underutilized… everybody go test that" ([Live, July 2](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-02-live-with-sarah.md) [0:50:50–0:51:38]).

## Deploy in a UGC script
| Script slot | Pattern | Example line |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | Invite the choice | "Swipe to choose your best shirt." ([Live, July 2](../../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-02-live-with-sarah.md) [0:09:00]) |
| Mid-script | Creator builds theirs on camera, viewer picks along | "I picked [A] and [B]… which third one would you add?" (illustrative, not from course) |
| CTA | Comment-to-choose or build-your-own link | "Comment which one you'd pick and why." (from the WhatsApp idea, [0:50:50]) |

**Visual cues:** carousel cards with a clear "pick one" frame; creator's hands assembling or customising; a visible 1-2-3 step indicator.

## Spot it in a competitor ad
- "Build your bundle", "design your own", "choose your…", quiz funnels, "which would you pick?" carousels, comment-to-choose.
- Tag `heur-ikea`.

## Rules of thumb
- **DO** cap the choice at 2–3 options per step.
- **IF** the product isn't customisable **THEN** make the *ad* the participation (swipe, pick, comment).
- **DON'T** confuse participation with complexity.

## Common mistakes
- Full-catalogue configurators in a prospecting flow.
- Asking for choice without a reason to care (no payoff for choosing).
