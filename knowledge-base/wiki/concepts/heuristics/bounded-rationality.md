# Bounded Rationality

> **"We make decisions based on limited information, not perfect logic."** (Prompt Library #15). In ads, use it to tell the viewer they "didn't fail—they just chose from bad or incomplete options." Then give them the better option.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** the viewer may feel guilty or stupid about their current solution (diets, skincare routines, old tools), after any "you've been doing it wrong" hook, or when a script risks blaming the customer.
- **Inputs → outputs:** the customer's current or past choice and why it made sense at the time → an absolution line + the missing option (our product).
- **Rules:**
  - DO "remind them that they didn't fail—they just chose from bad or incomplete options" (#15 step 2).
  - DO "make them feel smart for surviving, not stupid for struggling" (#15 step 2).
  - DO move the cause from the person (willpower, discipline) to the inputs or options ("better inputs").
  - DO follow with a low-friction alternative that "feels so rational, it's irrational not to try it now" (#15 step 3).
  - DON'T use it alone. It's the bridge between a [hindsight](hindsight-bias.md) or [reframe](reframing.md) hook and the offer.
- **See also:** [Prompt #15](../../courses/tether-lab/07-prompt-library/15-hindsight-bias-and-bounded-rationality.md) · [Hindsight bias](hindsight-bias.md) · [Reframing](reframing.md) · [Vulnerability](vulnerability.md)

## Definition
"Bounded Rationality – the idea that we make decisions based on limited information, not perfect logic." (Prompt Library #15, Skool page only. The prompt isn't in the PDF.)

Step 2 of the framework is titled "Show How the Brain Made the Best of a Bad Menu."

## Deploy in a UGC script
*Derived guidance. Example lines are illustrative, not from course.*

| Script slot | Pattern | Example line (illustrative) |
|---|---|---|
| After the hook | Absolve | "And honestly? That's not on you. Every option out there was [limitation]." |
| Mechanism | Introduce the missing option | "Nobody told me there was a version that [does X]." |
| CTA | Rational, low-friction switch | "Same routine, one swap. Link below." |

**Visual cues:** the creator's warm, peer-to-peer tone, the "bad menu" shown (a shelf of the same old options), then the new option as one simple item.

## Spot it in a competitor ad
- "It's not your fault", "You weren't doing it wrong", "It's not about willpower", "with what you had", "nobody told you…".
- Tag: `lever-bounded-rationality`.

## Common mistakes
- Absolution that slides into flattery with no new option. The point is the *incomplete menu*.
- Skipping it after a harsh hook, so the ad blames the customer.
