# Iteration vs Variation (and Creative Diversity)

> **"Variations is what variety actually should be mostly predicated on."** ([Chase Mohseni](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-guest-session-02-chase-mohseni.md) [0:21:46]). Keep the **core message** fixed and vary its expression (primitives and formats); don't chase net-new concepts at scale. Sarah: "This idea that we need net new concepts at scale… that's insane, nobody can come up with that many ideas." [0:39:00]

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** planning the next batch after a winner; deciding between a net-new concept and a variation; turning one winning message into a multi-format ad set; reacting to "fatigue"; mining a winner for what to test next.
- **Inputs → outputs:** a winning ad + its test history → (a) a variation plan (same message, new primitives/formats), (b) a "why did it win" hypothesis split into threads, (c) the bucket each new ad belongs to (experiment / scaling / variation).
- **Rules:**
  - DON'T change the core message when iterating. "I don't like to iterate the core message… I like to iterate the core message in different formats." Static → something that moves (GIF, carousel, animation) → bigger video formats. [May 7 0:14:42–0:15:48]
  - DO keep **three buckets** live: net-new experiments, scaling winners, variations of the scaling winners. Spend most effort on variations. [Chase 0:21:46–0:22:31]
  - DO read **creative diversity** as *formats*, not volume: "I need a GIF, a static, a UGC, a pro video, a founder video… a weird one." "Your ads compete with organic more than they compete with basically anybody else." [June 18 0:56:08–0:57:11]
  - DO build an ad set as **one concept, many formats**, and pick ~**4 core messages** for the brand, each phrased many ways. [June 18 0:57:11–0:59:15]
  - DO multiply messages × formats: "One message with four formats, then test five messages: 5 × 4 = 20 ads." One-variable headline-only tests on identical statics are too slow for today's algorithm. [June 11 0:51:49–0:52:52]
  - DO test heuristics by iterating on the winning ad, one heuristic per variant, headline only. [July 9 0:24:56–0:30:15]
  - DO mine a winning headline for the **type** of desire it hit, then test one variant per type ("The Coolest Lunch in the Room" → peer popularity / status / inner confidence). [Roast #3 0:43:07–0:46:24]
  - DO split "why did it win?" into hypotheses (funny vs lived) with ~3 statics each. [June 18 0:39:07–0:41:19]
  - DON'T call frequency 2 fatigue. If *you* are bored of a winning format, keep it and change the product treatment (close-up, one side, two products, shrink, in hand, crop, personify). [Roast #3 0:51:41–0:55:52]
  - DO prove a new concept with ~10 statics before paying for UGC/pro video. [June 18 0:59:15–1:01:19]

## Definition
- **Iteration** (Sarah's use): re-expressing a proven **core message** in new formats or with one new element (heuristic badge, headline phrasing, visual treatment). The message stays.
- **Variation** (Chase's use): a new version of a working ad made by swapping one [creative primitive](creative-primitives.md): "a new kind of copy, a new word in your copy, a different product shot from that same photo shoot, a background color… an icon" ([Chase](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-guest-session-02-chase-mohseni.md) [0:19:10]).
- **Net new**: a new concept (new message/audience). Necessary in small doses ("experiments"), but "people… are generating net new concepts at 3× what they need to" [0:21:38].
- **Creative diversity**: the spread of formats and expressions in an ad set. "Volume [isn't] necessarily necessary, but diversity is massive because this is a social platform" ([June 18](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-18-live-with-sarah.md) [0:56:08–0:57:11]).

Sarah notes the "iteration versus variation conversation that was big in 2022… now nobody's talking about that anymore" ([Chase session](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-guest-session-02-chase-mohseni.md) [0:39:00–0:39:31]).

## Why it matters
- Net-new-first burns you out, overspends and means "you're not actually going to know what you can double down on" ([Chase](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-guest-session-02-chase-mohseni.md) [0:22:31]).
- "Core message is everything. If you don't know what the message is, or you're a little fuzzy on it, people can feel it through the screen" ([June 18](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-18-live-with-sarah.md) [1:01:19]).
- Different phrasings reach different people: "psychology-based creative" vs "grow your business using psychology"… "you're going to get a different person" [0:57:11–0:59:15].
- Static → video conversions fail when the variation drops the message ([Roast #3](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md) [0:18:56–0:21:32]).
- A new *format* is not a new *emotion*: AG1's offer-UGC and comparison statics were all Zone 1 ([June 11](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-11-live-with-sarah.md) [0:05:19–0:07:22]); diversify zones too ([emotional stagnation](emotional-stagnation.md)).

## How to use it
1. **Lock the message.** Write the winner's core message in one line (and its zone, intensity, self).
2. **Diagnose why it won.** Write 2 hypotheses (e.g. "it's funny" vs "it's lived"; "it's the redhead" vs "it's the copy"; which *type* of desire).
3. **Variation batch.** 3 variants per hypothesis, one primitive each.
4. **Format ladder.** Static winner → GIF/animation/carousel → UGC, pro video, founder video, fake podcast ([June 18](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-18-live-with-sarah.md) [0:59:15–1:01:19]).
5. **Diversity check.** Each ad set: one concept in several formats; across the account: the 4 core messages, several zones.
6. **Buckets.** Label every new ad as experiment / scaling / variation; keep experiments a minority.
7. **Fatigue check.** Before abandoning a format, check frequency; if ~2, vary the product treatment instead.

### Format-fatigue ladder (one product only) [Roast #3 0:52:41–0:55:52]
1. Extreme close-up (recognisable by signature shape) · 2. Product pushed to one side · 3. Two products ("one for you, one for another person") · 4. Shrink to ~80% / tiny · 5. In hand (shows size) · 6. Crop (see only into the cup) · 7. A detail that is "its own message" (cherry on top) · 8. Personify it ([anthropomorphism](heuristics/anthropomorphism.md)).

| Situation | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Winning static → UGC | Script built around the static's core message; message in the first line/frame | Take the winning static's line and have ChatGPT write a script (humorous, serious, founder…) around it ([June 11](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-11-live-with-sarah.md) [0:32:53–0:33:57]) |

| Testing "why" | Two script threads | Thread A universal moments ("You skipped breakfast, so it's gelato time"); thread B lived ("You spent three hours on dinner and he hated it…") ([June 18](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-18-live-with-sarah.md) [0:40:12–0:41:19]) |
| Proven format, bored team | Same format, new product treatment | Two cones, tiny product, in hand (Roast #3) |

**Visual cues:** same message/zone across formats; varied product treatment; GIF/carousel versions of static winners.

## Spot it in a competitor ad
- In the ad library, cluster by message: many formats of one message = they found a core message and are iterating (strong signal); many unrelated concepts = net-new chasing.
- A long-running ad with many siblings in different formats is a scaled winner.
- Check whether their formats also vary zone/emotion; if not, it's an [emotional stagnation](emotional-stagnation.md) gap.
- Don't treat ad-library longevity alone as proof of a win for big brands ([June 11](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-11-live-with-sarah.md) [0:24:32]).

## Rules of thumb
- **DO** keep the core message; vary format and primitives. [May 7 0:14:42]
- **DO** use ~4 core messages × many phrasings × many formats. [June 18 0:58:14]
- **IF** under 8 figures **THEN** 3–5 new tests a week, ~20 a month. [June 18 0:51:57–0:54:59]
- **IF** a winner's reason is unclear **THEN** split into hypothesis threads. [June 18 0:39:07]
- **IF** a brand refuses negative valence **THEN** generate "a hundred different ways" to say the winner. [Roast #3 0:42:03–0:43:07]
- **DON'T** abandon a winning format because the team is bored. [Roast #3 0:53:44]

## Common mistakes
- Treating "creative diversity" as "make more ads".
- Iterating the message instead of the format, so learning resets.
- Changing several variables per variant.
- Video variations that bury the winning line.

## Contradictions / notes
- **Headline-only tests:** on [June 11](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-11-live-with-sarah.md) [0:51:49–0:52:52] Sarah calls same-image/new-headline tests too slow for today's algorithm; on [July 9](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-09-live-with-sarah.md) [0:29:12–0:30:15] she praises Aazar's headline-only heuristic test as "one of the best ways to figure something out", and [Roast #3](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md) endorses OmieBox's incremental headline test. Wiki reading: headline-only tests are fine for a specific learning goal (which heuristic / which desire) on a proven winner; for scaling an account, diversify formats.
- **Volume:** Chase "3–5 at a time"; Sarah "≤50 a month, 3–5 new a week"; both oppose volume below 8 figures (Chase: below ~$10–20M).
