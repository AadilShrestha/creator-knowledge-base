# Emotional Stagnation (One-Zone Accounts)

> **"What will tank the account, and what will stunt the growth overall, is getting stagnant with your emotions."** Sarah's answer to "what's the #1 thing that kills a good ad?" is not a missing heuristic. It's a whole ad account (or category) stuck in one [emotional zone](emotional-zones.md) ([Live, June 11](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-11-live-with-sarah.md) [0:08:23]).

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** auditing a competitor's ad library or our own account before briefing; choosing which zones a new batch of UGC scripts and statics must cover; explaining why an account plateaus even though individual ads "look good".
- **Inputs → outputs:** 10–20 ads from one brand (statics as images, videos transcribed) → a zone tally (Z1–Z4 count per ad), the dominant zone, the missing zones, and a brief for the next batch with at least one concept per missing zone.
- **Rules:**
  - DO tag every ad in the library by [valence](valence.md) and [intensity](intensity.md) → zone. If (nearly) every ad sits in one zone, call it stagnation ([Live, June 11](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-11-live-with-sarah.md) [0:07:22–0:08:23]).
  - DO test all four zones at the same time and **keep testing the losing zones** after one wins. "As soon as that one stops working, which usually happens as soon as the seasons change… the customer base changes, the market changes… then you're scrambling" [0:18:05–0:19:08].
  - DO treat zones as attributes of **people, not brands**: "The zones aren't technically attributed to the brand, they're attributed to the person" [0:17:00].
  - DO go negative by calling out a true **industry** belief and praising the viewer for noticing it. DON'T trigger a negative sense of self [0:20:12–0:21:13].
  - DON'T assume a new format or offer means a new emotion. AG1's UGC-style offer ad and its comparison chart landed in the same zone as its product ad [0:05:19–0:08:23].
  - DON'T judge a stagnant big brand as "winning" from ad-library longevity. Big brands can afford to leave ads on [0:24:32].
- **See also:** [Emotional zones](emotional-zones.md) · [Angle rut](angle-rut.md) · [Valence-intensity diagnostic](../frameworks/valence-intensity-diagnostic.md) · [Tone-shifting](../frameworks/tone-shifting.md) · [Competitor teardown playbook](../playbooks/competitor-ad-teardown.md) · [Von Restorff effect](heuristics/von-restorff-effect.md)

## Definition
An account (or a whole category) is emotionally stagnant when nearly all of its ads trigger the same valence × intensity combination, so they only ever recruit the people who are in that emotional state. Sarah: "The larger you get as a brand the more you get stuck in one specific emotional zone and you never branch out" ([Live, June 11](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-11-live-with-sarah.md) [0:07:22]).

It is the account-level, emotional version of the [angle rut](angle-rut.md) (same angle / same self forever). *(Wiki link, not Sarah's wording.)*

## Why it matters
- **No zone, no motivation.** "If you stay in Zone 1 and the whole brand is in Zone 1… you're never going to push motivation into your customers because you're just stuck emotionally" [0:08:23].
- **Dopamine is motivation, not reward.** "Dopamine is not a reward transmitter… it's just the motivational one." Ads need the spike *and* real motivation behind it: "grab them, capture them and bring them in" [0:08:23–0:09:27].
- **A one-zone brand only recruits one kind of person.** AG1 draws "only people who are real happy but not very intense about their happiness" and misses the buyer who says "I have tried every supplement on the planet and all of them upset my stomach" (Zone 3) [0:17:00–0:18:05]. "That is actually how you grow brands: you've got to open up the emotional set that you're working with" [0:18:05].
- **Whole categories stagnate too.** Women's hair loss is "too aspirational… almost always means they're in high valence, high intensity" (Zone 2), while r/FemaleHairLoss is raw Zone 3/4 emotion no brand uses ([Live, June 4](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-04-live-with-sarah.md) [0:28:12–0:30:17]).
- **Most stagnant accounts are stuck soft.** "We're either being too soft with our messages or we just have entirely the wrong message" ([Live, May 7](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-07-live-with-sarah.md) [0:35:37–0:38:29]).

## How to use it
1. **Collect** 15–20 of the brand's live ads. Transcribe the videos. (Sarah: "a good 15 to 20 of their ads, including some of the videos" [0:07:22].)
2. **Classify** each with the prompt "Analyze this ad for valence + intensity. Which zone is this ad in?" (verbatim on screen, [0:04:30]); add the self question if you also want an [angle-rut](angle-rut.md) read.
3. **Tally** zones. Wiki threshold: ≥80% in one zone = stagnant (Sarah said "every single one"; the number is a wiki heuristic).
4. **Name the missing zones** and the person each one would recruit (e.g. Zone 3: "tried everything, stomach upset").
5. **Brief** at least one concept per missing zone, keeping the core message. For negative zones, aim at an industry belief and end with hope ([Live, June 4](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-04-live-with-sarah.md) [0:30:17]).
6. **Structure** the test so zones are readable: campaign = emotion, ad sets = zones ([Live, June 11](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-11-live-with-sarah.md) [0:21:13–0:22:19]).
7. **Re-audit monthly.** Shift budget toward the winning zone but never switch the others off [0:22:19–0:23:27].

## Deploy in a UGC script
| Script slot | Pattern | Example line |
|---|---|---|
| Zone 3 hook (the missing person) | Situational frustration in the buyer's words | "I've tried every supplement on the planet and all of them upset my stomach." ([Live, June 11](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-11-live-with-sarah.md) [0:18:05]) |
| Zone 3/4 industry call-out | Negative about the industry, positive about the viewer | "A lot of greens have nasty stuff in them, and you're a smart shopper for noticing." ([0:20:12], paraphrase) |
| Honest admission | Name the unpleasant truth, then the fix | "Yeah, it's probably not going to taste super great, but it dissipates quicker than everybody else." ([0:25:35], paraphrase) |
| Negative-zone ending | Always end with hope | "We see you, this is hard… but there is hope." ([Live, June 4](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-04-live-with-sarah.md) [0:30:17]) |

**Visual cues:** match the zone, not just the copy (Zone 1 soft light and pastels; Zone 2 bold type and energy; Zone 3 sighs and tension; Zone 4 harsh, high-contrast, "visually wrong" frames). See [emotional zones](emotional-zones.md).

## Spot it in a competitor ad
- Every ad in the library shares the same tone, palette and music regardless of format (static, UGC, comparison, offer).
- Even the pain point is whispered (AG1's gentle "Bloated?") [0:07:05].
- Upbeat music under struggle stories (hair-loss category) [Live, June 4, 0:28:12].
- Tag: `concept-emotional-stagnation` + `zone-dominant-Z1..Z4`. The missing zones are your opening.

## Rules of thumb
- **DO** "test all four. Test them as often as you can." [0:09:27]
- **IF** the whole category lives in one zone **THEN** the empty zones are the cheapest differentiation (go the opposite way, [Von Restorff](heuristics/von-restorff-effect.md)).
- **IF** a brand refuses negative valence **THEN** diversify within the positive side (Zone 1 vs Zone 2) and say the winner "a hundred different ways" ([Roast My Ads #3](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md) [0:42:03–0:43:07]).
- **DON'T** mistake a format change for an emotional change.

## Common mistakes
- Reading one ad's zone and stopping. Stagnation only shows at library level.
- "Fixing" stagnation by going harsh. Intensity is word choice, not cruelty ([Live, July 16](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md) [0:18:30]).
- Going negative about the viewer instead of the industry.
- Stopping the other zones once one wins.
