# Self-Targeting
> Targeting **which internal self** of the customer an ad activates (Actual, Ideal or Ought) instead of targeting an avatar, a demographic or a new angle. You reach new buyers with the **same product, offer and funnel**.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** deciding the "who" of a new ad or UGC script. Pick the self first, then the emotion, hook and format.
- **Inputs → outputs:** product + offer + current ad set (ours or a competitor's) → a self assignment per planned variant (e.g. 1 Actual / 1 Ideal / 1 Ought per winner) with copy direction for each.
- **Rules:**
  - DO target "the actual internal self of your customer… not the angle, not the customer… not avatars" [L3 08:16].
  - DO keep offer, landing page and price constant. Self is the only variable [L1 03:33], [L7 00:38].
  - DO cover all three selves over time. Narrowing in on one identity "is a pretty unsafe way to scale" [L8 04:58].
  - DO choose the emotion (valence and intensity) *after* the self [L6 02:20].
  - DON'T confuse self-targeting with platform audience targeting. It's done in the creative, and the algorithm finds the people [L7 04:12].
- **See also:** [3 selves](three-selves.md) · [Three selves diagnostic](../frameworks/three-selves-diagnostic.md) · [Rewriting ads for new selves](../frameworks/rewriting-ads-for-new-selves.md) · [Testing ads by self type](../frameworks/testing-ads-by-self-type.md)

## Definition
The **3-Self Targeting System** ("The '3-Self' Targeting System: Break the 'Angle Rut' By Targeting the Right Buyer Identity") is Sarah Levinger's method for keeping an ad account fresh:
1. **Diagnose** which self your ads speak to ([diagnostic](../frameworks/three-selves-diagnostic.md)).
2. **Reframe** winners for the selves you're missing ([rewriting](../frameworks/rewriting-ads-for-new-selves.md)).
3. **Test** the three versions side by side ([testing](../frameworks/testing-ads-by-self-type.md)).

> "Diagnose who we're speaking to, reframe all of our angles for that kind of new emotional entry point, and then test it all using… a very systematized messaging program that matches how your customers see themselves today in three different versions." [L8 05:08]

## Why it matters
- **Only one self buys at any moment.** "We need to target one specific self of your customer that's in the driver's seat and keep track of it." [L1 03:18]
- **No new offer needed.** "You don't need a new offer. You don't need a new landing page. You really just need to shift which self your ad is speaking to so you can reach new buyers who've been ignoring you up until now." [L1 03:33]
- **Cheap reach.** "We're just switching who we're inviting in… opening an emotional door to a different window of themselves… unlock these new buyer types with zero extra creative cost." [L7 03:45–04:00]
- **Bigger TAM, same ad count.** "This is how we open up our TAM… target more people with the same amount of ads." [L4 07:28]
- **Algorithm leverage.** "Your algorithm will do the heavy lifting for you, but you'll get much closer, much faster, much cheaper if you just activate a different sense of self." [L7 04:12]
- **Acts like auto-targeting.** It makes ads "more of this auto-target kind of a system and reduce angle fatigue, angle rut." [L8 05:30]

## Self-targeting vs avatar targeting
| | Avatar / persona targeting | Self-targeting |
|---|---|---|
| Unit | One static customer profile | One of three internal selves the same person moves between "throughout the day… throughout the year" [L3 08:00] |
| Assumption | The customer is one person | "Your customer type is actually hundreds of different avatars, all kind of wrapped into one person" [L3 02:06] |
| What changes in a new ad | Audience, angle or offer | Only the self the message speaks to |
| Risk | Angle rut as one segment dries up | Requires disciplined testing |

## Common mistakes
- Changing the offer or CTA alongside the self, which makes results unreadable [L7 00:38].
- Re-selfing a long-proven brand core message. Keep those [L7 02:43].
- Reading only conversions. Self tests measure identity response: speed, strength, depth [L8 00:41].
