# Ideal Self
> **"Who I want to be."** The aspirational, future-focused self that chases growth and potential. It's the self almost every brand defaults to, which is why over-relying on it causes [angle rut](angle-rut.md).

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** classifying an ad as Ideal-Self (the usual competitor default), spotting an Ideal-Self rut, or writing an Ideal variant for awareness and CTR.
- **Inputs → outputs:** copy or script → Ideal score + cues. Or: product + the aspirational identity → Ideal hook and script lines.
- **Rules:**
  - DO look for or write: "step into", "unlock your next level", "the version of you who…", "for [legends] only", "finally becoming", "what if this is the thing that finally works?".
  - DO paint the specific future scene (who they'll be, how others see them).
  - IF most of a competitor's ads score Ideal ≥7 **THEN** treat it as their rut and attack with Actual and Ought variants of their best mechanism.
  - DON'T crown an Ideal variant on CTR alone. Check AOV and conversion [L8 02:50].
  - DON'T make Ideal the only self in a test batch.
- **See also:** [3 selves](three-selves.md) · [Angle rut](angle-rut.md) · [Rewriting ads for new selves](../frameworks/rewriting-ads-for-new-selves.md)

One of the [3 selves](three-selves.md), alongside the [Actual Self](actual-self.md) and the [Ought Self](ought-self.md).

## Definition
- Lesson 4: "Who you want to be, your dreams, your hopes, your aspirations. This self… responds really, really well to messaging that paints a **vivid picture of the future**, like a better, more fulfilled version of you." [02:20] "It's not about safety necessarily. **The ideal self wants potential.**" [03:11]
- E-book: "Chases growth, pride, and emotional reward. Ads here feel aspirational and exciting."
- Prompt doc: "Speaks to who they *want to become*. Aspirational, future-focused, emotionally rewarding." / "Aspiration and emotional growth (future you)."
- Orientation: **future**. "I'm trying to make the future better." [L4 05:50]

## Why it matters
- **It's every brand's default.** "A lot of brands default to ideal self. I can't even think of a brand that doesn't currently message to ideal self." [L4 02:56] In audits it's "almost always the ideal self", written for "the achiever, the dreamer, the doer" [L3 00:34].
- **It dries up.** "There's not enough people who are in ideal self while they're purchasing… for that to like last forever." Aspirational "you can do it… our product's going to change your lifestyle" messaging just echoes what you've said "for the last like five to 10 years" [L3 00:58–01:30].
- **Fine for brand, fatiguing for ads.** "Doing this for the brand side is not necessarily a bad thing if you want to attach your brand to aspiration. But in the ad account, this fatigues pretty quick." [L3 01:33]
- **Performance signature (L8): high CTR, low AOV, faster clicks.** Ideal-mode people are "really click happy because they're trying to figure it out… exploratory evaluative mode", but "they don't really trust the product yet". "Lots of engagement… just like not a whole lot of commitment." [02:50] Prompt 2's example: "High CTR, weak conversions."
- **Funnel fit (e-book):** Awareness ("tap into emotion") and Consideration ("show transformation"). "Pull them in with possibility."

## How to target it
- **Message:** "here's what your life could look like if you just said yes to this particular product." **Sell hope, aspiration, thrill of emotion, progress.** [L4 03:15]
- **Lines from the course:**
  - "Step into the version that's been waiting for you" · "Unlock your next level" · "What if this is the thing that finally works?" (L4)
  - "Cook like the version of you who doesn't cut corners." / "Cook Like the Future You" (e-book, prompt doc)
  - "The skincare routine your future self deserves." (e-book)
  - "For campfire legends only." (L5) · "Spill The Tea, Not Your Wine." (L7)
- **Customer language:** "One day I'll…" "Finally becoming…" **Emotion:** hope, inspiration. **Trigger:** comparing themselves to who they want to be.
- **E-book prompt:** "Write an ad that speaks to who the customer wants to become. Emphasize transformation, possibility, and pride."

## Examples

*"For campfire legends only": "you could be this person if you had this product… The language is very transformational… It's next level." [L5 02:04]*

- **Product-hero ads skew Ideal.** In the lesson 6 run, ChatGPT found that High Camp uses Ideal "most when elevating the product itself", e.g. "May cause excessive compliments" and "I've tried a lot of flasks" comparisons. Sarah: "I might not want to be doing that." [L6 06:57]

## Common mistakes
- Making it the *only* self in the account. That's the classic [angle rut](angle-rut.md).
- Reading high CTR as success without checking AOV and conversion.
- Assuming any "future self" wording is Ideal. "Your future self will thank you" is duty framing and scores higher on **Ought** (e-book example: Ideal 7, Ought 9).
