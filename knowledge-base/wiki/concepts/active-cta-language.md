# Active CTA Language (Buy Verbs, Not Browse Verbs)

> **"The add-to-cart button is a save-for-later button. That's all it is."** ([Roast My Ads #2](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-25-roast-my-ads-2.md) [0:48:44]). Every CTA should push the one behavior you want now (get, grab, order, see how it works), never browsing ("Shop Now", "Learn More", "Browse"), and every line around it should be about *you*, not *us*.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** writing the CTA beat of a UGC script, end cards, static CTA lines, primary text, landing-page buttons and section headers; tearing down a competitor's funnel copy.
- **Inputs → outputs:** the action you want (buy, sign up, book, pre-order) + funnel stage → a definitive CTA line, button labels, "you"-framed section headers, and placement notes.
- **Rules:**
  - DON'T use "Shop Now", "Learn More", "View More" as the CTA you write: they "encourage browsing behavior". Meta limits the button, so write a **definitive CTA in the description** ("See how it works", "Let us show you why this is going to be better for you"). [Roast #1 0:26:52–0:28:09]
  - DO use action words: "**Get** it, acquire it, **grab** it", "**Select** the look", "**Order yours now**", "**Pre-order now**", "Get your dress now". "Shop" is "too vague for the brain… I can get to it later." [Roast #2 0:12:19–0:13:47; 0:47:34–0:49:14]
  - DON'T label a true landing page's buttons "Add to cart" or "Browse by categories". Use "Order yours now" / "Find your favorites". [Roast #2 0:12:52; 0:48:38–0:49:14]
  - DON'T read add-to-cart as purchase intent. [Roast #1 0:28:09–0:29:09]
  - DO push language **out, not in**: "Our latest collections" → "Your next favorite". Keep "our" only for the USP (materials, manufacturing). [Roast #2 0:14:49–0:15:45]
  - DO put buy buttons where the visitor is convinced (after the first product video, after the fit section), not only at the bottom. [Roast #2 0:38:05; 0:41:15]
  - DO keep one action per page when you can ("Schedule a call" in every section). [Roast #2 0:53:57; 0:58:14]
  - DO use heat maps to choose the next CTA ("Shop our best sellers" → the most-clicked category). [Roast #2 0:20:04–0:21:10]
  - DON'T reference something the visitor hasn't seen yet ("Discount applied automatically" before any discount is mentioned). [July 16 0:58:45]
  - IF the action is a sign-up THEN "Sign up" is fine. [Roast #1 0:28:09–0:29:09]

## Definition
CTA and button copy written in **active, definitive, buyer-owned** language: a verb that names the purchase-side action ("get", "grab", "order", "pre-order", "see how it works") plus "you/your" framing, instead of passive or browsing verbs ("shop", "browse", "learn more", "add to cart"). Sarah calls swapping passive for active language a "**CRO-level test**": "changing your passive language over towards active language does amazing things for conversion rate" ([Roast #2](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-25-roast-my-ads-2.md) [0:49:14]).

## Why it matters
- "Shop Now encourages browsing behavior… people just sort of browse around and leave" and "it teaches people to come back" ([Roast #1](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-28-roast-my-ads-1.md) [0:26:58–0:28:09]).
- "Add to cart" became save-for-later "mostly due to Amazon" ([Roast #2](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-25-roast-my-ads-2.md) [0:48:38–0:48:55]).
- "You" language creates **ownership**: "this brand is *my* brand, not just some brand" [0:14:49–0:15:23]. "What we really love as humans is hearing about ourselves" ([May 21](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-21-live-with-sarah.md) [0:18:10]).
- In the [Fogg model](../frameworks/fogg-behavior-model.md), CTAs are the prompt; marketers over-prompt but the prompt still has to point at the right action ([July 16](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md) [0:05:50–0:06:15]).

## How to use it
1. Name the single action for this asset (buy, pre-order, sign up, book a call).
2. Write the CTA with a buying verb + "your": "Get your ___ now", "Order yours now", "See how it works".
3. Rewrite every "we/our" line nearby as "you/your", except the USP line.
4. Place a CTA at each convinced moment (after the demo, after proof, after fit).
5. Check order: never refer to an offer before it's introduced.
6. For sign-ups or high-ticket: qualify instead of push ("See if you qualify", [June 18](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-18-live-with-sarah.md) [0:19:49–0:20:52]).

## Deploy in a UGC script
| Script slot | Pattern | Example line |
|---|---|---|
| CTA (spoken) | Definitive verb + benefit | "See how it works, link's right there." (illustrative, built on Roast #1 [0:27:30]) |
| CTA (goal gradient) | Distance-to-done | "You're 1 click away from better underwear." (Net Positive line, [Roast #3](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md) [0:12:36]) |
| CTA (pre-order / drop) | Ownership + time context | "Pre-order yours now, the next drop comes in a week." (illustrative, built on Roast #2 [0:47:34] and Roast #3 [0:47:27]) |
| CTA (qualify) | Access, not urgency | "See if you qualify." ([June 18](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-18-live-with-sarah.md) [0:19:49]) |
| Pre-CTA friction | Call out the barrier | "Yeah, we're sketched out about the credit card too…" (see [barrier buster](barrier-buster.md)) |

**Visual cues:** a single high-contrast rounded button, an arrow pointing to it ("arrows are freaking amazing", [Roast #2](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-25-roast-my-ads-2.md) [0:55:46]), end card with one verb.

## Spot it in a competitor ad
- Button/primary-text verb: shop/learn/browse (passive) vs get/grab/order/see how (active).
- "Our/we" vs "your/you" density on the landing page.
- How many different actions the page offers.
- Offer references out of order.
- Tags: `cta-active`, `cta-browse` (gap), `copy-you-focus`, `copy-us-focus`.

## Rules of thumb
- **DON'T** use "Shop Now" on landing pages, "never ever ever". [Roast #1 0:28:09]
- **DO** use buying verbs everywhere a click can happen. [Roast #2 0:47:34]
- **DO** keep "our" for the USP only. [Roast #2 0:15:23]
- **DO** test passive → active as a CRO test. [Roast #2 0:49:14]
- **IF** high-ticket or exclusive **THEN** "See if you qualify", not "limited spaces". [June 18 0:18:44–0:20:52]

## Common mistakes
- Treating add-to-cart rate as a purchase-intent KPI.
- Writing "Shop Now" in primary text because that's the button.
- Several CTAs with different verbs competing on one page.

## Contradictions / notes
- ChatGPT's dog-chew story script ended "[Shop Now] and start the routine that even your vet will notice" and Sarah didn't object live ([May 14](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-14-live-with-sarah.md) [0:42:30]); she had prompted "final CTA - shop now" herself. Her explicit rule in the roasts is still: replace "Shop Now".
