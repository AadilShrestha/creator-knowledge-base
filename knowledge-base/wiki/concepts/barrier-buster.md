# Barrier Buster (Call Out the Friction)

> **"Honestly, freaking call it out."** Name the friction the buyer already feels (a credit card for a "free" trial, a bad taste, a slow ship time) and say why they're still fine: **"Yeah, we're sketched out about having to put in a credit card too."** ([Joanna Wallace](../people/joanna-wallace.md), [Guest Session 01](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-23-guest-session-01-joanna-wallace.md) [0:38:48–0:39:50])

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** a product has a known objection (taste, price, trial card, shipping time, fit, difficulty); writing the objection beat of a UGC script or checkout/landing microcopy; recovering after a joke went too far; tearing down a competitor that hides (or names) its weak spot.
- **Inputs → outputs:** the product's biggest friction point (from reviews, support tickets, the [Fogg](../frameworks/fogg-behavior-model.md) "ability crusher") → one call-out line + one reassurance line (+ optional humor), placed where the friction appears.
- **Rules:**
  - DO name the friction openly, then reassure: "Yeah, we know, and here's directly how… despite the thing you're concerned about, you're good" ([Guest Session 01](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-23-guest-session-01-joanna-wallace.md) [0:39:50–0:40:56]).
  - DO put the line **where the friction happens** (the card-entry page, the trial-expiry email) [0:38:48–0:39:50].
  - DO "call out what's actually true, because people are so tired of being sold… they're tired of not hearing the truth" ([Live, June 11](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-11-live-with-sarah.md) [0:25:35–0:26:35]).
  - DO find the product's **biggest ability crusher** and answer it visually or in 4–5 words ([Live, July 16](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md) [0:27:14]).
  - DO affirm research-heavy buyers: "we're not going to try and pull one over on you, here's all the information" ([Live, June 4](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-04-live-with-sarah.md) [0:35:35–0:36:36]).
  - DON'T hide it: "either they're gonna say 'they're trying to cover it up' or they respect you for being like 'yeah we know'" ([Guest Session 01](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-23-guest-session-01-joanna-wallace.md) [0:39:50–0:40:56]).

## Definition
"Barrier buster" is Sarah's name (coined on the spot in the Joanna session [0:39:50]) for copy that names a known purchase barrier instead of hiding it, defuses it (often with humor or self-deprecation), and tells the buyer how it's handled. Joanna's principle extends to any friction: "you've either gone too far with your comedy or there's a problem with your product or there's some sort of bottleneck in your funnel, call it out" [0:39:50].

In Fogg terms it raises **ability** ("is this for me / can I do this / is it safe?") instead of adding more prompts ([Live, July 16](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md) [0:05:50–0:06:30; 0:25:30–0:27:14]).

## Why it matters
- Buyers are scanning for "a reason not to buy… the brain is going to go through its negative checklist" ([Roast My Ads #3](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md) [0:15:46–0:16:50]). Naming the item ticks it off.
- Trial users look for a break: "I'm actually looking for one little piece of this that is just wrong enough so that I can get out" ([Live, July 9](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-09-live-with-sarah.md) [0:22:03–0:23:53]). Find and name (or fix) that snag first.
- Honesty is a differentiator in categories full of hype (supplements: "we've gone to great lengths to make sure there's nothing in ours that's going to hurt you or upset your stomach") ([Live, June 11](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-11-live-with-sarah.md) [0:25:35]).

## How to use it
1. List every friction point: price, card for trial, taste, ship time, fit, learning curve, "won't work for me" (reviews, support, competitor complaints).
2. Pick the biggest "ability crusher" ([Live, July 16](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md) [0:27:14]).
3. Write: **admit** ("Yeah, it's probably not going to taste super great…") → **handle** ("…but it's going to dissipate quicker than everybody else") ([Live, June 11](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-11-live-with-sarah.md) [0:25:35]).
4. Add humor if it fits the brand ("we're sketched out too").
5. Place it at the moment of friction: script beat right before the CTA, checkout microcopy, trial email.
6. Reframe the friction where possible ("the next drop comes in a week" instead of "ships in four weeks", [Roast My Ads #3](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md) [0:47:27–0:48:00]).

## Spot it in a competitor ad
- Lines like "I know what you're thinking…", "yes, it costs more, here's why", "it tastes like grass, we fixed that".
- Absence is also a signal: if every competitor hides the same friction, naming it is an opening.
- Tag: `concept-barrier-buster` + the friction (`friction-price`, `friction-trial-card`, `friction-taste`, `friction-shipping`, `friction-fit`).

## Rules of thumb
- **IF** there's product, funnel or comedy friction **THEN** call it out and reassure ([Guest Session 01](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-23-guest-session-01-joanna-wallace.md) [0:38:48–0:40:56]).
- **IF** the product is ingestible **THEN** say the uncomfortable truth (taste, side effects) and how you handle it ([Live, June 11](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-11-live-with-sarah.md) [0:24:32–0:26:35]).
- **DO** solve the biggest ability crusher first ([Live, July 16](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md) [0:27:14]).
- **DON'T** reference something before introducing it ("discount applied automatically" before any discount), which creates new friction [0:58:45].

## Common mistakes
- Naming the friction without the reassurance (now it's just a warning).
- Over-apologising until the friction looks bigger than it is.
- Burying the call-out in the footer instead of at the moment of friction.
