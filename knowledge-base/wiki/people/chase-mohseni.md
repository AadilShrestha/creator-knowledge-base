# Chase Mohseni
> **CEO of [CreativeOS](../tools/creativeos.md)**, Tether Lab's partner template/inspiration tool, and a long-time friend of [Sarah Levinger](sarah-levinger.md). He was the guest on **Guest Session 02 (June 2025)**, where he taught a statics-first, small-batch testing method built on the **3 Vs of creative: Vibe, Variety, Velocity**.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** planning creative test batches (how many, what to vary), deciding statics vs video, building variations from a winning ad, checking ad ↔ landing-page congruence, setting CAC expectations.
- **Inputs → outputs:** a winning or seed ad → 3–5 variations that each change one "creative primitive", checked against Vibe / Variety / Velocity.
- **Rules (his, distilled):** statics before video (sub-$5M brands skip video); test 3 at a time; variations over net-new concepts; vibe first, velocity last; "permission equals speed"; baseline on typical months, not outliers. Details and timestamps: [the session page](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-guest-session-02-chase-mohseni.md).
- **See also:** [CreativeOS](../tools/creativeos.md) · [3 Vs of Creative](../frameworks/three-vs-of-creative.md) · [Creative primitives](../concepts/creative-primitives.md)

## Key ideas (with links back)
| Idea | One-line summary | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Statics first** | Static ads are the best place to start scaling; "if you're sub five million dollars, you shouldn't mess around with video at all." Video needs volume and someone to "instrument" testing. | [0:04:18–0:06:32](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-guest-session-02-chase-mohseni.md) |
| **Creative primitives** | Borrowed from engineering's "data primitives": copy, product, background (plus color, icon, badge, crop) are the units you vary. | [0:06:25] |
| **Test in threes** | "Make three, test those, next batch of three"; find a beachhead before going wide. "Volume is evil until you have data." | [0:06:44; 0:07:58; 0:58:52] |
| **3 Vs of Creative** | **Vibe** (ad matches brand and post-click page; under half a second before bounce), **Variety** (what each ICP cohort wants to see), **Velocity** (idea → agreed live asset). Velocity is subservient to the other two. | [0:07:58–0:12:50] |
| **Templates as sandbox** | Give non-designers "the tooling and the spine" so their customer insight can flourish: "make anyone into a world-class creative in five minutes or less." | [0:13:50–0:15:12] |
| **Variety = variations** | "A new concept is a new color, a new icon." People make net-new concepts at 3× the rate they need. Keep net-new experiments, scaling winners and variations of winners in the account. | [0:19:10–0:22:31] |
| **Unique landing pages** | Only ~30% of ads he's seen go to a unique LP; clone the home page and change the copy. | [0:18:43–0:20:26] |
| **Leverage → margin** | Leverage creates time margin and financial margin, which fund experiments ("start small and then win big"). | [0:23:17–0:26:11] |
| **First principles** | Distrust advice from people selling you something; psychology "hits no matter what"; "we're still advertising the same way David Ogilvy did." | [0:26:24–0:27:53] |
| **Baselines vs outliers** | A $25 CAC month against a usual $50 is an outlier; grind 50 → 45 → 42 → 39. | [0:30:40–0:31:44] |
| **Agency expectation-setting** | Client management is the job; work from abundance; client pressure = fear about their P&L. | [0:33:47–0:37:58] |
| **Against AI volume testing** | Of 50–100 AI ads, the best might get $4 of spend and never signal; AI narrative video editing quality is poor. | [0:28:35; 0:42:06–0:44:13] |
| **Permission equals speed** | Get one seed asset approved, then ship variations without re-approval. | [0:48:08] |
| **Combination marketing / customer genomes** | Test copy × image pairs; different pairs win for different customer types. | [0:56:48–0:57:55] |
| **Commerce, not poetry** | Choose creative by the customer's response, not your taste. | [0:55:46] |

## Caveats an agent should know
- He has a "financially vested interest" in templates (his words) [0:13:50]. Treat CreativeOS roadmap claims from June 2025 (editor in ~3 weeks, project management in ~6 weeks, video analysis) as dated.
- His aside that a social-proof badge can show any number "whether you had five customers or 50,000" [0:19:42] **should not be followed**: fabricated proof breaks the course's honesty stance and advertising rules.
- Some numbers are hearsay or don't add up: HexClad ≈ $345M ("from what I have been told"); "2% → 3% CR on a $10M brand = +$7M" (≈ +$5M by the maths).
