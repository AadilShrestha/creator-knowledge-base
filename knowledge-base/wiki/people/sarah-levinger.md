# Sarah Levinger

> Creator and instructor of **Tether Lab**. She is a consumer-psychology and behavioral-science practitioner who designs psychology-led ad creative for DTC/e-commerce brands under the **Tether** ("tether. consumer insights") brand.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** you need Sarah's stance on a creative decision, the place in the course where she teaches a topic, or the list of her own client ads (the brand files marked "Sarah's client").
- **Inputs → outputs:** a question ("should we discount?", "AI UGC?", "how many tests a week?") → her position with a citation, and the lesson/session to read.
- **Rules:**
  - DO apply her priority order: **identity → emotion → behaviour/tactics**; heuristics are "secondary characters" ([May 7](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-07-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DO keep her honesty line: "Customers aren't dumb. Make it easier, not sneakier." No fabricated proof, scarcity or data.
  - DO check the [recurring positions](#recurring-positions-and-principles-from-the-live-sessions) table before contradicting her in a brief; flag it if a guest disagreed.
- **See also:** [People hub](README.md) · [Brands hub](../brands/README.md) · [Tools hub](../tools/README.md)

## Positioning and philosophy
- **Psychology over persuasion:** "Why psychology beats persuasion every time" (Heuristics 101 L1 page). "I don't think anybody should be out here convincing anybody to do things." The job is to make the brain's shortcuts easier ([L5 08:35](../courses/tether-lab/02-heuristics-101/05-stacking-psychology-for-growth.md)).
- **Anti-manipulation:** "Customers aren't dumb. Make it easier, not sneakier." (e-book p.17)
- **Subconscious-first:** "Good ads talk to the boss. The subconscious." (e-book p.3)
- **Simplicity:** "Please don't over design your way into trying to convince people to buy from you" ([L3 03:50](../courses/tether-lab/02-heuristics-101/03-emotion-clarity-urgency.md)). Processing fluency is the heuristic she uses "as much as I possibly can."
- **Beyond DTC defaults:** scarcity, social proof and authority are "very expected." The less-known heuristics are "doing the biggest numbers in the ad account" ([L2 06:11](../courses/tether-lab/02-heuristics-101/02-using-biases-that-sell.md)).
- **Practical, plug-and-play:** she wants members to "outperform 99% of the marketers who are out there still guessing" ([L5 08:02]). The prompts are designed to be "plug and play" ([Welcome 03:45]).
- **Favourites:** the framing effect ("might be my ultimate favorite") and the goal gradient effect ("I cannot get enough of this particular one") ([L4](../courses/tether-lab/02-heuristics-101/04-hook-attention-guide-behavior.md)).
- **Partner tool:** [CreativeOS](../tools/creativeos.md). Static templates are "the quickest, easiest way to put the ideas we teach into action."
