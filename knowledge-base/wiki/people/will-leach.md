# Will Leach
> Behavioural scientist and author of ***Marketing to Mindstates***, which Sarah calls "one of the best books I've ever read" and her **#1 book pick**. His model of **nine emotions people go through while purchasing** is the emotion list Sarah uses to code reviews and to structure emotion-based ad sets ([June 4 0:13:31](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-04-live-with-sarah.md); [July 9 0:13:23](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-09-live-with-sarah.md)).

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** mining reviews for the emotions behind a purchase, structuring an emotion test (ad set per emotion), or finding a brand's core emotion.
- **Inputs → outputs:** ~80 paragraph-length reviews → ChatGPT maps them to the nine purchase emotions → top 3 emotions → three nuanced ads per emotion.
- **Rules (Sarah's application):**
  - DO map reviews to the nine emotions and keep the **top three**. Most industries have ~3 (sometimes 4 for very generalised products): two shared across the category plus one brand-specific outlier ([June 4 0:13:31–0:14:34](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-04-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DO write **three nuanced ads per emotion** (esteem → confidence, control, appeal), then drill down with valence-intensity ([June 4 0:36:36–0:38:43](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-04-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DO get specific. "It's not fear that they're feeling, it's disappointment… The only reason ads are expensive is because we're using a generalized term" ([June 4 0:38:43–0:39:49](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-04-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DON'T treat the nine as the only valid emotions. "You could test embarrassment… disappointment… anxiety… as long as you're very careful with how you're saying it" ([June 4 0:16:41](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-04-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DO watch for tiny salient cues in the buying environment (his Frito-Lay story below).
