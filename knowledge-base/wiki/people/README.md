# People hub
> Everyone named in Tether Lab with a role in the method: the instructor, guests, the researchers and authors whose models the course uses, the community members whose ads were roasted, and people mentioned in passing. People with substance have their own page; the rest are listed here with what was said and where.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** you need the source of a model (Fogg, Higgins, Leach), the context of a guest's advice, or the member behind a teardown.
- **Inputs → outputs:** a name → their page, or a one-line summary with citation.
- **Rules:**
  - DO attribute models correctly in briefs and notes: B = MAP → BJ Fogg; Actual/Ideal/Ought → E. Tory Higgins; nine purchase emotions → Will Leach.
  - DO weigh guest advice against Sarah's: Chase's "put any number on a badge" aside is explicitly not to be followed ([Chase Mohseni](chase-mohseni.md)).
- **See also:** [Tools hub](../tools/README.md) · [Brands hub](../brands/README.md) · [Books](../resources/books.md)
