# BJ Fogg
> Stanford behaviour scientist behind the **Fogg Behavior Model (B = MAP)**: a behaviour happens when **Motivation, Ability and a Prompt** meet at the same moment. Sarah taught the whole July 16 session on it and applied it to seven product pages. Her main takeaway: marketers over-prompt and under-invest in **ability** ([July 16 0:02:50–0:06:30](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md)).

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** auditing a landing page/PDP or an ad for why people don't act; writing microcopy that makes the choice feel easy ("this will work for you").
- **Inputs → outputs:** a page or ad → each element sorted into Motivation / Ability / Prompt → the missing ability cues to add (fit labels, hair types, dimensions, time-to-result visuals, dollar savings).
- **Rules (Sarah's application):**
  - DO look for the ability gap first: "We're really good at prompting but we're pretty bad… about making it easy to do" ([July 16 0:06:15](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DO answer "does this fit me / will it work for me?" on the page: a sock brand's two width labels lifted conversion +10% ([July 16 0:06:30–0:10:24](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DO "soothe" when talking to ability: "it's okay, this is for you, we got you" ([July 16 0:35:20](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DO use the 3 selves to choose *whose* ability you address: "Self-discrepancy… does a better job of choosing whose abilities" ([July 16 0:32:40](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md)).
