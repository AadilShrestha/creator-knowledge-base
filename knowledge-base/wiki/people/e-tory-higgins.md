# E. Tory Higgins
> Psychologist (Edward Tory Higgins) who introduced **Self-Discrepancy Theory** in **1987**, the basis of Tether Lab's **3 Selves** system (Actual, Ideal, Ought). The gap between versions of yourself feels uncomfortable, and that discomfort motivates change or buying ([Self-Targeting L4 01:04](../courses/tether-lab/04-self-targeting-system/04-the-3-selves-that-drive-every-buying-decision.md)).

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** classifying which self an ad targets, rewriting a winner for another self, or choosing proof types and funnel placement per self.
- **Inputs → outputs:** an ad or script → the self it activates (Actual / Ideal / Ought) → rewrites for the missing selves.
- **Rules (Sarah's application of the theory):**
  - DO lead with Actual or Ideal in cold ads and save Ought for after qualification. Ought from a stranger "feels too accusatory" ([June 18 0:08:25–0:10:29](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-18-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DO match proof to self: peer reviews for Actual, authority/press for Ought ([Self-Targeting L7](../courses/tether-lab/04-self-targeting-system/07-how-to-rewrite-winning-ads-for-new-selves.md)).
  - IF your copy says "should", THEN you are activating Ought: "Anytime you use the word *should* in your marketing, you're trying to activate ought" ([June 18 0:12:31](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-18-live-with-sarah.md)).
  - DO use the theory with the Fogg model: SDT picks *whose* ability you're addressing ([July 16 0:32:40](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-16-live-with-sarah.md)).
- **See also:** [Three selves](../concepts/three-selves.md) · [Three selves diagnostic](../frameworks/three-selves-diagnostic.md) · [Rewriting ads for new selves](../frameworks/rewriting-ads-for-new-selves.md) · [3-Selves prompts](../resources/three-selves-prompts.md)
