# Tether Lab community members
> The recurring members of Tether Lab's live calls, **their brands or clients**, and the sessions they appear in. Use this page as context for the teardowns: most "real ads" in the course are members' ads, roasted live, and each member's questions show what was tested and what happened next.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** reading a teardown and needing context (whose ad, which niche, what was tried before and after); or looking for every case involving one member's brand.
- **Inputs → outputs:** member name or brand → their brand file(s), the sessions they appear in, and the results they reported (CTR, CPA, wins).
- **Rules:**
  - DO treat member-reported metrics as anecdotes from one account (e.g. Canvas Gelato CPA $22 → $6; Net Positive CTR 6.5–7%; the sock test +10% CR), not benchmarks.
  - DO follow a member across sessions: several cases have a "before" in one call and a result in a later one (Ahmad's gelato: Roast #1 → June 4 meme → June 18 burned-dinner winner).
  - DON'T copy members' invented claims (Darian's "Only 9 sets left" was made up). The course flags fabricated scarcity and proof.
  - Names come from Zoom tiles, chat and Skool posts; Whisper spellings vary. Spellings below follow the session pages' fixes.
- **See also:** [Brands hub](../brands/README.md) · [Live recordings](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/README.md) · [Sarah Levinger](sarah-levinger.md)
