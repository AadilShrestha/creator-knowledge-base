# Hooks library
> Every hook harvested from the Tether Lab wiki (215 unique, from 327 harvested rows), tagged by heuristic, emotional zone, self, TEEP stage, niche, format and origin, grouped by hook pattern family with a fill-in template for each.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** writing the first line / first 3 seconds of a UGC script or the headline of a static; labelling a competitor's hook during a teardown ("which family is this?"); generating hook variants for a test batch.
- **Inputs → outputs:** target self + zone + TEEP stage + niche (from the brief or teardown) → 3–10 matching real examples + the family template → new hooks written for **our** product in that template.
- **Rules:**
  - DO copy the *mechanism* (family template + heuristic), never the line. Fill every `{slot}` with our product's true claims, our customer's words and our proof.
  - DO filter by **self and zone first**, then family. A great curiosity hook for an Ideal buyer misfires on an Actual buyer ([three selves](../concepts/three-selves.md), [emotional zones](../concepts/emotional-zones.md)).
  - DO keep cold ads on Actual / Ideal; save Ought hooks ("start acting like it", "you should") for retargeting and warm audiences ([Live Jun 18](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-18-live-with-sarah.md) [0:05:15–0:10:29]).
  - DO write Actual-self hooks as **situations** ("You've checked the dashboard 20 times today…") and use typed crowds ("chai lovers", not "customers").
  - DO end any Zone 3/4 hook's ad with hope or a fix. DON'T shame the viewer; put the shame on a character (pet, kid, skit) or on the industry.
  - DON'T stack two emotional hooks in one ad: one hook, one core emotion ([one core emotion per ad](../concepts/one-core-emotion-per-ad.md)).
  - DO prefer **course-real** rows as evidence of what works. Treat **prompt-output** and **derived** rows as idea starters, not proof.
  - DO give each test batch at least two families and two zones, so the account doesn't stagnate ([emotional stagnation](../concepts/emotional-stagnation.md)).
- **See also:** [Angles library](angles.md) · [Rewrites library](rewrites.md) · [Heuristics hub](../concepts/heuristics.md) · [TEEP journey](../frameworks/teep-journey.md) · [Rewriting ads for new selves](../frameworks/rewriting-ads-for-new-selves.md) · [Tone-shifting](../frameworks/tone-shifting.md) · [Skeptic format](../frameworks/skeptic-format.md) · [Agent guide](../../AGENT-GUIDE.md)

## How to use
1. **Pick the filters** from your brief: self, zone, TEEP stage, niche group. Use the quick-filter tables below to get candidate hook IDs, then intersect the lists (e.g. *curiosity hooks for an Actual-self skincare buyer* = the Curiosity family ∩ Self: Actual ∩ Niche: Skincare; widen to neighbouring families such as Skeptic or Pain if that comes back empty).
2. **Read the family template**, then 2–3 examples, then write our version.
3. **Check the tags** on the example before you copy its move: an Ought/Zone 4 example isn't a template for a cold Actual-self ad.

**Column key**
- **Hook**: verbatim or near-verbatim, as recorded on the source page. `{…}` / `[…]` = a slot in the original.
- **Heuristic(s)**: linked to the heuristic or concept page. Plain text = a mechanism with no page yet.
- **Zone**: Z1 feel-good (high valence, low intensity) · Z2 hype (high, high) · Z3 pain-point whisper (low, low) · Z4 danger (low, high). `Z3 → Z1` = the line moves the viewer from one zone to another.
- **Self**: [Actual](../concepts/actual-self.md) / [Ideal](../concepts/ideal-self.md) / [Ought](../concepts/ought-self.md). `—` = the source doesn't say and it can't be inferred safely.
- **TEEP stage**: Trigger / Explore / Evaluate / Purchase ([TEEP journey](../frameworks/teep-journey.md)). Most are inferred from Sarah's classification cues: event call-out = Trigger; entertaining, weird or before/after = Explore; comparison, ingredients, founder or skeptic = Evaluate; offer, urgency, scarcity or guarantee = Purchase.
- **`*` = inferred tag.** Either the source page marked it "(wiki)" / "wiki tag" or this library inferred it. Untagged values were stated in the source row.
- **Origin**: **course-real** = Sarah, a guest or a member said or showed it (live sessions, lessons, e-book mock ads, and the example lines printed inside course prompts). **prompt-output** = generated by ChatGPT from a course prompt (Copper Cow zone rewrites, the Prompt-3 self rewrites, live ChatGPT story builds, the Prompt #3 demo). **derived** = a wiki-written illustration.
- **Source**: the page that records the hook, with `[timestamps]`. "also:" lists the other wiki pages that repeat it (merged duplicates).

## Coverage & gaps
- **Selves are lopsided:** 147 hooks touch Actual, 51 Ideal, only 30 Ought. Most Ought rows are slide one-liners, the Milky confrontation series, pet-parent guilt or prompt demos. There are few tested Ought hooks for skincare, apparel or food.
- **Zone 4 is thin (17 rows)** and is mostly prompt demos (reverse psychology), Copper Cow's prompt rewrite and Sarah's spoken insurance example. There's no tested Zone 4 UGC hook with a result attached.
- **TEEP is mostly inferred.** Only a handful of rows state a stage (trigger examples, Amazon evaluation, "1 click away" purchase, exploration stunts). Trigger-event hooks are rare (12 rows).
- **Performance data is scarce.** Only a few hooks carry results: burned-dinner gelato (CPA $22 → $6), "keeps selling out" (won a 5-way test), the Maelove blended static (ran 8 months), "Best for most legs" (+10% CR), "next drop comes in a week" (CVR up). Treat the rest as taught patterns, not proven winners.
- **Spoken UGC openers are fewer than static headlines.** Many rows are static headlines, badges or PDP copy. The true first-3-second UGC lines come mainly from May 14 (Maelove scripts, prompt-output), Joanna's HexClad/Lingopie examples and the Jul 9 hair-loss session.
- **Niches are uneven.** No hooks for tech, automotive or luxury beyond spoken notes; beauty is mostly Maelove and hair loss; food is mostly gelato, coffee and golden milk.
