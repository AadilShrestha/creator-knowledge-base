# Angles library
> Every angle harvested from the Tether Lab wiki (140 unique, from 152 harvested rows), grouped by the core desire or pain it serves and tagged by heuristic, zone, self, best format, niche and origin. Ends with recipes for spinning new angles out of one winner.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** (1) naming the angle of a competitor's winning ad in a teardown; (2) choosing angles for a new creative batch or creator brief; (3) turning one winning angle into a batch of variants (see [Angle generation recipes](#angle-generation-recipes)).
- **Inputs → outputs:** product + customer research (reviews, Reddit, survey phrases) + the competitor angle or our winner → 3–6 angles, each with core desire, heuristic, zone, self, format and one example line in our product's words → hooks from [hooks.md](hooks.md) to open each one.
- **Rules:**
  - DO name the angle by the **desire or pain** it serves, not by the product feature. "Last switch you'll ever make", not "serum with retinol".
  - DO find the angle in the customer's own words (reviews, calls, Reddit) before writing it ([customer research stack](../frameworks/customer-research-stack.md)). Don't let ChatGPT invent the customer.
  - DO keep [one core emotion per ad](../concepts/one-core-emotion-per-ad.md). Merge two winning angles only as headline + subhead ([blended emotions](../concepts/blended-emotions.md)).
  - DO cover more than one self and more than one zone per batch. Most competitors over-index on Ideal and Zone 1–2, so Actual, Ought (warm only) and Zone 3 are usually the open lanes ([three selves](../concepts/three-selves.md), [emotional stagnation](../concepts/emotional-stagnation.md)).
  - DON'T change the offer when you change the angle. Change the promise, the proof type and the visual.
  - DON'T copy a competitor's line or claim. Copy the mechanism (desire → heuristic → format) and prove it with our own facts.
  - DO treat rows whose origin says "line: derived" or "prompt-output" as idea starters, not evidence.
- **See also:** [Hooks library](hooks.md) · [Rewrites library](rewrites.md) · [Rewriting ads for new selves](../frameworks/rewriting-ads-for-new-selves.md) · [Tone-shifting](../frameworks/tone-shifting.md) · [Angle rut](../concepts/angle-rut.md) · [TEEP journey](../frameworks/teep-journey.md) · [Agent guide](../../AGENT-GUIDE.md)

## How to use
1. Find the theme that matches the customer's dominant desire or pain (table below), or filter by zone, self or niche.
2. Pick 2–3 angles from **different** zones or selves. Read the example line and the source page for context.
3. Write our version: desire → our proof → best format → a hook from [hooks.md](hooks.md) that matches the same self and zone.
4. To multiply a winner, run the [recipes](#angle-generation-recipes).

**Column key:** Angle and Core desire / pain are as recorded on the source page. **Heuristic(s)** are linked to their pages. **Zone** Z1–Z4 ([emotional zones](../concepts/emotional-zones.md)), where `A → B` = the angle moves the buyer between zones. **Self**: Actual / Ideal / Ought. **`*` = inferred tag** (marked "(wiki)" on the source page or inferred by this library). **Best format** and **Example line** come from the source; lines marked *Illustrative* were written by the wiki, not the course. **Origin**: **course-real** = said or shown in the course (including angles defined in course prompts); **prompt-output** = generated by ChatGPT from a course prompt (live story builds, Maelove scripts); "(line: derived)" / "(line: prompt-output)" = the angle is course-real but the example line isn't. **Source** = the page with `[timestamps]`; "also:" = other pages that repeat it.

## Angle generation recipes
How to spin a batch of new angles out of **one** winner (ours or a competitor's) without starting from scratch. Each recipe changes one variable and keeps the rest, so the test result tells you what worked. ### Recipe 1: change the self (Actual ↔ Ideal ↔ Ought)
Procedure: [Rewriting ads for new selves](../frameworks/rewriting-ads-for-new-selves.md) (3 Selves Prompt 3).
1. Classify the winner's self by its motivation frame: present identity = Actual · future becoming = Ideal · duty / should = Ought ([three selves diagnostic](../frameworks/three-selves-diagnostic.md)).
2. Rewrite for each missing self. Keep the offer and the visual. Change the **promise** and the **proof type**: Actual = peer reviews and identity call-outs; Ideal = transformation and legend status; Ought = authority, press, safety.
3. Worked example: High Camp's Ideal "Spill The Tea, Not Your Wine." (H202) → Ought "Don't Risk the Bottle… Real Adults Bring a Flask" (H147) → Actual "For Wine Lovers Who Know… You Already Bring the Good Stuff" (H046) ([3 Selves L7](../courses/tether-lab/04-self-targeting-system/07-how-to-rewrite-winning-ads-for-new-selves.md) [02:02–02:30]). Each move is a row in [rewrites.md](rewrites.md).
4. Run Ought only to warm traffic. Keep cold ads on Actual / Ideal ([Live Jun 18](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-18-live-with-sarah.md) [0:05:15–0:10:29]; angle A071).
5. Base angles for each self: A007 (Ideal), A008 (Actual), A009 (Ought).

### Recipe 2: change the zone (valence × intensity)
Procedure: [Tone-shifting](../frameworks/tone-shifting.md) (Valence–Intensity L5 and the four zone prompts).
1. Tag the winner's zone ([emotional zones](../concepts/emotional-zones.md)).
2. Pull the three levers, cheapest first: **verbs** (soft → sharp: "bought for a gift" → "obsessed… hoarded"), **visuals** (saturation, crop, light), **emotional promise** (indulgence → stimulation → relief → regret).
3. Run all four zone prompts on the same ad to get a Z1–Z4 set. Copper Cow's set: Z1 "Finally, a coffee that slows you down" (A003), Z2 "Straight-up dopamine in a mug" (A004), Z3 "Why are we still drinking stale beans?" (A005), Z4 "The coffee you should have gifted—before it sold out" (A006) ([Valence-Intensity L5](../courses/tether-lab/03-valence-intensity-model/05-fixing-the-tone-without-rebuilding-the-ad.md) [08:30]).
4. Test all four zones at once so the account doesn't stagnate in one ([Live Jun 11](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-11-live-with-sarah.md) [0:07:22–0:09:27]; [emotional stagnation](../concepts/emotional-stagnation.md)). Zone 1-only brands miss the Zone 3 buyer: "I've tried every supplement and all of them upset my stomach" (H024).
5. Going negative (Z3/Z4)? Blame the industry or the situation, never the viewer's self, and end on hope (A041, A020).

### Recipe 3: change the heuristic, keep the message
1. Keep the core message and the visual. Swap only the psychological mechanism, one per variant ([heuristics hub](../concepts/heuristics.md), [heuristic stacking](../concepts/heuristic-stacking.md)).
2. Worked example: Aazar's golden-milk control became five one-heuristic headlines: tribe + movement (H089), scarcity as demand evidence (H126, won), specific authority (H104), fluency + price-per-cup (H181), binary choice (H212) ([Live Jul 9](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-09-live-with-sarah.md) [0:25:12–0:26:20]).
3. Pick heuristics from the customer's belief system and the season, not a generic list ([Live Jul 2](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-02-live-with-sarah.md) [0:13:34–0:15:40]).

### Recipe 4: split the desire into its types
A winner tells you the emotion. The next test is *which type* of it. OmieBox's "The Coolest Lunch in the Room" (A055) split into peer popularity (H123), status popularity (H121) and inner confidence (H122) ([Roast My Ads #3](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md) [0:43:07–0:46:24]; [emotion specificity](../concepts/emotion-specificity.md)). Same move for confidence: "Confidence that attracts" → "Boardroom-level confidence" (A045).

### Recipe 5: vary the situation (universal vs lived)
Keep the core message and swap the moment. Canvas Gelato's "You already burned dinner…" winner (A132) branched into universal moments ("You skipped breakfast, so it's gelato time", H201) and lived ones ("You spent three hours on that dinner and he hated it…", H035) to learn whether "funny" or "lived" drives it ([Live Jun 18](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-18-live-with-sarah.md) [0:40:12–0:41:19]).

### Recipe 6: find the belief under the behaviour
When the trigger is negative but the behaviour is optimistic (greying hair → "you can slow it down"), test both a Zone 3 angle and a hopeful Zone 1–2 angle (A094; [Live Jun 11](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-11-live-with-sarah.md) [0:30:48–0:31:50]). For switchers, question the attachment instead of listing benefits (A096).

### Recipe 7: transfer a structure to a new category
Keep the sentence structure and the psychological move, and swap the category truth: "You're Lactose Intolerant. Start Acting Like It." (H141) → "You're on a diet. Start acting like it." (H143) ([Roast My Ads #3](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md) [1:00:00]). Same for the VITUS loss line moved to events (H138 → H139).

### Recipe 8: blend two winners
If two emotions each win on their own, merge them as headline + subhead rather than running a two-emotion ad: "The last skincare switch you'll ever make" (achievement) + "No more guessing. That's our promise." (security) ran 8 months (H076; [Live Jun 4](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-04-live-with-sarah.md) [0:47:21–0:49:22]; [blended emotions](../concepts/blended-emotions.md)). Gavin's nostalgia + freedom blend is the prompt version (A130).

### Recipe 9: multiply the story, not the message
- **New characters** around the customer (vet, trainer, neighbour, pet sitter, the pet), each a new arc (A137; [Live May 14](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-05-14-live-with-sarah.md) [0:33:17–0:34:00]).
- **New pain narratives** for each segment of one product (thinning, loss, itchy scalp), with one founder story each (A138).
- **New setting or image** with the same copy, to fight fatigue on a working message (A139, A140; [Guest 02](../courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-06-guest-session-02-chase-mohseni.md) [0:53:38]).
- **New story framework** for the same concept (Pixar UGC, Hero's Journey influencer, In Medias Res podcast) ([story frameworks for ads](../frameworks/story-frameworks-for-ads.md)).

### Quick decision table
| If the winner… | Spin next | Recipe |
|---|---|---|
| speaks to one self (usually Ideal) | the two missing selves, same offer and visual | 1 |
| sits in Zone 1 and CTR is flat, or the whole account is one zone | the Z2 / Z3 / Z4 versions | 2 |
| has a strong headline but you don't know why it works | one-heuristic variants of the same message | 3 |
| names a broad emotion (popularity, confidence) | 2–3 specific types of that emotion | 4 |
| is a situational line ("you already burned dinner") | universal vs lived situations | 5 |
| is a competitor's line in another category | the same structure with our category truth | 7 |
| is one of two separate winners | a headline + subhead blend | 8 |
| is fatiguing but the message still converts | new character / image / framework | 9 |

## Coverage & gaps
- **Ought is thin and mostly warm-audience:** 17 angles touch Ought (vs 85 Actual, 39 Ideal). Most are pet guilt, Milky's confrontation, accountability or prompt-defined. There are few Ought angles for apparel, food or beauty.
- **Zone 4 is rare (17 angles)**, and most are prompt-library definitions or Sarah's spoken insurance example rather than tested ads.
- **Prompt-library angles (Prompt #1–#15) are defined but untested.** Many have no example line, or a wiki-written illustrative line (15 rows have "line: derived"). Use them as generators, not proof. 7 rows are ChatGPT prompt-output (live story builds and the Maelove scripts).
- **TEEP stage isn't tagged here.** Few angle rows state a stage. See [TEEP journey](../frameworks/teep-journey.md) and the TEEP column in [hooks.md](hooks.md) when placing an angle.
- **Results are scarce.** Only a handful of angles carry a reported result (burned-dinner gelato CPA, golden milk "keeps selling out" won its test, the Maelove last-switch static ran 8 months, "1 click away" won at the purchase stage).
- **Niches are uneven.** Supplements, haircare, gelato, pets and services dominate. There's almost nothing for tech, home goods beyond flasks and cookware, or men's grooming.
