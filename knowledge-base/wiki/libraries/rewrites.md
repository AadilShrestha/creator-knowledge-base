# Rewrites library (before → after)
> Every unique before → after rewrite from the Tether Lab course (167 rows, deduplicated from 320 harvested rows), grouped by the one lever that changed. Use it to fix a weak line, or to make a controlled variant of a winner.

## 🤖 Agent use
- **Use when:** a competitor or own line, CTA, offer or visual underperforms or misfires and you need a minimal fix; when you're building iteration variants of a winning ad (change one lever, keep the rest); when you're writing a UGC script and want the sharper version of each line.
- **Inputs → outputs:** a weak line or element + the diagnosis (wrong zone, wrong self, vague, too loud or soft, browse-y CTA, cluttered) → find the matching lever section → apply the pattern to produce 2–3 rewritten variants, each labelled with lever + direction.
- **Rules:**
  - DO change **one lever per variant** so the test tells you what worked (same message, new visual or headline; Chase: "one primitive").
  - DO swap browse verbs for owning verbs: "Shop" / "Add to cart" / "Browse" → "Get it" / "Order yours now" / "Find your favorites". In Meta ads, put the real CTA in the copy.
  - DO replace faceless numbers and nouns with typed people: "+350,000 sold" → "350,000 tea drinkers love us"; "women" → "tired / frazzled / stressed-out women".
  - DO use the customer's own word, not the brand's ("nurse", not "feed"; "pots and pans", not "cookware").
  - DO sell the use moment, not the USP: "Get better sleep" → "Wake up better"; "Three months of weight loss" → "Buy a 36 pack, make 100 dinners".
  - DO express value in use units and dollars: "100 ml" → "3 sprays per day = 1 year"; "Save 10%" → "Save $X"; "Free trial" → "$0 trial".
  - DO make scarcity true and specific: "Sale ends in 10 days" → "101 special pieces… never again"; "20 left" → "53 left"; "limited spaces" → "opens once every six months".
  - DO move a winner to another zone or self with the prompts (tone-shifting, rewriting for new selves) and keep the offer unchanged.
  - DO soften shame by displacing it (the dog shames, not the brand) or adding empathy ("I know you don't want to…, but you are, honey").
  - DON'T stack two emotions or two competing claims in one static. Cut to one headline + one CTA, and lead with the message.
  - DON'T attack the category or the viewer ("matcha sucks"). Question the trend instead ("the girlies aren't doing matcha anymore").
- **See also:** [Tone-shifting](../frameworks/tone-shifting.md) · [Rewriting ads for new selves](../frameworks/rewriting-ads-for-new-selves.md) · [Offer and bundle psychology](../frameworks/offer-and-bundle-psychology.md) · [Active CTA language](../concepts/active-cta-language.md) · [Hooks library](hooks.md) · [Angles library](angles.md) · [Agent guide](../../AGENT-GUIDE.md)

## How to use
- **Columns:** *Before* = the original line or element; *After* = the course's rewrite (alternates separated by " / "); *Lever changed* = the one main lever (secondary changes are named in *Direction*); *Direction* = the move made; *Origin* = where the after-version came from; *Source* = the wiki page + `[timestamp]` (first link = where it was taught; "also:" = other wiki pages that cite the same example).
- **`*` = inferred tag:** the source didn't state it; the library added it. Untagged directions paraphrase the source's own "what changed" note.
- **Origin:** **course-real** = Sarah, a guest or a member said or showed it (live sessions, lessons, e-books, real ads). **prompt-output** = generated by a course prompt (e.g. the four zone prompts, the 3-selves rewrite prompt). **derived** = wording written by the wiki as an illustration.
- Rows are numbered R001… in page order. Same before with different afters = separate rows when they were taught as separate moves.
