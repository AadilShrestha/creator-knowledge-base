# 📕 Community Guidelines - Start Here · Tether Lab

_Source: `1. Start Here\4. 📕_Community_Guidelines_-_Start_Here_·_Tether_Lab.html`_

Our intention is to create a **safe and positive** group and community of like\-minded individuals who want to learn together, support each other, and create meaningful connections.

## **Group member rules:**

🚫 Spam

🚫 Self\-promotion

🚫 Job Promotion

🚫 Advertisement

🚫 Affiliate links

🚫 Links to PDF documents

🚫 Mention of your services

🚫 Mention of other similar Marketing services or Agencies

🚫 Hate/Disrespect Speech Tone



---

🔍 Search category before you post.

💬 Have meaningful conversations.

📝 Put effort into writing valuable posts.



---

### **If you don't follow these rules:**

❌ Your posts/comments will be deleted.

⛔️ Your account will be banned permanently (No second chances)

⚠️ Please "Report to Admin" any suspected spamming or suspicious behavior on this group. Your help in identifying these spam users quickly is appreciated. Thank you. 🙏

P.S. We reserve the right to remove any post that does not align with our theme. We aim to curate posts to create a quality group that members like to be part of and contribute to.
