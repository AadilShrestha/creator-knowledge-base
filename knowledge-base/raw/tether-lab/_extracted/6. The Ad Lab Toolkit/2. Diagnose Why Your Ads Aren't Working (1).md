## Page 3

When an ad has all the surface-level stuff (offer, copy,
visuals) but still flops, it’s almost always a tone problem.
Here’s what’s actually happening:
You wrote a solid message… but it doesn’t feel right to
the customer.
It speaks to logic, not emotion.
It skips the vibe test.
Your audience is emotionally elsewhere. And your ad isn’t
meeting them there.
You don’t need a new hook. You need to align the emotional
temperature of your ad with what your customer is feeling.

## Page 4

This entire framework hinges on two levers:
Valence = What emotion are we trying to evoke? (Good
vs. bad)
Intensity = How strongly are we hitting it? (Whisper vs.
scream)
Valence Examples:
Positive = joy, pride, comfort, hope
Negative = fear, FOMO, frustration, anxiety

VALENCE & INTENSITY

## Page 5

Intensity Examples:
Low = soft, reflective, reassuring
High = urgent, bold, aggressive
You map every ad into one of four emotional zones based

## Page 7

Zone 2: High Valence + High Intensity
The hype zone. Pride, thrill, anticipation. Great for:
Product launches
Performance gear
Celebratory moments
Think: bold type, energy visuals, verbs that punch.
Zone 3: Low Valence + Low Intensity
The pain-point whisper. Frustration, fatigue, unmet
expectations.
Good for soft problem awareness
Best in industries with thoughtful buyers
Think: sighs, subtle sarcasm, the tension before the
solution.
Zone 4: Low Valence + High Intensity
The danger zone. Fear, scarcity, urgency.
Great for high-stakes industries (safety, financial risk)
Use with care. This one can backfire.
Think: harsh lighting, high contrast, jarring visuals.

## Page 9

You don’t need to scrap your ad. You need to tune it.
Step 1: Shift Your Verbs
Headlines are tone-setters. Words like "find" vs. "seize"
trigger very different intensities.
Step 2: Shift Your Visuals
Zoom in = more intensity. Bold colors = more urgency.
Morning light = calm. Desaturated tones = trust.
Step 3: Shift the Emotional Promise
Ask: What emotional payoff are we selling? Indulgence?
Safety? Control? Change that, and the whole ad pivots.

## Page 10

Want to rewrite your ad into a new emotional zone? Use one
of these prompt starters:
Zone 1: “Rewrite as a gentle promise. Something
soothing. A sigh of relief.”
Zone 2: “Rewrite as a breakthrough. The reader is
unstoppable. Make them feel electric.”
Zone 3: “Rewrite as an ache. Quiet disappointment. A
truth they’ve avoided.”
Zone 4: “Rewrite as a snap warning. They’re about to
mess up. Make it matter.”
Match your visuals too. Calm lighting vs. jarring contrast
matters more than you think.
