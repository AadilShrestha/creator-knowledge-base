First Principles Thinking strips away assumptions and rebuilds logic from
the ground up. It’s about questioning “Why do we do this?” and
reconstructing a better, truer answer.
Loss Aversion triggers when people fear they’re wasting time, losing
money, or falling behind. It’s more powerful than gain-seeking—and drives
immediate decision-making.
Used together, this combo helps audiences realize they’ve been operating on
faulty logic—and that it’s costing them something valuable right now.

feel bold, clarifying, and slightly uncomfortable—like the truth just clicked, and
action is now urgent.
Each concept should include a compelling idea or narrative that can be applied
across multiple ad types (video, carousel, static, long-form copy, etc.).

Status Signaling is the act of broadcasting identity through behavior,
language, aesthetics, or association. It’s how we subtly (or not so subtly)
communicate where we belong in the social/aspirational hierarchy.
Open Loops are incomplete thoughts or stories that provoke the brain’s
need to resolve tension. Think cliffhangers, teaser lines, or insights that are
almost fully revealed.
Together, these two tactics make the viewer think:
“People like me are already doing this… and I need to know what I’m missing.”

These ads should feel like an exclusive peek into a world only a select few
understand… and the viewer is just one click away from joining them.
