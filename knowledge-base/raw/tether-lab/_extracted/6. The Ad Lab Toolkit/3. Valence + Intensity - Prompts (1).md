ALTERATION PROMPTS:

High Valence, Low Intensity
Rewrite this ad as if the reader is about to have their
breakthrough moment — and your product is the
catalyst.
What success, win, or identity shift would make them
instantly light up? Now imagine the image as a
celebration or transformation — what would make
someone feel unstoppable just by seeing it?
Rewrite this ad as if your product is a sigh of relief
they didn’t know they needed.
What gentle promise or soothing outcome would make
them finally feel seen?
Now imagine the image as an emotional exhale — what
would feel so calming it makes them pause and
breathe?

ALTERATION PROMPTS:
Low Valence, High Intensity
Low Valence, Low Intensity
Rewrite this ad as if the reader is about to make a big
mistake — and your product is the only thing that
stops it.
What fear or frustration would make them snap to
attention? Now imagine the image is an interruption in
their feed — what would feel visually wrong enough to
force the scroll to stop?
Rewrite this ad as if your product is quietly calling
out a truth they’ve been avoiding.
 What subtle disappointment, nostalgia, or emptiness
would make them reflect instead of scroll?
 Now imagine the image as a gentle ache — what
visual would whisper, “This used to mean something”?
