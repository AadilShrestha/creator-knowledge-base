This ad is **solution aware.**

**Solution Aware:** People who are aware of the solutions that exists for their problem, they just aren't convinced which one will solve it the best. You can start ads at this solution level without raising their existing beliefs on specific products they may have already seen or researched.

The difference between Solution Aware and Product aware is that the person who is solution aware is essentially still considering all of the different types of products to solve their problem

The people who are product aware know what product they want to solve their problem.
