**EPIC\_B2PMEC1\_V5** \- Winner  \- 

Super low Hook Rate \- 11%

The words “growth factor” resonated better, and “will restart your gains after 40.” seems to be more believable or more of a desire than moving your bench press PR.

I think it worked better because the target avatar doesnt desire to move their bench press weight, they desire the look and to be big, not just put up numbers.

**EPIC\_B2PMEC1\_V2** \- Loser

Super Low Hook Rate \- 12%

Actually show a compound, because we showed people a product they immediately knew they were being sold.

The word “Compound” sounds too much like steroids, doesn't resonate with audience.

I think the disconnect between “this compound” and showing a product immediately broke the belief that this will work from the start.

**Ultimately:**

The body sells, it clearly worked really well, because the winner version which only had a different hook of 3 seconds scaled to 13k spend and was one of the top for the account.   
Meanwhile the loser ad got 400 dollars in spend and the only difference was the first 3 seconds.

Both winner and loser had terrible hook rates of 11-12% meaning, we should of done an iteration on this exact body and only changed the visual hook, and maybe test some new copy hooks to see if we can bump it to 20 or 30% which could increase the performance significantly and could of been one of the top winners in the account.
