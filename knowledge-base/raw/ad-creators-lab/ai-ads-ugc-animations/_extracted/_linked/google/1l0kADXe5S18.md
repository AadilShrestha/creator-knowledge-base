This ad is **solution aware.**

**Solution Aware:** People who are aware of the solutions that exists for their problem, they just aren't convinced which one will solve it the best. You can start ads at this solution level without raising their existing beliefs on specific products they may have already seen or researched.

The difference between Solution Aware and Product aware is that the person who is solution aware is essentially still considering all of the different types of products to solve their problem

The people who are product aware know what product they want to solve their problem.

**Valence and Intensity Zone:**

**Zone 3 → Zone 1** (with a brief dip into Zone 4\)

* **Opens in Zone 3 (Low Valence, Low Intensity):** Annoyance, irritation, resignation. "Bunch up, stink a bit, wake up in the wet spot." The tone is exasperated but not panicked, dry humor, eye-rolling energy. This is the fatigue of someone who's just tired of bad solutions.  
* **Brief Zone 4 moment (Low Valence, High Intensity):** "No holding back your climax because you're scared of the mess" and "sh\*t, we're gonna have to clean this up." Anxiety, inhibition, the cost of the problem bleeding into intimacy itself. This is where stakes get real.  
* **Closes in Zone 1 (High Valence, Low Intensity):** "Pleasure should not give you anxiety or laundry." Relief, calm, permission. Not a hype close, a reassurance close. Soft landing. "You just need a Love Blanket" is a deep exhale, not a cheer.

**Stop pretending your crusty, spicy time towels work.** 

( pattern interrupt "stop pretending"

Stop pretending they work, is now talking to the audience and telling them their solution is not okay, telling them this is an actual problem that needs fixing.

uses visual words to help you see how much of a problem it is

"crusty"

Only thing better here is we could of visually paired this, and shown a crusty spicy time towel ) 

**They bunch up, stink a bit, and you still wake up in the wet spot.** 

( now we twist the knife and tell them exactly why their solution sucks )

**Towels leak. Those puppy pads crinkle like a damn hospital bed. Cheap waterproof bed sheets, they lie. They all leak.** 

( now we twist even further and explain why every solution they may have or will try wont work )

**This Love Blanket actually fixes the problem.** 

( very quickly we intro the product, since we start in a solution aware state, you dont need much intro or persuasion, simply shift their beliefs

Which previously was "my towel does the job"

To " my towel doesn't do the job, it's gross, and I need a better solution "

Which this ad did up to this point, so we can now enter the unique solution )

**The top soft layer absorbs everything.** 

**The inner layer blocks out all leaks.**

**Feels like a soft throw blanket.** 

**No plastic, no noise, no slipping around.** 

**You can literally soak it and your mattress stays bone dry.** 

( here were just benefit stacking, and comparing it to the other common solutions and showing how its a better option \- reversing the risk for the customer buying because were validating their new belief that this is the better alternative )

**Now, you don't have to change the sheets at 3:00 AM when you are tired and wanna stay in bed cuddling after a night of passion.** 

**No more stressing about ruining a \$1,500 mattress.** 

**No holding back your climax because you're scared of the mess, and actually enjoy the moment without thinking, "\[sh\*t\] we're gonna have to clean this up."**   
( now were tying in those benefits to the true experiences and desires this avatar has

We pulled these straight from the research, and its important to note this is the real reason they want the blanket, not the benefits, the benefits allow these in person experiences to happen, and ultimately is what the customer is buying )

**Pleasure should not give you anxiety or laundry. You just need a Love Blanket.**

( reinforcing a new belief " pleasure/intimacy shouldn't give you anxiety or laundry " when before they thought that was just part of it, towels were the best solution and some anxiety and laundry was necessary to get intimate

And now were exiting with that doesnt have to happen if you get a love blanket

Missed opportunity here for more risk reversal ( 30 night guarantee, creating urgency \- grab it now before stock runs out etc, and showing the offer ( 53% off ) )
