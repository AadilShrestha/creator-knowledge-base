This ad is **solution aware.**

**Solution Aware:** People who are aware of the solutions that exists for their problem, they just aren't convinced which one will solve it the best. You can start ads at this solution level without raising their existing beliefs on specific products they may have already seen or researched.

The difference between Solution Aware and Product aware is that the person who is solution aware is essentially still considering all of the different types of products to solve their problem

The people who are product aware know what product they want to solve their problem.

**Valence and Intensity Zone:**

**Valence:** Positive (warm, aspirational, ease-driven — no pain agitation)

**Intensity:** Low to moderate — Zone 1 to Zone 2

**My Italian friend's mom drinks wine every single night, and yet she wakes up at 6 a.m. and makes fresh focaccia.** 

( "my italian friends mom"

Starts off with authority ( this audience sees italians as an authority, because in their culture they can drink often and have little to no side effects, they can eat a lot and stay healthy and even lose weight )

calls out the target audiences desire ( how to drink every night without the side effects \- waking up with headache, feeling like garbage the next day ) 

uses juxtaposition to create an open loop / curiosity gap

"she wakes up at 6am and makes fresh focaccia" doesnt directly tell the audience that she felt perfectly fine the next morning, it allows them to infer that she mustve felt fine if she was up early and being productive

This had to happen because of compliance, we couldnt say she could wakeup without a headache

And it actually helped because it made the ( waking up without a headache ) very visual \- you can actually see someone in your head making bread, but its very hard to visualize someone not having a headache )

**When I asked how she does it, she said it's because her mom drinks dry wine, not sweet.** 

( "When i asked how she does it" \- creates a story, feels like a friend telling you something, very relatable wording not advertisey

introduces the new mechanism here

"her mom drinks dry wine, not sweet"

creates curiosity \- whats dry wine? 

And links it to the desire: being able to drink every night and wake up feeling good

What types of wine are dry? How do I get my hands on dry wine to achieve desire? )

**I found out about Mediterranean-style winemaking, where they ferment the wine all the way through, so there's zero sugar left.** 

( "Mediterranean-style winemaking" add to authority ( Mediterranean \- italians ) and the unique mechanism

Now the viewer is hooked and were educating them on the unique mechanism, which they want to be educated because it ties to their desire

now we connect the new mechanism to something they can easily associate 

Dry wine, mediterranean-style winemaking \= zero sugar

Which means zero sugar must be what I need in my wine to not wakeup with painpoint ( headache, not feeling good ) )

**That's actually how I ended up hearing about Gratsi wine from my friend.** 

( the line before and this one shows the research process of this level of market sophistication for this buyer

friend recommendation, learned about style of wine making, back to hearing from friend \--\> now intro brand )

**Zero sugar, nothing unnecessary.** 

**It comes in a box that holds the same as four bottles, and it stays fresh for 30 days.** 

( now intro product

This product is great, has amazing unique mechanisms so it sells itself

this part were connecting the belief that zero sugar means theyll get desire and showing how our product has this, which then means they will get desire

Remember everything leads back to them the customer, never veer off )

**So I can have a glass of wine on Wednesday and then another on Saturday and it's still perfect.**  
 

( so that principle here

stays fresh 30 days \-- so that i can have a glass of wine on wednesday and another saturday and its still perfect )

**There's never any pressure to finish it. No wine going bad in my fridge. It's like \$10 a bottle. And honestly, it just fits into real life.** 

( using customer language from our research for this specific avatar

Theyre not heavy drinkers, they just want to have it when they want it without having to drink half the bottle and now its waste, or feeling the pressure to chug the bottle so it doesnt go bad and they dont lose money )

**It's simply having a glass with dinner on a Tuesday without overthinking it or needing a special occasion.**

( reinforcing the idea that this wine can be enjoyed over time 

selling the emotional feeling here ( without overthinking )

note how we didnt sell wine here, we sold their desire, the emotional feeling, and the story )
