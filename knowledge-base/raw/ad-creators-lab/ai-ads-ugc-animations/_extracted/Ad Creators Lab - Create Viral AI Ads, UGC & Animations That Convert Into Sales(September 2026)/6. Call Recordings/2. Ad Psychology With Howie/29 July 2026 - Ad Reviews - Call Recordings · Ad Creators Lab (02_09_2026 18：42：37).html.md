## Meeting Purpose

Review e\-commerce ads and discuss creative strategy.## Key Takeaways

* **Hooks must be visually distinct** to avoid Meta's AI grouping them as duplicates; use split screens, green screens, or different settings.

* **Use B\-roll to visualize the script** and maintain viewer engagement; static talking\-head ads are ineffective.

* **Frame ads around indirect desires** (e.g., an armchair not digging into your belly) to create a powerful emotional gap between the viewer's current state and an ideal self.

* **Use open loops to filter for your ICP,** as only your target audience will understand the subtle references, improving ad account optimization.

## Topics

### Ad Review: Kegel Training Program

* **Problem:** The ad is a static talking\-head video, which causes high viewer drop\-off.

* **Solution:** Add visual stimulation to bring the script to life.
	+ **B\-roll:** Overlay visuals (medical animations, stop\-motion) that illustrate the script.
	
	+ **Split Screens:** Show the speaker on one side and B\-roll on the other.
	
	+ **PNGs/Animations:** Add small, contextual graphics.* **Problem:** Multiple ad hooks are visually identical, causing Meta's AI to group them as duplicates and under\-allocate budget.

* **Solution:** Create visual contrast for each hook to ensure they are treated as unique ads.
	+ **Visual Changes:** Vary the speaker's position, clothing, or setting.
	
	+ **Editing Techniques:** Use green screens or split screens with different B\-roll.* **Creative Idea:** Test an "ugly" AI avatar. Its unexpected appearance can increase hook rates by breaking the pattern of attractive people in feeds.

### Creative Strategy: Desire\-Based Advertising

* **Problem:** Most ads focus on obvious pain points, which is a saturated and less effective approach.

* **Solution:** Focus on indirect desires to create a stronger emotional connection.
	+ **Mechanism:** Frame the ad around an ideal self. The viewer's subconscious comparison to their current state creates an emotional gap that the product fills.
	
	+ **Example (Weight Loss):** Instead of "lose weight," use an indirect desire like "the armchair not digging into your belly."* **Execution:**
	+ **Hook:** Visually show the pain of the indirect desire (e.g., armchair digging in).
	
	+ **Body:** Use storytelling to bridge the gap from the viewer's current state to the ideal outcome.

### Ad Review: RV Mattress

* **Problem:** The hook text is confusing ("throw a 2\-inch slab of foam outside of the RV").

* **Solution:** Simplify the hook text for clarity (e.g., "throw the mattress away").

* **Problem:** The AI voiceover sounds robotic.

* **Solution:** Improve realism by cloning a voice from a high\-quality podcast and disabling Eleven Labs' "cut out background noise" feature to retain natural ambiance.

* **Problem:** The ad lacks a strong CTA.

* **Solution:** Add urgency (e.g., "sale ends tonight") and social proof (e.g., testimonials).

* **Missed Opportunity:** The ad doesn't highlight the product's unique mechanism (custom sizing, motion isolation), which is its key differentiator.

### Ad Review: Family Mattress

* **Strength:** Excellent visuals that effectively illustrate the script.

* **Hook Idea:** Simplify the hook's visual.
	+ **Execution:** Use a clean background with a pie chart graphic to display the "43% of working moms" statistic. This borrows authority from the statistic itself.* **Script Improvement:** Add an open loop to the "nightmare phase" explanation.
	+ **Execution:** After explaining the phase, add a cliffhanger or question to maintain curiosity.

### Ad Review: Beef Tallow Skincare

* **Strength:** Strong storytelling that builds emotional tension by teasing the product throughout the ad.

* **Improvement:** Shorten the final product reveal clip to maintain momentum.

### Agency Operations \& Client Selection

* **Lesson Learned:** Vetting clients is critical. A previous client's media buyer mixed AI ads with poor\-performing UGC, causing the account to underperform and leading to the client's departure.

* **Insight:** An ad getting spend is a winner, even if it's not immediately at KPI. It may be a top\-of\-funnel ad driving purchases later in the funnel.

* **Strategy:** Scale small brands. It is more profitable than taking on large brands with established scale.

* **Market Caution:** The skincare/makeup niche is highly competitive, requiring significant expertise to succeed against major brands.
