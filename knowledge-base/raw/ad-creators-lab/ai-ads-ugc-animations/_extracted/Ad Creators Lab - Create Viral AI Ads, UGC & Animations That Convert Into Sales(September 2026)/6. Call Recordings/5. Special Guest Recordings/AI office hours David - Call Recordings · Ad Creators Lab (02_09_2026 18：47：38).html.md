### **Community Call \- AI Video Development and Strategy Call**

### 1\. Developing Skill Files for Visual Styles

Developing "skill files" for Claude involves a systematic process of reverse\-engineering a specific visual style (e.g., Claymation or cinematic styles) to codify it into text prompts.The Workflow:* Identify Fundamentals: Break the style down into categories like lip\-syncing, voiceover with B\-roll, or a mix of both.

* Reference \& Extraction: Start with a reference video, extract specific frames, and provide them to a Claude agent.

* Iterative Testing: Use image generation tools (specifically Banana 2\) to test prompts.

* Refinement: Provide Claude with positive and negative feedback on results. For example, the speaker found that adding specific instructions about lighting was the "unlock" for a polished claymation look.

* Goal: Create a consistent text\-based definition of a style that can be applied to any new video.

### 2\. The 'Couple Dynamic' in Ad Copywriting

The "couple dynamic" is a proven format, particularly effective on platforms like TikTok Shop, because it allows for native\-looking storylines and creative transitions.Key Insights:* Storytelling: It shifts the narrative from a single person having a problem to a dynamic where one partner observes or solves a problem for the other (e.g., a girlfriend buying a product for her boyfriend).

* Engagement \& Humor: Using a couple allows for humor and "open loops" in the script, which buys goodwill from the audience.

* Shareability: Ads featuring couples are often more shareable, as viewers may send them to their own partners.

* Intentionality: The speaker noted that intentional character design (e.g., an attractive "gym couple") significantly improves the performance of the hook.

### 3\. Workflow for Cloning Competitor Ads

The strategy for cloning an ad depends on whether the product is in the same category as the competitor.For Same\-Category Products:* Pure Duplication: Break down the scenes and framing of the original ad.

* Execution: Replicate the visuals but swap the competitor's product for your own in the start frames and script references.

For Different Product Categories:* Identify Foundations: Determine what makes the original ad successful (e.g., the visual style, the humor, or the couple dynamic).

* Adaptation: Keep those successful foundations but apply them to a new product. For example, the same structure used for a creatine ad could be used to sell a lawnmower by maintaining the couple dynamic and humor.

* Layered Analysis: Break the competitor ad into three independent layers for analysis: Visual, Audio, and Script. Do not try to analyze everything at once.

### 4\. Effective Lip\-Syncing for Animated Ads

To handle lip\-syncing effectively and avoid common prompting issues, the speaker recommends a specific sequence:1. Full Voiceover: Generate the entire voiceover at once to ensure consistent tone and pacing.

- Clean \& Slice: Remove dead air and slice the audio into smaller segments.

- Syncing: Pass the audio slices and start frames into a model like SeaDance 2 Mini (for high quality) or P Video (for a cheaper option). This ensures the pacing of each clip matches the audio perfectly.
