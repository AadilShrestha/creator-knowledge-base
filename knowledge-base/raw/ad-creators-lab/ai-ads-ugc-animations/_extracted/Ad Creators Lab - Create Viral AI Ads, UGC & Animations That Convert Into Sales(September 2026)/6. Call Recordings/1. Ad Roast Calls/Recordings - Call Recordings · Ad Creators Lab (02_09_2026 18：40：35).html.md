[19th June](https://fathom.video/share/x6WZ4V7kLykUmzxCmtkMNuWv2yzGqw5s "https://fathom.video/share/x6WZ4V7kLykUmzxCmtkMNuWv2yzGqw5s") **Meeting Purpose**Review community\-submitted AI ads and provide constructive feedback.## **Key Takeaways**

* **The "Bridge" is Critical:** Ads often build strong tension but fail to connect it logically to the product. A "bridge" is needed to explain the solution's unique mechanism, making the product a natural, non\-abrupt conclusion.

* **Hook for Unaware Audiences:** For audiences unaware of the problem, a curiosity\-driven hook works better than a problem\-focused one. VSL ads, for example, should start with an open loop to pull viewers in.

* **Execution Drives Emotion:** High\-quality execution—visuals, sound design, and pacing—is essential for creating an emotional connection. Kristian's Shilajit ad, which used a Five Gum\-style voiceover to evoke a feeling, was a prime example.

* **Cost\-Effective AI Tools:** For budget\-conscious users, FAL and [Kia.ai](http://kia.ai/ "http://Kia.ai") are the cheapest options, though their UIs are less refined than premium tools like Higgsfield and Arcad.

[11th June](https://fathom.video/share/bpo8ChyvwjsKzeU325BLL5UsNBgL2j8J "https://fathom.video/share/bpo8ChyvwjsKzeU325BLL5UsNBgL2j8J")* **Prioritize "So That" Benefits:** Connect features to tangible outcomes (e.g., "7 floating heads, *so that* you get a smooth shave in under 2 minutes") to make ads relatable and avoid feature\-dumping.

* **Focus on One Core Argument:** For simple products, build the ad around one strong, educational point (e.g., full\-grain leather vs. genuine leather) to avoid overwhelming viewers.

* **Master Editing Pacing:** Use speed ramps and tight cuts to match the audio's rhythm, especially for pop songs, and eliminate dead air to maintain viewer engagement.

* **Build Credibility with "Show, Don't Tell":** Use B\-roll to demonstrate product use and benefits (e.g., taking supplements with breakfast) rather than just showing the product bottle.

[4th June](https://fathom.video/share/NSCZE3BUJUkQMyhsh8Uqv4WLgpgj_XTC "https://fathom.video/share/NSCZE3BUJUkQMyhsh8Uqv4WLgpgj_XTC")* **Ad testing:** Don’t over\-optimize too early; test lots of different concepts first.

* **Learnings:** If a change doesn’t move metrics, it means that hypothesis wasn’t the issue—try another one.

* **Budget strategy:** At low spend, run many different creatives in one ad set and let it sit; start deeper learnings later.

* **Meta structure:** Camilo said he stopped tinkering too much and now lets ad sets run so Meta can optimize.

* **Creative strategy:** Test different **angles, avatars, and formats** to find what resonates.

* **Mohammed’s question:** Camilo recommended starting with **crochet / claymation / AI UGC**, building a portfolio, and using research \+ proven scripts.

* **Scripting:** For animated ads, build visuals **line by line** from the script so audio and scenes match.

* **Supplement ads:** Focus on the problem, failed solutions, and reveal the product late; add tension before the payoff.

* **Hunter’s ad:** Camilo praised the ad but suggested making the solution transition more natural and the hook clearer faster.

* **Alfie’s question:** Match format to audience; some styles fit older demographics better than others.

* **Workflow/tools:** They discussed voiceover tools, editor SOPs, and balancing quality with higher volume.

[30th April](https://fathom.video/share/R4xHFhYCWb8U2zzYdxzeX67BJmFGo_3t "https://fathom.video/share/R4xHFhYCWb8U2zzYdxzeX67BJmFGo_3t")* **Creative Diversity Matters**:   
I showcased a wine brand scaling to $250k spend by using varied ad concepts (UGC, AI, educational angles, statics) targeting different audience segments rather than relying on one creative.

* **Punch/Proof Testing Framework**:   
Aga learned the testing methodology run 10 ads in "Punch" phase for 7 days, move 3 winners to "Proof" with audience restrictions (interest stacks), then scale winners without forcing budget allocation.

* **High\-Intent Ads Come from Data**:   
Richard discovered that effective ads use actual customer language/pain points from Reddit, comments, and reviews rather than made\-up hooks—dig deeper into *why* customers say things, not just what they say.

* **Creative Production Scales with Spend**:   
At $2k/day spend, you need 5\-10 new ad concepts weekly; don't obsess over video length (15 sec to 2\+ min all work); AI content performs equally to real content if strategy is solid.

* **Focus on One Brand First**:   
Suki emphasized getting one brand to $10k\+ days before splitting focus—it's harder to scale from $0\-10k than $20k\-50k, so build depth before breadth.
