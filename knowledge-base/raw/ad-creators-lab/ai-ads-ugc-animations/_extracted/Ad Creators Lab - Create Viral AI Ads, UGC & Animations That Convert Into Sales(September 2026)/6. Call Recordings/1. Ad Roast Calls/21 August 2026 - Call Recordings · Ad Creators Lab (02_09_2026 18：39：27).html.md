**Key Takeaways*** **Show, Don't Tell:** Use visuals to reinforce script points and build high purchase intent. For example, show a character walking up stairs to prove joint relief, rather than just stating it.

* **Align Ad \& Landing Page:** The ad's promise must be fulfilled immediately on the landing page to prevent high CTRs from leading to low conversion rates.

* **Master the Script:** A natural, conversational script is the foundation for realistic AI\-generated video. Amber's 6\-minute ad proved that editing a detailed script is more effective than starting from an AI draft.

* **Use Safe Zones:** Always use a safe zone template to ensure critical text and visuals are not covered by platform UI elements (e.g., "Learn More" buttons) in 9:16 ads.
