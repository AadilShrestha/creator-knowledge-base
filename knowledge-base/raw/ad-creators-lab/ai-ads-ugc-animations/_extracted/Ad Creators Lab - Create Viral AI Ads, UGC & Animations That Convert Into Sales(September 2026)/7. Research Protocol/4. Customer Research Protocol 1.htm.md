---

### 1\. Set Up Your RAW Doc

* Create a Google Doc (use `docs.new`)

* Title it **“Raw Data – \[Product Name]”**

* Paste everything raw, no formatting



---

### 2\. Gather Insights from Real People (RAW)

* Go to [**Reddit Answers**](https://www.reddit.com/answers/ "https://www.reddit.com/answers/") 

* Search your product category (e.g. “collagen powder”)

* Click threads, expand comments, and copy/paste full conversations into the doc

* Use follow\-up questions in GigaBrain to dive deeper (e.g. “What do people dislike about collagen?”)

* Copy these conversations (RAW) into your doc (don't worry about formatting)



---

### 3\. Collect Reviews \& Comments

* Copy **10\-30 product reviews** from your website or emails

* Copy **10\-30 competitor reviews** (Amazon, review sites, etc.)

* Grab **TikTok comments** on your videos and competitor videos—especially questions, concerns, and objections



---

### 4\. Create Your Research Document

* Download your raw document as a txt. doc

* Insert it into ChatGPT\-5, Claude (or any other LLM)

* Paste the prompt down below**\***

* Copy and paste the finished result into a Google Doc

* Name it "Research Document \- \[PRODUCT NAME]

  
**\*Research LLM Prompt:**You are an expert audience researcher and copy strategist.  
You are given audience data that includes the following fields:* pain\_points

* failed\_solutions

* desired\_outcomes

* objections

* misconceptions

* golden\_nuggets

* language\_notes

Objective:  
Analyze and synthesize this data into a clean, structured format using the following exact sections:  
Categorized Insights  
 Top Pain Points  
Format (in tabular form):* Pain Point → Short, clear title capturing the core frustration.

* Description → 1–2 lines summarizing the emotional reality behind it.

* Group similar issues where appropriate (e.g., brake issues \+ overheating \= Recurring Mechanical Failures).

 Only include pain points relevant to the brand’s ICP, product, and service. Ignore off\-topic or irrelevant entries.  
 Failed Solutions  
Format (in tabular form):* Attempt → What users tried.

* Why It Failed → Why it didn’t solve the problem.

* Group similar failed attempts if needed (e.g., dealership repairs, DIY fixes).

 Only include attempts tied to the brand’s core offer or target problem.  
 Desired Outcomes  
Format (in tabular form):* Outcome → Desired state the user wants.

* Explanation → Why that matters to them emotionally or practically.

 Objections  
Format (in tabular form):* Objection → User hesitation captured in simple, natural phrasing.

* Real Quote/Paraphrase → Write it like a Redditor would speak, using natural skepticism and casual tone.

 Misconceptions  
Format (in tabular form):* Misconception → Wrong belief the audience holds.

* Clarification → Correct understanding written simply.

 Golden Nuggets (Quotes from Reddit)  
Format:* Take the golden\_nuggets entries.

* If the golden nugget already sounds like a natural Reddit comment, keep it verbatim.

* If the quote sounds too formal or clunky, rewrite it using the style in language\_notes (DIY tone, slang, contractions, emotional phrasing).

* Maintain honesty — no invented quotes.

* Focus on quotes expressing:
	+ Frustration
	
	+ Skepticism
	
	+ Humor/sarcasm
	
	+ Hopelessness
	
	+ DIY struggle

 ONLY include nuggets directly tied to the brand’s ICP, product, or service. Ignore irrelevant ones.  
Strategy Implications for \[Brand Name]  
Format:  
Opportunity \| How \[Brand] Can Win  
\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\|\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-  
Summarize practical, marketing\-relevant opportunities from the audience’s real experiences.  
 ICP Language Analysis  
Based on language\_notes, summarize:* Natural tone (formal vs informal)

* Emotional style (skeptical, stressed, proud, fed up, hopeful)

* Vocabulary (specific mechanical terms, warranty phrases, slang words)

* Copywriting Tips: How to speak exactly like the ICP.

Use real examples where possible ("pads and discs", "lot\-pack", "safe to drive", "I don't get it").  
 Key Personas  
Format:* Persona Name ("Nickname")

* Age Range

* Quick Summary: 1–2 lines of their situation and emotional needs.

* Desire:

* Pain point:

* Objection:

* Lifestyle:

* How they perceive risk:

* What activities they do:

* Their opinions:

* Their interests:

* Their values:

* How they speak:

 Important Rules:* Stay 100% true to the CSV data.

* Use language from the raw threads and reviews to guide rewrites whenever golden nuggets need to sound more natural.

* Ignore and exclude any data irrelevant to the brand’s target audience, offer, or service.

* Group and synthesize carefully without inventing new ideas.

* Keep the final output clean, highly readable, and marketing\-useful.
