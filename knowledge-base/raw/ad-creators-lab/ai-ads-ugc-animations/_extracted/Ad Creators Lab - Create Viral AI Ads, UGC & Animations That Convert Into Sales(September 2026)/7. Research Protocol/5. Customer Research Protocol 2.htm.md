## ✅ Action Item: Add Brand Info \& Generate Personas

### 1\. Enrich Your Research Doc with Brand Details

* Open your existing Research doc.

* Add a new section titled **“Brand Information.”**

* Fill in:
	+ **Brand Name**
	
	+ **Unique Differentiator** (what makes you stand out)
	
	+ **Best‑Selling Product/Service**
	
	+ **Desired Brand Perception** (tone, values)
	
	+ **Seasonal Patterns** (busy periods, offers)
	
	+ **FAQs \& Common Claims*** Download your research doc as a **PDF**

### 2\. Generate Buyer Personas (Avatars)

* [Open the **“Avatar GPT”**](https://chatgpt.com/g/g-68357927192881919ce556d9ff3cfde3-buyer-avatar-gpt "https://chatgpt.com/g/g-68357927192881919ce556d9ff3cfde3-buyer-avatar-gpt") 

* Upload the **PDF** you just downloaded.

* Press **Enter** to run it.

* Wait for it to output **three personas** (e.g., Susan, the 32‑year‑old yoga instructor).

### 3\. Review \& Refine Personas

* Read each persona carefully:

* Tweak any details that don’t match your real customer data.

* If needed, ask for feedback from the team/community.

### 4\. Paste Personas into Your Research Doc

* In your original Research Doc, scroll to **“Key Personas.”**

* Paste in the three finalized avatars.

### 5\. Prepare for Concept Brainstorming

* Verify you have an empty **“Concepts”** section below your personas.

* Save your doc (or export again)
