# ✅ **My Editing Workflow**

1. **Import A\-Roll**
	* Drag your UGC clip into the Project panel’s **New Item** icon to auto\-create a sequence.
	
	* Rename the sequence to match your project.



---

2. **Reframe for Vertical (9×16\)**
	* Go to **Sequence \> Auto Reframe Sequence**.
	
	* Choose **Vertical 9×16**, enable **Analyze Clips**, click **Create**.



---

3. **Select \& Cut Your Hook**
	* Play through and find the best opening line (e.g. “It’s so nice\-looking, it’s the only appliance I leave out on my counter”).
	
	* Use **Q** (ripple\-delete) to trim everything before and after your chosen hook.



---

4. **Trim \& Align Core Feature Clips**
	* Locate key USP lines (“12\-in\-1 air fryer…”, “QuickSync technology…”).
	
	* Use **Q** to remove pauses/breaths; **W** to extend edit points if needed.



---

5. **Add B\-Roll \& Frame It**
	* Import your studio B\-roll folder.
	
	* Drag clips over A\-roll, right\-click → **Set to Frame Size** (or **Ctrl \+ F**) to fit.



---

6. **Create Jump Cuts \& Speed Ramps**
	* Split long action takes (e.g., “taking things out”) with the Razor tool and delete mid\-sections.
	
	* For slow\-motion: nest the clip, right\-click → **Speed/Duration** → set to 50%.



---

7. **Nest \& Zoom on Key Moments**
	* Right\-click clip → **Nest**, then adjust **Position \& Scale** to highlight phrases (“so they both end up exactly how you want them”).



---

8. **Add Subtitles**
	* Window → **Text** → **Transcribe**, then **Create Captions from Transcript**.
	
	* Set font/size, define Instagram safe\-zone, save a style preset (e.g., “Subs 1”) and apply globally.



---

9. **Insert Music \& Sound Effects**
	* Choose a hook track (e.g., playful/pop for the intro).
	
	* Layer educational/tech music under features, playful accents on “nothing’s worse…,” high\-note on CTA.



---

10. **Apply Transitions \& SFX**
	* Drag in whip/slide transitions between scenes.
	
	* Add matching sound\-effect clips under each visual transition.



---

11. **Color Grade \& Stabilize**
	* Apply **Warp Stabilizer** to shaky clips.
	
	* Adjust brightness, contrast \& saturation for a cohesive look.



---

12. **Add Final CTA \& Logo**
	* Drag your “Shop Now” or branded end\-card graphic onto the timeline.
	
	* Ensure it appears after your closing voice\-over: “Order your Emeril Lagasse Dual Zone Air Fryer today.”



---

13. **Export \& Share**
	* Review full edit for pacing and alignment.
	
	* Export with Instagram\-optimized settings (H.264, 9×16, \~30 fps).

Use this checklist to guide your edit from raw UGC to a polished direct\-response ad in under 30 minutes!
