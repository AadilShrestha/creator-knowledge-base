# ✅ **Action Items:**

1. **Understand the Workflow**
	* We provide ready\-to\-use static\-ad templates in **Canva** and **Figma**.
	
	* You’ll use [**THIS GPT**](https://chatgpt.com/g/g-6835d267d4b88191b88e786a95282b0c-ad-copywriter-pro "https://chatgpt.com/g/g-6835d267d4b88191b88e786a95282b0c-ad-copywriter-pro") to generate copy (headlines, benefits) and AI\-create or swap in product images.



---

2. **Generate Hook Headlines \& Benefits**
	* Open a ChatGPT conversation and prompt:
	> “I am making a creative in this style \[paste screenshot of template]. Come up with 10 headlines and 10 benefits I can use for my \[avatar].”
	
	* Example hook: **“For women who want smooth, confident skin by summer.”**
	
	* Note: This particular example is for a **collagen supplement**—ensure benefits match (e.g., “Supports skin elasticity,” “Visible results in 28 days,” etc.).



---

3. **Populate Your Template**
	* In [Canva](https://www.canva.com/design/DAGLTxffwzM/RNg1miaVgi1K7Ofq8M_WVQ/view?utm_content=DAGLTxffwzM&utm_campaign=designshare&utm_medium=link&utm_source=publishsharelink&mode=preview "https://www.canva.com/design/DAGLTxffwzM/RNg1miaVgi1K7Ofq8M_WVQ/view?utm_content=DAGLTxffwzM&utm_campaign=designshare&utm_medium=link&utm_source=publishsharelink&mode=preview") paste the chosen **headline** into the hook text field.
	
	* Paste each **benefit** into the designated benefit slots.



---

4. **Create or Swap in Your Product Image**
	* Start a new ChatGPT chat. Upload your product image and use a prompt like:
	> “Put this product on a kitchen counter with a clear mug of coffee next to it.”
	
	* Copy the generated image and drag it into your template’s image placeholder.
	
	* Resize or tweak color as needed to fit the design.



---

5. **Style\-Match an Existing Template Background**
	* If you love an existing static ad style:
		1. **Unlock** the background in your template and **bring it forward**.
		
		* Take a **screenshot** of that background design.
		
		* In ChatGPT, give a prompt like:
		> “Create the exact image with the leaf background, but place the product in it. Keep the same size and style.”
		
		* Copy the AI\-generated composite and replace the background in your template (delete old background, **right\-click → Layer → Send to Back**).



---

6. **Final Adjustments**
	* Resize or reposition elements for balance.
	
	* Tweak **brightness/contrast** or other styling so the product “pops.”
	
	* Confirm fonts, colors, and spacing remain consistent with your brand guidelines.



---

7. **Export \& Review**
	* Download your static ad in the required format (PNG/JPG, correct dimensions).
	
	* Double\-check copy for typos and ensure all placeholders are replaced.
