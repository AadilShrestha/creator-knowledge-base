### Key Steps

**1\. Locate the Community Hiring Tab** [0:00](https://loom.com/share/37880d7ad7cd403b9f66f585c3967340?t=0 "https://loom.com/share/37880d7ad7cd403b9f66f585c3967340?t=0")* Open the community area and navigate to **Sections**.

* Click **More** and find the **Hiring** tab.

* Use this tab as the primary place to post your editor hiring request.

  
**2\. Write a Clear Hiring Title** [0:11](https://loom.com/share/37880d7ad7cd403b9f66f585c3967340?t=11 "https://loom.com/share/37880d7ad7cd403b9f66f585c3967340?t=11")* Create a direct title that states exactly what type of editor you need.

* Use a format like: **Looking for \[type of editor] for \[brand/project]**.

* Example: **Looking for animation editors for a beauty brand**.

  
**3\. Define the Work Scope and Output** [0:26](https://loom.com/share/37880d7ad7cd403b9f66f585c3967340?t=26 "https://loom.com/share/37880d7ad7cd403b9f66f585c3967340?t=26")* Specify the type of content you need edited.

* Include how many videos you want completed per week.

* Be as specific as possible so applicants understand the workload and expectations.

  
**4\. Add Style References** [0:37](https://loom.com/share/37880d7ad7cd403b9f66f585c3967340?t=37 "https://loom.com/share/37880d7ad7cd403b9f66f585c3967340?t=37")* Include examples of the editing style you want.

* You can:
	+ Upload a YouTube video
	
	+ Upload a Loom video
	
	+ Upload a Vimeo link
	
	+ Paste direct links in the post* Use references that clearly show the style, pacing, and quality you expect.

  
**5\. State the Budget or Expected Earnings** [0:44](https://loom.com/share/37880d7ad7cd403b9f66f585c3967340?t=44 "https://loom.com/share/37880d7ad7cd403b9f66f585c3967340?t=44")* Add an approximate budget or earnings range in the post.

* This helps attract applicants who match your budget.

* Example budget ranges mentioned in the transcript:
	+ Basic video editing: **$20–$60 per video**
	
	+ Animation editing: **$60–$10 per video**
	
	+ AI video editing with tool costs covered by the editor: **$100–$200 per video**
	
	+ Monthly retainer example: **$1,500–$2,000 for around 20 ads per month**

### Cautionary Notes

* Do not post without clearly stating the type of editor you need; vague posts may attract unqualified applicants.

* Make sure your budget matches the complexity of the work, especially for animation or AI\-based editing.

* If you include style references, ensure they are relevant and easy to access.

* Be prepared to receive DMs or email responses after posting.

* The community includes different roles, so not every viewer will be an editor.
