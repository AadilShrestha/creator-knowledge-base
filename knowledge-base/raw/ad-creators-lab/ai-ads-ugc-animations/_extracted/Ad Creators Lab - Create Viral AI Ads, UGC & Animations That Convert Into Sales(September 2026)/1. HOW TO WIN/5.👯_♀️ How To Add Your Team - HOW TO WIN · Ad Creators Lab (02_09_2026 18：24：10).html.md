# F.A.Q ❓

* **Can't you just add my editor for me?**   
No, and the reason's above. Access has to be tied to a payment or there's no clean way to remove people when your team changes. Use your link. It's free for you and you earn from it, so you're genuinely better off.

* **When do I actually see the money?**   
Commissions are pending for 14 days, then they're payable.

* **Someone joined because of me but I got nothing.**   
Almost always one of three things: they didn't use your link, they clicked it more than 14 days before buying, or they bought on a different device where the cookie never followed them. Send it directly, ask them to do it there and then.

* **What happens if my editor leaves?** Their membership is theirs. It's tied to their payment, not to a seat you own. They cancel it or hand it over.

* **Does my editor need the same plan as me?**   
No. Standard is fine for someone doing production. It's got the frameworks. Premium is for the person making the scaling and strategy calls.
