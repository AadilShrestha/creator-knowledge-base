Start With the Real Strategy (Most People Skip This)

Before generating anything, figure out:**What failed solutions did customers try before your product?**Your ad angle becomes:**“I’m the failed solution explaining why I suck.”**Example script:
> “I’m your drugstore moisturizer.  
> I’m packed with cheap seed oils to keep my price low…  
> but those oils clog pores and irritate your skin.”

## 2\. Turn the Product Into a Character

Create an image where the **product becomes a character**.Seedance \- cheap but not good with textNanobanana \- pricier but elite at textTip:* Generate **4–6 variations**

* Choose the most expressive one.

## 3\. Turn the Image Into a Talking Video

Take the image and generate a **talking avatar video**.The image will:* move slightly

* lip sync (its fine if it's a little off) \- google veo is the best but pricier

* speak the script.

Test different models because results vary.Typical testing workflow:1️⃣ Cheap model → test timing  
2️⃣ Better model → final versionI tested all models in the video above.
