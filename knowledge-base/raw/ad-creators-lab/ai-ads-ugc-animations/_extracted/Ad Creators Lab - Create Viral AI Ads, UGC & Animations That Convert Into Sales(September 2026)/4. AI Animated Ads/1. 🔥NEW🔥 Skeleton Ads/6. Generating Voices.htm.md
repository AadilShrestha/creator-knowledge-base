# Generating Voices - AI Animated Ads · Ad Creators Lab

_Source: `Ad Creators Lab - Create Viral AI Ads, UGC & Animations That Convert Into Sales(September 2026)/4. AI Animated Ads/1. 🔥NEW🔥 Skeleton Ads/6. Generating Voices.htm` (Skool lesson page text)_

### Key Steps

 **1\. Prepare the script and open ElevenLabs** [0:10](https://loom.com/share/48f9fe0d234948ef90d3b4dfa6a18d2d?t=10 "https://loom.com/share/48f9fe0d234948ef90d3b4dfa6a18d2d?t=10")  
* Copy the finalized script you want converted into voiceover.

* Open ElevenLabs and navigate to the voice generation area.

* Keep the script ready to paste into the generator.

* Confirm the script is complete before moving forward.

 **2\. Choose a voice that matches the audience** [0:19](https://loom.com/share/48f9fe0d234948ef90d3b4dfa6a18d2d?t=19 "https://loom.com/share/48f9fe0d234948ef90d3b4dfa6a18d2d?t=19")  
* Review available voices in ElevenLabs.

* Select a voice that fits the target demographic and the message being delivered.

* If needed, experiment with different voice styles to find the best match.

* For story\-based content, consider a voice that feels natural and engaging rather than overly polished.

 **3\. Customize the voice style if needed** [0:30](https://loom.com/share/48f9fe0d234948ef90d3b4dfa6a18d2d?t=30 "https://loom.com/share/48f9fe0d234948ef90d3b4dfa6a18d2d?t=30")  
* If the default voice does not fit the content, use voice design or style adjustments.

* Define characteristics such as:
	+ Gender and age range
	
	+ Tone and energy level
	
	+ Delivery style (for example, assertive, strong, casual, or conversational)* Test a few variations if the first result sounds too artificial or too formal.

 **4\. Generate a sample speech** [0:55](https://loom.com/share/48f9fe0d234948ef90d3b4dfa6a18d2d?t=55 "https://loom.com/share/48f9fe0d234948ef90d3b4dfa6a18d2d?t=55")  
* Paste the script into the speech generation tool.

* Click **Generate Speech** to create an audio sample.

* Listen to the output and compare it against the intended tone.

* If the voice sounds too professional or AI\-like, adjust the style and regenerate.

 **5\. Refine the tone for realism** [1:09](https://loom.com/share/48f9fe0d234948ef90d3b4dfa6a18d2d?t=69 "https://loom.com/share/48f9fe0d234948ef90d3b4dfa6a18d2d?t=69")  
* Shift the delivery toward a more casual, conversational tone if needed.

* Aim for a voice that sounds like a real person speaking naturally.

* Use a tone that matches the content, such as someone casually sharing something interesting.

* Regenerate until the voice feels believable and aligned with the script.

 **6\. Use enhancements and finalize the voiceover** [1:26](https://loom.com/share/48f9fe0d234948ef90d3b4dfa6a18d2d?t=86 "https://loom.com/share/48f9fe0d234948ef90d3b4dfa6a18d2d?t=86")  
* If available, use the **Enhance** option to improve the delivery.

* Test the enhanced version and compare it with the original.

* Generate the final speech once the tone and pacing feel right.

* Download the completed voiceover file for use in the project.

 **7\. Review the final audio for quality control** [1:48](https://loom.com/share/48f9fe0d234948ef90d3b4dfa6a18d2d?t=108 "https://loom.com/share/48f9fe0d234948ef90d3b4dfa6a18d2d?t=108")  
* Listen to the downloaded voiceover from start to finish.

* Confirm that all words are pronounced correctly, especially brand names.

* Check that the pacing, tone, and clarity match expectations.

* If anything sounds off, return to the generator and make adjustments before publishing or handing off the file.

### Cautionary Notes

* Do not assume the first generated voice will be the best fit; AI voice output may require several iterations.

* Always verify pronunciation of brand names, product names, and any specialized terms.

* Avoid choosing a voice that sounds too polished if the content needs a casual or authentic feel.

* Make sure the selected voice matches the intended audience and content type.
