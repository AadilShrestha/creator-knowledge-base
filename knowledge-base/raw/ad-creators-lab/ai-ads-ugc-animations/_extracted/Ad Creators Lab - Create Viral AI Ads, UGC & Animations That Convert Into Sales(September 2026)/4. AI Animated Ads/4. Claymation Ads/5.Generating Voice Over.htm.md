# What you do (Voice section)

### Step 1: Go to voice generator

* Use a tool like **ElevenLabs**

* Go to *voice design*

### Step 2: Create a voice

* Describe the voice you want (age, tone, style)

* Keep it **calm, simple, not over\-the\-top**

### Step 3: Test the voice

* Generate a sample

* Listen before adding enhancements

### Step 4: Improve it

* Add enhancement if needed

* Make it sound more natural (less robotic)

### Step 5: Add your script

* Paste your script

* Generate full voiceover

### Step 6: Download

* Save the audio

* Use it in your video



---

# Key rule (important)

* Don’t make it too energetic

* Best ads \= calm, slightly serious, storytelling tone

👉 If it sounds like a YouTuber shouting → it’s wrong  
👉 If it sounds like a documentary → you’re close
