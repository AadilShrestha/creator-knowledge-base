## A few potential pitfalls:

* Don’t give the model actual, popular music tracks as inspiration since it will reject them due to copyright. Instead, describe the style you want in the styles box

* I’d advise against using the free version of Suno, since the paid one gives you access to their best model, which produces the best results

* Go through the entire song and make sure the pronunciation of all words is correct (e.g. the model has a hard time pronouncing “ashwagandha”)
