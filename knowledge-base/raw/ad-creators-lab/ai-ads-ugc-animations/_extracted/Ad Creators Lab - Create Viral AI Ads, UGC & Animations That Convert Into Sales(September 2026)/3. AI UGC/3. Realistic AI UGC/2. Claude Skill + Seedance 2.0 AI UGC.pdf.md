2)​Talking Head / Body script 
Guidelines: 
●​ This is your main speaking video. 
●​ Please express the points naturally, in your own storytelling voice. 
●​ Avoid words like “game changer”, “skyrocketed”, “level up”, “elevate”, “boost”, “synergy” 
unless they are part of the product name. 
●​ Change your outfit between the talking video and all B-rolls. 
●​ Please articulate clearly. 
Script Points: 
Whole body should be about a minute or less. 
The Setup 
●​ Briefly explain the test setup -  4 wines, 2 American 
(one bottle, one box), 2 Italian (one bottle, one box) 
●​ Mention you're going in completely blind 
●​ Build anticipation - "Let's see if I can actually tell the 
difference" 
●​ Keep it quick and conversational 
THE TASTING JOURNEY 
●​ Taste each wine and give genuine, unscripted 
reactions 
●​ Be honest about each one - some might be "fine," 
others might make you scrunch your face 
●​ When you get to the Gratsi wine, react authentically 
to the smoothness/cleanness 
●​ Don't reveal which is which yet - build the mystery 
●​ Natural phrases like "Okay this one's interesting..." or 
"Ooh, this is smooth" or "Something about this 
one..." 
THE REVEAL & 
REACTION 
●​ Rank your favorites (Gratsi Wine should be #1) 
●​ Remove the cardboard/reveal which wine is which

3)​B-roll Shot List 
​
Guidelines: 
●​ No voiceover. 
●​ B-roll is aesthetics: clean, well-lit, timeless 
●​ Avoid festive or seasonal decorations. 
●​ Change outfit from the talking video. 
Script to Film: 
Use this tick-off list to make sure all the content has been filmed:  
​Cardboard box with 4 holes — close-up showing the mystery setup with straws poking 
through 
​Wine glasses being filled — pour shots of each wine going into glasses (shoot from 
angle where labels aren't visible) 
​Flags being placed — tiny American and Italian flags being stuck into the cardboard 
​Overhead birds-eye of full setup — all 4 glasses with straws, cardboard barrier, clean 
countertop 
​Straw sipping close-up — tight shot on lips/straw as creator tastes 
​Reaction face montage — multiple angles of genuine reactions during tasting 
​Hand gesturing between glasses — creator deliberating, pointing at different straws 
​Cardboard being lifted/removed — the dramatic reveal moment
