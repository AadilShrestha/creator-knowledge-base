## How to Swap the On\-Screen Character in a Talking Ad Using AI

I will show you how to take a video of a man speaking, change the character, and maintain the same script and same visuals. Then I'll show you how to use it with changing gender and change in voice.  **1\. Identify the ad clip you want to modify** [0:00](https://loom.com/share/2bb94e42533e4d2b8c8c0275f6a77297?t=0 "https://loom.com/share/2bb94e42533e4d2b8c8c0275f6a77297?t=0")* Start with a talking\-head style ad where the creator is speaking on camera.

* The goal is to replace the person in the video while keeping the ad format and performance style intact.

* This works best for ads where the speaker is clearly visible and framed consistently.

 **2\. Capture a frame of the original creator** [0:14](https://loom.com/share/2bb94e42533e4d2b8c8c0275f6a77297?t=14 "https://loom.com/share/2bb94e42533e4d2b8c8c0275f6a77297?t=14")* Take a screenshot of a frame from the video.

* Use that screenshot as the base image for the character swap.

* The speaker’s pose and framing matter because the replacement works best when the new character matches the original position.

 **3\. Generate a replacement character image** [0:21](https://loom.com/share/2bb94e42533e4d2b8c8c0275f6a77297?t=21 "https://loom.com/share/2bb94e42533e4d2b8c8c0275f6a77297?t=21")![image.png](<../../../../linked/inline-images/3-ai-ugc-1-new-clone-winning-ads-1-change-creators-with-ai-h-2503ae/img01.png>)  
* Upload the screenshot into an image generator.

* I recommend using **Nano Banana Pro** instead of **GPT Image 2** because it produced more realistic results in testing.

* Prompt the generator to change the face or replace the man with a different person.

* Example prompt idea:
	+ “Change his face to look like xyz.”
	
	+ “Replace the man with a different white attractive male.”
	
	+ You can also specify other demographics like Hispanic or Black male.* Keep the lighting and realism in mind so the new image blends naturally into the ad.

 **4\. Avoid different gender replacements for now** [1:03](https://loom.com/share/2bb94e42533e4d2b8c8c0275f6a77297?t=63 "https://loom.com/share/2bb94e42533e4d2b8c8c0275f6a77297?t=63")![image.png](<../../../../linked/inline-images/3-ai-ugc-1-new-clone-winning-ads-1-change-creators-with-ai-h-2503ae/img02.png>)  
* The current limitation is that gender replacements do not work well.

* When the swapped video is run through Seedance, the voice and character change can become mismatched and sound unnatural.

* For now, the workable approach is to keep the replacement character the same gender as the original.
* Make sure the new character is in a similar pose and position as the original.
* Example: an arm position that matches the original will work better.

* If the pose is too different, the swap may fail or look unnatural.

  
**5\. Upload the original video and new image into Seedance 2\.0** [1:56](https://loom.com/share/2bb94e42533e4d2b8c8c0275f6a77297?t=116 "https://loom.com/share/2bb94e42533e4d2b8c8c0275f6a77297?t=116")* Go to **Seedance 2\.0**.

* Open the **reference video** section.

* Upload the original video.

* Upload the newly generated replacement image.

 **6\. Use a simple replacement prompt** [2:11](https://loom.com/share/2bb94e42533e4d2b8c8c0275f6a77297?t=131 "https://loom.com/share/2bb94e42533e4d2b8c8c0275f6a77297?t=131")* Use a straightforward prompt such as:
	+ “Replace the man in video 1 with the man in image 1\.”* Keep the video length about one second longer than the original.

* Generate the result and review the output for realism and consistency.

 **7\. Review the final swapped ad** [2:27](https://loom.com/share/2bb94e42533e4d2b8c8c0275f6a77297?t=147 "https://loom.com/share/2bb94e42533e4d2b8c8c0275f6a77297?t=147")  
* The output should preserve the original ad’s style while swapping in the new character.
