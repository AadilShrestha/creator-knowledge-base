# Realistic AI Podcasts Tutorial - AI UGC · Ad Creators Lab

_Source: `Ad Creators Lab - Create Viral AI Ads, UGC & Animations That Convert Into Sales(September 2026)/3. AI UGC/2. AI Podcasts (that look real)/2. Realistic AI Podcasts Tutorial.htm` (Skool lesson page text)_

### Objective

Create a realistic AI\-generated podcast\-style video ad by sourcing a strong visual reference.The goal is to produce content that **looks natural,** niche\-appropriate, and believable rather than obviously AI\-generated.### Key Steps

**1\. Find a strong podcast reference style on Pinterest** [0:39](https://loom.com/share/36753d2504be40268dcff181379ea016?t=39 "https://loom.com/share/36753d2504be40268dcff181379ea016?t=39")![image.png](<../../../../linked/inline-images/3-ai-ugc-2-ai-podcasts-that-look-real-2-realistic-ai-podcast-01519d/img01.png>)  
* Open **Pinterest** and search for a niche\-specific podcast style, such as **"girly podcast"** or another relevant niche term.

* Review both **images and videos**, but be cautious because some results may already be AI\-generated.

* Click into promising images to trigger more suggestions and explore related styles.

* Choose a reference that matches the type of content your audience already watches in that niche.

* If needed, cross\-check with **YouTube Shorts** or YouTube search to see what podcast backgrounds and setups are actually popular in the niche.

 **2\. Select a reference image that looks realistic and usable** [2:08](https://loom.com/share/36753d2504be40268dcff181379ea016?t=128 "https://loom.com/share/36753d2504be40268dcff181379ea016?t=128")![image.png](<../../../../linked/inline-images/3-ai-ugc-2-ai-podcasts-that-look-real-2-realistic-ai-podcast-01519d/img02.png>)  
* Keep scrolling until you find a podcast image that looks authentic and visually strong.

* Avoid references that look overly artificial, overly polished, or obviously AI\-generated.

* Pick a reference with a layout, lighting, and background that can be adapted without looking fake.

* Take a **screenshot** of the chosen image so you can use it as the base for editing.

 **3\. Edit the reference image to remove likeness issues** [2:34](https://loom.com/share/36753d2504be40268dcff181379ea016?t=154 "https://loom.com/share/36753d2504be40268dcff181379ea016?t=154")![image.png](<../../../../linked/inline-images/3-ai-ugc-2-ai-podcasts-that-look-real-2-realistic-ai-podcast-01519d/img03.png>)  
* Upload the screenshot into an image editor or AI image tool.

* Modify the person’s face so it no longer resembles the original creator or any recognizable person.

* Use a prompt that changes the face while preserving the overall style of the image.

* Example prompt direction: 
	+ Change the woman’s face shape.
	
	+ Adjust hair color and accessories.
	
	+ Change their clothes for xyz
	
	+ Keep the image style consistent with the original reference.* Preferred tools mentioned: **Nanobanana Pro** or **GPT Image 2**.
* Example prompt:  Change the persons face so it's more round, give her blonde hair and glasses. Replace her top for a grey blouse.

**4\. Refine the image style while keeping it realistic** [3:30](https://loom.com/share/36753d2504be40268dcff181379ea016?t=210 "https://loom.com/share/36753d2504be40268dcff181379ea016?t=210")![image.png](<../../../../linked/inline-images/3-ai-ugc-2-ai-podcasts-that-look-real-2-realistic-ai-podcast-01519d/img04.png>)  
* Review the generated variations and choose the one that best preserves the original camera texture and realism.

* Compare outputs from different tools if needed to find the most natural\-looking result.

* Keep the image looking like a real photo rather than a synthetic render.

* Prioritize realism in skin texture, lighting, and lens feel.

 **5\. Adjust the background and props to avoid direct copying** [4:03](https://loom.com/share/36753d2504be40268dcff181379ea016?t=243 "https://loom.com/share/36753d2504be40268dcff181379ea016?t=243")![image.png](<../../../../linked/inline-images/3-ai-ugc-2-ai-podcasts-that-look-real-2-realistic-ai-podcast-01519d/img05.png>)  
* If the original background is too recognizable or tied to a famous creator, edit it further.

* Use a prompt to change background elements such as: 
	+ Plant size or leaf shape
	
	+ Background color
	
	+ Clothing details* Make small changes that preserve the overall composition but reduce resemblance to the original source.

* Aim for a result that still feels like a real podcast set.

* AVOID DOING ALL IN ONE PROMPT

 **6\. Generate the first speaking video from the edited image** [4:44](https://loom.com/share/36753d2504be40268dcff181379ea016?t=284 "https://loom.com/share/36753d2504be40268dcff181379ea016?t=284")![image.png](<../../../../linked/inline-images/3-ai-ugc-2-ai-podcasts-that-look-real-2-realistic-ai-podcast-01519d/img06.png>)  
* Turn the edited image into a video using an image\-to\-video tool such as **Kling 3\.0 or Seedance 2\.0**.

* Use the image as the reference frame.

* Add a short line for the creator to say, such as a promotional hook.

* Prompt the voice carefully so it sounds like a real podcast speaker.

* Include details such as: 
	+ Accent
	
	+ Speaking speed
	
	+ Microphone quality
	
	+ Slight echo or reverberation
	
	+ Volume changes when the speaker moves away from the mic* Keep the clip short, around **5 seconds**, for testing.

* E.g. prompt: The creator speaks in a soft English British accent at a normal speed. The voice sounds like it's coming from a podcast mic with little echo and reverberation. As her mouth moves away from the microphone, the audio changes in volume. She speaks very assertively.She says: "Ad Creators Lab is the best place to learn how to make high\-converting AI ads, hands down."

* Template: The creator speaks in a \[accent] accent at \[speed]. The voice sounds like it's coming from a podcast mic with little echo and reverberation. As her mouth moves away from the microphone, the audio changes in volume. She speaks \[delivery style].She says: "\[line]"

  **7\. Create the second podcast speaker by mirroring and re\-editing the image** [6:22](https://loom.com/share/36753d2504be40268dcff181379ea016?t=382 "https://loom.com/share/36753d2504be40268dcff181379ea016?t=382")![image.png](<../../../../linked/inline-images/3-ai-ugc-2-ai-podcasts-that-look-real-2-realistic-ai-podcast-01519d/img07.png>)  
* Duplicate the original edited image and modify it to create a second speaker.

* Use an edit prompt to: 
	+ Flip the creator horizontally
	
	+ Change the person into a different\-looking woman
	
	+ Adjust age, hair color, glasses, ethnicity, and clothing* Make sure the second speaker looks distinct from the first.

* Align both speakers so they appear to be facing the same direction and occupying the same visual plane.

 **9\. Generate the second speaker’s video and reaction shots** [7:11](https://loom.com/share/36753d2504be40268dcff181379ea016?t=431 "https://loom.com/share/36753d2504be40268dcff181379ea016?t=431")![image.png](<../../../../linked/inline-images/3-ai-ugc-2-ai-podcasts-that-look-real-2-realistic-ai-podcast-01519d/img08.png>)  
* Create the second speaker with this prompt:  
  
Flip the creator horizontally and change it to be a \[age range] \[gender], \[hair color/style], \[glasses or no glasses], \[ethnicity/look]. \[Optional: any other identity markers].

* Animate the second speaker using the same image\-to\-video workflow.

* Prompt the second speaker’s voice with a different accent or style while keeping it realistic.

* Generate reaction clips such as short listening or response moments.

* Use simple prompts for reactions so the expressions feel natural and not overdone.
	+ e.g. She laughs then nods and agrees.

 **10\. Edit and stitch the scenes into a finished podcast\-style ad** [8:44](https://loom.com/share/36753d2504be40268dcff181379ea016?t=524 "https://loom.com/share/36753d2504be40268dcff181379ea016?t=524")![image.png](<../../../../linked/inline-images/3-ai-ugc-2-ai-podcasts-that-look-real-2-realistic-ai-podcast-01519d/img09.png>)  
* Assemble the clips in an editing timeline.

* Start with a strong hook from the first speaker.

* Add the second speaker’s response and any reaction shots.

* Keep the pacing tight and conversational.

* Ensure the audio remains consistent across cuts.

* Review the final sequence to confirm the podcast looks believable and the background movement feels natural.

### Cautionary Notes

* Do not use a recognizable person’s face or likeness without changing it significantly.

* Be careful with Pinterest references, since some images may already be AI\-generated.

* Avoid trying to change too many image elements in one prompt; large edits can produce strange results.

* If a background or prop is too iconic, modify it enough to avoid direct copying.

* Audio consistency matters: weak voice prompting can make the final video feel fake.

* Keep the final result realistic; overly polished visuals can make the content look artificial.

### Tips for Efficiency

* Use Pinterest as a fast reference library for niche\-specific podcast aesthetics.

* Search YouTube Shorts for real\-world examples of what viewers already engage with.

* Make small image edits in stages instead of one large edit.

* Reuse the same voice style prompt across scenes to save time and improve consistency.

* Generate short test clips first before producing the full sequence.

* Keep the composition simple: one speaker on each side, same plane, minimal background changes.
