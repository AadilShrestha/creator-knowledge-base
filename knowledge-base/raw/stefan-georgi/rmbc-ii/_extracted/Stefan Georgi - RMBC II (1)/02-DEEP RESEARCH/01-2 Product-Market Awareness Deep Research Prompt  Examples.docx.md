**<span class="mark">Prompt \#1:</span>**

I want your help evaluating the level of Market Awareness for a product in the <span class="mark">YOUR NICHE</span> space. The specific product is \_<span class="mark">PRODUCT</span>\_

I would like for you to dive deep to help me understand the current level of market awareness for this product in <span class="mark">\_GEOGRAPHY\_.</span>

**<span class="mark">Optional:</span>**

<span class="mark">Here are more details about the market \_DETAILS\_</span>

To do this, we can look at the classic levels of Product and Market awareness that is often attributed to Eugene Schwarz, which can be categorized as follows:

<span class="mark">1. Unaware: Prospects are not even aware of a problem or need that your product or service can solve.</span>

<span class="mark">2. Problem Aware: Prospects recognize they have a problem or need but are unaware of potential solutions.</span>

<span class="mark">3. Solution Aware: Prospects know that solutions exist for their problem, but they are not yet aware of your specific solution.</span>

<span class="mark">4. Product Aware: Prospects know about your solution, your product or service, and are evaluating its features and benefits.</span>

<span class="mark">5. Most Aware: Prospects are highly aware of your solution, its benefits, and are likely to make a purchase decision soon.</span>

<span class="mark">For the intents of this exercise, let’s not consider Product Aware or Most Aware to be for our specific product and brand. Instead let’s consider it to be an awareness about similar/nearly identical products and brands that offer those products.</span>

<span class="mark">In order to make this assessment please utilize a variety of signals including social media chatter, articles and blog posts, influencer content, search trends, any available sales data on the product category (and its growth), and so on.</span>

<span class="mark">Additionally:</span>

<span class="mark">1. Please provide an overview of the estimated TAM (total addressable market) for this product.</span>

<span class="mark">2. Please provide a rough estimate of what percentage of the TAM falls into each category of product/market awareness.</span>

<span class="mark">3. At the end of your review, please give me a FINAL selection for which stage of Product/Market Awareness the majority of individuals within the market fall.</span>

<span class="mark">Thanks!  
  
=================================
