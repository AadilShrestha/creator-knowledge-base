⚡ Quick Rule of Thumb 
1.​ Score each factor 1-5 for your offer: 
○​ Total: 
 
2.​ High Score =  lean, direct funnel. Low Score =  long, multi-step funnel 
 
3.​ Match Templates: 
○​ Lean (46+ pts)  
i.​
Ad → PDP → Cart. 
○​ Medium (26-45 pts)  
i.​
Structure #1: Static Ad or 30-60 Second Video Ad→ Quiz/Advertorial → 
8-15 min VSL or Longer Form Landing Page → Cart/Oto. 
○​ Long (26-45 pts)  
i.​
Structure #2: 3-10 In Feed Video Ad (Mini VSL) → Optional 
Quiz/Advertorial (test) → PDP Landing Page → Cart/Oto. 
○​ Heavy : (≤25 pts)   
i.​
Ad → Possible Story Presell → 35-45 min VSL/Webinar → 
Application/Checkout → Possible Ascension emails. 
 
4.​ Iterate: Adjust one factor at a time (e.g., add BNPL or stronger demo) to shift toward 
shorter funnels and boost CVR.​
