**Different Purpose of Product Pages:**

a\) Sometimes It’s To Do Heavy Selling (Longer-Format, Kind of Like the New Text Sales Letter)

b\) Sometimes It’s To Present a Branded Experience While Pushing Prospect Over The Hump

C\) And Sometimes It’s Both

**When is the PDP Longer?**  
  
Static Ads or Short Video Ads (\<60 Seconds) and/or Shorter Advertorial

**When is the PDP Shorter?**

Medium or Long Video Ads (60 Seconds +) and/or Longer Advertorial.
