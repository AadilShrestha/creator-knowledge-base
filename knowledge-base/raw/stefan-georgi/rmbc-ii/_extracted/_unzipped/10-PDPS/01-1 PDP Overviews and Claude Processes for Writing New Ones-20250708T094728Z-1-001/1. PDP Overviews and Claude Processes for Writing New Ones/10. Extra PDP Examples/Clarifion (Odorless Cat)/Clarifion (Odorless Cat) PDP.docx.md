How do I take care of my device?

The ODRx doesn’t require any filter replacements. However, over time and with continuous use, the UVC bulb may naturally lose some brightness. This can impact its power. If the bulb begins to change color or lose vibrancy, we recommend changing it for optimal performance. We recommend to change the bulb like 6-12 months.

What Kind Of Odors Can It Eliminate?

ODRx can eliminate cat odors, dog odors, bacteria, mold, airborne allergens, and other smells in your household, such as funky bathroom stinks and garbage stench.

How do I replace the UVC bulb?

It’s easy! Just follow these 4 simple steps: 1. Ensure the device is unplugged and cool to the touch. 2. Turn the device over and remove the UVC compartment cover using the “open tab.” Simply peel back the sticker and stick one side onto your pointer finger. Then, stick the other side onto the surface of the bulb to be replaced. Gently pull to dislodge the used bulb from the compartment. 3. Take a replacement bulb and insert the bottom two prongs into the compartment outlet and ensure the bulb is secure. You can use a dry brush or damp cloth to clean the compartment cover before attaching it back to the device. 4. Snap the compartment cover back into place, and you’re done!
