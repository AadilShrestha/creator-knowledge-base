- **Icon**

- **Feature Name**

- **Brief Description**

3.  Surface compatibility

4.  Safety/non-toxic

5.  Environmental benefit

6.  Sensory benefit (smell, feel)

## **7. PROBLEM/SOLUTION SECTION**

### **Problem Headline**

**Template:**

\[PRODUCT NAME\] Solves All Your \[PROBLEM CATEGORY\] Instantly

Stop \[CURRENT FRUSTRATING BEHAVIOR\]

### **Three Problem/Solution Blocks**

**Block 1: Core Problem**

- **Subheadline:** Targets All \[Problem Category\]

- **Description:** Problem identification + solution explanation

**Block 2: Efficiency Problem**

- **Subheadline:** Quickly & Easily \[Removes/Solves\] All \[Problems\]

- **Description:** Speed and ease benefits + process description

**Block 3: Cost Problem**

- **Subheadline:** Spend Less Money On \[Product Category\]

- **Three Cost Benefits:**

  - Money-Saving Solution

  - Reusable/Refillable aspect

  - Environmental benefit
