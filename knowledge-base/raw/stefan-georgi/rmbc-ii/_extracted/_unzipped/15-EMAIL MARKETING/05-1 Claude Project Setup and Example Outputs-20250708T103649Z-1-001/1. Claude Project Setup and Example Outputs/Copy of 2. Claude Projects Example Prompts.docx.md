**Unity Eye Serum - Abandon Cart Sequence**

**Prompt 1:**

To start, let's do a 7 day abandon cart sequence for Unity Eye Serum. Let's introduce a 20% off discount on Day 3, and increase it to a 40% off Discount on Days 5-7. Can you please outline this sequence for me and then after I approve I'll have you write it.

https://tryopal.com/products/unity-eye-serum

**Outline Feedback Prompt:**

Okay great, let's go ahead and write it. One note for the tone with Email 1, and in general, let's make sure it is empathetic and supportive. We want to call out pain points, but we want to do it more like: "maybe you've experienced this." Or "Some of our customers share that \_\_\_\_\_" when talking about negatives. What we don't want to do is make it sound like we're assuming things about the prospect, ie: "Hey you hate how you look in the mirror huh"?"
