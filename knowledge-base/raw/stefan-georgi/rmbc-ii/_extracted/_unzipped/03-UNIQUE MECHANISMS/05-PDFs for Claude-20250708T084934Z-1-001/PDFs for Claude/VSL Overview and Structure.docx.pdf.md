2) Lead (These Don’t Have to be in order) (Open a bunch of loops with fascinations, and
say - I’m going to get to that in just a moment, or a few minutes, or “and you’ll see that
in this video”
a) Call Out Problem ✅
b) Promise Solution to Pain Point + Promise to Save Time/Money ✅
c) Tease Emotional Discovery Story ✅
d) Tease Unique Mechanism Behind Solution ✅
e) Tease Contrarian Nature of Mechanism (This Flies in the Face of What You’ve
Been Told Before)✅
f) Working in “Fascinations” as Incentive For Continuing With Copy (I’ll Answer This
Riddle For You)
g) Briefly Mention Credibility Builders✅
h) Qualifiers – Who This Works For. Is It For Everyone? Is It For a Certain Subset?
Speak to Your Market. ✅
i) Briefly Address Skepticism. ✅
j) Include a Few Broad Testimonials if Available (About Benefits But Don’t Give
Away Product)
3) II. Background Story
a) Who I Am + Credibility Builders When Appropriate (ie. A Doctor or Expert) ✅
b) “Me or Someone Close to Me Was Just Like You (In Pain)” – Here’s The Emotional
Story About It✅

## Page 3

c) Traditional Solutions & Advice Not Working✅
d) Trigger Event – Problem or Pain Point Escalated to Where Avatar Realizes
Something MUST Change✅
e) Search for Truth/Answers ✅
4) III. Unique Mechanism of the Problem
a) Explaining the “Real Cause” of the Problem; You’ve Had 99% of the Answer But
This is the Missing 1% ✅
b) Should Be Surprising, Possibly Even Counterintuitive ✅
c) Backed by Credibility and Proof Elements ✅
5) Unique Mechanism of The Solution
a) Now That You Know the Real Cause of the Problem, This is the “Real Solution” on
a MACRO level.
b) This Should Be Logically Connected to the Problem
c) Once They Have the MACRO Solution (A Theory) – They Dig Deep To Prove The
Theory (And Get Into The MICRO).
d) Backed By Credibility and Proof Elements, Including Citations and Scientific
Sources.
6)
V. Product Buildup + Reveal
a) Now That Avatar Knows The Solution, He Looks For An “Out-of-the-Box” Version;
Something That Will Just End His Quest and Solve The Problem Right There
b) But Discovers That Out-of-the-Box Is Flawed
c) Has to Do It Himself And Create or Find A Novel New Solution
d) Starts Testing It or Working On It. There Are Problems and Setbacks
e) But Eventual Success/Breakthrough✅
f) Proof Product Works✅
g) Others Asking For It✅
h) PRODUCT Is Born!
7) VI. Close
a) Product Details (What’s Included, What Makes it Special, Unique Selling
Propositions)
b) Discuss and Dismiss Other Alternatives (Expensive, Ineffective, Side-Effects, Poor
Quality, Unproven)
c) Keep Building Up Value of -PRODUCT-

## Page 4

d) Additional Testimonials
e) How to Use The Product
f) Scarcity: Demand Is High, Supplies Are Limited, Out-of-Stocks Are Common
g) Personal Mission of Avatar to Make Things Better – Tied to Emotion
h) Price Justification & Reveal – Other “Solutions” Are More Expensive &
Consequences of Inaction are Costly (Cost Can Be Either Material or Immaterial)
i) This Solution is Better and Will Save You Money, It’s Normally $X Which is
Bargain, But Right Now You Can Get It For Just $Y!
j) Smart People Buy More
8) First Call to Action
a) What Happens After You Click The Button
b) Optional: Valuable Free Bonuses Revealed
c) Guarantee – You’ll Most Likely Have a Great Experience, But If For Any Reason
You Don’t, No Risk and No Hassle
9) Second Call to Action
a) Two Choices – You Can Go On Suffering, Or You Can Take Action Today and
Change Your Life forever; The Choice Is Yours
b) Urgency 2 – But You Really Must Hurry As This is The End of the Presentation And
There’s No Guarantee How Long Offer Will Be Available
10)Third Call to Action
a) Thank You For Watching
11)VII. FAQs
a) Back to Being Their Friend; Helping To Clear Up Any Confusion They May Have
b) Reminding Them of What Product Is, What It’s Used For, Importance of Taking
Action Today, Terms of Guarantee, and How to Buy!
