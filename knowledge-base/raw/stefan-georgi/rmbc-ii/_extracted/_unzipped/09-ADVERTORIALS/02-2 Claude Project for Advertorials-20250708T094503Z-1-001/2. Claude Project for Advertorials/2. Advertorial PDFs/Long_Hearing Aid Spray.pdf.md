What if I told you that all hearing problems, from mild 
intermittent ringing to almost total deafness, are caused by the 
ONE TYPE OF CELLS that every person has? 
 
It sounds counterintuitive, but it's true. 
 
And their name is cochlear cells. 
 
You see, the entire inside of our ears is covered with tiny hairs that 
are attached to special nerve endings - cochlear cells. These cells 
convert vibrations into impulses that travel to the brain, where the 
auditory analyzer cortex converts them into the sounds we hear.

receive sound signals and eventually die off.
