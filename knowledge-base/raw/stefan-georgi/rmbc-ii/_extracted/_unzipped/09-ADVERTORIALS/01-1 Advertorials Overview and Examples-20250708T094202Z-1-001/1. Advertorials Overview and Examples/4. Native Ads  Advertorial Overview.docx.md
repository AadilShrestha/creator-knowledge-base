**Problems For Native Ads Running to Long-Form VSL Landers**

Native Ad -\> VSL Lander

<u>The Issue Is NOT AOV or even necessarily conversions.</u>

**It’s:**

Compliance and Congruence

**It’s Solved By:**

Ad -\> Advertorial (interstitial page) -\> VSL Lander.

**Problems for Native Ads Running to a PDP:**

Native Ad -\> Traditional PDP

Here you DO have the same problem as with meta ad -\> PDP.

1\. Your “CPA” is Going to Be Way Too High

2\. Your “AOV” is Going to Be Way Too Low

PLUS you might have an additional problem with CONGRUENCE.

**Lack of Congruence Example \#1:**

Native Ad about an Oven Cleaner goes to PDP for an All-Purpose Cleaner.

“I clicked on what looked like an article about a miracle oven cleaner, now I’m on a product page about an all purpose cleaner, I feel bait-and-switched.”

Lack of Congruence Example \#2:

Native Ad about new \$99 hearing aid discovery goes to a PDP for a \$99 hearing aid discovery.

Okay here, it might seem congruent, but it’s *not*.

Why?

*“I clicked on what looked like an article and now I’m on a product page. I wanted to consume content, not shop for hearing aids.”*

**The Result Is The Same As WIth Meta:**

You will lose money every day, and never be able to scale.

**Why?**

Prospects are Not Educated Enough.

They Don’t Believe it Works

They Don’t Know Enough About the Product

Plus, the Pain Point Doesn’t Feel Urgent Enough

**How Do You Solve It?**  
  
Native Ad -\> <u>Congruent Advertorial</u> -\>PDP
