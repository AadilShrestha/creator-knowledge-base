1. Target Market Demographic Overview 
Primary Segments: Problem Aware & Solution Aware Women

●​ Age: 35-60+ (peak target: 40-55)

●​ Income: Middle to upper-middle class with disposable income for premium skincare

medical/nutritional literacy

●​ Problem Aware: Searches broadly ("how to get rid of dark circles," "under eye

●​ Previous skincare routines suddenly stop working

Language & Communication Style 
Use This Language: 
●​ Empowering: "Support," "nourish," "strengthen," "restore," "revitalize" 
●​ Scientific but accessible: "Clinically proven," "dermatologist-tested," "peptide 
technology" 
●​ Validating: "You deserve," "it's time," "you've earned this" 
●​ Realistic: "Gentle yet effective," "visible results," "gradual improvement" 
●​ Quality-focused: "Premium ingredients," "proven formulations," "expert-developed" 
Avoid This Language: 
●​ Ageist: "Anti-aging," "turn back time," "fountain of youth" 
●​ Desperate: "Miracle," "instant transformation," "overnight results" 
●​ Shame-based: "Hide," "fix," "flaws," "imperfections" 
●​ Overly clinical: Complex chemical names without explanation 
●​ Youth-obsessed: References to looking "20 again" or competing with younger women

●​ Gentle yet powerful formula safe for sensitive skin 
●​ Professional-grade results without the professional price tag or irritation 
Emotional Transformation Promises:

3. Primary Objections & How to Address Them 
"I've Tried Everything - Nothing Works" 
Objection: Deep skepticism from years of disappointing products Address: Lead with clinical 
studies, before/after photos, and detailed explanation of peptide technology differences 
"It's Too Expensive" 
Objection: Burned by expensive products that didn't work Address: Cost-per-use analysis, 
money-back guarantee, comparison to professional treatments 
"It Will Irritate My Sensitive Skin" 
Objection: Fear of reactions from previous bad experiences with active ingredients Address: 
Gentle formulation messaging, sensitivity testing, patch test recommendations 
"Peptides Are Just Marketing Hype" 
Objection: Skeptical of ingredient trends and marketing claims Address: Scientific explanation 
of peptide mechanisms, dermatologist endorsements, peer-reviewed research 
"I Don't Have Time for Complicated Routines" 
Objection: Already overwhelmed with life responsibilities Address: Simple application process, 
integration into existing routine, time-saving benefits 
"My Dark Circles Are Genetic - Nothing Can Help" 
Objection: Belief that some issues are unchangeable Address: Focus on improvable aspects 
(puffiness, fine lines, skin texture) rather than promising to eliminate genetic factors 
4. Existing Solutions & Their Inadequacies 
Mass Market Eye Creams (CeraVe, Olay, Neutrogena) 
What they tried: Basic moisturizers marketed for eye area Why inadequate: Generic 
formulations, minimal active ingredients, don't address specific aging concerns 
Prestige Department Store Brands (Estée Lauder, Clinique) 
What they tried: Higher-priced creams with basic anti-aging ingredients Why inadequate: 
Overpriced for results delivered, heavy textures, outdated formulations
