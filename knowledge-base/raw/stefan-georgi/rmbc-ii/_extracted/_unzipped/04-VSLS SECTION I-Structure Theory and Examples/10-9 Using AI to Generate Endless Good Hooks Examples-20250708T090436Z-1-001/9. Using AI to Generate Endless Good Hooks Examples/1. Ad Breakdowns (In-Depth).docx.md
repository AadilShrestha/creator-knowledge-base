# Ad Script Breakdown \#2 - Svelte 

**Part I. Opening Hooks and Attention Grabbers**

**Female Presenter (appearing concerned):** "Don't drink fruit smoothies first thing in the morning before watching this."

(A young woman with medium-length hair wearing a gray top appears concerned while holding what appears to be a green smoothie or juice. She is filmed in a casual home bed setting with white interior elements. Her facial expression conveys urgency and warning.)

**Female Presenter (playful tone):** "Did you know that grapes are loaded with sugar?"

(An attractive young woman with long dark hair in a striped tank top is shown playfully holding a bunch of grapes above her open mouth, appearing about to eat them. She has a flirtatious expression that creates viewer curiosity. Below her is a close-up shot of red grapes on a cutting board, establishing a visual pattern interrupt between the appealing woman and the "problematic" food.)

**Female Fitness Instructor (authoritative tone):** "Hold on, bananas are sugar bombs."

(Two fit women in athletic wear are shown in a modern kitchen with white cabinetry and large windows. One woman in a white mesh tank top is gesturing toward the other woman who is holding a banana. The first woman appears to be interrupting or correcting the second woman, creating a teaching moment pattern interrupt.)

**Pattern Interrupt - Visual Prop:** "Stop!"

(A dramatic visual pattern interrupt shows a person in a yellow top holding a large red stop sign directly in front of their face, completely blocking their identity. This creates a strong visual metaphor and fourth-wall break. The kitchen setting continues from the previous scene, maintaining environmental continuity while creating a jarring visual disruption.)

**Part II. Solution Introduction and Authority Building**

**Female Nutrition Expert (reassuring tone):** "So don't concern yourself with fruit sugars and learn what foods to add for faster weight loss results."
