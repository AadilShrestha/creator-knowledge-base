## **Saccharide Isomerate**

- **Deep, long-lasting hydration:** Saccharide isomerate is a plant-derived carbohydrate complex that **binds water to the skin like a magnet**, providing instant and cumulative moisture. It has been shown to provide **up to 72 hours of hydration** after application[<u>scitepress.org</u>](https://www.scitepress.org/PublishedPapers/2019/98375/98375.pdf#:~:text=The%20outstanding%20properties%20of%20Saccharide,higher%20moisturizing%20ability%20than%20glycerin). In fact, tests report it can outperform traditional humectants (retaining *38% more moisture than glycerin* after just 3 minutes)[<u>scitepress.org</u>](https://www.scitepress.org/PublishedPapers/2019/98375/98375.pdf#:~:text=lasting%20up%20to%2072%20hours,higher%20moisturizing%20ability%20than%20glycerin). This long-lasting hydration relieves dryness, flakiness, and gives a plumper, dewy appearance to the skin.

- **Strengthens the skin barrier:** Saccharide isomerate not only moisturizes but also **reinforces the skin’s barrier function**. Studies indicate it upregulates key epidermal proteins **filaggrin and loricrin** (vital for a healthy stratum corneum), leading to improved barrier integrity. By reducing transepidermal water loss and boosting these natural moisturizing factors, it keeps skin comfortably hydrated and less prone to irritation. (Notably, previous research documented a **\>20% reduction in TEWL** within two weeks, signifying a stronger barrier.)

## **Sodium Hyaluronate (Hyaluronic Acid)**

- **Intense hydration & plumping:** Hyaluronic acid is a superstar humectant capable of holding **around 1000 times its weight in water**[<u>pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov</u>](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8322246/#:~:text=modulating%20cellular%20immunity%2C%20regulating%20epidermal,29). Just 1 gram can bind roughly **6 liters of water**[<u>pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov</u>](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8322246/#:~:text=modulating%20cellular%20immunity%2C%20regulating%20epidermal,29), which it draws into the skin. This surge of moisture instantly **plumps up the skin**, smoothing out the look of fine lines and giving a supple, bouncy feel. The deep hydration also improves skin turgor (“springiness”), tackling dryness and crepiness often seen around the eyes.

- **Clinically reduces wrinkles:** Topical hyaluronic acid delivers both immediate and long-term anti-aging benefits. In a 6-week clinical study on photoaged skin, a hyaluronic serum led to a **63% increase in skin hydration** and significantly **diminished wrinkles and fine lines** (fine line depth reduced by ~30% on average)[<u>pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov</u>](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8322246/#:~:text=week%204%2C%20with%20statistically%20significant,%28Fig.%C2%A01). Participants showed marked improvements in skin smoothness, elasticity, and crow’s feet, with dermatologists attributing the results to HA’s filling and moisture-retaining action<u>[pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8322246/#:~:text=The%20investigator%20assessed%20hydration%20and,p)[pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8322246/#:~:text=week%204%2C%20with%20statistically%20significant,%28Fig.%C2%A01)</u>. These findings confirm hyaluronic acid’s role in visibly firming and rejuvenating the eye area.
