# **Scientific Claims for Key Eye Serum Ingredients**

## **Dipalmitoyl Hydroxyproline (DPHP)**

- **Clinically proven wrinkle reduction:** A cream with 1% DPHP produced a significant **32% decrease in wrinkle depth** compared to placebo in an in vivo study[<u>personalcaremagazine.com</u>](https://www.personalcaremagazine.com/story/2634/anti-ageing-performance#:~:text=The%20new%20anti,16). This peptide visibly smooths fine lines by strengthening the skin’s underlying support.

- **Protects and reorganizes collagen structure:** DPHP stimulates the skin’s own **TIMP (tissue inhibitor of metalloproteinase)** production, which in turn **suppresses collagen-degrading MMP enzymes**[<u>pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov</u>](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2923951/#:~:text=mimics%20the%20sequence%20found%20in,14). By curbing these collagen breakdown enzymes, DPHP preserves the dermal matrix and promotes a tighter, “re-woven” collagen architecture for firmer skin.

## **Pistacia Lentiscus Extract (Mastic Resin, Lakesis)**

- **Reactivates youth proteins & firms skin:** A lipid extract of *Pistacia lentiscus* (Lakesis) has been shown to **increase the expression of “youth proteins” Klotho and FOXO**, which revitalizes aging fibroblasts and boosts dermal rebuilding<u>[gavinpublishers.com](https://www.gavinpublishers.com/article/view/efficacy-of-an-anti-ageing-hydrating-and-emollient-facial-cream-containing-standardized-snail-secretion-sca-a-lipid-fraction-of-pistacia-lentiscus-tree-extract-lakesis-and-a-shiitake-mushroom-based-extract-actifcol-the-moire-trial-a-multicente#:~:text=extract%20,12%2C13%5D.%20This)[gavinpublishers.com](https://www.gavinpublishers.com/article/view/efficacy-of-an-anti-ageing-hydrating-and-emollient-facial-cream-containing-standardized-snail-secretion-sca-a-lipid-fraction-of-pistacia-lentiscus-tree-extract-lakesis-and-a-shiitake-mushroom-based-extract-actifcol-the-moire-trial-a-multicente#:~:text=the%20deterioration%20of%20collagen%2C%20favouring%2C,ageing)</u>. In studies, this extract **re-densified the dermis** by improving fibroblast activity and **reducing collagen degradation** – leading to noticeably improved skin firmness and elasticity[<u>gavinpublishers.com</u>](https://www.gavinpublishers.com/article/view/efficacy-of-an-anti-ageing-hydrating-and-emollient-facial-cream-containing-standardized-snail-secretion-sca-a-lipid-fraction-of-pistacia-lentiscus-tree-extract-lakesis-and-a-shiitake-mushroom-based-extract-actifcol-the-moire-trial-a-multicente#:~:text=the%20deterioration%20of%20collagen%2C%20favouring%2C,ageing).

- **Prevents collagen breakdown:** By reactivating Klotho/FOXO and delivering antioxidant polyphenols, *P. lentiscus* extract helps **inhibit the enzymes that degrade collagen fibers**[<u>gavinpublishers.com</u>](https://www.gavinpublishers.com/article/view/efficacy-of-an-anti-ageing-hydrating-and-emollient-facial-cream-containing-standardized-snail-secretion-sca-a-lipid-fraction-of-pistacia-lentiscus-tree-extract-lakesis-and-a-shiitake-mushroom-based-extract-actifcol-the-moire-trial-a-multicente#:~:text=the%20deterioration%20of%20collagen%2C%20favouring%2C,ageing). This protective effect preserves the skin’s collagen architecture, which translates to fewer wrinkles and less sagging over time (proven in a 12-week trial with significant improvements in skin density and firmness)[<u>gavinpublishers.com</u>](https://www.gavinpublishers.com/article/view/efficacy-of-an-anti-ageing-hydrating-and-emollient-facial-cream-containing-standardized-snail-secretion-sca-a-lipid-fraction-of-pistacia-lentiscus-tree-extract-lakesis-and-a-shiitake-mushroom-based-extract-actifcol-the-moire-trial-a-multicente#:~:text=the%20deterioration%20of%20collagen%2C%20favouring%2C,ageing).

## **Saccharide Isomerate**

- **Deep, long-lasting hydration:** Saccharide isomerate is a plant-derived carbohydrate complex that **binds water to the skin like a magnet**, providing instant and cumulative moisture. It has been shown to provide **up to 72 hours of hydration** after application[<u>scitepress.org</u>](https://www.scitepress.org/PublishedPapers/2019/98375/98375.pdf#:~:text=The%20outstanding%20properties%20of%20Saccharide,higher%20moisturizing%20ability%20than%20glycerin). In fact, tests report it can outperform traditional humectants (retaining *38% more moisture than glycerin* after just 3 minutes)[<u>scitepress.org</u>](https://www.scitepress.org/PublishedPapers/2019/98375/98375.pdf#:~:text=lasting%20up%20to%2072%20hours,higher%20moisturizing%20ability%20than%20glycerin). This long-lasting hydration relieves dryness, flakiness, and gives a plumper, dewy appearance to the skin.

- **Strengthens the skin barrier:** Saccharide isomerate not only moisturizes but also **reinforces the skin’s barrier function**. Studies indicate it upregulates key epidermal proteins **filaggrin and loricrin** (vital for a healthy stratum corneum), leading to improved barrier integrity. By reducing transepidermal water loss and boosting these natural moisturizing factors, it keeps skin comfortably hydrated and less prone to irritation. (Notably, previous research documented a **\>20% reduction in TEWL** within two weeks, signifying a stronger barrier.)

## **Sodium Hyaluronate (Hyaluronic Acid)**

- **Intense hydration & plumping:** Hyaluronic acid is a superstar humectant capable of holding **around 1000 times its weight in water**[<u>pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov</u>](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8322246/#:~:text=modulating%20cellular%20immunity%2C%20regulating%20epidermal,29). Just 1 gram can bind roughly **6 liters of water**[<u>pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov</u>](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8322246/#:~:text=modulating%20cellular%20immunity%2C%20regulating%20epidermal,29), which it draws into the skin. This surge of moisture instantly **plumps up the skin**, smoothing out the look of fine lines and giving a supple, bouncy feel. The deep hydration also improves skin turgor (“springiness”), tackling dryness and crepiness often seen around the eyes.

- **Clinically reduces wrinkles:** Topical hyaluronic acid delivers both immediate and long-term anti-aging benefits. In a 6-week clinical study on photoaged skin, a hyaluronic serum led to a **63% increase in skin hydration** and significantly **diminished wrinkles and fine lines** (fine line depth reduced by ~30% on average)[<u>pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov</u>](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8322246/#:~:text=week%204%2C%20with%20statistically%20significant,%28Fig.%C2%A01). Participants showed marked improvements in skin smoothness, elasticity, and crow’s feet, with dermatologists attributing the results to HA’s filling and moisture-retaining action<u>[pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8322246/#:~:text=The%20investigator%20assessed%20hydration%20and,p)[pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8322246/#:~:text=week%204%2C%20with%20statistically%20significant,%28Fig.%C2%A01)</u>. These findings confirm hyaluronic acid’s role in visibly firming and rejuvenating the eye area.

## **Cupuaçu Seed Butter and Jojoba Esters**

- **Cupuaçu butter – “super-moisturizer” for barrier repair:** Derived from the Amazonian *Theobroma grandiflorum* seed, cupuaçu butter is an **exceptional emollient and antioxidant-rich butter**. It can absorb water up to several times its weight, imparting deep hydration and restoring **skin elasticity and softness**[<u>formulabotanica.com</u>](https://formulabotanica.com/5-amazonian-butters/#:~:text=Presence%20of%20long%20chain%20fatty,TEWL%20of%20SLS%20caused%20irritation). In one study, a skin repair cream with 7.5% cupuaçu butter **significantly reduced irritation and transepidermal water loss** in a surfactant-induced dermatitis model[<u>formulabotanica.com</u>](https://formulabotanica.com/5-amazonian-butters/#:~:text=Presence%20of%20long%20chain%20fatty,TEWL%20of%20SLS%20caused%20irritation). This means cupuaçu butter not only moisturizes but also **calms inflammation and fortifies the skin’s moisture barrier** (even outperforming lanolin in moisture retention). Users often notice smoother, calmer skin with reduced redness and flaking due to cupuaçu’s high phytosterol content and its ability to **prevent moisture evaporation**.

- **Jojoba esters – barrier-mimicking and anti-inflammatory:** Jojoba esters (from *Simmondsia chinensis* oil) closely resemble human sebum, making them ideal for reinforcing the skin’s lipid barrier. Topically, jojoba has demonstrated a **remarkable ability to repair compromised skin**: its high content of wax esters helps fill in gaps in dry or damaged skin, improving hydration and resilience[<u>pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov</u>](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5796020/#:~:text=degradation%20,option%20for%20dermatoses%20with%20altered). Moreover, jojoba oil provides significant **anti-inflammatory benefits**. Research shows it can reduce the production of inflammatory cytokines (like IL-6, IL-8, TNF-α) by roughly 30%, comparable to a mild NSAID effect in skin[<u>pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov</u>](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5796020/#:~:text=match%20at%20L869%20acne%20,99%2C132). Thanks to these properties, jojoba esters help **soothe puffiness, calm redness, and alleviate irritation**, while also protecting the skin from environmental stress. The net result is a smoother, healthier under-eye area with less irritation and enhanced barrier function[<u>pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov</u>](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5796020/#:~:text=degradation%20,option%20for%20dermatoses%20with%20altered).

## **Vitamin C and Vitamin E**

- **Vitamin C – Collagen booster & brightener:** Vitamin C (ascorbic acid) is vital for collagen synthesis and is one of the most effective ingredients for anti-aging. In clinical trials, a daily topical Vitamin C treatment led to **significant wrinkle reduction and new collagen formation in the skin**[<u>pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov</u>](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11896774/#:~:text=unilaterally,placebo%20side%20showed%20unilateral%20improvement). After 3 months, dermatologists observed visible improvement in fine lines, firmness, and overall photodamage, corroborated by biopsies showing **increased collagen fibers** in the dermis<u>[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11896774/#:~:text=side%20was%20statistically%20significant%20,placebo%20side%20showed%20unilateral%20improvement)[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11896774/#:~:text=unilaterally,placebo%20side%20showed%20unilateral%20improvement)</u>. Vitamin C is also a potent antioxidant that **neutralizes free radicals**; by doing so, it helps prevent UV-induced collagen breakdown and can lighten hyperpigmented spots for a brighter under-eye (fading dark circles caused by UV or post-inflammatory pigmentation). *(In one study, a 5% vitamin C cream notably improved under-eye darkness and skin tone after 8 weeks[<u>pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov</u>](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11896774/#:~:text=Results%3A%20%20A%20statistically%20significant,treated%20side%20improved).)  *

- **Vitamin E – Antioxidant protector & soothing agent:** Vitamin E (tocopherol) is a fat-soluble antioxidant that shields cell membranes and lipids from oxidative damage. In synergy with Vitamin C, it **protects the skin from UV and pollution-induced aging**. Formulations combining **Vitamin C + E have shown an 4-8 fold increase in UV protection** for skin, significantly reducing redness, cell damage, and collagen breakdown after sun exposure[<u>pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov</u>](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2923951/#:~:text=with%20repeated%20muscle%20movement%20are,especially%20susceptible%20to%20wrinkle%20formation). Vitamin E also has moisturizing and anti-inflammatory properties: it strengthens the skin’s barrier, calms irritation, and has been reported to reduce swelling in clinical use[<u>pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov</u>](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2923951/#:~:text=with%20repeated%20muscle%20movement%20are,especially%20susceptible%20to%20wrinkle%20formation). By scavenging free radicals, the C&E duo helps **prevent the formation of wrinkles and dark spots**, effectively addressing the oxidative stress that contributes to fine lines, under-eye darkening, and loss of firmness.
