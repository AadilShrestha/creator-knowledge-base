# Being aware of angles - ✍️ Evolve Copywriting (NEW) · Evolve

_Source: `✍️ Evolve Copywriting (NEW)/18. Being aware of angles_✍️ Evolve Copywriting (NEW)_Evolve.html` (Skool lesson page text)_

What is the reason someone should buy this product? 

  
**🔗Angle**⇒The selling strategy \- how you're choosing to sell the product

**12\-Year\-Old Angle Definition:** The main reason you're giving someone to buy   
  
**Examples:**  
*"Your child will enjoy taking their greens" \- Grüns*

*"The easiest way to get your daily greens" \- Grüns*

*"It will help your digestive system" \- Grüns*

*"You won't be drinking microplastics" \- Liquid Death* 

*"Death to plastics" \- Liquid Death* 

*"You'll look cool while you drink us" \- Liquid Death*

*"You'll add more flavor to your meals" \- Spencer's Wagyu Chicken*

*"It will be easier for you to cook" \- Spencer's Wagyu Chicken*

  


***Key insight: Every ad needs an angle. But you most focus on one main angle per ad.***

***You likely have multiple of them based on the functions your product has***

*Even if your concept is "testing formats," you still need to define why someone is going to buy from you*
