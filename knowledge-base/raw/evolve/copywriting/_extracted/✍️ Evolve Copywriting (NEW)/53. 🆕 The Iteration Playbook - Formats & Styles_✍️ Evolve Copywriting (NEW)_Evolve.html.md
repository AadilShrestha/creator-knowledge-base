## **💸 Spend Winner \& 🏆 Breakthrough Ad Iterations**

***Problem We’re Trying To Solve: How to turn spend winners into breakthroughs and how to turn a single breakthrough into multiple breakthroughs that can scale further.*** 

  


## **🖼️ Breakthrough Ad Format/ Ad Style Iterations:**

### **\#1 \- Voiceover ↔ Text\-on\-Screen**

If the original had a voiceover, test a text\-on\-screen version.

If the original was text\-on\-screen, test a voiceover version.

**Example:**  
[VO Video Example](https://canva.link/1cc9dghl7xlx13f "https://canva.link/1cc9dghl7xlx13f")[Text On Screen Example](https://canva.link/wj5capmkclig119 "https://canva.link/wj5capmkclig119")

### **\#2 \- REAL UGC Creators Video**

Take the winning ad components and give it to MULTIPLE UGC creators to recreate.

Get at least 2\-3 different creators to execute the same winning **concept \&** **script** *(if possible)* in their own style. More context below

**Example:**  
[UGC Example](https://canva.link/rkd0dzegyiygbh1 "https://canva.link/rkd0dzegyiygbh1")

***^ Pro tip: Lukas Pakter from Haus ($5M/mo founder) \-\-\> recommends having new creators:***

* 1 Video \- Copy the top winning ad script (word for word if possible)
* 1 Video \- Iterate on the top winning ad (identify the components and do a new script)
* 1 Video \- Imitate from the top [daily viral ads](https://www.skool.com/evolve-8484/classroom/fc758841?md=a4db7083d9654e6590cfcf06b14e3393&utm_campaign=skool_link_classroom&utm_content=99f29f7059f347559e88fdeddd3f5516 "https://www.skool.com/evolve-8484/classroom/fc758841?md=a4db7083d9654e6590cfcf06b14e3393&utm_campaign=skool_link_classroom&utm_content=99f29f7059f347559e88fdeddd3f5516")

  


### **\#3 \- Animated Ingredients, Mechanisms**

Take the winning ad mechanism and personify that mechanism, or specific ingredients to communicate the same angle.

Usually turning your mechanism or ingredients into a hero, is what converts the best.

**Example:**  
[AI Ingredients](https://canva.link/3tca0hlynyrujcw "https://canva.link/3tca0hlynyrujcw")

  


### **\#4 \- AI Animated Solutions**

Take the winning ad components  and personify the other types of solutions that exist in your market.

Usually "villainizing" the other solutions and then showing yours as the hero converts the best.

**Example:**  
[Solutions Example](https://canva.link/qw1rox9r6sadcse "https://canva.link/qw1rox9r6sadcse")

  


### **\#5 \- AI Animated Body Parts or Hormones**

Take the winning ad components and personify specific body parts to communicate the same angle.

Rather than words coming from the mouth of a person, words come from specific body parts that immediately stop the scroll due to the visual juxtaposition 

Having the body parts communicate [new information](https://www.skool.com/evolve-8484/classroom/fc758841?md=7d9c9eab12d44136956da3db820460d7&utm_campaign=skool_link_classroom&utm_content=99f29f7059f347559e88fdeddd3f5516 "https://www.skool.com/evolve-8484/classroom/fc758841?md=7d9c9eab12d44136956da3db820460d7&utm_campaign=skool_link_classroom&utm_content=99f29f7059f347559e88fdeddd3f5516") and talk about the real issues causing the problem, will usually convert the best.

**Example:**  
[AI Body Parts](https://canva.link/bebvuk3tdv3szxf "https://canva.link/bebvuk3tdv3szxf")

### **\#7 \- AI Animated Story (No Human)**

In this version, you will take the winning ad components and turn them into an AI ad that has a **NON human** character telling the story.

You can create these in a variety of styles, whether it's pixar, crochet, claymation, etc.

Some common ad types for this are "Skeletons" or "Fictional characters" 

The main difference here is you're not having a human character tell the story. You're having a separate character tell it.

[AI Animal Example](https://canva.link/sc0pmcywckj4s3k "https://canva.link/sc0pmcywckj4s3k")

  


### **\#8 \- AI Animated Story (Human)**

Take the winning ad components and turn it into ad that has an human character telling the story 

Again same thing here. You can create these in a variety of different styles, whether it's Pixar, crochet, claymation, skeleton, etc.

[Claymation Story Example](https://canva.link/w63syuh2qoku4bq "https://canva.link/w63syuh2qoku4bq")

  


### **\#9 \- AI UGC Narration**

Using an AI person to narrate on top of a video to tell the story.

this is currently the way that we prefer to use AI UGC: we are not pretending the UGC is a real person. We are simply just having the UGC narrate a story.

 think of it as a "visual AI voiceover"

[AI Narration Example](https://canva.link/iqhrykgyzo0h45s "https://canva.link/iqhrykgyzo0h45s")

  
  


### **\#10 \- VSL Expansion**

Expand the core idea into a longer\-form VSL style ad. Go deeper on education, mechanism, and proof.

VSLs are much more labor intensive and require the ability to write a lot of copy that isn't just fluff... Not always easy to do.

But, by expanding the problem section, responding to objections, raising the stakes, creating urgency, (all things you should have learned in the script iteration section) you might find yourself with a "mini VSL".

To be able to write great VSLs, you take practice, and you've really got to be good at maintaining attention. 

If you want to learn how to get better VSLs, I'd recommend studying some of the [VSLs in the Winning Ads Doc.](https://canva.link/eb7bom0b70ohywv "https://canva.link/eb7bom0b70ohywv")

**Examples:**  
[VSL Example](https://canva.link/5ujxjtxknpkidyg "https://canva.link/5ujxjtxknpkidyg")

  


### **\#11 \- Native/Camouflage Ad**

Create a static ad with primary copy that mirrors the winning ad script and components. Feels like organic content, not an ad.

Again, a lot of you are already running native ads and should know what these look like.

Although I do sometimes talk down on these native ads, they are good ad types to test as well, especially if you have a breakthrough 

**Example:**  
[Native Ad Examples](https://canva.link/9j2rp5ojqe57s04 "https://canva.link/9j2rp5ojqe57s04")

  


### **\#12 \- Founder/Authority Delivery**

This is one of my favorite iterations to do if you want to create ads that can't be copied.

If you film a video as a brand founder using or an authority figure (doctor, specialist, expert) to deliver a winning script or NEW script based on the components...

You can 100% create some hyper\-scaling breakthrough ads as it takes the authority to another level

**Examples:**

[Founder Video Example](https://canva.link/aqs3ise04rwuf79 "https://canva.link/aqs3ise04rwuf79")

[Authority Figure Example](https://canva.link/qfroqmtiw3m2ogk "https://canva.link/qfroqmtiw3m2ogk")

  


### **\#13 \- Street Interview Style**

 this one is closer to the bottom because it does require some additional help, usually from third\-party agencies, due to the fact that getting good at cold interviews can be a skill in itself.

But if you have the capital for it, you can hire an interview agency or go and conduct street interviews following the same winning components for a COMPLETELY different ad style.

**Example:**  
[Street Interview Example](https://canva.link/hm20zkrxk4xne83 "https://canva.link/hm20zkrxk4xne83")

  


### **\#14 \- Podcast Style**

This is another great iteration to make if you are able to film this in person, even if it is just on a set. 

Reformat the winning script components into a podcast\-style conversation between two people discussing the topic can add more authority visually and stop a lot of scrolls.

**Example**  
[Podcast Style Example](https://canva.link/2m61n3c5bms54oc "https://canva.link/2m61n3c5bms54oc")

  


### **\#15 \- Stack Winners Mega\-Ad**

This one is more of a wild card, but if you have a few breakthrough ads that are working, you can try combining and stitching 2\-4 winning video clips/hooks from multiple ads together into one longer compilation ad.

 I do not have examples of this, as unfortunately I wasn't able to find any, and I can't share the ones that we've done for our clients. This is something that can be worth a try as long as you're able to stitch it in a way where it all makes sense.
