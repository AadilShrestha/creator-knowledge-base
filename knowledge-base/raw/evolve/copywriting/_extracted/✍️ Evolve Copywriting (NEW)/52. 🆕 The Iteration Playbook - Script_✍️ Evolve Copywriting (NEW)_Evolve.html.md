## **💸 Spend Winner \& 🏆 Breakthrough Ad Iterations**

***Problem We’re Trying To Solve: How to turn spend winners into breakthroughs and how to turn a single breakthrough into multiple breakthroughs that can scale further.*** 

The amount of iterations you have the ability to create can be overwhelming. But that is a good thing

  


## **✍️ Script Iterations:**

### **\#1 \- Components Rewrite**

Breakdown the current winning ad and see where it can be improved based on the 9 components ([Example Here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1GfqPd4bNjjerikandyBhzPqpjpzcVF3qs2D1z0J-ySE/edit?usp=sharing "https://docs.google.com/document/d/1GfqPd4bNjjerikandyBhzPqpjpzcVF3qs2D1z0J-ySE/edit?usp=sharing")) and the copywriting program. 

Identify what is working, what can be optimized, and how you can execute the same components differently. 

This includes testing a more optimized version of the hook, the bridge, and the hold.

Hook, bridge, and hold processes are all inside the [copywriting program:](https://www.skool.com/evolve-8484/classroom/9917539c?md=12282075479d484499b80b03e566e413&utm_campaign=skool_link_classroom&utm_content=99f29f7059f347559e88fdeddd3f5516 "https://www.skool.com/evolve-8484/classroom/9917539c?md=12282075479d484499b80b03e566e413&utm_campaign=skool_link_classroom&utm_content=99f29f7059f347559e88fdeddd3f5516")

### **\#2 \- Emotional Hook Testing**

As mentioned in number one above, you should have been able to identify the emotional feeling of the ad, Valence \& Intensity (1 of the 9 components) 

However, rather than keeping the SAME level of valence and intensity, in this script iteration you can also test DIFFERENT types of valence and intensity.

A lot of the times, we notice that breakthrough ads can benefit more intensity, and by cranking up the intensity, you will likely increase the urgency and raise the stakes, which makes the ad more powerful.

AGAIN BE ETHICAL \-\-\> DON'T BE A PIECE OF SHIT

**Breakthrough Iteration Example:**  
[https://canva.link/iuf83b1clr2xkgi](https://canva.link/iuf83b1clr2xkgi "https://canva.link/iuf83b1clr2xkgi")

You can see in this example if your dog eats grass every day. This is more neutral, but by saying why your dog's grass eating isn't normal, it raises the intensity \& is lower valence (more negative)

### **\#3 \- Bridge Remake**

Similar to the **\#1 \- Components Rewrite** above, your goal with this script iteration is to make the bridge in the ad establish more trust, create more urgency, and keep the audience hooked in.

Usually, you can improve the bridge by adding more authority in this and/or raising the stakes.

**Study bridge processes inside the** [**copywriting program**](https://www.skool.com/evolve-8484/classroom/9917539c?md=a7f1ac99679d462e8bec49e83506db13&utm_campaign=skool_link_classroom&utm_content=99f29f7059f347559e88fdeddd3f5516 "https://www.skool.com/evolve-8484/classroom/9917539c?md=a7f1ac99679d462e8bec49e83506db13&utm_campaign=skool_link_classroom&utm_content=99f29f7059f347559e88fdeddd3f5516")

**Example:**  
[https://canva.link/1mj1uxu5tcuetvr](https://canva.link/1mj1uxu5tcuetvr "https://canva.link/1mj1uxu5tcuetvr")

You can see in this example that the original bridge talks about how "It's not dry skin, it fungus, and although it sounds gross, it's pretty common" ... Whereas the winning iteration bridge says "it's not dry scalp, it's a yeast problem on your head and it's feeding on your scalp like a buffet"

### **\#4 \- In Media Res**

As always, credit to Sarah Levinger

In Media Res iterations are when you start the ad right in the action. AKA you make a cut on the winning ad to have the hook be "right in the action".

Rather than a traditional hook that "sets up" the ad, you just start the ad STRAIGHT in the action and get to the point in the first few seconds.

[Example Of In Media Res \+ Further Explanation](https://www.loom.com/share/cb1d8dc708bd4ae2b6319c45485ad209 "https://www.loom.com/share/cb1d8dc708bd4ae2b6319c45485ad209")

*^ NOTE we have applied this strategy to create an ad that has gone on to spend over $2,000,000\...*

  


### **\#5 \- Extended Problem Section**

This is one of the first iterations that we do on any winning ads that we find. 

You simply create a version with a longer problem/agitation section. 

You go deeper on the pain, add more specific pain points, and make the viewer feel it more before presenting the solution:

**Example:**  
[https://canva.link/bjgpfxinad6hxn3](https://canva.link/bjgpfxinad6hxn3 "https://canva.link/bjgpfxinad6hxn3")

*^ Although this is a spend\-winner iteration, this is a great example of sitting in the problem longer, and by doing so, creating more urgency \& building more relatability.* 

  


### **\#6 \- Awareness levels test**

Create variations for higher/lower awareness levels. 

 if your ad is more problem\-aware, try a solution\-aware style ad. If your ad is potentially more solution\-aware, try a more unaware style ad.

 You can find new breakthroughs by going both ways

### **\#7 \- Overcoming Objections**

If your ad is picking up traction, it should be getting some comments under the ad and also should be getting some objections, depending on how bulletproof your script writing is/

Which in that case, analyze the real objections from comments under your ad and simply create new variations that handle those objections.

You can also test "Comment Reply" style hooks as well, and make a new iteration/ad that handles that specific objections ( usually recommended if you're seeing a lot of the same objection)

**Example Comment:**  
"Does it work for X type of person, like me?" \-\-\> add in to the script that "it even works for X"

**Example Comment:**  
"I've tried X before and this is the same thing. It won't work" \-\-\> add straight into the hook as a comment response, or add it into the script to say *"I used to think X was the same of this, but here are the differences*" etc.  
  


### **\#8 \- Negative Framing**

Flip the entire ad to argue AGAINST the product sarcastically 

"Honestly, don't buy this. Here's why..." then disprove each point.

### **\#9 \- Optimize Script For a Different Sub Avatar**

This one does require more skill, because a lot of people will try to copy the exact same script in the exact same format over to a different sub\-avatar, but will actually end up making the ad worse.

Which is why it's important to understand your winning ad components 

That said, you will take the winning script but rewrite/adjust for a different sub avatar
