# Being aware of market awareness - ✍️ Evolve Copywriting (NEW) · Evolve

Now with how fast information available nowadays, market awareness levels have changed.

Eugene wrote the Breakthrough Advertising before cell phones, the internet, and widespread information was a thing... **(1966\)**

NOWADAYS 60 years later...

People progress through these stages VERY quickly.

Meaning

If you have a problem, you usually don't just sit in this problem\-aware stage.

What do you do?

You google it, search it on TikTok, you ask chat GPT.

When you search for a solution, your immediately bombarded with tons of different options and become aware of tons of solutions, products and product categories in a matter of minutes.

So when looking at market awareness levels in todays landscape, **we have to consider a few things:**

**\#1 \- Unaware**: Is still a very real category. And it's why it can dominate on social platforms. Your goal is to still make people aware of a problem they aren't consciously thinking about.

  


**\#2 \- Problem Aware:** is also still a real category.. BUT, your copy is not to identify the problem **(that's unaware)** it's to make **the prospect understand that they have a problem worth solving.** *"You dramatize the need vividly so the prospect realizes how badly they need the solution" \- Eugene*

  


**\#3 \- Solution Aware:** People who are aware of the solutions that exists for their problem, they just aren't convinced which one will solve it the best. You can start ads at this solution level without raising their existing beliefs on specific products they may have already seen or researched.

The difference between Solution Aware and Product aware is that the person who is solution aware is essentially still considering all of the different types of products to solve their problem

The people who are product aware know what product they want to solve their problem. 

## **EXAMPLE**

**Problem:** Dog is peeing in the house when i leave 

What do I do? \-\-\> I search how to fix dog peeing in the house when i leave

**Solutions**: Get pee pads, put the dog on meds, leave the dog outside, get a dog trainer, etc.

Now that I'm aware of all the solutions, I'll start to dive into the products to see which one will work best for me.

  


**\#4 \- Product Aware:** People are aware of your types of product \& it's category, but they aren't completely aware of all YOUR product does, they aren't convinced it does it well, and they aren't convinced it's right for them. 

## **EXAMPLE**

**Problem Aware:** Dog is peeing in the house every time I leave

What do I do? \-\-\> I search how to fix dog peeing in the house when I leave

**Solution Aware**: Get pee pads, put the dog on meds, leave the dog outside, get a dog trainer, etc.

**Product Aware:** Pee pads are what I want to get, but I'm not sure what one is the best

\-\-

Product aware is biggest it has ever been and is a lot bigger than you think. 

But it's also a lot more sophisticated than you think.

I can go from problem aware \-\-\> most aware in 20 minutes with tools like Amazon.

## **Continuing the Example**

I might Then search "best pee pads on amazon" buy those pee pads because they said that they were top rated on Amazon, but then after I used them, they still don't solve my problem

Now I might have the belief that "pee pads don't work" for my problem

Therefore, now am I NOT ONLY aware of Pee Pad products, I'm most aware of the brand that didn't work AND I now have a bias belief that pee pads don't solve this problem

So then I likely go look at other solutions. 

BUT, THIS PERSON IS STILL PRODUCT AWARE OF PEE PADS.

**Which is why if you can WIN at the product aware level, you will unlock massive scale.**

The problem is, you likely struggle with product aware because you have no positioning *(which I'm trying to help you with)* 

  


**\#5 \- Most Aware:** This one is now more prevalent then ever as well. 

These people have likely seen your ads or heard of your brand before, but they may not trust that your brand specifically can actually deliver to their needs or haven't got the right offer for it.

They essentially need something to push them over the edge to buy.
