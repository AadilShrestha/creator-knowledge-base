# **Ad Learning \& Iteration Process**



---

**Step 1: Identify the batch number**

Pull up the batch in your account. Find the ad in Meta before moving on.

[https://www.skool.com/evolve\-8484/classroom/fc758841?md\=9d61c97ac26848e5b289abc7e505ca07](https://www.skool.com/evolve-8484/classroom/fc758841?md=9d61c97ac26848e5b289abc7e505ca07&utm_campaign=skool_link_classroom&utm_content=99f29f7059f347559e88fdeddd3f5516 "https://www.skool.com/evolve-8484/classroom/fc758841?md=9d61c97ac26848e5b289abc7e505ca07&utm_campaign=skool_link_classroom&utm_content=99f29f7059f347559e88fdeddd3f5516")



---

**Step 2: Identify the outcome**

* 🏆 Breakthrough
* 💸 Spend Winner
* 🎯 KPI Winner
* ❌ Loser

Note the following:

* Total spend the ad received ($ amount)
* Total spend the campaign received in the same period ($ amount)

### 



---

**Step 3: Identify the ad type**

* 💡Ideation — *idea came from research*
* 🎭 Imitation — *idea came from another brand's ad*
* 🔁 Iteration — *idea came from one your existing ads*

### 



---

**Step 4: Identify the hooks verbatim**

Write out every hook exactly as it appears in the ad. Do not summarize.

If the ad received significant spend, also note:

* Hook rate
* Hold rate

*What counts as significant spend will depend on the account — use your judgment based on what's meaningful relative to the campaign (recommendation: $1k in spend minimum after 7 days)*

  




---

**Step 5: Verify components against the Growth Guide \& fill in the rest**

Double\-check what was stored in the Growth Guide against what was actually in the ad. 

Note any differences.

* Market awareness level → Does the copy match what you had planned?
* Valence \+ Intensity → What zone is it?
* Desire → Does the outcome match?
* Avatar → Does the copy communicate to them?
* Angle (the reason someone will buy) → Is the reason clear?
* Mechanism → Is there a new mechanism communicated?
* Belief → What are we doing to build trust? Is there a clear authority or strategic copy?
* Positioning → What are we doing that's different? How have we separated ourselves?
* 🆕Urgency → What is the reason(s) that someone needs to take action today?

*🚨Claude Code Skill will handle pulling what you originally had in the growth guide into your visual fig jam sheet… BUT it's important that you check to make sure that these components were actually executed properly based off of what you planned*

  




---

**Step 6: Write your hypothesis \+ ideas for iterations**

Why do you think this ad performed the way it did? 

What specifically worked or didn't work? 

Consider what is working in your other ads / winning ads in general to avoid making false learnings.

What could be some potential iterations you could make?

Be specific. 

### 



---

**Step 7: Link the original files**

Drop the links here so you can always find the ad later:

* Ad file ([Frame.io](http://frame.io/ "http://Frame.io") or Drive)

---

**Step 9: Follow the iteration playbook**

Based on the outcome from Step 2, go to the relevant section of the Iteration Playbook and identify exactly which iterations you can run next.  
  




---

**Step 10: Name the iteration and track it**

Before you launch ANY iteration, the iteration must be named correctly so you can go back and find it later.

Include **ITER\#\[N]\_BATCH\#\[ORIG\#]** right after the concept name at every level:

* **File:** Client \- Batch\#\[NEW] \- \[Concept] \- ITER\#\[N]\_BATCH\#\[ORIG\#] \- Format \- Editor \- CS \- V\#
* **Ad name:** Batch\#\[NEW]\_\[Concept]\_ITER\#\[N]\_BATCH\#\[ORIG\#]\_URL\_Format\_Editor\_CS\_V\#\_C\_\#\_H\_\#
* **Ad set:** EAM BATCH\#\[NEW] \- ITER\#\[N]\_BATCH\#\[ORIG\#] \- \[Concept] \- \[targeting]

N \= which iteration this is (1st, 2nd, 3rd...)

BATCH\#\[ORIG\#] \= the batch number you're iterating on
