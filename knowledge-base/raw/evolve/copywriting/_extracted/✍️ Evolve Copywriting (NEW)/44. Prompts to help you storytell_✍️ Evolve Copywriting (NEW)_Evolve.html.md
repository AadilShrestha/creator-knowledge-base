### **Brand/Market Focused:**

**Brand Element:**

*Chocolate bar using all natural ingredients*

**Brand Ad:**  
*“We use all natural ingredients to make a better chocolate bar for you"* 

***^No story, nobody cares***

  


***Market Story Ad:***  
*“Do you know what the \#1 ingredient in most chocolate bars is?" I'll give you a hint, it's not Cacao..."*

***^ Story, people care.***

  


***Brand Story Ad:***  
*“Can I make a better chocolate bar than Mr.Beast?"*

***^ Story, people care.***

#### ***Prompts:***

\#1 \- What is something that everyone thinks is true about your product, but isn’t?

\#2 \- What’s something about your product that would frustrate people?

\#3 \- What's a story that proves your product/service is different from competitors 

\#4 \- What made you angry that you had to create this product? (what is your enemy)

\#5 \- What do your competitors hope customers never figure out?

\#6 \- Who is this product NOT for?
