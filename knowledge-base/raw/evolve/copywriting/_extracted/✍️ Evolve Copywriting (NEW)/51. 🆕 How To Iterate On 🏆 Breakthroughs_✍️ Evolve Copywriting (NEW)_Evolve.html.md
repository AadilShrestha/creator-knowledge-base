## **🏆 Breakthrough Ad — Iteration Playbook**

***Problem We’re Trying To Solve: The ad is a breakthrough, but it is dying too fast AND you aren't sure on how to actually dissect and iterate on the ad to create more breakthroughs.*** 

When an ad is identified as a 🏆 Breakthrough Ad, you've accomplished step 1 of scaling your brand.

So, first of all, make sure you give yourself and your team some praise because creating breakthroughs are not easy, and it is literally the goal of every single marketing team.

However, now that you have accomplished creating a breakthrough, you can't just sit back and relax. You have to now push harder than before.

All of the hard work that you've done to crack the breakthrough angle and execution style will got waste UNLESS you learn how to analyze your ad correctly. 

Now, we will touch on the breakthrough iterations that are needed in the following module. However, it's important that we discuss the one issue that is inevitable after creating breakthrough ads that is everybody's worst nightmare.

**AD FATIGUE**

Ad fatigue (aka "the time issue") is a real thing. 

Depending on the type of breakthrough that you've created, sometimes this ad fatigue can hit a lot faster than people expect. 

For example:

Some breakthrough ads that we've created have continued to get spend and performance in our account for 6\+ months. 

Whereas other breakthrough ads last 2\-3 weeks before they start to come crashing down and instead of "maintaining" the NEW level of ad spend that you got to, you have to scale back down to "pre\-breakthrough" levels of spend.

**Extremely frustrating** 

All of a sudden, the holy grail breakthrough ad that was absolutely ripping for you is no longer ripping and you have NO IDEA how that is possible and as a result, usually start spamming "hook iterations" \& "format iterations" that you think are better, but don't convert.

**The \#1 mistake a lot of people make after finding a breakthrough is this:**

When they see a breakthrough ad start to slow down, the see the "ROAS" drop and "CPA inflate" and as a result TURN OFF the breakthrough...

**But need to be be VERY CAREFUL with turning off breakthroughs** 

These breakthrough ads, as you scale them, will naturally come down in performance. 

However, turning them off completely can be catastrophic to the account.

Now, when it comes to how long breakthrough ads can actually last, here is a list of all of the things that will typically cause your breakthrough ad to have a shorter life span and not be able to sustain breakthrough scale for months on end

  


1. **The ad can be easily copied or has a low barrier of entry to make (AI UGC, Native ads)**

When ads are “easy to make” it means they are “easy to replicate”. When ads are easy to replicate it typically means it likely will not sustain performance for a very long time, especially in competitive markets, because it will be copied by someone else. *(This is can happen even faster if you lack strong product differentiation or positioning)*
2. **The ad was copied from a direct competitor**

If you are just copying what someone else is doing and not trying to make something your own, again, it's only a matter of time until someone else copies you back and pulls from your market share.
3. **The Ad is going after a very niche sub avatar or angle that doesn't have a scaleable TAM**

In reference to the avatar training, if you're going after very niche pain points and specific issues, it's not a bad thing, but you might run into issues with how far you can scale it.
4. **The ad is purely offer focused/most aware** 

If you have created an ad that is more offer\-focused/bottom\-of\-funnel, again it might perform extremely well and help you scale for a short period. However, once you clear out that funnel, the ad will likely struggle to sustain scale.  
  
But, that said, there are times where offer ads can stay as strong KPI winners in the account with extremely efficient ROAS numbers/CPA numbers and provide the entire account with a higher overall performance.   
  
because a breakthrough ad doesn't stay as a breakthrough ad for a long period of time doesn't mean that's a bad thing (as long as your finding other breakthroughs, which hopefully, as a result of this training, you will)
5. **Seasonality / outdated visuals**

Another thing that causes breakthrough ads to die is seasonality. For example, when we were running our humidifier ads in January and February (In season), they absolutely crushed. But, in July they completely died down due to seasonality. 

The same thing applies for seasonal messaging and visual fatigue. You might have ads that absolutely crush for gifting during Black Friday and during holiday season, but die in Q1\. You might have ads that creators made in the winter crushing then, but die out in the summer.

Below you will find examples that show you the type of ads that are easily replicable, lack product differentiation, or go after a small TAM.

With that said, now that you have been able to crack a breakthrough ad, the most important thing for you to do is to begin the iteration process.

The iteration process we will cover in the next module starts with REALLY breaking down and reviewing your ads, identifying the components that are making the ad work, and strategically applying them or optimizing them in other formats to get the most out of this breakthrough.

If done correctly, you can have a 1 single breakthrough lead to multiples of other breakthroughs that can scale way past $100k/days

There are **tons** of examples of this in my $100k/day podcast, where people go from hitting their first $100k day to 1\-2 weeks later hitting $150k/day, $200k/day, and so on.

## **What Holds 🏆Breakthrough Ads Back**

### **\#2 \- TAM Issue (breakthrough explanation)**

Even though the ad is a breakthrough, it might not be a long\-term breakthrough due to the angle, messaging, or labels are too niche/specific. 

***Example:***  
For example, Mars Men has a breakthrough on the on the "Considering TRT" angle. However, that might not be as big as their breakthrough on "Beer Belly" angle. as I would assume, more men are dealing with the issue of having a beer belly than those considering TRT.

It doesn't mean that it's a BAD thing. It just means that some angles are just going to be more scalable than others

### **\#13 \- Easily Replicable 🆕**

Most of the time If your breakthrough ad is dying out quickly, it means that it could be easily replicated or "ripped" by competitors. 

Instances of this that we see are native ads and other very low\-effort ads.

 this gets amplified even more, especially if you have a product or a brand that lacks differentiation or positioning (explained below) which makes it even easier for competitors to copy your claims.

### **\#14 \- Product Differentiation 🆕**

Another issue that we see with breakthrough ads that die very quickly is that the brand and the product itself lack positioning or differentiation. 

Especially with AI nowadays, it doesn't take long for someone to build a store, copy your ads, and launch the exact same product inside a Facebook and steal market share.

It's an unfortunate part for those who do not have a strong brand or clear product differentiation. But. it's a real reason why breakthrough ads will die faster than usual.
