# **Video Ad Winner — Iteration Playbook**

**<u>Identify:</u>**

1.  What is the desire?

2.  Who is the avatar?

3.  What is the angle? (What is the problem we are solving)

4.  What is the mechanism?

5.  What is the authority building trust?

6.  What is the market awareness level?

7.  What is the emotion of the ad? (Valence + Intensity)

8.  What are we doing that's different? (positioning)

**<u>Video Example:  
<https://www.facebook.com/reel/944136188548580></u>**

**<u>Uproot Clean Video:</u>**

1.  **What is the desire?**

***Desire(s):***  
I want to have a clean washing machine → Why? → Because it’s making my clothes smell bad → Why *(do you care if they smell bad)*? → Because the whole point of cleaning your clothes is for them to smell good → Why? → *well if they smell bad, that could be a sign of a health hazard  *

2.  **Who is the avatar?**

Pet owner with a washing machine that does not smell good  
**Experiences:** clothes have smelled musty for months  
House smells like damp fur  
**Behaviors:**

Trying different detergents  
Scrubbing the inside rubber with bleach  
Hot cycles with vinegar  
The extra rinse cycles  
They tried SPECIFIC BRAND

**Emotions:**

Frustrated  
Gross  
**Demographic:** Pet owner

3.  **What is the angle? (What is the problem we are solving)**

Will fix the bad smell coming from your washing machine

4.  **What is the mechanism?**

Enzyme Tablet → further explained as well

5.  **What is the authority building trust?**

The 30-year washing machine Grandpa

The language being used Unaware parts in the video (explaining the deep down details)

6.  **What is the market awareness level?**

Solution Aware → Unaware parts later in the video

7.  **What is the emotion of the ad? (Valence + Intensity)**

Low Valence, Low Intensity

8.  **What are we doing that's different? (positioning)**

The enemy is surface level washing machine cleaners

Their positioning is they are the first-and-only to create an enzyme based cleaning tablet

## **Format & Style Iterations**

| **\#** | **Name** | **Description** | **Priority** |
|----|----|----|----|
| 9 | **VO ↔ Text-on-Screen** | If original had voiceover, test a text-on-screen only version. If original was text-on-screen, test a voiceover version. | 🔴 High |
| 10 | **Organic/Ugly Edit** | Re-edit the ad with ugly, simple editing like a normal person would make. Raw, native, unpolished. | 🔴 High |
| 11 | **VSL Expansion** | Expand the core idea into a longer-form VSL style ad. Go deeper on education, mechanism, and proof. | 🔴 High |
| 12 | **AI Animation Version** | Take the winning script and create an AI-animated version. Different visual style, same message. | 🟡 Medium |
| 13 | **Podcast Style** | Reformat the winning script into a podcast-style conversation between two people discussing the topic. | 🟡 Medium |
| 14 | **New Location/Setting/Vibe** | Same script and concept, different visual environment. New room, outdoor vs. indoor, different backdrop. | 🟡 Medium |
