Before you make any pitch, you have to:

### **\#1 \- Ensure you delivered your payoff**

* You've given your 4 reasons why
* You've filled the curiosity gap.
* You've delivered on your promise.

  


Great payoffs USUALLY lead to great shares because payoffs make people:

* Feel like they got value
* Feel like they discovered something new (they're first)
* Feel like it could help someone they know

  


**Side\-Tip:**  
Your payoff can be also be your HOOK *(Show the payoff/desire outcome in the hook, then explain how it got there)*

### **\#2 \- Ensure you've established belief.**

* You've built relatability
* You've provided a new mechanism
* You've provided proof
* You've leveraged authorities
* You've prevented/handled objections

  


**Once you have done both of those, only then does it make sense for you to pitch. But the way that you pitch matters.**  


### **Examples of how to pitch**

When it comes to pitching, a lot of brands nowadays choose to take the non\-biased approach and make "recommendations" rather than pitches.

However, if you have a real purpose behind your company, and a real reason behind your brand, you can share those reasons directly as a company.

Something that is also very important is **how to transition into the pitch**

*NOTE\* Of course for offer pitches and most aware style ads, we're pitching directly in the ad.*

**Transition patterns:**

* **"Now here's the thing..."** (shifts from education to recommendation)
* **"So if you're wondering where to start..."** (invitation, not push)
* **"The one caveat is..."** (natural objection that leads to product)
* **"Here's what I did..."** (personal story bridge)
* **"Now everyone in my comments asked about this"** (personal story bridge)

**Examples: Not From Company**

Non\-urgent

* *"An AUTHORITY recommend me to try this"*
* *"If you want the one I'm using, it's from X brand"*
* *"I found these from X company"*
* *"I chose this brand's because X reason, Y reason, Z reason"*
* *"Over X people switched to this brand because of X"*
* "If you're curious, link below"

Urgent:

* *"The ones linked below are viral and keep selling out"*
* *"This one is selling out fast, but it's linked below"*
* *"I think they have a huge sale going on right now but not sure how long it will last"*

  


**Examples: From Company**

Non Urgent:

* *"We started X brand because of Y issue"*
* *"If you want to try our products, we do have X guarantee"*

Urgent

* *"We do have a sale active right now, while supplies last"*
* *"We have sold out X times now, so if you want to try X, link is below"*
