# How to raise the stakes - ✍️ Evolve Copywriting (NEW) · Evolve

_Source: `✍️ Evolve Copywriting (NEW)/30. How to raise the stakes_✍️ Evolve Copywriting (NEW)_Evolve.html` (Skool lesson page text)_

People care MORE when there is MORE at stake.

**Stakes \= What you stand to GAIN or LOSE**

**The bigger the potential gain or loss, the more people will pay attention**  
  


Gameshow for $10,000 VS Mr.Beasts $5,000,000 on the line \= high stakes

Poker hand for $5 VS $1,000,000 \= high stakes

Proposing on a beach VS proposing in a stadium \= High stakes  
  




---

  
After writing ad, ask yourself, how can we have more at stake? How can we create more drama? How can we make the ad feel like a big deal? 

## **5 Ways to Raise Stakes**

  


**\#1 \- MAKE IT BIGGER (escalation)**

Increase the scale, severity, or extremity.

**Examples:**

* Dog pees in house → dog ruins $10,000 hardwood floors
* Kid struggles to talk → Kid is diagnosed as nonverbal
* increased energy → Wake up feeling like you could run through a wall
* The internet is raising my kids → I don't want the Kardashians raising kids


	+ "Internet" (not visual, not extreme) → Kardashians (very visual, very extreme)

**The question:** Can I make the problem/solution more extreme?

  


**\#2 \- ADD WITNESSES**

Introduce an audience, authority, or social pressure.

**Examples:**

* Dog pees in house → dog pees in front of our landlord
* Fishing under a bridge → Cop found us fishing under a bridge
* Losing the weight → Your kids watch you do the hard thing and learn they can too

**The question:** Who's watching? Who will see if this goes right/wrong?

  


**\#3 \- MAKE IT URGENT**

Add time pressure or point of no return.

**Examples:**

* Improves teeth → The damage is happening RIGHT NOW
* Dog pees in house → every pee in the house reinforces that behavior
* Now is the time to do it → You're at the PERFECT age to do this (gets harder every year)

**The question:** Why does this matter RIGHT NOW? 

  


**\#4 \- MAKE IT PERMANENT**

Show that damage can't be undone or opportunity won't come back. 

**Examples:**

* Dental issues → LIFETIME of problems
* Dental issues → LIFETIME of people complimenting you on your smile.
* Cheap razors → Ingrown hairs create PERMANENT scarring/dark spots
* High\-quality wallets → Your grandkids will use this wallet

**The question:** What's lost/gained forever if they don't act?

  


**\#5 \- MAKE IT COST MORE**

Add financial, relational, time\-based, or emotional price tags.

**Examples:**

* Crashes from coffee → every morning coffee is robbing you of 3 hours
* Snoring issue → Your wife sleeps in another room (intimacy gone)
* Dental Issue → Cosmetic surgery can costs $1,000s

  


**The question:** What's this REALLY costing them? (money, relationships, time)

  


## **Ad Specific Examples:**

**DNA Kit**

This DNA kit tells you your heritage \-\-\> Low stakes

This DNA kit could predict when you might die \-\-\> High stakes

***What creates the stakes?***   
You can find out when you might DIE? **(MAKE IT BIGGER)**

My wife thinks I forgot her birthday \-\-\> High stakes

***What creates the stakes?***   
Forgetting your wife's birthday could cost your relationship **(MAKE IT COST MORE)**
