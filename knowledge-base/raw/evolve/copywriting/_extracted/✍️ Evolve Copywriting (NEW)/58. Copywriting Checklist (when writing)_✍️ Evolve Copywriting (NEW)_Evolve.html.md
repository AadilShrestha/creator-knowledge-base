# Copywriting Checklist (when writing) - ✍️ Evolve Copywriting (NEW) · Evolve

_Source: `✍️ Evolve Copywriting (NEW)/58. Copywriting Checklist (when writing)_✍️ Evolve Copywriting (NEW)_Evolve.html` (Skool lesson page text)_

1. Who am I writing this for? (Sub avatar \+ beliefs)
2. Why am I writing it for them?
3. Am I starting broad or targeted?
4. Does my hook have a gap, trigger emotion, and have high stakes?
5. What is the mechanism?
6. What is the authority?
7. Is the copy visual?[***(Proofing your writing with AI module)***](https://www.skool.com/evolve-8484/classroom/9917539c?md=9a8286783bfc4331b7d073ef39792696&utm_campaign=skool_link_classroom&utm_content=99f29f7059f347559e88fdeddd3f5516 "https://www.skool.com/evolve-8484/classroom/9917539c?md=9a8286783bfc4331b7d073ef39792696&utm_campaign=skool_link_classroom&utm_content=99f29f7059f347559e88fdeddd3f5516")
8. Is the copy falsifiable (can somebody say it's true/false?)
9. Can nobody else write this? **(Search your headline on Google with “ “)**
10. What is this copy actually communicating?
11. Is this writing at a 6th grade reading level?
12. Can this be condensed further without losing meaning?
13. Would you say this door\-to\-door? (spoken not written)
