# Being aware of authorities - ✍️ Evolve Copywriting (NEW) · Evolve

_Source: `✍️ Evolve Copywriting (NEW)/20. Being aware of authorities_✍️ Evolve Copywriting (NEW)_Evolve.html` (Skool lesson page text)_

Your mechanism alone may NOT build enough belief. Which where authority is even more crucial.

Signal authority BEFORE you make your pitch, not during.

What authority do you have?

*"Your child will enjoy taking their greens" \- Grüns*

*Mechanism \-\-\> It's in a gummy bear format*  
  
***Authority \-\-\> I'm a mom of 5 and all my kids look forward to taking them***

  


*"You won't be drinking microplastics" \- Liquid Death* 

*Mechanism \-\-\> It's in can*

***Authority \-\-\-\>* 1L of bottle water can contain 240,000\+ plastic particles.**

  


*"You'll add more flavor to your meals" \- Spencer's Wagyu Chicken*

*Mechanism \-\-\> higher % of fat content*

***Authority \-\-\> The chicken used in Michelin starred restaurants*** 

  


*"A hoodie that supports mental health" \- Comfrt*

*Mechanism \-\-\> donating to charity*

***Authority \-\-\> Volume of creators and virality of the brand***
