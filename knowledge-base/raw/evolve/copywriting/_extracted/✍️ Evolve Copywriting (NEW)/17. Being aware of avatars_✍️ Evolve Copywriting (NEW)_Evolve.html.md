**Who am I writing this for?**   
  
Beginner at\-home chef  
*Desire \-\-\> Wants to impress her family  
Experience \-\-\> Overcooked Chicken breast badly  
Emotion \-\-\> Feels embarrassed when dinner doesn't taste great  
Behavior \-\-\> She never makes chicken dinner anymore*  
  


**Why do they care?**  
They want to impress their family. But they have the belief they can't cook chicken properly.  
  
Our product is a higher fat\-content chicken that allow them to over cook chicken without drying it out.   
  
Beginner Chef:  
**Never overcook chicken again**  
  
Professional Chef:  
**The wagyu of chicken**
