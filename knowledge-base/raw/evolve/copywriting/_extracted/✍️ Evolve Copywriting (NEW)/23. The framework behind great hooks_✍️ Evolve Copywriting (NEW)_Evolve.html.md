# The framework behind great hooks - ✍️ Evolve Copywriting (NEW) · Evolve

_Source: `✍️ Evolve Copywriting (NEW)/23. The framework behind great hooks_✍️ Evolve Copywriting (NEW)_Evolve.html` (Skool lesson page text)_

Hook is the \#1 job of the writer.

Your hook has to be STICKY ...

When your fishing, you don't just want the fish to bite on the hook and then fall of

You want the fish to STAY on the hook so you can reel it in.

Same idea applies to your hook on ads.

You might have a great hook that gets a lot of bites

But if you can't reel it in, who cares?  
  


## **Content Creators Goals:****90% retention for the first 6 seconds.**

### **Hook Must\-Haves**

* EMOTION
* CURIOSITY GAP
* HIGH STAKES

  
**Hook Example *(Full Credit Adley)***

*“This 25\-year\-old kid knows exactly how I’m going to die just from this.”* 

An entertainment wrapper that funnels all the way down to a genetic testing kit 

End of the day, it's a genetic testing kit...

But this one tells you “how you're going to die.” 

It’s HOW they positioned it… 

Insane example… 

Made themselves a category of 1\.  
  


**Hook Example:**

*“This is how my nonverbal toddler found his voice"*

Emotion \-\-\> Hope 

Curiosity \-\-\> How did a nonverbal toddler find it's voice?

High Stakes \-\-\> Diagnosed nonverbal 

  


**Hook Example:**

*“ I've had four cats in my life, and I'm embarrassed to say I just learned this "*

Emotion \-\-\> embarrassment 

Curiosity \-\-\> What did this authority just learn?

High Stakes \-\-\> Cat being thrown into a boat

### **Start in Action**

Don’t over explain it, START it right in the action (even for "yap sessions")

**Examples:**

* [Somebody literally choking (visual)](https://www.facebook.com/61550331875401/posts/122275313198011062 "https://www.facebook.com/61550331875401/posts/122275313198011062")
* [Authority talking about pros \& cons](https://www.facebook.com/100063662435276/posts/1711704544294944 "https://www.facebook.com/100063662435276/posts/1711704544294944")

  


**Have 0 backstory**

We are making videos that users were NOT planning on watching

You can’t make them feel like they are missing something about you

  


### **Zero Chaos**

Crazy editing, too much to focus on, sensory overload, a CONFUSED brain will scroll.

***Curious NOT confused.***

When in doubt, dumb it down.

  


### **Understandable Without Sound**

* Straight forward
