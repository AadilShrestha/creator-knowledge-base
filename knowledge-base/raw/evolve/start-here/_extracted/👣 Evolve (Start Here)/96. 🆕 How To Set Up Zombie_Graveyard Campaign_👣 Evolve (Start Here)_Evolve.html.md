**This campaign is a CBO Cost Cap Campaign for ads that have “failed.”**

Our goal with this campaign is a low\-maintenance way to try to get "more" out of each creative test without burning budget. 

This campaign will prioritize "failed ads" that can achieve the best CPAs and attempt to generate an ROI from each creative test. 

We have also found (in the [seasonal ads section](https://www.skool.com/evolve-8484/classroom/fc758841?md=7a349fe0741d4d828cac6be1dc532441&utm_campaign=skool_link_classroom&utm_content=99f29f7059f347559e88fdeddd3f5516 "https://www.skool.com/evolve-8484/classroom/fc758841?md=7a349fe0741d4d828cac6be1dc532441&utm_campaign=skool_link_classroom&utm_content=99f29f7059f347559e88fdeddd3f5516")) that, based on the market and things that happen in the real world, desires will shift, and Meta may favor ads that "failed" during specific testing periods, but now have an opportunity to drive some sales.
