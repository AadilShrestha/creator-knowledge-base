**Dopamine Definition:**  
Dopamine is often called the "**feel\-good**" hormone because it's released in the brain when we experience pleasure or reward. It plays a key role in our brain's reward system, motivating us to seek out enjoyable activities and experiences. Think of it as the chemical messenger that makes you want to do something again because it felt good the first time.
