**<u>List Of Reasons Your Ad Is Failing (Execution Wise)</u>**

1.  **Your copywriting lacks emotional impact**

    1.  Your ad doesn’t make people FEEL anything. You want them to FEEL something. The more it feels like an internal conversation your avatar would have in their head, the higher the emotional impact

2.  **Your ad doesn’t create curiosity**

    1.  Curiosity is one of the most important things to leverage in execution because, without it, nobody will care to watch your ad or click through. You need to naturally give people a reason to actually care, STOP what they are doing and watch/click your ad.

3.  **Your ad doesn’t create suspense *(video)***

    1.  There is no reason for the person to keep watching your ad. Once you've hooked somebody in, that's only the first step. You need to maintain the suspense in order for people to continue to watch your ad. If there is no REWARD coming, there's no reason for them to keep watching *(Mr. Beast example)  *

4.  **Your ad is too complex**

    1.  If your ad reads higher than a grade six level, it's probably going over people's heads. You need to use language that more people understand, and my favorite tool for using this is the [<u>Hemingway app</u>](https://hemingwayapp.com/).

5.  **Your ad uses empty claims**

    1.  This is one of the most common reasons why I see angles fail is that you have empty claims. For example, you say that you have the best X or you're better than Y. But you FAIL to provide an *<u>actual reason</u>* for people to believe it.

6.  **Your ad uses bossy claims**

    1.  Another one of my favorites is seeing people say in their ads, "watch these next 5- seconds" or “You need to stop scrolling”. Have these worked for some people? Sure, but most of the time, telling people what to do when they don't care who you are or what you have to say is a terrible strategy.

7.  **Your ad uses repetitive claims**

    1.  If you're making the same claims, the SAME WAY, that your competitors are… There is no reason for them to believe you. Remember, they have heard it all before. So you have to find NEW ways to provide the information.

8.  **Your ad has a written script & NOT a spoken one**

    1.  This is something that so many people forget. The way that you SPEAK is a lot different than the way that you write. So, if you write a script and give it to an AI for a voiceover, it is NOT going to sound natural. You need to make sure you upload scripts that sound SPOKEN ***(Using voice-to-text or asking AI to do that for you)  ***

9.  **You wrote the ad for yourself**

    1.  Your ad is written in the way that YOU want to write it and uses words that YOU use… Instead of using your customer's exact words and writing it in a way that your customer would like.

10. **The angle in your ad is too broad**

    1.  Now, because of this avatar training, hopefully this is not a problem for you, but some of you are just not making ads or writing your ads in a way that are relatable enough. Oftentimes, after doing this training, you just ignore the training and go back to copying templates.

11. **The angle in your ad is being said by everyone else (Sophistication)**

    1.  The angle that you're going after is a very competitive angle, and because you lack a skill set in copywriting, you are unable to stand out in your marketplace.

12. **The style of editing you're using doesn't resonate with the demographic**

    1.  Now, I know I say demographics don't matter. However, when it comes to consuming content, they definitely do… You might be making ads that YOU think look good as a 20- 25y/o, where you need to make ads that look good for a 55-65y/o. You need to align your LENS with your customer (VSL for a genz)  
        ***  ***

13. **You chose a bad format**

    1.  Potentially, the ad format that you chose to execute the video on just wasn't the best way to execute it. For example, if you decide to do a Static ad. Just because that format didn’t work doesn't mean the angle doesn't work. You may have just chosen a bad format, and you need to test a better execution of it.  
          
          
        Just know that the easier the format is, the BETTER the messaging and copy have to be.

1.  **Test Multiple Formats Per Angle  **

- Concept 1: Product photography + Headline

- Concept 2: Static/Graphic Design

- Concept 3: Voiceover with b-roll

If all 3 fail, it's likely a ***strategy.***

If 2 fail but one provides a direction, it is ***execution,*** and you just need to focus on communicating it better.

2.  **Use Proven Creative Templates**

Before testing new avatars/angles, validate your creative execution ability using:

- Winning ad structures from your account

- Similar winning ad structures from the industry as a whole

And when I say “Structures”, I mean HOW you wrote the video + edited the ad itself.

This allows you to eliminate a bit of the guessing on “does this angle work” because you’re using an execution style that previously worked.

**🚨2 IMPORTANT THINGS**

1.  If you are testing a vastly different avatar, then you might need to optimize a DIFFERENT structure for that avatar

2.  What is winning your account is obviously not winning because you want to scale further. So I like the idea of analyzing ads that are actually scaling further, rather than ads that have you stuck at your current spend.

### Creative Quality Benchmarks

These are not metrics that are going to 100% tell you that your ad is bad. But when I look across the board on our winning ads, these are some metrics that I see.

- Hook Rate is at least 25%

- Hold Rate is at least *30% o*f whatever the hook rate is *(thruplays/impressions)*

- CTR is at least 1%

If your ads are below these metrics, what you said or showed is likely not resonating enough for people to even care to stop and pay attention.

But again, these are soft metrics. There are ads with 50% hook rates that suck and ads with 20% hook rates that crush.

Use them as a guide to help you understand if people are actually engaging with your ads and clicking through.

**<u>FAQs</u>**

1.  **How long should you test a specific avatar/angle until you write off that avatar?**

There is no set amount.

With that said, if you have tested a Marksman concept and it didn't work. But, you were confident in that avatar, you can definitely test a 2nd batch using a sniper method approach to give it a true shot and try to validate it.

After that, if you complete a feedback loop on the ad and you're confident that it was an execution issue as to why it didn't convert, you can make a third.

However, anything after 3 is likely just a sign that you can't execute this angle properly, and so you might need to move on to a different angle.

2.  **How many concepts should I make on an angle?**

If it's a losing ad, it's the same as the above.

If it's a winning ad, you can make as many as you want. Just be careful of iteration overload because at some point you can overiterate on an angle, and you might need to take a break and focus on other angles instead to give your brain a ”reset” and “space” to create.
