In this module, I'm going to help you understand why we go through the things that we do in this upcoming series so you can TRULY understand the strategy behind Evolve.

A lot of courses tell you **<u>what</u>** to do

Some courses show you or tell you **<u>how</u>** to do it.

But very few actually explain **<u>WHY</u>** you should do it → *Other than the obvious “so you can make more money”*

So, my goal with this module is to help you understand the **<u>WHY</u>** behind every step in this section.

So…

**<u>WHY DOES THIS SECTION MATTER?</u>**

Because the REAL secret behind winning ads that most people don’t understand

Is the SHITLOAD of work winning ads required prior to making them

**AKA your preparation.**

The effort you put in before making ads is the difference between

Making ads that scale and making ads that don’t.

So…

Before you start to just “make ads”

You have to understand **<u>WHAT</u>** makes ads work

And in order to understand **<u>WHAT</u>** makes them work

You need to go deep on marketing fundamentals and learn the psychology behind why people buy

And that first begins with product

1.  **Why do you need to understand your product?**

Well, it's pretty hard to solve someone's problem or provide value to someone if you don't actually understand what value your product provides.

So going deeper on your product, allows you to know what value you can provide to others

All you're trying to do is convince someone that your product provides the same value (ideally more) than what you charge.

2.  **Why do you need to understand desire?**

Desire is simply what people WANT.

People buy things out of “wants” and “needs”

So if your going to convince someone to buy, you need to understand at a deep level WHY that person wants to buy

That “deeper why” is their desire

If your stuck on desires, use the “Why Train”

I want to get a new shirt → Why? → Because I want to improve my style → Why? → Because I want to look more attractive → Why? → Because I want to be in a relationship and fall in love

(Now we are getting somewhere)

^We use this information to help guide our ads.

3.  **Why do you need to understand market sophistication?**

As much as you know your product can provide value, and as much as you know that there are specific people who have desires that you can help

Most people will NOT believe you

OR

Have already tried your “thing” to fulfill their desire

The best way to explain market sophistication

Imagine you see an ad on Facebook that says, "I will make you a millionaire in ecom”

Are you going to believe that person?

Probably not, because you've heard other people make that claim.

That is market sophistication

And that is exactly how your customers feel

So if you don’t understand how to “cut through” that sophistication that exists in the market in order to build **<u>BELIEF</u>**

What your product does, and your customers desires, are irrelevant.

**Because you likely aren’t good at writing copy**

OR

But now that you are aware of that, you can push yourself to get better at either:
