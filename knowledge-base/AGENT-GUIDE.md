# AGENT-GUIDE: read this first
> This knowledge base is reference material for AI agents that make **performance ad creative** for our own DTC brands: tear down competitor ads that are working, work out which angles and psychology make them work, remix the **mechanism** for our product, and produce our own **UGC videos** and statics, then test and iterate. Today it holds two courses: **Tether Lab** by Sarah Levinger (consumer psychology for DTC ad creative: *why* ads work) and **Evolve Copywriting** by Spencer Pawliw (copy craft, video and static ad writing, and iteration after tests: *how to write and fix* them).

## How to navigate
1. **Task?** Find it in the table below and open that playbook. Follow its numbered steps and output its template.
2. **Playbook step names a framework** → open it for the scoring tables and decision rules.
3. **Need examples** → [libraries](wiki/libraries/README.md) (hooks, angles, rewrites, teardowns, prompts) and [brands](wiki/brands/README.md) for your niche.
4. **Need the evidence** → the session/lesson page cited, then `raw/` transcripts.
5. **Lookup by name** → [index](wiki/index.md).

## The core lenses (apply to every ad)
| Lens | Question | Values | Run with |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identity first | Which self does it speak to? | Actual / Ideal / Ought (0–10 each) | [three-selves diagnostic](wiki/frameworks/three-selves-diagnostic.md) |
| Emotion | Valence × intensity: which zone, and does it fit the buyer's state? | Z1 feel-good · Z2 hype · Z3 pain whisper · Z4 danger | [valence-intensity diagnostic](wiki/frameworks/valence-intensity-diagnostic.md) |
| Specific emotion | Which purchase emotion, how specific? | 9 mindstates → sub-emotion | [mindstate mapping](wiki/frameworks/mindstate-emotion-mapping.md) |
| Tactics | Which heuristics, stacked how? | 2–3, fluency as base | [heuristics hub](wiki/concepts/heuristics.md) |
| Journey | Which stage is it written for? | Trigger / Exploration / Evaluation / Purchase | [TEEP](wiki/frameworks/teep-journey.md) |
| Behaviour | Motivation, ability or prompt missing? | B = MAP; find the ability crusher | [Fogg](wiki/frameworks/fogg-behavior-model.md) |
| Story | Which structure carries it? | Pixar, In Medias Res, SCQA, Freytag, skeptic… | [story frameworks](wiki/frameworks/story-frameworks-for-ads.md) |
| Layout | What does the eye hit first? | heaviest → next heaviest | [visual weight](wiki/frameworks/visual-weight-hierarchy.md) |

Priority (Sarah): **identity → emotion → behaviour/tactics**; heuristics are "secondary characters".

**Copy lenses (Evolve; apply to every line you write):** three checks (visualise / falsify / nobody else can say it) · facts over opinions · spoken, grade ≤6 · awareness level · hook = emotion + curiosity gap + stakes, then a bridge · show, don't tell · payoff and belief before the pitch. Score with the [copy checklist scorecard](wiki/courses/evolve/copywriting/rubrics/copy-checklist-scorecard.md).

## Non-negotiable rules
1. **Copy the mechanism, not the asset.** Remix angles, structures and psychology. Never reuse competitors' footage, images, exact script lines, characters, catchphrases, trademarks, claims or stats. Use the fidelity check in [angle-remix](wiki/playbooks/angle-remix.md).
2. **Never fabricate** proof, testimonials, reviews, ratings, counts, press, endorsements, results, scarcity or urgency. Every claim needs a `truth_ref` in the product truth sheet ([template](wiki/playbooks/README.md#product-truth-sheet-required-before-remixing)). Course examples that broke this (Net Positive's invented "Only 9 sets left", ChatGPT's invented press bar) are warnings, not models.
3. **Flag instructors' uncited stats** (Sarah's and Spencer's; e.g. Evolve's "$1M ad", "96% of dog owners" and the GLP-1 percentages are illustrative or instructor claims). Sarah's examples: "0.48 s", "0.2 s", "60,000× faster", "+13.6% likability", "5.43×", "KFC +56%". Treat all of these as anecdotes. Never put them in ad copy or client decks as fact.
4. **When sources disagree**, apply the default in [contradictions](wiki/contradictions.md) (cross-creator rows X1–X29) or the [Evolve contradictions](wiki/courses/evolve/copywriting/contradictions.md) and say so.
5. **Don't shame the viewer.** Negative zones target situations or industry beliefs and end in relief or hope.
6. **Mark your inferences.** Tags, timings and thresholds that aren't Sarah's are labelled (derived), (wiki tag) or (pipeline design). Keep that labelling in your outputs.
7. **One learning goal per test, one core emotion per ad, one variable per iteration.**

## How to cite
- Cite the wiki page and the video moment: `[Roast My Ads #3](wiki/courses/tether-lab/05-live-recordings/2025-07-23-roast-my-ads-3.md) [0:19:30]`. Lessons use `[mm:ss]`, live sessions `[h:mm:ss]`; Evolve pages cite `[L26 03:15]` = lesson 26 at 03:15; documents use page numbers (e-book p.5).
- Quote verbatim lines in quotation marks; label paraphrase, transcription uncertainty and your own derivations.
- In artifacts, cite teardown cards by ID (`TD-…`), customer evidence by item ID (`REV-12`), and claims by `truth_ref`.

## How to add new knowledge
- New courses and sources follow the ingestion contract in the repository root [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) (creator-local paths, manifests, PROGRESS.md) and the schema and ingest workflow in [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) (page conventions, frontmatter, "Agent use" block, structured tables).
